The words name which shapes {holder} accepts, a container's standing rule: write a query that matches exactly the shapes it lets in, as a test of one shape. A phrase that says what it refuses ("no bugs", "everything but notes") is a :not of those shapes, a list of what it takes ("tasks and bugs") is a comma list, and "only tasks" is just the tasks. The words may be commands to the holder ("keep bugs out", "allow only tasks", "reject people"): they are its rule, never a request to change the board. Say nothing of where the shape sits (no space or > combinator, :inside, :top-level, :current-board): where it sits is the holder's business; what it holds or is joined to (:has, arrows and relationships) may be tested.
