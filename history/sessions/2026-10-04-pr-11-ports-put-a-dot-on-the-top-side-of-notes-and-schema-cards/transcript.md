# PR #11: Ports: put a dot on the top side of notes and schema cards

## Description

## What

Notes (`NoteLinks.swift`) and schema cards (`SchemaLinks.swift`) declared their ports on every side but the top, because a titled shape's pill sits in the row above its top edge. Skipping the top was a mistake. Both now call `ports(Channel.self)` and take `PortSide`'s default, so they have ports on top, right, bottom and left. Nothing in `Connections` changes: `PortState.dots(of:)` already draws a dot for any side in the kind's port table, and `ArrowPortsPlugin` already binds `.top`.

## Nothing was built or tested

This was written in an environment with no Swift toolchain. I could not compile, run `./test.sh`, or run the benchmarks. Every change below is from reading the code; please run `./build.sh && ./test.sh` (at least `CompositionTests`' "Ports, as shipped" and "The canvas skill", and `BenchmarkTests`' "Frame benchmarks") before merging.

## The pill and the top dot

The top dot is 12 view points above the middle of the top edge, which is inside the pill's row (9 to 27 points up). A title long enough to reach the middle of the top edge now runs under the dot. I left this alone rather than add behavior:

- Dots already draw above adornments (`PortsPlugin` comes after `AdornmentsPlugin`) and are pressed before them, as with the lock chip.
- The dot is a small, hover-only affordance, and the rest of the pill is still the shape's to grab.
- Keeping the pill clear of it would need Adornments or Titles to know about ports, which the layering doesn't allow.

The docs now say this.

## Tests changed

  - the Escape test and the shake test also cover the top dot.

It now presses at `region.minX + 220`, which my arithmetic puts clear of every top dot and title pill in the 300, 1,000 and 10,000-shape boards.

I checked the other tests that use shipped notes for presses near a note's top middle, and found none. The POC arrow transcript and goldens are untouched; their pointer positions are nowhere near a top dot.
