## Description

## Problem

Below 35% zoom an arrow's skeleton cut its arc where the label is and greeked the label's text in the cut. The greeked bars are translucent black, which is nearly invisible on the dark canvas, so every labelled arrow looked like it had a gap in its line.

## Change

- `ArrowSkeleton.swift`: zoomed out, the arrow draws its arc whole and leaves the label off. The cut, the clip and the greeked bars are gone, along with the now-unused `ArrowStyle.skeletonText`.
- The skeleton's outline (selected or hovered) is the plain arc, no longer the arc minus the label plus the label's rect.
- The render at 35% and above is untouched. The label's kept geometry (hit test, marquee, `labelFrame`) is unchanged.
- `LabelTests`: the zoomed-out half of "Arrow labels: drawn" now expects the body inked through the label (before, at both edges, the middle and after), and the whole picture identical to the same arrow with no label.
- Docs updated in the same commit: `.claude/rules/arrows.md`, `.claude/rules/drawing.md`, `docs/plugin-system.md`.

## How to test

1. `git fetch && git checkout claude/awesome-maxwell-ym2nfe`
2. `TEST_FILTER="Arrow" ./test.sh` (or `./test.sh` for everything). The suites to watch are "Arrow labels: drawn" and "Arrow skeletons".
3. `./build.sh && open build/InfiniteCanvas.app`, then open a board with labelled arrows (e.g. `canvas draw` with `edge "A" -> "B" text: "calls"`).
4. Zoom out below 35% (the status shows the percentage). The arrows should be unbroken lines with heads, with no gap and no dark marks where labels were.
5. Zoom back in past 35%. Labels should return, with the line cut around them as before.
6. At about 30%, select a labelled arrow. Its outline should follow the whole arc.
