# The clipboard

Cut, copy and paste: what a copy takes (boards and containers whole), where a paste lands and settles, cut and paste as a move, and shape metadata.

Related: [shape references](shape-references.md), [containers](containers.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [locking](locking.md), [context menus](context-menus.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [The clipboard](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-clipboard).

## Architecture

- The clipboard (Clipboard, platform, a thing you do): Clipboard's own commands `.cut`, `.copy`, `.paste` and `.duplicate`, each declared once with its key (⌘X, ⌘C, ⌘V and ⌘D, Keyboard's) and one rule over the shapes it's given (some can be copied; Duplicate's, and the board takes shapes; Paste's, the pasteboard holds shapes it can read and the board takes them), which every surface runs: Edit › Cut, Copy and Paste, AppKit's own items, which the first three answer on the board (`answering: .cut`…), Edit › Duplicate (in `EditMenu.CutCopyPaste`, `placed: .after(.pasteAtPointer)`, `.whenIdle`), and Cut, Copy, Duplicate (beside the shapes) and Paste in every context menu (items for the canvas, `MenuItem(command: .cut, in: ClipboardMenuGroup.self)` and the rest: shown while the command can act on the menu's shapes, showing ⌘X, ⌘C, ⌘D and ⌘V). Paste lands where ⌘V's rule says (below), or, from a context menu, centered on its click (the command reads its subject's `point`: `editor.paste(centeredAt:)`); the Shape menu leaves Paste out, as the menu bar has it. The pasteboard holds a small file (`BoardCodec` JSON, the app's own type; a `CanvasFile`, format 4 only when a copied shape's board has shapes, else as a board; format 5 only when a copied shape is in a container copied with it); with none of the app's shapes on it, text another app put there pastes when a plugin's reader reads it (below), and anything else on it is left alone. Copying a shape that owns a board copies what it holds, all the way down, under the original ids: on each board the shapes that can be copied, let go of what's left out on their board (a shape left out takes the boards under it), and only boards of kinds that own boards. Copying a container (`ShapeContaining`: a frame) copies what it holds, at any depth, that can be copied, each copy in the container it was copied with and in none when that one isn't copied (`AnyShapeRecord.copy(as:container:)`, through the paste's one map). A paste renames every id on every board with one map, puts each owner in with its boards (`insert(_:contents:)`), attaching none to a kind that owns none here, lands in place if any of it would be in view (else centered, with `transaction.translate`, the top level only), is put down there as one by the in-or-out rule, judged by the laid-out frames the transaction gives what it pasted (a pasted shape is laid out as the paste leaves the file, so a note and its type pasted together are sized right before the commit; a paste centered on a point centers those frames, a duplicate settles by them, [containers](containers.md)) (`transaction.settle`: into the container holding most of it, nudged in or out across an edge, gliding, `editor.glide`; over all of a container it's beside it, so a frame pasted on its original holds only its copies and takes none of the original's), and what isn't in another shape pasted with it is selected; a read-only container (a locked frame, a locked frame's copy too) takes nothing: a paste one would take puts nothing down, given up whole (`editor.transactWhole`) so no step is left (redo stays), and services hear the container refused (`editor.refuseChanges(to:)`: it shakes); a board that takes no shapes (a locked subboard's, at any depth: `editor.isBoardReadOnly`) takes no paste at all: `canPaste` is false there, so ⌘V passes, Edit › Paste is dimmed (the `.paste` command isn't enabled) and the context menu has no Paste, and `paste` does nothing, nothing refused and nothing shaking, since there's nothing to try, while Copy works there and Cut copies and deletes nothing, shaking what it tried, as for a locked shape (the delete is refused); what a copy put there pastes as copies, new ids all the way down, each copy's metadata naming its original, nested ones too (`record.copiedFrom`, `meta[CopiedFrom.self]`). Cut and paste is a move: a cut marks what it writes with a token beside the shapes (`com.example.InfiniteCanvas.cut`, a fresh UUID each cut, in the same write; a copy writes none, and any later write clears it), and a paste of what a cut put there keeps each id nothing in the file goes by (`CanvasFile.isUnused`: no shape has it, and no board, nor entry that didn't read as one, is kept under it), on whatever board it lands, so whatever names the shapes (a mention) names them again; a kept id is the same shape, moved, so it gets no `CopiedFrom` and keeps what it had (so `copiedFrom == nil` doesn't mean never pasted), and the rest are copies. No ledger of pastes: the first paste's shapes hold the ids, so pasting again copies, and undoing that paste frees them. Shapes bound to what a cut deletes (`ShapeReferencing`: an arrow's end) let go of it at the cut, as a delete does (or go with it, for a kind that takes bound shapes: `TakesBoundShapes`), and a move doesn't bind them again; only names survive it. A copy of shapes of a kind that takes bound shapes takes along what is bound only to shapes copied (`ShapeContext.takenAlong(by:)`, in `gather`: two ledgers copied, duplicated, ⌥-dragged or cut keep the link between them, selected or not; one that can't be copied is left out): a departure from tldraw, whose copy keeps a binding only for a shape among those given, scoped to taking kinds so notes copy as before. A cut names its step for what went with what it cut ("Cut Table and 3 Arrows": what the core's deletion took besides, the arrows bound to the shapes that really went, and what a container held that left with it, [selection](selection.md)'s `DeleteSteps` with the verb "Cut"), and is "Cut" when nothing did. The arrows the copy took along are not asked for by the cut: the deletion takes the ones bound to a shape that goes, so tables that cannot be cut keep the link between them, and a locked link is let go of, never refused (no shake); a paste of what was cut brings back a link of its own between the tables, as a locked shape that stays does. A right-click menu's Delete and Cut name their step as their keys do (a command item runs the command without naming the step after the item). Cut deletes only what it copied whole: a shape whose boards hold one that couldn't be copied stays, and pastes as a copy, since its id is in use; a container is whole without what it holds (deleting it lets go of what stays), so it goes with what it copied of it, and what it holds that couldn't be copied, or is read-only (copied all the same, so it pastes back as a copy), stays, let go of. Every kind is copyable unless it opts out (`x.uncopyable()`, `x.copyable { … }`; the `ShapeCopyability` point). Every kind whose props are bound to shapes adopts the core's `ShapeReferencing` (arrows, CanvasTesting's `Tag`): deleting a shape asks it of every shape bound to it, after `willDelete` handlers (arrows let go where they're drawn), and a copy is bound to the copies of the shapes copied with it and lets go of the rest; it returns nil when it can't let go of a shape it can't find, and a copy then leaves it out. Shape metadata (`AnyShapeRecord.meta`, typed by a plugin's `ShapeMetaKey`, saved under the owner's id, then the name) is the core's generic place for what a plugin keeps about a shape beside its props; what the shape itself knows is an added property. A per-kind rule table is `registry.contributionByKind(to:)`, and the union of shapes' bounds `shapeContext.bounds(of:)`.

- Paste readers (Clipboard's `PasteReaders` point, `PasteReaders.swift`, a list in plugin order, the report's `paste reader emoji`; the one point for what reads the pasteboard besides this app's shapes, which no other module declares): a `PasteReader` has a `name`, a role, a `sniff` (cheap; menus ask it; nil for the text sugar's, which can only be asked to read) and a `read` of the `PasteContent` the pasteboard holds (its `text`, and its local `files`, each with its small text when it reads as UTF-8, read when first asked: at most 2 MB, 20 files; **files come before text**: with any, `text` is nil, since Finder also puts a copied file's name there), which gives a `PasteResult`, either `.shapes(CanvasFile)` (its root's shapes, back to front, with the boards they own and what containers among them hold; anywhere) or `.put(DrawPut)` (a draw program, Draw's). The roles: `.recognizing` (the default, and the emoji reader's), asked by ⌘V, ⇧⌘V and the context menu's Paste, and `.plain`, asked only by Paste as Note (⌥⇧⌘V). The registrar's forms: `registrar.pasteReader("emoji") { text, editor in CanvasFile? }` (the text sugar, as it was: a recognizing reader of text that gives shapes), `pasteReader(_:role:sniff:read:)` (a `DrawPut?`) and `pasteReading(_:role:sniff:read:)` (a `PasteResult?`). A recognizing reader is asked only when the pasteboard holds none of this app's shapes (its type, `shapesType`, absent), in plugin order, the first whose read gives something being the paste; each pasteboard's content and each reader's reading are kept by `changeCount` and `editor.kits` (`readings`), so a reader reads once a change however many menus validate, and `canPaste` is true while this app's shapes are there or a recognizing reader's `sniff` says yes (a reader with none, while it reads something). `.shapes` are put down as this app's own are (`place(_:source:named:at:offsetBy:)`, one Paste step, settled, selected), as new shapes (`PasteSource.read`: each a new id, no `CopiedFrom`, a cut's ids never kept), centered on the click or the pointer where a context menu's Paste or Paste at Pointer says, else in the middle of what the view shows (`unobscuredViewport`), since they were copied from nowhere on the board. A `.put` is Draw's (`put(_:at:in:step:)`, [command line](command-line.md)): the shapes the file has by kind and title are restated, the nearest first, the rest made and laid out in rows, landing **beside the selection** (⌘V; the middle of the view with none), centered on the click (the context menu) or the pointer (⇧⌘V), selected (what is not in another shape landed), announced low priority ("Pasted 2 shapes"), in one step, `undoHistory.group("Paste <reader>")`, not an agent's, with the editing ended first; a put that throws (a kind nothing draws, a container that takes nothing, a value its writer refuses) leaves no step, nothing changed and the selection where it was, shakes the shapes it was beside and says why (`announce`); one that only restated shapes of other boards selects nothing and says "Pasted into shapes on other boards". A kit switches them (`KitSwitched`, [kits](kits.md)): a reader whose plugin's kit is off in the file is never asked, plain ones too, so text only it reads pastes nothing (off means off, the owner's call). Clipboard imports Draw (platform to platform; Draw imports no Clipboard); a plugin that reads pastes imports Clipboard, as StickyNotes does. The shipped reader: StickyNotes' plain one (the example kit's emoji reader went with the example kit; the engineering kit's SQL and OpenAPI readers come later).
- Paste as Note (Clipboard's `.pasteAsNote`, "Paste as Note", ⌥⇧⌘V, in Edit's cut-copy-paste group after Paste at Pointer, Duplicate after it; `.whenIdle`, so text being edited keeps the key; in no context menu): `editor.pasteAsNote()`, enabled (`canPasteAsNote`) while a `.plain` reader on in the file reads something and the board shown takes shapes, runs the first plain reader's result as a ⌘V does a recognizing one's but always beside the selection, in one step, "Paste as Note". It asks no recognizing reader, and ⌘V asks no plain one. StickyNotes' plain reader (`NoteStyle.pastedNote`, "Note") makes any text on the pasteboard, or a small text file's, of at most `NoteStyle.pastedNoteLimit` (50,000 bytes: more is no note, and Paste as Note is dim), one `sticky-note` statement with that text and no title, written through `MentionText(plain:)` so an `@[` is escaped and nothing turns into a mention; ⌘V of plain text pastes nothing, as before.
- Duplicating (`ClipboardState.duplicate`, `editor.duplicateBeside(_:)`, which `.duplicate` runs, and `duplicateSelection()` on the selection, `editor.duplicate(_:offsetBy:)`): `gather` builds the small file a copy would put on the pasteboard (shared with `copy`), and `place` puts it down as a paste does (one body for both, with a step name), without the pasteboard. A duplicate settles the copies where they are, then moves them `ClipboardStyle.duplicateOffset` (16) view points, divided by the zoom, down and right (a container's copy lies over its original and takes nothing in, the copies go in their originals' containers: settling after the move would absorb the original); the step is "Duplicate". ⌘D, Edit › Duplicate and the context menu's Duplicate (the `.duplicate` command, on the shapes it's given: `editor.duplicateBeside(_:)`) are off on a board that takes no shapes and with nothing copyable, and the key and the menu bar's item mid-gesture and while a shape is edited or text has the keyboard (`.whenIdle`). `registrar.copiesOnDrag` (Selection's `DragCopies` point) is Clipboard's claim for ⌥-drag: the select tool's `translationSession` asks it when a drag of the selection begins with ⌥ held (a lone box pressed with ⌥ waits for the drag threshold, as a copy is made past it), and drags the copies (`CopyDraggingSession`; Escape deletes them, what a copied container holds too, and reselects the originals). What a copy keeps is the paste's: a typed shape keeps its `is` (names kept, or renamed when the type is copied with it: [types](types.md)). Copies that shared a group are a group of their own (Clipboard builds on Grouping: `copiedGroups`, by board, `BoardGroups.copied`, a new id for each group two or more copies share, none for one only one is in; a shape that keeps its id, a cut's, keeps its groups), so a paste, ⌘D and a drag with ⌥ never join the originals' groups ([grouping](grouping.md); ClipboardTests' `groupCopyTests`).

- Paste at Pointer and Copy as Image. ⇧⌘V and Edit › Paste at Pointer (Clipboard's `.pasteAtPointer`, declared beside `.paste` with ⇧⌘V, placed in Edit right after Paste (`in: EditMenu.CutCopyPaste.self, placed: .after(.paste)`), `.whenIdle`, so text being edited keeps ⇧⌘V; its rule `canPaste`) is `editor.pasteAtPointer()`: `paste(centeredAt: pointerWorldLocation)` while `editor.isPointerOverCanvas` and that location is inside the view, else `paste()` as ⌘V does (so in place, or centered in the view), and off wherever Paste is (`canPaste`). How the editor knows the pointer is over the canvas: a core flag the host maintains (`CanvasEditor.isPointerOverCanvas`, not observed): `pointerMoved`, `pointerDown`, `panPointerDown` and `pointerLocated(at:)` set it, `pointerExited()` clears it, and `CanvasView` calls that for the minimap, the editing view, a view over the canvas or the pointer leaving, so it holds in every tool (Selection's `pointerOverCanvas` is nil in any tool but Select, and a tool's own hover would have been each tool's); it isn't cleared when the window stops being key (a pointer that moved off meanwhile pastes at the last place it was seen over the canvas, in view). Copy as Image (⇧⌘C) is the window's item, not a command ([host slots](host-slots.md)): Clipboard gives the host's slot `registrar.imageSource(isAvailable: { $0.canCopyAsImage }) { $0.imageShapes() }`, `imageShapes()` the shapes a copy of the selection would take (`ClipboardState.shapes(copying:)`, `gather`'s: the copyable shapes back to front with what the containers among them hold, nothing written to the pasteboard), or, with nothing selected, every shape of the board shown that can be copied (what's kept aside, or opts out, isn't drawn), which the window draws from the board's own file; `canCopyAsImage` is whether some of them can be, nothing when only shapes that can't are selected, and without the plugin nothing.

## Testing

- ClipboardTests' `takeAlongTests` hold the take-along over CanvasTesting's ledgers and links (two ledgers copied, duplicated and cut keep the link between them and a paste binds it to the copies; one ledger or two boxes take none; a link to a shape not cut goes with the delete and not back; "Cut Ledger and 2 links", "Cut" with nothing along; two ledgers that cannot be cut keep their link, a locked link is let go of with no shake, a corral cut names what it held, a right-click menu's Delete and Cut count, a link bound at one end only is taken along).

- ClipboardTests' `duplicateTests` hold ⌘D (offset, zoom, the pasteboard untouched, a container with what it holds, off cases) and the ⌥-drag (the original stays, one step, Escape, read-only refused, a copied container and what it holds taken away by Escape, off without ⌥ released more than 150 ms ago: `Pointer.modifiers` counts a key let go in the last 150 ms).
- ClipboardTests' `pointerPasteTests` (PointerPasteTests.swift) hold Paste at Pointer (centered on the pointer, one step, selected, ⌘V keeping its place; several shapes keeping their places among themselves under a zoomed, panned camera; off the canvas, in place or centered in the view as ⌘V; off where Paste is) and `imageFileTests` the shapes Copy as Image draws (the selection's, back to front, with the pasteboard and the undo history untouched; the whole board with nothing selected, an uncopyable kind left out; nothing for a selection of shapes that can't be copied; none without the plugin; the slot). CompositionTests' `copyAsImageShippedTests` hold the window's item on a pasteboard of its own (the PNG's size, a note's 200 points and 32 of margin at 2×, the whole board with nothing selected, the window's appearance, dimmed on an empty board).
- ClipboardTests' `readerTests` (ReaderTests.swift) hold the readers that give a draw program, over CanvasTesting's tiles, Draw and a private pasteboard: precedence (this app's shapes, then the first recognizing reader whose read gives something, in plugin order; else Paste dimmed and ⌘V passing), one read a change count, the three sites (beside the selection, centered on a click, on the pointer), the step's name and announcement and one Undo, ⌥⇧⌘V asking only the plain readers (and ⌘V none of them), a file before its name as text, a put that throws leaving no step and shaking what it was beside (a shape in a locked frame), a read-only board taking neither paste, an off kit's readers never asked, and the Edit menu's order and the key in the report. DrawPutTests (AutomationTests) hold `put` itself; CompositionTests' `pasteAsNoteShippedTests` hold Paste as Note in the app's window.
- ClipboardTests' `pasteReaderTests` (PasteReaderTests.swift) hold paste readers over boxes a reader of the test's own reads from "3 boxes": new shapes in the middle of the view, selected, one Paste step, copies of nothing, pasted again with new ids, text none reads pasting nothing, each pasteboard asked once; on the click's point; readers in plugin order, a refusing one passed, none asked while this app's shapes are there; a reader in an off kit never asked and read once the kit is on; the report.
- ClipboardTests' `readOnlyBoardTests` (ReadOnlyBoardTests.swift) hold a locked subboard's board (CanvasTesting's frozen pocket): Paste off by key, menu bar and context menu and nothing pasted or refused, on again on the root and once thawed; Copy working there, and Cut copying, deleting nothing, leaving no step and shaking the shape, its copy pasting as a copy.
- For the clipboard, never touch the user's pasteboard: give the plugin a private one (`ClipboardPlugin(pasteboard: NSPasteboard.withUniqueName())`, and `releaseGlobally()` it after). ShippedTesting's `ShippedPlugins.makeEditor(model:)`, and `configureBoards()` and `BoardWindow` in CompositionTests, take the test's own unless a test passes one (`ShippedPlugins.pasteboard`: made when the test first asks, shared by its editors, released as it ends). `ClipboardPlugin()`, made from nothing as the product's list makes it, is on the system's, which is why it's an `OutwardPlugin`: nothing but the app is made of it as listed ([build](build.md), What ships). Two editors on one pasteboard are two boards cutting and pasting between them; ClipboardTests' `moveTests` hold cut and paste to a move (the token, a copy clearing it, a second paste copying, an id in use, an orphan board or unreadable entry under a cut id, a refused deletion, a name left behind and a binding let go).
