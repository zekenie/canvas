# The clipboard

Cut, copy and paste: what a copy takes (boards and containers whole), where a paste lands and settles, cut and paste as a move, and shape metadata.

Related: [shape references](shape-references.md), [containers](containers.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [locking](locking.md), [context menus](context-menus.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [The clipboard](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-clipboard).

## Architecture

- The clipboard (Clipboard, a concern): ⌘X, ⌘C, ⌘V and ⌘D (Keyboard), the `.cut`, `.copy`, `.paste` and `.duplicate` commands, and Cut, Copy and Paste in every context menu (items for the canvas), on the selection; the menu's Paste lands centered on its click (`editor.paste(centeredAt:)`, the menu's `point`), where ⌘V keeps its rule, below. The pasteboard holds a small file (`BoardCodec` JSON, the app's own type; a `CanvasFile`, format 4 only when a copied shape's board has shapes, else as a board; format 5 only when a copied shape is in a container copied with it); anything else on it is ignored for now. Copying a shape that owns a board copies what it holds, all the way down, under the original ids: on each board the shapes that can be copied, let go of what's left out on their board (a shape left out takes the boards under it), and only boards of kinds that own boards. Copying a container (`ShapeContaining`: a frame) copies what it holds, at any depth, that can be copied, each copy in the container it was copied with and in none when that one isn't copied (`AnyShapeRecord.copy(as:container:)`, through the paste's one map). A paste renames every id on every board with one map, puts each owner in with its boards (`insert(_:contents:)`), attaching none to a kind that owns none here, lands in place if any of it would be in view (else centered, with `transaction.translate`, the top level only), is put down there as one by the in-or-out rule (`transaction.settle`: into the container holding most of it, nudged in or out across an edge, gliding, `editor.glide`; over all of a container it's beside it, so a frame pasted on its original holds only its copies and takes none of the original's), and what isn't in another shape pasted with it is selected; a read-only container (a locked frame, a locked frame's copy too) takes nothing: a paste one would take puts nothing down, thrown out of its transaction so no step is left (redo stays), and services hear the container refused (`editor.refuseChanges(to:)`: it shakes); a board that takes no shapes (a locked subboard's, at any depth: `editor.isBoardReadOnly`) takes no paste at all: `canPaste` is false there, so ⌘V passes, Edit › Paste is dimmed (the `.paste` command isn't enabled) and the context menu has no Paste, and `paste` does nothing, nothing refused and nothing shaking, since there's nothing to try, while Copy works there and Cut copies and deletes nothing, shaking what it tried, as for a locked shape (the delete is refused); what a copy put there pastes as copies, new ids all the way down, each copy's metadata naming its original, nested ones too (`record.copiedFrom`, `meta[CopiedFrom.self]`). Cut and paste is a move: a cut marks what it writes with a token beside the shapes (`com.example.InfiniteCanvas.cut`, a fresh UUID each cut, in the same write; a copy writes none, and any later write clears it), and a paste of what a cut put there keeps each id nothing in the file goes by (`CanvasFile.isUnused`: no shape has it, and no board, nor entry that didn't read as one, is kept under it), on whatever board it lands, so whatever names the shapes (a mention) names them again; a kept id is the same shape, moved, so it gets no `CopiedFrom` and keeps what it had (so `copiedFrom == nil` doesn't mean never pasted), and the rest are copies. No ledger of pastes: the first paste's shapes hold the ids, so pasting again copies, and undoing that paste frees them. Shapes bound to what a cut deletes (`ShapeReferencing`: an arrow's end) let go of it at the cut, as a delete does, and a move doesn't bind them again; only names survive it. Cut deletes only what it copied whole: a shape whose boards hold one that couldn't be copied stays, and pastes as a copy, since its id is in use; a container is whole without what it holds (deleting it lets go of what stays), so it goes with what it copied of it, and what it holds that couldn't be copied, or is read-only (copied all the same, so it pastes back as a copy), stays, let go of. Every kind is copyable unless it opts out (`x.uncopyable()`, `x.copyable { … }`; the `ShapeCopyability` point). Every kind whose props are bound to shapes adopts the core's `ShapeReferencing` (arrows, CanvasTesting's `Tag`): deleting a shape asks it of every shape bound to it, after `willDelete` handlers (arrows let go where they're drawn), and a copy is bound to the copies of the shapes copied with it and lets go of the rest; it returns nil when it can't let go of a shape it can't find, and a copy then leaves it out. Shape metadata (`AnyShapeRecord.meta`, typed by a plugin's `ShapeMetaKey`, saved under the owner's id, then the name) is the core's generic place for what a plugin keeps about a shape beside its props; what the shape itself knows is an added property. A per-kind rule table is `registry.contributionByKind(to:)`, and the union of shapes' bounds `shapeContext.bounds(of:)`.

- Duplicating (`ClipboardState.duplicate`, `editor.duplicateSelection()`, `editor.duplicate(_:offsetBy:)`, `canDuplicate`): `gather` builds the small file a copy would put on the pasteboard (shared with `copy`), and `place` puts it down as a paste does (one body for both, with a step name), without the pasteboard. A duplicate settles the copies where they are, then moves them `ClipboardStyle.duplicateOffset` (16) view points, divided by the zoom, down and right (a container's copy lies over its original and takes nothing in, the copies go in their originals' containers: settling after the move would absorb the original); the step is "Duplicate". ⌘D and Edit › Duplicate (the `.duplicate` command, `BoardWindowController.duplicate(_:)`) are off mid-gesture, on a board that takes no shapes and with nothing copyable, and while text has the keyboard (`BoardWindowController.textKeys`); there is no context-menu item. `registrar.copiesOnDrag` (Selection's `DragCopies` point) is Clipboard's claim for ⌥-drag: the select tool's `translationSession` asks it when a drag of the selection begins with ⌥ held (a lone box pressed with ⌥ waits for the drag threshold, as a copy is made past it), and drags the copies (`CopyDraggingSession`; Escape deletes them, what a copied container holds too, and reselects the originals). What a copy keeps is the paste's: a typed shape keeps its `is` (names kept, or renamed when the type is copied with it: [types](types.md)).

## Testing

- ClipboardTests' `duplicateTests` hold ⌘D (offset, zoom, the pasteboard untouched, a container with what it holds, off cases) and the ⌥-drag (the original stays, one step, Escape, read-only refused, a copied container and what it holds taken away by Escape, off without ⌥ released more than 150 ms ago: `Pointer.modifiers` counts a key let go in the last 150 ms).
- ClipboardTests' `readOnlyBoardTests` (ReadOnlyBoardTests.swift) hold a locked subboard's board (CanvasTesting's frozen pocket): Paste off by key, menu bar and context menu and nothing pasted or refused, on again on the root and once thawed; Copy working there, and Cut copying, deleting nothing, leaving no step and shaking the shape, its copy pasting as a copy.
- For the clipboard, never touch the user's pasteboard: give the plugin a private one (`ClipboardPlugin(pasteboard: NSPasteboard.withUniqueName())`, `ShippedPlugins.makeEditor(pasteboard:model:)`, `configureBoards(pasteboard:)` in CompositionTests) and `releaseGlobally()` it after. Two editors on one pasteboard are two boards cutting and pasting between them; ClipboardTests' `moveTests` hold cut and paste to a move (the token, a copy clearing it, a second paste copying, an id in use, an orphan board or unreadable entry under a cut id, a refused deletion, a name left behind and a binding let go).
