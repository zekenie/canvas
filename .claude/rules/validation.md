# Validation

Validation: rules and their issues, the schema rule, what each commit re-checks, rings on the canvas, a rule's place in a value and rechecking shapes no commit named, where issues show, and the issue walk (one issue at a time through the file, across boards, with scopes the owners of sources give; its band over the panel, its light, the "9 of 63" on a warning line, and its list by board over the current stop's Issues tab).

Related: [properties and schemas](properties-and-schemas.md), [titles](titles.md), [boards](boards.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [queries](queries.md), [command line](command-line.md), [adornments](adornments.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [types](types.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [A tab of status lines: Validation's Issues tab](../../docs/plugin-system.md#a-tab-of-status-lines-validations-issues-tab), [Walking issues](../../docs/plugin-system.md#walking-issues-validations-issue-walk), [Marks: Validation's dots](../../docs/plugin-system.md#marks-validations-dots).

## Architecture

- Validation (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait) is ESLint's model: rules (`x.validate { shape, context in … }`, `registrar.validate(P.self)`, and `scope.validate` on the kinds a scope reaches: `registrar.forShapes(holding: ColorProperty.self) { $0.validate { … } }`, Colors' on the kinds that hold a color, and `forEveryShape` for the schema rule every shape has; `ValidationRules` takes scopes, so a rule for every kind with none is refused, [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md)) find issues on a shape, each `.error`, `.warning` or `.incomplete` (`Severity`, in that order of worth): an error makes a shape invalid, a warning only flags it, and the third, under a warning, says the shape still needs something (a value its type calls needed, left empty; any rule may say it) and **shows nowhere on the board** (below). Validation's own rule, for every kind, checks each shape against its kind's schema (`registry.schemaViolations(of:)`): each violation is a warning about the property it's in ("Title is 95 characters long, and should be at most 80.", `field: "title"`). The overlay outlines a shape red with an error, orange with warnings alone, and not at all without issues that show (an `.incomplete` one is not among them), nor while it's being edited (`editingShapeID`: what it says is still being written), at every zoom. In full, each ring is stroked alone and follows the shape's outline, 3 view points outside where the canvas strokes that: a closed shape's outline stands off its edge `editor.outlineGap` (4) view points ([the editor](editor.md)), so its ring is 7 out of the edge and clear of a selected shape's accent line, the same for every kind, and outside the rings of tinted outlines the core draws round it (Neighbors' lights: `OverlayContext.tintedOutset(of:)`, 4 view points for each ring past the first, counting a selected shape's tint a ring further out and none for an open shape, so a shape two lights outline has its ring 7 + 4 = 11 out, and the overlay grows what it culls by `greatestTintedOutset`), and an open shape's (an arrow's) is on its line, so its ring is 3 out of it (its kind's `indicatorPath`, read through the core's `registry.indicatorPath(of:in:)`, grown by `CGPath.grownOutward(by:isClosed:)`, the core's path arithmetic, which also stands any outline that isn't a rect off its shape, and kept per shape until the shape or its outline changes, or the zoom by more than a tenth, or the standoff, or the tinted outset, `ValidationRings`), and is a rounded rect around the bounds, grown the same, for a shape under 32 points on screen; zoomed out (`OverlayContext.drawsSkeletons`), a map of where the problems are among thousands, each closed shape's is a ring around the bounds with square corners, grown from no outline (an open shape's, an arrow's, follows its line as in full, stroked alone after the run before it: a long slanted arrow's bounds were a box over what's beside it) (and by the tinted outset, 3 + 4 out of the frame for a shape two lights outline), a run of one color filled as one path in paint order, so a front shape's still covers a back one's (rects in one path cost a fraction of stroking each, 4,800 at 10% in 2.7 ms against 21, while rounded outlines in one path cost ten times each stroked alone); `ValidationResult.worst` allocates nothing, since the overlay reads it for every shape in view; it and `worstFirst` (the lists' order) are of the issues that show (`Severity.shows`: an error or a warning), so a shape whose issues are all `.incomplete` has no `worst` and an empty `worstFirst`, and `isValid` is true of it. The engine keeps such a shape's issues like any (its cache, its lookups, what a commit re-checks, `recheck`, `affected`, `touches`, `cachedResult`), and whoever asks for them reads `ValidationResult.issues`. A `ValidationIssue` may say where in its field's value it is (`path`, a JSON Pointer, `.error(_:field:path:)`; nil for the field as a whole), which the engine keeps with the issue and `validationIssues(of:in:)` returns, and what can be done about it (`actions`, `IssueAction`: a title and what it does, `@MainActor @Sendable`; two the same when their titles are, so a verdict checked again with the same issues is the same verdict), the buttons of its line in the Issues tab (LanguageRules' Ignore and Show Rule); an action may carry a `role` (`IssueActionRole`, `.ignore` for now: what it is for, for a reader that must find it other than by title; equality and hash stay by title). An issue may carry a `source` (`IssueSource(plugin:key:)`, `Hashable, Sendable`: an opaque key for where it comes from, so a plugin's issues are found by key and never by matching their sentence; nil unless the rule says, and defaulted nil on the init, `.error` and `.warning`; part of the issue's equality, kept by the engine and returned by `validationIssues(of:in:editor:)`; a sourceless issue is told apart by its shape, field and path, and the occurrence among equal ones, never by its words, which may carry figures a fix changes). LanguageRules' warnings carry one ([language rules](language-rules.md)). Rules see other shapes only through `ValidationContext` (`context.shape(_:)`, a lookup it notes, `context.reading`, the file as the shapes read it: the editor's committed one, a transaction's mid-way, nil where nothing is laid out, for a rule that answers from a service's kept state and must not where the file isn't the committed one (`reading.isChanged`), no lookup noted, and `context.registry`, for a rule over every kind that asks about kinds, which is no lookup; `context.service(_:)` for a rule over what a plugin keeps across the file, nil where shapes are checked without an editor; `context.committedService(_:)` for a rule that asks a plugin what it knows of the file as committed, the editor's, else the one lent to what checks the shapes (a transaction's, `canvas draw`'s check before it commits), never of what the shapes being checked change: whether a type's chip's query reads, [language rules](language-rules.md)); `ValidationEngine` re-checks the shapes each commit's `ChangeSet` names, those whose rules looked one up, and those a rule's `affected` names (`affected: { changes, editor in … }` on the same three and `ValidationRule`: for a rule that reads a service, the shapes a commit may change the issues of without changing them, on any board, O(changed), asked once every service has the commit (`documentDidSettle`, so a rule's answer may read any service's: Types' check, the colors), or first by a read of what it affected; on a board it keeps, they're checked once it's taken back). `engine.recheck(ids)` checks shapes again though no commit named them, for a rule that reads state outside the shape and the shapes it looks up that changed with no commit to say so (a service's: a field's caret left, a relationship's verdict moved; what a commit reaches is the rule's `affected`): on the board shown they are unchecked now and the ring redrawn, on a board it keeps they are filed for when it is taken back; it tells the table of contents itself (`contentsDidChange(for:)`, a no-op where there is none: a recheck may come with no commit at all), and adds the ids to what `affected(by:)` names for the current revision, which the table of contents asks once every service has the commit; nothing for none. `engine.affected(by:)` is what a commit may change the issues of beyond what it changed (`dependents(on:)`: those whose rules looked up a shape it changed, and, for a shape it only moved, only those whose rules read where shapes are; and what the rules named; given `elsewhere`, the table of contents' catch-up on going back, what they named there meanwhile): what `ValidationContentsPlugin`'s marks read again. A rule whose issues follow from what shapes are, never where, says so (`ignoringMoves: true`, on `x.validate`, `registrar.validate(P.self, …)`, `scope.validate` and `ValidationRule`; never a rule that measures distances or finds overlaps): a commit that only moves shapes (`ChangeSet.moved`, the shapes a commit only moved with `Transaction.moveBox`, which a drag and `translate` use: a drag moves every shape it drags, every event) runs it for none of them, neither those it moved nor those whose rule looked one of them up, each keeping what it found (kept per shape and per rule, so issues stay in the rules' order), while the other rules run as ever. Validation's schema rule ignores moves (and keeps each shape's verdict until the shape changes other than by moving, for the re-checks a lookup brings), and so does Mentions' (`MentionValidationPlugin`), so a drag of a subboard 100 notes mention re-checks none of them. It checks the board shown (a board never shown isn't checked: its issues show once you enter it), and keeps what it found (issues, lookups, what's left to check) per board in a `BoardStash`, so going back to a board checks nothing. `registry.validationIssues(of:in:editor:)` runs a shape's rules against any `ShapeContext` (a transaction's), with schema verdicts of its own, and the editor's services if given one (Validation's `ValidationQueriesPlugin` passes it). `engine.cachedResult(for:)` is a verdict as the engine holds it, checking nothing (`CachedVerdict`: `.current(result)`, nil for no issues, for a shape on the board shown once the engine has read that board, while the shape isn't waiting to be checked again (the rules' `affected` for the last commit asked first, if they haven't been); else `.unknown`): it never runs a rule, for a reader that mustn't make the engine read a board (a query's `:invalid` and `:warned`, through Validation's `ValidationQueriesPlugin`, which runs the rules itself where it's unknown). Validation shows the issues itself, in plugin values of its own that a list may leave out (it imports DetailPanel and TableOfContents for them): `ValidationDetailsPlugin` in the panel's Issues tab (worst first, each issue's `actions` its line's buttons, `DetailStatus.actions`; the footer stays empty), `ValidationContentsPlugin` as the dot on a row in the table of contents; and a module that imports Validation may add rules, as Mentions' `MentionValidationPlugin` warns about mentions that can't work ([mentions](mentions.md)) and Boards' `PathValidationPlugin` about paths two shapes share ([boards](boards.md#a-shapes-path-and-the-warning-of-one-two-shapes-share)). Its callers are Types': the caret coming to a field or leaving it, or the shape's editing ending (`FieldChecks.typing`, which Types' wrong-value rule leaves out while it is set), a board switch while a field has the caret, and a change of relationships (`RelationshipIndex.onChange`: the arrows and notes whose ends, fit or marks moved, so an arrow that no commit touched is a misfit or fits again, a drag's release among them, which is no commit); what a type's edit reaches is the rule's `affected` (`FieldChecks.affected(by:)`). A file put in place with a switch of board keeps nothing found on any board, as the table of contents keeps no rows: a board as it was may hold shapes whose rules read what the new file changed elsewhere (a type, a color's name, a title), which the switch names none of. The rule over added values (`TypeValidationPlugin`, [types](types.md#checks)) makes a wrong value an error and a needed one an issue of the third level (`.incomplete`: no ring, no Issues line, no dot, not `:invalid`, valid, and `:incomplete`); the Issues tab is made when the panel is, so it shows a caret's leaving the next time, and the ring waits for editing to end, as for any shape.
- VoiceOver says the issues that show of a shape on the board shown as states, after what it is: "Checkout, Note, 1 error, 2 warnings" (`ValidationPlugin.accessibleIssues`, `forEveryShape`, since every shape is checked; `ValidationStyle.errors`, `.warnings`); the third level says nothing, as it shows nowhere ([accessibility](accessibility.md)).
- Types' value rule also says a note's relationship rows: more than one where a row allows one is an error on the note (`field` the row's name), a Needed row left empty one of the third level, at the row's name ([relationships](relationships.md#the-check-on-a-notes-rows)); the index names the notes whose verdict moved and the check's `recheck` reaches them, and the check makes again the notes whose kept Only one issue says a row name the row is not called now (`rowErrors`), and marks stale the words of those whose kept Needed issue says it (`rowNeeded`, `recheckWords` below), since Validation keeps the words of each issue it holds.
- **What writes touch before they commit.** A rule's `affected` is asked of a commit; a run (`canvas draw`'s, which may never commit) is asked the same of writes still in a transaction: `touches: { changes, shapes in … }` (beside `affected:` on `x.validate`, `registrar.validate(P.self, …)`, `scope.validate` and `ValidationRule`), given what the writes did (`transaction.pending`, a `ChangeSet`: added, changed, `moved`, removed, and `elsewhere` for other boards) and the shapes as the writer has them (`transaction.shapes`: its `reading` is the file mid-way, `reading.service(_:)` and `shapes.service(_:)` the editor's services, committed), answers the other shapes, on any board of the file, whose issues the rule may now find differently. It may name more shapes than change (whoever asks compares each one's issues before and after) and never fewer, and it costs what was written and what that reaches, never the board. `registry.validationTouched(by:in:)` is the one function that answers for every rule: each rule's `touches`, and, where the context is an editor's or a transaction's and the engine has checked the board shown, the shapes whose rules looked a written shape up (`engine.dependents(on: changes)`: what the commit would check again, a shape that only moved counting for the rules that don't ignore moves alone), never one of the written shapes themselves. `transaction.pending` is what the commit would hand services, so a board the transaction takes (a subboard's delete) puts every shape the board held in `elsewhere`: a rule reaches what those shapes' going breaks (a relationship that ran to a note on the board, a type root notes were of). A rule that reads which shapes the run leaves (Boards' count of holders of a path) reads them through `reading.shape(inFile:)`, which has none of a board taken. So a rule that reaches other shapes through `context.shape(_:)` alone needs no `touches` (Mentions': a target a run changes so that it cannot be mentioned where it is reaches its mentioners through the kept lookups; a target deleted is no issue, its pill says so); one that reads a service does (Types' and Boards', below). Its caller is `ValidationChecksPlugin`, for `canvas draw`. ValidationTests' `touchesTests` hold it over fixture rules: what a rule names, the engine's lookups (none before it has checked the board, a move reaching only rules that read where shapes are, a shape the delete wrote too left to the writer), a board not shown both ways with its shape checked over `shapes.over(document)`, nothing for a write that reaches nothing, a part of the writes asked alone, and each rule asked once whatever the board holds.
- **Every shipped rule, and what a run can bring to a shape it did not write.** Validation's schema rule: nothing (a shape's own properties against its kind's schema). Types' schema-text rule (`SchemaReadings.issues`): nothing (the type's own text). Types' rule over values and relationships: `touches` is `FieldChecks.touched(by:reading:)` ([types](types.md#checks), [relationships](relationships.md#what-a-runs-writes-reach)): a type restated reaches its instances, a relationship drawn or deleted the notes at its ends, an end drawn or ruled its end types' notes. Types' rule over a type's own warnings (a `$ref` to a type that is gone, a `$title` that titles nothing): it answers over a run's reading (`FieldChecks.typeWarnings(of:reading:)`), and the types it reaches (those that refer to or build on a type written) are named by the rule over values. Mentions' rule: the kept lookups, above. Boards' rule (a path two shapes share): `touches` names the other holders of a path a run's writes give a shape or take from one, and what is inside a container a run renames or puts elsewhere, and the rule answers over the run's reading (`PathIndex.sharedKey(of:in:)`, a written shape's path worked out from the reading's records and `reading.board(containing:)`: [boards](boards.md#a-shapes-path-and-the-warning-of-one-two-shapes-share)). References' rule (a pill's query the engine cannot read): nothing (the pill's own query; a shape deleted or made private that a pill stands for is no issue of the pill's, which shows it; an arrow of a relationship type drawn at such a pill is Types').
- `ValidationChecksPlugin` (`ValidationChecks.swift`, Validation's own, filling Draw's `DrawChecks`) gives `canvas draw` the issues a shape it drew would show, checked with no editor, and, of what a run wrote, the other shapes to look at (`validationTouched(by:in:)`) with the command that lists what a run brought to more shapes than it says (`canvas query ":invalid, :warned, :incomplete"`: an error, a warning or what a shape still needs, since a run's count is of all three); a list without Draw leaves it out ([command line](command-line.md)).
- **Who reads a level, and what each does with `.incomplete`.** The rule is that an issue of the third level does what no issue does, everywhere a person looks, and is read where an agent asks. The overlay's ring and its color (`worst`; a shape's ring is let go once only such issues are left) and the zoomed-out ring: none. The Issues tab (`ValidationDetailsPlugin`: `worstFirst`, and `DetailStatus.Tone.init?(_:)`, nil for it) and so the tab's alert in the strip: no line, no tab. The table of contents' dot (`ValidationContentsPlugin`: `worst`, `worstFirst` for its tooltip): none, and a shape with a warning too lists the warning alone. `Severity.color`: clear, never asked. `isValid`: true. Draw's check (`ValidationChecksPlugin`, `DrawIssue.Level`): carried as `.incomplete`; Draw says it only of a shape its run did not write ([command line](command-line.md#what-a-run-brings-to-shapes-it-did-not-write)). The query words (`ValidationQueriesPlugin`): `:invalid`, `:warned` and `:incomplete` are each of their own level alone, so a shape that only needs something is neither of the first two, and each row lists its issues of that level as `problems` (`:invalid`'s `class` `wrong`, `:warned`'s `warning`, `:incomplete`'s `needed`); a query of any of the three waits while answers are still arriving (`queryPseudoClass(settles:)`, [queries](queries.md)), since a rule may answer later than its commit (LanguageRules' warnings, which the decision model answers: [language rules](language-rules.md)). A reader of `results` for the words of an issue of the third level (it may say a name that was just changed: a Needed row's end renamed, a keystroke a key) asks `result(for:)`, which has them current; `recheckWords(_:)` (Types calls it for the notes whose Needed row was renamed) marks a shape's words stale and checks nothing, so a keystroke in an end's label costs no frame the check of every empty note, `cachedResult(for:)` is unknown for it (a query runs the rules) and the shape is checked again by the next commit that names it, by `result(for:)`, or by a commit that only moves it (which checks it with every rule once, as a commit to a stale shape does, so the old words never come back as current). Types' own sources of issues for a type (the Schema tab's status lines read `SchemaReadings.issues`, not this engine) never make one. ValidationTests' `validationDetailsTests` hold a source and a role carried through `validationIssues(of:in:editor:)`, the engine and the Issues tab, equality by title, and an issue with no source. ValidationTests' `incompleteTests` hold each over a fixture rule of a plugin that is not Types, and AutomationTests' `drawIncompleteTests` Draw's part.
- **The issue walk** (`Sources/Validation/IssueWalk.swift`, `IssueScopes.swift`, `IssueWalkStyle.swift`; `IssueWalkPlugin`, id `issue-walk`, listed right after `ValidationDetailsPlugin`; it needs Topology, whose order the stops are listed in; Validation imports Selection, Keyboard, Topology and Titles for it). `editor.issueWalk` (`IssueWalk`, `@Observable`, an `EditorService` and a `ShapeWalkSource`, made in extensions as the plugin rules want) is a `ShapeWalk` (Selection's, arrival `.selects`) over its rows: `scope` (nil while no walk runs, `isRunning`), `rows` (`IssueRow`s: `key`, `shape`, `board`, `message`, `severity`, `state`), `openCount`/`total`, `number`, `currentRow`, `isListOpen` (state only: views of the walk read it), `isGathering` and `answers` (+1 each time an answer has been used, the first or one asked again: a reader that waits for an answer that does not set `isGathering`, a scope reworded, waits on it), `begin(_:at:forward:)` (`IssueEntry`: `.first`, `.afterSelection`, `.shape(id)`; a walk running is ended and begun anew; a scope with no stop is no walk), `step(forward:)` (with no walk, begins `IssueScope.all` at the first stop after the selection, or before it going back), `ignoreCurrent()` (whether the stop was set aside: an Ignore that writes nothing, a locked shape's, leaves it open, says nothing and answers false), `end()`, `place(of:source:)` (an `IssuePlace`: the number and total of the issue of a source, for a line that says "9 of 63") and `scopeDidChange(from:to:)`.
  - **A scope** (`IssueScope`: `id`, `includes(issue)`, `label(editor)` an `IssueScopeLabel` of title, owner shape and spoken words, nil once the scope no longer exists, `candidates(editor)` the shapes that may have an issue in it, `isChanged(changes, editor)` whether a commit changed what it is made of, `isSetAside(shape, editor)` whether a gone issue was set aside rather than fixed) is Validation's `.all` or what a plugin gives for the issues of a source it owns (`IssueScopes`, an `ExtensionPoint` Validation owns: `registrar.issueScopes { source, editor in IssueScope? }`; `registry.issueScope(for:in:)` asks the contributions of the source's plugin). Validation knows no source.
  - **Stops.** The issues that show (`Severity.shows`) that the scope includes; an `.incomplete` issue is never one. They are gathered from the scope's `candidates` at once, else from `queries.matches([QueryMatchRequest(":invalid, :warned")])` (ids in file order, no rows, awaited on the main actor: `isGathering`), and read with the engine's `cachedResult(for:)` while it is current on the board shown, else `registry.validationIssues(of:in:editor:)` over one `ShapeContext` a board, under `withoutObservingDocument`. A stop is named by an `IssueKey`: the shape and the issue's `source`, else (an issue with none) its field and path (never its words: a message that counts characters is the same stop as it is fixed, its row's words following), and the occurrence among equal ones, the worse first, so a shape with two issues is two stops; the Issues tab's line tells the walk its issue's place among the shape's (`willPerform(_:onIssueAt:among:of:)`), so an Ignore run from the second of two equal lines marks the second row. Rows are sorted by Topology's `boardOrderKey` (the order Find steps in), and `walkPlace(of:forward:)` places a selection by the same key: of several selected shapes, the walk goes on after the last of them in walk order (before the first, going back), a shape that holds rows counting as its last (first) row and one that holds none by where the key puts it (a binary search).
  - **Updates.** `ValidationEngine.recheck`, `recheckWords` and the shapes a rule's `affected` named (`askRules`, before `mark` files the ones on other boards away) tell the walk the ids as a set (`shapesMayHaveChanged`), read again on the next turn of `editor.scheduler`; the walk's `documentDidSettle` reads the commit (the shapes it added or changed but by moving, which it reads whatever a scope's candidates say, since the candidates are another service's and may not have heard the commit yet (a shape that stops ignoring a rule is a candidate by this very commit) unless more than `readLimit` were added; those it removed and those on other boards; a shape it only moved only when `engine.rulesReadPlacement(of: kind)` says a rule of its kind reads where shapes are, since a box moved has the same issues and the same place in board order; and `engine.affected(by:)`; more than `IssueWalkStyle.readLimit` shapes with a scope that has no candidates are found again by the query instead of read one by one; of the shapes a rule affected or a `recheck` named, a scope that has candidates reads only those among them or with a row, since no other can have an issue to arrive or change; what the engine told `shapesMayHaveChanged` while the commit was being read is read with it, once; what commits and rechecks name while the first answer is on its way (`isGathering`) is kept (`missed`) and read with it, since the answer may be older than they). Each shape's rows are brought to what is found: an issue still there is updated; one that is gone keeps its row with no stop, `.ignored` when the plugin that owns the issue's source says it was set aside (`IssueScope.isSetAside`, asked of `registry.issueScope(for: the row's source, in:)`, so in an All Issues walk as in a rule's: LanguageRules answers from `IgnoredRulesProperty`, so a redone Ignore and Ignore Rule ▸ read ignored as the walk's own Ignore does), else, for an issue whose owner gives none, `.ignored` if the last action run on it had role `.ignore` (from `ignoreCurrent`, or from its line in the Issues tab, which tells the walk before it runs: `willPerform`), else `.holds`; one that is back (undo) is open again; a shape that left the file loses its rows; an arrival joins in order. `ShapeWalk.rowsDidChange(keeping:)` keeps the current stop, and when its row went the place falls to the first row after it that stayed (`keeping(_:across:)`, found by the rows' keys, never by an index or a stale key the same commit shifted), none left wrapping as the walk does. A row whose issue is found again is changed where it stands (`merge`): the rows, their index (`placeByKey`, `byShape`) and the stops counted before each are made again only when a row arrives, leaves or is put elsewhere, and a row that becomes a stop or stops being one has the counts redone from it (`recount(from:)`); a message that changes tells only the observers of `rows`. **Board order**: a row keeps its `BoardOrderKey`, which holds places in the stack, so a commit that may have shifted them for shapes it did not name (`ChangeSet.mayHaveChangedBoardOrder(in:)`, Topology's, [topology](topology.md): shapes added, removed or restacked, another board reached, the document replaced, a shape put in another container) has every row's key taken again from one snapshot first (`retakeKeys`: O(rows × depth), some 0.2 µs a row measured; a row whose shape the commit named is also put on the board it is on now, so a shape moved onto another board (Move To ▸) has its key and its board agree before the runs are made) and the rows sorted again only if that put them in another order, the place kept; a row whose shape has no key now (gone, or on a board that cannot be shown: a pocket deleted with its board) is taken out there, as `read` counts such a shape, so a stale key is never compared with a fresh one, in the sort or in a board's run, and the current stop's successor is found across the rows as they were before the commit; `IssueLinePlaces` lets go of its lists by the same predicate. A task or a turn of the scheduler the walk waits on holds neither the walk nor the editor (`weak`) and gives up when the editor is gone (`isEditorGone`) before touching either; `end()` cancels the task. A board has one run in `boards` whatever the rows' order does (`setRows` leaves out a board met again after another's run, so the list never makes two heads with one id, and a debug build stops on it: a fault of the rows' order, which retaking the keys prevents). A scope that says it changed (`isChanged`: a rule reworded, in the type's schema, whether by the Schema tab, an undo or a redo) is followed in place: the rows are made again from the new scope, those of an issue the new scope still includes (a rule that only moved) kept as they were, ignored and holds ones too, and the place is kept on the shape it was on (`placeHint`, until that shape has a row again, or the selection is moved elsewhere), as the walk has it when the answer comes, not as it had it when the answer was asked for: a commit during the wait that deleted the current stop has moved the walk to the stop after it, which the rows made again keep); a scope whose `label` is nil (its rule deleted) ends the walk, and so does a file put in place.
  - **Arriving.** At each stop (`walkDidMove`) the Issues tab is chosen for the stop's kind (`DetailPanelState.choose`, the kind's earlier choice kept to give back by `end()`: `chosenTab(for:)`, `forgetChoice(for:)`), and `editor.announce` says "Issue 12 of 63, Texas, Roger Williams → Laken Riley Act, In line with the member's record."
