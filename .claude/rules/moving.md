# Moving

Moving: `/move-to` and `/move-near`, slash commands that move the shape whose text they're typed in into a frame, onto a subboard's board, or beside a shape, on any board of the file; what each takes, where it lands (`canvas draw`'s placing), and the commit.

Related: [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [command line](command-line.md), [containers](containers.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [transform](transform.md), [locking](locking.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Moving](../../docs/plugin-system.md#moving).

## Architecture

- Moving (a vertical that no kind opts into and nothing imports, as Jump To and `Examples/Reordering` are; plugin id `moving`) is two every-shape slash commands ([slash commands](slash-commands.md)), on SlashCommands, Canvas, DrawScript and Geometry: `MovingPlugin` (its only public type) registers `registrar.slashCommandsForEveryShape(where:)` on the kinds whose props are `BoxShapeProps` (the kinds a drag moves, read from `SlashCommandKind.definition` by opening the existential; a link, a tag or an arrow offers neither), `SlashCommand("move-to", argument: "@frame or subboard", kinds: { $0.containingKinds.union($0.boardOwningKinds) }, shapes:, step: "Move", perform:)` and `SlashCommand("move-near", shapes:, step: "Move", perform:)`, so the report's line is `moving /move-to /move-near → every shape`, a note's `/` list ends `title, lock, public, move-to, move-near` (`MovingPlugin()` is right before `ReorderingPlugin()` in the shipped list, after every plugin with an every-shape command), and both steps are "Move", a drag's name (`MovingStyle.stepName`). A list with it needs `SlashCommandsPlugin()` (`ownerNotInstalled`). Typed in a note's editor, the `@` picker on the line lists what the command takes ([mentions](mentions.md)): for `/move-to` only the titled frames and subboards (its `kinds`, members kept as a scope's, so it costs what they are), with nothing typed every one it takes on every board (the board shown's nearest first, then the boards it's inside, then the others, the nearest board first), for `/move-near` every shape; Return picks, a second Return runs the line. Both work across the file's boards, never only the board shown.
- **What each takes** (the `shapes:` rule, `Moving.swift`, also what the picker lists): `/move-to`'s is `Destination(of:into:in:)`, asked only of frames and subboards: a shape on a board the app can show (`editor.shape(inFile:)`, not kept aside), not the shape itself nor what goes with it (`goesWith(_:onto:_:in:)`: the target is the shape or in it at any depth, or the board it would land on has, on the file's path to it (`CanvasFile.path(to:)`), a board the shape or a shape it holds owns: the owners above the board against what moves, as `move(_:to:)`'s preconditions ask it, which trap on those), not read-only on its board (`registry.isReadOnly(_:in:)` against that board: a locked frame or subboard, or one in a locked frame, takes nothing), not where the shape is already (the frame it's directly in; the subboard whose board is shown), a frame no narrower and no shorter than the shape's box, and a frame on another board only if that board takes shapes (its owner not read-only on its own board, the rule `move(_:to:)` refuses by). A board owner is taken as a board (`.board(BoardID(owner:))`), else a container as `.container(id, on: board)`, as draw's `in` prefers a subboard. `/move-near`'s is `admitsNear`, which the picker asks of every shape as the person types (no kinds), so it costs next to nothing: not the shape itself and, only for a shape that holds shapes or owns a board (`takesAlong`, rare: a note does neither), nothing that goes with it; a read-only target is fine (it doesn't change), and whether its board takes shapes is the move's to say. Each `perform` asks its rule again (`Move.into`, `Move.near`): a target it no longer takes, or one gone, moves nothing.
- **Where it lands** (`Landing.swift`), with draw's placing (Automation's DrawLayout): `Surroundings(on:moving:in:)` reads the destination board in place (`editor.reading(of:)`, shown or not; nil for one `canShow` refuses), files every shape there with a solid (`ShapeContext.solidBounds(of:)`) in one `ObstacleGrid`, in paint order, each numbered by its document index, the moving shape left out on its own board and what it holds `carries` (never in its way), and makes each problem's one free box the shape's box size with no `current` (put down anew, as a new shape: where it is holds it nowhere). Near X (`near`): four `LayoutProblem`s, `right`, `below`, `left`, `above`, each a `.direction` clause against X fixed at its geometry bounds (its obstacle number if it's solid), draw's default gap (`normal`, 80) and, a lone direction, centered on X the other way; the solver pushes the box past what's in its way along that side's axis only (the other is held by the centering), with draw's clearances (40 across, 64 up and down); the side whose gap from X along its axis is least wins, the earlier on a tie (`1e-6`), so the first free side, else the one that frees up nearest; a side that throws is skipped; none placed, right of X at 80, centered, over what's there; X with no finite bounds, nothing. Into frame F (`inside`): draw's `in "F"`, `.inside` F `top-left` with F the holder, 40 in (`tight`), pushed clear of what's in F and kept within it, held directly (`Holder.direct`: nothing in F is a zone of it, so a frame in F is in its way like any shape there); no room that way (it throws: `no_room`), from F's top-right, bottom-left and bottom-right in turn (`MovingStyle.corners`, each pushed its own ways); none, at F's top-left `min(40, room / 2)` in per axis, over what's there. Onto a subboard's board (`anywhere`): the one free box with nothing to hang on, draw's spot search from the middle of the view that board would be shown with (`reading.view`, else `reading.shapes.camera.toWorld(.zero)`), ring by ring, `boardBounds` the grid's; fallback, centered there. A landing on the board shown carries the zones the solver says it sits on (`solution.zones`, by id) and, into F, F itself, to be raised above (draw's `raise`). Zones are draw's: a corral holding X at twice X's area is a region the box sits on, not in its way, so a shape moved beside a shape in a frame lands in the frame; a frame nested in F is never a zone of a shape moved to F, unlike draw's `in "F"` (where a shape landing wholly on a frame in F that holds something sits in that frame), so it goes in F itself wherever F has room from a corner; with none, over what's at F's top-left, the in-or-out rule may put it in a frame there.
- **The commit** (`Move.swift`, `commit`): one `editor.transact("Move")` with the core's tools as draw's commit uses them, the shape moved by where it lands less where it is (what it holds goes along, read-only or not). Landing on the board shown: `translate([id], by:)`, `move(_:above:)` each zone and F that paints above it, then `settle([id])`. On another board: `move([id], to:)` first, so what's bound to it across the edge lets go where it was drawn (an arrow's end stays where the shape was, as after a cut, not where it lands, a spot on the board shown it never reaches), then `translate([id], by:on:)` there (the core's, for this) and `settle([id], on:)`. Anything refused (the shape read-only, a read-only container settling would put it in, the read-only owner of the board it goes onto, a read-only shape among what a container would take onto another board, which `translate` carries on its board but `move(_:to:)` refuses) throws `Refused` with `transaction.refusedShapes`, so nothing commits and no step opens, and `editor.refuseChanges(to:)` makes them heard (they shake), as Transform's turns are. After a commit on the board shown: a shape being edited that moved is put down (`endEditing(discarding: false)`, as a `canvas draw` that moves it ends it), then what moved glides from where it was (`editor.glide`: the shape and what it holds by the translation, plus settle's nudges); the camera stays. Onto another board: the shape is gone from the board shown (the core ends its editing), nothing glides, and the step, made on the board shown, holds both boards whole, so Undo Move puts them back exactly. A move that lands where the shape is commits nothing: no step, editing goes on.
- Not built: moving several selected shapes, from a menu (Move to Subboard); following the shape to the board it went to; `/move-near` naming the side (`/move-near @X below`).

## Testing

- MovingTests use fixtures only (CanvasTesting's boxes, discs and tags, corrals, pockets, links, cards, `FreezingPlugin`, with `SlashCommandsPlugin()` and `MovingPlugin()`: `movingHarness`), driven headlessly through `editor.slashCommands` (`h.run(_:on:with:)`, a line's one pill as `SlashArguments([.shape(id, label:)])`, and `h.admits(_:on:_:)`, `OfferedSlashCommand.admits`), the view given with `viewportDidResize(to:)` where a test needs one: registration (the report's line, both offered on every box kind and neither on a link or a tag, none on a frozen shape, the hints, `admittedKinds`, the step names, `ownerNotInstalled` without SlashCommands), what each takes (`/move-to`: corrals and pockets here, on a pocket's board and a pocket's pocket, not a box or a link, not where it is, the corral above that one taken, the root's from inside a pocket, nothing frozen or in a frozen corral or on a frozen pocket's board, nothing too small, nothing on a board a box owns, nothing that goes with a corral or a pocket; `/move-near`: anything but itself and what goes with it, frozen targets and other boards' included), `/move-to` (40 in, clear of a box at the corral's top-left, in it, one step, undo and redo exactly, the glide; a full corral and one barely bigger; a corral with what it holds; a card being edited put down; a pocket's board at the view's middle and at the nearest clear ring spot, undo and redo exactly, a card edited there no longer edited; a corral on a pocket's board, and the root's from a pocket's board; a target gone not accepted), `/move-near` (each side in turn, all four taken with the nearest pushed out and a tie to the earlier, already beside committing nothing with editing going on, into a corral the target's in, out of the one it was in and beside it, raised above a zone, a corral with what it holds, the root from a pocket's board and a pocket's board from the root, settled there, a frozen target) and refusals (a frozen corral it would land in, a frozen pocket's board, a corral holding a frozen box moving on its board but not onto another: nothing changes, no step, the frozen shape heard); `Pin` (a fixture kind bound to a shape as an arrow's end is, which, let go, keeps where it's drawn) holds that a move onto a pocket's board lets go where the shape was, not where it lands. CanvasTests' MovingAcrossBoardsTests hold `translate(_:by:on:)`.
- CompositionTests' `movingShippedTests` (`MovingShippedTests.swift`, a pasteboard of its own) type into a note's editor in the app's canvas view: `/move-to @` lists only the titled frames and subboards (no note, nothing untitled), nearest first; Return picks one and a second runs the line, the note lands 40 in from the frame's top-left, in it, its line gone and editing over, and Undo Move takes it back, the typing before it; `/move-near @mil` puts it right of Milk, 80 away and centered; `/move-to @pla` moves it onto the subboard's board, the window staying on the root, editing over, and Undo Move brings it back; on a subboard's board, `/move-to @` lists the subboard beside it on the root with nothing typed, and picked and run moves the note onto that one's board, the window staying where it is. `slashCommandsShippedTests` and `transformShippedTests` pin the command lists with Moving's two last, and ShippedTests' report (and the guide's copy) `moving /move-to /move-near → every shape`.
