# Moving

Moving: `/move-to` and `/move-near`, slash commands that move the shape whose text they're typed in into a frame, onto a subboard's board, or beside a shape, on any board of the file; the table of contents' drops that move shapes into a frame, onto a subboard's board or out of frames (`ContentsMoves`); what each takes, where it lands (`canvas draw`'s placing), and the commit.

Related: [placing](placing.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [command line](command-line.md), [containers](containers.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [transform](transform.md), [locking](locking.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Moving](../../docs/plugin-system.md#moving).

## Architecture

- Moving (platform, a thing you do: no kind opts into it and nothing imports it, as with Jump To and `Examples/Reordering`; plugin id `moving`) is two slash commands on the kinds with a box ([slash commands](slash-commands.md)), and the table of contents' `ContentsMoves` (below), on SlashCommands, ContextMenus, TableOfContents, Draw, Boards (Move To's paths) and Canvas: `MovingPlugin` (its only public type) registers them in `registrar.forShapes(adopting: .box) { $0.slashCommands { … } }`, on the kinds whose props are `BoxShapeProps` (the kinds a drag moves; the core's capability `.box`, "with a box"; a link, a tag or an arrow offers neither), `SlashCommand("move-to", argument: "@frame or subboard", kinds: { $0.containingKinds.union($0.boardOwningKinds) }, shapes:, step: "Move", perform:)` and `SlashCommand("move-near", shapes:, step: "Move", perform:)`, so the report's line is `moving /move-to /move-near → every shape with a box · CanvasContextMenu → every shape with a box · ContentsMoves`, a note's `/` list ends `title, lock, public, move-to, move-near` (`MovingPlugin()` is right before `ReorderingPlugin()` in the shipped list, after every plugin with a command for other kinds), and both steps are "Move", a drag's name (`MovingStyle.stepName`). A list with it needs `SlashCommandsPlugin()` and `TableOfContentsPlugin()` (`ownerNotInstalled`). Typed in a note's editor, the `@` picker on the line lists what the command takes ([mentions](mentions.md)): for `/move-to` only the titled frames and subboards (its `kinds`, members kept as a scope's, so it costs what they are), with nothing typed every one it takes on every board (the board shown's nearest first, then the boards it's inside, then the others, the nearest board first), for `/move-near` every shape; Return picks, a second Return runs the line. Both work across the file's boards, never only the board shown.
- **What each takes** (the `shapes:` rule, `Moving.swift`, also what the picker lists): `/move-to`'s is `Destination(of:into:in:)`, asked only of frames and subboards: a shape on a board the app can show (`editor.shape(inFile:)`, not kept aside), not the shape itself nor what goes with it (`goesWith(_:onto:_:in:)`: the target is the shape or in it at any depth, or the board it would land on has, on the file's path to it (`CanvasFile.path(to:)`), a board the shape or a shape it holds owns: the owners above the board against what moves, as `move(_:to:)`'s preconditions ask it, which trap on those), not read-only (`editor.isReadOnly(inFile:)`: a locked frame or subboard, one in a locked frame, or one on a board that takes no shapes, a locked subboard's, at any depth, takes nothing; the rule `move(_:to:)` refuses by, which also covers a frame on another board that takes no shapes), not where the shape is already (the frame it's directly in; the subboard whose board is shown), and a frame no narrower and no shorter than the shape's box. A board owner is taken as a board (`.board(BoardID(owner:))`), else a container as `.container(id, on: board)`, as draw's `in` prefers a subboard. `/move-near`'s is `admitsNear`, which the picker asks of every shape as the person types (no kinds), so it costs next to nothing: not the shape itself and, only for a shape that holds shapes or owns a board (`takesAlong`, rare: a note does neither), nothing that goes with it; a read-only target is fine (it doesn't change), and whether its board takes shapes is the move's to say. Each `perform` asks its rule again (`Move.into`, `Move.near`): a target it no longer takes, or one gone, moves nothing.
- **Where it lands** is [placing](placing.md)'s (Draw's placement for plugins), Moving's solver moved out so any plugin can use it (`Landing.swift` keeps only the `Block` glue: `Surroundings(on:moving: block, in:)`, which hands it the block's box size, its shapes as the ones carried and what they hold as `held`). `/move-to` asks `inside` (a frame) or `anywhere` (a subboard's board), `/move-near` `near`, the drops all three; what each does, and why, is there.
- **The commit** (`Move.swift`, `Block.commit`): one `editor.transactWhole("Move")` with the core's tools as draw's commit uses them, the shape moved by where it lands less where it is (what it holds goes along, read-only or not). Landing on the board shown: `translate([id], by:)`, `move(_:above:)` each zone and F that paints above it, then `settle([id])`. On another board: `move([id], to:)` first, so what's bound to it across the edge lets go where it was drawn (an arrow's end stays where the shape was, as after a cut, not where it lands, a spot on the board shown it never reaches), then `translate([id], by:on:)` there (the core's, for this) and `settle([id], on:)`. Anything refused (the shape read-only, a read-only container settling would put it in, the read-only owner of the board it goes onto, a read-only shape among what a container would take onto another board, which `translate` carries on its board but `move(_:to:)` refuses) gives the whole move up (`editor.transactWhole`), so nothing commits and no step opens, and the refused shapes are heard (`editor.refuseChanges(to:)`: they shake), as Transform's turns are. After a commit on the board shown: a shape being edited that moved is put down (`endEditing(discarding: false)`, as a `canvas draw` that moves it ends it), then what moved glides from where it was (`editor.glide`: the shape and what it holds by the translation, plus settle's nudges); the camera stays. Onto another board: the shape is gone from the board shown (the core ends its editing), nothing glides, and the step, made on the board shown, holds both boards whole, so Undo Move puts them back exactly. A move that lands where the shape is commits nothing: no step, editing goes on.
- **Shapes that move together** (`Block`, `Move.swift`): on one board the app can show (any, not only the board shown), each with a box and not kept aside, back to front, less those another of them holds (it takes them along, `carried`), placed as one by the box around them all (`box`: the frames they are laid out in, `editor.frame(inFile:)`, which a shape on a board that isn't shown has too, so a note grown by its type is landed with room for what is drawn, and the delta is between origins, which a laid-out frame shares with its stored box), so they keep where they are to each other; a command's is one shape (`Move` wraps a `Block` of it). `Surroundings(on:moving:in:)` (Landing.swift) reads the destination with the block's shapes left out on their own board and what they hold carried. `Block.commit(_:finish:)` is the commit above for any of them from any board: landing on their own board (shown or not), `translate(_:by:on:)`, raised over the zones (`move(_:above:on:)`: when any of them is below one, all of them, together, in their order), `settle(_:on:)`; onto another, `move(_:to:)` from the board shown or `move(_:from:to:)` from another, then the same there; `finish` last in the transaction (the table of contents' restack), which throws to drop it all; glides only from the board shown onto it. Moving passes boxes to `move`, and writes no rule for what rides: an arrow bound only to shapes that move goes with them, the core's rule in `move(_:to:)` ([boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md)), so two related notes moved together keep their arrow; the core says what rode (`transaction.rodeAlong`) and the commit translates those with the boxes (`translate(ids.union(transaction.rodeAlong), …)`), so an arrow's free end lands as far from its note as it was. ArrowsTests' `ArrowMovingOntoBoardsTests` hold it with the real arrow for a move off a board that is not shown, onto the board shown or a third; CompositionTests' `movingShippedTests` hold it through `/move-to` (the table of contents' drops share `Block.commit`).
- **The table of contents' drops** (`Dropping.swift`, `Drop`; [table of contents](table-of-contents.md)): `registrar.contentsMoves(admits:move:)` with `Drop(shapes, to: place, in:)`, which is nil for shapes not all on one board, kept aside or with no box (`Block`), read-only, all in the place already, a place that goes with them (its container or neighbor one of them or in one, its board under one of them), a board the editor can't show or that's read-only (a locked subboard's, at any depth: `editor.isReadOnly(board:)`), or a container (not opaque, `isContainer`, not read-only) too small for the block coming in from elsewhere (by the block's laid-out frames; `/move-to`'s own `Destination` reads the shape's, `editor.shapeContext.frame(of:)`). Those not in the place (`block`) land one way (`Way`): **into** the container as `/move-to` puts a shape in a frame (`inside`, the container on the place's board), **onto** another board as `/move-to` puts one on a subboard's (`anywhere`), or **beside** the outermost container they leave (`outermostLeft`: going to a container they're in at any depth, or the board, from inside one; the first's chain decides for the block) as `/move-near` puts one beside it, kept within the container they go to if they go to one (`near(_:within:)`: each side's problem held by it, directly; no side placed there, `inside` it), on their own board; then every shape dropped (`dropped`) is restacked where the drop says (`ContentsPlace.restack`), in the same step, Move, unless the in-or-out rule left what moved out of the place (no room there clear of what it holds, put down over a frame in it, which takes them): then the step throws (`Transaction.Refused`, naming none) and nothing changes. `goesWith(_:onto:moving:in:)` is the one check of what goes with a move, the commands' and the drops'. Asked as a drag moves over the rows, `Drop.init` lays nothing out; `commit` does.
- **Move To ▸** (`MoveToMenu.swift`): the selection's context menu, which is also the menu bar's Shape menu, holds a Move To submenu on every selection of shapes with boxes (`contextMenuForCanvas` under `forShapes(adopting: .box)`, its own group `MoveToMenuGroup`, `.before(StateMenuGroup.self)`: after what changes what a shape is, right before Lock and Delete; Moving imports ContextMenus). It lists the places `Drop(shapes, to: place, in:)` admits for the selection, the same refusals as a table of contents drop: the frames and subboards of the board shown, nearest the selection's middle first, then the boards that enclose the one shown, innermost first, at most `MovingStyle.menuLimit` (20); no submenu where none admits it. A frame is named as its row is (`tableOfContents.name(of:)`; an untitled one as every path names it, `registry.containerName(of:)`: "Untitled Subboard", "Frame", the placeholder its row shows), an enclosing board by its owner (named as a row is, though its owner is on none of the boards shown),  the root by the file's name; two places called alike (case apart) say their path instead, the names of the shapes each is inside, outermost first, then its own name, as every path says them ("Pen 800 › Pen 300": Boards' `ShapePaths`, Topology's containers, each named by `registry.containerName(of:)` and joined by `ShapePath.separator`, from the root, never trimmed to the board shown, which would leave two places on different levels alike again: a frame in a subboard and the board above it), so a frame called Checkout in two frames is two items; without TopologyPlugin they keep their names. Choosing one is `Drop.commit()`, the table of contents' own move and landing, one step, Move, several shapes together. A plain `NSMenu`, which the keys walk and type to select in (principle 10 of [keyboard navigation](keyboard-navigation.md); the submenu's place in the Shape menu is its key). Settling on a nudge is not this menu's.
- Not built: moving several selected shapes with a command; following the shape to the board it went to; `/move-near` naming the side (`/move-near @X below`).
