# Publishing

Publishing: which shapes are public (the only ones another shape may stand for: every shape, until a person makes it private), the two dials, the crossed-eye chip a private shape wears, refusing to go private while something stands for a shape and where the panel then goes (Neighbors' Connections tab), and `/public` and `/private`.

Related: [topology](topology.md), [locking](locking.md), [adornments](adornments.md), [selection](selection.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [titles](titles.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [neighbors](neighbors.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Publishing](../../docs/plugin-system.md#publishing).

## Architecture

- Public by default (the owner's decision of 2026-11-06: "public is the right default", private the exception, shown on the private shapes, not the public ones; the look picked from renders, the crossed eye alone as the lock is a lock alone). Before, every shape was private until made public and wore "Public" (with "· 3" for what stood for it) once it was; `canvas apply` and the digest made public what their pills stood for. Now nothing makes a shape public or private on its own: a person does, from the chip, the menu or a slash command, and `canvas draw` and `canvas apply` refuse a pill for a private shape rather than make it public ([command line](command-line.md), [drawings](drawings.md)).
- Publishing (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait, on Adornments, ContextMenus, DetailPanel, Neighbors, Selection, SlashCommands, TableOfContents and Topology; Arrows builds on it, its arrow adopting `NeverPublic`) says which shapes another shape may stand for, so what many shapes point at needs no arrow from each: a stand-in (Topology's `standsIn`, no kind in Publishing) takes theirs. It writes the ids `private` (property), `publishing` (plugin), `PublishMenuGroup` and `PrivateChip`, reads no edge type id of its own (Topology's `standIns(for:)` for what holds a shape), and knows no reference. Private is something the shape knows: `PrivateProperty` (`private: true`, nil while public, an added property every kind opts into by `optInEveryShape(where:)`), read as `registry.isPublic(record)` (an always-public kind's every shape; else the kind holds the property, the record isn't private and isn't kept aside: one lookup in `PrivacyKinds`, the kinds that hold the property and the always-public ones, made once a registry, `registry.memo`) and, for any thread, `registry.publicShapes` (a `@Sendable` admission over the same table: a stand-in's, `standsIn(admitting: { $0.publicShapes })`, which Topology reads off the main actor too) and `registry.canBePublic(kind)`, set with `editor.makePublic(ids)` ("Make Public", one transaction, each shape on whichever board of the file it is, `transaction.update(inFile:)`, through `registry.makePublic(&record)`, the one writer that takes `private` off a record) and `editor.makePrivate(ids)` ("Make Private": the guarded way, which the menu, `/private` and the open eye's click use: shapes nothing stands for go private in one step, the held ones are refused with `refuseChanges(to:)`, so Selection's `RefusalShake` shakes them, nothing is written for them, and the panel showing one of them alone goes to Neighbors' Connections tab, where what stands for it is among what points to it; it returns the held ones; a shape of a kind that holds no property is passed over). What a stand-in stands for a shape is anything, direct or a dynamic match: `topology.standIns(for:)`. Two dials are capabilities on a kind's definition, adopted in an extension: `NeverPublic` (the kind holds no property: the arrow, and a kind that stands in) and `AlwaysPublic` (every shape is public, with no property, chip, menu item or command; no shipped kind); a definition adopting both is refused when the registry is built (`PublishingDials`, a point Publishing always contributes to so its `check` runs). A path with no guard (undo, redo, a paste, a hand-edited board, `canvas draw`'s `private: true`) writes private whatever stands for the shape: the stand-ins let go of it in that commit and making it public heals them. Old boards' `public` values are kept aside ([boards on disk](boards-on-disk.md); no user data yet, so no migration): their shapes are public.
- `PublishingState` (`editor.publishing`, a service) keeps no copy of which shapes are private: a commit's newly public shapes are those private before it (`ChangeSet.before`) and not now; it reads only what a commit added or changed other than by moving, shows the open eye through Adornments' `ChipFade` on each shape a commit makes public (an undo too; not a paste that arrives public) and takes it back when the shape goes private or is deleted, and tells Adornments once for all it changes. It follows nothing of Topology's: what it draws depends on the shape's own value, which a commit to the shape changes, and asks what stands for a shape (`isHeld`, `standIns(for:)`) only when one is to go private. `admitting` is a `@Sendable` closure made once (`admittingMade`, debug builds: 1) from the privacy table and `topology.standInKinds`, for whatever runs off the main actor; `admittable`, made with it, is what it turns away that Make Public would admit (private, of a kind that holds the property, not kept aside, not a stand-in): what a query that stands for public shapes alone holds back (References' count of a query's matches that aren't public, and what its Make Public writes).
- The chip (`PrivateChip`, a `ClickableAdornment`, `.aboveTopRight`, `.last` as the lock is, after it in plugin order, so 4 points left of it) is on the kinds that hold the property (one scope, `forShapes(holding: PrivateProperty.self)`, for it, the row mark and `/public` and `/private`), never on an always-public kind's, never on a skeleton, and only on a private shape: the crossed eye alone (`eye.slash`, `PublicStyle.privateSymbol`), a symbol with no word as the lock is a lock alone, the size of the lock's chip, so it has no compact face and leaves a title the same room at every width; VoiceOver calls it "Private" (`partTitle`, `PublicStyle.privateWord`, `nonisolated` since a part's title is asked off the main actor). A reference pill trails the same symbol for a shape that isn't public ([references](references.md)). For a moment after a shape goes public (any way: the chip, the menu, a command, an undo, a draw) it shows the eye open (`eye`), fading as the open lock does (1.5 s, 0.3 s; the pointer on it keeps it), which is no part. A click is a try of the command its face shows (`editor.attempt`, its rule not asked: [editor](editor.md), Commands): on the crossed eye, Make Public; on the open eye, Make Private, which makes an unheld shape private and refuses a held one (above). A locked shape is refused by the core either way.
- Make Public and Make Private are two commands, each declared once (`PublishMenus.swift`: `.makePublic` and `.makePrivate`, titled as the steps are, no key), which the menu's items, `/public` and `/private` and the chip run: Make Public's rule, some of its shapes are private and one isn't read-only, making those public; Make Private's, some are public and one isn't read-only (held or not: on a held one it is a try, as the open eye's click, so the key explains itself: nothing changes, the shape shakes and the panel goes to Connections), making the public ones private through `editor.makePrivate`, which refuses the held ones. The menu: one toggle in `PublishMenuGroup` (`.after(StateMenuGroup.self)`: right after Lock, before Delete), `MenuItem(command:… dims: true)`, items for the canvas in every menu rather than the scope's (on a mix with shapes of kinds that can't be private the commands pick out those that can: MenuTests' "a mix makes private only what can be"): Make Public while any shape selected is private, else Make Private (so a shape as it starts offers Make Private), each dimmed while its command can't act (every shape it would change read-only); `/private` on a public shape (`SlashCommand("private", command: .makePrivate, when: { $0[PrivateProperty.self] != true }, tries: true)`: offered on any public shape, held or not, and a try, so a held one shakes) and `/public` on a private one (`when` the shape is private, reaching exactly the shapes it would make public), on the kinds that hold the property, steps named "Make Private" and "Make Public" as the commands are, and, since SlashCommands offers nothing in a read-only shape's text, never there on a locked one. For the shapes selected each is a declared command and acts on all it reaches together (`SlashReach.together`), in one step: a public plaque and a private one selected list both, each `1 of 2`, `/private` making the public one private and leaving the other, `/public` the reverse; `/private` on a selection of a held plaque (something stands for it) and a free one is tried on both, so the held one shakes and nothing is written for it (`makePrivate` refuses it) while the free one goes private, in the one step; and a locked plaque in the selection is reached by neither, not even `/private`, a try: SlashCommands offers a read-only shape a declared command only where its rule can act on that shape alone, and neither rule can act on a locked one. A shape of a kind that holds no property (`NeverPublic`: an arrow, a reference; or `AlwaysPublic`) may be among the shapes selected but is reached by neither command. A row mark on a private shape's row in the table of contents: the crossed eye, "Private"; none on a public one's, however many things stand for it.
- What stands for a shape is listed in Neighbors' Connections tab ([neighbors](neighbors.md)), which Publishing adds nothing to: a stand-in points to the shape it stands for (arrows' light names `stands-for`), so it is among what points to it, a plain row of its kind. Publishing's part is the tab a refusal sends the panel to (`showWhatHolds`, called by `makePrivate` with the held shapes): `detailPanel.choose(.of(NeighborsTab.self), for:)` when exactly one is held and the panel shows it alone (it is the whole selection), and nothing else; Publishing imports Neighbors for that tab's type.
- A row's far pick makes a private note public in the same step as its pill and arrow (`editor.relate`, [relationships](relationships.md#relating-from-a-row)), and so does a ⌘L pick (`editor.connect(…, fulfil: true)`, [connections](connections.md)): a person's own pick, which the list says first (`will be made public`, `FarVerdict`, Draw's); `registry.standIns.fulfil` runs the stand-in kind's `fulfilling`, which is `registry.makePublic`. Nothing else fulfils: `canvas draw` and `canvas apply` refuse a pill for a private shape, and the digest makes nothing public or private.
- Left to others: the Reference kind is [references](references.md)' (it adopts `NeverPublic`), a `:private` query word (`[private]` reads the value), a picker's candidates (the catalog is mentionable and named; every shipped kind that can be public is mentionable, CompositionTests).
