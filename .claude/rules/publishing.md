# Publishing

Publishing: which shapes are public (the only ones another shape may stand for), the two dials, the chip and its count, refusing to go private while something stands for a shape and where the panel then goes (Neighbors' Connections tab), and `/public` and `/private`.

Related: [topology](topology.md), [locking](locking.md), [adornments](adornments.md), [selection](selection.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [titles](titles.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [neighbors](neighbors.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Publishing](../../docs/plugin-system.md#publishing).

## Architecture

- Publishing (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait, on Adornments, ContextMenus, DetailPanel, Neighbors, Selection, SlashCommands, TableOfContents and Topology; Arrows builds on it, its arrow adopting `NeverPublic`) says which shapes another shape may stand for, so what many shapes point at needs no arrow from each: a stand-in (Topology's `standsIn`, no kind in Publishing) takes theirs. It writes the ids `public` (property), `publishing` (plugin), `PublishMenuGroup` and `PublicChip`, reads no edge type id of its own (Topology's `StandingEdge.id` for the follower, `standIns(for:)` for the count), and knows no reference. Public is something the shape knows: `PublicProperty` (`public: true`, nil while private, an added property every kind opts into by `optInEveryShape(where:)`), read as `registry.isPublic(record)` (the value, or an always-public kind: one dictionary hit and a cast) and, for any thread, `registry.publicShapes` (a `@Sendable` admission made once, the always-public kinds read as it's made: a stand-in's, `standsIn(admitting: { $0.publicShapes })`, which Topology reads off the main actor too) and `registry.canBePublic(kind)`, set with `editor.makePublic(ids)` ("Make Public", one transaction, each shape on whichever board of the file it is, `transaction.update(inFile:)`) and `editor.makePrivate(ids)` ("Make Private": the guarded way, which the chip, the menu and `/private` use: shapes nothing stands for go private in one step, the held ones are refused with `refuseChanges(to:)`, so Selection's `RefusalShake` shakes them, nothing is written for them, and the panel showing one of them alone goes to Neighbors' Connections tab, where what stands for it is among what points to it; it returns the held ones). What a stand-in stands for a shape (D2) is anything now, direct or a dynamic match: `topology.standIns(for:)`. Two dials are capabilities on a kind's definition, adopted in an extension: `NeverPublic` (the kind holds no property: the arrow, and a kind that stands in) and `AlwaysPublic` (every shape is public, with no property, chip, menu item or command; no shipped kind); a definition adopting both is refused when the registry is built (`PublishingDials`, a point Publishing always contributes to so its `check` runs). A path with no guard (undo, redo, a paste, a hand-edited board, `canvas draw`) writes private whatever stands for the shape: the stand-ins let go of it in that commit and making it public heals them.
- `PublishingState` (`editor.publishing`, a service made after Topology's) keeps no copy of which shapes are public: a commit's privatized shapes are those public before it (`ChangeSet.before`) and not now; it reads only what a commit added or changed other than by moving (`ChangeSet.moved`: a drag reads nothing), shows "Private" through Adornments' `ChipFade` on each shape a commit makes private (an undo too; not a paste that arrives private) and takes it back when it goes public or is deleted, tells Adornments once for all it changes, and follows Topology (`follow`: the shapes a change to `stands-for` edges touches, a commit's or a provide's) to make chips and contents rows again (`adornmentsDidChange`, `contentsDidChange`). `admitting` is a `@Sendable` closure made once (`admittingMade`, debug builds: 1) from the always-public kinds and `topology.standInKinds`, for whatever runs off the main actor; `admittable`, made with it, is what it turns away that Make Public would admit (not public, of a kind that holds the property, not kept aside, not a stand-in): what a query that stands for public shapes alone holds back (References' count of a query's matches that aren't public, and what its Make Public writes).
- The chip (`PublicChip`, a `ClickableAdornment`, `.aboveTopRight`, `.last` as the lock is, after it in plugin order, so 4 points left of it) is on the kinds that hold the property (one scope, `forShapes(holding: PublicProperty.self)`, for it, the globe mark and `/public` and `/private`), never on an always-public kind's, never on a skeleton: "Public" with a globe (full white, `brightSymbol()`, as the lock is; Adornments' leading symbols are otherwise softer), "Public · 3" once three things stand for the shape, "Private" with a globe while the fade lingers and fades (1.5 s, 0.3 s; the pointer on it keeps it); compact faces (Adornments' rule: taken where the full chip would leave the title cut short or out, from the narrowest shape until the full chip and the whole title fit together, so the face changes once as the shape widens): the globe alone, "3" with the globe, and the globe at the fading opacity. A click is a try of the command its face shows (`editor.attempt`, its rule not asked: [editor](editor.md), Commands): on a public shape, Make Private, which makes an unheld one private and refuses a held one (above); on "Private", Make Public. A locked shape is refused by the core either way.
- Make Public and Make Private are two commands, each declared once (`PublishMenus.swift`: `.makePublic` and `.makePrivate`, titled as the steps are, no key), which the menu's items, `/public` and `/private` and the chip run: Make Public's rule, some of its shapes can be public, aren't, and one isn't read-only, making those public; Make Private's, some are public and one isn't read-only (held or not: on a held one it is a try, as the chip's click, so the key explains itself: nothing changes, the shape shakes and the panel goes to Connections), making the public ones private through `editor.makePrivate`, which refuses the held ones. The menu: one toggle in `PublishMenuGroup` (`.after(StateMenuGroup.self)`: right after Lock, before Delete), `MenuItem(command:… dims: true)`, items for the canvas in every menu rather than the scope's (on a mix with shapes of kinds that can't be public the commands pick out those that can: MenuTests' "a mix makes public only what can be"): Make Public while any shape that can be public isn't, else Make Private, each dimmed while its command can't act (every shape it would change read-only); `/public` on a private shape (`SlashCommand("public", command: .makePublic, when: { $0[PublicProperty.self] != true })`, offered by its rule, and by its `when` reaching exactly the shapes it would make public) and `/private` on a public one (`when` the shape is public, `tries: true`: offered on any public shape, held or not, and a try, so a held one shakes as at the chip), on the kinds that hold the property, steps named "Make Public" and "Make Private" as the commands are, and, since SlashCommands offers nothing in a read-only shape's text, never there on a locked one. For the shapes selected each is a declared command and acts on all it reaches together (`SlashReach.together`), in one step: a public plaque and a private one selected list both, each `1 of 2`, `/public` making the private one public and leaving the other, `/private` the reverse; `/private` on a selection of a held public plaque (something stands for it) and a free one is tried on both, so the held one shakes and nothing is written for it (`makePrivate` refuses it) while the free one goes private, in the one step; and a locked plaque in the selection is reached by neither, not even `/private`, a try: SlashCommands offers a read-only shape a declared command only where its rule can act on that shape alone, and neither rule can act on a locked one. A shape of a kind that holds no property (`NeverPublic`: an arrow, a reference; or `AlwaysPublic`) may be among the shapes selected but is reached by neither command. A globe contents mark, "Public" or "Public · 3 references".
- What stands for a shape is listed in Neighbors' Connections tab ([neighbors](neighbors.md)), which Publishing adds nothing to: a stand-in points to the shape it stands for (arrows' light names `stands-for`), so it is among what points to it, a plain row of its kind. Publishing's part is the tab a refusal sends the panel to (`showWhatHolds`, called by `makePrivate` with the held shapes): `detailPanel.choose(.of(NeighborsTab.self), for:)` when exactly one is held and the panel shows it alone (it is the whole selection), and nothing else; Publishing imports Neighbors for that tab's type.
- A row's far pick makes a private note public in the same step as its pill and arrow (`editor.relate`, [relationships](relationships.md#relating-from-a-row)): `registry.standIns.fulfil` runs the stand-in kind's `fulfilling`, which is `registry.makePublic`, so the record-level writer of `public` is this one, and the row's list says `will be made public` first (`farVerdict`); Referenced In names a note by its values like every list.
- Left to others: the Reference kind is [references](references.md)' (it adopts `NeverPublic`), a `:public` query word, a picker's candidates (the catalog is mentionable and named; every shipped publishable kind is mentionable, CompositionTests).

## Testing

- PublishingTests use fixtures only (`Plaque`, `Seal`, `Draft`, `Holder`, `Lens`, `Relay` in `Support.swift`; CanvasTesting's `Link` with a fixture wire; `StubLock`, a fixture chip that is `.last`, and `StubTitle`, a fixture pill saying a plaque's `name` as Titles' pill says a title, so the chip's room beside a title is held without Titles, which Publishing doesn't import; `FreezingPlugin(log:)` for read-only shapes and the refusals heard; a test that follows a refusal into the panel adds Neighbors' `NeighborDetailsPlugin()`, the tab's): DialsTests (the property in each kind's schema, `isPublic`, a definition adopting both refused, `admitting`, `admittable`, only a public shape stood for), ChipTests (place, none for a seal, a draft, a stand-in or a skeleton, the count through every change to the edges and a provide, the compact face over a sweep of title lengths (full while the title is whole beside it, compact for every longer one), and zoomed out to 50% across the widths, one point at a time: the compact chip from the narrowest to 220 (no title below 92 points, then cut short, whole from 179), the full chip from 221, one change in all; clicks: steps, fade timings on `ManualScheduler`, hover, public again, a refusal with no step, the shake, the panel's tab (Connections, only when the panel shows the held shape alone), locked shapes), MenuTests, SlashTests (`/public` and `/private` in a shape's text: offered on the kinds that can be public and not on a seal, a draft or a stand-in, run in one step each named as the command is, a held shape refused like the chip, none on a locked shape; and for the shapes selected: a public and a private plaque each `1 of 2` in the first shape's order, run to leave the other as it was, three plaques alike reached together, `/private` on a held plaque and a free one shaking the held one and sending the free one private in one step, a locked plaque not reached, a seal, a draft and a stand-in not reached), RowTests, PanelTests (a refused click with the panel hosted in an unshown window: it lists the shape, Neighbors' list being told which shape is on screen; a click that makes the shape private leaves the panel where it was), UnguardedTests (undo, redo, paste, hand edits, deletion, boards). CompositionTests' `publishingShippedTests` hold the shipped kinds (all but the arrow can be public, and are mentionable), the chip beside a real lock, a copy, and `canvas draw`; `Fixtures/added/public.v0.json` is the added property's fixture.
