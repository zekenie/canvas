# Publishing

Publishing: which shapes are public (the only ones another shape may stand for), the two dials, the chip and its count, refusing to go private while something stands for a shape, `/public` and `/private`, and the Referenced In tab.

Related: [topology](topology.md), [locking](locking.md), [adornments](adornments.md), [selection](selection.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [titles](titles.md), [mentions](mentions.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Publishing](../../docs/plugin-system.md#publishing).

## Architecture

- Publishing (a concern, on Adornments, ContextMenus, DetailPanel, Selection, SlashCommands, TableOfContents, Titles and Topology; Connections builds on it, its arrow adopting `NeverPublic`) says which shapes another shape may stand for, so what many shapes point at needs no arrow from each: a stand-in (Topology's `standsIn`, no kind in Publishing) takes theirs. It writes the ids `public` (property), `publishing` (plugin), `PublishMenuGroup`, `PublicChip` and `ReferencedInTab`, reads no edge type id of its own (Topology's `StandingEdge.id` for the follower, `standIns(for:)` for the count), and knows no reference. Public is something the shape knows: `PublicProperty` (`public: true`, nil while private, an added property every kind opts into by `optInEveryShape(where:)`), read as `registry.isPublic(record)` (the value, or an always-public kind: one dictionary hit and a cast, for a stand-in's admission) and `registry.canBePublic(kind)`, set with `editor.makePublic(ids)` ("Make Public", one transaction) and `editor.makePrivate(ids)` ("Make Private": the guarded way, which the chip, the menu and `/private` use: shapes nothing stands for go private in one step, the held ones are refused with `refuseChanges(to:)`, so Selection's `RefusalShake` shakes them, nothing is written for them, and the panel showing one of them alone goes to Referenced In; it returns the held ones). What a stand-in stands for a shape (D2) is anything now, direct or a dynamic match: `topology.standIns(for:)`. Two dials are capabilities on a kind's definition, adopted in an extension: `NeverPublic` (the kind holds no property: the arrow, and a kind that stands in) and `AlwaysPublic` (every shape is public, with no property, chip, menu item or command; no shipped kind); a definition adopting both is refused when the registry is built (`PublishingDials`, a point Publishing always contributes to so its `check` runs). A path with no guard (undo, redo, a paste, a hand-edited board, `canvas draw`) writes private whatever stands for the shape: the stand-ins let go of it in that commit and making it public heals them.
- `PublishingState` (`editor.publishing`, a service made after Topology's) keeps the public shapes of the board shown (a `BoardStash` for boards left, `boardsScannedForPublic`, debug builds), reads only what a commit added or changed other than by moving (`ChangeSet.moved`: a drag reads nothing), shows "Private" through Adornments' `ChipFade` on each shape a commit makes private (an undo too; not a paste that arrives private) and takes it back when it goes public or is deleted, tells Adornments once for all it changes, and follows Topology (`follow`: the shapes a change to `stands-for` edges touches, a commit's or a provide's) to make chips and contents rows again (`adornmentsDidChange`, `contentsDidChange`). `admitting` is a `@Sendable` closure made once (`admittingMade`, debug builds: 1) from the always-public kinds and `topology.standInKinds`, for whatever runs off the main actor.
- The chip (`PublicChip`, a `ClickableAdornment`, `.aboveTopRight`, order 310, 4 points left of the lock's 300) is on the kinds that hold the property, never on an always-public kind's, never on a skeleton: "Public" with a globe (full white, `brightSymbol()`, as the lock is; Adornments' leading symbols are otherwise softer), "Public · 3" once three things stand for the shape, "Private" with a globe while the fade lingers and fades (1.5 s, 0.3 s; the pointer on it keeps it); compact faces (Adornments' rule: taken where the full chip would leave the title cut short or out, from the narrowest shape until the full chip and the whole title fit together, so the face changes once as the shape widens): the globe alone, "3" with the globe, and the globe at the fading opacity. A click on an unheld public shape makes it private; on a held one it is refused (above); on "Private" it makes the shape public again. A locked shape is refused by the core either way.
- The menu: one toggle in `PublishMenuGroup` (order 310): Make Public while any shape that can be public isn't, else Make Private; each dimmed when every shape it would change is read-only (and Make Private when every public shape is held); `/public` (private shapes) and `/private` (public ones) on the kinds that hold the property, steps named "Make Public" and "Make Private" (SlashCommands' `step:`), and, since SlashCommands offers nothing on a read-only shape, never on a locked one. A globe contents mark, "Public" or "Public · 3 references".
- Referenced In (`ReferencedInTab`, order 300, beside Mentioned In): through `detailsForEveryShape(where: { _ in true })` (the `where` sees only the record) and `shownWhile` (the kind can be public and something stands for the shape, observing `revision(of:)`), `enabledWhileReadOnly`: a `DetailShapeRow` for each stand-in (`referencedInRows(of:)`, file order), the kind's symbol, line one the names connected through it (`edges(through:)`, the other end, once each, a title else the kind's name, two then " +n") else the board's name (an owner's title else its kind's name, the root "Board"), the board's name at the end when it isn't the board shown. A kind that stands in describes its row through `StandInRows` (`x.standInRow { shape, registry in StandInRow(symbol:tint:text:) }`, keyed by kind, used when present).
- Left to others: the Reference kind is [references](references.md)' (it adopts `NeverPublic` and describes its Referenced In row through `StandInRows`; `PublishingState.boardName(of:)` names a board for its panel), a `:public` query word, a picker's candidates (the catalog is mentionable and named; every shipped publishable kind is mentionable, CompositionTests).

## Testing

- PublishingTests use fixtures only (`Plaque`, `Seal`, `Draft`, `Holder`, `Lens`, `Relay` in `Support.swift`; CanvasTesting's `Link` with a fixture wire; `StubLock`, a fixture chip at order 300; `FreezingPlugin(log:)` for read-only shapes and the refusals heard): DialsTests (the property in each kind's schema, `isPublic`, a definition adopting both refused, `admitting`, only a public shape stood for), ChipTests (place, none for a seal, a draft, a stand-in or a skeleton, the count through every change to the edges and a provide, the compact face over a sweep of title lengths (full while the title is whole beside it, compact for every longer one), and zoomed out to 50% across the widths, one point at a time: the compact chip from the narrowest to 220 (no title below 92 points, then cut short, whole from 179), the full chip from 221, one change in all; clicks: steps, fade timings on `ManualScheduler`, hover, public again, a refusal with no step, the shake, the panel's tab, locked shapes), MenuTests, SlashTests, RowTests, ReferencedInTests (the tab, rows, names, boards, going to one) and PanelTests (a hosted panel), UnguardedTests (undo, redo, paste, hand edits, deletion, boards). CompositionTests' `publishingShippedTests` hold the shipped kinds (all but the arrow can be public, and are mentionable), the chip beside a real lock, a copy, and `canvas draw`; `Fixtures/added/public.v0.json` is the added property's fixture.
