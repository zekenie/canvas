# Frames

Frames, the `frame` kind: a region of its board that holds shapes; its look, its hollow hit test, the Frame tool, Create Frame, its opt-ins and fixtures.

Related: [containers](containers.md), [connections](connections.md), [selection](selection.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [titles](titles.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [locking](locking.md), [transform](transform.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Frames](../../docs/plugin-system.md#frames).

## Architecture

- Frames (product, a vertical, on Connections, ContextMenus, DetailPanel, Keyboard, Mentions, Selection, TableOfContents, Titles, Transform and Types; `docs/plugin-system.md` "Frames"): the `frame` kind, `Frame` (props: only `frame`, required; the audit takes the word as the core's box member, owned by nobody), a region of its board that holds shapes, the core's containment (`FrameDefinition: ShapeContaining`, nothing to implement: what's in it moves with it, paints above it, takes its lock, and nothing sits across its edge; [containers](containers.md)). Its look (`FrameStyle`, world units but the border's width and reach, view points): a rounded rect (12), an opaque surface a step up from the canvas (white; a 0.15 gray in the dark) in a hairline border just inside its edge (a view point at every zoom; black at 12%, white at 14% in the dark), no shadow, so the dots stop at its edge and what it holds sits on it; with a color (`frame.colorable()`, no default: `context.colors(of:)`) its surface is the color's `surface` and its border the color's `solid`, the same width, and with none it is as just described; its outline is its rounded edge; zoomed out its skeleton (`FrameSkeleton.swift`): its surface and its border's ring, square, two fills, in its colors in full (a colored frame's too: still fills only, so a plain frame costs what it did), its title's tab over it as in full; in the minimap and a board's preview its outline, as every container's silhouette is (`Silhouettes`' `hollow`). Pressed hollow (its `hitTest`): holding shapes, only its border, 6 view points either side of its edge (`FrameStyle.borderReach`, its `hitMargin`), so a press inside is the canvas (a click deselects, a drag is a marquee) and the pointer there hovers nothing; holding nothing (`ShapeContext.hasContents`), anywhere inside too, where a double-click still puts a note in it as one on the canvas does (it neither opens nor edits: Selection's double-click claim, [selection](selection.md)); its title's tab grabs it (`DecorationHits`), at every zoom, an untitled one's dimmed "Frame" too, in full; selected alone, its border is its resize sides (Selection's, a side pressed anywhere along it: [selection](selection.md)), and its empty inside is the selection's bounds (Selection's `isInSelectionBounds`), where a drag moves it and a right-click is its menu, as tldraw's; it's also moved by its tab, and by its border with one press while it isn't selected; an arrow's end binds to it anywhere inside it, though (Arrows' `Connectables`, tldraw's `hitFrameInside`: what it holds is in front of it, found first), never while it holds the arrow's other end ([connections](connections.md)). The marquee selects it only when it reaches its edge or holds it whole, never drawn wholly inside it (`BrushHitTesting`). The Frame tool (F, `rectangle.dashed`, `FrameTool`): a drag draws one from the press to the pointer (`DrawingFrameSession`: shown over what it's drawn on, plain (no color), its surface at 30%, until the release; never under its placement's `minimumSize` (`Frame.minimumSize`, 96 × 64, said once, on the placement its tool and `canvas draw` share, `Placement.minimumSize`, which resizing, Create Frame and draw, refusing a `size:` under it, read too), grown from the press toward the pointer; ⇧ a square, ⌥ about the press, as they're pressed or let go; the canvas scrolls at its edges; Escape puts nothing down, the tool staying), a click, or a press that goes under 12 view points from where it began along either axis (`FrameStyle.smallestDrawn`, a click's jitter), places one of 640 × 400 (`Frame.defaultSize`) centered on it, either through the core's `place` (a drawn one `place(_:in:)`): one step, "Add Frame", settled (into the frame holding most of it, taking in what's mostly inside it, nudging what hangs over its edge, gliding), selected, the default tool back; into a locked frame nothing, and that frame shakes; its placement is `registry.placement(for:)`'s, so `canvas draw` draws one (`frame.tool(label:systemImage:shortcut:placing:_:)`). Create Frame (`CreateFrame.swift`, `registrar.createFrameItem()`, after the shape block; the frame goes around the laid-out frames of the shapes, not their stored boxes, [containers](containers.md)): a command, `.createFrame` ("Create Frame", Frames' own `CanvasCommand`, ⇧⌘F, tldraw's ⌥⌘G being Group's here: its one rule, some of its shapes has a box; from a key, with nothing under way or being edited, `.whenIdle`), and an item for the canvas that runs it, `MenuItem(command: .createFrame, in: FramesMenuGroup.self)` showing the key, shown by the command's rule: in the menu of any shapes a frame can hold (one with a box, not kept aside; an arrow alone has none), in its own group (`FramesMenuGroup`, `.after(TransformMenuGroup.self)`: right after Transform's Turn into, before Cut, Copy and Paste), that puts an untitled frame down around them (`editor.createFrame(around:)`, in a group named "Create Frame", so the key's step is named as the item's, not "Add Frame") through the core's `editor.place(_:around:padding:minimumSize:)` ([containers](containers.md)): holding them where they are, `FrameStyle.padding` (32 world units, as the model is: tldraw's is 25 view points, which zoomed out would reach over the neighbors) past them and an arrow between two of them, never under its placement's `minimumSize` where the frame they're all in has room, in that frame, just below the lowest of them in the stack (tldraw's goes on top: an intended deviation, so it covers nothing beside them that was above them, and shows on a big shape under them), what it lands on beside them settled (mostly inside comes in, mostly outside is nudged out, gliding), selected, one step named as the item, "Create Frame"; all or nothing, as Turn into is (tldraw frames the rest around a locked one): a locked shape among them, or one in a locked frame, leaves it all as it was, and shakes. No menu bar item runs it, so the Shape menu carries the item ([keyboard](keyboard.md)) and the Keyboard Shortcuts window lists ⇧⌘F under Canvas; with a field focused ⇧⌘F goes nowhere (the menu bar leaves it, and it is not Find's ⌘F). Not built: Remove Frame and Fit to Content. Opt-ins: `title(placeholderInPill: "Frame")` (Titles keeps its name, no symbol) and `titleTab` (said in a tab on its top edge, at every zoom, in its surface and border: a colored frame's color's `surface` and `solid` from Colors' book, else `FrameStyle.fill` and `border`; no pill and no cover: [titles](titles.md)), a `TitleHeader` header ("Untitled Frame"), `tint(FrameStyle.tint)` (the kind's color, `NSColor.systemGray`, the core's: its mentions', and its rows' in Find and Jump To), `mentionable()` (by its title; following goes to it; an untitled one isn't offered), `resizable(minimumSize: place.minimumSize)` (the core settles what a resize leaves hanging or grows over, at its end, and a member by its laid-out frame, as a drag of the frame does: [containers](containers.md); the sides a handle's drag moves snap to the shapes in view, its own contents among them: [snapping](snapping.md)), `typeable(fillsFromWords: false)` (Types: a frame can be of types, never one, and has no words, so it holds `is` and `fieldValues` and no `autofill`: its type chips sit in the row above its top edge, lifted clear of its title's tab and starting where the tab does ([adornments](adornments.md)), its values are in the Types tab of its panel, which a typed frame opens on, and no rows are drawn on it; a type's color colors it when it holds none of its own; its issues from a relationship's rules on its rows are said on the frame; [types](types.md)), a row (`ContentsLabel`, "Untitled Frame", its symbol in the gray, named by Titles) and `turnable(through: ContainerChannel.self)` (Transform's container channel, the last of its opt-ins: it can be turned into whatever takes the channel, a subboard in the app, from its menu; Frames only offers it, naming no kind that takes it, and brings no Turn into item or command of its own: [transform](transform.md)); and ports (`FrameLinks.swift`, `frame.frameLinks()`, last: `FrameLink`, `frame-link`, the frame's gray, declared after its shape block, on its right, bottom and left, since its top edge is its tab's, which grabs it, and an arrow leaves by the side that faces where it goes whatever dot it was drawn from, starting at the frame's middle), so an arrow joins two frames, or a frame and a subboard (which has ports on every channel), never a frame and a note, nor a frame and a shape it holds, at any depth (Connections' `mayConnect`); its dots show from its edge and its tab and go as the pointer goes in among what it holds, and over its edge a dot takes only a press on its disc, so the edge moves and resizes it right up to its dots and those of what it holds ([connections](connections.md)). Shipped after Subboards (the toolbar's Frame (F), between Subboard (B) and Reference (R): the two ways of grouping shapes side by side), with its fixture, `Fixtures/kinds/frame.v0.json`; `Fixtures/files/frames.v5.json` is a whole file (frames two deep holding a note, a subboard and a schema card, a locked frame holding a note, a note on the subboard's board), read with its schema card kept aside (the kind is gone) and written back as read, but for that card's version, which a board then writes on the card (`v`), since it lists only the kinds the app writes (CompositionTests' `FrameFile`, whose Order is a type note). FramesTests (their list installs `TypesPlugin()`, as every list with `FramesPlugin` does) and CompositionTests' `TypedContainersShippedTests` (a frame of a type) hold the kind, its hollow test, its marquee, its look, its tool, Create Frame (CreateFrameTests: the item and its band, the frame 32 past the boxes, selected, one step, undone; ⇧⌘F the same step with the item showing the key, going on with nothing selected, refusing and shaking with a locked box; the default tool back from the Frame tool; 96 × 64 around a small box; a frame's own menu, the new frame holding it in the frame it was in; a locked box refusing, shaken) and what it offers Transform (TurnTests: the container channel in the report, no Turn into item or command until a kind takes it, Create Frame in a frame's menu all the same, and, with CanvasTesting's pocket as the test's target, a frame turned with its title, what it held and the frame inside it kept, a locked box or frame refusing, an empty frame) over CanvasTesting's boxes (frozen for read-only); CompositionTests' FramesShippedTests the shipped pieces (drops in and out and across an edge, a locked frame refusing one, frames moved (by the pill, by the border unselected, which resizes it selected, and selected by its empty inside), a double-click inside one, empty or holding a note, putting a note in it (none on its tab, nor into a locked one, which shakes), nested, resized, deleted and drawn (three bands holding notes, drawn into each other's places in one run; one under 96 × 64 refused), Create Frame first in the menu of two notes and the bent arrow between them, around all three, the arrow still bound, a note it mostly covers taken in, one step, undone, a locked frame's contents, the skeleton, a mention in the app's canvas view), and its TransformShippedTests a frame turned into a subboard.
- VoiceOver reads a frame by its title and kind ("Sprint, Frame") and what it holds directly as its value ("Empty", "1 shape", "2 shapes": `FrameStyle.accessibleContents`, `frame.accessibility`), asked only when an assistive app asks ([accessibility](accessibility.md)).
