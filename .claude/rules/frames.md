# Frames

Frames, the `frame` kind: a region of its board that holds shapes; its look, its hollow hit test, the Frame tool, its opt-ins and fixtures.

Related: [containers](containers.md), [selection](selection.md), [titles](titles.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [locking](locking.md), [transform](transform.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Frames](../../docs/plugin-system.md#frames).

## Architecture

- Frames (a vertical, on DetailPanel, Keyboard, Mentions, Selection, TableOfContents, Titles and Transform; `docs/plugin-system.md` "Frames"): the `frame` kind, `Frame` (props: only `frame`, required; the audit takes the word as the core's box member, owned by nobody), a region of its board that holds shapes, the core's containment (`FrameDefinition: ShapeContaining`, nothing to implement: what's in it moves with it, paints above it, takes its lock, and nothing sits across its edge; [containers](containers.md)). Its look (`FrameStyle`, world units but the border's width and reach, view points): a rounded rect (12), an opaque surface a step up from the canvas (white; a 0.15 gray in the dark) in a hairline border just inside its edge (a view point at every zoom; black at 12%, white at 14% in the dark), no shadow, so the dots stop at its edge and what it holds sits on it; its outline is its rounded edge; zoomed out its skeleton (`FrameSkeleton.swift`): its surface and its border's ring, square, two fills, in its colors in full; in the minimap and a board's preview its outline, as every container's silhouette is (`Silhouettes`' `hollow`). Pressed hollow (its `hitTest`): holding shapes, only its border, 6 view points either side of its edge (`FrameStyle.borderReach`, its `hitMargin`), so a press inside is the canvas (a click deselects, a drag is a marquee) and the pointer there hovers nothing; holding nothing (`ShapeContext.hasContents`), anywhere inside too; its title's pill grabs it (`DecorationHits`), an untitled one's dimmed "Frame" too; `editor.shape(at:)` (an arrow's end) goes by the same test. The marquee selects it only when it reaches its edge or holds it whole, never drawn wholly inside it (`BrushHitTesting`). The Frame tool (F, `rectangle.dashed`, `FrameTool`): a drag draws one from the press to the pointer (`DrawingFrameSession`: shown over what it's drawn on, its surface at 30%, until the release; never under `Frame.minimumSize`, 96 × 64, grown from the press toward the pointer; ⇧ a square, ⌥ about the press, as they're pressed or let go; the canvas scrolls at its edges; Escape puts nothing down, the tool staying), a click, or a press that goes under 12 view points from where it began along either axis (`FrameStyle.smallestDrawn`, a click's jitter), places one of 640 × 400 (`Frame.defaultSize`) centered on it, either through the core's `place` (a drawn one `place(_:in:)`): one step, "Add Frame", settled (into the frame holding most of it, taking in what's mostly inside it, nudging what hangs over its edge, gliding), selected, the default tool back; into a locked frame nothing, and that frame shakes; its placement is `registry.placement(for:)`'s, so `canvas draw` draws one (`frame.tool(label:systemImage:shortcut:placing:_:)`). Opt-ins: `title(placeholderInPill: "Frame")` (Titles keeps its name, no symbol in its pill), a `TitleHeader` header ("Untitled Frame"), `mentionable(tint:)` (by its title, in `NSColor.systemGray`, `FrameStyle.tint`; following goes to it; an untitled one isn't offered), `resizable(minimumSize: Frame.minimumSize)` (the core settles what a resize leaves hanging or grows over, at its end), a row (`ContentsLabel`, "Untitled Frame", its symbol in the gray, named by Titles) and `turnable(through: ContainerChannel.self)` (Transform's container channel, the last of its opt-ins: it can be turned into whatever takes the channel, a subboard in the app, from its menu; Frames only offers it, naming no kind that takes it, and brings no item or command of its own: [transform](transform.md)); no ports. Shipped after Subboards (the toolbar's Frame (F), between Subboard (B) and Schema (S): the two ways of grouping shapes side by side), with its fixture, `Fixtures/kinds/frame.v0.json`; `Fixtures/files/frames.v5.json` is a whole file (frames two deep holding a note, a subboard and a schema card, a locked frame holding a note, a note on the subboard's board), read and written back byte for byte (CompositionTests' `FrameFile`). FramesTests hold the kind, its hollow test, its marquee, its look, its tool and what it offers Transform (TurnTests: the container channel in the report, no item or command until a kind takes it, and, with CanvasTesting's pocket as the test's target, a frame turned with its title, what it held and the frame inside it kept, a locked box or frame refusing, an empty frame) over CanvasTesting's boxes (frozen for read-only); CompositionTests' FramesShippedTests the shipped pieces (drops in and out and across an edge, a locked frame refusing one, frames moved, nested, resized, deleted and drawn, a locked frame's contents, the skeleton, a mention in the app's canvas view), and its TransformShippedTests a frame turned into a subboard.
