# Placing

Placing: where a box of shapes lands on a board, beside a shape, in a frame, or anywhere, worked out as `canvas draw` places a shape. It is Moving's solver moved into the draw engine's module (Draw, an engine), so Moving (`/move-to`, `/move-near`, the table of contents' drops) and any plugin that puts a new shape down (the `+` chip, New Shape from Person, New Shape from These) place the same way.

Related: [moving](moving.md), [command line](command-line.md), [editor](editor.md), [build](build.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Moving](../../docs/plugin-system.md#moving).

## Architecture

- Placing is Draw's placement for plugins (`Sources/Draw/Surroundings.swift`, beside the script engine and no module of its own: Draw is an engine on the platform, so any plugin may import it, and Canvas, DrawScript and Geometry, all it needs, are Draw's already) with two files' worth of API: `Surroundings` (a `@MainActor` struct), `Landing` (the answer: `board`, `rect`, `above`, the shapes to raise it over) and `PlacingStyle` (`sides`, `corners`). `Surroundings(on:moving:carrying:held:in:)` is the one way in: a size for the box, and, for shapes that already exist and are moving, `carrying` (the shapes making the box: no obstacle on their own board) and `held` (what they hold, at any depth: carried, never in their way). A new shape passes neither. Nil for a board the editor can't show or a size that is not finite.
- `near(_:within:put:sides:)` (beside a shape; `sides`, all four by default, the sides tried in order: `PlacingStyle.sides(of:facing:)` gives the side of a shape facing a box, then below (facing above: above, left, right), never its far side, for a new shape whose arrows run to that box: New Version's copy, U7, UX walk 3 evolve-1; with none placed, on the first side asked, not always right), `near(_:avoiding:within:)` (beside a box that is not a shape of the board yet; `avoiding` are boxes a caller has planned and not put down, which the landing keeps clear of as of any shape there: each an obstacle numbered after the board's, in its own grid), `inside(_:)` (in a frame), `between(_:_:growing:room:)` (between two shapes) and `anywhere()`. A stand-in's landing (`StandInRoute.landing(of:at:in:avoiding:)`, [command line](command-line.md)) is `Surroundings.landing(of:at:avoiding:)`, internal: one free box of the pill's size (`Free.implied`, as draw's is) placed by the clauses of its `StandInSpot` (draw's own `Clause` values) against the shape it is beside, or the column's last pill, fixed at their geometry bounds, so the rect is the one a `canvas draw` run gives the same pill; one side, the spot's, never the four `near` tries. Against a box planned and not on the board (`StandInRoute.landing(of:at:against:in:avoiding:)`, `Surroundings.landing(of:at:planned:avoiding:)`) it is the same placement with the box in place of the shape the spot names: a pill beside a shape a move is bringing here, or past a pill planned and not put down (the next in a start's column), no obstacle of the board's, its `avoiding` holding it clear of what is moving ([moving](moving.md)'s arrows kept through pills).
- **How it lands** (`Surroundings.swift`), with draw's placing (Draw's DrawLayout): `Surroundings(on:moving:carrying:held:in:)` reads the destination board in place (`editor.reading(of:)`, shown or not; nil for one `canShow` refuses), files every shape there with a solid (`ShapeContext.solidBounds(of:)`) in one `ObstacleGrid`, in paint order, each numbered by its document index, the moving shapes (`carrying`) left out on their own board and what they hold (`held`) `carries` (never in their way), and makes each problem's one free box the box's size with no `current` (put down anew, as a new shape: where it is holds it nowhere). Near X (`near`): four `LayoutProblem`s, `right`, `below`, `left`, `above`, each a `.direction` clause against X fixed at its geometry bounds (its obstacle number if it's solid), draw's default gap (`normal`, 80) and, a lone direction, centered on X the other way; the solver pushes the box past what's in its way along that side's axis only (the other is held by the centering), with draw's clearances (40 across, 64 up and down); the side whose gap from X along its axis is least wins, the earlier on a tie (`1e-6`), so the first free side, else the one that frees up nearest; a side that throws is skipped (one that crosses the holder's edge only on the axis its lone direction centers it on does not throw: the solver slides the box along that axis to the inset, `LayoutSolution.slides`, and that side competes at the same gap, so a tall box beside a shape at a frame's top inset lands right of it, slid down, where it was once passed over; the slide is the solver's, so Insert, ⇧↩ beside, the table of contents' drops and Types' landings inside a frame all have it); none placed, right of X at 80, centered, over what's there; X with no finite bounds, nothing. Into frame F (`inside`): draw's `in "F"`, `.inside` F `top-left` with F the holder, 40 in (`tight`), pushed clear of what's in F and kept within it, held directly (`Holder.direct`: nothing in F is a zone of it, so a frame in F is in its way like any shape there); no room that way (it throws: `no_room`), from F's top-right, bottom-left and bottom-right in turn (`PlacingStyle.corners`, each pushed its own ways); none, at F's top-left `min(40, room / 2)` in per axis, over what's there. Onto a subboard's board (`anywhere`): the one free box with nothing to hang on, draw's spot search from the middle of the view that board would be shown with (`reading.view`, else `reading.shapes.camera.toWorld(.zero)`), ring by ring, `boardBounds` the grid's; fallback, centered there. A landing on the board shown carries the zones the solver says it sits on (`solution.zones`, by id) and, into F, F itself, to be raised above (draw's `raise`). Zones are draw's: a corral holding X at twice X's area is a region the box sits on, not in its way, so a shape moved beside a shape in a frame lands in the frame; a frame nested in F is never a zone of a shape moved to F, unlike draw's `in "F"` (where a shape landing wholly on a frame in F that holds something sits in that frame), so it goes in F itself wherever F has room from a corner; with none, over what's at F's top-left, the in-or-out rule may put it in a frame there.
- `landing(of:joinedFrom:of:bounds:avoiding:)` is where a pill lands that an arrow from a row reaches (UX walk connect 24 and the wave 8 capture: a calls arrow through a table, then ending under its pill's bottom; E4 panel 4 puts it below the API): given the row's rect, the spots in order, each `PlacingStyle.pillReach` (120) out, then two and three reaches: below the shape, the pill's top middle under the row's left end, then under its right end, each also slid out from under the shape by half the pill, in by half (only where the line cuts across none of the shape's rows under the row), and out by a whole (only where the row still leaves by that end: the end facing the pill's middle), the line meeting the pill's top; straight down from a row with rows under it runs along the shape's own side, so it is taken only for the shape's last row, else the pill lands wholly beside the row's end (E4 panel 4); then right of the shape, then left, the pill's middle at the row's middle (level), the line meeting its near side. The first spot wins where the pill is within the clear view (`reading.view`, when there is one), clear of every shape there but the one it is joined to and what holds it (a frame or a database round it is a region the pill sits wholly on, raised above it, `above`, or wholly off, never across its edge) and of `avoiding`, by draw's clearances, and the straight line from the row's end to where it meets the pill crosses none of them (`crosses`, Liang and Barsky's clip against each rect a point in from its edges, so running along an edge isn't crossing). `bounds` is the shape's where the caller's step grows it (the row and the shape are measured on the step's document, `reading:`, so a row the step adds counts). Nil where none does. `StandInRoute.placePill(…from:…)` asks it first for a pill from a row (Connections' ⌘L pick and `reachingFarEnds`, the kit's typed keys and an endpoint's card lines), and Draw's `addStandIn` for an edge from a row of a shape the run leaves where it is, naming no side, in a run that places no box but pills (its pill then exact, `ShapeItem.exact`, and an obstacle of the solver, so nothing the run places lands on it; a shape the run restates, the near shape or a neighbour, is not in the pill's way when it is chosen); else each lands as before. PlacingTests' `pillLandingTests` hold it over fixtures. A line between rows (U4, UX walk 2 connect new-1 and new-2: a key, its far row on the binding of a pill for its table; `level: true`, which `placePill(onBinding:)`, a row a kind makes no pill for, and draw's `landedFrom.level` ask) is tried level with the row first, left of the shape then right (T3 panel 4, T6 panel 3), then below with the pill slid out past the row's end by `PlacingStyle.markRoom` (36: the longest end mark, a bar and a circle, and the room to turn) and its half or whole width, never under the end, so the crow's foot lies on a straight line; an endpoint's call keeps below first (E4 panel 4). `labels` (connect new-4): the labels the arrows already at `near` carry (a channel's pill: `StandInRoute.labels(ofArrowsAt:in:document:)`, from their geometry) are crossed by no pill's line, so a calls line never runs under the writes pill and reads as labeled by it. A box of `avoiding` that holds `near` (the frame it moves with) is a region the pill may sit on, never across its edge, as a shape of the board that holds it is; `inView: false` for a move's pill, landed where the move leaves the shape, in view or not. `joinsClean` asks of a pill already there what `landing` asks of one it lands: the line crosses no shape, `avoiding` or label, cuts across none of `near`'s rows under the row, runs down no side with rows beside it, and for `level` is level with the row or leaves its end by `markRoom`, never straight under it (a key reusing such a pill would hook as connect new-1 shows). `joinsClean(_:of:bounds:to:standIn:avoiding:)` asks the same of a pill there already: the straight line from the row's end facing it to its nearest point crosses no shape but the row's own, what holds it and the pill, nor any of `avoiding`; `placePill` reuses a pill only where it does, and a move's rebinding too (Draw's `CrossingArrows`, [moving](moving.md)), else a fresh one is put down.
- `between(_:_:growing:room:)` is where a shape lands that two arrows will join to two others (a relationship type between its two types): both read `growing` taller (each is about to gain a row), then, along the axis they are further apart on, the gap between them if it holds the box and `room` (default `PlacingStyle.arrowRoom`, 80, draw's `normal` gap; a caller asks for more where its arrows carry names) on each side, the box centered in it and on the middle of the two across, if nothing of the board is within the horizontal and vertical clearances of it (the pair themselves aside, and the frames they are in, at any depth, which are regions the box sits on: the landing is raised above them, as the solver's zones are); else (the spot taken, or neither axis's gap holds it) beside the middle of the two as `near` lands beside a box, (the middle is a point with no area, so it is marked as the board: nothing under it is a zone the box sits on, and whatever fills the gap there is in the box's way), `avoiding` the two grown boxes, but only the sides across the line between them (below and above for a pair side by side, right and left for one above the other), the nearer winning, so it is never pushed along the line into one of them. With only one of the two drawn, `near` that one; neither, nil. The rule is judged on the real board, not here; PlacingTests hold the answers over fixtures (side by side, above, apart on both axes, `growing`, `room`, in a frame, overlapping, a spot taken on both sides, a third shape on the line, a gap too small, one or none drawn).
- Insert is a caller: Draw's plugin claims the core's insertion spot (`registrar.insertionSpot`, [editor](editor.md)) with `Surroundings.insertionSpot(of:beside:in:)` on the board shown: beside one shape `near(_:within:)` its container, beside several `near` the box around them within the container they share, with none `anywhere()` (nil with no view, so the core puts nothing down). So ⇧↩ (Insert › Note), a note's ⇧↩ and every Insert item land where `canvas draw` would put the box.
- For a person (U1: `PutBeside`, `near(_:within:put:)`, `anywhere(put:)`): Insert's spot and a new type note put a shape where it's seen. Beside a shape it is top-aligned on its right or left (`top level with`, as T1 panel 3 draws two tables; below and above stay centered), and a side seen beats one that isn't, the nearer winning among alike. The clear view is the reading's `view` less its `covered` corners (the minimap's, which the host reports: [editor](editor.md)'s `clearView`), a `ClearView` `PlacingStyle.viewMargin` in from both, so nothing lands under the minimap (U11, UX walk 4 firstminute-4-1). A side seen only by a pan that would take out of the clear view a shape it shows whole that is **joined to the shape put beside** (an edge of any type Topology knows, either way: a key, an arrow, a type's is-a, a mention; `topology.neighbors(of:)`) or holds it ranks under a side seen as the view is, and over one not seen (`PutBeside.pansAway` over `joined(to:in:)`): Insert ▸ Enum beside orders keeps customers, keyed to orders, in view (U7, UX walk 3 tables-1), while a chain of shapes joined to nothing goes right in one row, panning, as T1 panel 3 decided (U11, UX walk 4 firstminute-4-2: U7 counted every shape in view, and a ⇧Return chain snaked, its second row reading right to left). A put beside the selection (Insert's spot: `PutBeside.inOrder`) never takes a side that reads backward (left, above) over one that reads on (right, below) that is seen at all, with a pan or without, so no chain reads backward. So the first minute's Grace, beside Ada, which nothing joins to the Person type, goes right of Ada, the view panning (FirstMinuteKeysTests' P12 extends toward Ada with ⌥⇧⌘←). For Insert (`panned`, the view then panning to it with what it's beside kept) a side is seen when the least pan (`ClearView.shift`, as the camera pans) brings its box and the shape it's beside into the clear view together; so it holds for every put beside that pans (⇧Return, Insert, paste beside the selection and New Version's copy); for a settled put (a new type note, nothing panned to) one inside the clear view as it is (`Surroundings.shows(_:put:)`), else the nearest clear spot in view (`anywhere(put:)`). The row above the box (Adornments' `rowGap + rowHeight`, 27 points) counts as the box's. With nothing beside, `anywhere(put:)` hands DrawScript's solver `landsInView` (its `covered` corners too): the nearest ring spot inside the clear view with the row above it, out of the corners, before any out of it. `canvas draw` places as before.
- Room below what grows (U11, UX walk 4 paste-4-1): a layout that puts shapes down leaves at least the room a kind keeps clear below a shape as it grows (`Registry.roomKeptBelow(_:)`, the kit's `KitStyle.roomKeptBelow`) under each: Draw's row layout of a put (`Put.layOut`: the SQL and OpenAPI pastes' grid) spaces its rows by the larger of draw's normal gap and that room, and draw's solver, a box placed below one that keeps room (or above one, for a box that keeps it) with no `gap:` said, by at least that room (`LayoutProblem.Free.roomBelow`, `Fixed.roomBelow`, set by DrawLayout from the kind; a list's step too). A gap a script says wins. `Surroundings` (Insert, ⇧Return, a move beside a shape, New Version) gives its target and its box that room too, so a shape put below a table lands past it, as draw's solver would put it; the room is for the way down alone, never across a corner (`below-right of`). `joined` keeps in view every holder up the chain, whether or not an edge names it as well.
- Types is a caller: `newType(named:besides:)` (`/is-a`'s New type) reads the board with `Surroundings(on:moving: size, in:)` and lands the new note with `near(owner)`, or, beside several shapes, with `near(_:avoiding:within:)` for the box around them (`ShapeContext.bounds(of:)`, avoiding each one's frame), then places it with the core's `place(_:in:)` in its own step ([types](types.md)). Put down settled with no clear spot in view (the view full, UX walk 2 apis 44), it lands at a clear spot of: the side `near` found, `anywhere()` round the view's middle, and each side (`near(_:avoiding:sides:)`, one side at a time) of what the person is at (the row a card hangs from, else what it is kept beside, `keeping:`, an API and the types its line names) and of each owner; ranked by U11's rule for a put, the one whose least pan into view (Selection's `revealTakesAway(_:bringing:keepingFirstOf:)`, the pan the reveal will make) takes none of what the person works with out of the clear view (`keeping`, the owners, and what is joined to `keeping`, `Surroundings.joinedInView(to:)`, each wholly in view now) first, then the fewest, then the nearest what the person is at (UX walk 5 apis-5-1: a second New type from one line, Refund, panned NewRefund, which the same line names, out of view). The view then pans the least to it (Selection's `revealIfOffscreen(_:keepingFirstOf:)`), keeping all of `keeping` where it fits with it (the API and the line's types), else its first (the API), else the card's row, so the card stays up.
- Moving keeps the glue (`Landing.swift`: `Surroundings(on:moving: block, in:)`) (the block's size is the box around the frames its shapes are laid out in, so Placing is handed a size and knows no layout) and the commit (`Block.commit`): Placing only says where; changing documents is the caller's, in its own `editor.transact`.

## Testing

- PlacingTests' `insertingTests` hold Insert's landings with `DrawPlugin()` installed, each against what `Surroundings` answers: beside one box (80 right, centered), within a corral and held by it, beside two (the box around them), the middle of the view and a clear spot round a box there, and nothing with no view; for a person, a side whose pan loses a shape joined to the target (a fixture link, `JoinsPlugin`, Topology installed) against one joined to nothing, a run that never reads backward, and the minimap's corner (`coveredCorners`) no part of the clear view.
- PlacingTests (`// layer: test of Draw`, named for the part of Draw it tests) use CanvasTesting's boxes and corrals (`FixturesPlugin`, `CorralsPlugin`) and ask `Surroundings` directly, with no command: beside a shape (right when free, the next side when taken), the shapes carried no obstacle on their own board and what they hold carried with them, within a corral, inside a corral, anywhere, and no surroundings for a board that can't be shown or a size that isn't one. MovingTests hold the commands over it (the commits, the glide, the refusals).
