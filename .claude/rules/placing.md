# Placing

Placing: where a box of shapes lands on a board, beside a shape, in a frame, or anywhere, worked out as `canvas draw` places a shape. It is Moving's solver moved into the draw engine's module (Draw, a surface), so Moving (`/move-to`, `/move-near`, the table of contents' drops) and any plugin that puts a new shape down (the `+` chip, New Shape from Person, New Shape from These) place the same way.

Related: [moving](moving.md), [command line](command-line.md), [editor](editor.md), [build](build.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Moving](../../docs/plugin-system.md#moving).

## Architecture

- Placing is Draw's placement for plugins (`Sources/Draw/Surroundings.swift`, beside the script engine and no module of its own: Draw is a surface, so any plugin may import it, and Canvas, DrawScript and Geometry, all it needs, are Draw's already) with two files' worth of API: `Surroundings` (a `@MainActor` struct), `Landing` (the answer: `board`, `rect`, `above`, the shapes to raise it over) and `PlacingStyle` (`sides`, `corners`). `Surroundings(on:moving:carrying:held:in:)` is the one way in: a size for the box, and, for shapes that already exist and are moving, `carrying` (the shapes making the box: no obstacle on their own board) and `held` (what they hold, at any depth: carried, never in their way). A new shape passes neither. Nil for a board the editor can't show or a size that is not finite.
- `near(_:within:)` (beside a shape), `inside(_:)` (in a frame) and `anywhere()`.
- **How it lands** (`Surroundings.swift`), with draw's placing (Draw's DrawLayout): `Surroundings(on:moving:carrying:held:in:)` reads the destination board in place (`editor.reading(of:)`, shown or not; nil for one `canShow` refuses), files every shape there with a solid (`ShapeContext.solidBounds(of:)`) in one `ObstacleGrid`, in paint order, each numbered by its document index, the moving shapes (`carrying`) left out on their own board and what they hold (`held`) `carries` (never in their way), and makes each problem's one free box the box's size with no `current` (put down anew, as a new shape: where it is holds it nowhere). Near X (`near`): four `LayoutProblem`s, `right`, `below`, `left`, `above`, each a `.direction` clause against X fixed at its geometry bounds (its obstacle number if it's solid), draw's default gap (`normal`, 80) and, a lone direction, centered on X the other way; the solver pushes the box past what's in its way along that side's axis only (the other is held by the centering), with draw's clearances (40 across, 64 up and down); the side whose gap from X along its axis is least wins, the earlier on a tie (`1e-6`), so the first free side, else the one that frees up nearest; a side that throws is skipped; none placed, right of X at 80, centered, over what's there; X with no finite bounds, nothing. Into frame F (`inside`): draw's `in "F"`, `.inside` F `top-left` with F the holder, 40 in (`tight`), pushed clear of what's in F and kept within it, held directly (`Holder.direct`: nothing in F is a zone of it, so a frame in F is in its way like any shape there); no room that way (it throws: `no_room`), from F's top-right, bottom-left and bottom-right in turn (`PlacingStyle.corners`, each pushed its own ways); none, at F's top-left `min(40, room / 2)` in per axis, over what's there. Onto a subboard's board (`anywhere`): the one free box with nothing to hang on, draw's spot search from the middle of the view that board would be shown with (`reading.view`, else `reading.shapes.camera.toWorld(.zero)`), ring by ring, `boardBounds` the grid's; fallback, centered there. A landing on the board shown carries the zones the solver says it sits on (`solution.zones`, by id) and, into F, F itself, to be raised above (draw's `raise`). Zones are draw's: a corral holding X at twice X's area is a region the box sits on, not in its way, so a shape moved beside a shape in a frame lands in the frame; a frame nested in F is never a zone of a shape moved to F, unlike draw's `in "F"` (where a shape landing wholly on a frame in F that holds something sits in that frame), so it goes in F itself wherever F has room from a corner; with none, over what's at F's top-left, the in-or-out rule may put it in a frame there.
- Types is a caller: `newType(named:besides:)` (`/is-a`'s New type) reads the board with `Surroundings(on:moving: size, in:)` and lands the new note with `near(owner)`, then places it with the core's `place(_:in:)` in its own step ([types](types.md)).
- Moving keeps the glue (`Landing.swift`: `Surroundings(on:moving: block, in:)`) (the block's size is the box around the frames its shapes are laid out in, so Placing is handed a size and knows no layout) and the commit (`Block.commit`): Placing only says where; changing documents is the caller's, in its own `editor.transact`.

## Testing

- PlacingTests (`// layer: test of Draw`, named for the part of Draw it tests) use CanvasTesting's boxes and corrals (`FixturesPlugin`, `CorralsPlugin`) and ask `Surroundings` directly, with no command: beside a shape (right when free, the next side when taken), the shapes carried no obstacle on their own board and what they hold carried with them, within a corral, inside a corral, anywhere, and no surroundings for a board that can't be shown or a size that isn't one. MovingTests hold the commands over it (the commits, the glide, the refusals).
