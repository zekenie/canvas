# Containers

Shapes holding shapes on one board (`ShapeContaining`: a frame): membership, paint order, moving and deleting a container, settling what's put down (the in-or-out rule), what a place takes in (admission), and putting a container down around shapes.

Related: [frames](frames.md), [topology](topology.md), [clipboard](clipboard.md), [locking](locking.md), [command line](command-line.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Containers](../../docs/plugin-system.md#containers).

## Architecture

- Containers within a board (the core's; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Containers"): a shape of a `ShapeContaining` kind (a box kind; frames, CanvasTesting's `Corral` in tests, `CorralsPlugin`, apart from `FixturesPlugin`, and its `Paddock`, `PaddocksPlugin`, hit as a frame is, only on its edge while it holds any) holds the box shapes on its board that name it (`AnyShapeRecord.container`, public read, compared in `==`, in no schema; set only in a transaction: `setContainer(_:to:)`, of one shape or a list (which rise together; one at a time, the transaction keeps where each container tops out while the stack stays as it is, so a loop costs as much), refused for a read-only shape or into a read-only container, which services hear refused, never a loop, another board or a shape without a box; or an insert of a record that comes with one, `copy(as:container:)`, taken only if it's a container on the board by then, or below it on a board going in with its owner; a plain `copy(as:)` is in none; `update` never changes one). A container that can't hold (kept aside, of a kind not installed or not containing) is kept as written, and the shape counts as on its board directly: what the core goes by is `document.container(of:containingKinds:)`, nonisolated, with `contents(of:containingKinds:)` (back to front), `hasContents`, `descendants` and `containers(of:)` (nearest first) over the document's index of who names whom (kept by every change and `apply`, costing what they return); `ShapeContext` lends `container(of:)`, `contents(of:)` and `hasContents(_:)` (a frame's hit test), `registry.containingKinds`, `isContainer(_:)` and `container(of:in:)`; `ShapeContext.containers(of:)` (nearest first) and `nests(_:_:)` (whether one of two shapes holds the other, at any depth: what Connections' `mayConnect` keeps a connection from joining). Moving a container (`translate`, a drag) moves what it holds, each once, read-only or not (the transaction's `carrying`); deleting one lets go of what it holds into its own container (the nearest that stays), read-only or not, and so does turning one into a kind that holds nothing (`transaction.turn(_:into:)`: the shape keeps its id, place and container, so a corral turned into a pocket is still in the container it was in; one turned into a kind with no box, which can't be in a container, is on the board directly; into another container kind it keeps what it holds); members paint above their container (joining raises a shape, with what it holds, just above what the container holds if it was below; restacking a container takes its contents along, keys halving between two shapes; a member goes no lower than just above its container; a step past a shape it holds does nothing, so `bringForward` and `sendBackward` step past the nearest shape a container doesn't hold). The core restacks: `bringToFront` and `sendToBack` of a shape or a list (kept in their order among themselves), `move(_:above:)`, `move(_:below:)` (and on another board, `move(_:above:on:)`, `move(_:below:on:)`), tldraw's steps `bringForward(_:)` and `sendBackward(_:)`, of a list: past the nearest overlapping shape not moving, not held, not painted by anchor (`PaintOrderAnchored`), read-only shapes staying; and `bring(_:inFrontOf:)` and `send(_:behind:)`, of a list: each of them below the top of a shape and what it holds goes just above that top (in front of a container is in front of its contents), and each above a shape goes just below it, as `move(_:above:)` (`move(_:below:)`) puts one, in their order, over the replay, while one there already, the shape itself, one it holds, one that holds it, a read-only one and, going behind, one in a container the shape isn't above stays (never below its container); Reordering is only the Arrange menu and four slash commands over them ([slash commands](slash-commands.md)), and CanvasTests' `RestackingTests` hold the list forms, the steps (a container's contents going along, an anchored kind not counted) and the two relative to a shape (their order kept, those there staying, a corral's contents going along and never below it, in front of a corral above what it holds, a frozen box staying and heard). A list form restacks each shape in turn as its form of one shape does, over a replay of the stack (`StackReplay`: each shape's neighbors and key as the run leaves them, kept apart from the stack it began with, the order of two shapes their keys'), and changes the board once at the end (`CanvasDocument.restack(_:)`, the stack made again between the lowest and the highest place a shape leaves or comes to), so a board's worth costs the board, where moving each shape in the stack in turn cost each the shapes it passed (2.5 s to bring 10,000 shapes to the front, 22 s to send every tenth back a step); a step walks the stack from each shape for what it passes, and once a walk has gone `StepSearch.walked` shapes (256, 3 in debug builds) without finding one, the shapes after find it in a grid of the bounds of every shape that may be passed (`SpatialGrid`, made then, once), so a few shapes stepped cost the shapes near them and a board's worth the board. `RestackingTests` holds the list forms to the forms of one shape in turn, keys and refusals included, over seeded boards of heaped boxes, corrals holding some (one in another), banners and frozen shapes, and BenchmarkTests' `RestackingBenchmarks` each over every shape of the generated board, every other and every tenth ([benchmarks](benchmarks.md)). What a shape put down is judged by is its laid-out frame (`context.frame(of:)`, [editor](editor.md): a `LaidOutBox` kind's box origin at the size its layout inputs say, larger or smaller; the stored box for any other kind), never its stored box: `settle(_:)` reads the frames of what it judges from one `transaction.shapes` before it changes anything (a pasted or drawn shape is laid out as the transaction leaves it), `settle(container:)` the frames of the members and of the shapes at its level, each kept as a size and put at the shape's origin as it is when its turn comes (a turn before may have moved it), and `enclose` the frames of the shapes it goes around (the padding is past them). A container's own edge is always its stored box: the registry refuses a kind that holds shapes and is a `LaidOutBox`. A shape that grows later, by a layout input changing (a type gaining a field), is not settled again: it stays where it is, in or out of its container as it was, and may overlap what is beside it or hang over an edge. That holds until its container is put down, dragged or resized, which settles its members by their laid-out frames like any others: a member whose grown part hangs over the edge is nudged in, and one laid out larger than the container leaves it (the design's section 4: moving a frame puts down everything it reaches; droppingTests and resizeSettleTests hold both). A nudge by the keys settles as the key is let go, once, and first finishes a crossing it began, the way it went, out past the edge ahead or in past the near edge, so a press at a time gets a shape across where the area rule alone would send it back each press ([selection](selection.md); nudgeTests). `transaction.settle(ids)` is the owner's in-or-out rule for shapes put down (a drop, a placement, a paste, a draw): more than half a box's area in a container puts it in (the innermost it fits in), nudged fully in if it hangs over, else out, nudged clear of what it reaches the shortest way that keeps it in its container, 16 world units from the edge (`Style.containerGap`, `Settlement.gap` for plugins: a resize keeps a shape in a container that far inside its edge); grouped by the container each came from and judged by summed area, nudged by one translation (what can't be in a container, an arrow, riding along when there's one group), each alone when a group doesn't fit; a shape over all of a container is beside it, neither in it nor across it (a frame pasted on its original isn't put in it); read-only shapes stay; a read-only container put in by the same transaction (a locked frame's pasted copy) takes nothing it lands on; a read-only container refuses, changing nothing (`Settlement.refused(by:)`, a set: that container; noted in `refused`), as a place whose judge refuses does (below); each container put down then takes in what it lands on, `settle(container:)` (also after a resize: its members hanging over its edge, and the shapes at its level it reaches, each alone, read-only ones skipped); both hand back the nudges, what's carried included (`Settlement.nudges`), for `editor.glide`. `transaction.settle(ids, on: board)` is the rule on another board of the file, against that board as the transaction leaves it (`settle(_:)` in a transaction over it, staged if it changed): what `move(_:to:)` took there is put down as a drop would put it down on the board shown, a container among it taking in what it lands on there (those shapes in `elsewhere` too); what the transaction brought there (moved, or in with its owner) isn't held, as what it adds isn't; a refusal changes nothing there, for the caller to throw; its nudges are that board's, never glided; on the transaction's own board it's `settle(_:)`, and a board the editor couldn't show settles nothing. O(the board) at a gesture's end; a drag's hint finds the containers once and costs what they are each event. `editor.place(_:around:padding:minimumSize:)` (`Enclosing.swift`, the transaction's internal `enclose`; Frames' Create Frame) puts a new container (a record of a `ShapeContaining` kind, its id new) down around shapes on the board shown, holding them: those with a box, less what's in another of them (it goes with that one); its frame theirs, and an arrow's among them bound to some of them and to nothing else (`boundShapes(in:)`, as the container channel's `held`), grown by `padding`, kept the gap inside the innermost container they're all in (which it goes in) unless what it holds is nearer that edge, then grown to `minimumSize` about its middle as far as that room allows, shifted into it, and never a far edge an ulp short of one of them (an origin plus a size rounds; `CGRect.contains` would read it hanging over, and settling would nudge it); in the stack just below the lowest of them (`move(_:below:)`), so they keep their places and what was under them all (a big note behind them) stays under it; what it lands on at its level settled (`settle(container:)`: what comes in rises above what it holds); those it took out of a container that it then took in put down again with the others from it (`settle(_:)`), so they go back in rather than lying under it, while those from a container it didn't take in (one with no room to move clear) stay in it. One step, "Add" and its kind's label (`registry.label(of:)`, unless a group it's in names it), the default tool back, `didPlace`, the nudges glided; all or nothing: a read-only one among them (locked, in a read-only container, on a read-only board) throws out of the transaction, no step, heard refused (`refuseChanges`). A shape among them being edited (only a slash command typed in its text runs this mid-edit: the menu ends editing as it opens, and ⇧⌘F waits for none) is put down after the commit, its text kept, the new container being what's selected, as Insert ends editing as it places. Nil when nothing was put down. Its sibling for a kind that owns boards, `place(_:taking:)`, reads the same shapes and container (`putDown(for:)`) and takes them onto the new shape's board ([boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md)). Without a `ShapeContaining` kind none of it runs, and on a board without containers a drop commits nothing more: the POC transcript and goldens hold. CanvasTests' container suites (ContainerTests, SettlingTests) drive drops with a fixture drag tool, since CanvasTests can't use Selection. A stretch (`StretchesPlugin`, a box laid out by the gauge it names, [editor](editor.md)) stands for a note grown by its type: SettlingTests, droppingTests and EnclosingTests hold that it is judged, hinted and enclosed by its laid-out frame and not by the box it stores, and that a gauge that grows later settles nothing. TurningTests holds `turn` and `move(_:toBoardOwnedBy:)` with corrals and pockets, MovingAcrossBoardsTests `settle(_:on:)`, and EnclosingTests `place(_:around:)` with corrals (a test kind, `Sling`, bound to two shapes and bending below them, for an arrow; `didPlace` logged by a test service): padded and just below them, one step undone and redone; the minimum about its middle; in a corral, the gap inside its edge but where what it holds is nearer; the minimum within a small corral's room, near its corner; a fractional box flush with it held whole, not nudged; a big box under them staying under it; a record naming another container going where they are; a corral with what it holds going in whole; what it lands on in, out and gliding, a frozen one staying, above it; shapes from a small corral it takes in going back into it, from a big one nudged out, and from tight corrals that can't move clear staying in it, one from a corral deeper than it too; near a corral's far corner, the gap inside its far edges and the minimum grown back toward its room; the sling counted, one bound outside not; nothing to hold; and a frozen box, one in a frozen corral, and a frozen pocket's board refusing.
- What a place takes in (the core's, generic: it knows no rule; `Admission.swift`): a place is `ShapePlace`, `.container(id)` (directly in it) or `.board(id)` (a board's top level, in no container), and `registrar.admission { admission in … }` judges shapes coming into one from somewhere else, a list in plugin order (`Registry.admissionRules`), the first refusal refusing them all. The ask (`Admission`) holds each `Arrival` (the record as it would be put, its `container` the place's; `from`, where it was when the transaction began, on whichever board it was then (`Transaction.origin(of:)`, read from the file the editor made the transaction over, `fileBefore`, which a transaction over another board inherits), never where the transaction has had it on the way, nil for a shape new to the file and for one put back into it from where nothing says; `isNew`, made here (inserted by this transaction and on no board of the file before it, or a placement's put down earlier in the press that settles it, `Transaction.placing`) rather than moved, the shapes a caller says come back under their ids (`transaction.treatAsMoved(_:)`, said before the insert: Clipboard's for a cut's paste) moved, from nil; `to`; and `carried`, what it holds on its board at any depth, which comes into nothing new itself but into whatever its holder comes into: a rule reaching anywhere inside reads them, and what a board it owns holds through `context.reading`), a `ShapeContext` over the file as the transaction leaves it before they come in (the editor's committed one for a try), and `isChange` (false for a drag's hint and `editor.admits`, so a judge says only real refusals; `editor.admits(_:on:into:)` asks it of shapes on any board of the file, for a place on any board, which Moving's `Drop` does, so the table of contents, `/move-to` and Move To ▸ offer no place that would refuse the shapes). The verdict admits, or `.refuses(shaking:saying:)`: the shapes that shake (the holder of the rule; none says the arrivals themselves, never an empty refusal) and the words VoiceOver hears with the refusal (`editor.announce`, once each, by the transaction's `refusalNotes`, through `transact`'s refusals and `transactWhole`'s, unless its caller names what shakes; never for a try or a silent refusal). Asked only for box shapes coming in from somewhere else (`Admission.arrivals`, by `Transaction.whence(of:)`): one moved, going anywhere but where it was when the transaction began (so a shape that only passes through a place on the way back to its own is never asked about, and a drag within a container asks nothing); one new here, onto a board's top level or into a container it isn't already in (the insert that put it in one asked), never for what a deletion lets go of into the container above (`release`), for a turn, for an undo or a redo, for shapes going in on a board with their owner (`insert(_:contents:)`'s), and not at all with no judge (`registry.judgesAdmission`: the transcript and goldens hold, a board without one costs nothing more). Where: `settle(_:)` (every plan's members together, after the read-only check: `.refused(by:)` the shapes named, nothing changed; `Settlement.refused` holds a set, a read-only container's one id), `settle(container:)` (each shape alone, refused silently: it stays as it is, as one a read-only container would take does), `setContainer` (all or nothing for the list, heard refused), an insert that comes with a container (refused, it's on the board directly and heard, as into a read-only container; one put in with a container this transaction put in too, a paste's, comes with it and isn't asked: the container is judged where it's put down, carrying it; and one an insert puts back moved, `treatAsMoved` or deleted earlier in the transaction, that no settle judges after, is judged again where it is as the transaction ends, `crossing` and `judgeCrossings`, carrying what was put in it since, so a container inserted before what it holds is never let in on an ask that carried nothing), `place(_:around:)` (`enclose`: what it holds goes into the new container unasked, `Transaction.holdsAdmission`, since some only pass through it on the way back into the container they left once it takes that in; then what it holds directly is asked about together, each from where it began, with what it carries, all or nothing; what it lands on beside them is asked about, alone and silently, as it's taken in), `move(_:to:)` (asks nothing itself: the moving roots, what no other moving shape holds, are judged where they're put down on the destination board, by `settle(_:on:)` there, from where they began, so a rule on that board's top level never judges one that lands in a container there; one put down nowhere is judged where it is as the transaction ends, `Transaction.crossing` and `judgeCrossings`, by `transact`, `transact(on:)` and `transactWhole`, a refusal then committing nothing, since a move can't go back alone), and the drag's hint (`TranslatingSession`: no outline over a container that would refuse what's dragged, `editor.admits(_:into:)` asked as a try once each time the container under it changes, its placed shapes taken as new). The shipped judge is Constraints' (a move into a constraint's reach refused when its standing query answers no, a cut's paste by what was answered as it left: [constraints](constraints.md)). A refusal works as a read-only container's: a drop glides back with no step, a key's nudges go back (Selection's `settleNudge`), a placement is taken away, and an `editor.transactWhole` caller (paste, Moving, Clipboard, Draw, `place(_:around:)`) commits nothing, what there was to redo kept. A caller that reports a refusal in words of its own reads `refusalNotes` (public, read-only): Draw tells a lock from a place's refusal (`cannot_contain`, "isn't taken where it would go", the place's words after it, never naming the drawn shape itself). AdmissionTests hold it with a judge that refuses discs moved into the corrals it names and onto the boards of the pockets it names (letting new shapes in): a drop refused (back, gliding from where it was dropped, no step, the corral heard, its words announced, none for the hint), a box and a disc refused whole, the hint gone over a refusing corral and kept over another (tries, asked once a target, then the release as a change), a drop out of a corral onto the board allowed, `setContainer` refused (nothing committed, redo kept), a move onto a refusing pocket's board refused (the disc itself shaking, redo kept) and one onto another done, a corral moved and dropped with what it carries (let in, then refused by a rule reaching inside; moved onto a board and put down nowhere, judged as the transaction ends, a refusal committing nothing), a move onto a board settled into a corral there (asked once, from where it was, the board's refusing top level taking no part) and one settled on that top level refused, Create Frame around a disc in a refusing corral and a box beside (the corral taken in, the disc carried back into it and never asked about; a new container refusing discs anywhere inside refuses it whole), a shape put back under its id said to be moved (from nil, not new, refused, its insert's own ask too) and the same not said (new, let in), a corral put back moved into a deep-refusing one before the disc it holds, with no settle, judged carrying it as the transaction ends (refused whole; with a settle after, asked no more), a corral resized over a refused disc leaving it out silently, a shape staying where it is never asked, new shapes asked with no place they leave, and none asked with no judge.
- Keys step through containers (Selection's, [selection](selection.md#moving-between-shapes); a container is `registry.isContainer`, `registry.container(of:in:)` says what holds a shape): Tab and ⌥⌘ with an arrow go among the shapes in the container all the selected shapes are in (else the board's own), never leaving it, ⌘↓ or Return on a container that neither opens (`ShapeOpening`) nor edits (`Editable`), a frame, selects the first stop in it in reading order, and ⌘↑ ([boards](boards.md#stepping-out-then-up)) selects the container all the selected shapes are in, naming the Go item for its kind (`registry.label(of:)`: Enclosing Frame). They name no kind, and CanvasTesting's `Corral` (`CorralsPlugin`) holds them in SelectionTests and BoardsTests.
