# Containers

Shapes holding shapes on one board (`ShapeContaining`: a frame): membership, paint order, moving and deleting a container, settling what's put down (the in-or-out rule), and putting a container down around shapes.

Related: [frames](frames.md), [topology](topology.md), [clipboard](clipboard.md), [locking](locking.md), [command line](command-line.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Containers](../../docs/plugin-system.md#containers).

## Architecture

- Containers within a board (the core's; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Containers"): a shape of a `ShapeContaining` kind (a box kind; frames, CanvasTesting's `Corral` in tests, `CorralsPlugin`, apart from `FixturesPlugin`) holds the box shapes on its board that name it (`AnyShapeRecord.container`, public read, compared in `==`, in no schema; set only in a transaction: `setContainer(_:to:)`, of one shape or a list (which rise together; one at a time, the transaction keeps where each container tops out while the stack stays as it is, so a loop costs as much), refused for a read-only shape or into a read-only container, which services hear refused, never a loop, another board or a shape without a box; or an insert of a record that comes with one, `copy(as:container:)`, taken only if it's a container on the board by then, or below it on a board going in with its owner; a plain `copy(as:)` is in none; `update` never changes one). A container that can't hold (kept aside, of a kind not installed or not containing) is kept as written, and the shape counts as on its board directly: what the core goes by is `document.container(of:containingKinds:)`, nonisolated, with `contents(of:containingKinds:)` (back to front), `hasContents`, `descendants` and `containers(of:)` (nearest first) over the document's index of who names whom (kept by every change and `apply`, costing what they return); `ShapeContext` lends `container(of:)`, `contents(of:)` and `hasContents(_:)` (a frame's hit test), `registry.containingKinds`, `isContainer(_:)` and `container(of:in:)`. Moving a container (`translate`, a drag) moves what it holds, each once, read-only or not (the transaction's `carrying`); deleting one lets go of what it holds into its own container (the nearest that stays), read-only or not, and so does turning one into a kind that holds nothing (`transaction.turn(_:into:)`: the shape keeps its id, place and container, so a corral turned into a pocket is still in the container it was in; one turned into a kind with no box, which can't be in a container, is on the board directly; into another container kind it keeps what it holds); members paint above their container (joining raises a shape, with what it holds, just above what the container holds if it was below; restacking a container takes its contents along, keys halving between two shapes; a member goes no lower than just above its container; a step past a shape it holds does nothing, so Reordering's Bring Forward and Send Backward step past the nearest shape a container doesn't hold). What a shape put down is judged by is its laid-out frame (`context.frame(of:)`, [editor](editor.md): a `LaidOutBox` kind's box grown to what its layout inputs say; the stored box for any other kind), never its stored box: `settle(_:)` reads the frames of what it judges from one `transaction.shapes` before it changes anything (a pasted or drawn shape is laid out as the transaction leaves it), `settle(container:)` the frames of the members and of the shapes at its level, each kept as a size and put at the shape's origin as it is when its turn comes (a turn before may have moved it), and `enclose` the frames of the shapes it goes around (the padding is past them). A container's own edge is always its stored box: the registry refuses a kind that holds shapes and is a `LaidOutBox`. A shape that grows later, by a layout input changing (a type gaining a field), is not settled again: it stays where it is, in or out of its container as it was, and may overlap what is beside it or hang over an edge. That holds until its container is put down, dragged or resized, which settles its members by their laid-out frames like any others: a member whose grown part hangs over the edge is nudged in, and one laid out larger than the container leaves it (the design's section 4: moving a frame puts down everything it reaches; droppingTests and resizeSettleTests hold both). `transaction.settle(ids)` is the owner's in-or-out rule for shapes put down (a drop, a placement, a paste, a draw): more than half a box's area in a container puts it in (the innermost it fits in), nudged fully in if it hangs over, else out, nudged clear of what it reaches the shortest way that keeps it in its container, 16 world units from the edge (`Style.containerGap`, `Settlement.gap` for plugins: a resize keeps a shape in a container that far inside its edge); grouped by the container each came from and judged by summed area, nudged by one translation (what can't be in a container, an arrow, riding along when there's one group), each alone when a group doesn't fit; a shape over all of a container is beside it, neither in it nor across it (a frame pasted on its original isn't put in it); read-only shapes stay; a read-only container put in by the same transaction (a locked frame's pasted copy) takes nothing it lands on; a read-only container refuses, changing nothing (`Settlement.refused(by:)`, noted in `refused`); each container put down then takes in what it lands on, `settle(container:)` (also after a resize: its members hanging over its edge, and the shapes at its level it reaches, each alone, read-only ones skipped); both hand back the nudges, what's carried included (`Settlement.nudges`), for `editor.glide`. `transaction.settle(ids, on: board)` is the rule on another board of the file, against that board as the transaction leaves it (`settle(_:)` in a transaction over it, staged if it changed): what `move(_:to:)` took there is put down as a drop would put it down on the board shown, a container among it taking in what it lands on there (those shapes in `elsewhere` too); what the transaction brought there (moved, or in with its owner) isn't held, as what it adds isn't; a refusal changes nothing there, for the caller to throw; its nudges are that board's, never glided; on the transaction's own board it's `settle(_:)`, and a board the editor couldn't show settles nothing. O(the board) at a gesture's end; a drag's hint finds the containers once and costs what they are each event. `editor.place(_:around:padding:minimumSize:)` (`Enclosing.swift`, the transaction's internal `enclose`; Frames' Create Frame) puts a new container (a record of a `ShapeContaining` kind, its id new) down around shapes on the board shown, holding them: those with a box, less what's in another of them (it goes with that one); its frame theirs, and an arrow's among them bound to some of them and to nothing else (`boundShapes(in:)`, as the container channel's `held`), grown by `padding`, kept the gap inside the innermost container they're all in (which it goes in) unless what it holds is nearer that edge, then grown to `minimumSize` about its middle as far as that room allows, shifted into it, and never a far edge an ulp short of one of them (an origin plus a size rounds; `CGRect.contains` would read it hanging over, and settling would nudge it); in the stack just below the lowest of them (`move(_:below:)`), so they keep their places and what was under them all (a big note behind them) stays under it; what it lands on at its level settled (`settle(container:)`: what comes in rises above what it holds); those it took out of a container that it then took in put down again with the others from it (`settle(_:)`), so they go back in rather than lying under it, while those from a container it didn't take in (one with no room to move clear) stay in it. One step, "Add" and its kind's label (`registry.label(of:)`, unless a group it's in names it), the default tool back, `didPlace`, the nudges glided; all or nothing: a read-only one among them (locked, in a read-only container, on a read-only board) throws out of the transaction, no step, heard refused (`refuseChanges`). Nil when nothing was put down. Without a `ShapeContaining` kind none of it runs, and on a board without containers a drop commits nothing more: the POC transcript and goldens hold. CanvasTests' container suites (ContainerTests, SettlingTests) drive drops with a fixture drag tool, since CanvasTests can't use Selection. A stretch (`StretchesPlugin`, a box laid out by the gauge it names, [editor](editor.md)) stands for a note grown by its type: SettlingTests, droppingTests and EnclosingTests hold that it is judged, hinted and enclosed by its laid-out frame and not by the box it stores, and that a gauge that grows later settles nothing. TurningTests holds `turn` and `move(_:toBoardOwnedBy:)` with corrals and pockets, MovingAcrossBoardsTests `settle(_:on:)`, and EnclosingTests `place(_:around:)` with corrals (a test kind, `Sling`, bound to two shapes and bending below them, for an arrow; `didPlace` logged by a test service): padded and just below them, one step undone and redone; the minimum about its middle; in a corral, the gap inside its edge but where what it holds is nearer; the minimum within a small corral's room, near its corner; a fractional box flush with it held whole, not nudged; a big box under them staying under it; a record naming another container going where they are; a corral with what it holds going in whole; what it lands on in, out and gliding, a frozen one staying, above it; shapes from a small corral it takes in going back into it, from a big one nudged out, and from tight corrals that can't move clear staying in it, one from a corral deeper than it too; near a corral's far corner, the gap inside its far edges and the minimum grown back toward its room; the sling counted, one bound outside not; nothing to hold; and a frozen box, one in a frozen corral, and a frozen pocket's board refusing.
