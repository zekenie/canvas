# Containers

Shapes holding shapes on one board (`ShapeContaining`: a frame): membership, paint order, moving and deleting a container, and settling what's put down (the in-or-out rule).

Related: [frames](frames.md), [topology](topology.md), [clipboard](clipboard.md), [locking](locking.md), [command line](command-line.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Containers](../../docs/plugin-system.md#containers).

## Architecture

- Containers within a board (the core's; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Containers"): a shape of a `ShapeContaining` kind (a box kind; frames, CanvasTesting's `Corral` in tests, `CorralsPlugin`, apart from `FixturesPlugin`) holds the box shapes on its board that name it (`AnyShapeRecord.container`, public read, compared in `==`, in no schema; set only in a transaction: `setContainer(_:to:)`, of one shape or a list (which rise together; one at a time, the transaction keeps where each container tops out while the stack stays as it is, so a loop costs as much), refused for a read-only shape or into a read-only container, which services hear refused, never a loop, another board or a shape without a box; or an insert of a record that comes with one, `copy(as:container:)`, taken only if it's a container on the board by then, or below it on a board going in with its owner; a plain `copy(as:)` is in none; `update` never changes one). A container that can't hold (kept aside, of a kind not installed or not containing) is kept as written, and the shape counts as on its board directly: what the core goes by is `document.container(of:containingKinds:)`, nonisolated, with `contents(of:containingKinds:)` (back to front), `hasContents`, `descendants` and `containers(of:)` (nearest first) over the document's index of who names whom (kept by every change and `apply`, costing what they return); `ShapeContext` lends `container(of:)`, `contents(of:)` and `hasContents(_:)` (a frame's hit test), `registry.containingKinds`, `isContainer(_:)` and `container(of:in:)`. Moving a container (`translate`, a drag) moves what it holds, each once, read-only or not (the transaction's `carrying`); deleting one lets go of what it holds into its own container (the nearest that stays), read-only or not, and so does turning one into a kind that holds nothing (`transaction.turn(_:into:)`: the shape keeps its id, place and container, so a corral turned into a pocket is still in the container it was in; one turned into a kind with no box, which can't be in a container, is on the board directly; into another container kind it keeps what it holds); members paint above their container (joining raises a shape, with what it holds, just above what the container holds if it was below; restacking a container takes its contents along, keys halving between two shapes; a member goes no lower than just above its container; a step past a shape it holds does nothing, so Reordering's Bring Forward and Send Backward step past the nearest shape a container doesn't hold). `transaction.settle(ids)` is the owner's in-or-out rule for shapes put down (a drop, a placement, a paste, a draw): more than half a box's area in a container puts it in (the innermost it fits in), nudged fully in if it hangs over, else out, nudged clear of what it reaches the shortest way that keeps it in its container, 16 world units from the edge (`Style.containerGap`, `Settlement.gap` for plugins: a resize keeps a shape in a container that far inside its edge); grouped by the container each came from and judged by summed area, nudged by one translation (what can't be in a container, an arrow, riding along when there's one group), each alone when a group doesn't fit; a shape over all of a container is beside it, neither in it nor across it (a frame pasted on its original isn't put in it); read-only shapes stay; a read-only container put in by the same transaction (a locked frame's pasted copy) takes nothing it lands on; a read-only container refuses, changing nothing (`Settlement.refused(by:)`, noted in `refused`); each container put down then takes in what it lands on, `settle(container:)` (also after a resize: its members hanging over its edge, and the shapes at its level it reaches, each alone, read-only ones skipped); both hand back the nudges, what's carried included (`Settlement.nudges`), for `editor.glide`. O(the board) at a gesture's end; a drag's hint finds the containers once and costs what they are each event. Without a `ShapeContaining` kind none of it runs, and on a board without containers a drop commits nothing more: the POC transcript and goldens hold. CanvasTests' container suites (ContainerTests, SettlingTests) drive drops with a fixture drag tool, since CanvasTests can't use Selection. TurningTests holds `turn` and `move(_:toBoardOwnedBy:)` with corrals and pockets.
