# Containers

Shapes holding shapes on one board (`ShapeContaining`: a frame, the engineering kit's database): membership, paint order, moving and deleting a container, settling what's put down (the in-or-out rule), making room below a shape that grows (the push), and putting a container down around shapes.

Related: [frames](frames.md), [topology](topology.md), [clipboard](clipboard.md), [locking](locking.md), [command line](command-line.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Containers](../../docs/plugin-system.md#containers).

## Architecture

- Containers within a board (the core's; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Containers"): a shape of a `ShapeContaining` kind (a box kind; frames, CanvasTesting's `Corral` in tests, `CorralsPlugin`, apart from `FixturesPlugin`, and its `Paddock`, `PaddocksPlugin`, hit as a frame is, only on its edge while it holds any) holds the box shapes on its board that name it (`AnyShapeRecord.container`, public read, compared in `==`, in no schema; set only in a transaction: `setContainer(_:to:)`, of one shape or a list (which rise together; one at a time, the transaction keeps where each container tops out while the stack stays as it is, so a loop costs as much), refused for a read-only shape or into a read-only container, which services hear refused, never a loop, another board or a shape without a box; or an insert of a record that comes with one, `copy(as:container:)`, taken only if it's a container on the board by then, or below it on a board going in with its owner; a plain `copy(as:)` is in none; `update` never changes one). A container that can't hold (kept aside, of a kind not installed or not containing) is kept as written, and the shape counts as on its board directly: what the core goes by is `document.container(of:containingKinds:)`, nonisolated, with `contents(of:containingKinds:)` (back to front), `hasContents`, `descendants` and `containers(of:)` (nearest first) over the document's index of who names whom (kept by every change and `apply`, costing what they return); `ShapeContext` lends `container(of:)`, `contents(of:)` and `hasContents(_:)` (a frame's hit test), `registry.containingKinds`, `isContainer(_:)` and `container(of:in:)`; `ShapeContext.containers(of:)` (nearest first) and `nests(_:_:)` (whether one of two shapes holds the other, at any depth: what Connections' `mayConnect` keeps a connection from joining). Moving a container (`translate`, a drag) moves what it holds, each once, read-only or not (the transaction's `carrying`); deleting one lets go of what it holds into its own container (the nearest that stays), read-only or not, and so does turning one into a kind that holds nothing (`transaction.turn(_:into:)`: the shape keeps its id, place and container, so a corral turned into a pocket is still in the container it was in; one turned into a kind with no box, which can't be in a container, is on the board directly; into another container kind it keeps what it holds); members paint above their container (joining raises a shape, with what it holds, just above what the container holds if it was below; restacking a container takes its contents along, keys halving between two shapes; a member goes no lower than just above its container; a step past a shape it holds does nothing, so `bringForward` and `sendBackward` step past the nearest shape a container doesn't hold). The core restacks: `bringToFront` and `sendToBack` of a shape or a list (kept in their order among themselves), `move(_:above:)`, `move(_:below:)` (and on another board, `move(_:above:on:)`, `move(_:below:on:)`), tldraw's steps `bringForward(_:)` and `sendBackward(_:)`, of a list: past the nearest overlapping shape not moving, not held, not painted by anchor (`PaintOrderAnchored`), read-only shapes staying; and `bring(_:inFrontOf:)` and `send(_:behind:)`, of a list: each of them below the top of a shape and what it holds goes just above that top (in front of a container is in front of its contents), and each above a shape goes just below it, as `move(_:above:)` (`move(_:below:)`) puts one, in their order, over the replay, while one there already, the shape itself, one it holds, one that holds it, a read-only one and, going behind, one in a container the shape isn't above stays (never below its container); Reordering is only the Arrange menu and four slash commands over them ([slash commands](slash-commands.md)), and CanvasTests' `RestackingTests` hold the list forms, the steps (a container's contents going along, an anchored kind not counted) and the two relative to a shape (their order kept, those there staying, a corral's contents going along and never below it, in front of a corral above what it holds, a frozen box staying and heard). A list form restacks each shape in turn as its form of one shape does, over a replay of the stack (`StackReplay`: each shape's neighbors and key as the run leaves them, kept apart from the stack it began with, the order of two shapes their keys'), and changes the board once at the end (`CanvasDocument.restack(_:)`, the stack made again between the lowest and the highest place a shape leaves or comes to), so a board's worth costs the board, where moving each shape in the stack in turn cost each the shapes it passed (2.5 s to bring 10,000 shapes to the front, 22 s to send every tenth back a step); a step walks the stack from each shape for what it passes, and once a walk has gone `StepSearch.walked` shapes (256, 3 in debug builds) without finding one, the shapes after find it in a grid of the bounds of every shape that may be passed (`SpatialGrid`, made then, once), so a few shapes stepped cost the shapes near them and a board's worth the board. `RestackingTests` holds the list forms to the forms of one shape in turn, keys and refusals included, over seeded boards of heaped boxes, corrals holding some (one in another), banners and frozen shapes, and BenchmarkTests' `RestackingBenchmarks` each over every shape of the generated board, every other and every tenth ([benchmarks](benchmarks.md)). What a shape put down is judged by is its laid-out frame (`context.frame(of:)`, [editor](editor.md): a `LaidOutBox` kind's box origin at the size its layout inputs say, larger or smaller; the stored box for any other kind), never its stored box: `settle(_:)` reads the frames of what it judges from one `transaction.shapes` before it changes anything (a pasted or drawn shape is laid out as the transaction leaves it), `settle(container:)` the frames of the members and of the shapes at its level, each kept as a size and put at the shape's origin as it is when its turn comes (a turn before may have moved it), and `enclose` the frames of the shapes it goes around (the padding is past them). A container's own edge is always its stored box: the registry refuses a kind that holds shapes and is a `LaidOutBox`. A shape that grows later, by a layout input changing (a type gaining a field), is not settled again (a kind that makes room as it grows pushes what's below it instead, below): it stays where it is, in or out of its container as it was, and may overlap what is beside it or hang over an edge. That holds until its container is put down, dragged or resized, which settles its members by their laid-out frames like any others: a member whose grown part hangs over the edge is nudged in, and one laid out larger than the container leaves it (the design's section 4: moving a frame puts down everything it reaches; droppingTests and resizeSettleTests hold both). A nudge by the keys settles as the key is let go, once, and first finishes a crossing it began, the way it went, out past the edge ahead or in past the near edge, so a press at a time gets a shape across where the area rule alone would send it back each press ([selection](selection.md); nudgeTests). `transaction.settle(ids)` is the owner's in-or-out rule for shapes put down (a drop, a placement, a paste, a draw): more than half a box's area in a container puts it in (the innermost it fits in), nudged fully in if it hangs over, else out, nudged clear of what it reaches the shortest way that keeps it in its container, 16 world units from the edge (`Style.containerGap`, `Settlement.gap` for plugins: a resize keeps a shape in a container that far inside its edge); grouped by the container each came from and judged by summed area, nudged by one translation (what can't be in a container, an arrow, riding along when there's one group), each alone when a group doesn't fit; a shape over all of a container is beside it, neither in it nor across it (a frame pasted on its original isn't put in it); read-only shapes stay; a read-only container put in by the same transaction (a locked frame's pasted copy) takes nothing it lands on; a read-only container refuses, changing nothing (`Settlement.refused(by:)`, noted in `refused`); each container put down then takes in what it lands on, `settle(container:)` (also after a resize: its members hanging over its edge, and the shapes at its level it reaches, each alone, read-only ones skipped); both hand back the nudges, what's carried included (`Settlement.nudges`), for `editor.glide`. `transaction.settle(ids, on: board)` is the rule on another board of the file, against that board as the transaction leaves it (`settle(_:)` in a transaction over it, staged if it changed): what `move(_:to:)` took there is put down as a drop would put it down on the board shown, a container among it taking in what it lands on there (those shapes in `elsewhere` too); what the transaction brought there (moved, or in with its owner) isn't held, as what it adds isn't; a refusal changes nothing there, for the caller to throw; its nudges are that board's, never glided; on the transaction's own board it's `settle(_:)`, and a board the editor couldn't show settles nothing. O(the board) at a gesture's end; a drag's hint finds the containers once and costs what they are each event. `editor.place(_:around:padding:minimumSize:)` (`Enclosing.swift`, the transaction's internal `enclose`; Frames' Create Frame) puts a new container (a record of a `ShapeContaining` kind, its id new) down around shapes on the board shown, holding them: those with a box, less what's in another of them (it goes with that one); its frame theirs, and an arrow's among them bound to some of them and to nothing else (`boundShapes(in:)`, as the container channel's `held`), grown by `padding`, kept the gap inside the innermost container they're all in (which it goes in) unless what it holds is nearer that edge, then grown to `minimumSize` about its middle as far as that room allows, shifted into it, and never a far edge an ulp short of one of them (an origin plus a size rounds; `CGRect.contains` would read it hanging over, and settling would nudge it); in the stack just below the lowest of them (`move(_:below:)`), so they keep their places and what was under them all (a big note behind them) stays under it; what it lands on at its level settled (`settle(container:)`: what comes in rises above what it holds); those it took out of a container that it then took in put down again with the others from it (`settle(_:)`), so they go back in rather than lying under it, while those from a container it didn't take in (one with no room to move clear) stay in it. One step, "Add" and its kind's label (`registry.label(of:)`, unless a group it's in names it), the default tool back, `didPlace`, the nudges glided; all or nothing: a read-only one among them (locked, in a read-only container, on a read-only board) throws out of the transaction, no step, heard refused (`refuseChanges`). Nil when nothing was put down. Without a `ShapeContaining` kind none of it runs, and on a board without containers a drop commits nothing more: the POC transcript and goldens hold. CanvasTests' container suites (ContainerTests, SettlingTests) drive drops with a fixture drag tool, since CanvasTests can't use Selection. A stretch (`StretchesPlugin`, a box laid out by the gauge it names, [editor](editor.md)) stands for a note grown by its type: SettlingTests, droppingTests and EnclosingTests hold that it is judged, hinted and enclosed by its laid-out frame and not by the box it stores, and that a gauge that grows later settles nothing. TurningTests holds `turn` and `move(_:toBoardOwnedBy:)` with corrals and pockets, MovingAcrossBoardsTests `settle(_:on:)`, and EnclosingTests `place(_:around:)` with corrals (a test kind, `Sling`, bound to two shapes and bending below them, for an arrow; `didPlace` logged by a test service): padded and just below them, one step undone and redone; the minimum about its middle; in a corral, the gap inside its edge but where what it holds is nearer; the minimum within a small corral's room, near its corner; a fractional box flush with it held whole, not nudged; a big box under them staying under it; a record naming another container going where they are; a corral with what it holds going in whole; what it lands on in, out and gliding, a frozen one staying, above it; shapes from a small corral it takes in going back into it, from a big one nudged out, and from tight corrals that can't move clear staying in it, one from a corral deeper than it too; near a corral's far corner, the gap inside its far edges and the minimum grown back toward its room; the sling counted, one bound outside not; nothing to hold; and a frozen box, one in a frozen corral, and a frozen pocket's board refusing.
- Making room below a shape that grows (`MakingRoom.swift`, U3: the first UX walk's tables growing over the table below, the owner's "a best effort, and with complex layouts the person takes over"): the one case where a shape that grows later moves what's beside it. A kind opts in by adopting `MakesRoomAsItGrows` (no requirements; `registry.roomMakingKinds`): the kit's table, enum and API, LineRowsTests' `Tally`, CanvasTesting's `Pile` (`PilesPlugin`, apart from `FixturesPlugin`: a box `Pile.layer`, 20, tall a layer, and a set layout input, `Piles.waiting`, a layer more, as a waiting line). `transaction.makeRoom()` finds the shapes of those kinds the transaction changed (not only moved, not added) whose frame at rest reaches lower than it did when the transaction began (or when `makeRoom` last measured it: `roomBaselines`, so each growth makes room once), a move of its box aside; at rest is laid out in a context whose source counts every shape changed (`restingShapes(over:reading:)`: no value a plugin set, `setLayoutInput`, and none of the editor's kept sizes, so the waiting line, the focused row and a drag's fade push nothing; and a provider answers as at rest, `LayoutReading.isResting`: LineRows' width while typing answers nothing, and RowFolds folds the shape being edited, which it peels back only in the editing view, so a row committed into a folded table pushes by what its fold gains and not by the every row it shows while edited; a fold is the same before and after). Then level by level, innermost first: the level's members (a container's contents, or the shapes on the board in none) with a box, a frame read once for each (the transaction's `shapes`, a grown one's from its growth); a worklist top to bottom pushes each member whose top was at or under a pusher's old bottom, whose top is nearer its new bottom than `Style.roomReach` (twice `Style.containerGap`, 32 world units, whatever the zoom: it clears a title's row, 27 view points, at 100% down to 84%, without the core knowing titles; plain overlap left the walk's pill across the rows; at 50% a title's row is 54 world units and a gap the person left under that is theirs) and that overlaps it across (the pusher's widest, before and after, so a table that grew wider as well pushes what is under its new width), by the pusher's bottom delta (the larger, if two reach it). The members come, in a container or on a board with no index kept, from one read of the level sorted by top and found by halves; on the board shown with the editor's index kept (`LayoutSource.nearby`, `ShapeIndex.ids(touching:)`, never building the paint list for it) from the band a pusher reaches, the index's grid finding the shapes there beside those the step wrote, so a commit costs what it reaches and never the board. A member read-only (`holds`) stays and pushes nothing, which stops its column (asked only of those reached); a shape the step put in or moved is pushed as any is when a growth reaches where it put it, so a pushed shape never lands on one the step put down (a draw's new table), but it never grows by that put (`placedHere`: added, or its box origin not where it began less what `makeRoom` moved it and the containers that carried it, `roomShift(of:)`: it is where the step meant it); at most `Style.roomMoves` (200) moved or grown in a step, from the top. The pushed are translated (`translate`: a container carries what it holds; arrows bound to them follow). The level's container, unless read-only, grows (its stored box's height) to hold the gap under every member that went down from inside it, and is a pusher in its own level. It returns what it moved, by how far, and adds it to `madeRoom` (public; what `canvas draw`'s report reads). `editor.transact` and `transact(on:)` run it after the body (not while a file is put in place), `take(_:)` runs it for a step over another board (what the step moved or settled there is `placedElsewhere`, where it meant it, as on the board shown; what it pushed there is in `madeRoom` too), and a body may run it first. `Transaction.makesRoom` turns it off for a body that puts every shape where it goes at the size it will have: `canvas apply` and the digest (`DrawOptions.makesRoom`), never a draw or a paste. Only down, only by the growth, never sideways; shrinking pulls nothing up; a fold, an unfold and an editing view's waiting line write nothing, so push nothing. O(what it reaches) on the board shown, O(the level) in a container, a commit's cost only where something that makes room grew. CanvasTests' `makingRoomTests` hold it with piles, boxes and corrals: the push and the gap kept, one step named as the commit, what's beside and beyond reach staying, the gap closing until the reach, the cascade, Undo exact in the board codec's bytes and Redo, two growths and the larger, shrinking, a set input not counted, a corral growing and pushing on the board, another level untouched, a frozen box stopping its column, what the step put or moved pushed as any is and never run into, a shape the step moved and grew pushing nothing, a pile that grew wider pushing what is under its new width, the same pushes with the editor's index kept and without, a carried member grown later making room, the same at any zoom, a push on another board in the step (one Undo) and a pile moved there first pushing nothing, a container made read-only in the step not growing, `makeRoom` asked twice and `madeRoom`, the cap from the top, and a kind that doesn't opt in. BenchmarkTests' `roomPush` ([benchmarks](benchmarks.md)).
- Keys step through containers (Selection's, [selection](selection.md#moving-between-shapes); a container is `registry.isContainer`, `registry.container(of:in:)` says what holds a shape): Tab and ⌥⌘ with an arrow go among the shapes in the container all the selected shapes are in (else the board's own), never leaving it, ⌘↓ or Return on a container that neither opens (`ShapeOpening`) nor edits (`Editable`), a frame, selects the first stop in it in reading order, and ⌘↑ ([boards](boards.md#stepping-out-then-up)) selects the container all the selected shapes are in, naming the Go item for its kind (`registry.label(of:)`: Enclosing Frame). They name no kind, and CanvasTesting's `Corral` (`CorralsPlugin`) holds them in SelectionTests and BoardsTests.
