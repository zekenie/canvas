# Boards

Getting around a file's boards: opening a board, Enclosing Board, a board's name, the path's crumbs and their menus.

Related: [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [subboards](subboards.md), [selection](selection.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [titles](titles.md), [keyboard](keyboard.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

## Architecture

- Boards (a concern, `docs/plugin-system.md` "Boards"): getting around a file's boards, whatever kind owns them. A `BoardOwning` kind opts in with `x.opensBoard(placeholder:systemImage:)` (`registrar.opensBoard(P.self, …)` for another plugin's kind; the `BoardKinds` point, one per kind, whose `check` refuses a kind that isn't `BoardOwning`, or every kind), which brings Selection's opener (`editor.showBoard(BoardID(owner:))`: double-click, Return, ⌘↓; from its row, Return once it's picked, which in the app's panel is the canvas's Return, as a double-click on a row doesn't reach the list there), Open first in its context menu (`BoardsMenuGroup`, order 0; with several selected, the first) and a Board tab (`BoardTab`, order 50) with `DetailCommandButton("Open", command: .openSelection)`; so a list with it needs Selection, ContextMenus and DetailPanel. `BoardsPlugin` (id `board-navigation`: `boards` is the file's and the command line's word) claims `.enclosingBoard` on ⌘↑ (off the root, with no session and nothing being edited) running `editor.goToEnclosingBoard()`, which is `editor.goUp(to: parent)`: any board above on `boardPath`, selecting there the owner of the board below it on the path, one step back (`showBoard(_:selecting:)`). `editor.name(of:root:)` is a board's name (its owner's title, else its kind's placeholder, `registry.boardPlaceholder(of:)`, which a query's rows read too through QueryLexicon's `BoardQueriesPlugin`, else its kind's label; `root` for the root) and `symbol(of:)` its symbol. Shipped after Titles; in the app, the table of contents' header is the board shown's crumb (`ShippedPlugins.contentsHeading(for:rootName:)`: name, symbol, `up: .enclosingBoard`, called Enclosing Board with its key, "Enclosing Board (⌘↑)", from Keyboard's `registry.help(_:forCommand:)`; on the root, the file's name, as the window and the path's root crumb have it, else "Board"; the panel's Back is "Back (⌘[)", as Go › Back), the window's subtitle is the board shown's name off the root, empty on it (`BoardConfiguration.boardName`, which the app fills with `ShippedPlugins.boardName(of:)`, the last crumb's title; `BoardWindowController` reads it observed, so it follows a switch or a rename), and the Go menu has Back ⌘[, Enclosing Board ⌘↑ and Open Selection ⌘↓ (`BoardWindowController`'s `enclosingBoard:` and `openSelection:`, in its commands table), dimmed while text has the keyboard (`window.firstResponder is NSText`), where the menu bar (`MenuBar`, CanvasUI) leaves a disabled item's arrow key to the text, so it moves the caret (AppKit's menus would take it and beep). `BoardPath(editor:rootName:)` is data-canvas's path: a capsule pill of crumbs for over the top middle of the canvas (the host's `.overlay(alignment: .top)`, over the whole canvas, which the path's geometry reader fills; 8 points down, `BoardsStyle`), nothing on the root, root › … › parent › current (the crumbs separated by ›s alone; the current bold, not a button; more than four fold the middle into "…", `BoardCrumbs.layout(rootName:)`: a button built as a board above's crumb is, in its gray (a SwiftUI `Menu` draws as a pop-up button, apart from the crumbs), which shows an `NSMenu` of the boards it hides, root side first, each `goUp(to:)`, lined up with it and starting at the pill's bottom edge, as a crumb's menu does (popped up `BoardsStyle.systemMenuTopPadding` lower, AppKit's padding above a menu's first item), `BoardPath.foldedMenu(_:editor:)`, which a test reads and chooses from without showing it; to assistive apps a pop-up button, "Boards between", whose press shows that menu and whose children are its boards (an `accessibilityRepresentation`, a `Menu` with a primary action, since one without does nothing when pressed there); each crumb's name its tooltip), each crumb `goUp(to:)`; the app places it with `ShippedPlugins.boardPath(editor:rootName:)`, the root's crumb the file's display name (`BoardViewState.fileName`, kept by `BoardWindowController.synchronizeWindowTitleWithDocumentName`), and only the pill and a crumb's menu take clicks; `BoardPath.coveredHeight(on:)` is how far down it reaches (about 35 points; none on the root), the editor's `coveredTop` in the app, so a fit leaves a title's pill clear of it. It reads only `BoardCrumbs.crumbs` (a service: remade when another board is shown, or a commit changes a board above, by their revisions), so a commit on the board shown makes and redraws nothing (`remade`, `pathRedrawn`, debug builds). Every crumb has data-canvas's hover menu (`docs/plugin-system.md`, "A crumb's menu"), unmarked (the owner found carets beside the ›s busy): the pointer on the crumb 150 ms shows, 6 points below it and lined up with it, the rows of the crumb's own board (`BoardCrumbs.menu(of:)`, a `CrumbMenu`: that board, and the shape whose board is next on the path, the way you came, highlighted; nothing highlighted on the board shown's, which lists its own shapes and has no menu while it has none, `shownIsEmpty`, kept as commits change it so the pill never reads the document; nil without TableOfContentsPlugin; the owner chose this over data-canvas's later siblings, the board above) as the table of contents lists them (`ContentsOutline`), in a panel 300 wide, up to 360 tall before it scrolls, kept 8 points inside the canvas; it closes 150 ms after the pointer has left it and its crumb, another crumb's takes its place once the pointer has stayed there, and a click on a crumb or another board shown closes it (one at a time: `openMenu`, driven by `pointerEntered(crumb:)`, `pointerExited(crumb:)`, `pointerEnteredMenu()`, `pointerExitedMenu()` and `closeMenu()`, through `editor.scheduler`); `pick(_:)`, a row's click, closes it and follows the shape, `editor.follow(id)`: into a shape whose kind opens its board (`goInto`), else to the shape (`goTo`), one step back (the rule is `follow`'s, written once, which Jump To's picks call too). A menu showing or closing redraws only `CrumbMenuLayer`, never the pill.

## Testing

- For boards in the app's window, CompositionTests' `NestedWindow` (BoardWindowTests) opens `NestedFile` in a `BoardDocument`'s own window (unshown) as the app wires it: `crumbs` are the path's buttons over the canvas, `hit(_:)` what a click would land on, `goItems()` the app's menus with Go's items sent to its controller (an unshown window isn't the key window), and `menus.main.performKeyEquivalent` is the menu bar's turn at a key, before `window.sendEvent` gives it to the text. `SubboardFileTests` hold saving, reopening on the board left, the view memory, revert and Delete Hidden Shapes to files of subboards.
- `follow(_:)` is BoardsTests' `followTests`: into a drawer or a pocket (a kind that opens its board, from its own line or another plugin's), to any other shape on any board, one Back step each, nothing for a shape that isn't in the file, and the crumbs' `pick` as a follow.
- For the path, BoardsTests host `BoardPath` in an `NSHostingView` in an unshown window (`PathWindow`, the hosting view pinned to the window's size, as the host's overlay proposes the canvas's: the path fills what it's offered) with the real `TaskScheduler`: SwiftUI draws a borderless button's title, so its `NSButton` has none; read titles from `editor.boardCrumbs` and click crumbs by position. `boardCrumbs.pathRedrawn` (debug builds) counts the path's redraws; "…" is the button over the view its menu is placed against (`BoardPath.foldedAnchor`: `PathWindow.folded`, CompositionTests' `pathFolds(under:)`), never clicked but on screen (its menu is modal: `TEST_UI_MENUS=1`, which reads the menu shown as it begins tracking and closes it), and its menu is read and chosen from headlessly (`BoardPath.foldedMenu`, `performActionForItem(at:)`). A crumb's menu is driven headlessly (`pointerEntered(crumb:)` and the rest, `h.wait(150)`, `openMenu`, `pick`; MenuTests, with `TableOfContentsPlugin()`); in a window, SwiftUI's hover doesn't answer synthesized events, so a test calls `pointerEntered(crumb:)` and finds the menu's rows as buttons below the pill (`PathWindow.menuButtons`, CompositionTests' `NestedWindow.menuButtons`), each a `performClick` away from `pick`; `NestedWindow.move(to:time:)` moves the pointer on the canvas under the path, as AppKit tells the canvas.
