# Boards

Getting around a file's boards: opening a board, Enclosing Board, a board's name (the root's, the file's, which the editor holds) and where it heads other plugins' lists, the path's crumbs and their menus.

Related: [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [subboards](subboards.md), [selection](selection.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [find](find.md), [editor](editor.md), [titles](titles.md), [keyboard](keyboard.md), [queries](queries.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Boards](../../docs/plugin-system.md#boards), [The path](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-path), [A crumb's menu](../../docs/plugin-system.md#a-crumbs-menu).

## Architecture

- Boards (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait, `docs/plugin-system.md` "Boards"): getting around a file's boards, whatever kind owns them. A `BoardOwning` kind opts in with `x.opensBoard(placeholder:systemImage:)` (`registrar.opensBoard(P.self, …)` for another plugin's kind; the `BoardKinds` point, one per kind, whose `check` refuses a kind that isn't `BoardOwning`, or every kind), which brings Selection's opener (`editor.showBoard(BoardID(owner:))`: double-click, Return, ⌘↓; from its row, Return once it's picked, which in the app's panel is the canvas's Return, as a double-click on a row doesn't reach the list there), and Open first in its context menu (`BoardsMenuGroup`, order 0; with several selected, the first), and nothing in its panel (an opened board changes nothing of the shape, and the canvas, the menu and ⌘↓ open it: a subboard's tabs are the ones every shape has); so a list with it needs Selection and ContextMenus. `BoardsPlugin` (id `board-navigation`: `boards` is the file's and the command line's word) declares `.enclosingBoard` on ⌘↑, its item in Go (order 10, `.whenIdle`) ([below](#stepping-out-then-up): one step out of a group or a container, else up) and `.goUpBoard`, the board step alone (off the root, with no session and nothing being edited), running `editor.goToEnclosingBoard()`, which is `editor.goUp(to: parent)`: any board above on `boardPath`, selecting there the owner of the board below it on the path, one step back (`showBoard(_:selecting:)`). `editor.name(of:)` is a board's name (its owner's title, else its kind's placeholder, `registry.boardPlaceholder(of:)`, which a query's rows read too through Boards' `BoardQueriesPlugin`, else its kind's label; the root, which no shape owns, the file's name, else "Board", `BoardsStyle.rootFallback`) and `symbol(of:)` its symbol; `editor.name(of:root:)` is the same with the root called what the caller says. `registry.containerName(of: record)` is what a shape that others are inside is called in a path of where a shape is, "Alpha › Beta" (a `ContainerName`, `title` and `placeholder`, `text` the first that's there and `isPlaceholder`: its title, never an opaque record's, else what its kind is called, its board placeholder or, for a kind that opens none, a frame, its kind's label): the one rule Jump To's rows and a reference's row in the table of contents go by ([jump to](jump-to.md), [references](references.md)), so they agree with each other and with the crumbs; it needs no `BoardsPlugin` installed (a kind nobody opens has no placeholder), and PlaceholderTests hold it. The file's name is the core's to hold, since only the window knows it: `editor.rootName` (observed, set only when it changes; empty until the host says, and headless), which `BoardWindowController.synchronizeWindowTitleWithDocumentName` sets as it titles the window (`editor.nameRoot(_:)`, host API), and Boards reads wherever it names the root, so the path, the list's heading and Find's follow a rename and nothing carries the name to them ([editor](editor.md)). Shipped after Titles. Boards names a board over other plugins' lists from two plugin values of its own (`Sources/Boards/BoardHeadings.swift`), each after `BoardsPlugin` and the list's plugin, left out of a list without that plugin and refused in one with it and no owner (`ownerNotInstalled`), which is why Boards imports Find: `BoardContentsPlugin` (id `board-contents`) gives the table of contents' header (`registrar.contentsHeading`, its `ContentsHeadings` point: [table of contents](table-of-contents.md)) the board shown's crumb (name, symbol, `up: .goUpBoard`, called as the command is, Enclosing Board (`BoardsStyle.enclosingBoard`, its title), with no key in its tooltip, since ⌘↑ may step out of a group or a container instead: the button always goes up a board whatever is selected, and shows only off the root; on the root, the file's name, as the window and the path's root crumb have it, else "Board"; the panel's Back is "Back (⌘[)", as Go › Back), and `BoardFindPlugin` (id `board-find`) heads each board's rows in Find's list (`registrar.findHeading`, its `FindHeadings` point: [find](find.md)) with `FindHeading(editor.name(of: board), systemImage: editor.symbol(of: board))`. What the window hosts of it, `BoardsPlugin` gives the host's slots ([host slots](host-slots.md)): the window's subtitle, the board shown's name off the root, empty on it (`registrar.windowSubtitle`, the last crumb's title; `BoardWindowController` reads it observed, so it follows a switch or a rename), and the path over the top of the canvas (`registrar.hostOverlay(alignment: .top, coveredTop: { BoardPath.coveredHeight(on: $0) })`, below). The Go menu has Back ⌘[, Enclosing Board ⌘↑ (retitled for its step: Enclosing Group, Enclosing Frame) and Open Selection ⌘↓ (each placed in Go by its declaration, Selection's and Boards', run by the controller's one action, `.whenIdle`), dimmed while text has the keyboard (`window.firstResponder is NSText`), where the menu bar (`MenuBar`, CanvasUI) leaves a disabled item's arrow key to the text, so it moves the caret (AppKit's menus would take it and beep). `BoardPath(editor:)` is data-canvas's path: a capsule pill of crumbs for over the top middle of the canvas (the host's `.overlay(alignment: .top)`, over the whole canvas, which the path's geometry reader fills; 8 points down, `BoardsStyle`), nothing on the root, root › … › parent › current (the crumbs separated by ›s alone; the current bold, not a button; more than four fold the middle into "…", `BoardCrumbs.layout(rootName:)`: a button built as a board above's crumb is, in its gray (a SwiftUI `Menu` draws as a pop-up button, apart from the crumbs), which shows an `NSMenu` of the boards it hides, root side first, each `goUp(to:)`, lined up with it and starting at the pill's bottom edge, as a crumb's menu does (popped up `BoardsStyle.systemMenuTopPadding` lower, AppKit's padding above a menu's first item), `BoardPath.foldedMenu(_:editor:)`, which a test reads and chooses from without showing it; to assistive apps a pop-up button, "Boards between", whose press shows that menu and whose children are its boards (an `accessibilityRepresentation`, a `Menu` with a primary action, since one without does nothing when pressed there); each crumb's name its tooltip), each crumb `goUp(to:)`; a crumb says whether its board is read-only (`BoardCrumb.isReadOnly`, from where the path turns read-only, the editor's `readOnlyStart` (one climb to the root, kept with `isBoardReadOnly`; asking `isReadOnly(board:)` of each crumb would cost the path's depth squared, which the chain of 1,000 boards' editor showing its deepest felt), worked out as the crumbs are made: a locked subboard's board, and every board under it, so a lock landing in a commit on a board above, `transact(on:)` or a file put in place, remakes them, which the revisions of the boards above already did; the root's crumb never is), and the path draws a small lock (`lock.fill`, `BoardsStyle.readOnlySymbol`, secondary, 3 points after the name, "Read-only" its tooltip and to assistive apps) after the name of the first crumb whose board is (`BoardCrumbs.readOnlyStart`), once, so the crumbs below it have none: the lock above says it of them all; it's part of the crumb (the name is cut short before it, a click on it goes up), and folded into "…" it's the lock on that crumb's item in the menu (`NSMenuItem.image`, before the name: an attributed title with the symbol after it would change the item's `title`), none if the first read-only crumb isn't folded, and in "Boards between"'s children for assistive apps; the window places it (CanvasUI's `BoardChrome`, from the host's slot), the root's crumb the file's display name (`editor.name(of: .root)`, read in the pill's body, so a rename redraws it), and only the pill and a crumb's menu take clicks; `BoardPath.coveredHeight(on:)` is how far down it reaches (about 35 points; none on the root), its overlay's `coveredTop`, which the window tells the editor, so a fit leaves a title's pill clear of it. It reads only `BoardCrumbs.crumbs` (a service: remade when another board is shown, or a commit changes a board above, by their revisions), so a commit on the board shown makes and redraws nothing (`remade`, `pathRedrawn`, debug builds). Every crumb has data-canvas's hover menu (`docs/plugin-system.md`, "A crumb's menu"), unmarked (the owner found carets beside the ›s busy): the pointer on the crumb 150 ms shows, 6 points below it and lined up with it, the rows of the crumb's own board (`BoardCrumbs.menu(of:)`, a `CrumbMenu`: that board, and the shape whose board is next on the path, the way you came, highlighted; nothing highlighted on the board shown's, which lists its own shapes and has no menu while it has none, `shownIsEmpty`, kept as commits change it so the pill never reads the document; nil without TableOfContentsPlugin; the owner chose this over data-canvas's later siblings, the board above) as the table of contents lists them (`ContentsOutline`), in a panel 300 wide, up to 360 tall before it scrolls, kept 8 points inside the canvas; it closes 150 ms after the pointer has left it and its crumb, another crumb's takes its place once the pointer has stayed there, and a click on a crumb or another board shown closes it (one at a time: `openMenu`, driven by `pointerEntered(crumb:)`, `pointerExited(crumb:)`, `pointerEnteredMenu()`, `pointerExitedMenu()` and `closeMenu()`, through `editor.scheduler`); `pick(_:)`, a row's click, closes it and follows the shape, `editor.follow(id)`: into a shape whose kind opens its board (`goInto`), else to the shape (`goTo`), one step back (the rule is `follow`'s, written once, which Jump To's picks call too). A menu showing or closing redraws only `CrumbMenuLayer`, never the pill.

## Stepping out, then up

- ⌘↑ is one step out (`.enclosingBoard`, `editor.goToEnclosing()`, in `Boards.swift`): `editor.selectEnclosure()` (Selection's: [selection](selection.md)) selects the innermost group the selection is inside, whole, else the container all the selected shapes are in, bringing it into view if it is not; otherwise `goToEnclosingBoard()`. So ⌘↑ in a note in a frame on a subboard's board selects the frame, then goes up to the root with the subboard selected. `isEnabled` is `(editor.selectionEnclosure != nil || !board.isRoot)` with no session and nothing edited, so on the root it is enabled while it would select a group or a container, and its key passes when it would not. The Go item is named for the step (the `menuTitle:` of its declaration; `editor.menuTitle(of:)`, which `BoardWindowController.validateMenuItem` reads to retitle the item that runs a command, nil for one whose item keeps its title: [editor](editor.md)): `BoardsStyle.enclosingGroup` "Enclosing Group", `BoardsStyle.enclosing` and `registry.label(of:)`'s name of the container's kind ("Enclosing Frame"), else "Enclosing Board"; `registry.title(of: .enclosingBoard)` stays "Enclosing Board", the static name the Keyboard Shortcuts window lists the key under. The table of contents' header button stays board-only: it runs `.goUpBoard` (Boards' own, declared with no key or menu item: the same `goToEnclosingBoard()`, off the root only), so it is the same whatever is selected, shows only off the root, and its tooltip has no key (it said "Enclosing Board (⌘↑)" while ⌘↑ was only that). BoardsTests' `enclosingTests` hold it over CanvasTesting's corrals and a groups fixture (a group, groups in groups, a container, a group in a container, the root, never mid-press or while editing, the titles, the camera, `.goUpBoard`), and CompositionTests' `steppingShippedTests` over the shipped Frames, Grouping and subboards, with the Go item.

## Testing

- For boards in the app's window, CompositionTests' `NestedWindow` (BoardWindowTests) opens `NestedFile` in a `BoardDocument`'s own window (unshown) as the app wires it: `crumbs` are the path's buttons over the canvas, `hit(_:)` what a click would land on, `goItems()` the app's menus with Go's items sent to its controller (an unshown window isn't the key window), and `menus.main.performKeyEquivalent` is the menu bar's turn at a key, before `window.sendEvent` gives it to the text. `SubboardFileTests` hold saving, reopening on the board left, the view memory, revert and Delete Hidden Shapes to files of subboards.
- `follow(_:)` is BoardsTests' `followTests`: into a drawer or a pocket (a kind that opens its board, from its own line or another plugin's), to any other shape on any board, one Back step each, nothing for a shape that isn't in the file, and the crumbs' `pick` as a follow.
- BoardsTests' `headingTests` (`HeadingTests.swift`) hold what Boards calls a board for others: `name(of:)` for the root ("Board", then what `nameRoot` said), the path's root crumb following a rename (redrawn once, not for the same name), `BoardContentsPlugin`'s heading read as `editor.tableOfContents?.heading` (the root's, heard when the file's renamed; a drawer's crumb with the way up and its key), `BoardFindPlugin`'s as `editor.find?.heading(of:)`, and each refused without the plugin whose list it heads. CompositionTests hold them shipped: InspectorTests the heading and each face's place in the list (and a list without the four keeping the panel, the list and Find), FindShippedTests Find's headings, BoardWindowTests the window telling the editor the file's name.
- For the path, BoardsTests host `BoardPath` in an `NSHostingView` in an unshown window (`PathWindow`, which tells the editor its `rootName` as a window does, the hosting view pinned to the window's size, as the host's overlay proposes the canvas's: the path fills what it's offered) with the real `TaskScheduler`: SwiftUI draws a borderless button's title, so its `NSButton` has none; read titles from `editor.boardCrumbs` and click crumbs by position. `boardCrumbs.pathRedrawn` (debug builds) counts the path's redraws; "…" is the button over the view its menu is placed against (`BoardPath.foldedAnchor`: `PathWindow.folded`, CompositionTests' `pathFolds(under:)`), never clicked but on screen (its menu is modal: `TEST_UI_MENUS=1`, which reads the menu shown as it begins tracking and closes it), and its menu is read and chosen from headlessly (`BoardPath.foldedMenu`, `performActionForItem(at:)`). The lock is held in three places: PathTests headlessly (the crumbs' flags along a path two deep under a frozen drawer, every board under it, the root's never; the first read-only crumb moving down as a frozen shape lands on a board above; the crumbs made again by a lock landing or going in a commit on a board above and by a file put in place, once each; "…"'s menu with the lock on the item of the first read-only crumb when it's folded, on none when it isn't, and chosen as ever), WindowTests drawn (the pill's width and D's crumb's: one lock after the first read-only crumb's name, the same width whether that's the first board or the last on the path, none on a path with none), over CanvasTesting's `Frozen` (`FreezingPlugin`), never Locking. A crumb's menu is driven headlessly (`pointerEntered(crumb:)` and the rest, `h.wait(150)`, `openMenu`, `pick`; MenuTests, with `TableOfContentsPlugin()`); in a window, SwiftUI's hover doesn't answer synthesized events, so a test calls `pointerEntered(crumb:)` and finds the menu's rows as buttons below the pill (`PathWindow.menuButtons`, CompositionTests' `NestedWindow.menuButtons`), each a `performClick` away from `pick`; `NestedWindow.move(to:time:)` moves the pointer on the canvas under the path, as AppKit tells the canvas.
