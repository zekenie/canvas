# Boards

Getting around a file's boards: opening a board, Enclosing Board, a board's name (the root's, the file's, which the editor holds) and where it heads other plugins' lists, the path's crumbs and their menus.

Related: [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [subboards](subboards.md), [selection](selection.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [find](find.md), [editor](editor.md), [titles](titles.md), [keyboard](keyboard.md), [queries](queries.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Boards](../../docs/plugin-system.md#boards), [The path](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-path), [A crumb's menu](../../docs/plugin-system.md#a-crumbs-menu).

## Architecture

- Boards (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait, `docs/plugin-system.md` "Boards"): getting around a file's boards, whatever kind owns them. A `BoardOwning` kind opts in with `x.opensBoard(placeholder:systemImage:)` (`registrar.opensBoard(P.self, …)` for another plugin's kind; the `BoardKinds` point, one per kind, which takes one kind at a time (`PointScope.kinds`: every kind or a scope is refused) and whose `check` refuses a kind that isn't `BoardOwning`), which brings Selection's opener (`editor.showBoard(BoardID(owner:))`: double-click, Return, ⌘↓; from its row, Return on it: the list's, walked to by keys; after a click, which in the app's panel shows the shape's panel and gives the canvas the keyboard, the canvas's, as a double-click on a row doesn't reach the list there), and Open first in its context menu (`BoardsMenuGroup`, `.before(ShapeMenuGroup.self)`), which is Selection's `.openSelection` titled Open (`MenuItem(command:title:in:)`): it shows ⌘↓ and is asked of the menu's shapes, so it opens one, goes into a group of them selected whole as ⌘↓ does, and is left out for several (the owner's call; it once opened the first), and the Shape menu leaves it out, since Go › Open Selection runs the same command; and nothing in its panel (an opened board changes nothing of the shape, and the canvas, the menu and ⌘↓ open it: a subboard's tabs are the ones every shape has); so a list with it needs Selection and ContextMenus. `BoardsPlugin` (id `board-navigation`: `boards` is the file's and the command line's word) declares `.enclosingBoard` on ⌘↑, its item in Go (in Selection's `NavigationSection`, `placed: .after(.goForward)`, right after Forward; `.whenIdle`) ([below](#stepping-out-then-up): one step out of a group or a container, else up) and `.goUpBoard`, the board step alone (off the root, with no session and nothing being edited), running `editor.goToEnclosingBoard()`, which is `editor.goUp(to: parent)`: any board above on `boardPath`, selecting there the owner of the board below it on the path, one step back (`showBoard(_:selecting:)`). `editor.name(of:)` is a board's name (its owner's title, else its kind's placeholder, `registry.boardPlaceholder(of:)`, which a query's rows read too through Boards' `BoardQueriesPlugin`, else its kind's label; the root, which no shape owns, the file's name, else "Board", `BoardsStyle.rootFallback`) and `symbol(of:)` its symbol; `editor.name(of:root:)` is the same with the root called what the caller says: what Mentions' `@` picker says of a shape's board (`MentionState.boardName(ofShape:)`) and References' pill, header and match rows (each calling the root "Board"), so an untitled subboard's board is "Untitled Subboard" in every list, never its kind's label. `registry.containerName(of: record)` is what a shape that others are inside is called in a path of where a shape is, "Alpha › Beta" (a `ContainerName`, `title` and `placeholder`, `text` the first that's there and `isPlaceholder`: its title, never an opaque record's, else what its kind is called, its board placeholder or, for a kind that opens none, a frame, its kind's label): the one rule Jump To's rows and a reference's row in the table of contents go by ([jump to](jump-to.md), [references](references.md)), so they agree with each other and with the crumbs; it needs no `BoardsPlugin` installed (a kind nobody opens has no placeholder), and PlaceholderTests hold it. The file's name is the core's to hold, since only the window knows it: `editor.rootName` (observed, set only when it changes; empty until the host says, and headless), which `BoardWindowController.synchronizeWindowTitleWithDocumentName` sets as it titles the window (`editor.nameRoot(_:)`, host API), and Boards reads wherever it names the root, so the path, the list's heading and Find's follow a rename and nothing carries the name to them ([editor](editor.md)). Shipped after Titles. Boards names a board over other plugins' lists from two plugin values of its own (`Sources/Boards/BoardHeadings.swift`), each after `BoardsPlugin` and the list's plugin, left out of a list without that plugin and refused in one with it and no owner (`ownerNotInstalled`), which is why Boards imports Find: `BoardContentsPlugin` (id `board-contents`) gives the table of contents' header (`registrar.contentsHeading`, its `ContentsHeadings` point: [table of contents](table-of-contents.md)) the board shown's crumb (name, symbol, `up: .goUpBoard`, called as the command is, Enclosing Board (`BoardsStyle.enclosingBoard`, its title), with no key in its tooltip, since ⌘↑ may step out of a group or a container instead: the button always goes up a board whatever is selected, and shows only off the root; on the root, the file's name, as the window and the path's root crumb have it, else "Board"; the panel's Back is "Back (⌘[)", as Go › Back), and `BoardFindPlugin` (id `board-find`) heads each board's rows in Find's list (`registrar.findHeading`, its `FindHeadings` point: [find](find.md)) with `FindHeading(editor.name(of: board), systemImage: editor.symbol(of: board))`. What the window hosts of it, `BoardsPlugin` gives the host's slots ([host slots](host-slots.md)): the window's subtitle, the board shown's name off the root, empty on it (`registrar.windowSubtitle`, the last crumb's title; `BoardWindowController` reads it observed, so it follows a switch or a rename), and the path over the top of the canvas (`registrar.hostOverlay(alignment: .top, coveredTop: { BoardPath.coveredHeight(on: $0) })`, below). The Go menu has Back ⌘[, Enclosing Board ⌘↑ (retitled for its step: Enclosing Group, Enclosing Frame) and Open Selection ⌘↓ (each placed in Go by its declaration, Selection's and Boards', run by the controller's one action, `.whenIdle`), dimmed while text has the keyboard (`window.firstResponder is NSText`), where the menu bar (`MenuBar`, CanvasUI) leaves a disabled item's arrow key to the text, so it moves the caret (AppKit's menus would take it and beep). `BoardPath(editor:)` is data-canvas's path: a capsule pill of crumbs for over the top middle of the canvas (the host's `.overlay(alignment: .top)`, over the whole canvas, which the path's geometry reader fills; 8 points down, `BoardsStyle`), nothing on the root, root › … › parent › current (the crumbs separated by ›s alone; the current bold, not a button; more than four fold the middle into "…", `BoardCrumbs.layout(rootName:)`: a button built as a board above's crumb is, in its gray (a SwiftUI `Menu` draws as a pop-up button, apart from the crumbs), which shows an `NSMenu` of the boards it hides, root side first, each `goUp(to:)`, lined up with it and starting at the pill's bottom edge, as a crumb's menu does (popped up `BoardsStyle.systemMenuTopPadding` lower, AppKit's padding above a menu's first item), `BoardPath.foldedMenu(_:editor:)`, which a test reads and chooses from without showing it; to assistive apps a pop-up button, "Boards between", whose press shows that menu and whose children are its boards (an `accessibilityRepresentation`, a `Menu` with a primary action, since one without does nothing when pressed there); each crumb's name its tooltip), each crumb `goUp(to:)`; a crumb says whether its board is read-only (`BoardCrumb.isReadOnly`, from where the path turns read-only, the editor's `readOnlyStart` (one climb to the root, kept with `isBoardReadOnly`; asking `isReadOnly(board:)` of each crumb would cost the path's depth squared, which the chain of 1,000 boards' editor showing its deepest felt), worked out as the crumbs are made: a locked subboard's board, and every board under it, so a lock landing in a commit on a board above, `transact(on:)` or a file put in place, remakes them, which the revisions of the boards above already did; the root's crumb never is), and the path draws a small lock (`lock.fill`, `BoardsStyle.readOnlySymbol`, secondary, 3 points after the name, "Read-only" its tooltip and to assistive apps) after the name of the first crumb whose board is (`BoardCrumbs.readOnlyStart`), once, so the crumbs below it have none: the lock above says it of them all; it's part of the crumb (the name is cut short before it, a click on it goes up), and folded into "…" it's the lock on that crumb's item in the menu (`NSMenuItem.image`, before the name: an attributed title with the symbol after it would change the item's `title`), none if the first read-only crumb isn't folded, and in "Boards between"'s children for assistive apps; the window places it (CanvasUI's `BoardChrome`, from the host's slot), the root's crumb the file's display name (`editor.name(of: .root)`, read in the pill's body, so a rename redraws it), and only the pill and a crumb's menu take clicks; `BoardPath.coveredHeight(on:)` is how far down it reaches (about 35 points; none on the root), its overlay's `coveredTop`, which the window tells the editor, so a fit leaves a title's pill clear of it. It reads only `BoardCrumbs.crumbs` (a service: remade when another board is shown, or a commit changes a board above, by their revisions), so a commit on the board shown makes and redraws nothing (`remade`, `pathRedrawn`, debug builds). Every crumb has data-canvas's hover menu (`docs/plugin-system.md`, "A crumb's menu"), unmarked (the owner found carets beside the ›s busy): the pointer on the crumb 150 ms shows, 6 points below it and lined up with it, the rows of the crumb's own board (`BoardCrumbs.menu(of:)`, a `CrumbMenu`: that board, and the shape whose board is next on the path, the way you came, highlighted; nothing highlighted on the board shown's, which lists its own shapes and has no menu while it has none, `shownIsEmpty`, kept as commits change it so the pill never reads the document; nil without TableOfContentsPlugin; the owner chose this over data-canvas's later siblings, the board above) as the table of contents lists them (`ContentsOutline`), in a panel 300 wide, up to 360 tall before it scrolls, kept 8 points inside the canvas; it closes 150 ms after the pointer has left it and its crumb, another crumb's takes its place once the pointer has stayed there, and a click on a crumb or another board shown closes it (one at a time: `openMenu`, driven by `pointerEntered(crumb:)`, `pointerExited(crumb:)`, `pointerEnteredMenu()`, `pointerExitedMenu()` and `closeMenu()`, through `editor.scheduler`); `pick(_:)`, a row's click, closes it and follows the shape, `editor.follow(id)`: into a shape whose kind opens its board (`goInto`), else to the shape (`goTo`), one step back (the rule is `follow`'s, written once, which Jump To's picks call too). A menu showing or closing redraws only `CrumbMenuLayer`, never the pill.

## Stepping out, then up

- ⌘↑ is one step out (`.enclosingBoard`, `editor.goToEnclosing()`, in `Boards.swift`): `editor.selectEnclosure()` (Selection's: [selection](selection.md)) selects the innermost group the selection is inside, whole, else the container all the selected shapes are in, bringing it into view if it is not; otherwise `goToEnclosingBoard()`. So ⌘↑ in a note in a frame on a subboard's board selects the frame, then goes up to the root with the subboard selected. `isEnabled` is `(editor.selectionEnclosure != nil || !board.isRoot)` with no session and nothing edited, so on the root it is enabled while it would select a group or a container, and its key passes when it would not. The Go item is named for the step (the `menuTitle:` of its declaration; `editor.menuTitle(of:)`, which `BoardWindowController.validateMenuItem` reads to retitle the item that runs a command, nil for one whose item keeps its title: [editor](editor.md)): `BoardsStyle.enclosingGroup` "Enclosing Group", `BoardsStyle.enclosing` and `registry.label(of:)`'s name of the container's kind ("Enclosing Frame"), else "Enclosing Board"; `registry.title(of: .enclosingBoard)` stays "Enclosing Board", the static name the Keyboard Shortcuts window lists the key under. The table of contents' header button stays board-only: it runs `.goUpBoard` (Boards' own, declared with no key or menu item: the same `goToEnclosingBoard()`, off the root only), so it is the same whatever is selected, shows only off the root, and its tooltip has no key (it said "Enclosing Board (⌘↑)" while ⌘↑ was only that). BoardsTests' `enclosingTests` hold it over CanvasTesting's corrals and a groups fixture (a group, groups in groups, a container, a group in a container, the root, never mid-press or while editing, the titles, the camera, `.goUpBoard`), and CompositionTests' `steppingShippedTests` over the shipped Frames, Grouping and subboards, with the Go item.

## The path by keys

- Go › Board Path (⌥⌘P, `.boardPath`, `Boards.swift`, in Boards' `PathSection`, after Jump To…, which JumpTo places first there; enabled off the root, not while a shape is being edited) gives the pill the keyboard on the last crumb (`BoardCrumbs.requestKeyboard()`, which makes the pill's key view the first responder). The keys come to `PathKeyView` (a view behind the pill, `PathKeys` its representable, in `BoardPath.swift`), which answers them and draws nothing: SwiftUI's own focus and `.onKeyPress` were tried first and dropped, since a window that is not key (every test window) gives them no keys, and one owner for the keyboard in the pill and its menu is simpler than a focus state in each. Where the keyboard is is model state, `BoardCrumbs.focus` (a `PathStop`: a crumb by its board, or `.fold`), drawn as an accent outline (`CrumbFocus`). ← and → step between the crumbs and "…" and stop at the ends, Return or Space goes up to a crumb's board (`editor.goUp(to:)`; the board shown's goes nowhere) and gives the keyboard to the canvas, or shows "…"'s modal menu, ↓ shows a crumb's menu (`openFromKeyboard`, which opens it at once and holds it open while it has the keyboard) or "…"'s, Escape closes the menu onto its crumb (`closeMenuToCrumb`), then gives the keyboard back to the canvas, and Tab or ⇧Tab does the same in one step (a pill has no stops past its own). Another key does nothing (P8), except that Tab and Escape always move on, even with no stop to act on. The keys are read through `KeyMeaning` (`PathKey.init`), so Caps Lock changes none. Another board shown while the pill or its menu has the keyboard (a board picked from "…", Back or ⌘↑ from the menu bar) gives it back to the canvas (`BoardCrumbs.boardDidChange`, through the `onExit` the pill gave it: `giveKeyboardBack`), since the stop it was on is gone. The pointer keeps working: hover opens a menu as before, and one opened in place of a menu the keyboard is in takes the keyboard to its own rows.
- A menu opened by keys has the keyboard in its rows: `BoardCrumbs.menuKeyboard` is the `OutlineKeyboard` `ContentsOutline` is given (TableOfContents): the key view feeds it ↑ ↓ (a row above, below; they stop at the ends), → (opens a row's disclosure, then goes on to its first nested row) and ← (closes it, then goes to the row it is nested under); the outline answers with its `row` (and `rowName`), outlined and scrolled into view, first on the highlighted row (the way you came), else the first. Return or Space picks the row (`crumbs.pick`), Escape closes the menu. A row's disclosure triangle is no stop of its own: the arrows open and close it, and assistive apps get its action ("Checkout, expand").
- A click on a crumb, "…" or a row still leaves the keyboard with the canvas: every button's action ends with `onExit` (`CanvasFocus.take`, handed to the path by `registrar.hostOverlay`'s second argument). The crumbs, "…" and the rows no longer opt out of focus (`.focusable(false)` is gone from them), so Full Keyboard Access sees them as buttons, and `onExit` makes the click's focus moot. An outline given no keyboard (the table of contents' own users) keeps its rows non-focusable.
- Assistive apps: the pill is a group, "Board path", with the action "Show Contents" (on the group, for the crumb with the keyboard else the last, and on each crumb; it opens the menu as ↓ does, and the pill's key view takes the keyboard, `openFromKeyboard`); the key view is an `AXGroup` "Board path" whose value is where the keyboard is (the crumb, "…" or the row; a `valueChanged` is posted after each key); the row the menu highlights is read as "current path". The outline's keys are in [table of contents](table-of-contents.md#the-keyboard).

## Testing

- For boards in the app's window, CompositionTests' `NestedWindow` (BoardWindowTests) opens `NestedFile` in a `BoardDocument`'s own window (unshown) as the app wires it: `crumbs` are the path's buttons over the canvas, `hit(_:)` what a click would land on, `goItems()` the app's menus with Go's items sent to its controller (an unshown window isn't the key window), and `menus.main.performKeyEquivalent` is the menu bar's turn at a key, before `window.sendEvent` gives it to the text. `SubboardFileTests` hold saving, reopening on the board left, the view memory, revert and Delete Hidden Shapes to files of subboards.
- `follow(_:)` is BoardsTests' `followTests`: into a drawer or a pocket (a kind that opens its board, from its own line or another plugin's), to any other shape on any board, one Back step each, nothing for a shape that isn't in the file, and the crumbs' `pick` as a follow.
- BoardsTests' `headingTests` (`HeadingTests.swift`) hold what Boards calls a board for others: `name(of:)` for the root ("Board", then what `nameRoot` said), the path's root crumb following a rename (redrawn once, not for the same name), `BoardContentsPlugin`'s heading read as `editor.tableOfContents?.heading` (the root's, heard when the file's renamed; a drawer's crumb with the way up and its key), `BoardFindPlugin`'s as `editor.find?.heading(of:)`, and each refused without the plugin whose list it heads. CompositionTests hold them shipped: InspectorTests the heading and each face's place in the list (and a list without the four keeping the panel, the list and Find), FindShippedTests Find's headings, BoardWindowTests the window telling the editor the file's name.
- For the path, BoardsTests host `BoardPath` in an `NSHostingView` in an unshown window (`PathWindow`, which tells the editor its `rootName` as a window does, the hosting view pinned to the window's size, as the host's overlay proposes the canvas's: the path fills what it's offered) with the real `TaskScheduler`: SwiftUI draws a borderless button's title, so its `NSButton` has none; read titles from `editor.boardCrumbs` and click crumbs by position. `boardCrumbs.pathRedrawn` (debug builds) counts the path's redraws; "…" is the button over the view its menu is placed against (`BoardPath.foldedAnchor`: `PathWindow.folded`, CompositionTests' `pathFolds(under:)`), never clicked but on screen (its menu is modal: `TEST_UI_MENUS=1`, which reads the menu shown as it begins tracking and closes it), and its menu is read and chosen from headlessly (`BoardPath.foldedMenu`, `performActionForItem(at:)`). The lock is held in three places: PathTests headlessly (the crumbs' flags along a path two deep under a frozen drawer, every board under it, the root's never; the first read-only crumb moving down as a frozen shape lands on a board above; the crumbs made again by a lock landing or going in a commit on a board above and by a file put in place, once each; "…"'s menu with the lock on the item of the first read-only crumb when it's folded, on none when it isn't, and chosen as ever), WindowTests drawn (the pill's width and D's crumb's: one lock after the first read-only crumb's name, the same width whether that's the first board or the last on the path, none on a path with none), over CanvasTesting's `Frozen` (`FreezingPlugin`), never Locking. A crumb's menu is driven headlessly (`pointerEntered(crumb:)` and the rest, `h.wait(150)`, `openMenu`, `pick`; MenuTests, with `TableOfContentsPlugin()`); in a window, SwiftUI's hover doesn't answer synthesized events, so a test calls `pointerEntered(crumb:)` and finds the menu's rows as buttons below the pill (`PathWindow.menuButtons`, CompositionTests' `NestedWindow.menuButtons`), each a `performClick` away from `pick`; `NestedWindow.move(to:time:)` moves the pointer on the canvas under the path, as AppKit tells the canvas.
- `pathKeysTests` (`PathKeysTests.swift`) drive the path by keys with the shared key-walk harness: `KeyedPath` hosts `BoardPath` over a stand-in canvas in an unshown window whose `onExit` gives the keyboard to the stand-in, and a `FocusReader` reads where the keyboard is from `BoardCrumbs.focus` and `menuKeyboard`. They hold Go › Board Path (the pill takes the keyboard on the deepest crumb; dimmed on the root), ← → as an axis with "…" among the stops (`expectAxis`, wrapping `.stays`), Return going up, ↓ opening the menu and ↑ ↓ → ← walking and disclosing its rows, Return picking, the Escape ladder (`expectEscapeLadder`: the crumb, then the canvas), a click on a crumb and a row handing the keyboard back, `expectShows` on each stop, keys with no use changing nothing, and Tab leaving. The command is run with `editor.perform(.boardPath)` here (BoardsTests has no menu bar); CompositionTests' `boardWindowTests` press ⌥⌘P through the real menus and window. SwiftUI's accessibility tree of an unshown window is empty, so the disclosure's label and the row's "current path" value are not read by a test. The modal "…" menu is not opened by a test.
