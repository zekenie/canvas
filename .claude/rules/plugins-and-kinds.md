# Plugins, the registry, kinds and capabilities

Plugin values and the frozen registry; a kind, its stateless definition and its capability protocols; what a translating or placing session does with what it moves.

Related: [properties and schemas](properties-and-schemas.md), [editor](editor.md), [editing](editing.md), [selection](selection.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [containers](containers.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

## Architecture

- A plugin is a value (`CanvasPlugin`; a `ListedPlugin`, one with an `init()`, when the product's list names it, as every shipped plugin is: the list is `Package.swift`'s table and each plugin is made by its name, [build](build.md), What ships) that registers into a frozen `Registry`, which refuses a plugin list with a duplicate plugin, a contribution whose owner isn't installed or whose kind nobody registers, or two claims to one key. A rule spanning a point's contributions that keys can't express goes in that point's `static func check(_ registry:)`, which throws `RegistryError.invalid` or any error (reported as `invalid` for the point; the core runs it without knowing what it checks). A kind is its props type (`static let kind`, defined by its list of properties); its behavior is a stateless `ShapeDefinition` (only `render` is required for box kinds, and `geometry` drives hit testing and outlines); more comes from capability protocols (`Editable`, `CustomTranslation`, which moves a kind by its own rules through `beginTranslation` when a `TranslatingSession` moves it, `PaintOrderAnchored`, `DerivedProps`, whose `derive` puts what follows from the rest of the props in step, as the kind's setters would, in a shape made from properties, `registry.shape(_:properties:)`, or given some of its own, `shape(_:setting:)`, and in one handed to `registry.derived(_:)` with nothing set (`canvas draw`'s width alone, `size: 300x`, at the new width): a note's height, from its text, `CustomSkeleton`: `renderSkeleton`, drawn in `render`'s place zoomed out (below the editor's `skeletonZoom`, 35%) but for the shape being edited, fills and greeked text bars (`context.fillSkeletonText`, as long as their text's columns, `SkeletonText`), no text, shadows or pictures of its own (a picture only through `RenderContext`, a subboard's board preview), reading the shape, its kept geometry, `context.zoom` and what its plugin already keeps of the shape if it's there, never parsing or laying out text (a paragraph's lines are counted, `SkeletonTextBars`), and outlined there by its kept geometry, as a kind without one is (drawn as the default skeleton, its geometry in gray, `context.drawSkeleton(of:color:)`), unless it adopts `CustomSkeletonOutline` (`skeletonIndicatorPath`, its outline there, nil for its kept geometry's: an arrow's arc and its heads; found once, so no other kind's shape is asked), `StrokedShape`: a kind drawn as a line, an open shape, says how wide its line is (`lineWidth(of:in:)`, in world units: an arrow's `ArrowStyle.strokeWidth`), so the core covers a tinted outline of it, drawn in full, as wide as the line is on screen and a point more rather than striping it (`Outlines.tinted`, [drawing](drawing.md); found once, `registry.strokedShapes`, and `registry.lineWidth(of:in:)` for what decorates along a line), and `BoardOwning`: each shape of the kind owns a board, a subboard, with nothing to implement; [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md); and `ShapeContaining`: each shape of the box kind holds shapes on its own board, a frame, with nothing to implement; [containers](containers.md)). Escape on a `TranslatingSession` puts back only what the drag moved (a box's origin; a `CustomTranslation` kind's whole record from before its begin), so a plugin's write to a shape while it moves (a model's answer landing) stays. A `TranslatingSession` carries what a container it moves holds, read-only or not (in `TranslationStart.ids` too), outlines past the drag threshold the container a drop would go into (the hint, never a read-only one), and on a release past the threshold settles what was dropped (`Transaction.settle`, a container dropped taking in what it lands on) in the press's step (`end` runs before `undoHistory.pressEnded()`), gliding what's nudged; a read-only container refusing the drop sends what was dragged back, gliding from where it was dropped, and the press leaves no step. A shape just placed (`editor.place`) follows the pointer while the press that placed it lasts, whatever its placement (`place(_:in:)` puts one down with a given frame, as with no press under way, never following: what a tool that draws its shape's rect puts down at the release, the Frame tool) (`PlacingSession`, a `TranslatingSession` inside), settles on the release (at once, in the same step, placed with no press; refused by a read-only container, it's taken away and the container hears it, `didRefuseChanges`), an editing placement's shape is edited on the release, unglided, and Escape before that takes the shape away and puts back what the undo participants kept before the press (what was selected), so the press comes to nothing; placed with no press, an editing placement's is edited at once. A placement may say the smallest its kind's shapes are drawn (`placement(size:minimumSize:props:)`, `Placement.minimumSize`, zero for none), the one place a kind says it: a tool that draws its shape's rect keeps to it (the Frame tool's drag), the kind hands it to its resizing (`resizable(minimumSize:)`), and `canvas draw`, which places by `registry.placement(for:)`, refuses a `size:` under it ([command line](command-line.md)). Selection declares two more: `HandleProviding` (handles on the only selected shape), and `BrushHitTesting`, which the marquee asks only when it overlaps a shape without strictly containing it (default: `ShapeGeometry.isHit(byBrush:)`, from `geometry`). Behavior intrinsic to a kind is a capability; behavior any plugin can add is an extension point. A plugin that puts down a record it built makes its placement from the kind's: `placement.mapping { record in … }` (the record each shape is made as, the kind staying) and `placement.sized(_:)`, beside `setting(_:to:)`, since `Placement.init` is the core's; and reads one added value by its key as JSON with `record.addedJSON(for:)` (PlacementHelperTests). Conform to `ShapeDefinition` (and capabilities), `Tool`, `Session`, `EditorService`, `MentionScope`, `TransformChannel` and any extension point that implements `check` or `summary` (how the registry's report shows a contribution) in an extension: only there does the compiler flag a near miss on a defaulted requirement. `registry.memo(_:_:)` keeps a value a plugin works out of the registry once per `Hashable` key (the registry never changes; main actor): what a per-shape or per-frame question would otherwise rebuild, as `contributions(to:for:)` maps its array afresh on each call.
- A kind's label (`registry.label(of:)`, a `KindLabel`) is what it's called and its symbol (what it declares, `x.label(_:systemImage:)`, one per kind; else its placing tool's; else its id, with no symbol), and its tint (`x.tint(_:)`, one per kind, the report's `tint → sticky-note`; nil for a kind that declares none): its symbol's color wherever a shape is listed on a neutral surface, so a kind has one color in every list. A shape that has a color of its own is listed in that: `registrar.shapeTints { record, editor in NSColor? }` (a list, in plugin order, asked of one shape at a time; Colors gives a shape's own color's `solid`, as a dynamic color the book keeps, so two asks are the one color, and nothing for a shape with none: a default-yellow note is listed as it was) and `editor.tint(of: record)` (a `ShapeRecord` too) is the first a plugin gives, else the kind's; `editor.ownTint(of:)` is the plugins' alone, for a list that works out the kind's once a kind. The report says it as `shape tints`. It is asked per row shown, never per frame for every shape, and every list asks it as it makes or draws a row (Find's rows, Jump To's, Mentions' appearance, so the `@` picker, a pill on a neutral surface and what References and the Connections tab take from it, the table of contents' rows through `ContentsContext.color(of:)`, Neighbors' lists, the reference panel's matches). A shape's tint changes by a commit that names the shape, which each list hears as it hears a rename; one that changes with no commit to it (the file's colors) is told with `editor.shapeTintsDidChange()` (the plugin that gives tints calls it: Colors from `book.onChange`), which every service hears as `EditorService.shapeTintsDidChange(in:)` (defaulted), and a list that keeps what it shows makes it again there. Shipped: a note's yellow (`NoteStyle.tint`), a subboard's indigo (`SubboardStyle.border`), and a frame's gray (`FrameStyle.tint`); a type is a note, so its color is the note's. A row in the table of contents says its own color (`ContentsLabel(color:)`, from `context.color(of: shape)`). CanvasTests' tintTests hold the point (the first plugin that answers, the kind's otherwise, none, the services told) with CanvasTesting's `Frozen` flag standing in for a color a shape holds; every reader's tests do the same with a contributor of their own. CanvasTests' RegistryTests hold it: with a declared label and with a tool's, none for a kind that declares none, and a second tint for a kind a conflict.
- `LaidOutBox` (a capability of a box kind, `laidOutSize(of:in:)`) is a kind whose shapes are laid out at another size than the box they store, larger or smaller, by what their layout inputs say (a note with rows no record holds; a relationship type drawn small): its geometry, indicator path and `Editable` editing frame default to `context.frame(of:)`, the stored origin at the laid-out size, as the kind answers it (finite and above zero on both sides, else the box); conform in an extension, like the rest. If Swift calls those defaults ambiguous against the box kinds' own, the kind writes the one-line methods. A kind that is one is not resizable: Selection leaves it out of the kinds that show handles, so none shows on its stored box under a larger shape or past a smaller one. A kind that holds shapes (`ShapeContaining`) can't be one (the registry refuses the list: `x holds shapes, so it can't be laid out past its box`), `registry.isLaidOutBox(kind)`; the layout, what the core keeps and what lets it go are in [the editor](editor.md). CanvasTests' `laidOutTests` hold it with CanvasTesting's `StretchesPlugin`.
- A shape's id (`ShapeID`, Identity.swift) is its UUID kept as two words (`rawValue` gives the UUID back; it's written as the UUID's string): a layout every module knows, so a value that holds one (an edge, a record, a set or dictionary key) is copied, compared and hashed as plain words are, where Foundation's `UUID`, whose layout is its own to change, goes through witnesses. Hashes differ from a `UUID`'s, and nothing keeps one.
- A shape's solid (`ShapeContext.solidBounds(of:)`, on any context: the editor's, a transaction's, a board read in place): its bounds where its geometry is closed, finite and not flat; none for a shape kept aside (`isOpaque`), an open one (an arrow, a line), one that isn't there, or bounds that are null, not finite or flat. It's what shapes placed among others keep clear of: `canvas draw`'s obstacles, which DrawScript's `ObstacleGrid` files ([command line](command-line.md)). Both are the foundation's, so any plugin may place a shape among others the same way (Moving's commands do: [moving](moving.md)). ShapeIndexTests hold it, asked of the editor's context and of a plain one.
