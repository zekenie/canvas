# Adornments

Adornments, the small things shown on a shape (Titles' pill, Locking's chip): placing one, its places, layout, drawing, pointing and cost, and how to test them.

Related: [titles](titles.md), [locking](locking.md), [selection](selection.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [validation](validation.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Adornments](../../docs/plugin-system.md#adornments).

## Architecture

- Adornments (a surface, on Selection) lays out and draws the small things shown on a shape, just outside it or on its corner, from what the shape knows: Titles' pill (`TitlePill`) and Locking's lock (`LockChip`) are adornments. An adornment is a type (`Adornment`: its `placement` and its `order`, in the tabs' bands: 0 a kind's own, 50 a horizontal's feature, 300 a state that comes and goes), never a record. Any plugin places one, on kinds it defines or not: `x.adornment(A.self) { shape in … }` for a kind's own (a horizontal's opt-in sugar may call it, so opting in brings the adornment), `registrar.adornment(A.self, on: Kind.self) { … }` for another plugin's kind, and `registrar.adornmentForEveryShape(A.self, where: { kind in … }) { shape, context in … }`, where `where` (an `AdornableKind`, asked once per kind) picks kinds by their definition, by what the registry says of them (Titles: the kinds whose title shows a pill), or by the added properties they hold (`$0.holds(LockedProperty.self)`: Locking's lock goes only on kinds that can be locked). A kind's own takes the place of an every-shape one of the same type, and one adornment twice on a kind is a conflict (`ShapeAdornments` is keyed). A label is an `AdornmentLabel` (one line of text, after a small SF Symbol if it names one, `AdornmentLabel(title, systemImage:)`, in a place as wide as the text is tall, a little softer than the text; or an SF Symbol alone in a disc; `tone: .success` is green; `opacity(_:)`), or nil for none on that shape, and every plugin's looks alike. It reads only its shape, the registry, and services that call `editor.adornmentsDidChange(for:)` when what they keep changes (Locking's open lock, fading). Places (`AdornmentPlacement`): the row 9 points above the top edge, 18 tall, from its left end (`aboveTopLeft`: text gives way to what's at the other end, ending in "…", and needs 30 points, and its symbol's place more) and from its right end (`aboveTopRight`: whole); and the top-right corner (`onTopRightCorner`: 16 tall, centered on the top edge, from 6 points past the right edge). Each place is a run, 4 points apart, so on one shape none overlap, by construction (`AdornmentLayout`, pure); the row's right end and the corner reach no more than 200 points (what would is left out), and its left end stays within the shape's width, which bounds culling and hit testing; sizes are view points, constant down to 25%. Below 35% a shape drawn as a skeleton (`editor.drawsSkeleton(of:)`) shows no adornments: none is made, laid out, drawn or pointed at for it (what a title says is said on the shape instead, Titles' cover); the shape being edited, drawn in full, keeps its own, in full, text and all. Drawing, hit testing and `adornmentFrames(of:)` all go by `drawsSkeleton(of:)` (the overlay reads the shape being edited once a frame, and returns at once with nothing to draw), so what's hit is what's drawn (a lock can't be clicked on a skeleton, and the edited shape's pill is hit in full). `AdornmentState` keeps each shape's labels with their faces (text set and measured, symbols' images), made together for what's drawn or pointed at, and drops them for what each commit's `ChangeSet` names; read before it has heard a commit, it makes them afresh and keeps nothing; on `drawsSkeletonsDidChange` it lets everything kept go as skeletons start (the edited shape's are made again as it's drawn), and nothing more: Selection hears it too and looks again under a still pointer, buttons first, for a button come or gone. `AdornmentOverlay` draws them in one pass over `editor.paintOrder(in:)` (the view grown by `context.worldDrawingReach` too, so a shape shaken into view shows them), above every shape, where the shape is drawn (a shake moves them), never in the minimap; once labels are kept, a frame allocates nothing per adorned shape (one slots buffer a frame, `AdornmentLayout.slots(of:width:into:)`, and the hovered and pressed buttons read once and compared field by field, never boxed). A `ClickableAdornment` (the lock) is a canvas button, and its shape's only for the context menu; any other adornment is its shape for the select tool, hover and a double-click, and every adornment, the lock too, for the context menu (Selection's `DecorationHits`). `editor.adornmentFrames(of:)` is where each of a shape's is, in world units. Debug builds count the shapes whose labels are kept (`adornmentsKept`, those with none too), those that show something, their faces made (`adornmentFacesKept`), and the faces made (`adornmentFacesMade`).

## Testing

- For adornments, `AdornmentLayout.frames(of:width:)` is pure (AdornmentsTests' layout tests and their seeded property check), and `editor.adornmentFrames(of:)` gives a shape's headlessly, in world units, where it is (not where a shake draws it). Pixels go through `renderBitmap` and `changedPixels`; a clickable one's identity, as `editor.hoveredButton` holds it, is `AdornmentButton(A.self, on: id)`. AdornmentsTests use fixtures only, with adornments of their own (declare a test's adornment types internal, not private, so a conflict key names one as `Module.Name`). Any plugin list with Titles or Locking needs `KeyboardPlugin()`, `SelectionPlugin()` and `AdornmentsPlugin()` (and one with either `SlashCommandsPlugin()`, for their `/title` and `/lock`); put `AdornmentsPlugin()` where the shipped list has it, after Validation (so adornments draw over its outline) and before the plugins that place them. Zoomed out, AdornmentsTests' "Adornments zoomed out" hold that below 35% no adornment is drawn, laid out, made or kept for a shape (and that at 35% each is drawn in its frame), flip the level under a still pointer with `skeletonZoom` (a button let go and found again: Selection's look alone), hold a point where an adornment would be to being no adornment's, zooming out to making and keeping no faces (`adornmentFacesMade`, `adornmentFacesKept`), and the edited card's caption and stamp laid out, drawn (pixel for pixel as in full) and hovered in full at 30% while another card's aren't shown; TitlesTests hold no pill at 30% and the pill in full at 35%, and `CoverTests` the cover (the title as plain text inside the shape and nowhere else, centered, or at a container's top left; its ink by the kind's surface; its size the shape's, equal for equal shapes, 9 to 28 points; no word broken, one line truncated where a word can't fit; none for an untitled or too small shape, or a kind with no pill; a title cut to 120 characters ending in an ellipsis, and a long one in a small shape held inside it; a short card still has one; a shake moves it), LockingTests no chip on a skeleton, and none to click, ValidationTests the rings (square, hollow, in paint order, grown from no outline, and none on the shape being edited) and crossing rings in full against an in-test copy of the stroke from before the line width and join were set once a frame (each ring is still stroked alone in full), pixel for pixel.
