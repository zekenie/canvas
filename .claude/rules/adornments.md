# Adornments

Adornments, the small things shown on a shape (Titles' pill, Locking's chip): placing one, its places, layout, drawing, pointing and cost, and how to test them.

Related: [titles](titles.md), [locking](locking.md), [selection](selection.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [validation](validation.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Adornments](../../docs/plugin-system.md#adornments).

## Architecture

- Adornments (a surface, on Selection) lays out and draws the small things shown on a shape, just outside it or on its corner, from what the shape knows: Titles' pill (`TitlePill`) and Locking's lock (`LockChip`) are adornments. An adornment is a type (`Adornment`: its `placement` and its `order`, in the tabs' bands: 0 a kind's own, 50 a horizontal's feature, 300 a state that comes and goes), never a record. Any plugin places one, on kinds it defines or not: `x.adornment(A.self) { shape in … }` for a kind's own (a horizontal's opt-in sugar may call it, so opting in brings the adornment), `registrar.adornment(A.self, on: Kind.self) { … }` for another plugin's kind, and `registrar.adornmentForEveryShape(A.self, where: { kind in … }) { shape, context in … }`, where `where` (an `AdornableKind`, asked once per kind) picks kinds by their definition, by what the registry says of them (Titles: the kinds whose title shows a pill), or by the added properties they hold (`$0.holds(LockedProperty.self)`: Locking's lock goes only on kinds that can be locked). A kind's own takes the place of an every-shape one of the same type, and one adornment twice on a kind is a conflict (`ShapeAdornments` is keyed). A label is an `AdornmentLabel` (one line of text, after a small SF Symbol if it names one, `AdornmentLabel(title, systemImage:)`, in a place as wide as the text is tall, a little softer than the text unless the label says `brightSymbol()` (as white as the text: Publishing's globe, a symbol that is itself the news); or an SF Symbol alone in a disc; `tone: .success` is green; `opacity(_:)`, `compact(_:)`: a narrower face the label takes at the row's right end when the full one would leave the left end's text, a title's pill, cut short or out and the narrower one gives it more room, below), or nil for none on that shape, and every plugin's looks alike. It reads only its shape, the registry, and services that call `editor.adornmentsDidChange(for:)` when what they keep changes (`ChipFade`'s fading chips). Places (`AdornmentPlacement`): the row 9 points above the top edge, 18 tall, from its left end (`aboveTopLeft`: text gives way to what's at the other end, ending in "…", and needs 30 points, and its symbol's place more) and from its right end (`aboveTopRight`: whole); and the top-right corner (`onTopRightCorner`: 16 tall, centered on the top edge, from 6 points past the right edge). `ChipFade` (a public service of `AdornmentsPlugin`, per editor) is the linger-and-fade state every chip that shows a moment shares (Locking's open lock; Publishing's "Private"): a plugin `show`s a chip on a shape (`fade.show(A.self, on: id)`: in full for 1500 ms, re-looked at every 500 ms while the pointer's on it, then 300 ms of fading in 16 ms frames on `editor.scheduler`, each frame `adornmentsDidChange`), reads `opacity(of:on:)` from its label (nil when not showing), and `hide`s it (returns whether it was showing) or asks `isShowing`; keyed by chip (its type's identity, so asking makes no `AdornmentID`) and shape, so two chips on one shape fade apart, every ask a dictionary hit and nothing while no chip shows (a commit asks about each shape it deletes, a frame about each in view; Locking and Publishing find the service once), `show` on a showing chip starts its linger over, a deleted shape's chips go (told once) and another board shown stops all; the caller tells Adornments for what `show` and `hide` do, once for all it changes (`AdornmentStyle` holds the times). The compact face is the layout's (`AdornmentLayout.Item.compact`, pure): after the row is laid out in full, if some left-end text isn't whole (cut short or out), the right end's items that have a compact face take it, nearest the edge first: the fewest that give the left-end texts as much width in all as every one of them does (so one already whole goes no farther), and every one when even that shows no text; if the full faces show every text whole, or the row has no text at its left end, none is. So as a shape widens, however narrow it starts, the faces change once: compact, until the full faces and the whole text fit together, then full (a public note's title at 50% zoom: compact from the narrowest, no title below 92 points, "Payments ro…" beside the globe and the count from 92, whole from 179, the full "Public · 3" from 221), where the earlier rules flipped the chip full, compact and full again; only `aboveTopRight` items take one, an item without one costs nothing, the compact face is made with the label (`LaidOutAdornments.compactFaces`, counted by `adornmentFacesMade`), is the label `adornmentFrames(of:)` reports where taken, and fades with the label (`opacity(_:)` sets both). Each place is a run, 4 points apart, so on one shape none overlap, by construction (`AdornmentLayout`, pure); the row's right end and the corner reach no more than 200 points (what would is left out), and its left end stays within the shape's width, which bounds culling and hit testing; sizes are view points, constant down to 25%. Below 35% a shape drawn as a skeleton (`editor.drawsSkeleton(of:)`) shows no adornments: none is made, laid out, drawn or pointed at for it (what a title says is said on the shape instead, Titles' cover); the shape being edited, drawn in full, keeps its own, in full, text and all. Drawing, hit testing and `adornmentFrames(of:)` all go by `drawsSkeleton(of:)` (the overlay reads the shape being edited once a frame, and returns at once with nothing to draw), so what's hit is what's drawn (a lock can't be clicked on a skeleton, and the edited shape's pill is hit in full). `AdornmentState` keeps each shape's labels with their faces (text set and measured, symbols' images), made together for what's drawn or pointed at, and drops them for what each commit's `ChangeSet` names; read before it has heard a commit, it makes them afresh and keeps nothing; on `drawsSkeletonsDidChange` it lets everything kept go as skeletons start (the edited shape's are made again as it's drawn), and nothing more: Selection hears it too and looks again under a still pointer, buttons first, for a button come or gone. `AdornmentOverlay` draws them in one pass over `editor.paintOrder(in:)` (the view grown by `context.worldDrawingReach` too, so a shape shaken into view shows them), above every shape, where the shape is drawn (a shake moves them), never in the minimap; once labels are kept, a frame allocates nothing per adorned shape (one slots buffer a frame, `AdornmentLayout.slots(of:width:into:)`, and the hovered and pressed buttons read once and compared field by field, never boxed). A `ClickableAdornment` (the lock) is a canvas button, and its shape's only for the context menu; any other adornment is its shape for the select tool, hover and a double-click, and every adornment, the lock too, for the context menu (Selection's `DecorationHits`). `editor.adornmentFrames(of:)` is where each of a shape's is, in world units. `editor.adornmentExtent(of:)` is how far above its top edge a shape reaches with the adornments it would show at 100% zoom (`AdornmentLayout.extent(of:in:)`, pure: 27 view points for the row, 8 for the corner, 0 for none, text left out for want of room counting for nothing), asked of any record, on a board or not, and keeping nothing for it (`AdornmentState.laidOut(_:keeping:)`): `canvas measure`'s `above` ([command line](command-line.md)). Debug builds count the shapes whose labels are kept (`adornmentsKept`, those with none too), those that show something, their faces made (`adornmentFacesKept`), and the faces made (`adornmentFacesMade`).

## Testing

- ChipFadeTests hold the fade on fixture chips (`Ghost`, `Echo`: the timings, hover, restart, hide, two chips apart, a deleted shape, another board); CompactTests the compact face, pure on fixture items (`AdornmentLayout.frames`: kept in full with room, taken to give a title the most width they can (whole, or cut short where it would have vanished or been cut shorter) with the fewest that do, all of them where no title shows even then, nearest the edge first, only the right end, one change of face across a sweep of widths from the narrowest, a seeded property check) and through a board (frames, a title the full chip pushes out drawn cut short beside the disc at 100% and at 50%, pixels, opacity).
- For adornments, `AdornmentLayout.frames(of:width:)` is pure (AdornmentsTests' layout tests and their seeded property check), as `AdornmentLayout.extent(of:in:)` is, and `editor.adornmentFrames(of:)` gives a shape's headlessly, in world units, where it is (not where a shake draws it). Pixels go through `renderBitmap` and `changedPixels`; a clickable one's identity, as `editor.hoveredButton` holds it, is `AdornmentButton(A.self, on: id)`. AdornmentsTests use fixtures only, with adornments of their own (declare a test's adornment types internal, not private, so a conflict key names one as `Module.Name`). Any plugin list with Titles or Locking needs `KeyboardPlugin()`, `SelectionPlugin()` and `AdornmentsPlugin()` (and one with either `SlashCommandsPlugin()`, for their `/title` and `/lock`); put `AdornmentsPlugin()` where the shipped list has it, after Validation (so adornments draw over its outline) and before the plugins that place them. Zoomed out, AdornmentsTests' "Adornments zoomed out" hold that below 35% no adornment is drawn, laid out, made or kept for a shape (and that at 35% each is drawn in its frame), flip the level under a still pointer with `skeletonZoom` (a button let go and found again: Selection's look alone), hold a point where an adornment would be to being no adornment's, zooming out to making and keeping no faces (`adornmentFacesMade`, `adornmentFacesKept`), and the edited card's caption and stamp laid out, drawn (pixel for pixel as in full) and hovered in full at 30% while another card's aren't shown; TitlesTests hold no pill at 30% and the pill in full at 35%, and `CoverTests` the cover (the title as plain text inside the shape and nowhere else, centered, or at a container's top left; its ink by the kind's surface; its size the shape's, equal for equal shapes, 9 to 28 points; no word broken, one line truncated where a word can't fit; none for an untitled or too small shape, or a kind with no pill; a title cut to 120 characters ending in an ellipsis, and a long one in a small shape held inside it; a short card still has one; a shake moves it), LockingTests no chip on a skeleton, and none to click, ValidationTests the rings (square, hollow, in paint order, grown from no outline, and none on the shape being edited) and crossing rings in full against an in-test copy of the stroke from before the line width and join were set once a frame (each ring is still stroked alone in full), pixel for pixel.
