# Grouping

Grouping: shapes grouped together, selected as one: the groups a shape is in (`groups`), what counts as a group on a board (`BoardGroups`), Group and Ungroup (their keys, the context menu, Arrange), copies, and what agents write and read of groups.

Related: [selection](selection.md) (what a click, the marquee, the hover, Escape, Tab and ⌘↓ and ⌘↑ do with groups), [clipboard](clipboard.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [keyboard](keyboard.md), [locking](locking.md), [properties and schemas](properties-and-schemas.md), [command line](command-line.md), [queries](queries.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Grouping](../../docs/plugin-system.md#grouping), [Groups selected as one](../../docs/plugin-system.md#groups-selected-as-one).

## Architecture

- Grouping (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait, on ContextMenus, Keyboard, Queries and Selection; `GroupingPlugin`, id `grouping`, shipped after Selection's points' owners and before Clipboard and Locking, so Group and Ungroup come before Lock in a shape's menu): shapes grouped are selected as one until they're ungrouped (tldraw's and Keynote's). A group is something its shapes have, as a lock is, not a shape (the owner's choice over a container kind, 2026-10-18: no row in the table of contents, no title, an arrow can be in one, and a shape grouped with one outside its frame stays in its frame): `GroupsProperty` (`groups`, `[String]`, the ids of the groups the shape is in, outermost first, a UUID's string each, nil on a shape in none, `isNone` for an empty list), an added property every kind opts into (`optInEveryShape`), `unlisted()` (the core's `x-canvas-unlisted`: neither draw nor queries list, read or write it, [properties and schemas](properties-and-schemas.md)), its fixture `Fixtures/added/groups.v0.json`. What counts is `BoardGroups` (pure, `Sendable`, made from the paths a board's shapes hold, back to front, so the board shown, a transaction, a query and a copy read groups alike): a group is its shapes on one board and counts while it holds two parts or more there (a part is a shape, or a group in it that counts; worked out by passes that drop the groups of fewer than two until none drops; a path naming a group twice counts it once), `paths` (each shape's groups that count, outermost first), `shapes` (each group's, at any depth, back to front), `path(of:)`; a shape whose group no longer counts (its other shapes deleted, moved to another board, regrouped) holds the id harmlessly until something writes its path again, which writes only groups that count. `grouping(_:as:)`: a new group inside the groups the shapes are all in less those they hold whole (`level(of:)`, as the selection is inside them: Selection's focus), each shape keeping the groups past the first it's in that they hold whole (a group held whole stays a group in the new one; one held in part is left), nil under two parts (tldraw's); `ungrouping(_:)`: the outermost groups they hold whole there taken apart, a level, each of their shapes' paths less that group, nil when they hold none; `canGroup`, `canUngroup`; `copied(_:mint:)` (static): the paths copies hold, each group two or more of them share under a new id, one only one holds gone. `GroupIndex` (a service, the `SelectionGroupSource` Selection is given, `registrar.selectionGroups`): the board shown's stored paths and their `BoardGroups`, kept by each commit's added, removed and changed less moved shapes (a path compared, so typing and a drag cost a comparison a shape), regrouped (O(grouped shapes)) only when a path changed or the stack did with groups on the board, then `editor.selectionGroupsDidChange()`; a `BoardStash` of each board left (`boardsScannedForGroups`, debug builds). The editor's: `boardGroups`, `canGroup(_:)`, `canUngroup(_:)`, `group(_:)` and `ungroup(_:)` (one transaction, "Group" or "Ungroup", writing each shape's path; all or nothing, `editor.transactWhole`: a read-only shape refuses, nothing commits, no step, the refused shake; false when nothing was written); the shapes stay selected and Selection works out what the selection is inside again once the index hears the commit; nothing restacks (tldraw's group takes the stack place of its topmost shape: an intended deviation). The commands `.group` (⌥⌘G) and `.ungroup` (⌥⇧⌘G), Grouping's own, each one declaration (the owner's shape for every command: `registrar.command(.group, title: "Group", menu: "Arrange", order: 0, shortcut: KeyChord("g", [.command, .option]), runs: .whenIdle, isEnabled: { $0.editor.canGroup($0.shapes) }) { $0.editor.group($0.shapes) }`; [editor](editor.md), Commands; [keyboard](keyboard.md): Arrange › Group and Ungroup), Keynote's and Freeform's keys, since ⌘G is Find Next (tldraw's ⌘G toggles and ⇧⌘G ungroups; its ⌥⌘G frames a selection, which this app hasn't built); one rule each, over the shapes they're given: of kinds that hold `groups`, two parts or more for Group, a group held whole for Ungroup; from a key or the menu bar, with nothing under way or being edited (`.whenIdle`). Context menu (items for the canvas in `StateMenuGroup`, before Lock, `MenuItem(command: .group, in: StateMenuGroup.self)` and `.ungroup`'s, showing their keys, each shown by its command's rule over the menu's shapes; the Shape menu leaves them out, as the menu bar has them in Arrange): Group for a group and a shape, Ungroup for a group, neither for one shape. Copies: Clipboard gives copies that shared a group a group of their own ([clipboard](clipboard.md)). Not built: resizing a group as one (tldraw scales its shapes; a selection of several has no handles here), a group's title or row, and tldraw's rule that refuses to group an arrow bound to a shape left out (here it's grouped like any shape).
- Agents: `canvas draw`'s `group "A" and "B"` and `ungroup "A"` ([command line](command-line.md); `GroupDrawPlugin`, id `group-draw`, after Colors' draw words, fills Draw's `DrawGroups`: `group` makes exactly the shapes named one group of their own, flat, `unchanged` when they already are one; `ungroup` takes each out of every group, `unchanged` for a shape in none that counts; a read-only shape is `read_only`, as Draw reads the transaction's refusals); `canvas query`'s `:grouped-with(S)` (`GroupQueriesPlugin`, id `group-queries`, after Colors' query words in the shipped list): a `reads: .file` relation over `GroupedFile` (a `QueryFileFact`, the file as the query takes it up, O(1)), each board's `BoardGroups` read once a query off the main actor on its first ask (`GroupMates`, a lock), related to the shapes of its outermost group that counts, never itself, so `:grouped-with(*)` is every grouped shape, file-wide; the skill's query reference lists it ([queries](queries.md)).

- Keys through groups (Selection's, [selection](selection.md#moving-between-shapes); Grouping knows nothing of them, the source gives the paths): a group is one stop for Tab and ⌥⌘ with an arrow (at the middle of its shapes' bounds), and inside a group (`editor.selectionFocus`) the stops are its parts, so Tab goes round it; ⌘↓ and Return with a group selected whole select its first part one level in, in reading order; ⌘↑ ([boards](boards.md#stepping-out-then-up)) with the selection inside a group selects the innermost group whole, as Escape does, naming the Go item "Enclosing Group". SelectionTests hold them over `GroupsFixture`, CompositionTests' `steppingShippedTests` over Grouping's own.

## Testing

- GroupingTests: `boardGroupsTests` the pure reading (what counts, groups in groups, a group whose one part is a group, grouping and ungrouping at every level, inside a group, copies), `groupingTests` the plugin over CanvasTesting's boxes (the property in every kind's schema, unlisted; ⌥⌘G and ⇧⌥⌘G (a key let go within 150 ms still counts: wait 200 ms before the next click), the menu's items, a frozen shape refusing and shaking, a delete leaving a group of one, nothing while a marquee is under way, another board's groups and coming back with nothing scanned), `groupQueriesTests` the word over tiles named by their labels and a pocket's board (its outermost group, file-wide, a group of one none, `readsOnlyTheFile`, the vocabulary, `[groups]` unknown). Selection's rules are SelectionTests' `groupSelectionTests`, over a fixture source of its own (`GroupsFixture`), never Grouping; draw's statements over a fixture writer are AutomationTests' `drawGroupTests` and `GroupDrawPlugin` GroupingTests' `groupDrawTests` (flat, a shape leaving a group of three, a nest flattened, `unchanged`, `ungroup`, a stale id); copies are ClipboardTests' `groupCopyTests`; CompositionTests' `drawGroupsShippedTests` hold `canvas draw` as built and `groupingShippedTests` the menu bar (⌥⌘G and ⌥⇧⌘G through `MenuBar` past Find Next, off while a note is edited) and the shipped menu (Group before Lock, a right-click selecting the group).
