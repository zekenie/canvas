# Transform

Transform: turning a shape into another kind under its own id: channels, the kinds that offer them and the kinds that take them, the menu item and the command, and a frame into a subboard.

Related: [frames](frames.md), [subboards](subboards.md), [containers](containers.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [locking](locking.md), [titles](titles.md), [mentions](mentions.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Transform](../../docs/plugin-system.md#transform).

## Architecture

- Transform (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait, on ContextMenus and SlashCommands; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Transform"): which kinds turn into which is a graph, and its edges are channels, so that a kind that offers one and a kind that takes it never name each other (a product module imports no other: the audit refuses even the string `"subboard"` in Frames). A `TransformChannel` (conform in an extension: `admits` has a default) has a `Source`, a `name` (the report's word: "container"), `admits(_:)` (which kinds may offer it, given a `TransformKind`: the kind, its definition and the registry; every kind by default) and `source(of:in:)`, what a shape coming through it hands the target, read in the transaction that turns it before the target changes anything, nil if it has nothing (the target's turn doesn't run). A kind offers a channel (`x.turnable(through: C.self)`, `registrar.turnable(K.self, through:)`; the `TransformSources` point, keyed by kind and channel, so offering one twice is a `conflict`; its `check` refuses a kind the channel doesn't admit, and every kind: `invalid`), a target takes it and says how (`registrar.turnable(into: P.self, through: C.self, label:) { source, transaction in … }`; the `TransformTargets` point, keyed by the kind turned into, so a kind takes one channel and a second claim is a `conflict`, as a second channel into one kind would share its menu item and its command; never every kind), and a shape can turn into each target that takes a channel its kind offers (`registry.turnTargets(of:)`: plugin order, never its own kind). The core knows no such graph, and lends only `transaction.turn(_:into:)` and `transaction.move(_:toBoardOwnedBy:)` ([editor](editor.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md)) for a target's closure to turn with; it changes the document only through the transaction it's given, and what it throws commits nothing.
- The container channel (`ContainerChannel`, "container"; `admits` a kind whose definition is `ShapeContaining`: [containers](containers.md)) hands the target a `TurningContainer`: its `id`, its `frame` and `inside`, read when its turn comes. `inside` is what's in it at any depth (fewer by what a container inside it that turned before it, innermost first, took onto its own board); the arrows bound to some of those and to nothing else (an arrow between two of its notes) go with them when they move, which is the core's rule for any move (`move(_:to:)`: [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md)), not the channel's; one bound to anything that stays, the container included, or to nothing, stays. Nil for a shape that isn't a container the core goes by or has no frame.
- A target's sugar brings, besides its `TransformTargets` entry, Turn into <label> in the context menu and `/turn-into-<kind id>` as a command. The item is an item for the canvas (as Show What Mentions is, since whether a shape can turn is what the registry knows, not the record), in `TransformMenuGroup` (order 60: after Mentions' 50, before Cut, Copy and Paste's 100), shown when every shape of the menu `canTurn` into the kind; the label is what the kind's schema is titled (`Subboard`), else its id, unless given. The command is every shape's (`slashCommandsForEveryShape`) on the kinds that offer the channel, the target's own apart, so it's `/turn-into-subboard → every shape` in the report, in plugin order like every shape's (the target's plugin's: Subboards', before Titles' `/title` and Locking's `/lock`), takes no arguments, and needs a kind id that can be typed as a name (else `invalid`, from `ShapeSlashCommands`). The undo step is named as the item is ("Turn into Subboard": [slash commands](slash-commands.md) name theirs in title case for it). `TransformPlugin()` contributes nothing but its service, and a list with a kind that offers a channel or takes one needs it (`ownerNotInstalled`); a target needs `ContextMenusPlugin()` and `SlashCommandsPlugin()` too, which its item and command belong to.
- `TransformState` (`editor.transforms`, a service; nil without the plugin) keeps nothing per shape (each kind's targets are worked out once, the registry being frozen, and `boardDidChange` is overridden with nothing): `targets(of:)` (the kinds a shape can be turned into now; none for a shape off the board shown or kept aside), `canTurn(_:into:)` (a read-only shape can: turning it is refused, and it shakes) and `turn(_:into:)`, which turns those that can, each once, innermost first (the deepest in containers, then back to front), so a container inside another has moved what it held by the time the outer one reads what it holds, in one `editor.transact("Turn into <label>")`: a shape that's read-only (locked, in a locked container, or on a locked subboard's board: `transaction.isReadOnly`) is refused before its target runs, the target turns the rest (none once one is refused: the turn will be thrown away), a turned shape that hangs over the container it's in (by its laid-out frame, read from `transaction.shapes` after the turn) is shifted back inside it, `Settlement.gap` in, where it fits (it keeps its container: the in-or-out rule judges by area and would put one mostly outside out), and `transaction.settle(turned)` puts what turned down as a placement is (nudged clear of another container it reaches; what moved glides there, `editor.glide`; a read-only container that would take one refuses). All or nothing: any shape refused (those, what the target would move, `transaction.refusedShapes`, a container that would take one) throws out of the transaction, so nothing changes and no step is left, and the refused shapes are heard (`editor.refuseChanges(to:)`: Locking shakes them); a target that throws turns nothing either. It returns whether it turned any.
- The first edge is frame → subboard. Frames only offers (`frame.turnable(through: ContainerChannel.self)`, the last of its opt-ins); Subboards takes it, after its shape block: `registrar.turnable(into: Subboard.self, through: ContainerChannel.self) { container, transaction in guard transaction.turn(container.id, into: Subboard(frame: CGRect(origin: container.frame.origin, size: Subboard.defaultSize))) else { return }; transaction.move(container.inside, toBoardOwnedBy: container.id) }` (the guard because a refused turn leaves a frame, which owns no board, onto which `move` moves nothing). So the subboard is 240 × 160 at the frame's top-left corner and keeps the frame's id: what mentions or is bound to it, its place in the stack, the frame it's in and its title (an added property both kinds hold, so it carries over) go on as they were; everything it held goes onto its board under the same ids and in the same places (frames inside stay frames there, holding what they held, and a subboard inside takes its board with it), on top of whatever that board held; what's bound only to what moves goes along (a locked one of those, which a move would leave behind with both ends let go, is named among what moves, so the turn is refused whole and the arrow shakes, as for a locked note inside), a binding across the edge is let go of as a cut's is (an arrow's end stays where it's drawn), and names (mentions) are kept. An empty frame becomes an empty subboard, with no board kept for it. Selected together, a frame and the frames inside it all turn, in one step, so nested frames all become subboards. Not built: the reverse (subboard → frame), a transform in `canvas draw`, a `/` typed on the canvas; no shipped kind that has text offers the channel, so `/turn-into-subboard` is typeable nowhere in the app yet.
- Shipped: `TransformPlugin()` right after `BoardsPlugin()`; the report has a `transform` line (`service TransformState`), `offers channel container → frame` last on `framing` but for its Create Frame item (`CanvasContextMenu`), and `takes channel container → subboard · CanvasContextMenu · /turn-into-subboard → every shape` last on `subboards`.

## Testing

- TransformTests use fixtures only, as Transform knows no shipped kind: CanvasTesting's corrals (they hold shapes, as a frame does) offer the container channel and turn into pockets (they own a board, as a subboard does), with a target that mirrors Subboards' (`corralsTurnIntoPockets()`), `Tagline` an added property both kinds hold (a title), boxes turn into discs through `BoxChannel`, a channel of the tests' own (only a box may offer it, and it hands over a box's id if it's narrow), which holds the mechanism apart from containers, and `LooseChannel` admits every kind; `transformHarness` has Keyboard, Selection, ContextMenus, SlashCommands, Transform, the fixtures, Links, Naming and Freezing. They hold registration (the report's entries, the graph in plugin order, conflicts, a channel's `admits`, `ownerNotInstalled` and `unknownKind`, a command name that can't be typed), resolution (`targets(of:)`, `canTurn`: not for a shape of the target's kind, off the board shown, kept aside, of a kind that offers nothing or another channel), the menu (a right-click: the item in its band, choosing it, two nested corrals selected turning innermost first, no item for a corral and a box, one item for each target a shape has, a source that says no), the command (offered where it should be, run headlessly as one step named as the item is, no arguments), all or nothing (a frozen box in the corral, a frozen corral, one in a frozen corral, one on a frozen pocket's board, the corral heard refused and thawed above it turns, one deep in nested corrals, a target never run for a read-only shape, a target that throws: no change, no step, and the frozen shapes heard refused), the in-or-out rule and bindings (a link inside rides, one across lets go, a name is kept). FramesTests' and SubboardsTests' `TurnTests.swift` hold each end of the first edge over a partner of its own, since neither can use the other: Frames offers the channel with a pocket as the target (the same how, a title carried over, a frame inside still a frame holding its box, a Turn into item and command only once something takes the channel, a locked box or frame refusing, an empty frame); Subboards takes it from corrals that offer (the size and the corner, the title, what the corral held on its board, a link inside riding and one across let go, undo and redo, the table of contents, Return entering it, `/turn-into-subboard`, nested corrals selected together, an empty corral). CompositionTests' `transformShippedTests` (`TransformShippedTests.swift`) hold the shipped pieces together in the app's plugin list, with a pasteboard of its own: a frame titled Groceries, holding two notes and a frame that holds a note, a note beside it that mentions it and arrows bound to the notes, turned from its menu (the item between Show What Mentions and Create Frame, before Cut; the subboard, its title, its board; the mention live, and the row, the title's pill and the panel's tabs a subboard's; containment as the tree it was; an arrow between the notes riding along and one to the note outside let go of its end; the canvas drawn without what moved, and as it was after Undo), `/turn-into-subboard`, a locked note or a locked frame leaving it all as it was (the locked note shakes), a locked arrow joining two of the frame's notes leaving it all as it was (the arrow shakes), a frame and the one inside it selected together (and a note it moves, selected too, leaving the selection), a frame on a subboard's board turned there (its board one deeper in the file, and Undo from the root showing the board it was made on), the frames file's Roadmap (a frame holding a frame, a subboard with a board of its own and a type note) turned with every board in its place and its locked neighbor refusing, and the file after a turn read back through `BoardCodec` and written again byte for byte; the registry report (ShippedTests, and the guide's copy of it) pins the three entries above.
