# Undo

The core's undo: steps, groups and coalescing, `DocumentDelta`, one history across a file's boards, and testing it.

Related: [editor](editor.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [boards on disk](boards-on-disk.md), [selection](selection.md), [editing](editing.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Undo](../../docs/plugin-system.md#undo).

## Architecture

- Undo (the core's, `editor.undoHistory`, `docs/plugin-system.md` "Undo") records every commit into steps, tldraw's model, not `NSUndoManager`'s: a step is an action (a press to its release, a key on the canvas with its repeats while it's held, until it's let go (`editor.keyUp`, `keysReleased`) or anything else happens, so a held arrow key's nudges are one step, a command, `undoHistory.group("Name") { … }`; nested, the outermost counts), an editing session (with the action that began it: placing a note and typing is one step), a run of `transact(coalescing: key)` commits at no action's depth (a panel field typed into, until another commit, an action or `undoHistory.seal()`), a card's commits (`transact(coalescing: card.id)` while a card with a `stepName` shows, `editor.showCard`; from the card showing until it closes: `undoHistory.keep(_:named:)` and `letGo()`, the editor's alone, and `willCommit`'s branch for the kept key between editing's and the run's; a card made with `adoptsPress` and shown inside an action (`keep(_:named:adopting:)` remembers `depth > 0`) has `actionEnded` turn the open `.action` step's holder into `.kept(key)`, a branch after editing's, so the step stays open past the release with the action's own name and the card's commits join it, and `letGo()` (the card closing, an undo, another card) clears the flag and closes the step; an action beginning, a save, a commit from elsewhere end it as they end any kept step, and the card's next commit opens another; `seal()` and `BoardWindow`'s mouse-downs and key moves do not end it, an action beginning, any other commit, a save (`breakStep`) and the card closing do, and undo closes the card first, so the step undone is the card's; a keyed commit inside a press or a group is that action's step), or else one commit. What a step finishes with: `undoHistory.finishing(key) { … }` (public; `willFinish(key)` asks) runs a body as the step open now closes, whatever closes it (a held key let go, another action, Undo, another board shown, a save), as an action of that step (`close()` makes the holder `.action` and the depth one deeper around it), so its commits are the step's last; one a key a step (asked again, the later body replaces it), nothing with no step open (it answers false), and dropped where the step closes inside a commit (`editor.canTransact` false, or `willCommit` under way: the transaction has built its document, and its commit would write over what the finish committed). So a transaction on the board shown whose commit would close a step with something to finish (`willCommit`'s `opening(coalescing:origin:)`, the one rule for what a commit opens) closes it before its body reads the document (`undoHistory.willTransact`): a commit from elsewhere while a key is held (`canvas draw` waits only for a press) settles the nudges first, in their step, and is a step after them. Selection's nudges and key resizes settle so, once, as the key is let go, never between its repeats ([selection](selection.md)). A step that ends where it began, or whose changes were all refused, is dropped; opening one clears redo. It's named by its outermost group, else its first named transaction (`transact("Move")`), else, for an editing session, what its kind's `Editable.editingStepName` says, else "Typing". A transaction's body may name its step once it sees what it did (`transaction.stepName`, nil until set; `transact(_:)` and `transact(on:_:)` hand it to the step in place of their own `name`, and a group's name still wins): Delete and Cut name theirs for what went along ("Delete Note and 2 Arrows": [selection](selection.md), [clipboard](clipboard.md)), so Edit › Undo says what an Undo brings back. `editor.undo()`/`redo()` (`canUndo`, `canRedo`: not while a session runs) end editing, close the open step, and apply its `DocumentDelta` (each touched record before and after, exact: keys, meta, added and kept values, opaque records) as an ordinary commit with an exact `ChangeSet` (whose `before(_:)` reads the board shown and the boards the step holds as they were before it's applied), below the read-only rule (undoing Lock unlocks, no shake), never through a transaction (no service may transact while it hears any commit: [editor](editor.md)); `UndoParticipant` services get back their snapshot (Selection's selection: undoing Delete reselects); the camera never moves (a board shown for a step has the camera it was left with). The core claims `.undo`/`.redo`, Keyboard gives them ⌘Z/⇧⌘Z. A file has one history, whatever board is shown: a step is made on the board shown (`DocumentDelta.board`; a switch closes the one under way and is no step, nor is entering or leaving a board), and undoing or redoing one from another board shows its board first (`showBoard`), then applies it there, its selection with it. A step holds every other board it reached whole, before and after (`DocumentDelta.boards`, and `unreadableBoards`; a board is a value that shares what a commit did not write, so holding it costs the chunks that commit wrote: [editor](editor.md)): the boards a deletion took under a shape, a paste brought (`insert(_:contents:)`), a move took shapes onto or settled them on (`move(_:to:)`, `settle(_:on:)`, the root too), a write changed shapes on (`transaction.update(_:on:_:)`, `translate(_:by:on:)`: a `willDelete` handler that takes back, on other boards, what the deleted shape gave does it in the delete's own step, so one undo, and one redo, put every board back exactly and services hear each one's change; `transaction.delete(_:on:)`, a delete on a board that isn't shown, in the step of the board that is), or Delete Hidden Shapes deleted from (`ChangeSet.boardsChanged`), so undoing a deletion brings back every board under the shape with every id, and undoing a paste takes its boards out; each board a step changes gets a new revision, so nothing kept of it is taken back stale. `DocumentDelta` is the one before/after type and `CanvasEditor.apply(_:restoring:)` the one place a step is applied. `replaceFile` (and `replaceDocument`) clears the history.

- A rehearsal's changes taken in (`editor.adopt(_:named:byAgent:)`, `CanvasEditor+Rehearsal.swift`; [editor](editor.md)): everything a rehearsal of the editor committed, on every board and to the file's values, is one commit and one new step named as asked: `DocumentDelta(from:to:on:)` (the board shown shape by shape, every other board that differs whole, the entries that didn't read as boards, the file's values that differ) applied as undo applies a step (`commit(_:changes:applying:)`, its `ChangeSet(delta)` marked `byAgent`, not `restoredStep`), recorded as a commit is (`willCommit`, `didCommit`: a single step, which clears redo), the participants keeping what they hold; a step that finishes with something closes first (`willTransact`); refused, changing nothing, for a rehearsal of another editor, a file changed since it was made, or while nothing may commit. `canvas apply` is its caller ([drawings](drawings.md)). CanvasTests' `adoptTests`.
- A file value ([properties and schemas](properties-and-schemas.md), File values) set in a transaction (`setFileValue`) is in that commit's step with whatever else it changed: `DocumentDelta.fileValues` holds each one a step changed, before and after (`DocumentDelta.FileValue`, nil where the file held none), the builder noting it from before the first commit that names it (`ChangeSet.fileValues`), and `CanvasFile.apply` puts it back or again. A step of file values alone names no shape and reaches no board; it is still made on the board shown (or `transact(on:)`'s), so undoing it from another board shows that board first. A run of commits that ends with the value it began with is dropped like any step that ends where it began, and undo and redo say `fileValues` in their `ChangeSet` with `restoredStep`.

- A row typed in a note that is one (Types' rows editor) commits through the field writer, `transact(name, coalescing: FieldRunKey)`, which is no editing view's commit: it ends the words' `Typing` step (the editing session's step) and opens a run named for the field (`Change Email`), which Tab to another row, `seal()` (a click, the keyboard changing owner) or any other commit ends; the next word typed opens another `Typing` step. So words, a row, words are three steps, and typing in one row is one. Typing a value in and deleting it all nets to nothing (no step); ⌘Z with the caret in a row is the field's own text undo first, as in the words.

## Testing

- CanvasTests' `takingTests` hold `stepName` (the step takes it in place of the transaction's name, a group's name wins, none leaves the name, on a board that is not shown too), the cascade's undo and redo as whole files and `BoardCodec` bytes, and CompositionTests' `undoTests` Edit › Undo's title through `BoardWindow.validateMenuItem` ("Undo Delete Note and 2 Arrows" for a relationship type, "Undo Delete" for a note).

- CanvasTests' `CardTests` hold the card's step (`CardProbe`, CanvasTesting's, is the card's view): three commits under the key are one step named by the card, `seal()` between them changes nothing, a commit from elsewhere (no key, another key) ends it and the card's next commit starts another by the same name, a card with no step name holds nothing, `setCardStep`, `breakStep`, a group, a card shown inside a press, a card that adopts the press (one step for the press and the card after the release, named for the press; one Undo after the card closes, or an Undo that closes it; a group is an action too; `breakStep`, a commit from elsewhere and a new press split it as they split a kept step; a press that changed nothing holds nothing; not adopting, shown outside a press, or closed before the release, as without it), a commit on another board, an editing session's typing around the card's commits.

- A step recorded on a board that isn't shown (`recordStep(on:)`, `transact(on:)`) closes the open step first when its builder holds that board or any board the commit reaches (`Builder.holds(_:)` of the board and of `changes.boardsReached`, changed, added or removed: a card's kept step that reached it whole), so a step recorded on a board meanwhile is never inside the open one's before and after; CardTests hold it (undo, undo, redo, redo, for a write, a delete and an owner taken on a third board), and the far delete under a card's key (one undo). CanvasTests' `DeletingAcrossBoardsTests` and `AcrossBoardsCommitTests` hold `delete(_:on:)`: what the commit says (`added`, `changed`, `removed`, the boards) and undo and redo as whole files and `BoardCodec` bytes, however the boards cross.

- CanvasTests' `WritesAcrossBoardsTests` hold a `willDelete` handler's writes on other boards in the delete's step (one undo and one redo, round trips with `BoardCodec` bytes, what services hear, a board shown before, a board that goes with the delete, read-only), and any transaction's.
- CanvasTests' `fileValueTests` hold a file value's steps: alone, with shapes in the same transaction, coalesced under a key, from a board that isn't the root and from `transact(on:)`, a throw, a write that changes nothing, and the delta built and applied by hand.
- For undo, `h.editor.undo()`/`redo()` and `undoHistory.undoName` headlessly, `undoHistory.seal()` between panel writes where a user would click elsewhere; compare round trips with `==` and `BoardCodec` bytes. Change counts are synchronous (no run-loop spin for `isDocumentEdited`). For Edit › Undo, use a `BoardWindow` with `window.editor` set: `validateMenuItem` for the title, `window.undo(nil)` to run it (CanvasUITests' BoardWindowTests, CompositionTests' UndoTests).
