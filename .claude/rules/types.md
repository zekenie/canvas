# Types

Types: a shape that holds a JSON Schema is a type, named by its title; making one (`/is-a type`) and taking it back (Remove Type); the file's types and what is what (`TypeIndex`); what a shape is (`is`, the `is-a` edge); its words and its schema kept saying the same thing by the model (`TypeSync`); references between types; the Schema tab's JSON, its completions and issues; highlights of its words, and their popovers; and their tests.

Related: [adornments](adornments.md), [topology](topology.md), [language models](language-models.md), [prose maps](prose-maps.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [titles](titles.md), [publishing](publishing.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [validation](validation.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [editing](editing.md), [shape references](shape-references.md), [find](find.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Types](../../docs/plugin-system.md#types-shapes-that-hold-a-schema).

## Architecture

- Types (a concern, on Adornments, Canvas, ContextMenus, DetailPanel, Find (a note that is one has its values found: `FieldFindPlugin`), LanguageModels, Mentions, Placing, ProseMaps, Selection, SlashCommands, TextEditing (the rows' one-line field), Titles, Topology and Validation; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Types") knows no kind. A kind opts in with sugar its plugin calls: `typeWords(_:drawnIn:fitting:)` hands Types its words (a key path to its `MentionText`, the placement its render draws them in, and what a write of them changes besides: a note's height), a contribution to the `TypeWords` point (one per kind, its `words`, `placement` and `write`, the last a transaction's update that fits the shape as the kind fits it), and `canBeType()` opts the kind into `TypeSchemaProperty` (key `schema`, a `SchemaText`), which the registry refuses without `typeWords` (`TypeWords.check`). StickyNotes calls both, so a note is the shipped type; TypesTests' `Memo`, a box of words of the tests' own, shows Types needs no note. A shape is a type exactly while it holds `schema` (`record[TypeSchemaProperty.self] != nil`); nothing else marks it, and Type is in no list of a shape's types. Its name is its title (Titles' `TitleProperty`, `registry.title(of:)`): a type's schema keeps no root `"title"` of its own, and one the person types in the JSON is kept as typed and never the name. `SchemaText` is one string in a board, kept as typed (its order and half-typed text survive), and `ShapeNaming` through `urn:uuid:` `$ref`s (`SchemaReferences.refs(in:)`, `uri(of:)`, `target(of:)`, read in any case, whole or with a fragment), so a copy of a type with the types it refers to refers to the copies and one alone to the originals, and `canvas draw` warns of a `$ref` to a shape not in the file; the key `schema` is an ordinary id to the audit (`ORDINARY_IDS`), a board's own old member. `Fixtures/added/schema.v0.json` is its fixture.
- Making one: `/is-a type` (the `Type` row of `/is-a`'s list, below) or `editor.makeType(_:)` (`makeTypes(_:)` for several, one transaction), one step "Make Type", on a shape that holds the property and none yet. Its schema is the starter (`TypeSchemaProperty.starter`, an object with no properties) unless the shape's words are a JSON object (`TypeWordsSchema.schema(fromWords:)`: nil if the words hold a mention, are empty, or trimmed are not an object; an array, a number or text get the starter; `{}` is a schema), in which case the schema is the words, trimmed of whitespace at both ends and otherwise as written, with no model call, and the sync then writes the words from the schema: `TypeSync.changed`, on a shape made a type by the person, sets the last-synced sides to the words and `{}` when the words equal the schema, so the next commit finds the schema ahead of the words and asks for the words (the `toWords` direction), and with `{}` words, which say nothing, nothing is asked. A type does not start public (it is found by `/is-a` and by its title; `/public` is the person's to say, on a type as on any shape). `editor.removeType(_:)` (`removeTypes(_:)`), one step "Remove Type", takes the schema away and leaves the words, the title and whether it's public; undo brings the schema back. Each plural form leaves out the shapes that cannot take the change and transacts nothing when none is left; a read-only shape stays in, so the core refuses it and it shakes while the rest change. Is a ▸ in a shape's menu is the other way. An agent makes a type with draw's `schema:` (an added property's attribute, as every one is): a note drawn with it, named by its title.
- The file's types: `TypeIndex` (`editor.types`, an `EditorService`) reads every board's types as the file opens (the kinds that `canBeType` only, a lookup a shape), then keeps them by each commit's `ChangeSet` alone (added, removed, changed, `elsewhere` with the kind first, `replacedDocument`, and a whole file put in place, `boardDidChange`'s `isFileReplaced`): `isType(_:)`, `types`, `name(of:)` (its title), `revision(of:)` (moves when its schema or its name does) and `onChange(_:)` (the ids that came, went, or whose schema, name or own types changed, after each commit that did), `schemaText(of:)` and `chainRevision` (internal: the stamp the field book keeps its resolutions by, moved when a type comes, goes, its schema's text changes or its own `is` list does, at one integer add; not for a rename, an instance's list or another shape's text). It reads nothing per frame and nothing on a move. `referenceTargets(from:)` is a `$ref`'s completions: every other titled type, by name, its fields counted, in the order of the names. `popover(at:on:origin:)` is an editing view's way to its highlights' popovers.
- What a shape is: `is`, an added property (`IsAProperty`, key `is`, an ordered `[TypeLink]`, none twice: `uniqueItems`; empty is none, so emptying it takes the property off), on the kinds that call `typeable()` (it needs `typeWords`, `TypeWords.check`): a note and an arrow. `TypeLink` is a one-field wrapper of a `ShapeID`, `ShapeNaming`, coded by hand as the bare uuid string, `contentMediaType` `text/x-canvas-type` (`TypeLink.mediaType`, what draw and queries read the entries by, later): a name, not a binding, so a copy of an instance copied with its type is an instance of the copy, one copied alone stays an instance of the original, and a type that is deleted leaves its name behind (its chip dashed) until the delete is undone. `ShapeID` itself must not be `ShapeNaming` (an arrow's bindings are ids). The order is the order added: a later entry wins where two say the same (a later slice reads it). `Fixtures/added/is.v0.json` is its fixture. Types declares the `is-a` edge type (`IsAEdge`, a root, "from a shape to each type it is, in the order of its list") and derives it with Topology's property rule (`registrar.edges(in: IsAProperty.self, as: IsAEdge.self)`: slot `is`, from the instance to each type, a name of a pill forwarded to what it stands for, a move reading nothing). `build.sh`'s `ORDINARY_IDS` holds `is`. A kind that is `typeable()` says where its words are, as one that `canBeType()` does: an arrow's `typeWords` places its label against the shapes around it (`typeWords(_:drawnIn:fitting:)` has an overload whose `drawnIn` is given the `ShapeContext`; the contribution's `placement` takes it).
- What a shape holds for its types' fields: `fieldValues`, an added property (`FieldValuesProperty`, a `FieldValues`: one JSON object over `JSONObject`, coded as the bare object) on the kinds that call `typeable()` (it opts in beside `is`; `isTypeable` still means "holds `is`"). A value that is empty is not kept, at any depth (`FieldValues.pruned`, the one rule: null, text with no characters, a list left with no items and a group left with no members once their own empties are dropped; `false`, `0` and text of spaces are values), and every way in prunes (`init(_:)`, `init(from:)`, the subscript; `values` is `private(set)`), so `isNone` is `isEmpty` and a board read, a draw or a set that is only empties holds none. Written by people and agents, never `readOnly`; `canvas draw`'s `fieldValues:` replaces the whole object (a statement naming it), `null` takes it off, an edge takes it; a query reads a path into it (`[fieldValues.status="paid"]`). Nothing reads a type for it: it knows no field, so a value no type names is kept. Not `ShapeNaming` yet. `Fixtures/added/fieldValues.v0.json` is its fixture. Beware `JSONValue` is `ExpressibleByNilLiteral`: a `nil` beside a `JSONValue` in a ternary is `.null`.
- `/is-a` (`registerIsACommand`, every shape that holds `is`: a note, an arrow in its label; Types' one command, after `title` and the other plugins' every-shape commands): a slash command that takes one choice (`SlashCommand(_:argument:choices:step:perform:)`, [slash commands](slash-commands.md); `argument` `type`). Its rows (`TypeChoices.rows(for:in:)`, in `TypeChoices.swift`, asked once when the list opens) are `Type` (group 0, title `TypesStyle.typeChoice`, symbol `curlybraces`, step "Make Type") only while the shape can be a type (`registry.canBeType`) and is not one, never on an arrow's label or a type, then the file's types the shape may be (`TypeIndex.choices(for:)`, group 1, the same symbol, step "Add Type"): every type for which `TypeIndex.admits(_:for:)` is true (a type, never the shape itself, one it is already, one that reaches the shape (`reaches`: no cycles), a pill (a stand-in kind), a shape that isn't there; the one private `mayBe` is where the custom shapes of a later slice join), on any board, ordered by name (lowercased once and compared as plain text, the id the tie: the locale's collation cost most of opening the list), an untitled one last and called `Untitled`; the first type is `isPreferred`, so with nothing typed it is the highlight, and `Type` is when there are none. A row's id is `type` or the type's uuid (`TypeChoice`). A pick runs at once: `Type` calls `makeType`, a type `addTypeLink(_:to:)` (the type goes at the end of the list, a later line wins, one step, "Add Type", on a shape on any board; `addTypeLink(_:to: [ShapeID])` for several shapes in one step); a read-only shape is offered nothing (the state), and the core refuses and shakes the one that became so. `editor.removeTypeLink(_:from:)` is the one way an entry goes (one step, "Remove Type", the property off with the last; the plural form for several), the chips' and the panel's Remove call it, so what a later slice settles when a type is given or taken back is settled inside these, in the person's own step. `@` typed on `/is-a`'s line is a letter (Mentions asks `listsChoices(at:)`). `/type` is gone.
- New type (`/is-a company`, TypesStyle `newType`, "New Type"): for what was typed, `/is-a`'s `otherwise:` offers the row `New type “Company”` (`TypeChoices.newRow(typed:owner:editor:)`, `TypeChoice.new(name)` in the row's `id`), which the list puts last, after the ranked matches, and not at all where a row is titled what was typed (`/is-a type` on a shape that has the `Type` row, `/is-a company` with a type called Company: case aside, spaces folded) or nothing is typed ([slash commands](slash-commands.md)); where nothing matches it is the only row and highlighted, with matches the best match stays the highlighted row (`/is-a co` with a Company lists Company and then `New type “Co”`, and Return picks Company). The name is the typed text folded to one line (`TitleProperty.folded`) with its first letter capitalized and the rest as typed, in the typographic quotes; none for nothing typed, a name over a title's 80 characters, the list's own word (`type`, case aside: on a shape that is a type already `/is-a type` offers nothing), no kind that can be a type, or one with no placement (`registry.placement(for:)`, read from a kind's tool). The pick is `editor.newType(named:besides:)` (public, discardable result): in `undoHistory.group("New Type")` it takes the kind (the owner's if it can be a type, else the first in registry order that can and has a placement), reads the board shown once for the landing beside the owner (`Surroundings(on:moving:in:).near(owner)`, as `/move-near` places one: right if free, then below, left, above), puts the placement down titled with the starter schema (`Placement.setting`, and `mapping` to learn the new record's id: the core's `place` is unchanged) and then links the owner (`addTypeLink`). The note is selected and edited as any placed note is, which ends the owner's editing, and the step stays open while the new note is edited, so words typed into it before that ends are in New Type and one undo takes them with the note and the link; a read-only board or a container that refuses it puts nothing down (the core refuses; nil, no link, no step, and the board's owner or container shakes), as does an owner not on the board shown. The new type is not public and its words are empty, so the sync has nothing to read until the person writes. TypesTests' `newTypeTests` (`IsALine`, Support.swift: `IsAView`, a text view that hosts slash commands over a memo, in an unshown window, so the real list is typed into and picked from) and CompositionTests' `typesShippedTests` hold it, and the gallery's `Flow2-p4-list-match-and-new-type`; `Budget.newTypePick` ([benchmarks](benchmarks.md)).
- Is a ▸ (`TypeMenus.swift`, `registerTypeMenus`: an item for the canvas, `MenuItems(in: StateMenuGroup, id: "is-a")`, so in Lock's group with no line between them, after it in plugin order): a submenu `Is a` in the menu of every shape the selection holds, made when the menu opens (`IsAMenu`). Items: `Type` (group `ShapeMenuGroup`, only when every selected shape can be a type), a line (the group `IsATypesMenuGroup`, order 1), then the types some selected shape is or may be (`TypeIndex.menuChoices(for:)`, in `choices(for:)`'s order), each titled by its name or `Untitled`. A check by an item every selected shape has (`Type`: all are types); there is no mixed state, so a type some have is unchecked and choosing it gives it to the rest. An unchecked item calls `makeTypes` or `addTypeLink(_:to:)` (steps "Make Type", "Add Type": one step for the whole selection), a checked one `removeTypes` or `removeTypeLink(_:from:)` ("Remove Type"). Never dimmed: a read-only shape is refused by the core and shakes while the rest change in the one step. The submenu is left out for a selection with a shape that holds no `is` (a frame, a subboard), and where it would hold nothing (an arrow in a file with no types). An arrow's menu has it with the file's types and no `Type`. A list that installs `TypesPlugin` installs `ContextMenusPlugin` too (the registry refuses a contribution whose owner isn't installed).
- A type's note is white paper (StickyNotes': `NoteStyle.typePaper`, white in the light appearance and 0.92 in the dark, the plain yellow note unchanged in both; the text's ink is the note's on both): its render and its skeleton fill it, the skeleton resolving the dynamic color once for the appearance drawn (`NoteStyle.typePaperCG`; [benchmarks](benchmarks.md)), and its cover's ink is chosen for it (`titlePillLook` with `coverSurface`). It is yellow again once the schema goes.
- A type's color (`TypeColors`, public; the table is `TypesStyle.colorTable`): one of eight by the id, never stored, so two types can share one; the index is the first eight bytes of the id's UUID, each summed with its position, modulo eight (never `hashValue`; TypesTests' `typeColorsTests` hold a table of ids that never changes, the spread, and the validator: white on each `base` at 4.5:1, each ink on its 14% tint over the light canvas (0.965) and the dark (0.11) at 4.5:1, none within 0.05 of red's hue, the eight distinct). `base` is fixed in both appearances; the ink follows the appearance. `TypeColors.solid(id)` is the pill's tint, `light(id)` the chips', `base(id)` the color alone, `gray` (0.40, `TypesStyle.grayChip`) what has no color. `/is-a`'s rows are chips (`SlashChoice.chip`, [slash commands](slash-commands.md)): `Type` and an untitled type gray, the others in their color, white on it, the `{}` first. A type's pill is Titles' (`registrar.titlePillLook(forShapesHolding: TypeSchemaProperty.self)`, [titles](titles.md)): its title after `curlybraces`, solid in its color; untitled, a dimmed gray `Untitled` placeholder (the neutral look every placeholder pill has); a note that holds no schema keeps the plain pill; Remove Type and its undo switch it.
- Chips (`TypeChips`, an `Adornment` and `ClickableAdornment`, aboveTopLeft, order 100, on every kind that holds `is`; a group, tagged): one chip per entry of `is`, in order, light (Adornments' `tinted(_:)`) in the type's color (`TypeColors.light`) with its type's live title (`TypeIndex.name(of:)`; an untitled type a gray placeholder "Untitled", no color), `.missing()` (dashed, italic, gray) for a type that is gone or no longer a type, with the last title it was known by, else "Deleted"; the ones that don't fit whole fold into Adornments' "+N" (gray). A type's note shows its own pill and then the chips of the types it builds on; there is no Type chip (Is a ▸ Type in the menu makes and unmakes one). The index tells Adornments to make the chips of a type's instances again when it is renamed, deleted, brought back or no longer one. A click (`TypeChipMenus.menu(for:in:)`, popped up below the chip's rect with `editor.popUpMenu`): on a type's chip `Go to “Person”` (`editor.goTo`, left out for a type that isn't in the file) and `Remove “Person”` (`removeTypeLink`, left out while the shape is read-only); on "+N", those for every type, a separator between types. Nothing is shown on a shape drawn as a skeleton. On an arrow the row sits above its label (Adornments' anchor).
- The panel's Types tab (`TypesTab`, order 50, a section "types" over `IsAProperty`, `enabledWhileReadOnly`): a row per type in order, a click on it goes to the type (`editor.goTo`, on the scheduler), a minus removes it (`removeTypeLink`; off while the shape is read-only), the row dimmed with the last title once the type is gone. A type that has supertypes has the Schema tab and this.
- Duplicating a typed shape (⌘D, a ⌥-drag, copy and paste, cut and paste: [clipboard](clipboard.md)) keeps `is`; copied with its type, the copy's names are renamed among the copies by the paste's one map (`TypeLink` is `ShapeNaming`), so the copies are the copy's instances and the original keeps its own; pasted into another file the names point at shapes it lacks (dashed chips).
- What is what: `TypeIndex` (`@Observable`, `editor.types`) also keeps `links(of:)` (a shape's types, in the order of its list; a name of a shape that is gone included), `instances(of:)` (the shapes whose list names it, directly), `lineage(of:)` (what a shape's types lead to, in the one order they are read in, `TypeOrder.linearized`: a type before every type it builds on, and where a list leaves a choice the later added first, which is C3, Python's method resolution order; where C3 has no order, as when a note was given Employee and then Person, a depth-first walk breaks the ties and the rule still holds, so Person never beats Employee; each once, never the shape itself, a cycle stopping where it comes back; a list is read latest first, `TypeGraph.parents(of:)`), `TypeGraph.reaches(_:from:)` (reachability alone, no order: what `admits` and the query ask), `affected(by:)` (the instances of the shapes, near and far, each once: what a change to a type changes) and `name(of: TypeLink)` (`TypeLinkName`: `.live(title)` while the shape is a type, else `.missing(lastKnown:)`, the last title it was known by in this session; observed per type). Kept by each commit's `ChangeSet` as the types are (a shape that only moved reads nothing: `recordsRead` in debug builds), read again for a file put in place and for a commit heard out of turn (`heardRevision`, as Titles' index does; `boardDidChange` always catches up), and it tells Adornments (`editor.adornmentsDidChange(for:)`) to make the chips of the instances of every type again whose name or being a type changed (renamed, deleted, brought back, no longer one).
- The chain (`Chain.swift`, pure: `Chain.resolve(types:in:)` over a `ChainCatalog` of `ChainNode`s, so one answer serves a shape, every shape with the same types, and a test with no editor): what an instance's types give it, as a `Resolved`. `order` is `TypeOrder.linearized` of the instance's list read latest first, less the shapes the catalog has nothing for (a gone name, or a shape that is no longer a type, ends its branch: its notes keep their words and show a dashed chip, while `:is-a` still follows its list). A field's winner is the first type in `order` to name it, and its whole `ChainField` is used (nothing merged, `required` with it, which is the root's own; a `required` name with no property names no field). `fields` is in row order: `order` reversed, each type's fields in the order written, a name taking the place it first appears at, so a field a more specific type restates keeps the more general type's place with the restater's definition (`source` the winner, `introducedBy` the type whose block the row sits in). A type cannot take a field away. `rules` are every type's root keywords across fields (`allOf anyOf oneOf not if then else dependentRequired dependentSchemas`, none overridden, in row order of their types), `directives` the root's `$title`-like properties, raw, one per name, the first in `order` (so an Employee gets Person's `$title`; nothing reads them yet). The root's `patternProperties`, `$ref`, `additionalProperties`, `unevaluatedProperties`, `propertyNames`, `minProperties` and `maxProperties` are none of these: they speak of the whole object, which holds every type's fields and values no field names, and those are never wrong. `schema` is the as-written composition (`properties`, the winners' `required`, `allOf` of the rules; what is empty is left out) for the check a later slice makes. A level is read from a type's JSON (`ChainLevel(schema:)`) with every `#` reference written out at the reading (`SchemaInliner`, over `SchemaPositions` alone: `enum`, `const`, `default`, `examples`, `contentSchema` and what is no schema are copied as written; siblings win over the target, the target's order kept; a reference to nowhere is dropped; one that leads back into itself or past six deep is dropped and the field's `holdsItself` set; `$defs`, `definitions`, `$schema`, `$id` and `$anchor` are left out of what is written). A `urn:uuid:` `$ref` (another type, bare or into a part of it) is kept as written: a bare one is "another type's fields", one with a fragment gives no rows (rows read it as Other), and the check answers both with the type's own composed schema. `Resolved.Field.field.schema` is a `SchemaValue`, so a group's rows and a type's card have their order.
- The field book (`FieldBook`, `editor.fieldBook`, an `EditorService` registered right after the index; it reads nothing as it is made, so `typeIndex10k` does not see it): `fields(types:)` (and `fields(of:)`, a record's `is` list) is the chain's `Resolved` for a list, resolved once for each distinct list as the shape holds it (the same types in another order are another list: the order decides who wins) and kept while `revision` (the index's `chainRevision`) stands. A list is resolved when first asked, never eagerly, so a type's edit costs the lists asked afterwards, not all: each valid keystroke in a type's Schema tab drops every kept list (the plan's named risk, held by `Budget.fieldListsResolved`'s second figure). A type's own note asks `fields(types: [itsID])`, the list an instance of it asks too. `members(of:)` gives the fields a group holds in order (its `properties`, its own `required`) or, for a field that is a bare `urn:uuid:` `$ref` to a type, that type's fields with their own sources, inherited first (empty for a type that is gone, a part of one, a pointer, anything else; a `$ref` with `properties` beside it shows the type's only), and `item(of:)` the field for a list's `items`, named "" (nil for none and for tuples); both are given a `Resolved.Field`, so slices that unwrap a schema (a "may be null" dropped) can ask of what they built. `ChainBook` (internal) is the catalog under it: each type's level read once per text through `SchemaReadings`' `shownRoot`, so a type whose JSON is broken mid-edit keeps the rows of its last good JSON (this session's: a file opened with a broken schema has none until it's mended, and undo back to a good text is that text's rows) and a type whose text was never an object has none; `prune()` forgets the levels of shapes no longer types. `resolutionsMade` (debug builds) counts resolutions, as `TypesPlugin.schemasRead` counts reads. It keeps nothing per shape or per board, so it hears a board switch with no change set (`boardDidChange` empty; the default would work one out, which BoardsTests refuses of every shipped service) and follows a file put in place through the index's stamp. A type that is gone, its name and its color are the index's, not the book's. Nothing here draws or checks.
- The sync: `TypeSync<Schema>` (`editor.typeSync` for a type's, `TypeSync<TypeSchemaProperty>`) keeps a shape's words and a `SyncedSchema` property (one whose value is a `SchemaText`: the property it writes, the prompts it sends, `SchemaSyncPrompts`, its update step's name, and whether the shape's name is the schema's, `namesTheShape`) in step through `editor.languageModel`, as the schema card's did: only the person's edits sync (not its own write, a board read again, a move, a lock, a rename, an agent's write, `ChangeSet.byAgent`, which is taken as in step), on a commit (editing the words ended however it ended, `editingDidEnd`, an edit still waiting committed the next turn; ⌘↩ or the keyboard leaving in the panel's JSON) or 3 s after the last (`TypeSync.idleDelay`, `editor.scheduler`), the wait held while the JSON's completion list is open (`completing`); words that changed rewrite the schema (whole: past 34 KB never sent, "Schema too long to rewrite", `TypeSyncFailure.tooLongMessage`), else a schema changed in meaning (its root `"title"` left out of the comparison) rewrites the words; never for text that isn't a JSON object, a read-only shape (`editor.isReadOnly(inFile:)`, asked as the sync starts and as an answer lands), or a shape with no fields and no words; with no model at all (a plugin list without LanguageModels) a side to write fails, saying `No language model` (`TypeSyncFailure.noModelMessage`, no Retry). A shape seen for the first time is in step, but words over a schema with no fields, never answered, are ahead; a shape the person makes a type (`/is-a type`) waits as an edit does and is committed on the scheduler's next turn (an undo or a draw in the make's turn lands first, and a shape that is no type then asks nothing): the read starts at once, with no 3 s wait. A rows' spot reads the sync through `onSpotChange` (told a shape's id for each status change and each flip of the wait), `isWaiting` and `hasAnswered` (its words at the last sync are not empty: a read has answered). The name: the type's title goes in as the root `"title"` of each schema sent (`TypeNaming.titled`, unless the schema has one the person typed), the model's comes out of what it writes back (`TypeNaming.untitled`), and an untitled shape is named by it in the same step. While a call is out the side it writes is read-only (the JSON, `detailUpdating`; the note's editor, read-only and faint, showing what lands); its answer is dropped if that side changed. Its write is a step of its own ("Update Schema", "Update Text"), on the shape's board shown or not (`transact(on:)`), the words through the kind's `TypeWords.write`; undo and redo start nothing (`restoredStep`) and the pair last synced goes back with the steps (`UndoParticipant`). Its status (`TypeSyncStatus`, `TypeSide` `.words`/`.schema`, `TypeSyncFailure` and its remedy) shows under the JSON ("Updating schema…", "Updating text…"). What it asks is `SchemaPrompts` (the file name, constant names and prompt literals byte for byte, since `scripts/schema-prompts` parses them; they still say "prompt" for the words): Haiku 5.5 at `medium`. The model never sees an id: each type the texts name is `@` and a word (`SchemaReferenceNames(words:schemas:name:isType:)`), a mention of a type its word and a `$ref` that word as its URI; a mention of a shape that isn't a type reads as its label, words. A rewrite of the words that reads like a preamble, a fence or `NO_CHANGE` inside text is unusable, unless the words it replaces read so themselves (`SchemaPrompts.parse(_:_:words:)`): a note that begins "Here are the fields" is a note.
- Readings: `SchemaReadings` keeps a reading per shape and text (`versionsPerShape`, 4; past 4,096 those used longest ago go; `TypesPlugin.schemasRead` counts reads in debug builds), its `shown` the last that was a JSON object while the text isn't one; `issues` are Validation's (`TypeValidationPlugin`, `validateEveryShape`): not JSON or not an object an error; `required` naming no property, a `type` that isn't JSON Schema's, and whatever else the meta-schema refuses (`MetaSchema` through the core's `SchemaValidator`, its own sentences, a `type` left to the rule of its own), warnings, 20 at most. `SchemaDocument`, `JSONReader`, `JSONCaret`, `JSONCompleter`, `JSONPointer` and `MetaSchema` came over from Schemas as they were. `SchemaValue` is a schema as it was written: `JSONValue` with an object's members in the order written (a name twice: the last value at the first's place; equality is order-sensitive, as a property moved is a change to its rows), `SchemaValue(node)` from a reading, `.json` for the core's validator (the one function, where `SchemaReadings` and the chain each had a copy). `SchemaPositions` (`named`, `definitions`, `single`, `lists`) is where a schema holds a schema: what walks one (`SchemaWalk`) and what rewrites one (the chain's reading of a level) go by it.
- The panel (`TypeDetailsPlugin`): every shape that holds the property has the Schema tab (`TypeSchemaTab`), its JSON section (`TypeSchemaCode`): `SchemaCodeEditor` (public, for any schema kept as a shape's text: colored by `JSONReader.tokens`, `SchemaCodeColors`; completed by `TypeCompletions.completions(in:at:types:extra:)`, the meta-schema's keywords with no root `title`, a `required` list's names, a `$ref`'s targets, its own definitions then the file's other titled types, and what a caller adds where a value goes, `extra`), its issues a status line each, and the sync's line (`TypeSyncLine`: Retry, Open Settings…, or nothing). Writes through it are one step a run, "Change JSON". The header is the kind's (a note's title).
- Highlights: a prose map of the words into the schema (`SchemaTarget`, its guide `SchemaPrompts.highlightsGuide`, `highlightsReferences` its context when the words name types), asked once the shape is drawn in full and in step, read through `ProseHighlights` (generic over a target whose addresses are JSON Pointers, keyed by shape and target: the texts as the model reads them, made once per text, and the map moved onto the drawn text, `MentionReading.drawnRange`; `forget(_:)`, `forgetEverything()` in debug builds). A kind's render calls `context.fillTypeHighlights(of:in:)` with the placement it draws its words in, between its fill and its text (a note's), which costs one added-value lookup on a shape that isn't a type: a field's pale blue, a rule's pale amber (`TypesStyle`). Resting the pointer on them shows that part of the schema, or of the type a `$ref` names, in the core's popover: on the canvas through Selection's `shapePopovers` (`TypeHighlights.popover(at:on:in:)`), in a note's editor through `EditContext.hoverPopover` (the editor asks `editor.types`). `TypeHighlights.placed(for:in:)` and `lines(for:in:)` read them for tests.
- Rows: see Rows on a type's note, below (`TypeRows.extent` is what a kind lays itself out with, `RowBook` provides it).
- StickyNotes: the note's render fills the highlights under its text; its editor (`NoteEditor`) follows the sync, read-only and faint while the model writes the words, showing what lands (`MentionTextView.show`, typing's undo dropped), ⌘↩ ending editing while read-only, and asks for the highlights' popovers as the pointer rests. Every list that installs `StickyNotesPlugin` installs `TypesPlugin` (the registry refuses an owner not installed).
- Composition: `TypesPlugin(), TypeValidationPlugin(), TypeDetailsPlugin()` right after `PublishingPlugin()`, so a note's commands list `title`, `lock`, `public`, `is-a`, then Moving's. The schema card, its tool, its `schema-link` channel and Schemas are gone: a board that holds a card keeps it aside.
- In draw and queries (DrawLexicon's `TypeNamesPlugin`, QueryLexicon's `TypeQueriesPlugin`; both depend on Types): `is: "Employee card"` on a shape or an edge is a `drawTextFormat` for `TypeLink.mediaType`: the title names a type as draw finds titles (`context.shape(called:admitting:)`, file-wide, or drawn in the same run, `writing` carrying the keys the statement writes, so a note given `schema:` there is a type), and what it admits is `TypeIndex.canBeNamed(_:registry:writing:)`, the one pure function over a record and the registry that says which shapes a script may name in `is` (a type that is no pill; the custom shapes extend it to frames holding `isShape`), anything else refused "which isn't a type or a shape"; it writes the id (the entry's coded form), and refuses the shape itself (`DrawTextContext.owner`, the shape whose attribute it is: "can't be its own type") and a shape already built on the owner (a cycle through shapes of the file, by the index's `graph.reaches`, read through `DrawTextContext.service`: "is already built on this shape, which would make a cycle"; a cycle between shapes a single script draws isn't checked, `reaches` is cycle-safe). A quoted value for a list of text is a list of one (Automation's `KindVocabulary.value(of:)`), several are JSON. Queries: `:is-a(S)` is `queryRelation(… reads: .file, transitive: true, namesTypes: true)` over `TypeFacts`, a `QueryFileFact` of `TypeIndex.graph`, a `TypeGraph` (`links` and `names`, copy on write, kept by the index beside its own tables, so a query takes it up in O(1)); `related` is `TypeGraph.parents(of:)` (a shape's types, later first), and `TypeIndex.lineage(of:)` is the same graph's `lineage(of:)`, the order rows are read in, where `:is-a` asks only reachability (`graph.parents`, transitive: no order). `:edge-to(S, is-a)` stays direct. A `queryTextFormat` for `TypeLink.mediaType` reads an entry as its type's title (`TypeGraph.names`; a gone type stays its id), so `[is~="Person"]` and a row's `is` read titles; `.Person` is refused toward `:is-a("Person")`.

## What rows, cards and the panel share

The vocabulary the rows, the card, the panel's Fields section and the check all speak, in `Sources/Types`, so they cannot disagree. All pure but the services; each file is small.

- `FieldPath` (`steps`: `.key` by the field's key, never its title, `.index` by the *stored* index; the list is dense, empties are pruned, so a card maps its display line to the stored index by counting the non-blank lines before it): `field`, `parent`, `isPrefix(of:)` (equal counts), `isRoot` (a problem across fields), `pointer` (JSON Pointer's escapes), and `init(violation:)` from a `SchemaViolation`'s path (the root is `FieldPath()`; `FieldPath([])` is ambiguous). `FieldName`: `words(of:)` is the field's own `title` trimmed of surrounding spaces, else its key in lower-case words (`lineItems`, `line_items`, `postalURL` is `postal url`), `stepName(of:)` the undo step of every write of that field, whatever the surface: `Change Line Items`. `FieldWords`: every string a row, card or panel says (`add gift note`, `No fields`, `+12 more`, `from Person`, `from 2 types`, `4 fields`, `2 items`, `edited as JSON in the panel`), beside `FieldKind.typeLabel`'s.
- `FieldKind` (`FieldBook.kind(of:)`, a pure function of the field's schema: it reads no other type, so a gone type still reads as `typeFields(target)`; a reader that wants it often keeps it): the first that fits, after a "may be null" is dropped: `holdsItself` is `other`; a pointer (`type: string`, `contentMediaType: "text/x-canvas-link"`, `contentSchema.$ref` a bare `urn:uuid:`; an array of them is `pointers`); a bare `urn:uuid:` `$ref` is `typeFields` (a fragment is `other`); `enum` of scalars or a `const` is a choice (a non-scalar one is `other`); an array (`choices` for `uniqueItems` over an `enum`, `pointers`, `listOfTypeFields`, `listOfGroups`, `list(of:)`, else `other`: no `items`, tuples, `prefixItems`, a list of lists); an object (`group` for `properties`, then `other` for `patternProperties`, `allOf`, `not`, then `pairs` for a plain `additionalProperties`); `boolean`, `integer`/`number`, a `date` format as `date`, a `date-time` one as `dateTime`, any other string as `text`; two branches (a `type` list, `anyOf`, `oneOf`) each a kind but `other` as `either`, three or more `other`; anything else `other`. `hasMore` is whether a row on a note ends in `›` and opens a card (everything but `plain`, `pointer` and an either of those); `opensOnType` is the type's note's own column, board 5 panel 7 cell for cell (`Choices`, `List of Text` and the links have no `›` there). `typeLabel(memberCount:)` is the kind's words on a type (`Either` past 22 characters or with a branch that is not plain). `field(at:types:)` names the field of a value path (`members(of:)`, `item(of:)`, `pairValue(of:)`, an either's first branch that has the step), `branches(of:)` an either's.
- `FieldValues.value(at:)` and `setting(_:at:)`: the pruning rule at any depth (setting empty takes the element out and the rest shift up; an index at or past the count appends; a parent that holds the other shape of value is replaced, but removing from under one changes nothing, and a path that starts with an index changes nothing). `keys(unnamedBy:)` is the members no field names, the panel's dimmed values.
- `FieldText`: what typed text is stored as, and how a value shows; every function takes a defaulted `locale:`. A number is stored as one only when it reads back as typed (`9.50` is 9.5, `12.00` is 12; `02134`, `1e3`, `1.`, `-`, a 17-digit id are text); a date is a full date however it is written (`2026-03-03`, `Mar 3, 2026`, `3 Mar 2026`, `3/3/26` in the locale's order), stored `2026-03-03`, and in a `dateTime` field (`format: date-time`) as UTC midnight, `2026-03-03T00:00:00Z`, which a date-time format check accepts; both forms show as the day; a part of a date stays text; nothing is refused. The locale's formatters are made once per locale and kept (a `DateFormatter` costs far more to make than to use), since rows draw dates every frame in view. `shown` of a value of another JSON type shows it as its own type would (the check is what calls it wrong). `value(from:asOneOf:)` and `shown(_:asOneOf:)` are for a field that takes either of two plain kinds.
- `FieldSummary.of(_:field:in:types:)`: what a row with more in it reads (a group leaves out a member edited as JSON: a sentence is not a value) (`empty`, `value`, `items`, `count`, `pair`, `json`; `pills` is slice 14's), `text` the same as one string. `FieldCheck`, `FieldProblem` and `FieldClass`: the check's answer as a value (`mark(at:)` the worst at or under a path, `problems(at:)` exactly there, `leaving(out:)`). `RowList` (`init(_:own:)`, `count(of:own:)`): nine fields or fewer all show; past nine, two rows or more inherited (introduced by a type not on the shape's own list) fold into one `from Person` row in the place of the first, and of what is left nine or fewer show, else eight and `+N more`: a fold shows only when it hides two rows or more, so a note has at most ten rows.
- The services other tracks fill are stubs, each registered after `TypeSync`, each with an empty `boardDidChange` in its `EditorService` extension (the default works out a switch's change set, which BoardsTests refuses of every shipped service), each reached through an accessor (`editor.fieldWriter`, `fieldChecks`, `fieldCards`; nil without `TypesPlugin`): `FieldWriter` is a plain transaction named for the field (`FieldName.stepName`) that coalesces each top-level field by `FieldRunKey { shape, field }`, answers false for a read-only shape (the core shakes it), a shape not on the board shown, a kind that is not `typeable()` and a path with no key, and true for a write that changes nothing (no step); `FieldChecks` answers `.clean` (`check(of:)`, and `fullCheck(of:)` with `typing` ignored, for queries) and stores `typing`; the step of removing a value no field names is `Remove Nickname` (the key's words, capitalised); `FieldCards.open` answers false; `FieldForm` shows the summary and `›`. `FieldCardRequest` is the card's whole request, a public init with `folded` and `rowRectNow`.
- The shared fixtures are `FieldFixtures` in CanvasTesting (strings and fixed ids only, so every test target reads the same: Person, Address, Employee, Order as board 5 draws them, `everyKind` with nine fields in each of two types, `wide(_:)`); `fieldEditor()` in TypesTests (a free function, also `FieldFixtures.fieldEditor()`) puts them in an editor (Employee a Person). The gallery's states are one array a track (`rowStates`, `valueStates`, `cardStates`, `checkStates`), beside the look's.

## Rows on a type's note

A type's note has, under its words, a hairline and one row for each field its chain gives it (`fields(types: [id])`, so an Employee with none of its own has Person's); a note that is one has its fields there with its values (below). Types owns it, a kind opts in, and the core learns nothing.

- A kind says where its rows go: `registrar.typeRows(drawnIn: { shape, context in … })` (`TypeRowsPlaces`, a `KeyedExtensionPoint`, one contribution a kind, `Registry.typeRows(of:)`; `check` refuses a kind that holds neither `schema` nor `is`) and its render calls `context.drawTypeRows(of:in:)` (StickyNotes: `NoteLayout.block`, after the highlights, before the editing guard, so the rows draw under the editor too). Only such a kind is given `TypeRows.extent`: an arrow says nowhere and has no rows.
- `RowList` is the fold, pure. `RowBook` (`editor.rowBook`, a service registered last in `TypesPlugin`, made with the others, reading no shape as it is made) keeps a template for each distinct (list of types, isType): the `RowList`, and for each row its name (`FieldName.words`), `FieldKind`, `typeLabel` and chip, whether it is needed, its tick and its measured name, and the name column's width; stamped with the index's `chainRevision` and dropped whole when it moves (a rename moves no size: only a template that says a type's name, `from Person`, is made again, by a rename count). `templatesMade` and `rowsShown` count in debug builds. `rows(of:)` is the rows as drawn with their rects (world units; tests, the gallery), `rowsRect(of:)` the kind's rect, `spot(of:)` what a type's note says where its rows go.
- The spot (`RowSpot`), in order, for a shape that holds `schema`: its template has rows: `.rows`; the sync's status is `.updating(.schema)`: `.reading` (three bars); `.failed`: the reason with `.retry`, `.settings` (a key missing or refused) or no action (`TypeSyncFailure.Remedy.editByHand`: too long, declined, no model); its words are empty: nothing (blank, 200 wide, no hairline); an edit is waiting and no read has answered (`isWaiting`, `hasAnswered`): nothing, so `No fields` never flashes under the first words typed; else `.noFields`. Rows win over a failure and a read in progress: a type with rows read again keeps them and the note says nothing of a failed read (the panel has it).
- The extent is `RowsExtent(minimumWidth: TypesStyle.rowsMinimumWidth (320), height: (hasWords ? rowsLead : 0) + rows × rowHeight (22))`, rows 3 for reading, 1 for `No fields` and a reason; none for a shape that is no type and has no types with fields. The provider (`RowBook.provide`) tests the cheapest first (no `schema` and no `is`: nil, two added-value lookups, which is every plain note), then the book's kinds; answered from the committed index when `reading.isCommitted`, else (a draw, a paste, a duplicate, `/is-a` itself, a type and its instance written together) resolved over `reading.shape(inFile:)` with no template and no text measured: a type's level is the field book's own (`FieldBook.level(of:)`) while the text is the committed one, so a type whose JSON is broken mid-edit gives a pasted instance the rows of its last good JSON, else read from the text once, by (id, text).
- Announcements: `RowBook` is told by `TypeIndex.onChange` and `TypeSync.onSpotChange`. A type's change that moves `chainRevision` drops the templates and calls `editor.shapesDidChangeLayout` once for the changed types, their instances near and far (`affected(by:)`) and the instances the commit itself added or changed (the index has filed them before it tells): a service ahead of Types may have asked for their frames against the index of the file before. A rename redraws. A status or wait changing for a type with no rows lays that note out again with no commit. An edit to a note's own record needs none: the core lets its size go. A file put in place drops the tables.
- `TypeSync` reports for the spot: `onSpotChange` (a shape's id for each status change and each flip of the wait), `isWaiting`, `hasAnswered` (its words at the last sync are not empty). A shape the person makes a type is committed on the scheduler's next turn (guarded), so the bars show at once with no 3 s wait; with no model at all a side to write fails with `TypeSyncFailure.noModelMessage` (`No language model`, no Retry).
- Drawing (`RowsDrawing.swift`): clipped to the block widened at the left by the tick's room (`tickInset` + `tickWidth`, in the paper's padding): the hairline (black at 12%), then the spot (bars at 10%, one row of text) or the rows: tick, name (the note's ink, 45% for an inherited row, 55% on a note that is one), the amber dot 5 after it, and from the right `›`, the chip, the label (an inherited row is faint all through: its label and `›` at the name's 45%, its chip drawn at that alpha). Text is Core Text lines kept by (text, style, width cut to) in `FieldLines` (8,192, then the lot is let go), fixed colors since the paper is light in both appearances; the chip is Adornments' (`AdornmentLabel.draw(at:in:)`, drawn as in the light appearance), built with `TypeChips.label` so untitled, gone and renamed types read as above a note. `RowGeometry` is the one place that says where a row's parts are (the name column is the longest name, at most `nameShare` (45%) of the rows' width, the same on a type's note and on a note that is one). Constants are `TypesStyle`'s (`rowsMinimumWidth`, `rowHeight`, `rowsLead`, `nameGap`, `nameShare`, `tickWidth`, `tickInset`, …).
- Clicks (`RowFaces.swift`, one `shapeFaces` Types registers; `RowFace { shape, row, part, at }`): nothing in a type's rows is edited. A row whose kind `opensOnType` (`›`) and a fold ask `editor.rowBook?.openCard` for a read-only card (a `FieldCardRequest`: `mode: .kinds`, `isReadOnly`, the row's rect, a fold's `folded` rows, `rowRectNow`, which finds the row again by its field or by which fold it is and answers nil once it is gone); the book does the asking for every door (`opened`, debug builds, is what it asked), and `FieldCards.open` is the card's, answering false until it lands. A click on the value of a `Choice` or `Choices` row pops `RowMenus.options` (the options in the schema's order, every item disabled) under it; `Retry` is `typeSync.retry`, or `refuseChanges` (the shake) on a read-only type; `Settings…` is `AnthropicSettingsView.showSettings()`, locked or not. Anywhere else a press is the note's. A face is asked on every move over a shape and costs the provider's lookups (a note that is one has the values' faces, below; never for the shape being edited or a skeleton). `doubleClick` runs the same for `›` and options, so the second press of a fast pair does not begin an edit, and does nothing for Retry and Settings…. `FailureLayout` is where the reason and its action's words go, for the render and for a click; `RowBook.actionRect(of:)` is the words' rect for tests and the gallery.
- While the words are edited (`RowsView`, `Sources/Types/RowsView.swift`): the note's editing view covers the paper, so a press over the rows would put the caret at the end of the words. A kind's editing view lays a `RowsView` over its rows (`RowBook.makeRowsView(for:host:)`, a `RowsHost` of the editing frame's origin, `end`, `keepInView`, `focusWords`; StickyNotes' `NoteEditor` adds it above its text view). It draws nothing but the focus outline (the render under it draws the rows), shows the arrow, does what a click on the canvas does for the part under the press, and nothing elsewhere in the rows: a press there moves no caret. On a note that is one it is the rows editor (below). The book keeps one weak (the session's) and calls `reposition()` after each commit and each layout change it announces; the note's editor does after a size change and a keystroke.
- Zoomed out (`fillTypeRowBars`, `showsTypeRowBars`, `RowBook.bars`): a kind's skeleton asks `context.showsTypeRowBars` (zoom at least `skeletonRowsZoom`, 14%, where a row is three pixels tall) before it works out where its rows are, then fills one bar a row (`skeletonRowBarHeight` 8, as wide as the name's columns by `SkeletonText.columns`, `FieldRowTemplate.barWidth`, never the measured width: no skeleton shows a letter, and SkeletonsShippedTests holds two boards that differ only in letters to one picture), a fold as three dots, the reading state as its three bars, `No fields` and a reason as one bar (`spotBarShares[0]` of the block), through `fillSkeletonText`. The cover title of a titled note is drawn by Titles over the bars, as it is over any skeleton.

## Rows on a note that is one

The same rows, with the note's values at the right (`RowShown.swift`, `RowsValuesDrawing.swift`): the name muted at the left, the value left-aligned in the column after it (`›` follows a summary), as board 5 draws them.

- `RowBook.rows(of:)` for a note that is one pairs its template with its `fieldValues` (`ShownRows`, kept by shape, 2,048 at most then the lot is let go; rects from the block's top-left so a drag lets go of nothing and `rows(of:)` after it adds the block's origin; let go of for the shapes a commit added, removed or changed other than by moving, and those of `changes.elsewhere`, whole with the templates, and when the width, the lead or the rename count it was built for moved; `rowsShown` counts the builds). Each row's `content`, by the field's kind: a plain value `.text` (as `FieldText.shown`/`FieldSummary` say it, kept to `longestShown` characters; the cut to the column is the drawer's), a yes or no `.box` (unticked for none and for `false`), a choice `.choice(label)` (`nil` for none, a value no option names as itself), either of two plain kinds `.text`, a link (slice 14's) `.text` of the stored name, anything with more in it `.summary` (a list or choices cut to the column with the rest counted, `gift, mugs +2`, by measuring; the rest as `FieldSummary.text`), `.empty("add gift note")` for none (no `›` while empty, D15), `edited as JSON in the panel` for what no control suits (empty or not), and `.gone(type)` for another type's fields, or a list of them, whose type is gone (its dashed chip, the value kept and not checked). A default fills nothing.
- Marks are not kept: each draw reads `check(of:)` once a note (`FieldChecks.check`, or `RowBook.checks` in debug builds, which tests set to draw marks before and beside the check, with `typing`'s path taken out of its answer as the real one does) and `mark(at:)` once a row (a fold: the worst of its folded fields). `RowLook.of(content:mark:)` is the whole of what a mark does to a row: `.wrong` is the red ink with a wavy line (1 point, a 4-point wave, 1.25 high, under the text), `.needed` on an empty row the same words in amber, `.neededInside` a summary or fold in amber, a box is never needed, a gone type is never marked. The text inks are the dot's amber and the wrong red darkened until each reads at 4.5 to 1 on a note's yellow and on white (`neededTextInk`, `wrongInk`; the dot stays `neededInk`). The value of the row `FieldChecks.typing` names for this shape is not drawn (the row's own field draws it).
- The outline of the row whose card is open (`FieldCards.openTarget`, a fold by `openFolded`) is drawn while the note is not edited: 1.5 points in the accent, round the value as it hugs it (`hug(_:)`, at least `fieldFocusMinimum`).
- Zoomed out a filled row has a second bar, `skeletonValueShare` of the value column (a box's width for a box), never as wide as what it says; none for an empty one, red (`skeletonWrong`) for a wrong one; a fold's dots are red when it carries a wrong one.

## Typing in a row

A note that is one has two places for the caret in its one editing session: the words, and a row. Types' `RowsView` (`makeRowsView`, which StickyNotes' `NoteEditor` adds over the rows) is the rows editor; Types imports TextEditing for it.

- **A typed value** (text, a number, a date, either of two such kinds) is edited by a `RowField` (`RowField.swift`): an `EditingTextView` on TextKit 2 with a container that never wraps, no autocorrect, smart quotes or dashes, replacement or spelling marks, no mentions and no slash commands (`@` and `/` are letters), laid over the value in a clip view as wide as the text hugs it (`fieldFocusMinimum` to the value's column, 4 points out each side) and slid so the caret stays in it. The view draws the outline (`fieldFocusWidth`, the accent). Its text is a text value as it is, a number or a date as the row shows it (`RowsView.text(of:kinds:)`); while it has the caret it holds what was typed (`9.50`), and the row shows the stored form (`9.5`) once it leaves. A value written from outside (an agent's draw, an undo) while the caret is there is shown in the field (the view remembers what it wrote and compares it to the stored value at each commit), and drops the field's own undo.
- **A line break** is never in the field's text: where a value has one (`\n`, `\r\n`, `\r`, `\u{2028}`, `\u{2029}`) the field has one space that carries `RowField.lineBreak`, the break it stands for; `RowField.value` puts each back. Three rules keep it apart: `typingAttributes` always answers the row's plain attributes (nothing typed beside a stand-in takes the attribute), every real break in the storage (only a paste or a drop brings one) is swapped for a stand-in after each change, and the field's undo carries attributes (it is plain text, and still does). A stand-in is deleted, selected and stepped over as the one space it shows as.
- **Each keystroke is a commit** through the one writer (`RowBook.write`: `FieldWriter.setTyped`, or `set` of `FieldText.value(from:asOneOf:)` for an either), so typing in one field is one undo step named for it (`Change Email`), another field another; the writer's run key does it. A row's commit ends the words' `Typing` step and the next word starts another (`undo.md`): accepted. Nothing is refused, clamped or changed (`FieldText`).
- **The check is told** which field is typed in: a field's becoming first responder sets `editor.fieldChecks?.typing = (shape, path)`; its resigning clears it, and only if `typing` still names that shape and path (the panel's boxes set it too, and only whoever sets it clears it), so does the view's going out of its window. A box, a choice and a summary never set it. The render skips the value of the typed row (the field draws it); `RowBook.check(of:)` takes the typed path out of an overridden answer as `FieldChecks.check` does of its own, so nothing under the caret is red.
- **Where a session starts**: `NoteEditor` takes `EditContext.startPoint` into the rows view (`begin(at:clickCount:)`): a press on a typed value gives the field the keyboard with the caret at the character pressed (a double-click, the word), one on a row with more in it focuses the row and asks for its card on the scheduler's next turn (only if the session is still this one), anywhere else (a name, the paper, a box, a choice) the words take it. `EditingView.firstResponder` is that responder. A click on the canvas that begins it is the faces' (`RowFace`, below).
- **Keys** (the table is in `RowsView.swift`): Tab and ⇧Tab, ↓ and ↑ walk every row, typed or not, folds included (Tab onto a row with more only focuses it); off the last row Tab ends editing, ⇧Tab and ↑ from the first go back to the words, ↓ on the last does nothing; ↓ from the words' last line (the caret first moves as it always has: if it stayed on its line there was no line below) focuses the first row; Return and Escape end editing, ⌘↩ too, but Return and Space on a row with more ask for its card (`openCard`, whose `closed` focuses the row again and whose `tabbedOut` goes on); Space toggles a focused box and pops a focused choice's menu (`RowMenus.choice`: the options in the schema's order, a check by the stored one, a separator, `None` last). A row that is not typed is focused with the view itself as first responder, outlined (a choice with `▾` after its value), and **swallows every key it has no use for**: up the responder chain are the canvas's shortcuts, where Backspace deletes the selection, which is the note being edited (`noteRowEditingTests`).
- **While the note is edited** a press in the rows is the rows': on a typed value it moves the caret there, on a box it toggles it and focuses the row, on a choice it focuses the row and pops its menu, on a row with more it focuses the row and asks for its card; on a name, the lead or the paper nothing, and the caret stays where it was. The pointer is the I-beam over a typed value and the arrow over the rest.
- **If the rows change under a focused row** the focus stays with the row of the same field (a fold by which fold it is); if that row is gone or folded the caret goes back to the words. A note that becomes read-only ends the session (the core's) and the view goes with it.
- **A click on a value acts at once** (`RowFaces.swift`: `RowFace.Part.value`, `.name`; `valuePart(at:on:)` finds them from the shape's kept rows alone, so a hover builds nothing it does not find kept). It selects the note alone and then, by the row's `RowClass`: a typed value, a row with more in it and the fold begin the note's session (`editor.beginEditing(shape, at: face.at)`, so `NoteEditor` starts it in that row as above); a box writes the other of ticked and not (`RowBook.toggle`); a choice pops `RowMenus.choice` below its value; a row that reads `edited as JSON in the panel` asks `openCard` and begins nothing. A click on a field's name, or anywhere in a row off its value, only selects the note, also when it is the only one selected (where a click elsewhere on it edits its words); its double-click is `editor.open(…, clickCount: 2)`, which edits the words. A box, a gone type's chip and a link are value faces only on the thing itself, and the last two (`.still`) have no value face: a click there is the note's. Their names, like every name, are `.name` faces, so a click on one only selects, also on the only selected note (a plain click on the note would edit its words). On a read-only note a typed value or a box or a choice shakes it and writes nothing (`beginEditing` and the writer refuse, `refuseChanges` for the menu), but a row with more opens its card to be read (`isReadOnly: true`, `closed` and `tabbedOut` nothing) and does not shake: reading is no try. `doubleClick` on a value is the click again (two clicks on a box are two writes), so the second press of a fast pair begins no edit by accident. The cursor over a value stays the arrow on the canvas; it is the I-beam only once the row is edited.
- **⌘F finds a value** (`FieldFind.swift`, `FieldFindPlugin` after Find and Types; Types depends on Find): a note that is one gives Find a text for each field with a value, as its row shows it, a row with more in it every text under it; a hit marks the row's value (the fold for a folded field). `RowBook.reached(by:)` is its `affected` and has the index hear the commit early; the rules for it are in [find](find.md).
