# Types

Types: a shape that holds a JSON Schema is a type, named by its title; making one (`/is-a type`) and taking it back (Remove Type); the file's types and what is what (`TypeIndex`); what a shape is (`is`, the `is-a` edge); its words and its schema kept saying the same thing by the model (`TypeSync`); references between types; the Schema tab's JSON, its completions and issues; highlights of its words, and their popovers; and their tests.

Related: [adornments](adornments.md), [topology](topology.md), [language models](language-models.md), [prose maps](prose-maps.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [titles](titles.md), [publishing](publishing.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [validation](validation.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [editing](editing.md), [shape references](shape-references.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Types](../../docs/plugin-system.md#types-shapes-that-hold-a-schema).

## Architecture

- Types (a concern, on Adornments, Canvas, ContextMenus, DetailPanel, LanguageModels, Mentions, ProseMaps, Selection, SlashCommands, Titles, Topology and Validation; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Types") knows no kind. A kind opts in with sugar its plugin calls: `typeWords(_:drawnIn:fitting:)` hands Types its words (a key path to its `MentionText`, the placement its render draws them in, and what a write of them changes besides: a note's height), a contribution to the `TypeWords` point (one per kind, its `words`, `placement` and `write`, the last a transaction's update that fits the shape as the kind fits it), and `canBeType()` opts the kind into `TypeSchemaProperty` (key `schema`, a `SchemaText`), which the registry refuses without `typeWords` (`TypeWords.check`). StickyNotes calls both, so a note is the shipped type; TypesTests' `Memo`, a box of words of the tests' own, shows Types needs no note. A shape is a type exactly while it holds `schema` (`record[TypeSchemaProperty.self] != nil`); nothing else marks it, and Type is in no list of a shape's types. Its name is its title (Titles' `TitleProperty`, `registry.title(of:)`): a type's schema keeps no root `"title"` of its own, and one the person types in the JSON is kept as typed and never the name. `SchemaText` is one string in a board, kept as typed (its order and half-typed text survive), and `ShapeNaming` through `urn:uuid:` `$ref`s (`SchemaReferences.refs(in:)`, `uri(of:)`, `target(of:)`, read in any case, whole or with a fragment), so a copy of a type with the types it refers to refers to the copies and one alone to the originals, and `canvas draw` warns of a `$ref` to a shape not in the file; the key `schema` is an ordinary id to the audit (`ORDINARY_IDS`), a board's own old member. `Fixtures/added/schema.v0.json` is its fixture.
- Making one: `/is-a type` (the `Type` row of `/is-a`'s list, below) or `editor.makeType(_:)` (`makeTypes(_:)` for several, one transaction), one step "Make Type", on a shape that holds the property and none yet. Its schema is the starter (`TypeSchemaProperty.starter`, an object with no properties) unless the shape's words are a JSON object (`TypeWordsSchema.schema(fromWords:)`: nil if the words hold a mention, are empty, or trimmed are not an object; an array, a number or text get the starter; `{}` is a schema), in which case the schema is the words, trimmed of whitespace at both ends and otherwise as written, with no model call, and the sync then writes the words from the schema: `TypeSync.changed`, on a shape made a type by the person, sets the last-synced sides to the words and `{}` when the words equal the schema, so the next commit finds the schema ahead of the words and asks for the words (the `toWords` direction), and with `{}` words, which say nothing, nothing is asked. A type does not start public (it is found by `/is-a` and by its title; `/public` is the person's to say, on a type as on any shape). `editor.removeType(_:)` (`removeTypes(_:)`), one step "Remove Type", takes the schema away and leaves the words, the title and whether it's public; undo brings the schema back. Each plural form leaves out the shapes that cannot take the change and transacts nothing when none is left; a read-only shape stays in, so the core refuses it and it shakes while the rest change. Is a ▸ in a shape's menu is the other way. An agent makes a type with draw's `schema:` (an added property's attribute, as every one is): a note drawn with it, named by its title.
- The file's types: `TypeIndex` (`editor.types`, an `EditorService`) reads every board's types as the file opens (the kinds that `canBeType` only, a lookup a shape), then keeps them by each commit's `ChangeSet` alone (added, removed, changed, `elsewhere` with the kind first, `replacedDocument`, and a whole file put in place, `boardDidChange`'s `isFileReplaced`): `isType(_:)`, `types`, `name(of:)` (its title), `revision(of:)` (moves when its schema or its name does) and `onChange(_:)` (the ids that came, went or changed, after each commit that did). It reads nothing per frame and nothing on a move. `referenceTargets(from:)` is a `$ref`'s completions: every other titled type, by name, its fields counted, in the order of the names. `popover(at:on:origin:)` is an editing view's way to its highlights' popovers.
- What a shape is: `is`, an added property (`IsAProperty`, key `is`, an ordered `[TypeLink]`, none twice: `uniqueItems`; empty is none, so emptying it takes the property off), on the kinds that call `typeable()` (it needs `typeWords`, `TypeWords.check`): a note and an arrow. `TypeLink` is a one-field wrapper of a `ShapeID`, `ShapeNaming`, coded by hand as the bare uuid string, `contentMediaType` `text/x-canvas-type` (`TypeLink.mediaType`, what draw and queries read the entries by, later): a name, not a binding, so a copy of an instance copied with its type is an instance of the copy, one copied alone stays an instance of the original, and a type that is deleted leaves its name behind (its chip dashed) until the delete is undone. `ShapeID` itself must not be `ShapeNaming` (an arrow's bindings are ids). The order is the order added: a later entry wins where two say the same (a later slice reads it). `Fixtures/added/is.v0.json` is its fixture. Types declares the `is-a` edge type (`IsAEdge`, a root, "from a shape to each type it is, in the order of its list") and derives it with Topology's property rule (`registrar.edges(in: IsAProperty.self, as: IsAEdge.self)`: slot `is`, from the instance to each type, a name of a pill forwarded to what it stands for, a move reading nothing). `build.sh`'s `ORDINARY_IDS` holds `is`. A kind that is `typeable()` says where its words are, as one that `canBeType()` does: an arrow's `typeWords` places its label against the shapes around it (`typeWords(_:drawnIn:fitting:)` has an overload whose `drawnIn` is given the `ShapeContext`; the contribution's `placement` takes it).
- `/is-a` (`registerIsACommand`, every shape that holds `is`: a note, an arrow in its label; Types' one command, after `title` and the other plugins' every-shape commands): a slash command that takes one choice (`SlashCommand(_:argument:choices:step:perform:)`, [slash commands](slash-commands.md); `argument` `type`). Its rows (`TypeChoices.rows(for:in:)`, in `TypeChoices.swift`, asked once when the list opens) are `Type` (group 0, title `TypesStyle.typeChoice`, symbol `curlybraces`, step "Make Type") only while the shape can be a type (`registry.canBeType`) and is not one, never on an arrow's label or a type, then the file's types the shape may be (`TypeIndex.choices(for:)`, group 1, the same symbol, step "Add Type"): every type for which `TypeIndex.admits(_:for:)` is true (a type, never the shape itself, one it is already, one whose lineage holds the shape (no cycles), a pill (a stand-in kind), a shape that isn't there; the one private `mayBe` is where the custom shapes of a later slice join), on any board, ordered by name (lowercased once and compared as plain text, the id the tie: the locale's collation cost most of opening the list), an untitled one last and called `Untitled`; the first type is `isPreferred`, so with nothing typed it is the highlight, and `Type` is when there are none. A row's id is `type` or the type's uuid (`TypeChoice`). A pick runs at once: `Type` calls `makeType`, a type `addTypeLink(_:to:)` (the type goes at the end of the list, a later line wins, one step, "Add Type", on a shape on any board; `addTypeLink(_:to: [ShapeID])` for several shapes in one step); a read-only shape is offered nothing (the state), and the core refuses and shakes the one that became so. `editor.removeTypeLink(_:from:)` is the one way an entry goes (one step, "Remove Type", the property off with the last; the plural form for several), the chips' and the panel's Remove call it, so what a later slice settles when a type is given or taken back is settled inside these, in the person's own step. `@` typed on `/is-a`'s line is a letter (Mentions asks `listsChoices(at:)`). `/type` is gone.
- Is a ▸ (`TypeMenus.swift`, `registerTypeMenus`: an item for the canvas, `MenuItems(in: StateMenuGroup, id: "is-a")`, so in Lock's group with no line between them, after it in plugin order): a submenu `Is a` in the menu of every shape the selection holds, made when the menu opens (`IsAMenu`). Items: `Type` (group `ShapeMenuGroup`, only when every selected shape can be a type), a line (the group `IsATypesMenuGroup`, order 1), then the types some selected shape is or may be (`TypeIndex.menuChoices(for:)`, in `choices(for:)`'s order), each titled by its name or `Untitled`. A check by an item every selected shape has (`Type`: all are types); there is no mixed state, so a type some have is unchecked and choosing it gives it to the rest. An unchecked item calls `makeTypes` or `addTypeLink(_:to:)` (steps "Make Type", "Add Type": one step for the whole selection), a checked one `removeTypes` or `removeTypeLink(_:from:)` ("Remove Type"). Never dimmed: a read-only shape is refused by the core and shakes while the rest change in the one step. The submenu is left out for a selection with a shape that holds no `is` (a frame, a subboard), and where it would hold nothing (an arrow in a file with no types). An arrow's menu has it with the file's types and no `Type`. A list that installs `TypesPlugin` installs `ContextMenusPlugin` too (the registry refuses a contribution whose owner isn't installed).
- A type's color (`TypeColors`, public; the table is `TypesStyle.colorTable`): one of eight by the id, never stored, so two types can share one; the index is the first eight bytes of the id's UUID, each summed with its position, modulo eight (never `hashValue`; TypesTests' `typeColorsTests` hold a table of ids that never changes, the spread, and the validator: white on each `base` at 4.5:1, each ink on its 14% tint over the light canvas (0.965) and the dark (0.11) at 4.5:1, none within 0.05 of red's hue, the eight distinct). `base` is fixed in both appearances; the ink follows the appearance. `TypeColors.solid(id)` is the pill's tint, `light(id)` the chips', `gray` (0.40) what has no color (`Type` and an untitled type in `/is-a`'s list). A type's pill is Titles' (`registrar.titlePillLook(forShapesHolding: TypeSchemaProperty.self)`, [titles](titles.md)): its title after `curlybraces`, solid in its color; untitled, a dimmed gray `Untitled` placeholder (the neutral look every placeholder pill has); a note that holds no schema keeps the plain pill; Remove Type and its undo switch it.
- Chips (`TypeChips`, an `Adornment` and `ClickableAdornment`, aboveTopLeft, order 100, on every kind that holds `is`; a group, tagged): one chip per entry of `is`, in order, light (Adornments' `tinted(_:)`) in the type's color (`TypeColors.light`) with its type's live title (`TypeIndex.name(of:)`; an untitled type a gray placeholder "Untitled", no color), `.missing()` (dashed, italic, gray) for a type that is gone or no longer a type, with the last title it was known by, else "Deleted"; the ones that don't fit whole fold into Adornments' "+N" (gray). A type's note shows its own pill and then the chips of the types it builds on; there is no Type chip (Is a ▸ Type in the menu makes and unmakes one). The index tells Adornments to make the chips of a type's instances again when it is renamed, deleted, brought back or no longer one. A click (`TypeChipMenus.menu(for:in:)`, popped up below the chip's rect with `editor.popUpMenu`): on a type's chip `Go to “Person”` (`editor.goTo`, left out for a type that isn't in the file) and `Remove “Person”` (`removeTypeLink`, left out while the shape is read-only); on "+N", those for every type, a separator between types. Nothing is shown on a shape drawn as a skeleton. On an arrow the row sits above its label (Adornments' anchor).
- The panel's Types tab (`TypesTab`, order 50, a section "types" over `IsAProperty`, `enabledWhileReadOnly`): a row per type in order, a click on it goes to the type (`editor.goTo`, on the scheduler), a minus removes it (`removeTypeLink`; off while the shape is read-only), the row dimmed with the last title once the type is gone. A type that has supertypes has the Schema tab and this.
- Duplicating a typed shape (⌘D, a ⌥-drag, copy and paste, cut and paste: [clipboard](clipboard.md)) keeps `is`; copied with its type, the copy's names are renamed among the copies by the paste's one map (`TypeLink` is `ShapeNaming`), so the copies are the copy's instances and the original keeps its own; pasted into another file the names point at shapes it lacks (dashed chips).
- What is what: `TypeIndex` (`@Observable`, `editor.types`) also keeps `links(of:)` (a shape's types, in the order of its list; a name of a shape that is gone included), `instances(of:)` (the shapes whose list names it, directly), `lineage(of:)` (what a shape's types lead to, level by level, nearest first: its own types later first, then those types' own, each once, never the shape itself, a cycle stopping where it comes back; the one place the order is decided is `TypeGraph.parents(of:)`), `affected(by:)` (the instances of the shapes, near and far, each once: what a change to a type changes) and `name(of: TypeLink)` (`TypeLinkName`: `.live(title)` while the shape is a type, else `.missing(lastKnown:)`, the last title it was known by in this session; observed per type). Kept by each commit's `ChangeSet` as the types are (a shape that only moved reads nothing: `recordsRead` in debug builds), read again for a file put in place and for a commit heard out of turn (`heardRevision`, as Titles' index does; `boardDidChange` always catches up), and it tells Adornments (`editor.adornmentsDidChange(for:)`) to make the chips of the instances of every type again whose name or being a type changed (renamed, deleted, brought back, no longer one).
- The sync: `TypeSync<Schema>` (`editor.typeSync` for a type's, `TypeSync<TypeSchemaProperty>`) keeps a shape's words and a `SyncedSchema` property (one whose value is a `SchemaText`: the property it writes, the prompts it sends, `SchemaSyncPrompts`, its update step's name, and whether the shape's name is the schema's, `namesTheShape`) in step through `editor.languageModel`, as the schema card's did: only the person's edits sync (not its own write, a board read again, a move, a lock, a rename, an agent's write, `ChangeSet.byAgent`, which is taken as in step), on a commit (editing the words ended however it ended, `editingDidEnd`, an edit still waiting committed the next turn; ⌘↩ or the keyboard leaving in the panel's JSON) or 3 s after the last (`TypeSync.idleDelay`, `editor.scheduler`), the wait held while the JSON's completion list is open (`completing`); words that changed rewrite the schema (whole: past 34 KB never sent, "Schema too long to rewrite", `TypeSyncFailure.tooLongMessage`), else a schema changed in meaning (its root `"title"` left out of the comparison) rewrites the words; never for text that isn't a JSON object, a read-only shape (`editor.isReadOnly(inFile:)`, asked as the sync starts and as an answer lands), a shape with no fields and no words, or without a model. A shape seen for the first time is in step, but words over a schema with no fields, never answered, are ahead; a shape the person makes a type (`/is-a type`) waits as an edit does, so the next commit asks for its schema. The name: the type's title goes in as the root `"title"` of each schema sent (`TypeNaming.titled`, unless the schema has one the person typed), the model's comes out of what it writes back (`TypeNaming.untitled`), and an untitled shape is named by it in the same step. While a call is out the side it writes is read-only (the JSON, `detailUpdating`; the note's editor, read-only and faint, showing what lands); its answer is dropped if that side changed. Its write is a step of its own ("Update Schema", "Update Text"), on the shape's board shown or not (`transact(on:)`), the words through the kind's `TypeWords.write`; undo and redo start nothing (`restoredStep`) and the pair last synced goes back with the steps (`UndoParticipant`). Its status (`TypeSyncStatus`, `TypeSide` `.words`/`.schema`, `TypeSyncFailure` and its remedy) shows under the JSON ("Updating schema…", "Updating text…"). What it asks is `SchemaPrompts` (the file name, constant names and prompt literals byte for byte, since `scripts/schema-prompts` parses them; they still say "prompt" for the words): Haiku 5.5 at `medium`. The model never sees an id: each type the texts name is `@` and a word (`SchemaReferenceNames(words:schemas:name:isType:)`), a mention of a type its word and a `$ref` that word as its URI; a mention of a shape that isn't a type reads as its label, words. A rewrite of the words that reads like a preamble, a fence or `NO_CHANGE` inside text is unusable, unless the words it replaces read so themselves (`SchemaPrompts.parse(_:_:words:)`): a note that begins "Here are the fields" is a note.
- Readings: `SchemaReadings` keeps a reading per shape and text (`versionsPerShape`, 4; past 4,096 those used longest ago go; `TypesPlugin.schemasRead` counts reads in debug builds), its `shown` the last that was a JSON object while the text isn't one; `issues` are Validation's (`TypeValidationPlugin`, `validateEveryShape`): not JSON or not an object an error; `required` naming no property, a `type` that isn't JSON Schema's, and whatever else the meta-schema refuses (`MetaSchema` through the core's `SchemaValidator`, its own sentences, a `type` left to the rule of its own), warnings, 20 at most. `SchemaDocument`, `JSONReader`, `JSONCaret`, `JSONCompleter`, `JSONPointer` and `MetaSchema` came over from Schemas as they were.
- The panel (`TypeDetailsPlugin`): every shape that holds the property has the Schema tab (`TypeSchemaTab`), its JSON section (`TypeSchemaCode`): `SchemaCodeEditor` (public, for any schema kept as a shape's text: colored by `JSONReader.tokens`, `SchemaCodeColors`; completed by `TypeCompletions.completions(in:at:types:extra:)`, the meta-schema's keywords with no root `title`, a `required` list's names, a `$ref`'s targets, its own definitions then the file's other titled types, and what a caller adds where a value goes, `extra`), its issues a status line each, and the sync's line (`TypeSyncLine`: Retry, Open Settings…, or nothing). Writes through it are one step a run, "Change JSON". The header is the kind's (a note's title).
- Highlights: a prose map of the words into the schema (`SchemaTarget`, its guide `SchemaPrompts.highlightsGuide`, `highlightsReferences` its context when the words name types), asked once the shape is drawn in full and in step, read through `ProseHighlights` (generic over a target whose addresses are JSON Pointers, keyed by shape and target: the texts as the model reads them, made once per text, and the map moved onto the drawn text, `MentionReading.drawnRange`; `forget(_:)`, `forgetEverything()` in debug builds). A kind's render calls `context.fillTypeHighlights(of:in:)` with the placement it draws its words in, between its fill and its text (a note's), which costs one added-value lookup on a shape that isn't a type: a field's pale blue, a rule's pale amber (`TypesStyle`). Resting the pointer on them shows that part of the schema, or of the type a `$ref` names, in the core's popover: on the canvas through Selection's `shapePopovers` (`TypeHighlights.popover(at:on:in:)`), in a note's editor through `EditContext.hoverPopover` (the editor asks `editor.types`). `TypeHighlights.placed(for:in:)` and `lines(for:in:)` read them for tests.
- StickyNotes: the note's render fills the highlights under its text; its editor (`NoteEditor`) follows the sync, read-only and faint while the model writes the words, showing what lands (`MentionTextView.show`, typing's undo dropped), ⌘↩ ending editing while read-only, and asks for the highlights' popovers as the pointer rests. Every list that installs `StickyNotesPlugin` installs `TypesPlugin` (the registry refuses an owner not installed).
- Composition: `TypesPlugin(), TypeValidationPlugin(), TypeDetailsPlugin()` right after `PublishingPlugin()`, so a note's commands list `title`, `lock`, `public`, `is-a`, then Moving's. The schema card, its tool, its `schema-link` channel and Schemas are gone: a board that holds a card keeps it aside.
- In draw and queries (DrawLexicon's `TypeNamesPlugin`, QueryLexicon's `TypeQueriesPlugin`; both depend on Types): `is: "Employee card"` on a shape or an edge is a `drawTextFormat` for `TypeLink.mediaType`: the title names a type as draw finds titles (`context.shape(called:admitting:)`, file-wide, or drawn in the same run, `writing` carrying the keys the statement writes, so a note given `schema:` there is a type), and what it admits is `TypeIndex.canBeNamed(_:registry:writing:)`, the one pure function over a record and the registry that says which shapes a script may name in `is` (a type that is no pill; the custom shapes extend it to frames holding `isShape`), anything else refused "which isn't a type or a shape"; it writes the id (the entry's coded form), and refuses the shape itself (`DrawTextContext.owner`, the shape whose attribute it is: "can't be its own type") and a shape already built on the owner (a cycle through shapes of the file, by the index's `lineage`, read through `DrawTextContext.service`: "is already built on this shape, which would make a cycle"; a cycle between shapes a single script draws isn't checked, `lineage` is cycle-safe). A quoted value for a list of text is a list of one (Automation's `KindVocabulary.value(of:)`), several are JSON. Queries: `:is-a(S)` is `queryRelation(… reads: .file, transitive: true, namesTypes: true)` over `TypeFacts`, a `QueryFileFact` of `TypeIndex.graph`, a `TypeGraph` (`links` and `names`, copy on write, kept by the index beside its own tables, so a query takes it up in O(1)); `related` is `TypeGraph.parents(of:)` (a shape's types, later first), and `TypeIndex.lineage(of:)` is the same graph's `lineage(of:)`, one chain for settling and for the word. `:edge-to(S, is-a)` stays direct. A `queryTextFormat` for `TypeLink.mediaType` reads an entry as its type's title (`TypeGraph.names`; a gone type stays its id), so `[is~="Person"]` and a row's `is` read titles; `.Person` is refused toward `:is-a("Person")`.

## Testing

- TypesTests: every test over `Memo` (`MemosPlugin`: titled, mentionable, `typeWords`, `canBeType`) with `typePlugins(model:)` (a scripted model always; `SCHEMA_LIVE=1`, which test.sh never sets, runs `liveTests` against Claude), `typeRecord`, `addType`: the JSON reader, caret, completer and meta-schema as they were (`readerTests`, `caretTests`, `completionTests` with a type's `$ref` targets and `extra`, `metaSchemaTests`, `fieldTreeTests`); the sync (`syncTests`: both ways, timers, completing, failures, too long, read-only, an agent's write, the name put in and taken out, an untitled type named, a typed root title kept, a rename asking nothing, `/is-a type` waiting, JSON words made a type asking for the words, an agent's or undo's type in step, Remove Type dropping its call), the prompts (`promptTests`), undo (`undoTests`), boards not shown (`schemaBoardsTests`), the panel (`panelTests`, `panelCompletionTests`, `TypePanelWindow`), highlights (`highlightTests`, `highlightShownTests`), references (`referenceTests`, `referenceNameTests`, `referenceSyncTests`, `referenceShownTests`), `makeType`, JSON words as the schema and Remove Type (`commandTests`, with `Slip`, a kind that can't be one and one refused without `typeWords`), the index (`indexTests`), the rules and reading (`ruleTests`) and the words' write (`wordsTests`).
- TypesTests' `isAMenuTests` (over an `EditorHarness`, reading the menu with `secondaryClick(at:)` and `outline`, choosing with `choose("Is a", "Person")`: the submenu's items and line, the checks, one step for three selected and undo exact, `Type` as Make Type and Remove Type, a type listed when a shape may be it, no `Type` for a kind that cannot be one, none for a selection with a shape that holds no `is`, none where empty, and read-only shapes shaking while the others change), and CompositionTests' `typesShippedTests` hold Is a ▸ beside Lock in a note's menu, an arrow's and a frame's, and a locked note's.
- TypesTests' `isAPropertyTests`, `isAEdgeTests` and `typeLinkTests` (over `Memo`, which is `typeable()` too; `instanceRecord`/`addInstance(_:of:)`, `typesOf`; a local `Echo` that stands for the shapes it cites; a `ChaserPlugin` before Types that commits while hearing a commit): the property (declared, opted in, the report line, `typeable()` refused without `typeWords`, the schema's `text/x-canvas-type` and `uniqueItems`, saved as the bare ids in order and read back, empty is none, `ShapeID` not `ShapeNaming`, a copy renaming the ones copied along and keeping the rest, a delete leaving names dangling and undo whole), the edges (one per entry in the order of the list, slot `is`, followed commit by commit, forwarded through a stand-in, a move reading no record), and the index (three boards, each commit, a move reading nothing, a shape moved to another board and a board taken and brought back, the lineage level by level with cycles and a deleted type, `affected(by:)`, names live and missing and heard by their readers, a file put in place, a commit heard out of turn).
