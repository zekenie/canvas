# Locking

Locking: the locked property, read-only shapes, the chip and the row's lock, and `/lock` (the shake is Selection's, the fade Adornments').

Related: [editor](editor.md), [publishing](publishing.md), [adornments](adornments.md), [selection](selection.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [containers](containers.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Locking](../../docs/plugin-system.md#locking).

## Architecture

- Locking (a concern, on Adornments, ContextMenus, SlashCommands and TableOfContents): any shape can be locked, from the context menu (Lock, or Unlock once every shape the menu is for is locked, as tldraw's toggle; items for the canvas in `LockMenuGroup`, order 300; no key; each goes by the shapes' own locks, of those whose lock can change: a shape in a locked frame, at any depth, or on a locked subboard's board, is locked with it (the core makes it read-only) and can't change its own lock either, so neither item is for it: a menu for only such shapes has neither, and one for some of them and others acts on the others, trying none of them, `isLockedByContainer`, which goes by the shape's container if it's in one, else by its board) or with `/lock` (below), and unlocked from the menu or with a click on its chip (`LockChip`, a clickable adornment). Once a shape is unlocked, however, its chip shows the lock open (Adornments' `ChipFade`: 1.5 s, more while the pointer's on it, then a fade over 0.3 s) ; a click on the open lock locks the shape again, so a click by mistake can be taken back. Locked is something the shape knows: `LockedProperty` (`locked: true`, nil when unlocked), an added property every kind opts into (`optInEveryShape`), read as `record.isLocked`, set with `editor.lock(ids)` and `editor.unlock(ids)`; a locked shape's copy is locked. Locking tells the core locked shapes are read-only (`registrar.readOnlyShapes`), so nothing changes, moves, restacks, edits or deletes one (a drag moves the rest of the selection, Delete and Cut take the rest, the panel is disabled, there are no handles), while it's still selected, copied, listed and bound to. Each try shakes it, and Locking does nothing for that: Selection's `RefusalShake` hears every refusal the core reports (`didRefuseChanges`) and plays it, whoever made the shape read-only (a click doesn't count, it isn't a try; the shake itself, its curve, a shape whose board holds a locked shape, and containers are in [selection](selection.md)). A locked shape that owns a board (a subboard) is entered like any other, since entering isn't a change to it, and its board is read-only, and every board under it, at any depth (the core's `editor.isBoardReadOnly`, [editor](editor.md), the rule for a locked frame's contents applied to a board: Locking adds only what's its own): every shape on it refuses every change, its own lock included, each try shaking the shape tried, nothing goes on it, neither Lock nor Unlock is offered for what's on it, and a shape locked on its own there keeps its chip, whose click is refused (it shakes, and stays locked: the unlock is a transaction the board refuses); a subboard on it opens, and its board is read-only too though that subboard isn't locked. Unlocking the subboard frees them all at once. What else notices a read-only board (the toolbar's tools and their keys, Paste, port dots, `canvas draw`, the path) is in [keyboard](keyboard.md), [clipboard](clipboard.md), [connections](connections.md), [command line](command-line.md) and [boards](boards.md). On another board, `LockState` takes back its locked shapes as it kept them (a `BoardStash`, while the board's revision is unchanged; else it finds them again, one pass; `boardsScannedForLocks`, debug builds, counts the passes) and `ChipFade` drops the open locks (and `RefusalShake` the shakes) of the board left. A chip (`LockChip`: an 18-point disc with a lock, on the kinds that hold `LockedProperty`, at the right end of the row above the top edge, order 300, so a title's pill stops 4 points short of it; lighter hovered, darker pressed; below 35%, on a shape drawn as a skeleton (a locked shape always is: it can't be edited), none: no adornment is shown there, so it can't be clicked) marks it, and so does a lock at the end of its row in the table of contents (a `ContentsMark` with `symbol: "lock.fill"`, "Locked", on the kinds that hold `LockedProperty`, read from the record alone), so the list shows which are locked: a shape's own lock, so what a locked frame holds shows neither, the frame alone does; its open, fading lock is a label read from `ChipFade` (`LockState.openLockOpacity(of:)`): `LockState` shows it (`fade.show(LockChip.self, on:)`) when a commit unlocks a shape and hides it (`hide`) when one locks the shape or deletes it, telling Adornments once for all it changes (`editor.adornmentsDidChange`), and a click on it (`isShowing`) locks the shape again. `/lock` is a slash command every kind that holds `LockedProperty` offers (`registrar.slashCommandsForEveryShape(where: { $0.holds(LockedProperty.self) })`, offered `when` the shape isn't locked, running `editor.lock([id])` with `perform`): typed on a line of its own in a shape's text (a note's), with Return, it takes the line out, locks the shape and ends its editing, one step named "Lock" like the menu's, after the typing that has the line's removal in it; there's no `/unlock`, since a locked shape's text can't be edited and so nothing could be typed (the report shows it as `/lock → every shape`). A plugin list with Locking needs `SlashCommandsPlugin()` (the registry refuses a contribution whose owner isn't installed), which the shipped list has. Core and horizontal tests use CanvasTesting's `FreezingPlugin` and `Frozen` for read-only shapes, never Locking.

## Testing

- LockingTests hold what Locking adds: a locked shape still shakes (the drag, Delete and edit tests in ShakeTests: the shake itself is `RefusalShakeTests` in SelectionTests, on CanvasTesting's `Frozen`), and its open lock still shows, fades and goes (ChipTests: the fade machine itself is `ChipFadeTests` in AdornmentsTests, on fixture chips). BoardTests hold locking across boards, over CanvasTesting's pockets for subboards: a locked pocket entered, and on its board, and on a pocket's on it, a drag, Delete and a double-click each refused and shaking the shape, no Lock or Unlock in the menu, a box locked on its own keeping its chip and a click on it refused, and unlocking the pocket freeing both boards; CompositionTests' LockedShapeTests hold the same with the shipped plugins (a locked subboard holding a note and a subboard with a note: no tool that places shapes, nor its key, no paste, and unlocked, all of it again).
