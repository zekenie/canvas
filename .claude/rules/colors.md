# Colors

Colors: the color a shape of a kind that opted in holds (`color`: a named color or a hex value), the color another plugin gives a shape that holds none (`InheritedColors`: a type's, to its notes), the built-in names and a file's own, the book that says what every color looks like (`editor.colors`), how a kind draws its color, where one is picked (the panel's row, `/color`, Color ▸), what `canvas draw` and `canvas query` say of it, and what each shipped kind does with its color.

Related: [palette](palette.md), [properties and schemas](properties-and-schemas.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [validation](validation.md), [command line](command-line.md), [queries](queries.md), [types](types.md), [subboards](subboards.md), [frames](frames.md), [arrows](arrows.md), [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md), [boards on disk](boards-on-disk.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Colors](../../docs/plugin-system.md#colors).

## Architecture

- Colors (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait, on Canvas, Palette, ContextMenus, DetailPanel, Draw, Queries, SlashCommands and Validation; plugin id `colors`; loosely tldraw's color system: named colors, each drawn in roles per appearance, and a document's own colors) knows no kind. A shape of a kind that opted in has a color; every other shape has none. What a color looks like is [Palette](palette.md)'s `Swatch`, seven roles in the light appearance and the dark, worked out from the one value the same way for a name and for a hex; which names there are, and what each shape's color is, is the book's. What a kind does with its color is the kind's.
- **A color** (`ShapeColor`): `.named(String)` or `.value(RGB)`, one string on a board (`blue`, `Brand Blue`, `#4465E9`). `ShapeColor(text)` reads one as it's written: a hex in either case and in three or six digits, else a name folded to one line with no spaces at its ends; nil for nothing and for what starts `#` and isn't a hex. Decoding never refuses a board: a string that isn't a hex is a name, which the file has or lacks. A name is kept as written and found whatever its case (`ShapeColor.key(of:)`). Its schema is a string with `contentMediaType` `text/x-canvas-color` (`ShapeColor.mediaType`), which draw and queries read it by. No alpha.
- **The property** (`ColorProperty`, key `color`, nil on a shape with no color of its own; `record.ownColor`; fixture `Fixtures/added/color.v0.json`; `color` is an ordinary id to the audit, `ORDINARY_IDS`, a word any data may hold). A kind opts in inside its registration: `note.colorable(default: .yellow)`, or `frame.colorable()` for a kind whose shapes look as the kind does on its own with no color (`registrar.colorable(K.self, default:)` for another plugin's kind): the opt-in to the property and one contribution to `ColorDefaults` (a `KeyedExtensionPoint`, one a kind; its `check` refuses a default that names a color no file is sure to have, anything but a built-in name or a value; the report's `colorable (yellow) → sticky-note`). `registry.isColorable(kind)`, `registry.defaultColor(of: kind)`. A list with a kind that opts in needs `ColorsPlugin` (the registry refuses a contribution whose owner isn't installed), and `ColorsPlugin` needs `SlashCommandsPlugin` and `ContextMenusPlugin`, as Locking does.
- **The built-in names** (`BuiltInColors.all`, in the order every list shows them): gray, red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, indigo, purple, pink, each a `NamedColor` (`name`, `value`, `isBuiltIn`, `swatch`, `color`). Each value is one sRGB color chosen from renders of its roles on both canvases, dark enough that white reads on its solid (yellow's and orange's take black), and its roles are `Swatch(value)` but for three pins that keep what the app drew before colors had names: yellow's paper is the note's yellow (`BuiltInColors.notePaper`), and indigo's line in the dark (`#5E5CE6`) and its surface (`cardLight`, `cardDark`) are the subboard's. ColorsTests hold every role of every one to its floor on both canvases.
- **The book** (`ColorBook`, `editor.colors`, a service; in a render `context.service(ColorBook.self)`): `names` (the built-in ones, then the file's), `named(_:)`, `canonical(_:)` (a name in its list's spelling, nil for one the file lacks), `has(_:)`, `swatch(for: color)`; `color(of: record)` is the color a shape is drawn in, its own where the file has it, else the one it's given (`inheritedColor(of:kind:)`, below) where the file has that, else its kind's default, else nil (a name the file lacks is drawn as though the shape held none, and warned of), `swatch(of: record)` its roles, and `color(_ role:, of:)` one role as an `NSColor` that follows the appearance (of a record, or of a kind's own typed shape: a title's cover's surface). It keeps nothing per shape or per board (its `boardDidChange` reads no shape): what it keeps is per color, the roles and their `CGColor`s in each appearance (`SwatchColors`), made the first time a color is asked for, let go of when the file's colors change, and at most 4,096 (a picker dragged across its wheel asks for thousands of values), and per kind, its default and the contributions that give its shapes colors; the color and the kind asked for last are kept apart, since shapes drawn one after another mostly share both, and whether the appearance being drawn in is dark is asked once an appearance, not once a shape. `onChange { … }` is called when the file's colors change, for a plugin that keeps something made from a color.
- **A color given** (`InheritedColors`, an `ExtensionPoint` of Colors', `.scoped`, unkeyed; `InheritedColor`; the report's `inherited color → every shape holding is`): a plugin says what the shapes of the kinds a scope reaches are drawn in where they hold no color of their own, `registrar.forShapes(holding: P.self) { $0.inheritedColor { id, editor in ShapeColor? } }`, answered from what a service keeps, since it's asked as each such shape is drawn, listed and shown in the panel's row. Asked in plugin order, only of a kind with colors and only for a shape whose own color the file lacks or that holds none (the contributions that reach a kind are found once and kept by the book), the first answer the file has wins; a name the file lacks is passed over. Nothing is written to the shape. When what a plugin gives changes with no commit naming the shapes, it calls `editor.colors?.inheritedColorsDidChange()`, once it has the change: the canvas draws again, the lists make their tints again (`editor.shapeTintsDidChange()`) and what observes `color(of:)` (the panel's row: `inheritedColor(of:kind:)` reads an observed revision) reads it again. Shipped: Types' `TypeColorsPlugin` ([types](types.md)), a shape of a type takes its type's color, the one its pill shows (the color its type's shape holds, else the one its id says), and a type that holds none the one its id says. ColorsTests' `inheritedColorTests` hold it with a giver of their own.
- **Drawing** (`context.colors(of: shape)`, a `RenderContext` extension, `book.colors(of:)`): the seven roles as `CGColor`s in the appearance being drawn, of the color `color(of:)` says, or nil for a shape drawn as its kind looks on its own. One lookup a shape (and its giver's, for a kind a contribution reaches), read in a render and a skeleton alike. A kind draws the role that suits each part: `paper` for a note's sheet, `solid` for a line or a border, `surface` for what holds shapes, `ink` for text in the color.
- **Writing**: `editor.setColor(_ color: ShapeColor?, of: [ShapeID])`, one step, "Change Color", on whichever boards the shapes are; nil takes the color away; a name is stored in its list's spelling; kinds with no colors are left out; a read-only shape is refused by the core and shakes; a change that changes nothing is no step.
- **A file's own colors** (`FileColors.swift`): a list of a name and a value, kept on the file as a file value (`FileColorsProperty`, key `colors`, a `FileColorList` of `FileColor`s, each `{ "name": …, "value": "#RRGGBB" }`, in the order added; [properties and schemas](properties-and-schemas.md), File values; fixture `Fixtures/file/colors.v0.json`, held by PersistenceTests through CanvasTesting's `expectFilePropertyFixtures`), listed after the built-in ones in that file only. A name is one line (`ShapeColor.folded`), 1 to 40 characters (`ColorsStyle.maxNameLength`), never starting `#`, unlike any other of the file's whatever the case, and never a built-in one: `ColorNameRefusal` (`empty`, `startsWithHash`, `tooLong`, `builtIn`, `taken`) is why one is refused, as a value with no words (`ColorNameRefusal.check(_:takenKeys:)`, `book.refusal(ofName:renaming:)`). Reading is lenient, so a board written by hand is never refused for them: an entry that isn't a name and a hex, or whose name the rules refuse, is dropped. The book follows the file: `fileColors` is read as the book is made, on a commit that changed the value (`ChangeSet.fileValues`: an edit, an undo, a redo) and on a file put in place (`BoardChange.fileValues`), all through `fileColorsDidChange(to:)`, which lets go of what was made from the old colors, calls the `onChange` handlers and redraws; a switch of board reads nothing. On `CanvasEditor`, each one step, returning whether it did anything: `defineColor(named:value:)` ("Add Color"); `setColorValue(of:to:coalescing:)` ("Change Color": the list's entry alone, so every shape that names it is recolored and none rewritten; with a key, a run is one step, a drag in the color panel); `renameColor(_:to:)` ("Rename Color": the entry, and `color` on every shape of every board that holds the name, whatever its case); `removeColor(_:)` ("Remove Color": the entry, and each shape that held it takes its value as a hex, so it looks as it did); `nameColor(of:_:)` ("Add Color": the value a shape holds becomes a color of the file's and the shape names it). A rename, a removal or a naming that would rewrite a read-only shape refuses whole (nothing changes, no step, those shapes shake): done shape by shape it would leave a locked shape under a name the file lacks, drawn as its kind's default. A shape on a board that can't be shown is not reached. A name the file lacks (a shape pasted from another file) draws as its kind's default and is warned of.
- **The panel's row** (`ColorDetailsPlugin`, `ColorPanel.swift`, `ColorPanelModel.swift`): one line, `Color`, in the Style tab (`StyleTab`, DetailPanel's, after Connections), on every kind that has colors: a headerless section named for its steps (`DetailSection(in:id:).named("Color")`, [detail panel](detail-panel.md)), on the kinds that have colors (`registrar.forShapes(holding: ColorProperty.self) { $0.details { … } }`, a section over the whole record, since a shape with no color of its own holds no value for a section over the property's value to show). The row is a dot of the color itself (its value, as a swatch shows one; its roles are what a shape draws) and what it's called (`ColorRowModel.title`: a named color's `NamedColor.title`, a built-in name capitalized and a file's as written, as Color ▸ says it; a value as its hex; a shape with no color of its own shows the color it's given, else its kind's default, dimmed, or `None` dimmed, and so does one that names a color the file lacks). A click opens the grid (`ColorGrid`, a popover): the named colors as dots, seven a row, the built-in ones and under them the file's, the shape's own ringed, each dot its name as tooltip and accessibility label; then `None`, `Other…` (the system color panel, `ColorPanelTarget`, started at the color itself, never a role of it: each color it reports written as a hex in sRGB, a drag one step; the panel floats on after the row shows another shape or goes, so the row lets go of it then, `release`, and what is picked after is written nowhere) and, while the shape holds a value of its own, `Name…`, which shows a field in the grid's place (`ColorNameForm`: the button dimmed while the name is refused, Return gives it) and makes the value a color of the file's. A dot of the file's has a menu: `Rename…`, `Change…` (the color panel, a drag one step) and `Remove`. Writes of the shape's color go through the section's binding (`ColorRowModel.apply(_:to:)`, `ColorChoice`), so the step is "Change Color" and a locked shape's row is disabled; the file's colors' through the editor's calls above. `ColorRowModel` has no view, so tests drive it.
- **`/color`** (`ColorCommand.swift`, registered by `ColorsPlugin` on every kind that holds `color`, in the one scope it gives `/color` and Color ▸, `forShapes(holding: ColorProperty.self)`): a command that takes one choice and acts on all the shapes it is typed for together (`SlashCommand.together`, reach `together`: [slash commands](slash-commands.md)). Typed in a shape's text it is that one shape's; typed for a selection (headless until the command bar) it is the selected shapes that have colors, a shape of a kind with none (a reference) not among them, and the list says how many it acts on where that is fewer (`3 of 4`). Its rows (`ColorCommandRows.rows(for: owners, in:)`, asked once for all of them, never once a shape) are the named colors in the book's order, each a chip in its `solid` with its title in `onSolid` (`SlashChoice.chip`, `chipInk`: black on yellow and orange), the built-in ones in one group and the file's in the next, then `None` while any of the shapes has a color of its own (a pick of it takes the color away from those that have one and leaves the rest as they are), then Edit Colors…; one shape gets the rows its text always had; what is typed as a hex value is offered as its one row, as it's stored (`/color #46e`: `#4466EE`). A pick is `editor.setColor(_, of: owners)`, all of them in one step, Change Color, which one undo takes back whole. A read-only shape is offered no command in a text, and is left out of a selection's (the command is not a declared one, so it never reaches a locked shape): it keeps its color, and its color is not what `None` is offered for.
- **Color ▸** (`ColorMenu.swift`, the scope's item for the canvas in `StateMenuGroup`, after Is a ▸ in plugin order, no line between): a submenu in the menu of a selection whose shapes all have colors (the scope's, so the item asks nothing of kinds itself): the built-in names capitalized, a line, the file's, a line, `None`; a check by the color every selected shape holds (none where they differ, `None` where none holds one); a pick colors the whole selection in one step; never dimmed (a read-only shape is refused and shakes while the rest change). Plain titles: `MenuItem` shows no image.
- **Keys** ([keyboard navigation](keyboard-navigation.md); `ColorList.swift`, `ColorGridMap` in `ColorPanelModel.swift`): the grid is one `.focusSection()` with a `@FocusState` of `ColorGridStop` (a dot by its group and place, or one of the plain rows under the dots); the keyboard starts on the ringed dot, else the first, the arrows move through `ColorGridMap` (left and right dot to dot across the groups, up and down by `dotsPerRow` keeping the column, then on to the plain rows; `onMoveCommand`), Return or Space chooses, and the stop with the keyboard has an accent outline of its own (a plain button is a stop only with Keyboard navigation on, so the grid draws and takes its own). The name form's Escape (`.onExitCommand`) goes back to the grid, dropping the name typed; a second Escape closes the popover. **Edit Colors…** is the last item of Color ▸ (a group of its own, `ColorEditMenuGroup`, after None; so also in the Shape menu) and the last row of `/color`'s list (id `"\n"`, which no name has), the way to change a file's colors with no right-click and with Keyboard navigation off. It opens `ColorListPanel` (one utility panel a file, `ColorListPanel.show(for:)`, so another file's list stays open; it holds the file's book weakly and closes when the book goes with its document (`ColorBook.onRelease`), and its model listens through a `ColorBook.Subscription` that ends with it; `ordersFront` off keeps a test's off the screen): a `List` of the file's own colors, a row a dot, its name and a Change… button. Up and Down walk the rows, Return makes the name a field (a held key acts once: the repeats of Return and Delete are taken and do nothing; an input method composing in the field keeps its Return and Escape; Return gives it, refused names and all via `ColorRowModel.refusal`; Escape drops it, back on the row), Delete and Forward Delete remove the row ("Remove Color": every shape that held it keeps its value; a removal a locked shape refuses leaves the keys on the row), Change… opens the system color panel on the row's value, and Escape closes the list. Every write goes through `ColorRowModel.apply` (`ColorRowModel(book:)`, a model with no shape) and so the editor calls the right-click menu makes; `ColorListModel` is the list with no view.
- **Draw and queries** (`ColorWords.swift`, each a plugin value of its own): `ColorNamesPlugin` gives `canvas draw` a `drawTextFormat` for the color's media type: a name is kept in its list's spelling (`BLUE` is `blue`), a hex as `#RRGGBB`, and a name the file lacks, or a `#…` that is no hex, is `invalid_value` on its line, listing the names (`canvas kinds` says the attribute takes `text (a color's name or #RRGGBB)`). An edge takes `color:` for its arrow. `ColorQueriesPlugin` gives `canvas query` a `queryTextFormat` that reads the stored text (a hex normalized): `[color="blue"]` matches as written, `[color]` a shape with a color of its own, and the engine compares the literal as written, so another case needs `i` (`[color="BLUE" i]`). The canvas skill says both ([command line](command-line.md)).
- **The warning** (`ColorValidationPlugin`, `ColorValidation.swift`, a rule on the kinds that hold a color, `forShapes(holding: ColorProperty.self)`): a shape whose own color is a name the file lacks carries `Color “brand” isn’t in this file.` (a warning, `field` `color`); a hex and a built-in name never do. The rule reads the shape and the book, so a commit costs the shapes it names; when the file's names change (one added, renamed or removed; a color given another value changes no warning, so a drag in the color panel looks through nothing), the rule's `affected` (`ColorWarnings`) names every shape of the file that holds a name, a pass over the file that happens only then (asked again for that commit, by the rule of another kind, it answers the same).
- **A listed shape's symbol** (`ColorTints.swift`): `registrar.shapeTints` (the core's point, [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md)) answers a shape's own color's `solid` where the file has it, else the given one's, and nil otherwise, so a shape drawn in its kind's default is listed in its kind's tint as it always was (`book.tint(of:)`, `tint(for:)`: the same `NSColor` each time, so a list that compares appearances sees no change). The book tells the editor when the file's colors change, and when what's given changes (`editor.shapeTintsDidChange()`), which every list that shows a tint hears.
- **What each kind does with its color**: a note's paper is `paper`, its type's color without one of its own (below), else yellow, and a type's note stays white ([editing](editing.md)); a type's color is the color its note holds, else one of eight names by its id, drawn as its pill, its notes' chips and `/is-a`'s rows, and written in its schema as `"$color": {"const": …}`, which every write keeps saying the note's color (a color set here, renamed or removed is written there in the same step; one typed there is the note's: [types](types.md)); a type's color is the color of the shapes of it that hold none of their own, and the type's own where its note holds none (`TypeColorsPlugin`, through `InheritedColors`: for a shape of a type, the first color its chain holds, a later type first and a type before what it builds on, as fields resolve, else the one its first type's id says; for a type, the one its id says); a frame's surface is `surface` and its border `solid`, none without one ([frames](frames.md)); a subboard's card is `surface` and its border and symbol `solid`, indigo without one ([subboards](subboards.md)); an arrow's line and heads are `solid` and its label `ink`, its relationship type's color without one of its own, else none ([arrows](arrows.md)). Each says its title's cover surface from the color it's drawn in (`x.titleCoverSurface`, `book.color(\.paper, of: shape)`, [titles](titles.md)), and each keeps its color zoomed out. A reference has no color.

## Testing

- ColorsTests are on the tests' own kinds (`Support.swift`: `Sheet`, colorable, yellow by default, drawn as its paper; `Pane`, colorable with no default, drawn as its solid, gray with none; CanvasTesting's box has no colors; `colorPlugins()`, `colorHarness()`, `render(_:dark:)`, which draws in either appearance where CanvasTesting's `renderBitmap` draws in the light one, and `expectPixel`). `Imports.swift` imports every module the target's line names, so the line holds whichever suites are filled. The suites: `shapeColorTests` (written and read, what is no color, a board's string), `builtInColorTests` (the ten, every role at its floor on both canvases rounded to 8 bits, white on every solid but yellow's and orange's, the pins), `colorRegistryTests` (the opt-in, the report, the schema, the refusals), `colorBookTests`, `setColorTests`, `colorRenderTests` (a default, a color, a value, the dark appearance, a name the file lacks), `fileColorTests` and `fileColorsOnDiskTests` (each action and its undo, the refusals, a rename across two boards, a removal keeping each shape's look, the read-only refusal, a file put in place, the codec and a real `BoardDocument`), `colorPanelTests` and `colorPanelFileTests` (the row's model; writes through the binding the panel hands a section, caught by a probe section, since SwiftUI's buttons can't be found in an unshown window; the color panel faked by an opener), `colorCommandTests` (through `SlashCommandState`: the rows, the pick, the step and `Edit Colors…` for a shape's text, and for a selection: one sheet's is its text's, three sheets' rows with `None` only while one of them holds a color of its own, a pick, a typed hex and `None` each coloring or clearing all three in one "Change Color" that undo and redo take back whole, a pane and a box `1 of 2` (the row's detail and what VoiceOver hears) with the box untouched, a frozen sheet among three left out (`2 of 3`) and keeping its color, a shape named twice colored once), `colorMenuTests` (a secondary click), `colorDrawTests` and `colorQueryTests` (the engines installed, with `CardsPlugin` for a kind with no colors), `colorValidationTests`, `colorTintTests` and `inheritedColorTests` (`InheritedTests.swift`, a giver of the tests' own over `ColorProperty`: drawn, its own color winning and taken away, the book's answers, a name the file lacks passed over for the next giver, no giver asked for a shape with its own color or a kind with none, the tint, the panel's row dimmed, `inheritedColorsDidChange()`'s redraw, tints and observation, the report).
- CompositionTests' `colorsShippedTests` hold the shipped pieces: which kinds have a color and their defaults, `/color` typed in a note's editor in the app's canvas view, `/color` for two notes and a frame selected (headless, through `editor.slashCommands`: one list, a pick coloring all three in one step, a reference selected too left out, `3 of 4`, and `None` taking all three back in one step), Color ▸ in a note's, an arrow's and a frame's menu and not a reference's, and a script drawn and queried through `canvas` over a socket of its own. Each kind's own tests hold its look (StickyNotesTests' `coloredNoteTests`, FramesTests' `frameColorTests`, SubboardsTests' `subboardColorTests`, ArrowsTests' `arrowColorTests`, TypesTests' `typeColorsTests` and `instanceColorTests`), and each list's its tint.
- The gallery's `colorStates` (`GalleryColors.swift`): a board with a note in each built-in color, a colored frame, subboard and arrow, in full and at 30%; the panel's row on a plain note, a blue one and a frame; the grid with two of the file's colors, and for a value; `/color`'s list, narrowed, and for a hex; a type with a chosen color beside one with its id's; and a note's menu as text. `GalleryStage.viewPicture` draws a SwiftUI view alone (the grid, which is a popover in the app).
