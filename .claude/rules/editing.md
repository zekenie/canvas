# Editing in place

Editing a shape in place (`Editable`): the editing view and its frame, where editing begins, `EditContext`; and holding an editor's text to its shape's render, as a note's is.

Related: [editor](editor.md), [text editing](text-editing.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [selection](selection.md), [undo](undo.md), [types](types.md), [arrows](arrows.md), [drawing](drawing.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Capabilities](../../docs/plugin-system.md#capabilities), [A vertical plugin](../../docs/plugin-system.md#a-vertical-plugin).

## Architecture

- Editing in place (`Editable`): the editing view is laid over the shape's editing frame (`editingFrame(of:in:)`, world units, looked at again as the shape or the camera changes: a box kind's box by default, so only a kind without one implements it, an arrow its label's, which grows with what's typed; host SPI `editor.editingFrame`, and `EditContext.frame`, the origin of the view's coordinates); only a box kind has an editing placement. `editor.beginEditing(id, at: point, clickCount:)` says where the press that began it was (world units) and how many clicks it was, which the editing view reads as `EditContext.startPoint` and `startClickCount` to put its selection there as a text view would (a caret, a double-click's word), and the editor keeps as `editingStartPoint` and `editingStartClickCount` while editing lasts; Selection's double-click passes its press and count, a click on the only selected shape of an `Editable` kind edits it from the release (tldraw's: that press waits for the drag threshold, as a face's does, and a read-only shape is only selected), each only for a press on the shape itself (on what's drawn for it outside, a title's pill, a double-click passes no point and a click doesn't edit), and not for the click that brings the window forward (`Pointer.activatesWindow`, from `CanvasView.acceptsFirstMouse`); Return and a placement pass none, and the view chooses as it always has (an `EditingTextView` puts it there with `placeSelection(at:selectingWord:)`, TextKit 2's, as a note's editor does). A note too short for its text as editing begins (saved before it grew with its text, or drawn with `size:`) grows to it then, in the editing's step. A kind that is a `LaidOutBox` (a note with rows) has its editing frame default to its laid-out frame (`context.frame(of:)`), so the view lies over the paper; a note's editor is as big as the paper, its text wrapping where the render's does (`NoteLayout.words`), while the fit as editing begins and on every keystroke stays on the stored frame, the stored width and the stored height, so nothing of the rows is ever written and the stored box is the note as it is with no rows. `EditContext.service(_:)` lends an editing view the editor's services, as the panel's, the table of contents' and adornments' contexts do; `EditContext.shape` is the shape as it is now (what something other than typing writes while it's edited: a type note's words the model writes, read as the sync's status says it landed, since the document observed hears every commit); `EditContext.end()` ends only its own session, so a view may end it a turn later (the card's, as the keyboard leaves the canvas); and `EditContext.keepInView(_:)` brings a rect of it (in its coordinates: world units from the shape's box) into the clear part of the view, 16 points in, panning at once, no step: a note's editor keeps its caret in view (`EditingTextView.caretFrame`) as the note grows past the window's edge, or an arrow key moves it. Two `Editable` hooks shape the end of a session: `discardsWhenEditingEnds(_:)` (default false), asked by `endEditing` after the view has saved and before the step closes, deletes the shape inside the open editing step so a shape placed and left empty nets to nothing (no step), and is not asked when an undo, redo, command, draw (`endEditing(discarding: false)`), board switch or file put in place ends the session, nor while any action is under way (`UndoHistory.isInAction`): that action owns the step; `editingStepName` (default nil: "Typing") names the step a session opens (`UndoHistory.editingBegan(stepName:)`), an action's own name (a placement's "Add ...") winning. Tests: CanvasTests' EditingHooksTests (a fixture slip). Services hear editing end, however it ends (Escape, a click elsewhere, another board, an undo), what it wrote committed (`EditorService.editingDidEnd`), its step closed unless what ended it is an action still under way (an undo about to apply a step, a command, a draw, a file put in place), so a service that answers it waits a turn (`editor.scheduler`): a type's sync takes its words as done then. Every editor of words on the canvas (a note's, an arrow's label's) checks their spelling as the app does (`EditingTextView.spelling = .app`, [text editing](text-editing.md)); its marks show only while it's edited, since the canvas draws a shape's text with string drawing, which marks nothing.
- A note is laid out by its words and what goes under them (`NoteLayout`, StickyNotes; a `LaidOutBox`, [editor](editor.md)): the paper (`frame`), where its words are drawn (`words`) and where a type's rows go (`block`, nil with none), from one read of `context.layoutInput(TypeRows.extent, of:)` ([types](types.md): Types provides it for a note that is a type or is one, and the note says where its rows go with `typeRows(drawnIn:)`, drawing them in its render, under the editor too). With no rows it is the stored frame and its words that inset by `NoteStyle.padding`, nothing measured: the note as it was drawn before it had any. With rows it is `max(stored width, rows.minimumWidth)` wide, its words wrapping at that width less the padding, and `max(stored height, needed)` tall, `needed` being the padding, the words' height (`NoteWords`), `NoteStyle.blockGap` when there are words, the rows' height and the padding, and never under a new note's height (`NoteStyle.size`, as `fittedHeight` is never under it): paper left over is under the rows. The render fills the paper, draws the words in `words` and the pills and highlights follow it (`mentionText` and `typeWords` take the context); the outline is the paper's; the skeleton fills the laid-out frame (the kept one: no provider asked, no words measured, its bars counted in it inset by the padding). `NoteWords` keeps each text's lines' height by what it says and how wide (16,384 at most, then the lot is let go), so the same words are never measured twice, and measures only for a note with rows; it is measured as `derive` does, each mention by the name the text holds, so a renamed pill can still wrap past it until the note is edited. `NoteStyle.linesHeight` is the one rounding `fittedHeight` and `NoteWords` share. Debug builds: `NoteWordsCounts.measured` and `block(of:in:)`, for tests.

## Testing

- For an editing view's text against its shape's render, `renderBitmap(editor, size:scale:editing:)` (CanvasTesting) draws the view from `editor.makeEditingView()` over the canvas as `CanvasView` lays it out (frame in view points, bounds in world units); `lineTops(_:over:in:scale:)` is where each line starts, against the same drawing without the text, and `largestDifference(_:_:in:scale:)` holds every pixel to anti-aliasing (StickyNotesTests' `noteEditorTests`, at 1× and 2×, zoomed in and out). The note's render (string drawing) and its editor (TextKit 2) share `NoteStyle.text` (a `MentionTextStyle`), whose paragraph fixes each line at 19 points, string drawing's own pitch, and the editor's text starts a point higher (`NoteStyle.editorInset`), since TextKit 2 sets a line's glyphs a point lower in it; so nothing moves when editing starts. A note is as tall as its text, never shorter than 200 (`NoteStyle.fittedHeight`, string drawing's measure, pills and all): its editor writes the height with the text, one step, and its text view grows with the editor over it; `DerivedProps` fits a note made from properties (`canvas draw`), each pill measured live by the name it holds (`MentionTextStyle.measuredString`), so it's as tall as its editor fits it and beginning to edit writes nothing. With pills, each pill's frame in the editor (TextKit 2's frame for its attachment) equals the canvas's (`editor.mentionPills(of:)`), and every pixel off the pills is held to anti-aliasing; at a whole number of pixels to a point the lines start alike and the pills' pixels are within a quarter, while at 60% string drawing draws a pill part way into a pixel and TextKit 2 on the nearest, a pixel apart.
- StickyNotesTests' `noteLayoutTests` (`NoteLayoutTests.swift`; on real types, a type of n text fields and notes that are one, the arithmetic read from `TypesStyle`): the stored frame and the words' inset with no rows and nothing measured; four rows on a new note, 320 wide and 200 tall; the padding, words, gap, rows and padding when they need more, and paper under the rows when it stores more; no gap with no words; the words wrapping at the laid-out width; the editor's lines on the render's at that width (1× and 2×, zoomed in and out) with the view over the paper; typing storing the words' height at the stored width and nothing of the rows; the type removed, the note its stored size again (and back with undo); the skeleton's paper the laid-out frame; a pill and a type's highlight in the words' box; each words measured once per width (`NoteWordsCounts`); a plain note drawn in full asking the provider and measuring nothing.
