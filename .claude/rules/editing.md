# Editing in place

Editing a shape in place (`Editable`): the editing view and its frame, where editing begins, `EditContext`; and holding an editor's text to its shape's render, as a note's is.

Related: [editor](editor.md), [text editing](text-editing.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [selection](selection.md), [undo](undo.md), [types](types.md), [arrows](arrows.md), [drawing](drawing.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Capabilities](../../docs/plugin-system.md#capabilities), [A vertical plugin](../../docs/plugin-system.md#a-vertical-plugin).

## Architecture

- Editing in place (`Editable`): the editing view is laid over the shape's editing frame (`editingFrame(of:in:)`, world units, looked at again as the shape or the camera changes: a box kind's box by default, so only a kind without one implements it, an arrow its label's, which grows with what's typed; host SPI `editor.editingFrame`, and `EditContext.frame`, the origin of the view's coordinates); only a box kind has an editing placement. The core lays the shape out afresh as editing begins (before its view is made) and again as it ends (`shapesDidChangeLayout`), so what lays a shape out may read whether it is the one edited (`editor.editingShapeID`: a typed note keeps a line for its words, [types](types.md)). `editor.beginEditing(id, at: point, clickCount:)` says where the press that began it was (world units) and how many clicks it was, which the editing view reads as `EditContext.startPoint` and `startClickCount` (`EditContext.update` changes the shape's props, and `EditContext.transact(_:_:)` anything else of it, an added value such as a title, both as the session's step, named for the first commit's name where nothing else names it) to put its selection there as a text view would (a caret, a double-click's word), and the editor keeps as `editingStartPoint` and `editingStartClickCount` while editing lasts; Selection's double-click passes its press and count, a click on the only selected shape of an `Editable` kind edits it from the release (tldraw's: that press waits for the drag threshold, as a face's does, and a read-only shape is only selected), each only for a press on the shape itself (on what's drawn for it outside, a title's pill, a double-click passes no point and a click doesn't edit), and not for the click that brings the window forward (`Pointer.activatesWindow`, from `CanvasView.acceptsFirstMouse`); Return and a placement pass none, and the view chooses as it always has (an `EditingTextView` puts it there with `placeSelection(at:selectingWord:)`, TextKit 2's, as a note's editor does). A note too short for its text as editing begins (saved before it grew with its text, or drawn with `size:`) grows to it then, in the editing's step. A kind that is a `LaidOutBox` (a note with rows) has its editing frame default to its laid-out frame (`context.frame(of:)`), so the view lies over the paper; a note that shows a type's rows hosts Types' `RowsView` over them (`makeRowsView`, above its text view), so a press on the rows is the rows' (on a type's note Retry, a card, an options list, no caret moved; on a note that is one a second place for the caret, [Typing in a row](types.md#typing-in-a-row): a press on a typed value begins the session in its field (`rows.begin(at:clickCount:)` answers the responder `EditingView.firstResponder` is), ↓ from the words' last line goes to the first row, ⇧Tab and ↑ from the first come back, and a row's commit is the writer's, never `EditContext.update`) and one on the words is the text's; a note's editor is as big as the paper, its text wrapping where the render's does (`NoteLayout.words`), while the fit as editing begins and on every keystroke stays on the stored frame, the stored width and the stored height, so nothing of the rows is ever written and the stored box is the note as it is with no rows. `EditContext.service(_:)` lends an editing view the editor's services, as the panel's, the table of contents' and adornments' contexts do; `EditContext.shape` is the shape as it is now (what something other than typing writes while it's edited: a type note's words the model writes, read as the sync's status says it landed, since the document observed hears every commit); `EditContext.end()` ends only its own session, so a view may end it a turn later (the card's, as the keyboard leaves the canvas); and `EditContext.keepInView(_:)` brings a rect of it (in its coordinates: world units from the shape's box) into the clear part of the view, 16 points in, panning at once, no step: a note's editor keeps its caret in view (`EditingTextView.caretFrame`) as the note grows past the window's edge, or an arrow key moves it. Two `Editable` hooks shape the end of a session: `discardsWhenEditingEnds(_:)` (default false), asked by `endEditing` after the view has saved and before the step closes, deletes the shape inside the open editing step so a shape placed and left empty nets to nothing (no step), and is not asked when an undo, redo, command, draw (`endEditing(discarding: false)`), board switch or file put in place ends the session, nor while any action is under way (`UndoHistory.isInAction`): that action owns the step; `editingStepName` (default nil: "Typing") names the step a session opens (`UndoHistory.editingBegan(stepName:)`), an action's own name (a placement's "Add ...") winning. Tests: CanvasTests' EditingHooksTests (a fixture slip). Services hear editing end, however it ends (Escape, a click elsewhere, another board, an undo), what it wrote committed (`EditorService.editingDidEnd`), its step closed unless what ended it is an action still under way (an undo about to apply a step, a command, a draw, a file put in place), so a service that answers it waits a turn (`editor.scheduler`): a type's sync takes its words as done then. Every editor of words on the canvas (a note's, an arrow's label's) checks their spelling as the app does (`EditingTextView.spelling = .app`, [text editing](text-editing.md)); its marks show only while it's edited, since the canvas draws a shape's text with string drawing, which marks nothing.
- A kind whose content is its rows written as lines (a table, an enum) makes its editing view with LineRows' `LineRowsView.make(_:context:model:title:)` ([line rows](line-rows.md)): its title typed in its pill (Titles' `TitleField`) and a line field moved to the row with the caret; each row commits as the caret leaves it, an ordinary commit and a step of its own the kind names, never the session's, so it asks `editor.canTransact` (public) before writing as the view is torn down; the title is the session's step. Its editing frame is the laid-out frame and the pill's row above it (`TitleField.editingFrame(around:zoom:)`), the kind laying itself out a line taller while it's edited (LineRows' layout inputs, which the core's lay-out-afresh at the start and end of editing reads).
- A note's editor goes on to another note (StickyNotes' `NoteHops`, a service; tldraw's adjacent notes), through its text view's `onKeyDown` ([text editing](text-editing.md)): ⇧↩ (Return or Enter with ⇧ alone; ↩ and ⌥↩ are the text's; in a typed note's row too, `RowsHost.addBeside`, and held it is one press) ends the edit and puts a new note down beside it as Insert › Note does (`editor.insert(ToolID(placing: StickyNote.kind), beside: [id])`, [editor](editor.md): where Placing puts a note's box beside a shape, `Surroundings.near(id, within: its container)`, [placing](placing.md), the side that lands nearest, the right first; in the note's own color, StickyNotes' `madeBeside`; one step, Add Note, selected and edited as any placed note; nowhere to put it, the note shakes); Tab and ⇧Tab (`AdjacentDirection.next`, `.previous`) edit the next and previous note in reading order among the notes in its container, read-only ones passed over (Selection's `editor.shape(_:from:among:)`, [selection](selection.md)), the note selected and brought into view if it isn't (`revealIfOffscreen`), the edit before kept in its own step; with no other note Tab does nothing, in the text too. A note with rows has its rows first: Tab in its words goes to its first row and Tab off its last row goes on to the next note (Types' `RowsHost.tabbedOut`), and ⇧Tab begins the previous note at its last row (`NoteHops.startsAtLastRow`, asked once as its editor is made), so Tab and ⇧Tab undo each other stop by stop; with no other note, Tab off the last row comes round to the words and ⇧Tab from the words goes to the last row ([Typing in a row](types.md#typing-in-a-row)). Key hints (`registrar.keyHint`, `.text`) list both. StickyNotesTests' `noteHopsTests` hold them.
- A note is laid out by its words and what goes under them (`NoteLayout`, StickyNotes; a `LaidOutBox`, [editor](editor.md)): the paper (`frame`), where its words are drawn (`words`) and where a type's rows go (`block`, nil with none), from one read of `context.layoutInput(TypeRows.extent, of:)` ([types](types.md): Types provides it for a note that is a type or is one, and the note says where its rows go with `typeRows(drawnIn:)`, drawing them in its render, under the editor too). With no rows it is the stored frame and its words that inset by `NoteStyle.padding`, nothing measured: the note as it was drawn before it had any; except a relationship type, which Types says is a pill (`context.layoutInput(TypeRows.pill, of:)`, asked only where there are no rows): a capsule of the size the input says, centered on the middle of the stored frame (the note answers the anchor (0.5, 0.5), `laidOutAnchor`: [editor](editor.md)), its words `.zero` (nothing is drawn in them), no rows, the skeleton a capsule in the type's color with no letters, the outline a capsule, the ports, an arrow's end and the extent all the capsule, and nothing written when it grows or shrinks; edited, its view is a line for its title over the capsule (`TypePillEditor`, [types](types.md#a-relationship-type-is-a-pill)), never the words. With rows it is as wide as it stores (rows never widen a note: one width for every note, `StickyNote.defaultSize`, 260 by 200, so giving a note a type moves nothing beside it, the owner's ask of 2026-10-22, where a typed note's jump to 320 wide covered its neighbors in a frame), its words wrapping where they do with none, and `max(stored height, needed)` tall, `needed` being the padding, the words' height (`NoteWords`), `NoteStyle.blockGap` when there are words, the rows' height and the padding, and never under its least height (`StickyNote.leastHeight`: the height it was given, else a new note's, `NoteStyle.size`, as `fittedHeight` is never under it): paper left over is under the rows. The render fills the paper (its color's `paper` role, `context.colors(of:)`; yellow without a color of its own, and a type's note white whatever its color: `note.colorable(default: .yellow)`; the editing view paints no ground, so the paper shows under it and its ink, the note's gray 0.12, is `paperInk`), draws the words in `words` and the pills and highlights follow it (`mentionText` and `typeWords` take the context); the outline is the paper's; the skeleton fills the laid-out frame (in the same paper) (the kept one: no provider asked, no words measured, its bars counted in it inset by the padding). `NoteWords` keeps each text's lines' height by what it says and how wide (16,384 at most, then the lot is let go), so the same words are never measured twice, and measures only for a note with rows; it is measured as `derive` does, each mention by the name the text holds, so a renamed pill can still wrap past it until the note is edited. `NoteStyle.linesHeight` is the one rounding `fittedHeight` and `NoteWords` share. Debug builds: `NoteWordsCounts.measured` and `block(of:in:)`, for tests.
- A note resizes with Selection's handles on its paper ([selection](selection.md)): the note's definition is `CustomSizing` (the core's, [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md)), so a resize, and `canvas draw`'s whole `size:`, give it a frame through `registry.sized`: it takes the origin and the width (a side's handle that gives no width leaves it), keeps a height it's given as the least it is (`StickyNote.givenHeight`, an unlisted member of its props, version 4, no attribute of a script's: `size:` writes it; nil for a note never given one, whose least is a new note's), and is as tall as its words past it (`derive`). So the words rewrap at the width, a side dragged past them stops there (the side opposite staying put, Selection's), typing grows it past that height and brings it back to it, and a typed note is resized on the frame its rows lay it out in, as wide as dragged and never shorter than its words and rows; never smaller than 80 by 52 by hand (`StickyNote.minimumSize`, a few letters and the padding round one line, its resizing's alone: draw draws one smaller). A relationship type's pill isn't resized (`isResizable`: its size is its name's). StickyNotesTests' `noteResizeTests` and `noteLayoutTests` hold it, CompositionTests' `notePillTests` the pill.

## Testing

- For an editing view's text against its shape's render, `renderBitmap(editor, size:scale:editing:)` (CanvasTesting) draws the view from `editor.makeEditingView()` over the canvas as `CanvasView` lays it out (frame in view points, bounds in world units); `lineTops(_:over:in:scale:)` is where each line starts, against the same drawing without the text, and `largestDifference(_:_:in:scale:)` holds every pixel to anti-aliasing (StickyNotesTests' `noteEditorTests`, at 1× and 2×, zoomed in and out). The note's render (string drawing) and its editor (TextKit 2) share `NoteStyle.text` (a `MentionTextStyle`), whose paragraph fixes each line at 19 points, string drawing's own pitch, and the editor's text starts a point higher (`NoteStyle.editorInset`), since TextKit 2 sets a line's glyphs a point lower in it; so nothing moves when editing starts. A note is as tall as its text, never shorter than its least height (`NoteStyle.fittedHeight`, string drawing's measure, pills and all; `StickyNote.leastHeight`, the height it was given, `givenHeight`, by a resize or `canvas draw`'s `size:`, else a new note's 200): its editor writes the height with the text, one step, growing past the least and coming back to it as the words go, and its text view grows with the editor over it; `DerivedProps` fits a note made from properties (`canvas draw`), each pill measured live by the name it holds (`MentionTextStyle.measuredString`), so it's as tall as its editor fits it and beginning to edit writes nothing. With pills, each pill's frame in the editor (TextKit 2's frame for its attachment) equals the canvas's (`editor.mentionPills(of:)`), and every pixel off the pills is held to anti-aliasing; at a whole number of pixels to a point the lines start alike and the pills' pixels are within a quarter, while at 60% string drawing draws a pill part way into a pixel and TextKit 2 on the nearest, a pixel apart.
- StickyNotesTests' `coloredNoteTests` (`PaperTests.swift`): a plain note the built-in yellow's paper (equal to `NoteStyle.color`), a named color and a hex value their paper in full, at 30% and in the dark, `setColor(nil)` and its undo, a type's note white whatever its color and its color's paper again after Remove Type, the editing view over the paper, a name the file lacks yellow, and the note's ink at 4.5:1 on every built-in paper and on the paper of any color.
- StickyNotesTests' `noteLayoutTests` (`NoteLayoutTests.swift`; on real types, a type of n text fields and notes that are one, the arithmetic read from `TypesStyle`): the stored frame and the words' inset with no rows and nothing measured; four rows on a new note, 260 wide and 200 tall; the padding, words, gap, rows and padding when they need more, and paper under the rows when it stores more; no gap with no words; as wide as it stores, wide or narrow; the words wrapping as they do with no rows; the editor's lines on the render's at the stored width (1× and 2×, zoomed in and out) with the view over the paper; typing storing the words' height at the stored width and nothing of the rows; the type removed, the note its stored size again (and back with undo); the skeleton's paper the laid-out frame; a pill and a type's highlight in the words' box; each words measured once per width (`NoteWordsCounts`); a plain note drawn in full asking the provider and measuring nothing.
