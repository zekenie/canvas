# The engineering kit

The engineering kit (`Sources/Engineering`, product): database tables, their enums and the databases that hold them; the module and its parts, the kinds and what they keep, the column and enum grammars, a table's columns and an enum's values written a line each (what a line offers, its steps), the looks at rest and zoomed out, the ways in (the tools, `/table`, `/database`, Create Database, ⇧↩), `canvas draw`'s `columns:` and `values:`, and what VoiceOver says.

Related: [kits](kits.md), [line rows](line-rows.md), [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md), [titles](titles.md), [containers](containers.md), [frames](frames.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [command line](command-line.md), [boards on disk](boards-on-disk.md), [palette](palette.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Kits](../../docs/plugin-system.md#kits), [The engineering kit](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-engineering-kit). The plan is the owner's doc "Engineering kit" (https://claude.ai/artifact/7bkKR1idCbPUmrZT8FVXBf); its K1, "Tables at rest", and K2, "Write columns", have landed.

## Architecture

- **The module** is one product, in parts (`Package.swift`'s table): `Model` (the kinds' props, `TypeRef`, `KitStyle`), `Grammar` (the line grammars, pure; uses Model) and `Tables` (the three kinds drawn, laid out, placed and written, and their rows written as lines through LineRows; uses both). One module, not a product for tables and one for APIs: an API's grammar will offer tables and its writes reach tables, and no product sees another. The module's own source (`Engineering.swift`) is the kit (`EngineeringKit`, id `engineering`, "Engineering") and its plugin (`EngineeringPlugin`, `engineering`, shipped last), which joins the kit: its tools, their Insert items (Insert ▸ Engineering), `/table`, `/database` and Create Database are the kit's and gone from a file with it off; its kinds, looks, the title field and its draw words are never switched ([kits](kits.md)).
- **The kinds**, each complete at version 0, a fixture each in `Tests/CompositionTests/Fixtures/kinds` holding every member: `table` (`DatabaseTable`: `frame`, `columns`, a `Column` each: `id` a `PartID`, `name`, `type` a `TypeRef`, `nullable`, `unique`, `primaryKey`, `default`, `onDelete`, `onUpdate`, `unread`, the words its line held that no part reads, kept as written), `enum-type` (`DatabaseEnum`: `frame`, `values`, an `EnumValue` each: `id`, `text`; `enum` is a word other modules write, so the kind isn't it) and `database` (`Database`: `frame`, `ShapeContaining`, a region that holds shapes as a frame does, its hollow hit test and marquee rule Frames'). Their names are Titles' (`title`). A new table holds `id uuid pk` (`DatabaseTable.starter()`). A table's and an enum's frame keeps only its width as theirs: the height is laid out from the rows (`LaidOutBox`, 8 above, 24 a row, 12 below, `KitStyle.height(rows:)`, a row's room with none), so a row added or (later) folded writes no box; `DerivedProps` keeps the stored height in step where a shape is made from properties (`canvas draw`), so draw places it at its true size, and `CustomSizing` takes a resize's or `size:`'s place and width and keeps the height its rows'. `TypeRef` is the words as typed and the shape they name once one matches (a column's enum), a name not a binding (`ShapeNaming`: on any board, kept when its shape goes, renamed by a copy), coded `{"words", "shape"}`, its schema `engineering.type-ref`.
- **Parts.** A table's columns and an enum's values are their parts (`PartedShape`, the core's, [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md)): each by its id and name, "Column" or "Value", a key's symbol leading a primary key and a "1" a unique column; none stands alone. Each row's rect is in the kind's geometry (`ShapeGeometry.parts`), as wide as the paper, 24 tall.
- **The grammars** (`Grammar`, pure): `ColumnGrammar` reads a line `name type [null] [unique] [default …] [pk]` (a `?` after the type is null, `not null` and `primary key` read too, the grammar's words in any case, a quoted or parenthesized run one word, as SQL writes `numeric(10, 2)` and `'a b'`) and prints a column back as it reads, `name type null unique default … pk unread`; what it can't place stays in `unread`, a key's `→ table.column` (`->` typed for it) among it until keys land. `read(lines:)` reads a block, a column a line, each a new id; `merge(_:onto:)` joins what a statement wrote onto what a table holds by name: a column it names takes its line, keeping its id, on delete, on update and its type's bound shape while its words say the same; a new name goes last; one it leaves out stays. `EnumGrammar` is a value a line, joined by text. The tests hold that printing what was read reads back as the same column and keeps every word of the line.
- **Looks at rest** (`KitStyle`, shared, in Model; `TableStyle`, `EnumStyle`, `DatabaseStyle` in Tables, a skeleton's constants beside the full look's): the paper #EBEBEB at radius 6 with the note's shadow, the same in both appearances, so its inks are fixed: names 15 points at 35 in, in #111113; types monospaced 13 points, right-aligned 14 from the edge, in the frames' grey made to read on the paper at 4.5 (`Swatch.reading`); a nullable's `?` after its type in the names' ink (the brief's "at full ink"); in the gutter a primary key's key (`key.horizontal`, the frames' gold made a line color on the paper: it measured 2.9) else a unique column's `1.square` in the grey; an enum-typed column's type as a pill (Adornments' `draw(at:in:)`) saying the enum's name now, in Palette's swatch of the frames' purple on the paper (a light tint, its ink at 4.5). An enum's values monospaced 14 points at the names' inset. Text is set once a string and role as a Core Text line (`RowText`, kept to 8,192) and drawn from it; a render draws only the rows its clip reaches. Title pills: a table's `tablecells`, an untitled one's dimmed "Table"; an enum's solid purple (`Swatch(purple)`'s solid, its text the swatch's `onSolid`) after `list.bullet`; a database's `cylinder` in a tab on its top edge, as a frame's (`titleTab`, its surface and border). The database is a region as a frame is: white, or 0.15 grey in the dark, a hairline border (black 12%, white 14%), radius 12, no shadow.
- **Zoomed out** (below the skeleton zoom): the paper square, ringed in black 16% on the light canvas, a bar a name and a bar a type (x-height bars, 0.52 em a column for names and 0.6 for types, at most 40 columns) in the canvas's text-bar fill, an enum-typed column's bar in the purple, a second fill; the database its surface and its border's ring, its tab over it. Their titles are bars where their pills are (Titles' `title(bars: true)`, [titles](titles.md)), the enum's in its purple, never a cover printed over the rows (E8's 30%).
- **Edited**, a table or an enum is LineRows' editing view ([line rows](line-rows.md)): its name typed in its pill (Titles' `TitleField`, its first stop; `editingFrame` the paper and the pill's row above it), then a line a row and the waiting line. Put down by its tool, by Insert or by ⇧↩ (each an editing placement), untitled, the caret is in its name and what's typed joins the step that placed it ("Add Table", "Add Enum"), Return going on to its empty row; `/table orders` puts down a titled one with the caret on its empty row (T1, panel 2); Return on one selected, a double-click, edits it at its empty row, ↑ from its first row back to its name, a name typed again its own step ("Rename Table", "Rename Enum", `editingStepName`).
- **Rows written as lines** (`Tables/Lines.swift`): the kinds opt in with `lineRows(TableLines())` and `lineRows(EnumLines())`. `ColumnLineGrammar` reads a line through the Grammar part's `ColumnGrammar.scan` (the column, and each word's role and UTF-16 range: name, type, keyword, a default's value, the arrow, unread), drawn as the frames draw a focused line: the name in the names' font, the type in code, an enum's name in its purple pill (the at-rest pill's color at 0.14 behind the purple's ink) standing for that enum, the grammar's words and the arrow grey code, a default's value code, what nothing reads plain; the gutter a primary key's key or a unique column's "1". A type's words that a printed or picked run stands for name that enum whatever else is so called; typed afresh they're found by name, the nearest first (on the shape's board, else anywhere in the file: `KitNames`, a service keeping the file's enums by name until `editor.revision` moves), so the column's `TypeRef` binds its enum and its pill says the enum's name now. It prints a column as `ColumnGrammar.print` does, an enum's name as it's called now. Offers: after a name, SQL's types (Postgres's, the common first: `in` lists int, int8, integer, interval, inet first) and the file's enums, each a purple chip lit on the board while highlighted; after a type and a space, null, unique, default, pk and → (what the line says already left out); nothing after `default`, an arrow or words nothing reads. `->` typed becomes `→`; a `→ table.column` stays unread words until K5 makes keys of it. `EnumLineGrammar` is a value a line, as typed, trimmed. `TableLines` keeps what a line doesn't say (on delete, on update) and names its steps Add Column, Paste Columns, Rename Column (the name alone changed), Change Column, Delete Column, Move Column; `EnumLines` Add Value, Paste Values, Rename Value, Delete Value, Move Value (`TableStyle`, `EnumStyle`). Their style is the kit's at rest (`LineLooks`): names 15 points at 35 in, types 13 monospaced, an enum's values 14 monospaced. `EngineeringLines` gives the two grammars to what reads lines as typing does (the tests' corpora).
- **While edited**, a table and an enum read LineRows' layout inputs (`KitRows`): laid out a line taller while the waiting line has the caret, the rows after it a line down, in their render, geometry and skeleton, and the row the field draws not drawn.
- **Ways in**: the Table, Enum and Database tools (no keys; the kit's toolbar segment, Insert ▸ Engineering; the database's a plain placement, 640 × 400, least 96 × 64, a click putting one down); `/table NAME` (a command about the bar's target, `SelectionSlashCommand.atOnce` with its step "Add Table": the starter titled NAME, put down beside the selection or in view through `editor.insert(_:named:beside:)`, the table tool's editing placement, selected and edited at its empty row); `/database NAME` ("Create Database": round the selection; with nothing selected round every shape on the board in no container, the bar's rule; on an empty board an empty one in view); Create Database (`.createDatabase`, run when idle, in the canvas's menu of shapes that hold a table or an enum, `DatabaseMenuGroup`), all through `editor.createDatabase(named:around:)`: the core's `place(_:around:)`, 32 past them, never under its least size, selected, all or nothing, a locked shape among them shaking. ⇧↩ on a table makes a table beside it (the core's insert beside, the table tool's editing placement).
- **`canvas draw`**: `columns:` and `values:` take text, a row a line in the grammars (or the rows as JSON, an id given to an item with none), through Draw's value writers that take text (`drawValueWriter(for:describes:…)`, [command line](command-line.md)); `canvas kinds` says `lines (name type [null] [unique] [default …] [pk]) or JSON [{…}]`. A column's type whose words are an enum's title in the file (found as draw finds a title) is bound to it. Joined by name, so a run again says `unchanged`; an enum's box that grows a row says `moved`, as a note's does with its text. A database is drawn with `size:` and its tables `in` it in a later run. The canvas skill's `reference/engineering.md` teaches it, its blocks run by SkillTests.
- **Keys** (`Tables/Keys.swift`): a key is its arrow and nothing else, on the kit's channel `ForeignKey` (`foreign-key`, no title so no pill, `isOnEveryChannel` false so a subboard takes none, its dots the frames' link blue, its edge type under `arrow`). A table's columns take it both ways (`table.partPorts(ForeignKey.self)`): a dot at each end of the hovered row, a drag drawing a key bound to both rows ("Add Arrow"), the far tables outlined and never an enum or a database (they have no ports). `KeyIndex` (a service) keeps each table's keys, the far row of each column that starts one, read from Topology's `foreign-key` edges with their parts (`forEachEdge(of:.outgoing…view:.literal)`), worked out when asked and forgotten only for the tables a change to those edges touched (`topology.follow`), all of them on a rebuild or a new file: a frame asks it once a table with rows in view. The render reads it for the gutter's link (`TableStyle.linkSymbol`, `linkColor`, the link blue made to read on the paper), a primary key's key before it and a unique's "1" after. The table, the enum and the database adopt the core's `TakesBoundShapes`: deleting one takes its keys ("Delete Table and 3 Arrows"), a column taken out (⌘⌫ then ⌫, LineRows' `removeParts`) its keys ("Delete Column and 1 Arrow": LineRows counts even one), a copy of tables the keys among them. A table and an enum take rows out by id (`PartedShape.removing`, `derive`d to their rows' height) for draw's `delete "orders".x`. The `→ customers.id` chip and the focused line's words, which the line's `→` writes, are K5's; the frames draw neither at rest.
- **VoiceOver** (`x.accessibility`, Titles naming it): "orders, Table, 5 columns", "order_status, Enum, 7 values", "shop, Database, 4 shapes"; the title field is "Table name" or "Enum name".
- Not built yet (later landings of the plan): keys' marks and level curves (K4), a line's `→` making a key and its chip (K5), the row's card (⌘↓), ⌘L from a row, the SQL reader, rows as items, folds, rows across boards, APIs.
