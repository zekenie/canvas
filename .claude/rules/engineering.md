# The engineering kit

The engineering kit (`Sources/Engineering`, product): database tables, their enums and the databases that hold them; the module and its parts, the kinds and what they keep, the column and enum grammars, a table's columns and an enum's values written a line each (what a line offers, its steps), the looks at rest and zoomed out, the ways in (the tools, `/table`, `/database`, Create Database, ⇧↩), `canvas draw`'s `columns:` and `values:`, and what VoiceOver says.

Related: [kits](kits.md), [line rows](line-rows.md), [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md), [titles](titles.md), [containers](containers.md), [frames](frames.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [command line](command-line.md), [boards on disk](boards-on-disk.md), [palette](palette.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Kits](../../docs/plugin-system.md#kits), [The engineering kit](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-engineering-kit). The plan is the owner's doc "Engineering kit" (https://claude.ai/artifact/7bkKR1idCbPUmrZT8FVXBf); its K1, "Tables at rest", and K2, "Write columns", have landed.

## Architecture

- **The module** is one product, in parts (`Package.swift`'s table): `Model` (the kinds' props, `TypeRef`, `KitStyle`), `Grammar` (the line grammars, pure; uses Model) and `Tables` (the three kinds drawn, laid out, placed and written, and their rows written as lines through LineRows; uses both). One module, not a product for tables and one for APIs: an API's grammar will offer tables and its writes reach tables, and no product sees another. The module's own source (`Engineering.swift`) is the kit (`EngineeringKit`, id `engineering`, "Engineering") and its plugin (`EngineeringPlugin`, `engineering`, shipped last), which joins the kit: its tools, their Insert items (Insert ▸ Engineering), `/table`, `/database` and Create Database are the kit's and gone from a file with it off; its kinds, looks, the title field and its draw words are never switched ([kits](kits.md)).
- **The kinds**, each complete at version 0, a fixture each in `Tests/CompositionTests/Fixtures/kinds` holding every member: `table` (`DatabaseTable`: `frame`, `columns`, a `Column` each: `id` a `PartID`, `name`, `type` a `TypeRef`, `nullable`, `unique`, `primaryKey`, `default`, `onDelete`, `onUpdate`, `unread`, the words its line held that no part reads, kept as written), `enum-type` (`DatabaseEnum`: `frame`, `values`, an `EnumValue` each: `id`, `text`; `enum` is a word other modules write, so the kind isn't it) and `database` (`Database`: `frame`, `ShapeContaining`, a region that holds shapes as a frame does, its hollow hit test and marquee rule Frames'). Their names are Titles' (`title`). A new table holds `id uuid pk` (`DatabaseTable.starter()`). A table's and an enum's frame keeps only its width as theirs: the height is laid out from the rows (`LaidOutBox`, 8 above, 24 a row, 12 below, `KitStyle.height(rows:)`, a row's room with none), so a row added or (later) folded writes no box; `DerivedProps` keeps the stored height in step where a shape is made from properties (`canvas draw`), so draw places it at its true size, and `CustomSizing` takes a resize's or `size:`'s place and width and keeps the height its rows'. `TypeRef` is the words as typed and the shape they name once one matches (a column's enum), a name not a binding (`ShapeNaming`: on any board, kept when its shape goes, renamed by a copy), coded `{"words", "shape"}`, its schema `engineering.type-ref`.
- **Parts.** A table's columns and an enum's values are their parts (`PartedShape`, the core's, [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md)): each by its id and name, "Column" or "Value", a key's symbol leading a primary key and a "1" a unique column; none stands alone. Each row's rect is in the kind's geometry (`ShapeGeometry.parts`), as wide as the paper, 24 tall.
- **The grammars** (`Grammar`, pure): `ColumnGrammar` reads a line `name type [null] [unique] [default …] [pk]` (a `?` after the type is null, `not null` and `primary key` read too, the grammar's words in any case, a quoted or parenthesized run one word, as SQL writes `numeric(10, 2)` and `'a b'`; a quote opens a run only where a word starts, so an apostrophe in a name is a letter, and a quote or parenthesis never closed is a letter too, the line's other words staying words; `null`, `unique`, `pk` and `default` are read once, said again they stay with the unread words, in order) and prints a column back as it reads, `name type null unique default … pk unread`; what it can't place stays in `unread`, a key's `→ table.column` (`->` typed for it) among it as read. `key(in:)` says a key's words apart (`KeyWords`: `→` and the word after it, the table, the column, nil for a table alone, and the unread words without them), `target(_:)` reads `table.column`, `table` or `"a table".column` (the column after the last dot), and `written(table:column:)` writes one back, quoting a table's name that holds a dot, a space or a quote. `read(lines:)` reads a block, a column a line, each a new id; `merge(_:onto:)` joins what a statement wrote onto what a table holds by name: a column it names takes its line, keeping its id, on delete, on update and its type's bound shape while its words say the same; a new name goes last; one it leaves out stays. `EnumGrammar` is a value a line, joined by text. The tests hold that printing what was read reads back as the same column and keeps every word of the line.
- **Looks at rest** (`KitStyle`, shared, in Model; `TableStyle`, `EnumStyle`, `DatabaseStyle` in Tables, a skeleton's constants beside the full look's): the paper #EBEBEB at radius 6 with the note's shadow, the same in both appearances, so its inks are fixed: names 15 points at 35 in, in #111113; types monospaced 13 points, right-aligned 14 from the edge, in the frames' grey made to read on the paper at 4.5 (`Swatch.reading`); a nullable's `?` after its type in the names' ink (the brief's "at full ink"); in the gutter a primary key's key (`key.horizontal`, the frames' gold made a line color on the paper: it measured 2.9) else a unique column's `1.square` in the grey; an enum-typed column's type as a pill (Adornments' `draw(at:in:)`) saying the enum's name now, in Palette's swatch of the frames' purple on the paper (a light tint, its ink at 4.5). An enum's values monospaced 14 points at the names' inset. Text is set once a string and role as a Core Text line (`RowText`, kept to 8,192) and drawn from it; a render draws only the rows its clip reaches. Title pills: a table's `tablecells`, an untitled one's dimmed "Table"; an enum's solid purple (`Swatch(purple)`'s solid, its text the swatch's `onSolid`) after `list.bullet`; a database's `cylinder` in a tab on its top edge, as a frame's (`titleTab`, its surface and border). The database is a region as a frame is: white, or 0.15 grey in the dark, a hairline border (black 12%, white 14%), radius 12, no shadow.
- **Zoomed out** (below the skeleton zoom): the paper square, ringed in black 16% on the light canvas, a bar a name and a bar a type (x-height bars, 0.52 em a column for names and 0.6 for types, at most 40 columns) in the canvas's text-bar fill, an enum-typed column's bar in the purple, a second fill; the database its surface and its border's ring, its tab over it. A column's type is a pill when the enum it names is still in the file, the same test as at rest. Where a row is under 2 view points (`TableStyle.skeletonLeastRow`, far out: 6% and less) no bars are drawn for the rows, only the paper and the title's bar, so a board of thousands of tables costs a card each (`TableBenchmarks`: 10,000 tables, some 4,000 in view at 6%, 20 ms with the bars and 10 without). Their titles are bars where their pills are (Titles' `title(bars: true)`, [titles](titles.md)), the enum's in its purple, never a cover printed over the rows (E8's 30%).
- **Edited**, a table or an enum is LineRows' editing view ([line rows](line-rows.md)): its name typed in its pill (Titles' `TitleField`, its first stop; `editingFrame` the paper and the pill's row above it), then a line a row and the waiting line. Put down by its tool, by Insert or by ⇧↩ (each an editing placement), untitled, the caret is in its name (the pill the glyph and the caret, no placeholder, and ringed only while its text has the keys) and what's typed joins the step that placed it ("Add Table", "Add Enum"), Return going on to its empty row; `/table orders` puts down a titled one with the caret on its empty row (T1, panel 2); Return on one selected, a double-click, edits it at its empty row, ↑ from its first row back to its name, a name typed again its own step ("Rename Table", "Rename Enum", `editingStepName`).
- **Rows written as lines** (`Tables/Lines.swift`): the kinds opt in with `lineRows(TableLines())` and `lineRows(EnumLines())`. `ColumnLineGrammar` reads a line through the Grammar part's `ColumnGrammar.scan` (the column, and each word's role and UTF-16 range: name, type, keyword, a default's value, the arrow, unread), drawn as the frames draw a focused line: the name in the names' font, the type in code, an enum's name in its purple pill (the at-rest pill's color at 0.14 behind the purple's ink) standing for that enum, the grammar's words and the arrow grey code, a default's value code, what nothing reads plain; the gutter a primary key's key or a unique column's "1". A type's words that a printed or picked run stands for name that enum whatever else is so called; typed afresh they're found by name, the nearest first (on the shape's board, else anywhere in the file: `KitNames`, a service keeping the file's enums by name until `editor.revision` moves), so the column's `TypeRef` binds its enum and its pill says the enum's name now. It prints a column as `ColumnGrammar.print` does, an enum's name as it's called now. A line's key (K5): the first `→` and the word after it name a key once that word names a table's row on the shape's board: the run's reference (printed, or picked from the list), on the whole target or on the column after its dot, else by name (`KitNames.tableNamed(_:besides:)`, the board's tables by title; a table alone is its primary key's first column), and only where something draws connections (`registry.drawsConnections`). The row read is a `ColumnLine` (public: the `Column` and a `ColumnKey`, `.stored` for a column as the board has it, `.none`, or `.to(PartAddress)`), and a key read leaves the column's `unread` without its words; a `→` that names nothing stays there, plain. Its target is drawn in the link blue's pill (`LineLooks.keyFill`, `keyInk`; `TableStyle.keyPillFill` 0.14), the table's name already as `customers.` is typed, and the gutter shows the link while the line has a key. A focused line prints the key the line holds, or for a column as stored the key index's, `→ customers.id` by the far table's and column's names now (a reference to the row), so a rename follows with nothing written; VoiceOver's help says "key to id in customers". Offers: after a name, SQL's types (Postgres's, the common first: `in` lists int, int8, integer, interval, inet first) and the file's enums, each a purple chip lit on the board while highlighted; after a type and a space, null, unique, default, pk and → (what the line says already left out); right after the `→`, the tables on the shape's board Mentions' search finds for what's typed (`candidates(…kinds: [table], admitting:)`, nearest and recent first with nothing typed), each one's key columns as `customers.id` with its type (a key column is its primary key's or a unique one; all of a table's where it has none), and after `customers.` that table's key columns by name, the offer's range what follows the dot; each lit while highlighted (its row's rect: LineRows' light); nothing after `default`, a key's target or words nothing reads. `->` typed becomes `→`. `EnumLineGrammar` is a value a line, as typed, trimmed. `TableLines` (made with the table and the key index for a session, `TableLines(table:keys:)`) keeps what a line doesn't say (on delete, on update), writes its changes itself (`apply`: the columns, then each line's key with its column in the row's one transaction, `KeyArrows.write`: the arrow made by `registry.drawConnection(…parts:)`, re-aimed by moving its far end with `movingParts` so it keeps its id and bend, or the column's arrows deleted where the line names none; a column as stored writes no key; a key that can't be drawn refuses the commit and the table shakes) and names its steps Add Column (a new row, with its key or not), Paste Columns, Rename Column (the name alone changed), Add Key, Change Key and Delete Key (the key alone made, re-aimed or taken out, judged against the board's for a row as stored), Change Column, Delete Column, Move Column; `EnumLines` Add Value, Paste Values, Rename Value, Delete Value, Move Value (`TableStyle`, `EnumStyle`). Their style is the kit's at rest (`LineLooks`): names 15 points at 35 in, types 13 monospaced, an enum's values 14 monospaced. `EngineeringLines` gives the two grammars to what reads lines as typing does (the tests' corpora).
- **While edited**, a table and an enum read LineRows' layout inputs (`KitRows`): laid out a line taller while the waiting line has the caret, the rows after it a line down, in their render, geometry and skeleton, and the row the field draws not drawn.
- **Ways in**: the Table, Enum and Database tools (no keys; the kit's toolbar segment, Insert ▸ Engineering; the database's a plain placement, 640 × 400, least 96 × 64, a click putting one down); `/table NAME` (a command about the bar's target, `SelectionSlashCommand.atOnce` with its step "Add Table": the starter titled NAME, put down beside the selection or in view through `editor.insert(_:named:beside:)`, the table tool's editing placement, selected and edited at its empty row); `/database NAME` ("Create Database": round the selection; with nothing selected round every shape on the board in no container, the bar's rule; on an empty board an empty one in view); Create Database (`.createDatabase`, run when idle, in the canvas's menu of shapes that hold a table or an enum, `DatabaseMenuGroup`, after Transform's as Create Frame's is, so the two sit together), all through `editor.createDatabase(named:around:)`: the core's `place(_:around:)`, 32 past them, never under its least size, selected, all or nothing, a locked shape among them shaking. ⇧↩ on a table makes a table beside it (the core's insert beside, the table tool's editing placement).
- **`canvas draw`**: `columns:` and `values:` take text, a row a line in the grammars (or the rows as JSON: an id given to an item with none, and only the members an item names joined onto the column of its name, so a restated column keeps the rest, its on delete and on update unless it gives them, and a new row whose id another has is given one of its own), through Draw's value writers that take text (`drawValueWriter(for:describes:…)`, [command line](command-line.md)); `canvas kinds` says `lines (name type [null] [unique] [default …] [pk] [→ table.column]) or JSON [{…}]`. A line's `→ customers.id` is an edge the value implies (`implies:`, `RowsDraw.keys`): `edge "orders".customer_id -> "customers".id channel: foreign-key` on the statement's line, so the column keeps none of its words; a table alone (`→ customers`) is its primary key's row for a table the file has (one the same script draws needs its column named, `invalid_value` otherwise); an explicit edge between the same rows is that edge; a line without `→` leaves a key alone, and a `→` that aims a column elsewhere than its key is that key re-aimed, as the line and ⌘L's pick do (a column has the one key its line says: the run deletes the column's other edges of the `foreign-key` type, `DrawImpliedEdge.replacesType`, but an `edge` the script writes itself stays; `delete edge "orders".customer_id -> "customers".id` takes one). `→ customers` alone is refused for what is wrong, an ambiguous table's name as any, or a table the file doesn't have yet (name the column). A column's type whose words are an enum's title in the file (found as draw finds a title) is bound to it; one that is called by two enums is refused, `ambiguous_title`, as any such name is, not left words without a word, while a type of SQL's own, or a table's title, stays words. Joined by name, so a run again says `unchanged`; an enum's box that grows a row says `moved`, as a note's does with its text. A database is drawn with `size:` and its tables `in` it in a later run. The canvas skill's `reference/engineering.md` teaches it, its blocks run by SkillTests.
- **Keys** (`Tables/Keys.swift`): a key is its arrow and nothing else, on the kit's channel `ForeignKey` (`foreign-key`, no title so no pill, `isOnEveryChannel` false so a subboard takes none, its dots the frames' link blue, its edge type under `arrow`). A table's columns take it both ways (`table.partPorts(ForeignKey.self)`): a dot at each end of the hovered row, a drag drawing a key bound to both rows ("Add Arrow"), the far tables outlined and never an enum or a database (they have no ports). `KeyIndex` (a service) keeps each table's keys, the far row of each column that starts one, read from Topology's `foreign-key` edges with their parts (`forEachEdge(of:.outgoing…view:.literal)`), worked out when asked and forgotten only for the tables a change to those edges touched (`topology.follow`), all of them on a rebuild or a new file: a frame asks it once a table with rows in view. The render reads it for the gutter's link (`TableStyle.linkSymbol`, `linkColor`, the link blue made to read on the paper), a primary key's key before it and a unique's "1" after. The table, the enum and the database adopt the core's `TakesBoundShapes`: deleting one takes its keys ("Delete Table and 3 Arrows"), a column taken out (⌘⌫ then ⌫, LineRows' `removeParts`) its keys ("Delete Column and 1 Arrow": LineRows counts even one), a copy of tables the keys among them. A key never goes through a pill yet (a reference pill for a table has no `foreign-key` ports: `KindPorts.standing(for:)` skips what its target takes on rows only, `connect` refuses a row on another board, and a move lets a crossing key go: [moving](moving.md)); that is K10's. A table and an enum take rows out by id (`PartedShape.removing`, `derive`d to their rows' height) for draw's `delete "orders".x`. `KeyIndex` keeps each key's arrows too (`arrows(of:in:)`), for a line that re-aims or takes its key out and a pick that replaces it. No `→ customers.id` chip at rest: the frames draw none; the focused line prints the key (above).
- **How a key looks** (K4, `KeyLooks` in `Tables/Keys.swift`, the kit's answer to Connections' `ConnectionLooks`, [connections](connections.md)): for an arrow on `foreign-key`, from the column its start is at, a crow's foot at that end (`many`), two bars when the column is unique (`one`: it says `unique`, or it is its table's one primary key column; one of two isn't), and at the row it references two bars (`one`), a bar and a circle when the column may be null (`zeroOrOne`); the line `KitStyle.keyLineWidth`, 2.5, and ink as every line is (the colors of marks, pills and dots elsewhere are Palette's swatches, decided 25). A key whose start is at a table as a whole has a plain column's marks. Nothing is stored: Arrows asks when it works out the key's geometry, and the column is read through the shapes it is handed, so a column made unique or nullable (its line, draw, an undo) redraws its keys' marks with nothing written to them (T3 panel 4, T4 panel 2). Every key is a level curve, its ends at its rows' sides ([arrows](arrows.md)).
- **Where a line to a table meets it**: the table's geometry gives its first shown row's two sides as its entries (`ShapeGeometry.entries`, `ShapeEntry.sides(of:)`, the core's), so a level arrow bound to the table as a whole (a row's arrow to a table, E8 panel 2) meets that row at the side facing it; a table with no rows gives none, and an enum or a database none.
- **VoiceOver** (`x.accessibility`, Titles naming it): "orders, Table, 5 columns", "order_status, Enum, 7 values", "shop, Database, 4 shapes"; the title field is "Table name" or "Enum name".
- **⌘L's picker on a table** (`KeyPicks`, the table kind's `ConnectPicks`): it lists only tables on the board shown (never an enum, a database or another kind). Opened on a row being edited (LineRows' ⌘L, [line rows](line-rows.md)) it lists the tables and, by their paths, their columns (Mentions' catalog rows: `orders cust`, `orders.cust`); a column picked is the key's far row, a table its primary key's; the pick draws the key in place of any the column had (the plan's `prepare` deletes its arrows), step "Connect". Opened on the table, a table picked makes the key column for it (`KeyPicks.keyColumnName`: its name lowercased, its spaces underscores, singular by English's commonest endings, `_id`, `_2` after a name the table has; typed as the far primary key's type, a serial's an integer, `keyType(of:)`), put after the table's own keys (its primary key's columns and those with a key), its key drawn, one step, "Add Key Column" (`TableStyle.addKeyColumnStep`).
- **Keys typed, as reviewed.** A focused line prints a key's target even when the far table has no name yet, as the pill's placeholder (`Table.id`) standing for the row (its run's reference), so editing the column keeps the key; a line that reads a key it prints is never one with none. A step's name says what changed on the board: a key's words kept before their table existed read as a key when it does, and the first edit draws it ("Change Column"). A table's name typed in `→` is found as draw finds a title (its whitespace folded), and a column is never its own key (`→ orders.customer_id` on that column: words, no arrow). A locked table writes no key (`TableLines.apply` is false for a read-only shape, and the table shakes). Rows of a table with no primary key are in ⌘L's picker from a row, the table as a whole is not (it has no row to key to), and Itself is not on the primary key's own row (it would key it to itself). The key column's name (`keyColumnName`) keeps `status`, `campus` and `analysis` and singularizes `statuses`, `buses` and `analyses`. ⌘L in a row has a key hint ("⌘L in a Row": "Connect the Row"), and the menu's Connect, while a row has the caret, opens the picker on the row as the key does.
- Not built yet (later landings of the plan): the row's card (⌘↓), the SQL reader, rows as items, folds, rows across boards (a key typed or picked to a table on another board: K10; the line names only tables on its own board), APIs.
