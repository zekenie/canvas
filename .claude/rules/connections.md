# Connections: ports, channels and the connector

Connections: ports and their dots, channels, the one connector, connections made by name, and the port table every question about ports reads.

Related: [arrows](arrows.md), [topology](topology.md), [selection](selection.md), [command line](command-line.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Ports](../../docs/plugin-system.md#ports).

## Architecture

- Connections (a concern, on Selection, TableOfContents, TextEditing and Topology) holds ports, channels and the arrow, in three plugin values (`PortsPlugin`, `ArrowsPlugin` and `ArrowPortsPlugin`), so a list can have arrows without ports (ArrowsTests) or ports with a connector of its own (PortsTests); it owns the arrow, the shape a connection is drawn as (tldraw's arc arrows: [arrows](arrows.md)). Ports (data-canvas's) are dots just outside a shape's sides, from which a drag draws a connection (an arrow) that may end only on a shape that takes its channel. A channel is a kind of connection, a type (`Channel`: `static let id: ChannelID`, `static var color` for its dots, computed, gray by default, and `static var summary`, nil by default, its edge type's), declared by the plugin that owns what it means (`registrar.channel(NoteLink.self)`, the `Channels` point; one declaration per id), which declares Topology's edge type of the same name too, under the connector's family (a root without a connector). `ChannelID` is what a connection keeps, so it never changes once shipped, and the id audit holds its literal to the module that declares it. A kind has ports on a channel with `x.ports(C.self, sides:role:)` (`registrar.ports(K.self, on: C.self, sides:role:)` for another plugin's kind; the `ShapePorts` point, one per kind and channel, and a kind may have ports on several): a dot at the middle of each of `sides` (`PortSide`, all four by default), and a `PortRole`, `.both` (the default), `.source` (dots; nothing ends on it) or `.target` (connections end on it; no dots). `ShapePorts.check` refuses ports on a channel no installed plugin declares, or on the connector's own kind. One plugin draws connections, the connector (`registrar.connects(K.self, edgeType: F.self, canConnect:make:ends:begin:)`, the `Connectors` point, at most one): `F` is the family of edges connections are (an `EdgeType` it declares; with `ends`, a connection bound at both ends is one edge, of its channel's type, else `F`'s, and `F`'s too for a channel that names a declared type that isn't a channel's; a free end, none), `K` opts into `ChannelProperty` (key `channel`, nil by default, so only a connection drawn on a channel has one; one `canvas draw` makes with `channel: none` has none), `canConnect` says which shapes one may start from and end on in a `ShapeContext` (the editor's for the dots, a transaction's for a connection made by name: `registry.canConnect(_:in:)`), `begin` gets the `Connection` (`shape`, `side`, the dot's `channels` in the port table's order, `origin`, where it was pressed, world units) and returns the session the press hands the drag to (nil: nothing), and the optional `make` and `ends` connect shapes without a drag: `registry.connection(_:existing:in:)` makes a `ConnectionRequest` (ends, optional sides, channel, both heads) into a connection, not inserted, or changes `existing` (only named sides and the heads; `existing` itself when nothing would change, so `==` tells unchanged), and `registry.connectionEnds(of:)` is which shapes one joins. Without a connector no shape shows ports. Every question about ports reads one port table per kind (`KindPorts`, PortTable.swift: plugin order, a kind's first contribution on a channel its only one there), the registry's lookups too (`connectorKind`, `makesConnections`, `dotChannels(of:side:)`, `takes(_:_:as:side:)`, `connectionChannel(from:fromSide:to:toSide:)`, …), so a drag and a connection made by name pick channels alike (an edge `canvas draw` draws with no channel written is on `connectionChannel`'s, as a drag's is on `editor.channel(of:endingAt:)`'s). `editor.channel(of:endingAt:)` is the first of a connection's channels a shape's kind takes as a target (nil for the shape it starts from, or one kept aside); `registry.accepts(shape, on:as:)` and `registry.declares(_:)` are what rules for connections already drawn go by. The dots are Selection's canvas buttons, dragged (`PortDot`, from `PortState.dot(atViewPoint:)`), shown for one shape, the host: the one whose dot the pointer is on (any shape's, so a dot reached straight from empty canvas shows its shape's, as data-canvas's halo does), else the shape with dots under the pointer, kept while the pointer stays within reach of its dots (its view bounds grown by 24 points) over empty canvas or a shape without ports; none while a handle is under the pointer (it takes the hover), and none once the tool changes or the shape is deleted. A shape has dots when its kind has a side that isn't `.target`, it isn't kept aside, and `canConnect` allows it. They show in the select tool only, at 25% zoom and up (`PortStyle.minZoom`), while the pointer's over the canvas (`editor.pointerOverCanvas`) and, where it is now (`editor.pointerWorldLocation`), within reach of them, and nothing is under way but a dot's press: each 12 view points out from the middle of its side of the shape's bounds, a disc 8 points across in its first channel's color at 60% in a white 1.5-point ring, and hovered or pressed 10 points across at full color in a halo 24 points across at 24% (`PortStyle`'s radii: 4, 5, 12); constant on screen, following the host's drawing offset (a locked shape's shake). A dot shows, and is hit, only while no selectable shape painted above its own covers its center (an arrow leaving the shape there, a neighbor on top; `PortState.liveDots(of:)`), so a shape lying on a bigger one shows its dots over it. The nearest showing dot within 12 view points is pressed (none where the select tool hasn't seen the pointer move, just after a tool change or a first click into the window, since none shows there); past the drag threshold the connector draws, and while it does each shape in view the connection could end on is outlined faintly, as a hover (along the core's `editor.outlinePath(of:)`, so on the line a hover's is, stood off the edge; the one it would end on now gets the hint). `PortsPlugin` comes after `AdornmentsPlugin` in the shipped list, so a dot is found before a lock chip (a clickable adornment) and draws above it. Locked shapes keep their ports: connecting doesn't change them. `ArrowPortsPlugin` makes arrows the connector (its family `ArrowEdge`, `arrow`, its id made from `Arrow.kind`; `canConnect` is `shapes.canBindArrow(to:)`, `begin` is `editor.beginArrow(from:at:canBind:complete:)`, internal to Connections, `canBind` a shape that takes one of the port's channels (`editor.channel(of:endingAt:)`), and `complete` sets the arrow's `ChannelProperty`; `make` binds both ends, an unnamed side at the center (imprecise) and a named one at its middle (precise, not exact), its channel set before `registry.arrowMayBind` asks the rules), and its `arrowBindingRule` keeps an arrow with a channel some installed plugin declares to shapes that take it at that end (the start as `.source`, the end as `.target`), re-aimed or moved; one without (`channel: none`'s), or with an undeclared channel, binds wherever an arrow can. Shipped: notes have ports on `NoteLink` (`note-link`, a brown amber) on all four sides (`NoteLinks.swift`), and schema cards on `SchemaLink` (`schema-link`, the card's accent) on all four too (`SchemaLinks.swift`; the top dot is in the row above the shape where a titled shape's pill is, so a title long enough to reach the middle of the top edge runs under it: the dot draws above the pill and is pressed before it, as before a lock chip), each declared by its kind's plugin, so a note links only to a note and a schema card only to a schema card.

## Testing

- For ports, PortsTests use fixtures only, and no arrow: `portsHarness` installs Keyboard, Selection, Topology, the fixtures, Ports, `channelsPlugin` (boxes have ports on its `Wire` channel, `sides:` and `role:` as a test asks, discs on `Pipe`) and a recording connector over CanvasTesting's `Tag` (`connectorPlugin(ConnectionLog())`: `begun` lists each `Connection`, its session records each drag in `drags`, and `canConnect` and `draws` refuse; its family is `Hookup`, and `edgeTests` hold connections as edges). A 200 × 100 box at (100, 100) (`boxFrame`) has its dots at `topDot`, `rightDot`, `bottomDot` and `leftDot` at zoom 1. Move onto a dot before pressing, as a user does (`h.hoverDot` comes from the shape's middle): dots draw only while `editor.pointerOverCanvas` is set. ArrowPortsTests draw real arrows between fixtures on a `PortBoard` (boxes A and B where the POC's first two welcome notes are, taking `Wire`, and a disc C below B taking `Pipe`; `board.connect(to:speed:release:)` hovers A and drags from its right dot, `aRightDot`, at `fast` or `slow`), and read `board.arrows`, their bindings and their `ChannelProperty` (`arrowEdgeTests`, their edges).
