# TextEditing

TextEditing, the foundation under every editable text: `EditingTextView`, and the app's one caret list (`CaretList`, `CaretListWindow`, `CaretListMatcher`).

Related: [mentions](mentions.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [editing](editing.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [TextEditing](../../docs/plugin-system.md#textediting).

## Architecture

- TextEditing (a foundation module that depends on no other of the project's, so it knows no canvas, registry, plugin or panel; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "TextEditing") is the text under every editable text in the app (a note's editor, a schema card's description, an arrow's label, the detail panel's boxes) and the one list shown at a caret. `EditingTextView` is an open `NSTextView` with what a host needs and `TextEditor` doesn't give, which a plugin subclasses for text of its own (the panel's `DetailTextContent`s, Mentions' `MentionTextView`): `onCommit`, `commitsOnReturn`, `onExit`, `onLeave` (the keyboard leaving, whatever for) and `placeholder` do nothing until set, so without them it's a plain text view, and `commitsOnLeaving` off leaves only ⌘↩ to `onCommit`, as a note's editor on the canvas, whose ⌘↩ ends editing; `commitsWhileReadOnly` lets ⌘↩ commit read-only text, for a commit that only ends editing (a schema card's description while the model writes it); it draws its placeholder in `drawBackground`, since AppKit gives a class that overrides `draw(_:)` TextKit 1, in the view's font or else `TextEditingStyle.placeholderFont` (the system's, 11 pt); `highlights` (`TextHighlight`: an `NSRange` and a fill) are drawn there too, under the text, as rounded rects a mention's pill tall that reach 2 points past the text, a rect a line (`frames(of:)`, TextKit 2 only, as the caret's frame is), and are let go of when the view is made read-only, so an owner marks text with nothing for a host to forward; `showEnabled(_:)` dims it, by default by its text's color, the label's or the secondary label's. A subclass that shows a list at its caret keeps its window, `caretListWindow` (a `MentionTextView`'s `@` picker or slash commands' list, a code editor's completions), and `EditingTextView` does the rest for every such list alike: while one's open it `holdsKeys`, so the list's commands (`CaretList.perform`) are the list's, before the delegate's turn (through the window's `pickHighlighted()` and `closeList()`, which run the owner's), and Return and Escape are never `onCommit`'s nor `onExit`'s, while ⌘↩, where the text commits, closes the list and commits, as with none; the keyboard leaving, the view leaving its window and its being made read-only close it; what's left to the subclass is when it opens, what it lists and what a pick puts in (a subclass may `holdsKeys` for something else too). ⌘., which the window sends up the responder chain as `cancelOperation:`, closes an open list (the view's `cancelOperation(_:)`, answered only while one is open, so with none it goes on up the chain as though the view weren't there). Tests read a list through `caretListWindow`. Such a list is the app's one caret list, whatever text it's over (Mentions' `@` picker; slash commands' list; a code editor's completions): `CaretList`, the model, headless (`CaretListRow`s: headers, items and one empty state; a `CaretListItem` is a title with its `matched` characters semibold, in an optional `font`, an optional symbol and `tint` before it, a `trailingSymbol` after it, a `detail` at the trailing end, which takes what the title leaves, or `TextEditingStyle.caretListDetailShare` of the row where both are long, and what VoiceOver says, `spoken`, the title and detail by default; the first item highlighted, ↑ ↓ wrapping and a page, `rowsShown`, stopping at the ends; `perform(_:)` takes the text view's commands: ↑ ↓ ⌃P ⌃N, Page Up and Down, Return and Tab pick, but not with nothing highlighted, Escape, ⌥⎋ and F5 close), sized by its owner's `CaretListMetrics` (width, row height, rows shown, offset below the anchor; its look is `TextEditingStyle`'s), and shown in a `CaretListWindow(list, accessibilityLabel:over:anchor:pick:close:post:)`, a borderless child window that never takes the keyboard (not deferred: a click's events name it, shown or not, and a board's window leaves clicks in its children alone, so a pick with the mouse is part of the typing), placed under the anchor range's `firstRect` (its rows' text under the anchor's start, above the line where there's no room below, on screen), following every ancestor's frame and bounds, asking its owner to `close` once the anchor leaves the text's visible rect or the window resigns key, and telling VoiceOver through `post` (Mentions routes it through `MentionAccessibility`); its owner calls `listDidChange()` after each change and `dismiss()` as it closes, and decides when it opens and closes, what the query is and what a pick inserts. A list over text that its own substitutions would rewrite (an `@query`, a `/name`) takes them as it opens, turns them off while it's open and puts them back as they were once it closes: `CaretListSubstitutions` (spelling correction, text replacement, smart quotes and smart dashes: `init(of: view)` takes the view's, `off` is all four off, `apply(to:)` sets a view's). Every list ranks typed text with one matcher, `CaretListMatcher` (the tiers are `CaretListRanking`; `rank` is the two passes every list makes, over labels given by index, nil for one it leaves out, the typo tier only when fewer than 8 matched above, which Mentions' catalog makes over its keys and `matches(_:in:)` over a list of labels).

## Testing

- For text editing, TextEditingTests (the caret list's Suite, Caret lists, over TestKit and CanvasTesting alone) hold the model and matcher headlessly, the window over a plain `EditingTextView` in an unshown window (`ListedText`), where it's placed but never ordered in: read its `frame`, and call the rows view's mouse methods for a click, and a subclass's list's keys and closing, `EditingTextView`'s (`ListingTextView`). Its Highlights suite holds `frames(of:)` (a rect a line, centered, padded, none for a range that isn't in the text), the redraw when they change and the fill drawn in the view's coordinates. The view with a box's value and keys is held where the box is, DetailPanelTests'.
