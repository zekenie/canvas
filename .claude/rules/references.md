# References

References, the `reference` kind: a pill that stands for another shape anywhere in the file; its props and writers, its look in every state, what it stands for, going to the original, its row, its panel and its fixture; and the mirror, the pill expanded into its original drawn again under it.

Related: [publishing](publishing.md), [topology](topology.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [selection](selection.md), [shape references](shape-references.md), [editing](editing.md), [connections](connections.md), [command line](command-line.md), [language models](language-models.md), [queries](queries.md), [neighbors](neighbors.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [References](../../docs/plugin-system.md#references).

## Architecture

- References (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait, not a vertical: any public shape can be stood for, a kind written after it too, and a pill holds nothing of its own, only another shape shown in a second place; it declares its `ShapeKind` all the same, the one platform module that does; what it imports and why are [build](build.md)'s Who builds on whom, and a plugin list with it needs their plugins (but `BoardsPlugin`: Boards is only for what a container is called in its row's path, `registry.containerName(of:)`), `PortsPlugin` and `ValidationPlugin` among them: the registry refuses a contribution to `ShapePorts` or `ValidationRules` without its owner; without `QueriesPlugin` a dynamic reference finds nothing and says so, and without `QueryTranslationPlugin` (and a model) words stay not interpreted: nothing is asked and no Interpret is offered) is a pill that stands for a shape, so a shape too many others point at needs no arrow from each: an arrow into the pill is, for everything that reads the graph (queries, the table of contents, Topology's readers), an arrow into the shape ([topology](topology.md): the kind says `standsIn`; the resolved view does the rest). Only a public shape can be stood for ([publishing](publishing.md): `admitting: { registry in registry.publicShapes }`, read on any thread). It writes the ids `reference` (kind), `reference` (tool, key R, after Schema in the shipped list, `editingPlacement`: a click places an empty 120 by 40 pill and edits it, step "Add Reference"), `references` (plugin), the command `goToOriginal` and `ReferencesMenuGroup`; it is not mentionable, not titled, never public (`ReferenceDefinition: NeverPublic`, so it has no `private` property and no chip), and has no `resizable` (its width is its text's), no opener (Return and a double-click edit) and ports on every channel on its left and right (`portsOnEveryChannel`, so it takes what it stands for takes: [connections](connections.md), "Ports at a stand-in").
- The props (`Reference: BoxShapeProps`; fixture `Fixtures/kinds/reference.v1.json` pins all five, and breaks the mode rule on purpose, since a fixture proves only the encoding; the kind is at version 1 (`PropsMigration("height 40")`): the pill is 40 tall instead of 32, for its place over its name, and a pill stored before keeps its center (its frame's origin moves down by half the difference); `reference.v0.json` stays as the pill was stored): `frame` (height 40, the width stored and refitted), `target: ReferenceTarget?` (a `ShapeNaming` leaf, `{id, label}`: a name, not a binding, so it crosses boards, outlives its shape, is renamed by a copy that takes the shape along and kept by a copy that doesn't, by cut and paste, and by a deletion, which writes nothing; `label` is what it was called when picked, shown dashed once the shape is gone), `words` (what was typed), `query: String?` (the query those words mean, canonical, nil until interpreted; schema format `x-canvas-query`, so `canvas draw` checks it) `all` (every match, or the first), `look` and `scale` (version 2; below). `target` and `words` are `readOnly()` (a script can't write them: `unknown_attribute`), `query`, `all`, `look` and `scale` are drawable (`reference query: "…" all: true`, `reference query: "…" look: mirror scale: 0.5`; `look: banana` is `invalid_value` listing `pill|mirror`, a `scale` past its schema bounds 0.1 to 4 is drawn and warned about, as every bound is, and the render clamps it; a direct pill takes neither: its id is `invalid_value` and an edge takes no `look:`, so a mirror of one shape is a reference of its own with `query: "#<id>"`; `Tests/CompositionTests/ReferenceLookShippedTests.swift`; a query reads them as `reference[look="mirror"]`, `reference[scale>0.5]`), and `canvas kinds` reports no edges for it (it has no title to name it by). A later script names one that has a query by its id, which the run's `created` row and a query's row give (Draw's `registry.drawCanName(_:)` of a record takes a stand-in that stands for what's provided, [command line](command-line.md), ids as names): an edge into or out of it, drawn straight on its own ports (the other end's first channel), a restatement of its `query` or `all`, a placement's target, a group, a delete, which takes its arrows; a direct one stays named by the shape it stands for, its id `invalid_value`. What it stands for is read by `:edge-from(#<id>, stands-for)`, which waits for its answer as any query that reads edges does (the skill's `reference/language.md`, References). The mode is derived, never stored: `reference.mode` is `.direct` if `target` is set (it wins), else `.dynamic` for words or a query, else `.empty`; `reference.text` is a dynamic one's words, else its query. `ReferenceEdits` (`pick`, `find(words:query:)`, `clear`, `width(of:place:)`) is how a reference is written: each keeps exactly one mode and refits `frame.width` to what it now says: `ReferenceStyle.padding` (46: the pads, the symbol and the gap) and the wider of the label at the wider of its plain and italic setting with what may trail it (a dynamic pill's badge room or a direct one's glyph room: `ReferenceStyle`'s) and, for a direct pill, its place's line folded at 234 (`ReferenceStyle.placeRoomMost`, `placeLine`; Chrome's `PlaceFold` in SF 11, a board's name semibold), clamped 64 to 280, growing rightward: a long address never pads the pill to the whole of it, and `ReferenceDefinition: DerivedProps` fits a reference `canvas draw` makes the same way.
- Made by name (`standsIn(needs: "public", makes:)`): `makes` is `ReferenceEdits.pick` with the shape's id and the name to show, so `registry.standIns.make(Reference.kind, standingFor:, label:)` is a direct reference fitted to its label as a picked one is, which is how `canvas draw` puts one beside a shape for an edge to a shape on another board ([command line](command-line.md)); `seats` is `ReferenceEdits.fit(&props, place:)` with what `ReferenceState.place(of:at:in:)` (`ReferencePath.swift`) says of the seat, steps (`PlaceStep`, Chrome's): the containers the shape is inside that the seat isn't, outermost first, each with whether it opens a board, and the board that leads when the shape is on another board and none of them opens one (`placeSteps(of:seatedIn:on:)` for a seat in the file, read through the state's paths as `placeOfTarget` reads the pill's own containers once it sits there; a written one's names compared as written, what they share from the top left out, those the seat's `opening` names in their board weight, and, as in the file, the board that leads when the shape is on another board than the pill (the last container of its names that opens one, else the file's name) and none of the steps opens one), nil for none, so every pill draw puts down is as wide as it then says, as a pick is, whatever its title; `fulfilling` is `registry.makePublic(&record)`, so a caller that must stand for a private shape makes it public in its own step (`standIns.fulfil`: a person's own pick; `canvas draw` and `canvas apply` never call it and refuse a private shape); `calls` gives `registry.standIns.calls(pill)` the label a direct pill keeps (a dynamic one's words), which a dashed pill keeps of a gone note across a reopen; `needs` is the word a message says of a shape that can't be stood for ("only a public shape can be stood for"). A script still can't write `target` or `words`, and the pill has no title. Several of a script's edges may run to one pill: `share: true` or `share: "A"` on an edge reuses a direct reference to the same end beside the end that stays's container, or the one A's arrow goes into, and `canvas measure` says how wide a pill for a title is (`standIns.make`'s box, 280 at most) before one is drawn ([command line](command-line.md)).
- What it stands for (`standsIn`, the kind's contribution): `.shapes([target.id])` for a direct reference, admitted while the shape is public, so the commit that picks, re-points, renames-to-private or deletes changes the graph in that commit; `.provided(ReferenceState.key(query:all:))` for one with a query (pending until `ReferenceState` provides what the query found, below; meanwhile arrows into it stay at the pill). In the Connections tab of the shape it stands for, a pill is among what points to it (the arrows' light names `stands-for`), a plain shape row of its kind, with no row of its own to describe ([neighbors](neighbors.md)).
- `ReferenceState` (`editor.references`, a service before the table of contents in the shipped list, so it hears a commit first) keeps no index of which references name which shape: Topology does, for every board the app can show (`directReferences(naming:)` is `standIns(naming:)`'s references, `target(of:)` `directTarget(of:)`'s, a reference being a stand-in of its kind, `standInKind(of:)`: [topology](topology.md)), and a pill on a board the app can't show, which has no row and is drawn nowhere, is in none of it; a commit's references are those it touched that Topology says are (`referencesTouched(by:)`: those it added, changed but for moves, took off the board shown to put elsewhere, or touched `elsewhere`, a dictionary hit a shape and none while the file has no stand-in, never a lookup in the file), and those of its shapes that are no reference now but something is kept of (a reference deleted, or taken with its board) are let go of, by id. It is the one place the panel, the list and the menu read what a reference stands for, `state(of:)`: a `ReferenceCondition`, derived from the props and the file, never stored (`ReferenceCondition.of`, which the render also calls with its own lookups, since a render can't ask a service for the editor; a direct pill's is kept from frame to frame, `drawnCondition(of:target:_:)`, with the target it was worked out for, let go of by a commit that changes the reference or a shape it names (Topology's `standIns(naming:)`) and by a file put in place, so a frame of a thousand pills looks up none of their shapes): its cases and readers are said on it; the service's `.live` has the tint the shape has of its own, `editor.mentionAppearance(of:)`, where the pill's, drawn in a render with no editor, has the kind's. A dynamic one's condition follows `ReferenceState.status(of:)` (a `ReferenceStatus`), which `state(of:id:)` passes to `ReferenceCondition.of(_:status:…)`; the render reads `answer(of:)`, the same without observing, through `RenderContext.service`.
- What a dynamic reference finds (`ReferenceAnswers.swift`, `ReferenceState`): each query is asked as `request(_:all:)` makes it (the recorded view, public matches alone, `admission`, eight of them for a first-match one and every one for an All one (`QueryMatchRequest.every`: no cap, the owner's ask of 2026-10-22, past the 50 it was), and `holdingBack: editor.publishing?.admittable`, so the answer counts apart, as `QueryMatches.heldBack`, the matches that would be stood for once made public: the owner's pick of 2026-10-21, after an audit found a query's private matches left out without a word while every new shape starts private); `dynamic`, every reference with a query on a board the app can show (Topology takes no answer for one on a board it can't; a `Finding`: its key, `all`, the outcome for that key, `applied` per key, the key a batch asked, `dirty`), kept from `ChangeSet` (`added`, `changed` but moves, `removed`, `elsewhere`; `boardsAdded`, `boardsRemoved` and `reordered` count as other changes) and read afresh at open or for a file put in place (`rebuildDynamic`); the key is `ReferenceState.key(query:all:)`, the forwarding's. Triggers: a commit that only moved shapes returns after one test (`documentDidChange`'s first line); a reference whose own key changed (came, went, its query or flag: a gesture, an undo) is asked at once from a zero-delay hop of `editor.scheduler` (`answerSoon`), which first provides the answer kept for that key (`provideRemembered`: exempt from the stale rule, Topology takes it since the key change dropped what it held); any other commit marks every dynamic one dirty and re-arms one wait of `ReferenceStyle.settle` (250 ms) (`armDebounce`). `ask()` and landing (`land`) do what their doc comments say (`editingDidEnd` asks for the one being edited then); a batch's answers are worked out for Topology off the main actor before they land (`providing`, what `land` would hand over, read and changing nothing; `prepare`, a `TopologyIndex.preparer()` taken as the batch answers and its `prepare` on a detached task; `land(_:revision:prepared:)` hands both to `provide(_:prepared:)`, [topology](topology.md)), so a landing takes in what came and went, never every match: an All reference of 6,086 notes with 100 arrows into it costs the main actor some 0.1 ms a commit (`referenceEveryMatchMain`, [benchmarks](benchmarks.md)); a landing is kept in the reference's memo (kept past its deletion for its undo, for at most `ReferenceStyle.memosKept` (256) references, naming at most `ReferenceStyle.memoIDsKept` (1,000,000, 16 MB of ids) shapes between them, `memoIDs`: each landing or use stamps it with `memoClock`, and a memo made past either bound lets go of the least recently landed or used, never the one just landed, so a session's deleted references don't pile up and an answer of every shape doesn't hold every one four times over; every batch lands every live reference again, so deleted ones' go first; `memoCount` and `isRemembered(_:)`, debug builds), then told, when the answer says something new (other matches, count or matches held back, another refusal, a failure: every quiet asks every reference again and most answers are what they were, which tells nobody; `says(_:theSameAs:)`): `answered` (an `@Observable` per-reference revision the panel and `shownWhile` observe), `editor.setNeedsDisplay()`, `editor.contentsDidChange(for:)`. `unknown_title`, `unknown_id`, `cannot_contain` and any other refusal are no matches with the message (an empty provide, so nothing stays pending); `too_slow` is `.failed(.tooSlow)` (`retry(_:)` asks again); `cancelled` and `board_closed` are asked again. It is a settle source (`topology.keepsSettling { isFinding }`: a wait armed, a hop due, a batch out; `settleSourceChanged()` when it goes idle), so `canvas query` after a draw reads through what it found; `.finding(slow: true)` ("Finding…") only once a batch has been out `ReferenceStyle.findingDelay` (250 ms).
- The pill (`ReferenceStyle`, world units, 40 tall, colors dynamic; `ReferenceLook` is what a condition draws: leading symbol, label, the place over it or the word for a gone original, trailing symbol, dashed, the colors it wears, and the room a dynamic one reserves; `ReferenceDefinition.draw`): a capsule filled with its original's wash and edged in its color (Color, below), or, standing for nothing, unfilled with a dashed border; a leading symbol (the target's kind's, in the original's `ink` where it is drawn in a color, secondary with none; `magnifyingglass` for a dynamic one, in the label's color at 70% (`ReferenceStyle.dynamicTint`; secondary read as faint beside the kinds'); `square.dashed` for a missing or unavailable one), then the label (one line, tail-truncated; italic at 65% for a pill that stands for nothing and the shape's live name when it's not public; words italic at 65% not interpreted, no matches, refused, failed or with nothing to ask, at 50% finding; the placeholder "Reference" for an empty one); a not-public pill trails `eye.slash` (`ReferenceStyle.eyeColor`: secondary all but vanished on the dashed pill; the label's room shortened by it, and on the name's line when the pill has two, so the place keeps the room the pill was fitted for). Every dynamic pill leads with the magnifier (at 65% of its strength when it stands for nothing) and reserves the count's room; what trails it: an All one's count in a capsule (`ReferenceStyle.badge`: as it is below a thousand, then "1.2k", "12k", "1.2M", cut down and never rounded up; the room reserved, `badgeRoom`, 6 + 37, is the widest of those below a hundred thousand, "9.9k"), a first-match one's first match's kind symbol in its tint (13), "0" for no matches or a refusal, the eye a direct pill trails for a shape that isn't public for one whose query found shapes and none of them public (`ReferenceCondition.privateMatches`, its count the matches held back), the warning for a failure (a mismatch's warning takes the place of the count); dashed when it stands for nothing, solid and dimmed while finding. A pill whose shape is somewhere other than the pill says where it is (a shape's identity is its path, "Alpha › Beta"): its steps (`ReferenceState.placeOfTarget`, `[PlaceStep]`: the containers the shape is inside less those the pill is inside too, as the row's path is, each with whether it opens a board, Boards' `ContainerName.opensBoard`, and whether it is untitled), and, when the shape is on a board other than the pill's and no step opens a board, that board leading (`leadStep(of:)`: the file's name for the root, `editor.name(of:)`, "Board" only when the file has none; else its owner's, called as a path calls a container), so a shape on an enclosing board in no frame has a line too; folded by Chrome's `PlaceFold` in the room the label has (`PlaceLine`: the width less 46, less the count's `badgeRoom` for a dynamic one; the line the pill is fitted for, the fold at 234, wherever it fits in that room, `ReferenceStyle.placeLine`, so a fitted pill draws the line its width was made for, where a fold in exactly the width a line came to can cut a name shorter, a cut backing up to a word; what trails a direct pill, the eye or a channel's warning, sits on the name's line, `ReferenceLook.layout`, so it takes nothing of the place's room), and drawn over the name in two lines: the place in SF 11 regular at the label color's 60%, a board's name semibold, the `›` and `…` and an untitled container's name at 30% (`DrawnPlace.string`, built once and kept with the place; baseline 16 of the pill's 40, `ReferenceStyle.placeBaseline`), the name SF 14 medium under it (baseline 31, `nameBaseline`), both from the label's x, tail-cut in the label's room. A pill with no line to say, a pill in its shape's place (the same frame, or the same board with none), an empty line (under 24 points of room) and any pill drawn under 60% (`ReferenceStyle.placeZoom`, read from `context.zoom`, down to the skeleton zoom) has its name alone, centered in the 40 as a one-line pill's is; the symbol and a dynamic pill's count and first match stay centered whatever the lines, a direct pill's eye and warning centered on the name's line when there are two. A gone or unavailable original has "Deleted" or "Not available" for its place line (`ReferenceLook.word`, in the place's look: no place of its own to say, no board to say); a not-public pill keeps its place line, dashed, its name italic, with the eye; a dynamic pill whose answer is its first match says that match's place (`drawnPlace(for:of:)`, kept for the width the pill had and let go of with the other places and when the match itself changes: put in another frame or onto another board), a finding one, a one for every match and one with no matches have no line. `ReferenceState.drawnPlace(of:)` (a `PlaceLine`) and `drawnPlaceSteps(of:)` (the steps whole) keep the place with the condition drawn (`drawnCondition`, so a frame looks up no path and folds nothing), for the width the pill had, let go of with it and when a shape it was read from changes (`drawnPlaceSources`: the containers of the shape and of the pill, renamed, put elsewhere or deleted, or an owner of a board named; every kept condition whose place was worked out goes then, one that came to none too, since a container moved can part a pill from its shape's place), and when the file is renamed while the line leads with its name (`DrawnPlace.rootName`: the name changes with no commit); the names are Boards' (`ShapePaths`, the state's `paths`: `registry.containerName(of:)`, its title, else its kind's placeholder, "Untitled Subboard", as the path says it); the header's "Kind · path" and a match's row say where the shape is from the board shown, as Mentions' lists do (`MentionState.place(ofShape:)`: less the containers the board shown is inside, when the shape is inside it too; a shape directly on the board shown says the kind alone, and a match nothing; the top board is the file's name, "Board" while it has none). The header's line is folded at its width (`ReferenceHeaderContent.lead` and `place`, the steps; `subtitle` is the whole, the line's help), always one line; the Reference tab shows the whole place, wrapped, under Go to Original (`ReferencePlaceView`, `panelPlace(of:id:)`: the steps from where the pill sits, boards semibold; none for an original in the pill's own place). The width is not a function of the board (a note that moves boards writes no pill, and the pill keeps its width and folds the new place into it): a pick from the list fits it to the place the pill will say once it names the shape (`ReferenceState.placeOfPick(from:_:)`, `ReferenceEdits.pick(_:_:place:)`, `width(of:place:)`: 46 and the wider of the name with its glyph room and the line folded at 234), and a pill written without the place (`standIns.make`) is as wide as its name, so the place shows only in what room it has; a writer that wants it whole passes `place:`, or seats the pill (`standIns.seat`, Made by name above), as `canvas draw` does for a shape whose title another shape has and `canvas measure` for an end written as a path. A direct pill's width keeps room for what may trail it (`ReferenceStyle.glyphRoom`, the gap and the 11 pt glyph), so a short name gone private, or with a channel's warning, shows whole; widths fit the label at the wider of its plain and italic setting. While it's edited the editing view draws the label, and what's typed stands for nothing yet, so it isn't dressed as if it did (no dashes; no symbol, but the magnifier once the list has only the words row left). Labels and symbols are memoized (`ReferenceMemo`; symbols as bitmaps per appearance and power-of-two scale, since drawing a symbol's vector image costs ten times a bitmap's), the capsule is one `fillStroke`, and the fill and border colors are worked out once per appearance (`ReferenceStyle.surface`); `referenceLooksBuilt` (debug builds) counts what's been built. Zoomed out (`CustomSkeleton`), one square fill, no text and no name: a block of the color the pill wears (below), else `skeletonFill`, and the lighter one (`skeletonMissingFill`, the color's at 65%) for a pill that stands for nothing: it asks `ReferenceCondition.standsForNothing(_:registry:lookup:)` (a dictionary hit, mentionable and public checks), which PillTests hold to the full condition's answer in every state, and `ReferenceState.skeletonColors(of:)`.
- Color (`PillColors.swift`, `ReferenceState`; the color spec of the reference pills build): a pill wears the color its original is drawn in, as the color book says it (Colors' `ColorBook.swatch(of:)`: its own color, else the one a plugin gives it, a type's, for a note, a subboard or a frame of a type and a type's own note, else its kind's default; nil for a shape drawn as its kind looks on its own, a frame with no color, and a kind with no colors: References imports Colors and knows no kind or type), the roles of that color's `Swatch`: the fill its `wash`, the edge its `solid` 1 pt run through `Swatch.reading` against the canvas and the raised frame (so in the dark the indigo's lifts to `#605EE8` and the purple's to `#A841C8`, every other built-in as it is), the symbol its `ink` (unblended), the name the label's color, the place the label's at 60%, the count's capsule its `solid` at 30% over the fill, zoomed out its `solid` at 50% over the canvas. With no color the pill is neutral: today's fill, an edge of the label's color at 45%, a secondary symbol, a count on the label's color at 8%. States: not public is no fill, dashes in the edge color and the symbol at 65%; deleted and not available, neutral dashes at 45% (no color is left to wear); empty, being typed into and every query pill that stands for nothing (no matches, only private, failed, not interpreted, interpreting), neutral, zoomed out too (an answer that fails lets go of the colors kept); a direct pill being edited keeps its color until what is typed changes it; the channel's warning is `#B96B00` in light (`ReferenceStyle.warningTint`: the system's orange is 1.7:1 on a wash), the system's in dark. A query's first match wears that match's color (and its symbol's `ink` trails the pill), one for every match the color they share, gray with mixed colors, a match with none, or past `ReferenceStyle.sharedColorMost` (2,000) matches (`ReferenceState.answerColors`, worked out as an answer lands and kept on its `Finding`, with the answer's change told when only the color changed; a commit that recolors a match is heard as every commit is, asked again after the wait for quiet); a pill looked for again keeps the color of its last answer at half strength (`PillColors.dimmed`, also the fill, edge and count), gray before one. `PillColors` is made once a swatch and shared (`pillColors(for:)`, a pure function of the roles, both appearances together, the symbol a dynamic `NSColor`), and a direct pill's is kept beside its condition (`drawnColorsKept`, found by `drawnColors(of:_:)` in the render and `skeletonColors(of:)` in the skeleton, so a pill never drawn in full has its entry made on its first skeleton frame), let go of exactly when the condition is (a commit to the pill or to a shape it names: a recolor is one; a file put in place) and, with the dynamic answers' (`refreshAnswerColors()`), by `shapeTintsDidChange`, which the color book calls when the file's colors change or what a plugin gives changes; so a frame of a thousand pills looks up no color. The selection's ring and the go button's hover are the core's and Selection's, as they were.
- Resting the pointer on a pill (`ReferenceState.popover(on:)`, Selection's `shapePopovers`; the canvas's popover takes attributed text): a pill whose place line is folded or cut, or isn't drawn (zoomed out under 60%), says the whole place, a board's name semibold, then the name, in two lines; "Not public" (a pill for a shape that isn't, a query whose matches all aren't) under them or alone; a gone original's word ("Deleted", "Not available") where the pill doesn't say it whole (`cutWord`: its width is its name's, fitted while the original was there, so a short name's pill cuts "Not available"; or zoomed out under 60%); a line that fits, a pill in its place, a gone original whose word is whole on it and words say nothing. VoiceOver (`ReferenceState.accessibleTraits(of:)`, `reference.accessibility`, [accessibility](accessibility.md)): the name, the kind, and "in" the whole place, never folded, as `placeOfPick(from:_:)` says it ("Acme, Reference, in Shop › Policies"), "not public" after it; a gone original "Deleted" or "Not available"; a query its words and what it found ("12 matches", "First of 7" and its first match's place, "No matches").
- Editing (`ReferenceEditor`, `ReferenceTextView`, `Editable`): an `NSView` framed in view points with its bounds in world units, as the arrow-label editor is, holding a one-line `EditingTextView` subclass in a clip (the label's room: the pill less its trailing pad and, for words, the count's 36, scrolled so the caret is in it when 200 characters outrun a 280-wide pill), in the pill's font from the label's place (the render's label line starts at `frame.minY + ReferenceStyle.lineTop(of:in:)`, which centers its capitals in the pill as the symbols are, a point higher than centering the whole line; the count's digits are placed by the same rule; the editor's text the same: held to the render by `renderBitmap(editing:)` against the plain render, at most one channel step at zoom 1 and 2 and device scale 1 and 2, and on a pill at a fraction of a point, where TextKit 2 sets text on the nearest device pixel and string drawing between, its line on the render's row); the render draws the capsule and the symbol and no label while it's edited, and a strength-1 label is `labelColor` itself (Palette's `fading(to:)` replaces alpha, so black), as the editor's text is. It starts with what the pill says (a direct reference's name, the words, a query's text), all selected, and the list open on that text.
