# References

References, the `reference` kind: a pill that stands for another shape anywhere in the file; its props and writers, its look in every state, what it stands for, going to the original, its row, its panel and its fixture.

Related: [publishing](publishing.md), [topology](topology.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [selection](selection.md), [shape references](shape-references.md), [editing](editing.md), [connections](connections.md), [command line](command-line.md), [language models](language-models.md), [queries](queries.md), [neighbors](neighbors.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [References](../../docs/plugin-system.md#references).

## Architecture

- References (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait, not a vertical: any public shape can be stood for, a kind written after it too, and a pill holds nothing of its own, only another shape shown in a second place; it declares its `ShapeKind` all the same, the one platform module that does; what it imports and why are [build](build.md)'s Who builds on whom, and a plugin list with it needs their plugins (but `BoardsPlugin`: Boards is only for what a container is called in its row's path, `registry.containerName(of:)`), `PortsPlugin` and `ValidationPlugin` among them: the registry refuses a contribution to `ShapePorts` or `ValidationRules` without its owner; without `QueriesPlugin` a dynamic reference finds nothing and says so, and without `QueryTranslationPlugin` (and a model) words stay not interpreted: nothing is asked and no Interpret is offered) is a pill that stands for a shape, so a shape too many others point at needs no arrow from each: an arrow into the pill is, for everything that reads the graph (queries, the table of contents, Topology's readers), an arrow into the shape ([topology](topology.md): the kind says `standsIn`; the resolved view does the rest). Only a public shape can be stood for ([publishing](publishing.md): `admitting: { registry in registry.publicShapes }`, read on any thread). It writes the ids `reference` (kind), `reference` (tool, key R, after Schema in the shipped list, `editingPlacement`: a click places an empty 120 by 32 pill and edits it, step "Add Reference"), `references` (plugin), the command `goToOriginal` and `ReferencesMenuGroup`; it is not mentionable, not titled, never public (`ReferenceDefinition: NeverPublic`, so it has no `public` property and no chip), and has no `resizable` (its width is its text's), no opener (Return and a double-click edit) and ports on every channel on its left and right (`portsOnEveryChannel`, so it takes what it stands for takes: [connections](connections.md), "Ports at a stand-in").
- The props (`Reference: BoxShapeProps`; fixture `Fixtures/kinds/reference.v0.json` pins all five, and breaks the mode rule on purpose, since a fixture proves only the encoding): `frame` (height 32, the width stored and refitted), `target: ReferenceTarget?` (a `ShapeNaming` leaf, `{id, label}`: a name, not a binding, so it crosses boards, outlives its shape, is renamed by a copy that takes the shape along and kept by a copy that doesn't, by cut and paste, and by a deletion, which writes nothing; `label` is what it was called when picked, shown dashed once the shape is gone), `words` (what was typed), `query: String?` (the query those words mean, canonical, nil until interpreted; schema format `x-canvas-query`, so `canvas draw` checks it) and `all` (every match, or the first). `target` and `words` are `readOnly()` (a script can't write them: `unknown_attribute`), `query` and `all` are drawable (`reference query: "…" all: true`), and `canvas kinds` reports no edges for it (it has no title to name it by). A later script names one that has a query by its id, which the run's `created` row and a query's row give (Draw's `registry.drawCanName(_:)` of a record takes a stand-in that stands for what's provided, [command line](command-line.md), ids as names): an edge into or out of it, drawn straight on its own ports (the other end's first channel), a restatement of its `query` or `all`, a placement's target, a group, a delete, which takes its arrows; a direct one stays named by the shape it stands for, its id `invalid_value`. What it stands for is read by `:edge-from(#<id>, stands-for)`, which waits for its answer as any query that reads edges does (the skill's `reference/language.md`, References). The mode is derived, never stored: `reference.mode` is `.direct` if `target` is set (it wins), else `.dynamic` for words or a query, else `.empty`; `reference.text` is a dynamic one's words, else its query. `ReferenceEdits` (`pick`, `find(words:query:)`, `clear`, `width(of:)`) is how a reference is written: each keeps exactly one mode and refits `frame.width` to what it now says (the pads, the symbol, the label at the wider of its plain and italic setting, and a dynamic pill's badge room or a direct one's glyph room: `ReferenceStyle`'s, clamped, growing rightward), and `ReferenceDefinition: DerivedProps` fits a reference `canvas draw` makes the same way.
- Made by name (`standsIn(needs: "public", makes:)`): `makes` is `ReferenceEdits.pick` with the shape's id and the name to show, so `registry.standIns.make(Reference.kind, standingFor:, label:)` is a direct reference fitted to its label as a picked one is, which is how `canvas draw` puts one beside a shape for an edge to a shape on another board ([command line](command-line.md)); `fulfilling` is `registry.makePublic(&record)`, so a caller that must stand for a private shape makes it public in its own step (`standIns.fulfil`); `calls` gives `registry.standIns.calls(pill)` the label a direct pill keeps (a dynamic one's words), which a dashed pill keeps of a gone note across a reopen; `needs` is the word a message says of a shape that can't be stood for ("only a public shape can be stood for"). A script still can't write `target` or `words`, and the pill has no title. Several of a script's edges may run to one pill: `share: true` or `share: "A"` on an edge reuses a direct reference to the same end beside the end that stays's container, or the one A's arrow goes into, and `canvas measure` says how wide a pill for a title is (`standIns.make`'s box, 280 at most) before one is drawn ([command line](command-line.md)).
- What it stands for (`standsIn`, the kind's contribution): `.shapes([target.id])` for a direct reference, admitted while the shape is public, so the commit that picks, re-points, renames-to-private or deletes changes the graph in that commit; `.provided(ReferenceState.key(query:all:))` for one with a query (pending until `ReferenceState` provides what the query found, below; meanwhile arrows into it stay at the pill). In the Connections tab of the shape it stands for, a pill is among what points to it (the arrows' light names `stands-for`), a plain shape row of its kind, with no row of its own to describe ([neighbors](neighbors.md)).
- `ReferenceState` (`editor.references`, a service before the table of contents in the shipped list, so it hears a commit first) keeps no index of which references name which shape: Topology does, for every board the app can show (`directReferences(naming:)` is `standIns(naming:)`'s references, `target(of:)` `directTarget(of:)`'s, a reference being a stand-in of its kind, `standInKind(of:)`: [topology](topology.md)), and a pill on a board the app can't show, which has no row and is drawn nowhere, is in none of it; a commit's references are those it touched that Topology says are (`referencesTouched(by:)`: those it added, changed but for moves, took off the board shown to put elsewhere, or touched `elsewhere`, a dictionary hit a shape and none while the file has no stand-in, never a lookup in the file), and those of its shapes that are no reference now but something is kept of (a reference deleted, or taken with its board) are let go of, by id. It is the one place the panel, the list and the menu read what a reference stands for, `state(of:)`: a `ReferenceCondition`, derived from the props and the file, never stored (`ReferenceCondition.of`, which the render also calls with its own lookups, since a render can't ask a service for the editor; a direct pill's is kept from frame to frame, `drawnCondition(of:target:_:)`, with the target it was worked out for, let go of by a commit that changes the reference or a shape it names (Topology's `standIns(naming:)`) and by a file put in place, so a frame of a thousand pills looks up none of their shapes): its cases and readers are said on it; the service's `.live` has the tint the shape has of its own, `editor.mentionAppearance(of:)`, where the pill's, drawn in a render with no editor, has the kind's. A dynamic one's condition follows `ReferenceState.status(of:)` (a `ReferenceStatus`), which `state(of:id:)` passes to `ReferenceCondition.of(_:status:…)`; the render reads `answer(of:)`, the same without observing, through `RenderContext.service`.
- What a dynamic reference finds (`ReferenceAnswers.swift`, `ReferenceState`): each query is asked as `request(_:all:)` makes it (the recorded view, public matches alone, `admission`, eight of them for a first-match one and every one for an All one (`QueryMatchRequest.every`: no cap, the owner's ask of 2026-10-22, past the 50 it was), and `holdingBack: editor.publishing?.admittable`, so the answer counts apart, as `QueryMatches.heldBack`, the matches that would be stood for once made public: the owner's pick of 2026-10-21, after an audit found a query's private matches left out without a word while every new shape starts private); `dynamic`, every reference with a query on a board the app can show (Topology takes no answer for one on a board it can't; a `Finding`: its key, `all`, the outcome for that key, `applied` per key, the key a batch asked, `dirty`), kept from `ChangeSet` (`added`, `changed` but moves, `removed`, `elsewhere`; `boardsAdded`, `boardsRemoved` and `reordered` count as other changes) and read afresh at open or for a file put in place (`rebuildDynamic`); the key is `ReferenceState.key(query:all:)`, the forwarding's. Triggers: a commit that only moved shapes returns after one test (`documentDidChange`'s first line); a reference whose own key changed (came, went, its query or flag: a gesture, an undo) is asked at once from a zero-delay hop of `editor.scheduler` (`answerSoon`), which first provides the answer kept for that key (`provideRemembered`: exempt from the stale rule, Topology takes it since the key change dropped what it held); any other commit marks every dynamic one dirty and re-arms one wait of `ReferenceStyle.settle` (250 ms) (`armDebounce`). `ask()` and landing (`land`) do what their doc comments say (`editingDidEnd` asks for the one being edited then); a batch's answers are worked out for Topology off the main actor before they land (`providing`, what `land` would hand over, read and changing nothing; `prepare`, a `TopologyIndex.preparer()` taken as the batch answers and its `prepare` on a detached task; `land(_:revision:prepared:)` hands both to `provide(_:prepared:)`, [topology](topology.md)), so a landing takes in what came and went, never every match: an All reference of 6,086 notes with 100 arrows into it costs the main actor some 0.1 ms a commit (`referenceEveryMatchMain`, [benchmarks](benchmarks.md)); a landing is kept in the reference's memo (kept past its deletion for its undo, for at most `ReferenceStyle.memosKept` (256) references, naming at most `ReferenceStyle.memoIDsKept` (1,000,000, 16 MB of ids) shapes between them, `memoIDs`: each landing or use stamps it with `memoClock`, and a memo made past either bound lets go of the least recently landed or used, never the one just landed, so a session's deleted references don't pile up and an answer of every shape doesn't hold every one four times over; every batch lands every live reference again, so deleted ones' go first; `memoCount` and `isRemembered(_:)`, debug builds), then told, when the answer says something new (other matches, count or matches held back, another refusal, a failure: every quiet asks every reference again and most answers are what they were, which tells nobody; `says(_:theSameAs:)`): `answered` (an `@Observable` per-reference revision the panel and `shownWhile` observe), `editor.setNeedsDisplay()`, `editor.contentsDidChange(for:)`. `unknown_title`, `unknown_id`, `cannot_contain` and any other refusal are no matches with the message (an empty provide, so nothing stays pending); `too_slow` is `.failed(.tooSlow)` (`retry(_:)` asks again); `cancelled` and `board_closed` are asked again. It is a settle source (`topology.keepsSettling { isFinding }`: a wait armed, a hop due, a batch out; `settleSourceChanged()` when it goes idle), so `canvas query` after a draw reads through what it found; `.finding(slow: true)` ("Finding…") only once a batch has been out `ReferenceStyle.findingDelay` (250 ms).
- The pill (`ReferenceStyle`, world units, colors dynamic; `ReferenceLook` is what a condition draws: leading symbol, label, trailing symbol, dashed, and the room a dynamic one reserves; `ReferenceDefinition.draw`): a filled, bordered capsule, or, standing for nothing, unfilled with a dashed border; a leading symbol (the target's kind's, its tint (the shape's own color where it has one, `editor.mentionAppearance(of:)`, else its kind's; the file's colors changing, `shapeTintsDidChange`, lets go of the kept conditions and draws the pills again) blended 40% toward the label's color, secondary with none; `magnifyingglass` for a dynamic one, in the label's color at 70% (`ReferenceStyle.dynamicTint`; secondary read as faint beside the kinds'); `square.dashed` for a missing or unavailable one), then the label (one line, tail-truncated; italic at 65% for a pill that stands for nothing and the shape's live name when it's not public; words italic at 65% not interpreted, no matches, refused, failed or with nothing to ask, at 50% finding; the placeholder "Reference" for an empty one); a not-public pill trails `eye.slash` (`ReferenceStyle.eyeColor`: secondary all but vanished on the dashed pill; the label's room shortened by it). Every dynamic pill leads with the magnifier (at 65% of its strength when it stands for nothing) and reserves the count's room; what trails it: an All one's count in a capsule (`ReferenceStyle.badge`: as it is below a thousand, then "1.2k", "12k", "1.2M", cut down and never rounded up; the room reserved, `badgeRoom`, 6 + 37, is the widest of those below a hundred thousand, "9.9k"), a first-match one's first match's kind symbol in its tint (13), "0" for no matches or a refusal, the eye a direct pill trails for a shape that isn't public for one whose query found shapes and none of them public (`ReferenceCondition.privateMatches`, its count the matches held back), the warning for a failure (a mismatch's warning takes the place of the count); dashed when it stands for nothing, solid and dimmed while finding. A pill whose shape is on another board than the pill says that board's name after the shape's name, gray, in what room the width leaves: tail-cut, and left out under `boardRoomLeast` (24) of room, so it never changes the name's place; live and not-public pills only (a gone or unavailable pill has no board to say), and a pill on its note's board looks as it did. `ReferenceState.drawnBoard(of:)` keeps the name with the condition drawn (`drawnCondition`, so a frame looks up no board), let go of with it and when an owner of a board it names changes (a rename); the name is Boards' (`editor.name(of:root:)`: its owner's title, else its kind's placeholder, "Untitled Subboard", as the path says it), "Board" for the root (`ReferenceStyle.rootBoardName`); the header's "Kind · Board" and a match's row say it the same way. The width is not a function of the board (a note that moves boards writes no pill): a pick from the list of another board fits it (`ReferenceEdits.pick(_:_:board:)`, `width(of:board:)`: the gap and the board's name more), and a pill written without the board (`standIns.make`, so `canvas draw` and `canvas measure`; the pill's size they read is the name's alone) is as wide as its name, so the board's name shows only in what room it has and a writer that wants it whole passes `board:`. A direct pill's width keeps room for what may trail it (`ReferenceStyle.glyphRoom`, the gap and the 11 pt glyph), so a short name gone private, or with a channel's warning, shows whole; widths fit the label at the wider of its plain and italic setting. While it's edited the editing view draws the label, and what's typed stands for nothing yet, so it isn't dressed as if it did (no dashes; no symbol, but the magnifier once the list has only the words row left). Labels and symbols are memoized (`ReferenceMemo`; symbols as bitmaps per appearance and power-of-two scale, since drawing a symbol's vector image costs ten times a bitmap's), the capsule is one `fillStroke`, and the fill and border colors are worked out once per appearance (`ReferenceStyle.surface`); `referenceLooksBuilt` (debug builds) counts what's been built. Zoomed out (`CustomSkeleton`), one square fill, `skeletonFill` or `skeletonMissingFill` for a pill that stands for nothing, no text and no name: it asks `ReferenceCondition.standsForNothing(_:registry:lookup:)` (a dictionary hit, mentionable and public checks), which PillTests hold to the full condition's answer in every state. Resting the pointer on a pill that stands for nothing says why (Selection's `shapePopovers`: "Deleted", "Not available", "Not public", the last for a query whose matches all aren't too).
- Editing (`ReferenceEditor`, `ReferenceTextView`, `Editable`): an `NSView` framed in view points with its bounds in world units, as the arrow-label editor is, holding a one-line `EditingTextView` subclass in a clip (the label's room: the pill less its trailing pad and, for words, the count's 36, scrolled so the caret is in it when 200 characters outrun a 280-wide pill), in the pill's font from the label's place (the render's label line starts at `frame.minY + ReferenceStyle.lineTop(of:in:)`, which centers its capitals in the pill as the symbols are, a point higher than centering the whole line; the count's digits are placed by the same rule; the editor's text the same: held to the render by `renderBitmap(editing:)` against the plain render, at most one channel step at zoom 1 and 2 and device scale 1 and 2, and on a pill at a fraction of a point, where TextKit 2 sets text on the nearest device pixel and string drawing between, its line on the render's row); the render draws the capsule and the symbol and no label while it's edited, and a strength-1 label is `labelColor` itself (`fading` replaces alpha, so black), as the editor's text is. It starts with what the pill says (a direct reference's name, the words, a query's text), all selected, and the list open on that text. Each change writes `find(words:)` in the editing step, which refits the width as it goes (`ReferenceEdits.width`: the label's width and the pads, a count's room for words, 64 to 280, growing rightward); text back to what the field opened with, or blank, puts the pill back as it was (so unchanged text makes no step, and an emptied pill that said something is kept); the text is at most 200 characters (a paste is cut), pasted line breaks and tabs are spaces, ⇧Return does nothing, quotes and dashes aren't made curly. Nothing is picked implicitly: leaving (Return with the list closed, a second Escape, ⌘Return, a click away) keeps the text as the pill's words (`settleWords`, before editing ends and as the keyboard leaves, drops the white space at its ends and, for changed text that reads as a query, writes that query), and a pick is Return or Tab on a shape's row, or a click on it (`pickTarget`: `ReferenceEdits.pick`, `MentionState.didPick`, editing ends); Return on the words row and ⌥Return (any list) commit the words (`commitWords`: nothing written when they're what a dynamic pill says already and it has a query or they don't read as one, else `find(words:query:)`, so ⌥Return on a pill that stands for a shape, opened and unchanged, makes it words); words that `queries.check` takes are that query, canonical, in the same step (D5: no model is asked; `canonical` of the plan), as is the query the model wrote for exactly these words while they rested, if it came (the draft, below); others stay not interpreted (no query, `.notInterpreted`) until the model's step (below); ⌘Return leaves as Return with the list closed does, the text already the pill's words (a pill that stands for a shape, left unchanged, still does). `discardsWhenEditingEnds` is true for an empty pill, so a new one left empty (or typed with white space alone) is deleted inside its step (none left), and `editingStepName` is "Edit Reference" (a placement's own "Add Reference" wins).
- The list over the field (`ReferencePicker`, a headless model on TextEditing's `CaretList`; `ReferenceTextView` shows it in a `CaretListWindow`, the view's `caretListWindow`, so the base class's keys are every list's: ↑ ↓ wrap, Return and Tab pick, Escape closes, ⌘. too; it opens as the view comes into a window and again whenever something is typed, anchored at the whole text, and closes as the keyboard or the window goes): `PublicShapes: MentionScope` (admits `registry.isPublic`) asked of `MentionState.candidates(matching:in:for:)`, so ranking, recency and nearness are the `@` picker's (a scope's members are kept, [mentions](mentions.md)), size 280 by 24, 8 rows, 4 off (`ReferenceStyle.pickerMetrics`, Mentions' numbers); nothing typed lists the recent picks and the nearest shapes, with no headers, and, when none is recent or near (the board shown has no public shape), the first 50 of the file's, so the list isn't empty while a shape can be picked; shapes with no name aren't in the catalog; rows are the kind's symbol in its tint, the label with the matched characters, and the board's name as the detail when that isn't the board shown, or, when two rows share a label, "Kind · Board" (the kind alone on the board shown); while the text isn't blank the words row is last (`magnifyingglass` and the text verbatim, `separated` from the shapes above it, none when it's alone), the first shape highlighted (but the one a direct pill stands for, while the text is what the field opened with, so Return on a pill opened and left alone keeps it though another shape is called alike and nearer: `ReferencePicker`'s `current`), or, with no shape left, the words row (`isHandoff`: `ReferenceState.setHandingOff`, and the pill, while edited, leads with the magnifier, `ReferenceLook(editing:handsOff:)`, its text moved over for it and the canvas drawn again); with no public shape in the file and nothing typed, one dimmed state row, "No public shapes" (nothing highlighted, so Return commits, and an empty new pill is discarded).
- Natural language (`ReferenceInterpreting.swift`; design 7.3, decision D9): what typed words become, by `editor.queryTranslator`, with nothing in the document until a step has it. `ReferenceState.interpretations` keeps, per reference, an `Interpretation` (its words, its `Stage`, the generation an answer or a wait ending must still be, its tasks and its `Draft`, each said on it; `.resting` arms a wait of `ReferenceStyle.interpretDelay` on `editor.scheduler`; the draft's one extra `queries.matches` request provides nothing). `ReferenceEditor.textDidChange` tells it the text after the editing step wrote it (`typed(_:in:interprets:)`, `interprets` once `picker.isHandoff`; text back to what the field opened with, or blank, tells it nothing): any keystroke drops what was asked (the wait, the call, the draft); text that `queries.check`s is a draft at once (D5: no model); else the wait is armed, and when it fires `translate(words, about: .reference)` runs in a `@MainActor` task, `.interpreting` meanwhile (the panel's status, the pill's look, the row). Its answer, while the pill is still typed into, is the draft (`shown(_:id:)` puts its query on the props the header, the Query section and the matches read, so the panel shows words, query, status and matches as they arrive; the render never sees it); a failure is `.failed` (a cancelled task says nothing). Commit: `commitWords` and `settleWords` write a landed draft's query for exactly these words in the editing step (`draftQuery(for:words:)`, beside D5's `check`), no second call; `committed(words:of:)` (⌥Return, the words row) notes words the list still had shapes for; `editingDidEnd` (a hop of the scheduler later, so an undo that ended editing has already taken the words away) starts a translation for words that were typed or committed and rested not yet, leaves a call out alone, and lands a draft that wasn't written. A call that outlives editing lands in `land(_:for:words:)`: only if the reference (on any board) still has these words, no query and no target and isn't read-only (`editor.isReadOnly(inFile:)`: locked, in a locked frame, or on a locked subboard's board, on whichever board it is), `editor.transact(on: board, "Interpret")` writing `ReferenceEdits.find(words:query:)`, which refits the width; a locked one is dropped with no transact (so no shake), status idle, and Interpret is offered once unlocked. `hearInterpretations` (every commit, after the move test, O(1) with none asked about) drops the entries whose reference is gone, a pick, has a query or has other words (an undo, a keystroke), cancelling their calls; `editingBegan` (the editing view opening) cancels a call too, and a file put in place drops all. The service going with its editor (a file closed; an `isolated deinit`, the main actor's) cancels everything out for it: the model's call (so it doesn't run on for up to a minute spending tokens on an answer nothing lands), every `Interpretation`'s wait, call and draft request, the batch, the debounce, the hop and the finding timer; the editor can go while a call is out because `QueryTranslator` takes what it needs of it at once (`inputs()`) and holds it in no local across the call (ClosingTests). Never on undo, redo, paste, `canvas draw` or open: only typing, a commit, the panel's Interpret (the command `interpretReference`, on the only selected reference: `canInterpret`: words with no query, a translator installed, none asking, nobody typing, not read-only; `interpret(_:)`) and Retry ask, and `languageModelKeyDidChange` asks again those that failed with `.noKey` or `.keyRejected`. With no translator installed nothing is asked and no Interpret is offered; with a translator but no model or queries, an attempt says `.unavailable` (the pill as nothing to ask). The Query section's lines for words: busy "Interpreting…"; a failure's message (the model's own words, "Key rejected", "No query for these words", "Unusable answer") with Open Settings… (`AnthropicSettingsView.showSettings()`) for a key and Retry otherwise (`retry(_:)`: live while typed, else as Interpret); the Interpret row (`DetailCommandButton(command: .interpretReference, systemImage: "sparkles")`, titled as the command is, "Interpret", borderless, in the status line's place, only for words nobody has asked about and while they aren't typed; dimmed while locked); the Match ☐ All row disabled while interpreting.
- Going to the original: the leading symbol is a canvas button (`GoButton`, `registrar.canvasButtons`), in full only, never while the pill is edited, and only on the shape the select tool would press there (a shape in front takes the press): the symbol's box and 4 view points all round, wherever there's an original (live or not public); hovered it draws the accent's `arrow.up.forward.circle.fill` over the symbol (`ReferenceButtonOverlay`, pressed a step darker) and the pointing hand shows. A click goes as following a mention does (`editor.mentions?.follow`: to the shape, or into it for a kind that opens, on the next scheduler turn, one step back, the pill not selected first). "Go to Original" is also the context menu's first item (`ReferencesMenuGroup`, `.before(ShapeMenuGroup.self)`, `MenuItem(command: .goToOriginal, … when: offered, dims: true)`: dimmed where there's nothing to go to, not offered for a reference of words) and the panel's button, both the command `goToOriginal`, declared once, with one rule (`canGoToOriginal`) over the reference it's given (the menu's, the selection's); a locked reference goes all the same.
