# Subboards

Subboards, the `subboard` kind: a card that owns a board; its opt-ins, its look and preview, its skeleton, and its fixtures.

Related: [boards](boards.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [titles](titles.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [transform](transform.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Subboards](../../docs/plugin-system.md#subboards).

## Architecture

- Subboards (a vertical, on Boards, Connections, DetailPanel, Keyboard, Mentions, TableOfContents, Titles and Transform; `docs/plugin-system.md` "Subboards"): the `subboard` kind, `Subboard` (props: only `frame`, required; 240 × 160 placed by the Subboard tool, B, `rectangle.stack`, one step "Add Subboard"), whose `SubboardDefinition` is `BoardOwning`. It's all opt-ins: `sub.title(symbolInPill: true)` (Titles keeps its name, shown in its pill above the card after the kind's symbol, as a schema card's), `sub.opensBoard(placeholder: "Untitled Subboard", systemImage: "rectangle.stack")`, `sub.mentionable(tint:following: .opens)` (by its title, else "Untitled Subboard"; its symbol in the card's indigo; following a mention enters it), a row (`ContentsLabel` with the placeholder, its symbol in the same indigo, named by Titles), a `TitleHeader($sub, placeholder:systemImage:)` header and ports (`sub.subboardLinks()`, SubboardLinks.swift: `ports(SubboardLink.self)`, then `portsOnEveryChannel()`, on all four sides, both ways; its own channel is first, so its dots are its indigo (a dot is its first channel's color) and a drag to another subboard is a `subboard-link`, while every channel after it lets a note or a schema card connect to it on its own channel and it to either on theirs (a note and a schema card still don't connect); the top dot is in its title's pill's row, as a schema card's is; a frame has no ports: [connections](connections.md)); and, after its shape block, it declares that channel (`registrar.channel(SubboardLink.self)`: `subboard-link`, `NSColor.systemIndigo` as its border is, summary "an arrow drawn on the subboard-link channel", before it takes the container channel, so that stays last on its report line) and takes Transform's container channel (`registrar.turnable(into: Subboard.self, through: ContainerChannel.self)`), so a shape that holds shapes (a frame) turns into a subboard: under its id, 240 × 160 at its top-left corner, holding on its board what it held, with Turn into Subboard in its menu and `/turn-into-subboard` (the closure turns it with `transaction.turn` and, only if that took, moves what it held with `transaction.move`; Subboards names no kind that offers the channel: [transform](transform.md)). The card (`SubboardStyle`, world units) is the whole frame, its extent its box: a rounded card with the canvas's shadow and an indigo border (`NSColor.systemIndigo`), in colors that follow the appearance it's drawn in (a faint indigo over white in the light, a raised indigo-gray in the dark: a subboard is a place, not a sheet of paper as a note is), showing its board's preview, fitted 12 points in at no more than a tenth of its size (`context.drawBoardPreview(ofBoardOwnedBy:in:maxScale:)`, `SubboardStyle.previewMaxScale`; sharp however far in it's zoomed, since a preview shown larger than its mask is drawn as paths: [drawing](drawing.md)), or until that's built, says what it holds, `context.shapeCount(inBoardOwnedBy:)` ("12 shapes"), and for an empty board the kind's symbol over "Empty". Its `indicatorPath` is its rounded edge, so its outline (the selection's, a hover's, the hint) is the core's, stood off the edge like every closed shape's (`outlineStandoff`, in [the editor](editor.md)), which keeps the accent line, 1.5 points and close in hue to the card's indigo border, off it: nothing of the card's own. Zoomed out it's its skeleton (`SubboardSkeleton.swift`): fills alone, square (so its outline is its frame's, as every rect's is: a ring on the edge over its half-point border, which still reads as selected, filled in ⌘A's one pass; no outline stands off zoomed out, since stroking each subboard in view is what ⌘A at 10% on the benchmark's boards, a fifth of them subboards, mustn't pay for), no shadow, symbol or count: its border's indigo, at least half a view point, the card inside it, and its board's preview, asked for as the full card asks (its rect, a tenth of its board at most; drawn once built, else nothing); its title is Titles' cover, over it. Shipped after StickyNotes (the toolbar's Subboard (B), before Frame (F) and Schema (S)), with its fixture, `Fixtures/kinds/subboard.v0.json`; `Fixtures/files/nested-arrows-mention.v4.json` is a whole file, boards two deep, each with notes and a bound arrow, read and written back byte for byte (its notes and arrows mention nothing), `nested-mentions.v4.json` the same file from before arrows' text held mentions and `nested.v4.json` from before notes' did, each read with its notes and arrows brought up to date, and `nested-arrows-mention-across.v4.json` the same with a note on the root and one two boards down mentioning each other, live opened on either board, read and brought up to date (`nested-mentions-across.v4.json`, from before arrows', too) (CompositionTests' `SubboardFileTests`, `NestedFile`). A locked subboard locks its board, and every board under it, at any depth: the card opens as any does, and what's on its board refuses every change (each try shakes it) and takes nothing new, since the core makes a board whose owner is read-only read-only itself (`editor.isBoardReadOnly`, [editor](editor.md), [locking](locking.md)); the kind does nothing for it (its card's own dots still show on the board it's on, which takes arrows to it), and CompositionTests' `LockedShapeTests` hold it as shipped (a locked subboard with a note and a subboard with a note: entered; no tool that places shapes, nor its key; no paste; each shape refusing; and unlocked, both boards editable). SubboardsTests install `PortsPlugin()` (`subboardPlugins()`; the registry refuses a channel or ports without it, and with no connector no dots show), and its Subboards suite holds its ports' table (all four sides, its own channel first and then every channel another plugin declares, whichever of them is declared first), CompositionTests' `portsShippedTests` its dots and its links to and from a note, a schema card and a subboard; SubboardsTests' The card's preview holds a card at 8 pixels a point sharp, the mask blurred for the control; The card and The card zoomed out hold its outline's standoff (`indicatorPath`'s box; the selection's and a hover's pixels clear of the card, a gap between, and on its frame zoomed out), and its TurnTests the channel it takes, from CanvasTesting's corrals, which offer it (the report; the size and the corner; the title; what the corral held on its board, a corral inside still holding its box; a link inside riding and one across let go; undo and redo; its row and its board's rows; Return entering it; `/turn-into-subboard`; corrals selected together; an empty corral).
