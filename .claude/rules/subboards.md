# Subboards

Subboards, the `subboard` kind: a card that owns a board; its description, its opt-ins, its look and preview, its skeleton, and its fixtures.

Related: [boards](boards.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [titles](titles.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [transform](transform.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Subboards](../../docs/plugin-system.md#subboards).

## Architecture

- Subboards (product, a vertical, on Boards, Colors, Connections, DetailPanel, Keyboard, Mentions, TableOfContents, Titles, Transform and Types; `docs/plugin-system.md` "Subboards"): the `subboard` kind, `Subboard` (props: `frame`, required, and `description`, a `MentionText`, empty by default, its one migration, `PropsMigration("description")`, making the kind's version 1; 240 × 160 placed by the Subboard tool, B, `rectangle.stack`, one step "Add Subboard"), whose `SubboardDefinition` is `BoardOwning`. Its description is what it's for, mention text as a note's text is, edited in its panel and drawn nowhere on the canvas (so Find, which marks text where it's drawn, doesn't search it); holding mention text makes the kind one of `registry.namingKinds`, so with no code of its own its mentions are `mention` edges (slot `description`), listed in its Connections tab's Mentions and in what it mentions' Mentioned By with the description quoted (`quote(of:around:)`), lit by a hover, warned of by `MentionValidationPlugin`, renamed by a copy that takes their targets along, read by queries (`:mentions`, `[description*=…]`) and written by `canvas draw`'s `description:` (`@[Title]`, through Mentions' text format). It's all opt-ins: `sub.title(symbolInPill: true)` (Titles keeps its name, shown in its pill above the card after the kind's symbol), `sub.opensBoard(placeholder: "Untitled Subboard", systemImage: "rectangle.stack")`, `sub.colorable(default: .indigo)` ([palette](palette.md) and Colors: the card's color, indigo until it has one of its own), `sub.tint(SubboardStyle.border)` (the kind's color, the core's: system indigo, which the colored card's border does not follow), `sub.mentionable(following: .opens)` (by its title, else "Untitled Subboard"; its symbol in that indigo; following a mention enters it), a row (`ContentsLabel` with the placeholder, its symbol in the same indigo, named by Titles), Types (`sub.typeWords(\.description, drawnIn: { _ in nil })` and `sub.typeable()`: a subboard can be of types, never one; its description is its words, drawn nowhere, so it holds `is`, `fieldValues` and `autofill` and its fields are filled from its description as a note's from its text; its type chips follow its title's pill, its values are in the Types tab of its panel and no rows are drawn on the card; a type's color replaces its default indigo while it holds none of its own; [types](types.md)), its panel (`sub.subboardPanel()`, SubboardPanel.swift, one `details` call, so one `ShapeDetails → subboard` on its report line: a `TitleHeader($sub, placeholder:systemImage:)` header, and its own tab, Description (`DescriptionTab`, a kind's own tab, `.first`, before Types, Connections and Style, and the tab the panel opens a subboard on), whose one headerless section, `.named("Description")`, is `MentionTextArea($sub, \.description, height:placeholder:)`, the panel's mention text box ([mentions](mentions.md)): pills, the `@` picker, a pill's click and double-click, slash commands on an empty line, spelling as the app's; Return a new line, no commit; a run of typing one step, "Change Description"; two text areas tall, `SubboardStyle.descriptionHeight`, with the placeholder "Add a description" (`descriptionPlaceholder`); read-only (a locked subboard), dimmed, no picker) and ports (`sub.subboardLinks()`, SubboardLinks.swift: `ports(SubboardLink.self)`, then `portsOnEveryChannel()`, on all four sides, both ways; its own channel is first, so its dots are its indigo (a dot is its first channel's color) and a drag to another subboard is a `subboard-link`, while every channel after it lets a note connect to it on its own channel and it to a note on the note's, and a frame and it to each other on `frame-link`; the top dot is in its title's pill's row, as a note's is: [connections](connections.md)); and, after its shape block, it declares that channel (`registrar.channel(SubboardLink.self)`: `subboard-link`, `NSColor.systemIndigo` as its border is, summary "an arrow drawn on the subboard-link channel", before it takes the container channel, so that stays last on its report line) and takes Transform's container channel (`registrar.turnable(into: Subboard.self, through: ContainerChannel.self)`), so a shape that holds shapes (a frame) turns into a subboard: under its id, 240 × 160 at its top-left corner, holding on its board what it held, with Turn into Subboard in its menu and `/turn-into-subboard` (the closure turns it with `transaction.turn` and, only if that took, moves what it held with `transaction.move`; Subboards names no kind that offers the channel: [transform](transform.md)). The card (`SubboardStyle`, world units) is the whole frame, its extent its box: a rounded card with the canvas's shadow, its border and its empty card's symbol the color's `solid` and the card its `surface` (`context.colors(of: shape)`, in the appearance being drawn; the built-in indigo is pinned to system indigo's `#5856D6` light and `#5E5CE6` dark and to the card's old faint indigo over white in the light and raised indigo-gray in the dark, so a card with no color looks as it always did: a subboard is a place, not a sheet of paper as a note is), showing its board's preview, fitted 12 points in at no more than a tenth of its size (`context.drawBoardPreview(ofBoardOwnedBy:in:maxScale:)`, `SubboardStyle.previewMaxScale`; sharp however far in it's zoomed, since a preview shown larger than its mask is drawn as paths: [drawing](drawing.md)), or until that's built, says what it holds, `context.shapeCount(inBoardOwnedBy:)` ("12 shapes"), and for an empty board the kind's symbol over "Empty" (`SubboardStyle.glyph(in:)`: one image a color and appearance, made when first asked for and kept in a cache of 64, since tinting as it draws would remake the symbol's mask every frame). Its `indicatorPath` is its rounded edge, so its outline (the selection's, a hover's, the hint) is the core's, stood off the edge like every closed shape's (`outlineStandoff`, in [the editor](editor.md)), which keeps the accent line, 1.5 points and close in hue to the card's indigo border, off it: nothing of the card's own. Zoomed out it's its skeleton (`SubboardSkeleton.swift`): fills alone, square (so its outline is its frame's, as every rect's is: a ring on the edge over its half-point border, which still reads as selected, filled in ⌘A's one pass; no outline stands off zoomed out, since stroking each subboard in view is what ⌘A at 10% on the benchmark's boards, a fifth of them subboards, mustn't pay for), no shadow, symbol or count: its border's color (`solid`), at least half a view point, the card (`surface`) inside it, and its board's preview, asked for as the full card asks (its rect, a tenth of its board at most; drawn once built, else nothing); its title is Titles' cover, over it. Shipped after StickyNotes (the toolbar's Subboard (B), before Frame (F) and Reference (R)), with its fixtures, `Fixtures/kinds/subboard.v0.json` and `subboard.v1.json` (a description with mentions, an escaped `]` and backslash in a label, and an escaped `@[`); `Fixtures/files/nested-described.v4.json` is a whole file, boards two deep, each with notes and a bound arrow, its subboards' descriptions empty, read and written back byte for byte (its notes and arrows mention nothing), `nested-arrows-mention.v4.json` the same file from before subboards had a description, `nested-mentions.v4.json` from before arrows' text held mentions and `nested.v4.json` from before notes' did, each read with its subboards, notes and arrows brought up to date, and `nested-described-across.v4.json` the same with a note on the root and one two boards down mentioning each other, live opened on either board, read and brought up to date (`nested-arrows-mention-across.v4.json` and `nested-mentions-across.v4.json`, from before, too) (CompositionTests' `SubboardFileTests`, `NestedFile`); `Fixtures/files/frames.v5.json`'s subboard, from before, is written back with an empty description ([frames](frames.md)). A locked subboard locks its board, and every board under it, at any depth: the card opens as any does, and what's on its board refuses every change (each try shakes it) and takes nothing new, since the core makes a board whose owner is read-only read-only itself (`editor.isBoardReadOnly`, [editor](editor.md), [locking](locking.md)); the kind does nothing for it (its card's own dots still show on the board it's on, which takes arrows to it), and CompositionTests' `LockedShapeTests` hold it as shipped (a locked subboard with a note and a subboard with a note: entered; no tool that places shapes, nor its key; no paste; each shape refusing; and unlocked, both boards editable). SubboardsTests install `TypesPlugin()` (as every list with `SubboardsPlugin` does) and `ColorsPlugin()` (it needs slash commands and context menus) and `PortsPlugin()` (`subboardPlugins()`; the registry refuses a channel or ports without it, and with no connector no dots show), and its Subboards suite holds its ports' table (all four sides, its own channel first and then every channel another plugin declares, whichever of them is declared first), CompositionTests' `portsShippedTests` its dots and its links to and from a note and a subboard; SubboardsTests' The card's color holds the card unset (indigo, both appearances, full and zoomed out), a named and a hex color, `setColor` with undo and a name the file lacks; The card's preview holds a card at 8 pixels a point sharp, the mask blurred for the control; The card and The card zoomed out hold its outline's standoff (`indicatorPath`'s box; the selection's and a hover's pixels clear of the card, a gap between, and on its frame zoomed out), its The description (DescriptionTests, the panel beside the canvas in an unshown window, `PanelWindow`, over two titled subboards) the tab first and the box (its owner, its placeholder), typing and an `@` pick written as they go, one step "Change Description", the mention edge and its quote, a rename shown in the pill and writing nothing, and a frozen subboard's box (CanvasTesting's `FreezingPlugin`) taking no typing and opening no picker; CompositionTests' `connectionsShippedTests` a subboard's description in the Connections lists both ways, one target on its own board, `drawMentionsTests` `canvas draw`'s `description:` naming a note two boards down, and ShippedTests the panel's tabs, and its `TypedContainersShippedTests` a subboard of a type (its chip, Is a ▸, `/is-a` on an empty line of its Description box, the Fields, a needed issue, a type's color, filling from its description); and its TurnTests the channel it takes, from CanvasTesting's corrals, which offer it (the report; the size and the corner; the title; what the corral held on its board, a corral inside still holding its box; a link inside riding and one across let go; undo and redo; its row and its board's rows; Return entering it; `/turn-into-subboard`; corrals selected together; an empty corral).
- VoiceOver reads a subboard by its title and kind ("Plans, Subboard") and what its board holds as its value, as its card's caption says it (`SubboardStyle.caption(of:)`: "Empty", "1 shape", "4 shapes"), asked only when an assistive app asks ([accessibility](accessibility.md)).
