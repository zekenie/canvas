# Benchmarks

What `./build.sh bench` measures, and every budget it holds.

Related: [drawing](drawing.md), [queries](queries.md), [build](build.md), [testing](testing.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

## Benchmarks

- `./build.sh bench` builds BenchmarkTests in release and runs it: saving, opening, validation's first read (every shape, checked against its kind's schema too) and its read after a drag event, after one of a subboard 100 notes mention (their mention rule looked it up, and ignores moves) and after moving every shape, a hover and a drag frame (titles' pills and locks in view), an empty board's frame on Retina at 100%, 70%, 35% and 10% (the background and the dot grid, the camera still, panning and pinching), the first frame and each after zoomed out to fit the board (at 10,000 shapes, as far as the 10% zoom goes), where shapes are skeletons, which show no adornments (a title is said on a shape big enough on screen: Titles' cover), a pan's frame with every shape selected (⌘A) at 100% (the camera a point from the last frame's, so the selection's box is worked out again, as in a pan), a board all out of view (what it adds to an empty board's frame, at 100% and 10%, with nothing selected and with every shape), a drag that snaps with every shape of the board in view (at 10%: its first event, which reads them, in a frame; each after within a drag event's input budget), a hover on a note's port dot and a drag from it, a hover onto a title's pill and onto a mention's, reading the table of contents' rows after a commit, a keystroke in the `@` picker (the `@` that opens it too, on a file of two boards typed inside the subboard, and on a board of empty notes where only 3 notes can be mentioned), a rename of a subboard mentioned 100 times (the rows and validation's read after it too), the panel of that subboard, its Mentioned In tab showing, read after a drag event of another note and after a mentioner's rename, what's connected to a shape with 100 edges (`topology.neighbors(of:)`), a completion near the end of a schema card's JSON as long as the model writes (34,000 bytes), a `canvas draw` of 20 notes each pushed along its row of the grid, the minimap's first picture (a frame at a time) and a drag frame with it, ten subboards' previews filling in (the longest frame, and how many), and undoing and redoing a drag (with its frame), a tenth of the board's move and deletion, and every shape's deletion, on generated boards of 1,000 and 10,000 shapes (three notes in ten mention a subboard and a shape that isn't there, so frames draw pills), against their budgets (the persistence plan's: open 10,000 shapes in under 1 s, Topology's index and Mentions' catalog within that in 50 ms, a frame in 16 ms, zoomed out too (and ⌘A's at 100%), which undo and redo of a drag fit with its frame, and of a tenth of the board's move or deletion without one; an empty board's frame in 2 ms, the thread's CPU time (`Budget.emptyFrame`); validation's first read of 10,000 in 100 ms, and undoing or redoing their deletion; the rows, validation and the panel's layout after a commit, in 1 ms (`Budget.panelAfterCommit` the last); a picker keystroke, and a schema card's completion, in 2 ms; a rename of a shape mentioned 100 times, its commit and validation's read after it, in 1 ms more than one mentioned by none; the neighbors in 1 ms; a board of 10,000 all out of view in 1 ms over an empty board's frame, selected or not, since nothing off screen is drawn and a shape outlined out of view costs a look at its kept extent; and the draw in 150 ms, time the window doesn't draw). Frames (`FrameBenchmarks.swift`): a note dropped across a frame's edge, its release settling it in (`Budget.settle`, 2 ms), a frame resized over the board's shapes, its release raising what it takes in above it (`Budget.settleResize`, half a frame), and a frame dragged holding a row of the board (a drag event's input, 1 ms) and every box shape on it (reported: moving thousands of shapes an event costs what it does with them selected, some 22 ms at 7,826). Across boards, on generated files (`generatedFile(root:children:depth:)`, the shipped subboards): opening 10,000 shapes spread over nested boards (under 1 s); entering a subboard of 10,000 cold with ⌘↓ (the switch, a frame, the rows and validation's read, together, under 250 ms, `Budget.enterCold`; who mentions whom, Topology's, and Mentions' catalog are the file's, read as it opens, so entering reads none of them), and going back up with ⌘↑ (with the root listed whole after it, too) and down again warm (the same, in a frame); a hover, a drag and the rows after it beside a subboard of 10,000 and inside one while the root holds as many (a frame; 1 ms), and the drag event's input alone (1 ms, and at most 3× what it costs on a file of that board alone, so a commit's upkeep of the whole file shows); the minimap's picture of the board entered (a frame at a time); a crumb's menu inside a subboard, the root's rows after a move on the board shown (1 ms: a move makes none again) and after a rename there (a frame: every note's row up there is made again); and deleting a subboard whose boards hold 10,000 (in a frame and 2 ms, `Budget.subboardDeletion`: the generated file holds 2,608 titles twice, which Titles' `TitleIndex` (`TitleValidationPlugin`'s service) takes out), and undoing or redoing that (100 ms). Queries (`QueryBenchmarks.swift`, `extension Budget`, 1,000 / 10,000 shapes): a query's main time, its reading and the turn that takes up the file (`queryMain`, 0.2 / 0.5 ms, also after a drag event that moved every shape), and the vocabulary's first build (1 ms); off the main actor, `sticky-note[text*="note" i]:not(:inside(*))` first after opening, 100 rows (`queryScan`, 5 / 40 ms), a note by its name (1 / 5 ms, and its derived names in slices on a board looked at, 0.5 / 4 ms; no card is read cold, since the schema cards' sync reads every card as a file opens and as it changes), notes with an edge to a subboard 100 mention (0.5 ms, brief rows, from the anchor's edges; 2 ms with their attributes, `queryByIDRows`, since Mentions' text format parses each note's text again), a traversal along a chain through every shape and one component of them all (3 / 30 ms), and the pathological queries (25 / 250 ms); `:invalid` over boards not shown (15 / 150 ms of main time, in slices), each slice within 3 ms (`querySlice`: 2 ms and the record that crosses it); `* --limit 10000` and its JSON off the main actor (15 / 150 ms), the command's main time within `queryMain` and `queryTitleMain` together (`queryRowsMain`: its rows' derived names) and beyond its query's own within `queryMain`; a commit while a query holds the file (at most 0.1 / 0.5 ms more, `queryHeldCommit`), and a drag event's input with queries installed and a query run first (at most 0.01 ms more, `queryUpkeep`); a drag's frames at 60 Hz while `:invalid`'s slices run, each within a frame (`queryFrame`, the one query budget on the wall clock, its frames driven by a strict main-queue dispatch timer, `FrameDriver`, since a run-loop timer in a windowless process fires up to 7 ms late on its own); and `--ask`'s request without the model (1 / 5 ms main, 3 / 10 ms off). It only reports ⌘A's pan frame zoomed out, the first frame zoomed in to 35% from skeletons (every text in view laid out), a frame just under 35% with covers on the titled shapes in view, a pinch from 100% to 10% and back, a frame at each zoom (in full just above 35%, a frame of the generated board takes more than 16 ms), the Mentioned In tab's lines for the subboard mentioned 100 times (`backlinks(of:)`, about 1 ms: read only while the tab shows and a mentioner changes), the inspector's list replaced on a switch between boards of 10,000 (a 50 ms target it misses by far: every row is replaced, about a second at 10,000, which the switch budgets leave out; "Not built yet"), a crumb's menu's first read of the root's rows (some 23 ms at 10,000) and the menu shown in the window (some 35 ms the first time, 10 after), a chain of 1,000 boards written, read and walked (one record can't be split). `./test.sh` runs it small, in debug, without the budgets.
