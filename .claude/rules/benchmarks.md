## Benchmarks

- `./build.sh bench` builds BenchmarkTests in release and runs it: saving, opening, validation's first read (every shape, checked against its kind's schema too) and its read after a drag event, after one of a subboard 100 notes mention (their mention rule looked it up, and ignores moves) and after moving every shape, a hover and a drag frame (titles' pills and locks in view), an empty board's frame on Retina at 100%, 70%, 35% and 10% (the background and the dot grid, the camera still, panning and pinching), the first frame and each after zoomed out to fit the board (at 10,000 shapes, as far as the 10% zoom goes), where shapes are skeletons, which show no adornments (a title is said on a shape big enough on screen: Titles' cover), a pan's frame with every shape selected (⌘A) at 100% (the camera a point from the last frame's, so the selection's box is worked out again, as in a pan), a board all out of view (what it adds to an empty board's frame, at 100% and 10%, with nothing selected and with every shape), a drag that snaps with every shape of the board in view (at 10%: its first event, which reads them, in a frame; each after within a drag event's input budget), a hover on a note's port dot and a drag from it, a hover onto a title's pill and onto a mention's, reading the table of contents' rows after a commit, a keystroke in the `@` picker (the `@` that opens it too, on a file of two boards typed inside the subboard, and on a board of empty notes where only 3 notes can be mentioned; and on the line of a command that takes few shapes, five subboards among 10,000 named notes, by the kinds it names as `/move-to`'s are: the first `@`, which makes the kinds' members in one pass over the catalog (some 0.5 ms, reported), then the `@` (listing all five) and the slowest keystroke, each within the picker's 2 ms of the thread's CPU (some 0.01 ms); the same command with its rule alone, which asks every shape the nearby search gathers, took some 4.6 ms for the `@` at 10,000 and 0.4 at 1,000, reported), a rename of a subboard mentioned 100 times (the rows and validation's read after it too), the panel of that subboard, its Connections tab showing, read after a drag event of another note and after a mentioner's rename, what's connected to a shape with 100 edges (`topology.neighbors(of:)`), a completion near the end of a type's JSON as long as the model writes (34,000 bytes; some 0.3 to 0.4 ms), a `canvas draw` of 20 notes each pushed along its row of the grid, the minimap's first picture (a frame at a time) and a drag frame with it, ten subboards' previews filling in (the longest frame, and how many), previews drawn larger than their masks, as paths (ten cards of 100 shapes at 100% on a 2× screen, the frame within 16 ms, some 5.5 against 8.3 with masks; a subboard of the board's worth at 800%, its preview drawn alone within `Budget.previewPaths`, 3 ms, some 1 to 1.5 against the mask's 15, and the frame reported beside the card alone's), a shape holding all the view at 800% on a 2× screen, on its middle (a subboard 2400 × 1600, a note, a type note of six lines: within `Budget.coveredShape`, 3 ms, some 0.4 to 0.9, its shadow left out since its fill hides it, where blurring it took 24 to 26), and with its left edge down the view's middle or its corner in the middle, reported (its shadow blurred on the CPU, some 8 to 15 ms; on screen the GPU's, at 60 fps), and undoing and redoing a drag (with its frame), a tenth of the board's move and deletion, and every shape's deletion, on generated boards of 1,000 and 10,000 shapes (three notes in ten mention a subboard and a shape that isn't there, so frames draw pills), against their budgets (the persistence plan's; each said on its constant in `Benchmarks.swift`'s `Budget`). Frames (`FrameBenchmarks.swift`, its head comment and budgets say what each holds): the frame dragged holding every box shape on it is reported, some 22 ms at 7,826 (moving thousands of shapes an event costs what it does with them selected). Across boards, on generated files (`generatedFile(root:children:depth:)`, the shipped subboards): opening 10,000 shapes spread over nested boards (under 1 s); entering a subboard of 10,000 cold with ⌘↓ (`Budget.enterCold`), and going back up with ⌘↑ (with the root listed whole after it, too) and down again warm (the same, in a frame); a hover, a drag and the rows after it beside a subboard of 10,000 and inside one while the root holds as many (a frame; 1 ms), and the drag event's input alone (1 ms, and at most 3× what it costs on a file of that board alone, so a commit's upkeep of the whole file shows); the minimap's picture of the board entered (a frame at a time); a crumb's menu inside a subboard, the root's rows after a move on the board shown (1 ms: a move makes none again) and after a rename there (a frame: every note's row up there is made again); and deleting a subboard whose boards hold 10,000 (`Budget.subboardDeletion`, whose comment says why it is a frame and 6 ms, the owner's word included), and undoing or redoing that (100 ms). Queries (`QueryBenchmarks.swift`, 1,000 / 10,000 shapes; what each holds said on its `Budget` constant, with most figures, the `:incomplete` decision and `FrameDriver`'s reason). Beyond those: a note by its name reads no type's schema cold, since the types' sync reads every type as a file opens and as it changes; `queryProblemsMain` measured 8.0 and 70.7 ms alone; `:incomplete`'s count, measured alone in release, went from 0.13 ms to 7.2 at 1,000 shapes and from 0.14 to 70 at 10,000 when it became Validation's word, and `incompleteRelations10k` went from 28 to 85 of its 150; the batch of 20 queries by title and kind (`referenceBatchMain`, `referenceBatchOffMain`) was some 0.10 ms and 2.6 ms at 1,000 shapes, 0.12 and 25 at 10,000, release, measured 2026-10-09 with the generated board's types as titled notes (0.22, 2.3, 1.6 and 26 while it held schema cards): a derived name costs some 4 µs and the batch reads each once, never once a query, which a debug build counts by `derivedTitlesAsked`. Find (`FindBenchmarks.swift`, on generated boards of 1,000 and 10,000; what each holds said on its `Budget` constant): the index's whole build is reported, some 25 ms at 10,000; the keystrokes are "n" (some 22,000 hits), "note 5", "somthing" (a typo that hits every note with "something") and "zzzzq" (nothing: a typo tried at every word); `findFrameAdded`'s frame itself, which the generated board alone takes more than 16 ms to draw at 36%, is reported, with the frames it takes to lay every hit in view out and each after. Stand-ins (`StandInBenchmarks.swift`; what each holds said on its `Budget` constant; the bench's own `Mirror` and `Lens`, so a budget holds the mechanism, not a kind (the Reference kind has its own, below), against `StandInBenchPlugin(plain: true)`, the same kinds standing for nothing): `provideFanOut` is some 6 ms in release since a `ShapeID` is two words, 13 to 17 while ids were `UUID`s copied through witnesses. It only reports ⌘A's pan frame zoomed out, the first frame zoomed in to 35% from skeletons (every text in view laid out), a frame just under 35% with covers on the titled shapes in view, a pinch from 100% to 10% and back, a frame at each zoom (in full just above 35%, a frame of the generated board takes more than 16 ms), the inspector's list replaced on a switch between boards of 10,000 (a 50 ms target it misses by far: every row is replaced, about a second at 10,000, which the switch budgets leave out; "Not built yet"), a crumb's menu's first read of the root's rows (some 23 ms at 10,000) and the menu shown in the window (some 35 ms the first time, 10 after), a chain of 1,000 boards written, read and walked (one record can't be split). `./test.sh` runs it small, in debug, without the budgets.
- Jump To's (JumpToBenchmarks.swift; [jump to](jump-to.md)), the thread's CPU, `./build.sh bench` holding them and `./test.sh` only reporting, what each holds said on its `Budget` constant: the keystrokes typed are the test's lists, on a board, ten boards deep (`generatedFile(root: 0, children: [10,000], depth: 10)`) and on `titledTree` (10,000 notes called "Checkout n" spread under three levels of named containers, 1,110 of them), where "alpha gamma checkout" matches every note's title by its last piece and costs the most, some 3.3 ms; the catalog's whole build, some 17 to 58 ms at 10,000 shapes, is reported (the slice is 2 ms and a shape's read overshoots by one title); a rename's upkeep reads the shape, counted in debug builds by `entriesRead`.

- Laid-out boxes' (`LayoutBenchmarks.swift`; [editor](editor.md)), the thread's CPU, `./build.sh bench` holding them and `./test.sh` only reporting, what each holds said on its `Budget` constant, with its runs: stretches naming gauges, and notes with rows through Types (the generated board of 10,000 shapes, some 7,000 of them notes, plus a type of four text fields; every sixth note, every note or none an instance of it; each run on words nobody has measured, zoomed to fit); `typeEditReach`'s budget is three times the median where +4 was measured and 15 guessed. In `./test.sh` (debug, small) it also holds that each note with rows and words had its words measured exactly once, that the second frame measures nothing and that a field added to the type does not measure again. The words' height is not stored: it is paid once for each distinct text and width in a run of the app and never per frame, the realistic board (a sixth of its notes typed) pays some 50 ms on a first frame that already takes 100, and the worst board (every note typed, one a script drew) a quarter of a second once; storing it would put a number in every note's props that follows from Types' constant and from whether the note has rows, kept by every write of its words (typing, the model, draw and paste) with a migration and a fixture. If the worst board ever matters, the cheaper lever is the measure itself and it changes nothing on disk.
