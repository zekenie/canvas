# Benchmarks

What `./build.sh bench` measures, and every budget it holds.

Related: [drawing](drawing.md), [queries](queries.md), [find](find.md), [jump to](jump-to.md), [neighbors](neighbors.md), [build](build.md), [testing](testing.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

## Benchmarks

- `./build.sh bench` builds BenchmarkTests in release and runs it: saving, opening, validation's first read (every shape, checked against its kind's schema too) and its read after a drag event, after one of a subboard 100 notes mention (their mention rule looked it up, and ignores moves) and after moving every shape, a hover and a drag frame (titles' pills and locks in view), an empty board's frame on Retina at 100%, 70%, 35% and 10% (the background and the dot grid, the camera still, panning and pinching), the first frame and each after zoomed out to fit the board (at 10,000 shapes, as far as the 10% zoom goes), where shapes are skeletons, which show no adornments (a title is said on a shape big enough on screen: Titles' cover), a pan's frame with every shape selected (⌘A) at 100% (the camera a point from the last frame's, so the selection's box is worked out again, as in a pan), a board all out of view (what it adds to an empty board's frame, at 100% and 10%, with nothing selected and with every shape), a drag that snaps with every shape of the board in view (at 10%: its first event, which reads them, in a frame; each after within a drag event's input budget), a hover on a note's port dot and a drag from it, a hover onto a title's pill and onto a mention's, reading the table of contents' rows after a commit, a keystroke in the `@` picker (the `@` that opens it too, on a file of two boards typed inside the subboard, and on a board of empty notes where only 3 notes can be mentioned; and on the line of a command that takes few shapes, five subboards among 10,000 named notes, by the kinds it names as `/move-to`'s are: the first `@`, which makes the kinds' members in one pass over the catalog (some 0.5 ms, reported), then the `@` (listing all five) and the slowest keystroke, each within the picker's 2 ms of the thread's CPU (some 0.01 ms); the same command with its rule alone, which asks every shape the nearby search gathers, took some 4.6 ms for the `@` at 10,000 and 0.4 at 1,000, reported), a rename of a subboard mentioned 100 times (the rows and validation's read after it too), the panel of that subboard, its Mentioned In tab showing, read after a drag event of another note and after a mentioner's rename, what's connected to a shape with 100 edges (`topology.neighbors(of:)`), a completion near the end of a type's JSON as long as the model writes (34,000 bytes; some 0.3 to 0.4 ms), a `canvas draw` of 20 notes each pushed along its row of the grid, the minimap's first picture (a frame at a time) and a drag frame with it, ten subboards' previews filling in (the longest frame, and how many), previews drawn larger than their masks, as paths (ten cards of 100 shapes at 100% on a 2× screen, the frame within 16 ms, some 5.5 against 8.3 with masks; a subboard of the board's worth at 800%, its preview drawn alone within `Budget.previewPaths`, 3 ms, some 1 to 1.5 against the mask's 15, and the frame reported beside the card alone's), a shape holding all the view at 800% on a 2× screen, on its middle (a subboard 2400 × 1600, a note, a type note of six lines: within `Budget.coveredShape`, 3 ms, some 0.4 to 0.9, its shadow left out since its fill hides it, where blurring it took 24 to 26), and with its left edge down the view's middle or its corner in the middle, reported (its shadow blurred on the CPU, some 8 to 15 ms; on screen the GPU's, at 60 fps), and undoing and redoing a drag (with its frame), a tenth of the board's move and deletion, and every shape's deletion, on generated boards of 1,000 and 10,000 shapes (three notes in ten mention a subboard and a shape that isn't there, so frames draw pills), against their budgets (the persistence plan's: open 10,000 shapes in under 1 s, Topology's index and Mentions' catalog within that in 50 ms, a frame in 16 ms, zoomed out too (and ⌘A's at 100%), which undo and redo of a drag fit with its frame, and of a tenth of the board's move or deletion without one; an empty board's frame in 2 ms, the thread's CPU time (`Budget.emptyFrame`); validation's first read of 10,000 in 100 ms, and undoing or redoing their deletion; the rows, validation and the panel's layout after a commit, in 1 ms (`Budget.panelAfterCommit` the last); a picker keystroke, and a type's completion, in 2 ms; a rename of a shape mentioned 100 times, its commit and validation's read after it, in 1 ms more than one mentioned by none; the neighbors in 1 ms; a board of 10,000 all out of view in 1 ms over an empty board's frame, selected or not, since nothing off screen is drawn and a shape outlined out of view costs a look at its kept extent; and the draw in 150 ms, time the window doesn't draw). Frames (`FrameBenchmarks.swift`): a note dropped across a frame's edge, its release settling it in (`Budget.settle`, 2 ms), a frame resized over the board's shapes, its release raising what it takes in above it (`Budget.settleResize`, half a frame), and a frame dragged holding a row of the board (a drag event's input, 1 ms) and every box shape on it (reported: moving thousands of shapes an event costs what it does with them selected, some 22 ms at 7,826). Across boards, on generated files (`generatedFile(root:children:depth:)`, the shipped subboards): opening 10,000 shapes spread over nested boards (under 1 s); entering a subboard of 10,000 cold with ⌘↓ (the switch, a frame, the rows and validation's read, together, under 250 ms, `Budget.enterCold`; who mentions whom, Topology's, and Mentions' catalog are the file's, read as it opens, so entering reads none of them), and going back up with ⌘↑ (with the root listed whole after it, too) and down again warm (the same, in a frame); a hover, a drag and the rows after it beside a subboard of 10,000 and inside one while the root holds as many (a frame; 1 ms), and the drag event's input alone (1 ms, and at most 3× what it costs on a file of that board alone, so a commit's upkeep of the whole file shows); the minimap's picture of the board entered (a frame at a time); a crumb's menu inside a subboard, the root's rows after a move on the board shown (1 ms: a move makes none again) and after a rename there (a frame: every note's row up there is made again); and deleting a subboard whose boards hold 10,000 (in a frame and 2 ms, `Budget.subboardDeletion`: the generated file holds 2,608 titles twice, which Titles' `TitleIndex` (`TitleValidationPlugin`'s service) takes out), and undoing or redoing that (100 ms). Queries (`QueryBenchmarks.swift`, `extension Budget`, 1,000 / 10,000 shapes): a query's main time, its reading and the turn that takes up the file (`queryMain`, 0.2 / 0.5 ms, also after a drag event that moved every shape), and the vocabulary's first build (1 ms); off the main actor, `sticky-note[text*="note" i]:not(:inside(*))` first after opening, 100 rows (`queryScan`, 5 / 40 ms), a note by its name (1 / 5 ms, and its derived names in slices on a board looked at, 0.5 / 4 ms; no type's schema is read cold, since the types' sync reads every type as a file opens and as it changes; no shipped kind's names are derived now, so this measures the slices with none to ask), notes with an edge to a subboard 100 mention (0.5 ms, brief rows, from the anchor's edges; 2 ms with their attributes, `queryByIDRows`, since Mentions' text format parses each note's text again), a traversal along a chain through every shape and one component of them all (3 / 30 ms), and the pathological queries (25 / 250 ms); `:invalid` over boards not shown (15 / 150 ms of main time, in slices), each slice within 3 ms (`querySlice`: 2 ms and the record that crosses it); `* --limit 10000` and its JSON off the main actor (15 / 150 ms), the command's main time within `queryMain` and `queryTitleMain` together (`queryRowsMain`: its rows' derived names) and beyond its query's own within `queryMain`; a commit while a query holds the file (at most 0.1 / 0.5 ms more, `queryHeldCommit`), and a drag event's input with queries installed and a query run first (at most 0.01 ms more, `queryUpkeep`); a drag's frames at 60 Hz while `:invalid`'s slices run, each within a frame (`queryFrame`, the one query budget on the wall clock, its frames driven by a strict main-queue dispatch timer, `FrameDriver`, since a run-loop timer in a windowless process fires up to 7 ms late on its own); a batch of 20 queries by title and kind, ids only (`matches`), on the generated board, whose types are titled notes (`referenceBatchMain`, `queryTitleMain`'s 0.5 / 4 ms, held though no shipped kind's names are derived now: a derived name costs some 4 µs and the batch reads each once, never once a query, which a debug build counts by `derivedTitlesAsked`; off the main actor `referenceBatchOffMain`, 20 / 100 ms; some 0.10 ms and 2.6 ms at 1,000 shapes, 0.12 and 25 at 10,000, release, measured 2026-10-09 with the generated board's types as titled notes; 0.22, 2.3, 1.6 and 26 while it held schema cards), and `--ask`'s request without the model (1 / 5 ms main, 3 / 10 ms off). Find (`FindBenchmarks.swift`, `extension Budget`, on generated boards of 1,000 and 10,000): the index's build on the first ⌘F, each slice of the main actor within 3 ms (`findIndexSlice`: 2 ms, `FindStyle.buildSlice`, and the shape that crosses it; the whole build reported, some 25 ms at 10,000), a keystroke for "n" (some 22,000 hits), "note 5", "somthing" (a typo that hits every note with "something") and "zzzzq" (nothing: a typo tried at every word), its main time within 0.5 ms (`findKeystrokeMain`: the fold, the handover, the answer taken, its places and counts made off the main actor) and its search off it within 10 ms (`findKeystrokeOffMain`, `cpu`), what Find adds to a commit before the first ⌘F and to a drag event's input while it's open, against the shipped list without Find (at most 0.01 ms, `findUpkeep`), what it adds to the first frame after each of five keystrokes with some 500 hits in view at 36% (at most 5 ms, `findFrameAdded`: its layouts within `FindStyle.layoutBudget`, 2 ms, and its marks; the frame itself, which the generated board alone takes more than 16 ms to draw there, reported, with the frames it takes to lay every hit in view out and each after), and a frame at 10% with every note a hit (`findFrame`, 16 ms). Stand-ins (`StandInBenchmarks.swift`, `extension Budget`; the bench's own `Mirror`, standing for what its record names, and `Lens`, for what's provided, so a budget holds the mechanism, not a kind (the Reference kind has its own, below); `StandInBenchPlugin(plain: true)` is the same kinds standing for nothing, what a stand-in costs is measured against): Topology's index as a file opens with a hundredth of its shapes stand-ins for one note and a tenth arrows into them (100 and 1,000 at 10,000; a stopwatch of the main thread's CPU around Topology's service, `ThreadStopwatch`; `topologyOpenStandIns`, the index's 50 ms at 10,000), what's connected to a note through 100 stand-ins (`neighborsThroughStandIn`, 1 ms), re-pointing a stand-in 100 arrows end at (the commit, `repointStandIn`, 1 ms), a drag event's input dragging 50 lenses answered and the two notes they stand for against the same shapes standing for nothing (`standInDragUpkeep`, 0.01 ms more, `queryUpkeep`'s: a move reads nothing), a commit adding an arrow into one of 500 stand-ins while a query holds the file (`heldCommitStandIns`, `queryHeldCommit`'s 0.1 / 0.5 ms more), and a provide changing a lens's 50 shapes with 100 arrows into it and another's whose arrow to a third is held to 256 pairs, some 10,600 edges out and in (`provideFanOut`, a frame: the design's 2 ms is past what diffing and filing those edges costs; some 6 ms in release since a `ShapeID` is two words, 13 to 17 while ids were `UUID`s copied through witnesses). It only reports ⌘A's pan frame zoomed out, the first frame zoomed in to 35% from skeletons (every text in view laid out), a frame just under 35% with covers on the titled shapes in view, a pinch from 100% to 10% and back, a frame at each zoom (in full just above 35%, a frame of the generated board takes more than 16 ms), the Mentioned In tab's lines for the subboard mentioned 100 times (`backlinks(of:)`, about 1 ms: read only while the tab shows and a mentioner changes), the inspector's list replaced on a switch between boards of 10,000 (a 50 ms target it misses by far: every row is replaced, about a second at 10,000, which the switch budgets leave out; "Not built yet"), a crumb's menu's first read of the root's rows (some 23 ms at 10,000) and the menu shown in the window (some 35 ms the first time, 10 after), a chain of 1,000 boards written, read and walked (one record can't be split). `./test.sh` runs it small, in debug, without the budgets.
- Jump To's (JumpToBenchmarks.swift; [jump to](jump-to.md)), the thread's CPU, `./build.sh bench` holding them and `./test.sh` only reporting: a keystroke in the palette with one piece over 10,000 shapes (`Budget.jumpToKeystroke`, 2 ms, the `@` picker's: the best 50 of the catalog's titles by `FuzzyMatcher.rank`; "p", "pl", "pla", "plan", "plan1", "pn11", "title", "recor", "paln", "zzzzq", "a", "e" on a board of them, and on the same shapes ten boards deep, where one piece reads no tree) and with several (`Budget.jumpToPathKeystroke`, 4 ms: the last piece against every title, then the shapes it matches by their paths, a container's row made once per query; "plan note", "p t 40", "p t 4", "plan 11", "paln 11", "title 40", "p r 1", "a e" on `generatedFile(root: 0, children: [10,000], depth: 10)`, and eight more on `titledTree`: 10,000 notes called "Checkout n" spread under three levels of named containers, 1,110 of them, where "alpha gamma checkout" matches every note's title by its last piece and costs the most, some 3.3 ms); the catalog's build, the first slice at once and the rest on the scheduler, on a board, two boards, ten boards deep and `titledTree` (its longest slice `Budget.jumpToSlice`, 3 ms, held; the slice is 2 ms and a shape's read overshoots by one title, and the whole build, some 17 to 58 ms at 10,000 shapes, reported); and what a commit costs with the plugin in the list over what it costs without it (`Budget.jumpToUpkeep`, 0.1 ms), before the first ⌘K and with the catalog read, for a drag's move (reading nothing) and a rename (reading the shape, counted in debug builds by `entriesRead`).
- References (`ReferenceBenchmarks.swift`, `extension Budget`): a frame of 1,000 pills in view at 40% (just over where shapes become skeletons), half for notes on the board and half for notes on another board of the file, each frame drawn with the camera a point from the last one's, and zoomed out to 30%, where each pill is one square fill, within a frame (`Budget.referencePills`, 16 ms, the thread's CPU time; release, some 12 ms at 40% and 2.8 zoomed out, where a pill asks only whether it stands for nothing, never for a name; at 40% a direct pill's condition and its channel mismatch are kept by `ReferenceState` from frame to frame, a dictionary hit each (looking them up was some 4 ms, 15 to 16 in all), its capsule is one `fillStroke` and its symbol a kept bitmap). And a keystroke in the list over a pill's field, the opening included, over 10,000 shapes of which 20 are public (`Budget.referencePickerKeystroke`, the picker's 2 ms; release, 0.02 ms to open and 0.05 the slowest keystroke, where scanning the catalog for what's public took 5 ms: the scope's members, `MentionState`, are kept and ranked alone; the first ask makes them, one pass over the catalog, 1.9 ms at 10,000). And twenty dynamic references answered after a commit, by title and by kind, on the batch benchmark's board (its types titled notes) with every note public: `ReferenceState`'s main-actor time from the wait for quiet firing to the answers landing, the batch's main part and the landing's provide and telling (`Budget.referenceAnswersMain`, 1 / 5 ms, the thread's CPU time).
