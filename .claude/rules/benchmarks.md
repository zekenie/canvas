## Benchmarks

- `./build.sh bench` builds BenchmarkTests in release and runs it: saving, opening, validation's first read (every shape, checked against its kind's schema too) and its read after a drag event, after one of a subboard 100 notes mention (their mention rule looked it up, and ignores moves) and after moving every shape, a hover and a drag frame (titles' pills and locks in view), an empty board's frame on Retina at 100%, 70%, 35% and 10% (the background and the dot grid, the camera still, panning and pinching), the first frame and each after zoomed out to fit the board (at 10,000 shapes, as far as the 10% zoom goes), where shapes are skeletons, which show no adornments (a title is said on a shape big enough on screen: Titles' cover), a pan's frame with every shape selected (⌘A) at 100% (the camera a point from the last frame's, so the selection's box is worked out again, as in a pan), a board all out of view (what it adds to an empty board's frame, at 100% and 10%, with nothing selected and with every shape), a drag that snaps with every shape of the board in view (at 10%: its first event, which reads them, in a frame; each after within a drag event's input budget), a hover on a note's port dot and a drag from it, a hover onto a title's pill and onto a mention's, reading the table of contents' rows after a commit, a keystroke in the `@` picker (the `@` that opens it too, on a file of two boards typed inside the subboard, and on a board of empty notes where only 3 notes can be mentioned; - Sizes and growth (`Scaling.swift`): `./build.sh bench` runs the size-driven benchmarks at 1,000 and 10,000 shapes; `BENCH_SIZES=10000,100000 ./build.sh bench` runs them at those sizes instead and holds no budget (`holdsToBudgets` false: a budget is for the sizes it was set at), and `BENCH_SUITE=n` runs the one suite at place n of `main.swift`'s `suites` (the build, then the executable, which reads both; `TEST_FILTER` narrows to tests, as ever, and `TEST_FILTER=zzz ./build.sh bench` builds in release and runs nothing). `./test.sh` runs them in a debug build at one size (300), holding no budget and with nothing for Scaling to pair, and takes at most three timings of a figure (`measuredRuns`: `median(runs:)` and the loops that repeat a measurement, a middle of what they took; `debugRuns:` says how many a benchmark whose runs build on each other needs, as a drag that must pass `Style.dragThreshold` before it moves anything): a check that counts the runs (a rename's name, a commit each, the entries read) counts `measuredRuns(n)`, and `./build.sh bench` takes the runs written. At any two sizes the last suite, Scaling, pairs each report line at the smallest size with the same line at the largest (a line is the size `sizes` was walked to when it was reported: `BenchSizes` says so as it's walked, and each test starts at none) and fails any timing of 1 ms or more at the larger size that grew more than twice as much as the board did (20 times from 10,000 to 100,000: linear work grows some 10 to 15 times there, the larger board falling out of the caches, a square 100), printing the five that grew most. A run with `TEST_FILTER` leaves it out unless the filter names it.
