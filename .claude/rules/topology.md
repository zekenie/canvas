# Topology

Topology: the typed graph of edges derived from records, edge types (a child may be declared under another plugin's: a channel's under `arrow`) and sources, stand-ins and the views of edges through them, answers provided for stand-ins, the one delivery of every change to the edges and settling, containment as one tree, and snapshots for other threads.

Related: [connections](connections.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [jump to](jump-to.md), [publishing](publishing.md), [queries](queries.md), [containers](containers.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [types](types.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Topology](../../docs/plugin-system.md#topology).

## Architecture

- Topology (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait, on the core alone; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Topology") is what connects and contains what, whatever is where: edges, a typed graph (an arrow bound at both ends, a mention), and containment, one tree of two kinds of link (the container a shape is in on its board, a frame, else the board it's on); spatial relations (a note within another shape's bounds) are geometry, not Topology's. The carrier shape is the system of record (an arrow's bound ends and `channel` are its edge, a note's text its mentions): `TopologyIndex` (`editor.topology`, a service) derives the whole file's edges from the records, by carrier, by each end and by type, in memory, never saved (nothing in props, meta or the file: tldraw's binding records would be a second truth to keep in step, and a mention outlives what it names), and never transacts. An `Edge` is its `type` (the most specific), `source`, `target`, `carrier`, `slot` ("" for the carrier itself, else the property's name; `isConnection` is the empty one, the convention Queries and Types read and nobody else spells), `count` (repeats in that slot, a value, not identity) and `origin` (`EdgeOrigin`: `.emitted`, the init's default; `.forwarded(source:target:)`, naming the stand-in at each end it went through, boxed (`indirect`), so an origin is one word; `.standing`; `literalSource`, `literalTarget` and `literal` are the edge as emitted); only a connection bound at both ends is one (no loose edges), and a shape kept aside, or on a board the editor can't show, carries none. Edge types are types (`EdgeType`: `static let id: EdgeTypeID`, `parent` and `summary` nil by default: a lowercase line, no period, saying what it joins and which way, which a query's vocabulary shows, `EdgeTypeTree.summary(of:)`, a channel's through `Channel.summary`) declared by the plugin that owns the meaning (`registrar.edgeType(T.self)`, or `edgeType(_:parent:summary:)` with a parent resolved from the frozen registry; the `EdgeTypes` point, one per id, whose `check` refuses an undeclared parent and a loop), a tree (`EdgeTypeTree`: `registry.edgeTypes`, or `topology.types`, kept); shipped: `stands-for` (Topology's own `StandingEdge`, a root, declared by `TopologyPlugin`, so every vocabulary lists it first), `arrow` (Arrows' `ArrowEdge`) with each channel's type (`note-link`, `subboard-link`) under it, `mention` (Mentions' `MentionEdge`, a root), and `is-a` (Types' `IsAEdge`, a root, from a shape to each type its `is` names, in the slot `is`, the one record of what a shape is that Types' index reads, as drawn (a shape's list in order, a type's instances), so a shape on a board the app can't show is of no type there: the property rule's first shipped use, `registrar.edges(in: IsAProperty.self, as: IsAEdge.self)` on every kind that opts into `is`, notes and arrows, so an entry that names a pill is forwarded to what it stands for and one that names a deleted type is an edge to a shape that isn't there until the delete is undone). A type nobody declares (a newer app's channel) is kept as written, with no parent. Queries filter by `EdgeTypeFilter`: `.all`, `.within(t)` (t and every type under it), `.only(t)`, each also over a sequence (an empty one admits nothing). Sources (the `EdgeSources` point; several per kind add up) say what a record carries: `x.edges { shape, registry, sink in … }`, `registrar.edges(K.self) { record, registry, sink in … }`, `scope.edges { record, registry, sink in … }` on the kinds a scope reaches (`registrar.forShapes(adopting: .namingShapes) { $0.edges { … } }`, Mentions'; `EdgeSources` takes scopes: [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md)), and the property rule `registrar.edges(in: P.self, as: T.self)` (every shape a `ShapeNaming` added property's value names, slot its key, on the kinds that hold it, `forShapes(holding: P.self)`; it doesn't declare `T`); `sink.emit(type, from:to:slot:)` counts repeats and keeps the order of first emission, and drops an edge in the slot `stands-for` or of that type (Topology's own; `reservedEdgesDropped` counts them in debug builds). A source is a pure function of one record and the registry (never other shapes, geometry, services or time, nor where a box is), so each commit reads again only what it removed, added, changed other than by moving (`ChangeSet.moved`), or touched `elsewhere`, and undo is exact; `registry.edges(of:)` is one record's. Queries cost what they return: `edges(of:_:types:)` (`.outgoing`, `.incoming`, `.either`; an edge to itself once; `hasEdges(of:_:types:where:)` stops at the first, making nothing; `forEachEdge(of:_:types:view:_:)` hands each to a closure, making no array, for a walk over many shapes' edges, Types' over instances of instances; an edge as emitted is taken as it is in every view), `neighbors(of:_:types:)` (the other ends by type: what's connected, and how), `edges(ofType:)`, `edges(carriedBy:)`, `end(_:)` (`EdgeEnd`: `.live(board)`, `.unavailable`, `.missing`), `revision(of:)` (observed per shape, counted from its first read) and `touched` (the last change's to the edges, a commit's or a provide's; `touched(types:)`, of some types). Containment, never an edge, reads boards and containers on them as one tree, with no index of its own (the boards keep who's in which container, and only containers the core goes by count, `document.container(of:containingKinds:)`, nonisolated): `parent(of:)` (the container a shape is in on its board, else its board's owner; nil on the root in no container), `children(of:)` (what a container holds, back to front, then, for an owner, its board's shapes in no container), `ancestors(of:)` (nearest first), `descendants(of:)` (every shape inside one at any depth, in file order, on the index too since Boards' path index files again what is inside a container renamed), `isInside(_:_:)`, only on boards the editor can show, one piece of code (`Containment`, a value over a `CanvasFile`, the kinds that own boards and the containing kinds) for the index and its snapshot; `containerKinds` (on `Registry`, the index and the snapshot) is which kinds contain: those that own boards and `registry.containingKinds` (a kind that's both lists what it holds before its board); a move costs it nothing, and a board without containers what it did before there were any (`CanvasDocument.namesContainers`, O(1)). `topology.snapshot()` is Topology as it is now for any thread (`TopologySnapshot`, `Sendable`, O(1), reading no record and observing nothing: it shares the index's tables, `EdgeTables`, and the file, read without observing it, and answers through the same code, so it answers as the index would have as it was taken, though not what changed: no `revision(of:)` or `touched`), with more reads of the whole file: `forEachShape(inFileOrder:)` (every shape on a board the app can show, kept-aside ones too, with its board: the root's shapes in no container back to front, each followed at once by what's inside it, what it holds then, for an owner, its board's, before the next; iterative, so a chain of any depth; an error its body throws stops it, as the body's own type, `throws(E)`), `fileOrderKey(of:)` (a key that sorts as that walk visits, each ancestor's place among what's directly inside its parent, root down; O(depth), nil off the boards the app can show), `topLevel` (the root's shapes in no container) and `isTopLevel(_:)`, `container(of:on:)` (the container a record is directly in on its board, O(1), with no lookup for one in none: a query's walk), `descendants(of:)` (file order), `shape(_:)` (kept-aside ones too) and `presentTypes`. Hold one for a task, never past it: a commit copies each table it changes, once, while one's held; and one taken as services hear a commit is taken after looking the index up (`editor.topology`), so it has the commit: a snapshot of an index that hadn't would hold the commit's file with the commit before's edges. It reads every board the editor can show as it opens (a kind with no source costs a lookup), again for `replaceFile` and `replaceDocument`, and nothing on a switch. A service that reads the index as it hears a commit finds it up to date, wherever the two are in a plugin list: looking it up (`editor.topology`) has it hear the commit first ([editor](editor.md)); so does one that holds a reference it kept from its `make` (MentionState's, the relationship index's), since what a service looked up as it was made takes each change in before it. `TopologyPlugin()` is right after `SelectionPlugin()` in the shipped list. Every plugin list with Ports or Mentions needs it (and one with Queries, whose every query is `no_topology` without it). `canvas query` reads it through a snapshot, and `canvas draw` matches an edge by its ends, not its type.
- Stand-ins (`Forwarding.swift`; guide, "Stand-ins: forwarding"): a kind whose shapes stand for others says so, `x.standsIn(admitting:) { shape, registry in Forwarding? }` (the `EdgeForwarding` point, keyed by kind; the index caches the rules, each kind's admission (`registry.standInAdmissions`, made once a registry, which `registry.standIns` reads too) and `standInKinds` in `init`, as it does emitters, and a snapshot carries `standInKinds`; `admitting` is a `ForwardAdmissionMaker`, `{ registry in { record in Bool } }`: the admission it makes, once a registry, is `@Sendable` and reads the record alone, what it needs of the registry read as it's made (References' is Publishing's `registry.publicShapes`), so what a stand-in stands for can be worked out off the main actor): `.shapes(ids)`, named in the record, or `.provided(ForwardingKey)`, whatever is provided for the key the record holds (`.pending`, standing for nothing, in `pendingStandIns`, until an answer for that key comes). The rule and the admission are pure (records and registry, never position), so a move reads nothing. What one stands for (`effective`): the named ids in order, each once, that are on a board the editor can show, not kept aside, not itself, not of a stand-in kind (one hop) and admitted, however many (no cap: the owner's ask of 2026-10-22, when a reference stood for at most 50). Every edge a non-stand-in carries with a stand-in at an end resolves to one edge per pair of what its ends stand for (an end standing for nothing stays itself, so nothing dangles because of one), `.forwarded`, source-major, every pair; a stand-in's own edges stay as emitted, and it carries `.standing` edges of `StandingEdge` (`stands-for`, a root, Topology's own, declared by `TopologyPlugin`) in the slot `stands-for` to each shape it stands for. Three views (`EdgeView`): `.resolved` (the default of every read), `.recorded` (forwarded only through `.shapes` stand-ins: a function of records alone, what provided answers are worked out from) and `.literal` (as emitted, no standing edges); every edge read on the index has an overload with `view:` (the old signatures stay, as `.resolved`, so protocol witnesses keep matching), and `snapshot(_ view:)` answers every edge read in its view. Reads: `forwards(of:)`, `standIns(for:)` (also on a snapshot), `standIns(naming:)` (on the index: the stand-ins whose record names a shape, whether or not they stand for it now, no order; a reader that treats a direct stand-in as the shape it names, Types' relationships, finds a not admitted pill's edges through it), `directTarget(of:)` (the other way round: the one shape a stand-in's record names, `Forwarding.onlyShape` as the index read it, O(1)) and `standInKind(of:)` (a stand-in's kind, O(1)): References keeps no index of who names what, and asks these three, `forwarding(of:)` (`ForwardingState`), `edges(through:)`, `hasStandIns` (whether any shape the editor can show stands in, O(1): a follower looking for stand-ins among a change's shapes looks at none in a file with none). A kind a plugin may put down beside a shape by name (`canvas draw`'s edge to a shape on another board, [command line](command-line.md)) says how in its rule: `x.standsIn(admitting:, needs: "public", makes: { props, target, label in … })` (`ForwardingMaker`: given the props the kind's tool places, the shape to stand for and the name to show; `needs` is one word a message says of a shape that can't be stood for, read by no rule), and, if what it shows can say where that shape is, how one so made takes where it sits, leaving its props in step as its writers would: `seats: { props, seat, context in … }` (`ForwardingSeating`, given a `StandInSeat`: `.file(container:board:)`, the frame it sits in or the board it sits on, read in the file through `context`, or `.written(shape:seat:)`, the names of the containers the shape is in and of those it sits in, as a script wrote them, where there's no file to read: a measure's). `registry.standIns` (`StandIns`, main actor, nothing kept) reads the rules without an index: `kinds`, `isEmpty`, `forwarding(of: record)`, `makers` (the kinds whose rule says how and that a tool places, plugin order), `admits(kind, target)` (the kind's admission, never for a shape kept aside or a stand-in: one hop), `needs(kind)`, `fulfil(kind, &target)` (makes a shape something the kind may stand for, by its `standsIn(fulfilling:)` run on a copy kept only if `admits` then holds, so true and unchanged for one already admitted, false and unchanged when the kind can't; opaque to Topology, which learns nothing of publicness, and it doesn't ask leave to write: the caller's transaction does), `calls(standIn)` (what a stand-in calls what it stands for, from its kind's `standsIn(calls:)`: the label a direct one keeps, the words of a query one; nil if the kind doesn't say, so a gone note's name outlives it), `directTarget(of: record)` (the one shape a direct stand-in's record names, whether or not it may stand for it now: `Forwarding.onlyShape`, `.shapes` naming one shape, once or over again; nil for a query one, none or several; the one rule every reader that treats a direct stand-in as that shape goes by, Types' relationships and Draw's route, of a record so a run's own shapes read as the file's), `make(kind, standingFor:, label:)` (a new record in no document: the tool's shape at the origin, made to stand for the shape, put in step through `DerivedProps`; it never asks `admits`, whoever puts it down does) and `seat(&record, at:, in:)` (the kind's `seats` run on a record `make` made, which leaves it in step as the kind's writers would: it isn't derived again, since a kind's derive may take a pill fitted to its place at the tool's width for one placed, as References' does; a kind that doesn't say, or a shape that doesn't stand in, is left as it is: what Draw asks for a pill whose shape's title another shape has, and what `StandInRoute.seat` asks for a writer that is not a script, [command line](command-line.md)). Forwarded on read, in `EdgeTables` (shared by a snapshot): it keeps what carriers emitted and no copy, `byCarrier`, `byNode` (an edge filed under its source and target as emitted) and `byType`, with `forwards`/`standIns`/`forwardKind`, `standInCarriers` (the stand-ins, whose own edges are never forwarded) and each stand-in's kinds; every view is read from them as it's asked (`forwarded(_:_:)`: what an end forwards to in a view, none in the literal one, only `.record` ones in the recorded), so a read at the far end of an arrow into a stand-in makes its copies, a read at one of the shapes it stands for makes only its own (its own edges, then a stand-in's standing edges, then through each stand-in that stands for it, in the order they came to, the copies of the edges at that stand-in that end there and its standing edge: `visit`, which `hasEdges` stops in at the first), a standing edge is made from `forwards`, and `edges(ofType:)` reads standing edges by stand-in in the order they came to stand for something (`ArrivalOrder`). A read costs what it returns and two lookups an edge where anything stands for anything; what a stand-in stands for costs what came and went to change, never those shapes times the edges at it (`storedEdgeCount`, debug builds, is what carriers emitted whatever stands for what). The kinds a stand-in stands for (`standingKinds(of:)`, the first shape's kind first, then the rest by name, each once; on the index) are counted as shapes come and go (`forwardedKinds`, a shape's kind kept while something stands for it, and moved by a commit that turns it into another kind, `setKind`, which notes each stand-in of it and it in `touched` under `stands-for`, so a reader of the stand-in's kinds kept by its revision reads them again though no edge changed), so a reader of a stand-in's ports reads a few kinds (Connections' `PortState`, References' channel mismatch). Upkeep per commit: for each changed record, its rule if it's a stand-in (declared first, so its own edges are never forwarded), then its edges, set as emitted, the ends of the edges that came or went noted with every shape a stand-in at an end stands for; then each stand-in it read again worked out whole (`effective`), and, for each that names a shape it changed (`declaredBy`, `providedBy`), those shapes alone looked at again (`recheck`: one that may be stood for now comes in its place among what it names, `place(of:in:among:)`, one pass comparing ids, and one that can't goes; `targetsRead`, debug builds, counts the shapes looked at), and for each whose shapes changed the ends of what changed noted (`touchForwards`: its standing edges to what came and went, and, for each edge another shape carries at it, the two ends it was emitted with, what came and went, and what the other end reads as). A record's kind is looked up once (its sources and rule together, `KindUpkeep`), and the stand-in tables (`standing`, `declaredBy`, `providedBy`, `resolvedByCarrier`) only while they hold something, so a file with no stand-in reads and sets a shape as it did before there were any; the upkeep reads records through no closure (each would check it runs on the main actor). `touched` and revisions note every end and literal end (a carrier set twice in a commit may add a shape reached only in between: an extra bump, never a missed one). Rebuild sets each carrier as it reads it, then works out every stand-in there before or now, so a file with none costs what it did; it lets go of every answer provided (they were for the file as it was). `Edge` hashes its two ends alone (equality is every field), which the tables' diffs lean on, and a `ShapeID` is two words (Canvas: `UUID`'s layout is Foundation's to change, so a value holding one is copied, compared and hashed through witnesses), so an edge costs what plain words do. Draw's `share:` lets several edges go through one stand-in rather than a stand-in each, and `canvas measure` reads `make`'s size for a title without a board, seated as written for an end written as a path ([command line](command-line.md)).
- Provided answers (`Following.swift`; guide, "Provided answers, the one delivery, settling"): `topology.provide([id: ProvidedForwarding(key:shapes:revision:)])` gives `.provided` stand-ins what they stand for, never saved or undone, no commit; an answer for another key than the record's now, for a shape that isn't such a stand-in, or older (`revision`) than the one held is dropped, touching nothing; one that names what the one held names is taken as newer and changes nothing else (every commit asks a reference again, and most answers say what they said); the answer's shapes go through `effective` as named ones do, and `providedBy` has a commit to one of them look at that one again (`recheck`). **Answers worked out off the main actor** (`Preparing.swift`): `topology.preparer()` (O(1), the tables, the file's containment, what each stand-in declares, the answers held and each kind's admission, shared as a snapshot is, at the index's `generation`) is handed to another thread, whose `ForwardingPreparer.prepare(answers)` works out, pure, what `provide` would make of each, by the same two rules the index goes by (`AnswerTaking`, what an answer is to the one held, and `Effective.taking`, which named shapes a stand-in takes) (`PreparedAnswer.Outcome`: dropped; `newer`, the same shapes; or `stands`, what it will stand for, the `ForwardsChange` from what it stands for, and what the answer names that the one held didn't and no longer names), each named shape read once and each board's showing asked once; `provide(answers, prepared:)` takes each answer the preparation holds as given (`==`, which the same ids' storage answers at once) as it was worked out, what came and went alone, unless a change since the preparer was taken read again the stand-in or a shape the answer names (`changes(since:)` over `changeLog`, the shapes each change read again, answered or whose forwards changed, kept for the last 64 changes once a preparer has been taken, a change of more than 4,096 shapes or a file read afresh saying "everything"), when it's worked out on the main actor as one not prepared is (`preparedTaken`, `preparedRedone`, debug builds). References prepares every batch (Release, 2026-10-22: an answer adding one of 6,956 notes to a lens with 100 arrows into it, 0.07 ms on the main actor, 3.2 worked out there; [benchmarks](benchmarks.md)). A commit that changes a stand-in's key, deletes it or hides its board, and every rebuild, lets go of its answer (so a memo given again after an undo is taken whatever its revision). It runs the commit's stand-in pass seeded with the answered stand-ins, bumps revisions, sets `touched`, and tells followers (`.provide`); one that changes no edge leaves `touched` as it was. Never call it inside `documentDidChange`/`boardDidChange` or from a follower (a debug assert holds the latter): from a scheduled callback or a task's continuation.
- The one delivery: `topology.follow { change in … }` (`TopologyChange`: `touched`, `touchedByType`, `touched(types:in:)`, `cause` `.commit`/`.provide`/`.rebuild`) is called once after every change to the edges, whatever caused it, with the index as the change left it (a commit's or a file's once the index has taken it in, `editor.onceHeard`, so a follower may read it and what it looks up may read the index; a provide's at once); a change that touches no edge (a move) isn't told. It lasts while its `TopologyFollowing` is held (or until `cancel()`), followers in the order they began. It's THE way to keep state off edges: reading `touched` in `documentDidChange` misses every provide. Mentions follows it (its mention-typed ends: which shapes' resolutions it keeps), References' `ReferenceState` does (the channel mismatches its pills draw), and Arrows' `ArrowRowNesting` does on a registry with a stand-in kind, looking only while the file has a stand-in (`hasStandIns`), and Neighbors' `NeighborState` (when `touched` holds the shape hovered or one the last answer matched, what's lit for it is asked again) and `NeighborList` (the panel's lists, asked again at once for a change no commit made): [neighbors](neighbors.md).
- Settling: whoever provides answers registers `keepsSettling(while: { busy })` (a `SettleSource` token) and says `settleSourceChanged()` when it goes idle with no provide or commit to say so; `isSettling` is whether any source is busy, `pendingStandIns` the `.provided` stand-ins with no answer for their key, and `settled(timeout:) async -> Bool` waits (true at once if settled; false when the timeout, on `editor.scheduler`, runs out, or the task is cancelled), woken by every delivery, every provide and every source change or cancel. `QueryState.run(settle:)` ([queries](queries.md)) is the waiter in the shipped list: before it takes up the file, so it holds no snapshot and no other query waits for it, only for a plan that reads edges, at most `QueryBudget.settleWait` (and never past the query's deadline), then a `pending` warning.
- A channel's edge type is a child declared under another plugin's: `note-link` (StickyNotes'), `subboard-link` (Subboards') and `frame-link` (Frames') are under `arrow`, which `arrow-ports` declares (`registrar.channel(_:)` declares the channel's type under the connector's family, read from the registry). `EdgeTypes.check` refuses only a parent no installed plugin declares and a loop. Queries and lights that say `arrow` take its children in (descendants); `only arrow` leaves them out.
