# Topology

Topology: the typed graph of edges derived from records, edge types and sources, containment as one tree, and snapshots for other threads.

Related: [connections](connections.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [queries](queries.md), [containers](containers.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Topology](../../docs/plugin-system.md#topology).

## Architecture

- Topology (a surface on the core alone; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Topology") is what connects and contains what, whatever is where: edges, a typed graph (an arrow bound at both ends, a mention), and containment, one tree of two kinds of link (the container a shape is in on its board, a frame, else the board it's on); spatial relations (a note within another shape's bounds) are geometry, not Topology's. The carrier shape is the system of record (an arrow's bound ends and `channel` are its edge, a note's text its mentions): `TopologyIndex` (`editor.topology`, a service) derives the whole file's edges from the records, by carrier, by each end and by type, in memory, never saved (nothing in props, meta or the file: tldraw's binding records would be a second truth to keep in step, and a mention outlives what it names), and never transacts. An `Edge` is its `type` (the most specific), `source`, `target`, `carrier`, `slot` ("" for the carrier itself, else the property's name) and `count` (repeats in that slot); only a connection bound at both ends is one (no loose edges), and a shape kept aside, or on a board the editor can't show, carries none. Edge types are types (`EdgeType`: `static let id: EdgeTypeID`, `parent` and `summary` nil by default: a lowercase line, no period, saying what it joins and which way, which a query's vocabulary shows, `EdgeTypeTree.summary(of:)`, a channel's through `Channel.summary`) declared by the plugin that owns the meaning (`registrar.edgeType(T.self)`, or `edgeType(_:parent:summary:)` with a parent resolved from the frozen registry; the `EdgeTypes` point, one per id, whose `check` refuses an undeclared parent and a loop), a tree (`EdgeTypeTree`: `registry.edgeTypes`, or `topology.types`, kept); shipped: `arrow` (Connections' `ArrowEdge`) with each channel's type (`note-link`, `schema-link`) under it, and `mention` (Mentions' `MentionEdge`, a root). A type nobody declares (a newer app's channel) is kept as written, with no parent. Queries filter by `EdgeTypeFilter`: `.all`, `.within(t)` (t and every type under it), `.only(t)`, each also over a sequence (an empty one admits nothing). Sources (the `EdgeSources` point; several per kind add up) say what a record carries: `x.edges { shape, registry, sink in … }`, `registrar.edges(K.self) { record, registry, sink in … }`, `edgesForEveryShape(where:)` (asked once per kind), and the property rule `registrar.edges(in: P.self, as: T.self)` (every shape a `ShapeNaming` added property's value names, slot its key, on the kinds that opt in; it doesn't declare `T`); `sink.emit(type, from:to:slot:)` counts repeats and keeps the order of first emission. A source is a pure function of one record and the registry (never other shapes, geometry, services or time, nor where a box is), so each commit reads again only what it removed, added, changed other than by moving (`ChangeSet.moved`), or touched `elsewhere`, and undo is exact; `registry.edges(of:)` is one record's. Queries cost what they return: `edges(of:_:types:)` (`.outgoing`, `.incoming`, `.either`; an edge to itself once; `hasEdges(of:_:types:where:)` stops at the first, making nothing), `neighbors(of:_:types:)` (the other ends by type: what's connected, and how), `edges(ofType:)`, `edges(carriedBy:)`, `end(_:)` (`EdgeEnd`: `.live(board)`, `.unavailable`, `.missing`), `revision(of:)` (observed per shape, counted from its first read) and `touched` (the last commit's; `touched(types:)`, of some types). Containment, never an edge, reads boards and containers on them as one tree, with no index of its own (the boards keep who's in which container, and only containers the core goes by count, `document.container(of:containingKinds:)`, nonisolated): `parent(of:)` (the container a shape is in on its board, else its board's owner; nil on the root in no container), `children(of:)` (what a container holds, back to front, then, for an owner, its board's shapes in no container), `ancestors(of:)` (nearest first), `isInside(_:_:)`, only on boards the editor can show, one piece of code (`Containment`, a value over a `CanvasFile`, the kinds that own boards and the containing kinds) for the index and its snapshot; `containerKinds` (on `Registry`, the index and the snapshot) is which kinds contain: those that own boards and `registry.containingKinds` (a kind that's both lists what it holds before its board); a move costs it nothing, and a board without containers what it did before there were any (`CanvasDocument.namesContainers`, O(1)). `topology.snapshot()` is Topology as it is now for any thread (`TopologySnapshot`, `Sendable`, O(1), reading no record and observing nothing: it shares the index's tables, `EdgeTables`, and the file, read without observing it, and answers through the same code, so it answers as the index would have as it was taken, though not what changed: no `revision(of:)` or `touched`), with more reads of the whole file: `forEachShape(inFileOrder:)` (every shape on a board the app can show, kept-aside ones too, with its board: the root's shapes in no container back to front, each followed at once by what's inside it, what it holds then, for an owner, its board's, before the next; iterative, so a chain of any depth; an error its body throws stops it, as the body's own type, `throws(E)`), `fileOrderKey(of:)` (a key that sorts as that walk visits, each ancestor's place among what's directly inside its parent, root down; O(depth), nil off the boards the app can show), `topLevel` (the root's shapes in no container) and `isTopLevel(_:)`, `container(of:on:)` (the container a record is directly in on its board, O(1), with no lookup for one in none: a query's walk), `descendants(of:)` (file order), `shape(_:)` (kept-aside ones too) and `presentTypes`. Hold one for a task, never past it: a commit copies each table it changes, once, while one's held; and one taken by a service hearing a commit before Topology holds that commit's file with the commit before's edges (`catchUp` first). It reads every board the editor can show as it opens (a kind with no source costs a lookup), again for `replaceFile` and `replaceDocument`, and nothing on a switch. `TopologyPlugin()` is right after `SelectionPlugin()` in the shipped list, so its service hears each commit before those that read it; a service that may hear first calls `topology.catchUp(changes)` (nothing once heard). Every plugin list with Ports or Mentions needs it (and one with Queries, whose every query is `no_topology` without it). `canvas query` reads it through a snapshot, and `canvas draw` matches an edge by its ends, not its type.

## Testing

- For topology, TopologyTests use fixtures only (`GraphPlugin`: `wire`, `hot-wire` under it, `quote` and `cite` declared, `tether` not, over CanvasTesting's `Link`, `CitingTag`, `Tag`, `Memo` and, through the property rule, `CitationsProperty`; `graph(file)` opens a harness with `PocketsPlugin`, on `Nest`, pockets two deep, or `hiddenFile()`, a board the app can't show; `signalsPlugin()`, a source that reads only a disc's id, over `newerDiscsFile()`'s discs kept aside): the registry's checks, the tree and filters, emission, every way a commit comes (each held to `recordsRead`, debug builds: a move reads nothing, nor does a switch), `end(_:)`, containment, `revision(of:)` and `touched`, and `catchUp`; their Containers suite holds containers on boards as the tree's second link with CanvasTesting's corrals (`graph(file, more: [CorralsPlugin()])`, `held(_:in:)`, `Pen`: corrals nested on the root holding a pocket whose board holds a corral holding a pocket; parent, children, ancestors and file order with its keys, containers the core doesn't go by, a move reading no record, put, take, delete and undo, a test-local `Coffer` both `BoardOwning` and `ShapeContaining`, and a loop of containers never walked into); their Snapshots suite holds `snapshot()` to the index on every question over a seeded run of every kind of commit, switch and undo on pockets two deep beside a board the app can't show, corrals among them (shapes put in and taken out, containers moved with what they hold and deleted; each shape's parent held to the core's reading of its board), a snapshot taken a step before keeping its old answers, with file order and its keys, orphans and loops, a chain of 2,000 boards, `offMain` reads and no record read or observation made taking one, and CanvasTests' ShowableTests hold `file.canShow` to `editor.canShow`. PortsTests' `edgeTests` and ArrowPortsTests' `arrowEdgeTests` hold the connector's edges, MentionsTests' `edgeTests` mention edges, CompositionTests' `topologyShippedTests` the shipped graph (`.within` against `.only` on `arrow`), and BenchmarkTests `neighbors(of:)` at degree 100.
