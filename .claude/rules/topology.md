# Topology

Topology: the typed graph of edges derived from records, edge types (a child may be declared under another plugin's: `field-link` under `mention`) and sources, stand-ins and the views of edges through them, answers provided for stand-ins, the one delivery of every change to the edges and settling, containment as one tree, and snapshots for other threads.

Related: [connections](connections.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [jump to](jump-to.md), [publishing](publishing.md), [queries](queries.md), [containers](containers.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md), [types](types.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Topology](../../docs/plugin-system.md#topology).

## Architecture

- Topology (a surface on the core alone; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Topology") is what connects and contains what, whatever is where: edges, a typed graph (an arrow bound at both ends, a mention), and containment, one tree of two kinds of link (the container a shape is in on its board, a frame, else the board it's on); spatial relations (a note within another shape's bounds) are geometry, not Topology's. The carrier shape is the system of record (an arrow's bound ends and `channel` are its edge, a note's text its mentions): `TopologyIndex` (`editor.topology`, a service) derives the whole file's edges from the records, by carrier, by each end and by type, in memory, never saved (nothing in props, meta or the file: tldraw's binding records would be a second truth to keep in step, and a mention outlives what it names), and never transacts. An `Edge` is its `type` (the most specific), `source`, `target`, `carrier`, `slot` ("" for the carrier itself, else the property's name), `count` (repeats in that slot, a value, not identity) and `origin` (`EdgeOrigin`: `.emitted`, the init's default; `.forwarded(source:target:)`, naming the stand-in at each end it went through, boxed (`indirect`), so an origin is one word; `.standing`; `literalSource`, `literalTarget` and `literal` are the edge as emitted); only a connection bound at both ends is one (no loose edges), and a shape kept aside, or on a board the editor can't show, carries none. Edge types are types (`EdgeType`: `static let id: EdgeTypeID`, `parent` and `summary` nil by default: a lowercase line, no period, saying what it joins and which way, which a query's vocabulary shows, `EdgeTypeTree.summary(of:)`, a channel's through `Channel.summary`) declared by the plugin that owns the meaning (`registrar.edgeType(T.self)`, or `edgeType(_:parent:summary:)` with a parent resolved from the frozen registry; the `EdgeTypes` point, one per id, whose `check` refuses an undeclared parent and a loop), a tree (`EdgeTypeTree`: `registry.edgeTypes`, or `topology.types`, kept); shipped: `stands-for` (Topology's own `StandingEdge`, a root, declared by `TopologyPlugin`, so every vocabulary lists it first), `arrow` (Connections' `ArrowEdge`) with each channel's type (`note-link`, `subboard-link`) under it, `mention` (Mentions' `MentionEdge`, a root), and `is-a` (Types' `IsAEdge`, a root, from a shape to each type its `is` names, in the slot `is`: the property rule's first shipped use, `registrar.edges(in: IsAProperty.self, as: IsAEdge.self)` on every kind that opts into `is`, notes and arrows, so an entry that names a pill is forwarded to what it stands for and one that names a deleted type is an edge to a shape that isn't there until the delete is undone). A type nobody declares (a newer app's channel) is kept as written, with no parent. Queries filter by `EdgeTypeFilter`: `.all`, `.within(t)` (t and every type under it), `.only(t)`, each also over a sequence (an empty one admits nothing). Sources (the `EdgeSources` point; several per kind add up) say what a record carries: `x.edges { shape, registry, sink in … }`, `registrar.edges(K.self) { record, registry, sink in … }`, `edgesForEveryShape(where:)` (asked once per kind), and the property rule `registrar.edges(in: P.self, as: T.self)` (every shape a `ShapeNaming` added property's value names, slot its key, on the kinds that opt in; it doesn't declare `T`); `sink.emit(type, from:to:slot:)` counts repeats and keeps the order of first emission, and drops an edge in the slot `stands-for` or of that type (Topology's own; `reservedEdgesDropped` counts them in debug builds). A source is a pure function of one record and the registry (never other shapes, geometry, services or time, nor where a box is), so each commit reads again only what it removed, added, changed other than by moving (`ChangeSet.moved`), or touched `elsewhere`, and undo is exact; `registry.edges(of:)` is one record's. Queries cost what they return: `edges(of:_:types:)` (`.outgoing`, `.incoming`, `.either`; an edge to itself once; `hasEdges(of:_:types:where:)` stops at the first, making nothing), `neighbors(of:_:types:)` (the other ends by type: what's connected, and how), `edges(ofType:)`, `edges(carriedBy:)`, `end(_:)` (`EdgeEnd`: `.live(board)`, `.unavailable`, `.missing`), `revision(of:)` (observed per shape, counted from its first read) and `touched` (the last change's to the edges, a commit's or a provide's; `touched(types:)`, of some types). Containment, never an edge, reads boards and containers on them as one tree, with no index of its own (the boards keep who's in which container, and only containers the core goes by count, `document.container(of:containingKinds:)`, nonisolated): `parent(of:)` (the container a shape is in on its board, else its board's owner; nil on the root in no container), `children(of:)` (what a container holds, back to front, then, for an owner, its board's shapes in no container), `ancestors(of:)` (nearest first), `isInside(_:_:)`, only on boards the editor can show, one piece of code (`Containment`, a value over a `CanvasFile`, the kinds that own boards and the containing kinds) for the index and its snapshot; `containerKinds` (on `Registry`, the index and the snapshot) is which kinds contain: those that own boards and `registry.containingKinds` (a kind that's both lists what it holds before its board); a move costs it nothing, and a board without containers what it did before there were any (`CanvasDocument.namesContainers`, O(1)). `topology.snapshot()` is Topology as it is now for any thread (`TopologySnapshot`, `Sendable`, O(1), reading no record and observing nothing: it shares the index's tables, `EdgeTables`, and the file, read without observing it, and answers through the same code, so it answers as the index would have as it was taken, though not what changed: no `revision(of:)` or `touched`), with more reads of the whole file: `forEachShape(inFileOrder:)` (every shape on a board the app can show, kept-aside ones too, with its board: the root's shapes in no container back to front, each followed at once by what's inside it, what it holds then, for an owner, its board's, before the next; iterative, so a chain of any depth; an error its body throws stops it, as the body's own type, `throws(E)`), `fileOrderKey(of:)` (a key that sorts as that walk visits, each ancestor's place among what's directly inside its parent, root down; O(depth), nil off the boards the app can show), `topLevel` (the root's shapes in no container) and `isTopLevel(_:)`, `container(of:on:)` (the container a record is directly in on its board, O(1), with no lookup for one in none: a query's walk), `descendants(of:)` (file order), `shape(_:)` (kept-aside ones too) and `presentTypes`. Hold one for a task, never past it: a commit copies each table it changes, once, while one's held; and one taken by a service hearing a commit before Topology holds that commit's file with the commit before's edges (`catchUp` first). It reads every board the editor can show as it opens (a kind with no source costs a lookup), again for `replaceFile` and `replaceDocument`, and nothing on a switch. `TopologyPlugin()` is right after `SelectionPlugin()` in the shipped list, so its service hears each commit before those that read it; a service that may hear first calls `topology.catchUp(changes)` (nothing once heard). Every plugin list with Ports or Mentions needs it (and one with Queries, whose every query is `no_topology` without it). `canvas query` reads it through a snapshot, and `canvas draw` matches an edge by its ends, not its type.
- Stand-ins (`Forwarding.swift`; guide, "Stand-ins: forwarding"): a kind whose shapes stand for others says so, `x.standsIn(maxTargets: 50, admitting:) { shape, registry in Forwarding? }` (the `EdgeForwarding` point, keyed by kind; `check` refuses `maxTargets` below 1; the index caches the rules and `standInKinds` in `init`, as it does emitters, and a snapshot carries `standInKinds`): `.shapes(ids)`, named in the record, or `.provided(ForwardingKey)`, whatever is provided for the key the record holds (`.pending`, standing for nothing, in `pendingStandIns`, until an answer for that key comes). The rule and the admission are pure (records and registry, never position), so a move reads nothing. What one stands for (`effective`): the named ids in order, each once, that are on a board the editor can show, not kept aside, not itself, not of a stand-in kind (one hop) and admitted, up to `maxTargets`. Every edge a non-stand-in carries with a stand-in at an end resolves to one edge per pair of what its ends stand for (an end standing for nothing stays itself, so nothing dangles because of one), `.forwarded`, source-major, at most `EdgeForwarding.maxPairsPerCarrier` (256) per carrier with every emitted edge keeping one copy; a stand-in's own edges stay as emitted, and it carries `.standing` edges of `StandingEdge` (`stands-for`, a root, Topology's own, declared by `TopologyPlugin`) in the slot `stands-for` to each shape it stands for. Three views (`EdgeView`): `.resolved` (the default of every read), `.recorded` (forwarded only through `.shapes` stand-ins: a function of records alone, what provided answers are worked out from) and `.literal` (as emitted, no standing edges); every edge read on the index has an overload with `view:` (the old signatures stay, as `.resolved`, so protocol witnesses keep matching), and `snapshot(_ view:)` answers every edge read in its view. Reads: `forwards(of:)`, `standIns(for:)` (also on a snapshot), `forwarding(of:)` (`ForwardingState`), `edges(through:)`, `hasStandIns` (whether any shape the editor can show stands in, O(1): a follower looking for stand-ins among a change's shapes looks at none in a file with none). A kind a plugin may put down beside a shape by name (`canvas draw`'s edge to a shape on another board, [command line](command-line.md)) says how in its rule: `x.standsIn(admitting:, needs: "public", makes: { props, target, label in … })` (`ForwardingMaker`: given the props the kind's tool places, the shape to stand for and the name to show; `needs` is one word a message says of a shape that can't be stood for, read by no rule). `registry.standIns` (`StandIns`, main actor, nothing kept) reads the rules without an index: `kinds`, `isEmpty`, `forwarding(of: record)`, `makers` (the kinds whose rule says how and that a tool places, plugin order), `admits(kind, target)` (the kind's admission, never for a shape kept aside or a stand-in: one hop), `needs(kind)`, and `make(kind, standingFor:, label:)` (a new record in no document: the tool's shape at the origin, made to stand for the shape, put in step through `DerivedProps`; it never asks `admits`, whoever puts it down does). Storage is sparse, in `EdgeTables` (shared by a snapshot): `byCarrier` keeps what each carrier emitted, `resolvedByCarrier` a carrier's resolved edges only where they differ, `byNode`/`byType` the resolved edges (an edge filed under its ends and literal ends), and `forwards`/`standIns`/`forwardKind`; the other views map each forwarded edge back through its origin (copies collapse). Upkeep per commit: for each changed record, its rule if it's a stand-in (declared first, so its own edges are never forwarded), then its edges, set at once through the stand-ins as they stood; then look again at the stand-ins it changed and those whose record names a shape it changed (`declaredBy`), and for each whose targets changed set again every carrier filed under it (those just set among them) and itself. A record's kind is looked up once (its sources and rule together, `KindUpkeep`), and the stand-in tables (`standing`, `declaredBy`, `providedBy`, `resolvedByCarrier`) only while they hold something, so a file with no stand-in reads and sets a shape as it did before there were any; the upkeep reads records through no closure (each would check it runs on the main actor). `touched` and revisions note every end and literal end (a carrier set twice in a commit may add a shape reached only in between: an extra bump, never a missed one). Rebuild sets each carrier as it reads it, then runs the same stand-in pass over every stand-in there before or now, so a file with none costs what it did; it lets go of every answer provided (they were for the file as it was). `Edge` hashes its two ends alone (equality is every field), which the tables' diffs and collapses lean on, and a `ShapeID` is two words (Canvas: `UUID`'s layout is Foundation's to change, so a value holding one is copied, compared and hashed through witnesses), so an edge costs what plain words do. Draw's `share:` lets several edges go through one stand-in rather than a stand-in each, and `canvas measure` reads `make`'s size for a title without a board ([command line](command-line.md)).
- Provided answers (`Following.swift`; guide, "Provided answers, the one delivery, settling"): `topology.provide([id: ProvidedForwarding(key:shapes:revision:)])` gives `.provided` stand-ins what they stand for, never saved or undone, no commit; an answer for another key than the record's now, for a shape that isn't such a stand-in, or older (`revision`) than the one held is dropped, touching nothing; the answer's shapes go through `effective` as named ones do, and `providedBy` has a commit to one of them look at the stand-in again. A commit that changes a stand-in's key, deletes it or hides its board, and every rebuild, lets go of its answer (so a memo given again after an undo is taken whatever its revision). It runs the commit's stand-in pass seeded with the answered stand-ins, bumps revisions, sets `touched`, and tells followers (`.provide`); one that changes no edge leaves `touched` as it was. Never call it inside `documentDidChange`/`boardDidChange` or from a follower (a debug assert holds the latter): from a scheduled callback or a task's continuation.
- The one delivery: `topology.follow { change in … }` (`TopologyChange`: `touched`, `touchedByType`, `touched(types:in:)`, `cause` `.commit`/`.provide`/`.rebuild`) is called once after every change to the edges, whatever caused it, with the index as the change left it; a change that touches no edge (a move) isn't told. It lasts while its `TopologyFollowing` is held (or until `cancel()`), followers in the order they began. It's THE way to keep state off edges: reading `touched` in `documentDidChange` misses every provide. Mentions follows it (its mention-typed ends; a commit's backlinks are bumped as it hears the commit, a provide's at once), References' `ReferenceState` does (the channel mismatches its pills draw), and Connections' `ArrowRowNesting` does on a registry with a stand-in kind, looking only while the file has a stand-in (`hasStandIns`), and Neighbors' `NeighborState` (when `touched` holds the shape hovered or one the last answer matched, what's lit for it is asked again: [neighbors](neighbors.md)).
- Settling: whoever provides answers registers `keepsSettling(while: { busy })` (a `SettleSource` token) and says `settleSourceChanged()` when it goes idle with no provide or commit to say so; `isSettling` is whether any source is busy, `pendingStandIns` the `.provided` stand-ins with no answer for their key, and `settled(timeout:) async -> Bool` waits (true at once if settled; false when the timeout, on `editor.scheduler`, runs out, or the task is cancelled), woken by every delivery, every provide and every source change or cancel. `QueryState.run(settle:)` ([queries](queries.md)) is the waiter in the shipped list: before it takes up the file, so it holds no snapshot and no other query waits for it, only for a plan that reads edges, at most `QueryBudget.settleWait` (and never past the query's deadline), then a `pending` warning.
- `field-link` (Types': `FieldLinkEdge`, summary "a field's link to a note, from the note that holds it to the note it names") is declared under `mention`, a parent another plugin declares: `EdgeTypes.check` refuses only a parent no installed plugin declares and a loop, and Types imports Mentions. Its source is `edgesForEveryShape(where: isTypeable)`, pure over the record: a token (`MentionToken.read`) anywhere in `fieldValues`, in the slot of the top-level field's key (a pointer in a group is in the group's), once for each time it is held, in key order; a field called `stands-for` makes none (Topology's reserved slot, pinned by a test). Queries and lights that say `mention` take it in (descendants); `only mention`, `:mentions` and Mentioned In leave it out.

## Testing

- Provided answers, following and settling: TopologyTests' Providing suite (`ProvidingTests.swift`, over `LensesPlugin`'s `Lens` in `StandInFixtures.swift`, standing for what's provided for its `asks`, `most` and `gated` like a gate; `lensing(file)`, `answer(asks, shapes, revision)`) holds pending and answered lenses in each view (an edge between a stand-in for what's named and a lens: the recorded view reverts only the lens's end), dropped answers (another key, older, not a lens) touching nothing, a key changed by a commit and undone (the memo given again), an answer's shapes held to the rule and looked at again by a commit to one, the cap on a provide's fan-out, followers told once per change of each cause with `touched` the last change's, and settling (a source, a provide waking a wait, the scheduler's timeout, cancellation, a source cancelled); `expectAnswered` holds the index to a fresh one given the same answers, and a seeded run (`Turnover(seed:lenses: true)`, the Forwarding suite's churn with lenses and answers given between commits, undo and boards it can't show) holds it so after every step. MentionsTests hold Mentions on `follow` unchanged. BenchmarkTests' stand-in benchmarks (`StandInBenchmarks.swift`, the bench's own `Mirror` and `Lens`, so a budget holds the mechanism, not a kind) hold the index opening with stand-ins, neighbors through 100, a re-point, a drag, a held commit and a provide's fan-out ([benchmarks](benchmarks.md)).
- Making a stand-in for a shape: TopologyTests' Making suite (`MakingTests.swift`: `EchoesPlugin`'s `Echo`, which says how it's made and `needs` "ajar", admitting only a shape whose `Ajar` metadata is true, a `Mute` that says nothing, and a `Phantom` whose rule says how but that no tool places, over `StandInsPlugin`'s kinds) holds `registry.standIns`: `makers` in plugin order, `kinds`, `forwarding(of:)` on records, `admits` (never a stand-in or a shape kept aside), `needs`, and `make` (the record from the tool's shape, made to stand for the shape, fitted by `DerivedProps`, in no document, standing for nothing where it's inserted for a shape the kind doesn't admit).
- Stand-ins: TopologyTests' Forwarding suite (`StandInFixtures.swift`: `StandInsPlugin` with `Proxy`, standing for the shapes its `stands` citations name, `most` its `maxTargets`, `next` a wire it carries itself, and `Gate`, admitting only shapes whose `Open` metadata is true; `forwarded(…)` and `stands(_:for:)` build expected edges) holds the rule's checks and report line, each view of an edge into and out of a stand-in, what can't be stood for (missing, kept aside, unavailable, itself, a stand-in, not admitted), duplicates and `maxTargets`, one hop, the loop, the 256-pair cap with each emitted edge keeping one, the reserved slot, re-pointing and deleting with `touched` and revisions, `recordsRead` on a move, a chain of 2,000 boards, sparse storage (`resolvedCarrierCount`, debug builds), snapshots in each view off the main actor, and a seeded run (`Turnover`) holding the index to a fresh one in every view, the literal view to what `registry.edges(of:)` emits, the recorded view to the resolved one, and the resolved edges to the rule worked out in the test from the records alone.
