# Titles

Titles: named and derived titles, the pill and the looks other plugins give it (a type's, one a field hides), the cover zoomed out, `TitleHeader`, `/title`, and the warning about titles two shapes share.

Related: [adornments](adornments.md), [validation](validation.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [jump to](jump-to.md), [queries](queries.md), [command line](command-line.md), [types](types.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [A horizontal plugin](../../docs/plugin-system.md#a-horizontal-plugin), [Warnings: titles two shapes share](../../docs/plugin-system.md#warnings-titles-two-shapes-share), [Renaming in the header: TitleHeader](../../docs/plugin-system.md#renaming-in-the-header-titleheader).

## Architecture

- Titles (a concern) is what a shape is called, a trait a kind opts into. A title someone names is Titles' to keep: `x.title()` opts the kind into `TitleProperty` (key `title`, nil until named), so the kind's schema has it with its bounds, 1 to 80 characters (`TitleProperty.maxLength`), and a longer title is kept and warned about (Validation's schema rule). No kind keeps a title of its own: notes handed theirs over. A blank title is none (`TitleProperty.isNone`), so no shape holds one: naming a shape "  " leaves it untitled, and a board's empty title reads as none. A title can instead be derived from what the kind keeps (`x.title { ... }`), and isn't in its schema; a kind may be both, named and derived for the shapes nobody named (TitlesTests' discs; no shipped kind is), which `registry.titleIsDerived(kind)` tells from only named; every kind a tool places can be named (a type's name is its note's title): a kind opts in with `x.title()` (another plugin's kind with `registrar.title(K.self)`) and puts `TitleHeader` in its detail header (`x.details(header: …)`, or for another plugin's kind `registrar.details(K.self, header: Self.header) {}`, a function, since a closure needs SwiftUI's `ViewBuilder`), so a person renames or clears a name a script gave. Any shape may have none, and whatever shows or matches a title folds it as `TitleProperty.folded` does (one line; nil for what `isNone` calls none; an ASCII title that reads as written already, checked a byte at a time, comes back as it is, so a query reading every title splits none). A titled shape shows a pill just outside its top-left corner (`TitlePill`, an adornment Titles places on every titled shape: the row above the top edge, from its left end, as long as the title and no longer than the row leaves it, less what's at its right end, a lock; after the kind's symbol, `registry.label(of:)`, small, where the kind asks, `symbolInPill: true`, as a subboard does, whose look alone doesn't say what it is; none below 35% on a shape drawn as a skeleton (no adornment is shown there: the title is said on the shape, as a cover, below), and in full on the shape being edited; `pill: false` for a kind that draws its own: no pill, and no cover); an untitled shape shows none, unless its kind gives it one to grab (`x.title(placeholderInPill: "Frame")`, on every form of the sugar): the placeholder in the title's place, laid out as a title is, after the symbol where the kind asks, its text dimmed (`AdornmentLabel(placeholder:)`, Adornments', white at 55%, secondary text on a dark surface), none zoomed out as any pill (nor a cover: a placeholder is for grabbing a shape by), and its shape's for the select tool, hover and the context menu as any adornment (`DecorationHits`), but never a title (`registry.title(of:)` stays nil, the row stays the kind's; a blank placeholder is none); and its title names its row in the table of contents (`contents: false` keeps the kind's row). Zoomed out, where shapes are skeletons and show no adornments, a titled shape says its title on itself, large: a cover (`TitleCoverStyle`, drawn by Titles' `TitleCoverOverlay` over every shape drawn as a skeleton, back to front, so a frame's can't be hidden by what it holds; `editor.titleCover(of:)` says where one is, in world units, what it says and how large). It's plain text on the shape, no plate: in a dark ink on a light surface and a light one on a dark one, by the surface the kind says its skeleton is drawn on (`x.title(coverSurface:)`, `registrar.title(K.self, coverSurface:)`: a note's yellow, a frame's and a subboard's colors, which follow the appearance; the default skeleton's gray without one), with a halo of that surface around the glyphs, so it stays clear over what lies under it (the notes a frame holds, a note's greeked lines); centered on the shape, or at the top left of a container (`registry.isContainer`), where its pill goes. Its size follows the shape's, not the title's, so equal shapes have equal titles (0.22 of the shorter side, 9 to 28 view points, to half points), made smaller only as far as the title needs: where it wouldn't fill the room (the room the shape less a 4-point inset, which gives way where the shape is short, so a shape 15 points tall still has one), where a line would be taller than it, and until the widest word, as it's measured, fits a line, so a word is never broken (a word over 16 columns, or text with no spaces, breaks where the line does; a word that doesn't fit at 9 points sets the title on one line, truncated); wrapped on whole words, its last line truncated by AppKit with an "…" where the title goes on past the room (the string cut to 120 characters first, with an "…" of its own, so a title of thousands costs what one of a hundred does). A shape under 40 × 15 view points on screen, an untitled one, a kind with `pill: false` and the shape being edited get none, and it goes where its shape is drawn (a shake). A shape is passed over by its kept bounds alone before its title is looked up, so a frame of thousands zoomed out costs a look at each shape in view; only the shapes big enough lay text out. It's no face and no decoration for the pointer (a frame zoomed out is pressed on its border, as always). A plugin that is not the kind's gives the pills of some shapes a look (`TitlePillLooks`, Titles' extension point, any number of contributions, unkeyed): `registrar.titlePillLook(forShapesHolding: P.self) { shape, title in TitlePillLook(...) }` is asked of the shapes of the kinds that hold `P` and of no other kind, `x.titlePillLook { … }` of a kind's own, and the fields of what they answer merge in plugin order, a kind's own first (the first non-nil of `symbol`, `tint`, `placeholder`, `coverSurface`; `hidesPill` if any): the symbol is first in the pill (in place of the kind's), the tint is Adornments' (`tinted(_:)`), the placeholder is what an untitled shape's pill says, dimmed, where the kind gave none (the pill then shows), the cover surface is what the cover's ink is chosen for, and `hidesPill` removes the pill (the cover stays). It is asked where the pill's label is made (a commit that names the shape; the label is kept, `registry.titlePill(of:in:)`), where Find reads the title (`TitleFindPlugin`) and for a titled skeleton's cover in view (`registry.titleCover(of:)`), after the shape is found big enough to say its title, never per frame otherwise. A look may take a third argument, `TitleLookContext`, which gives the editor's services (`context.service(S.self)`) where an editor is asking (the pill's label, with the adornment's context; Find's texts, with its editor) and none for a cover, which a frame asks for every titled skeleton in view: a look that needs a service answers nil there, and the cover wants only its `coverSurface` (so a cover stays for a pill a look hides). The two-argument forms are the same look that ignores the context; the report shows `title pill look → every shape` or `→ note`, and a list with a look needs `TitlesPlugin()`. TitlesTests' `pillLookTests` hold it, the context included. `TitleHeader($x)` renames the title Titles keeps in the detail panel (`TitleHeader($card.text)` renames a derived one at its source), in the panel's `DetailHeaderField`, which checks the title's spelling as the app does ([detail panel](detail-panel.md)); a kind places it in its own header (`note.details(header: { $note in TitleHeader($note) }) {}`), since Titles contributes no header. `/title` is a slash command every kind that holds `TitleProperty` offers (`registrar.slashCommandsForEveryShape(where: { $0.holds(TitleProperty.self) })`, in `TitlesPlugin` after its pill, offered always, so on a titled shape it renames, running an `edit` over the record that sets what the arguments read as text, a pill in the line "@Label"): typed on a line of its own in a shape's text (a note's), with Return, it takes the line out and names the shape, one step named "Title" after the typing that has the line's removal in it, and editing goes on; with nothing after its name it isn't entered, and Return is a new line. So it's on notes, subboards and frames. Titles depends on SlashCommands for it, so a plugin list with Titles needs `SlashCommandsPlugin()` (the registry refuses a contribution whose owner isn't installed), which the shipped list has, and a shape's list shows `title` before `lock` (every shape's commands are offered in plugin order, Titles before Locking); the report shows it as `/title → every shape`. Nothing refuses two shapes one title, but titles name shapes (`canvas draw` refuses a title two shapes have), so `TitleValidationPlugin` (Titles', on Validation, after `MentionValidationPlugin` in the shipped list) warns on each shape whose title (`registry.title(of:)`, named or derived; an untitled note's first line is none) another shape in the file has, compared exactly as folded, on every board the editor can show but shapes kept aside: “Checkout” is another shape’s title too., `field: "title"` where Titles keeps it. Its `TitleIndex` (a service) is the file's titles, read as the editor opens (as Mentions' catalog is, so no check pays for it, a query's slice), kept up to date by each commit wherever it changed a title (`elsewhere` too; never a move), and names, through the rule's `affected`, the shapes a commit gives a title's first other holder or takes its last from, so the rule ignores moves and reads no other shape (TitlesTests' `TitleValidationTests.swift`, fixtures only; `TitleValidationPlugin.titlesChecked`, debug builds). `TitleFindPlugin` (Titles', after Find and Titles in the shipped list: [find](find.md)) gives Find each title (`registry.title(of:)`, field `title`) on every kind that holds `TitleProperty` or derives one; a hit rings the pill's whole capsule (it may cut the title short), its frame Adornments' (`adornmentFrames(of:)`, `followsZoom`: in view points), and zoomed out it's tinted where the cover is (`titleCover(of:)`); a shape whose pill a look hides (`hidesPill`, asked with the editor's context) gives no title text, since there is no pill to ring and the look's plugin says the name elsewhere, and its text is back as soon as a commit on the shape makes the look stop hiding; TitlesTests' `titleFindTests` hold it. A kind's title contribution and the pill-look contributions that apply to it (its own, and a concern's whose property it holds) are found once per kind (`registry.memo`), not per shape: a cover asks for every titled skeleton in view on every frame. `TitleQueriesPlugin` gives queries a shape's name, `"Checkout"` and the `title` attribute, named or derived ([queries](queries.md)).
- `TitleNamesPlugin` (`TitleNames.swift`, Titles' own, filling Draw's `DrawNames`) gives `canvas draw` what a shape is called, the property a title is written to, whether a kind derives its names, and how a written title is folded ([command line](command-line.md)); a list without Draw leaves it out.
