# Titles

Titles: named and derived titles, the pill and the looks other plugins give it (a type's, one a field hides), the tab a kind that holds shapes takes in its place, the cover zoomed out (or a bar where the pill is, for a kind that asks), a title typed in its pill (`TitleField`), `TitleHeader`, `/title`. The warning about two shapes called one thing is Boards' now, of a path two shapes share ([boards](boards.md#a-shapes-path-and-the-warning-of-one-two-shapes-share)).

Related: [adornments](adornments.md), [validation](validation.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [jump to](jump-to.md), [queries](queries.md), [command line](command-line.md), [types](types.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [A horizontal plugin](../../docs/plugin-system.md#a-horizontal-plugin), [Renaming in the header: TitleHeader](../../docs/plugin-system.md#renaming-in-the-header-titleheader).

## Architecture

- Titles (platform, a horizontal and a shape trait) is what a shape is called, a trait a kind opts into. A title someone names is Titles' to keep: `x.title()` opts the kind into `TitleProperty` (key `title`, nil until named), so the kind's schema has it with its bounds, 1 to 80 characters (`TitleProperty.maxLength`), and a longer title is kept and warned about (Validation's schema rule). No kind keeps a title of its own: notes handed theirs over. A blank title is none (`TitleProperty.isNone`), so no shape holds one: naming a shape "  " leaves it untitled, and a board's empty title reads as none. A title can instead be derived from what the kind keeps (`x.title { ... }`), and isn't in its schema; a kind may be both, named and derived for the shapes nobody named (TitlesTests' discs; no shipped kind is), which `registry.titleIsDerived(kind)` tells from only named; every kind a tool places can be named (a type's name is its note's title): a kind opts in with `x.title()` (another plugin's kind with `registrar.title(K.self)`) and puts `TitleHeader` in its detail header (`x.details(header: …)`, or for another plugin's kind `registrar.details(K.self, header: Self.header) {}`, a function, since a closure needs SwiftUI's `ViewBuilder`), so a person renames or clears a name a script gave. Any shape may have none, and whatever shows or matches a title folds it as `TitleProperty.folded` does (one line; nil for what `isNone` calls none; an ASCII title that reads as written already, checked a byte at a time, comes back as it is, so a query reading every title splits none). A titled shape shows a pill just outside its top-left corner (`TitlePill`, an adornment Titles places on the kinds whose title shows one, `forShapes(adopting: .titlePill)`, Titles' capability: the row above the top edge, from its left end, as long as the title and no longer than the row leaves it, less what's at its right end, a lock; after the kind's symbol, `registry.label(of:)`, small, where the kind asks, `symbolInPill: true`, as a subboard does, whose look alone doesn't say what it is; none below 35% on a shape drawn as a skeleton (no adornment is shown there: the title is said on the shape, as a cover, below), and in full on the shape being edited; `pill: false` for a kind that draws its own: no pill, and no cover); an untitled shape shows none, unless its kind gives it one to grab (`x.title(placeholderInPill: "Frame")`, on every form of the sugar): the placeholder in the title's place, laid out as a title is, after the symbol where the kind asks, its text dimmed (`AdornmentLabel(placeholder:)`, Adornments', white at 55%, secondary text on a dark surface), none zoomed out as any pill (nor a cover: a placeholder is for grabbing a shape by), and its shape's for the select tool, hover and the context menu as any adornment (`DecorationHits`), but never a title (`registry.title(of:)` stays nil, the row stays the kind's; a blank placeholder is none); and its title names its row in the table of contents (`contents: false` keeps the kind's row). Zoomed out, where shapes are skeletons and show no adornments, a titled shape says its title on itself, large: a cover (`TitleCoverStyle`, drawn by Titles' `TitleCoverOverlay` over every shape drawn as a skeleton, back to front, so a container's can't be hidden by what it holds, and after them over the shapes waiting to be placed (`editor.placeLater`: `OverlayContext.pendingShapes`, laid out in `pendingContext`), faint, inside `drawingPending`; `editor.titleCover(of:)` says where one is, in world units, what it says and how large). It's plain text on the shape, no plate: in a dark ink on a light surface and a light one on a dark one, by the surface the kind says its skeleton is drawn on (`x.title(coverSurface:)`, `registrar.title(K.self, coverSurface:)`: a note's yellow (every color's paper is about as light as it, so its cover's ink reads on any), a subboard's colors, which follow the appearance; the default skeleton's gray without one; and where the surface is each shape's own and not the kind's, the kind says it shape by shape, `x.titleCoverSurface { shape, context in … }` (the `TitleCoverSurfaces` point, one a kind, asked for every titled skeleton of the kind in view with the editor's services, so it answers from what a service keeps: a note's paper, a colored subboard's surface, [colors](colors.md)'s `book.color(\.paper, of: shape)`, the color it's drawn in (its own, else its type's, else its kind's default); a look's own `coverSurface` still wins, so a type's note is white whatever its color), with a halo of that surface around the glyphs, so it stays clear over what lies under it (the notes a container holds, a note's greeked lines); centered on the shape, or at the top left of a container (`registry.isContainer`), where its pill goes. Its size follows the shape's, not the title's, so equal shapes have equal titles (0.22 of the shorter side, 9 to 28 view points, to half points), made smaller only as far as the title needs: where it wouldn't fill the room (the room the shape less a 4-point inset, which gives way where the shape is short, so a shape 15 points tall still has one), where a line would be taller than it, and until the widest word, as it's measured, fits a line, so a word is never broken (a word over 16 columns, or text with no spaces, breaks where the line does; a word that doesn't fit at 9 points sets the title on one line, truncated); wrapped on whole words, its last line truncated by AppKit with an "…" where the title goes on past the room (the string cut to 120 characters first, with an "…" of its own, so a title of thousands costs what one of a hundred does). A shape under 40 × 15 view points on screen, an untitled one, a kind with `pill: false` or a tab (below), and the shape being edited get none, and it goes where its shape is drawn (a shake). **A tab, for a kind that holds shapes**: `x.titleTab { shape, context in TitleTabLook(surface:edge:) }` (or `registrar.titleTab(K.self) { … }`; the `TitleTabs` point, one a kind, the report's `title tab → frame`) puts the title in `TitleTab`, Adornments' `onTopEdge` ([adornments](adornments.md), **The tab on the top edge**), in place of the pill (`.titlePill` leaves the kind out, `.titleTab` takes it: titled, not `pill: false`, and a tab given) and of the cover (`titleCover` is nil for it): on the top edge, the same size on screen at every zoom and shown zoomed out too, giving way to other shapes' tabs (titles before placeholders, then the outermost's first), with the row of chips above the edge lifted over it ([adornments](adornments.md)), turned down the left edge on a column; its label is the pill's (`titleLabel(of:in:)`: title or placeholder, symbol, a look's tint) tinted in the shape's colors (`TitleTabStyle.tint`: solid in the surface, its `edge` the line inside, the ink the cover's dark or light as the surface resolves in the appearance drawn, a dynamic color made off the main actor, `inkProvider`, since AppKit may resolve it on any thread; tints and inks kept by their colors, so the same colors make the same tint), unless a look gives a tint, which wins; asked where the label is made (a commit naming the shape) with the editor's services, so a colored frame answers from Colors' book. Its placeholder shows in full and not zoomed out, and it's its shape's for the pointer at every zoom. The cover grew with its shape as the board shrank, so a frame's was the biggest text far out and printed over its frames' own: the owner's call (2026-10-22), from wireframes, was a tab on the edge. Frames is the one shipped kind with one. TitlesTests' `pendingCoverTests` hold a plaque waiting to be placed: zoomed out, its title on it where an untitled one's has none, fainter than a placed one's, gone with Escape; in full, its pill above it. TitlesTests' `titleTabTests` hold it on CanvasTesting's corrals (a tab and no pill at 100%, 30% and 5%, the pill's rect its tab's, no cover, the report; the colors and the ink on a light and a dark surface, the same tint again; the placeholder in full and none zoomed out; a look's tint winning). A shape is passed over by its kept bounds alone before its title is looked up, so a frame of thousands zoomed out costs a look at each shape in view; only the shapes big enough lay text out. It's no face and no decoration for the pointer (a frame zoomed out is pressed on its border, as always). A plugin that is not the kind's gives the pills of some shapes a look (`TitlePillLooks`, Titles' extension point, any number of contributions, unkeyed): `registrar.forShapes(holding: P.self) { $0.titlePillLook { shape, title in TitlePillLook(...) } }` is asked of the shapes of the kinds that hold `P` and of no other kind (`TitlePillLooks` takes scopes: [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md)), `x.titlePillLook { … }` of a kind's own, and the fields of what they answer merge in plugin order, a kind's own first (the first non-nil of `symbol`, `tint`, `placeholder`, `coverSurface`; `hidesPill` if any): the symbol is first in the pill (in place of the kind's), the tint is Adornments' (`tinted(_:)`), the placeholder is what an untitled shape's pill says, dimmed, where the kind gave none (the pill then shows), the cover surface is what the cover's ink is chosen for, and `hidesPill` removes the pill (the cover stays). It is asked where the pill's label is made (a commit that names the shape; the label is kept, `registry.titlePill(of:in:)`), where Find reads the title (`TitleFindPlugin`) and for a titled skeleton's cover in view (`registry.titleCover(of:)`), after the shape is found big enough to say its title, never per frame otherwise. A look may take a third argument, `TitleLookContext`, which gives the editor's services (`context.service(S.self)`) where an editor is asking (the pill's label, with the adornment's context; Find's texts, with its editor) and none for a cover, which a frame asks for every titled skeleton in view: a look that needs a service answers nil there, and the cover wants only its `coverSurface` (so a cover stays for a pill a look hides). The two-argument forms are the same look that ignores the context; the report shows `title pill look → every shape holding schema` or `→ note`, and a list with a look needs `TitlesPlugin()`. TitlesTests' `pillLookTests` hold it, the context included. `TitleHeader($x)` renames the title Titles keeps in the detail panel (`TitleHeader($card.text)` renames a derived one at its source), in the panel's `DetailHeaderField`, which checks the title's spelling as the app does ([detail panel](detail-panel.md)); a kind places it in its own header (`note.details(header: { $note in TitleHeader($note) }) {}`), since Titles contributes no header. `/title` is a slash command every kind that holds `TitleProperty` offers (`registrar.forShapes(holding: TitleProperty.self) { $0.slashCommands { … } }`, in `TitlesPlugin` after its pill, offered always, so on a titled shape it renames, running an `edit` over the record that sets what the arguments read as text, a pill in the line "@Label"): typed on a line of its own in a shape's text (a note's), with Return, it takes the line out and names the shape, one step named "Title" after the typing that has the line's removal in it, and editing goes on; with nothing after its name it isn't entered, and Return is a new line. So it's on notes, subboards and frames. It is one shape's (`reach: .one`, SlashCommands' word for how a command acts on several shapes): a title is how a shape is told apart, and several shapes given one title would be several paths alike, which Boards warns of, so for the shapes selected it is listed for a selection of one shape and not for two or more, whatever they are. TitlesTests' `titleSlashTests` hold it in a shape's text and for a selection (listed for one titled shape, not for two, and run for one). Titles depends on SlashCommands for it, so a plugin list with Titles needs `SlashCommandsPlugin()` (the registry refuses a contribution whose owner isn't installed), which the shipped list has, and a shape's list shows `title` before `lock` (the scopes' commands are offered in plugin order, Titles before Locking); the report shows it as `/title → every shape holding title`. Nothing refuses two shapes one title, and Titles warns of none: a shape is told apart by its path (its containers' names, then its title), and the warning of a path two shapes share, which for a shape in no container is its title, is Boards' (`PathValidationPlugin` and its `PathIndex`, which took the place of Titles' `TitleValidationPlugin` and `TitleIndex`: [boards](boards.md#a-shapes-path-and-the-warning-of-one-two-shapes-share)); Titles imports no Validation. `TitleFindPlugin` (Titles', after Find and Titles in the shipped list: [find](find.md)) gives Find each title (`registry.title(of:)`, field `title`) on every kind that took a title (`.titled`: one that holds `TitleProperty` or derives one); a hit rings the pill's whole capsule (it may cut the title short), its frame Adornments' (`adornmentFrames(of:)`, `followsZoom`: in view points), and zoomed out it's tinted where the cover is (`titleCover(of:)`), else where the tab is (`titlePillRect(of:)`); a shape whose pill a look hides (`hidesPill`, asked with the editor's context) gives no title text, since there is no pill to ring and the look's plugin says the name elsewhere, and its text is back as soon as a commit on the shape makes the look stop hiding; TitlesTests' `titleFindTests` hold it. A kind's title contribution and the pill-look contributions that apply to it (its own, and a horizontal's whose property it holds) are found once per kind (`registry.memo`), not per shape: a cover asks for every titled skeleton in view on every frame. `TitleQueriesPlugin` gives queries a shape's name, `"Checkout"` and the `title` attribute, named or derived ([queries](queries.md)).
- `TitleNamesPlugin` (`TitleNames.swift`, Titles' own, filling Draw's `DrawNames`) gives `canvas draw` what a shape is called, the property a title is written to, whether a kind derives its names, and how a written title is folded ([command line](command-line.md)); a list without Draw leaves it out.
- `editor.titlePillRect(of:)` (Titles.swift, beside `TitlePill`) is the rect of a shape's pill, or its tab, in world units, at the zoom now, where the shape is (not where a shake draws it): `adornmentFrames(of:)` filtered to the `TitlePill` or `TitleTab` adornment, nil where the shape shows none (a plain untitled one, a look that hides the pill, a shape drawn as a skeleton with no tab, where its title is its cover, a pill left out for want of room, one not on the board). Find's ring is the one reader of the frame, now through it; a card that hangs from a shape's title pill hangs from it, and Types cannot name `TitlePill`'s id without importing Adornments, so the wrapper is Titles'. TitlesTests' `pillRectTests` hold it.
- **A title typed in its pill** (`TitleField.swift`): `TitleField` is the line a shape's name is typed in, laid over its pill, for a kind whose shapes are named as they land (the engineering kit's table and enum: "the caret in its name", T1): Adornments' chip with no text (`AdornmentLabel.drawChip(width:at:scale:in:)`, its capsule and symbol, in the kind's tint, as wide as what's typed needs, `chipWidth`) and over it one line of text (`EditingTextView`, nothing substituted, no undo of its own) in the pill's font and ink at the pill's size in world units (`AdornmentLabel.rowFont`, `textStart`, `textInk`), a focus ring round the chip; presses only on the chip. Each change is the title, a blank one none, in the editing session's step (`EditContext.transact`): the placing step where the shape was put down by an editing placement ("Add Table"), else the kind's `editingStepName` ("Rename Table"); Return, Escape, Tab and ⇧Tab end it (`Ending`, read through `KeyMeaning`), and ↓ is a host's where it says (`onDown`: a table's first row). Its text has no undo of its own, so Edit › Undo is the board's while it has the keyboard (`UndoesBoardWhenEmpty`, [undo](undo.md)). A kind hosts it in its `Editable` conformance: `editingFrame` is `TitleField.editingFrame(around:zoom:)` (its laid-out frame and the pill's row above it, `pillRow(above:zoom:)`: from its left end, as tall as the pill at the zoom, `AdornmentLayout.scale(atZoom:)`, 200 points past its right edge), and `makeEditingView` is `TitleField.editingView(for:context:systemImage:tint:name:)`, a `TitleFieldHost` (the field on the row at its top, sized from its own frame and bounds; nothing else of it takes a press), or, for a kind with rows written as lines (the kit's table and enum), LineRows' editing view, whose first stop the field is ([line rows](line-rows.md)). The pill under it is drawn as ever; the chip over it is the same chip, so nothing moves as typing starts. TitlesTests' `titleFieldTests` hold it on a sign of the test's own (the editing frame and the field on the pill's row at 100%, 200% and 50%, the font the pill's in world units, presses only on the chip; each change the title in the session's step, Return, Escape and Tab, a blank title none, the chip growing; put down by its tool, named in "Add Sign").
- **Titles as bars zoomed out** (`title(bars: true)`, `TitleBar`, `TitleBars`): for a kind drawn zoomed out as a card of bars (the engineering kit's), its title is a bar where its pill is, as long as its title's columns read at the pill's 11 points (a column 0.52 em, and the pill's padding and symbol), no wider than the shape, 40% of the pill's height, in the pill's gray (lighter on the dark canvas) or a look's tint (an enum's purple), with no cover (`titleCover` is nil for such a kind); the overlay gathers every skeleton's bar in view and fills them a color at a time. E8's 30%: the cover printed a name over the rows. `titleFieldTests` holds it (a bar where the pill is, thinner than it, as long as the title, no cover, a look's tint, none untitled).
- VoiceOver says a titled shape's title first, before its kind ("Checkout, Note"): Titles contributes it as the name of every kind with a title (`forShapes(adopting: .titled) { $0.accessibility { … AccessibleTraits(name:) } }`, [accessibility](accessibility.md)); an untitled shape is its kind.
