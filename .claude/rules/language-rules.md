# Language rules

Rules in English on types: where a type keeps them (`$rules`, a directive Types holds aside), the shapes that are checked (`ignoredRules`, the property a kind opts into), what a rule sees of a shape (`RuleContext`), the words it is asked in (`Shipped/rule-prompt.txt`), the checks (`RuleChecks`) and their warnings, the one writer of rules and ignores (`RuleWriter`), the panel (a type's Rules tab, a checked shape's Rules section in the Issues tab, the light) and the menus that do what its buttons do, and the prototype's evaluations.

Related: [decision models](decision-models.md), [types](types.md), [validation](validation.md), [queries](queries.md), [topology](topology.md), [command line](command-line.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [keyboard navigation](keyboard-navigation.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Language rules](../../docs/plugin-system.md#language-rules). The design, with its frames: the doc "Language rules" (its Plan tab).

## Architecture

- LanguageRules (platform, a shape trait: on Adornments (its chip), Canvas, CanvasRPC, ContextMenus, DecisionModels, DetailPanel, Mentions, Palette, Queries, QueryLanguage, QueryTranslation, Selection, TextEditing, Titles, Topology, Types and Validation) knows no kind. `LanguageRulesPlugin` declares `ignoredRules` (`IgnoredRulesProperty`, `[String]`, the sentences a shape ignores, `unlisted()`: no language lists it; `Fixtures/added/ignoredRules.v0.json`), holds `$rules` in a type's schema (`registrar.typeDirective("rules")`, [types](types.md)), makes the checks (`RuleChecks`, `editor.ruleChecks`) and the writer (`RuleWriter`, `editor.ruleWriter`); `RuleValidationPlugin`, a plugin value of its own after it, is their warnings (a Validation rule on every shape holding `ignoredRules`), so a list without Validation leaves them out; `RuleDetailsPlugin` (id `rule-details`), another after the panel and the menus, is the panel, the light and the menus (below), so a list without them leaves those out. A kind opts in with `checkedByRules()`: StickyNotes' note and Arrows' arrow call it, so every list with either lists `LanguageRulesPlugin()` (the registry refuses an opt-in to an undeclared property).
- **Where a rule is kept.** `"$rules": {"const": [{"says": "…", "find": [{"phrase": "…", "query": "…"}]}]}` in the root's `properties` of a type's schema, beside `$color`. Types holds it aside as it holds `$color` and `$autofill`: the sync is never sent it, a schema written whole (draw's `schema:`, the model's answer, words that are JSON) keeps it unless it says it, and a change to it alone is no change to the type's fields (`HeldDirectives`, `registry.heldDirectives`). `RuleBook.rules(inSchema:)` reads a type's rules (a rule with no words, and anything that isn't a list of them, none; a chip with no phrase none, one with no query, or an empty one, words not interpreted yet: `RuleChip.query` nil), `member(_:)` writes them (a chip's query only when it has one), `rules(of:in:)` a type's by id, `chain(for:in:)` a shape's: every rule of every type along `TypeIndex.lineage(of:)` (C3, a later type in its list the more specific) reversed, so the most general type's first, each type's in the order written (a type is checked by none), and `noun(for:in:)` the most general type's name as a noun (`noun(of:)`: its first letter lower-case unless the name reads as an acronym, "PR", or is written so, "iPhone").
- **The words** (`RulePrompts`, from `Shipped/rule-prompt.txt`, the product's, in sections after `## <name>` lines: `preface`, `rule`, `together` and `each`, each question's text then `true:`, `false:` and `flags when:`): `request(state:rules:noun:)` is the preface naming the noun and the rules' lines, most general first, each `(on <type>)`, then the question for the rules read together (`all`: "Read together, does this car meet them?", "It meets them" or "It breaks one", a no flags) and one for each rule (`r0`, `r1`…: "Read with the others, does this car fail rule 1?", a yes flags); `flagging(_:yes:)` turns an answer into how likely it flags. The words are the prototype's composed, "should hold" wording word for word (round 1 found rules that qualify others need both). `LanguageRulesPlugin()` reads them through the core's `ShippedText`: an executable that isn't the product has none, and asks nothing (a test of notes lists the plugin for the property); a test that checks passes its own (`init(prompts:)`).
- **Chips** (`RuleChips`, `editor.ruleChips`, a service of `LanguageRulesPlugin`'s): a chip is a phrase of a rule's sentence with a stored query bound to the shape checked (`:scope`, `:scope-start`, `:scope-end`, Queries' words: [queries](queries.md)); where it is in the sentence is found from the text (`Rule.chipRanges`: each phrase after the one before, else from the start, as words of its own, `Rule.range(of:in:from:)`, never inside a longer word; a chip whose phrase isn't there has none), `Rule.saying(_:)` keeps the chips whose phrase is still in new words, and `Rule.bracketed` is the sentence with each phrase in brackets, as a request says it. A chip is made in a rule's box with `@` (below): a shape picked is a chip of its name whose query is its title in quotes where no other shape has that title, else its id (`#…`), and nothing is asked; words taken that `checkStored(_:binding: .scope)` reads are that query, canonical, no model asked; any other words go to the model at once through the lifecycle every host of words shares (`editor.interpretations`, [language models](language-models.md)), `RuleChips` its host: the words keyed `InterpretationKey(shape: type, slot: "rule chip: " + phrase)`, asked as `.stored(binding:sentence:)` (`StoredBinding`: the type's name, "notes" or "arrows", arrows for a relationship type, whose shapes have ends; the sentence `bracketed`), `isEditing` while a box of the type holds a draft with those words (so an answer is a draft), `land` writing the query into the stored chip that still has those words and no query, one step, "Interpret" (`RuleWriter.setQuery(_:ofChip:in:of:step:)`), a commit that takes the chip away letting the words go. While a box is typed into its rule is a draft (`rule(in:stored:)`, `typed(_:in:stored:)`), and `commit(_:)` writes it as the type's rule (emptied, it goes), its chips' answers that came while it was typed with it, in the same step, "Change Rules", and what's still asked lands later. What a chip finds for the line under its rule (`preview(of:type:)`, a `QueryPreview.Content`): its query (the draft's while typed), the words' state (`InterpretationStatus`), and, for a query that uses the scope words, the sample, the type's shapes in file order one at a time (`step(_:by:)`, round the ends; an arrow named by its ends, "Lloyd Smucker → Limit, Save, Grow Act"), then what it found for it by name (one request, `QueryMatchRequest(query, scope: sample)`, kept until a commit that writes anything), each container followed by what it holds ("13 inside"), at most `RulesStyle.foundRows` (4) rows and how many more; `write(_:forChip:of:type:)` writes a query typed by hand if the engine reads it bound, else gives back its refusal; `editWords` puts the keyboard in the rule's box on the phrase.
- **What a rule sees** (`RuleContext.build(for:in:finds:)`, off the main actor, over a `RuleWorld` one turn takes: Topology's snapshot through every stand-in (`.resolved`), the types' `TypeGraph`, Mentions' labels (words read as plain text with live names), each kind's reading (`RuleKindReading`: its label lower-case as the noun, "type" for a shape that holds a schema, and its mention-text property, by the kind's schema's `contentMediaType`) and the day): the prototype's state under its labels (`today`; `shape`, in full: kind, title, `is`, words, values; `its types`, most specific first, each with its words and what it builds on; `in`, the board's top level or the container and its path; for an arrow `starts at`, `ends at` in full and `beside it at its start (other <types> arrows at <title>)` and at its end; else `along its edges`, each arrow's far end by name and its values; those arrows by their far end's title and then their id, never the order Topology filed them in (a commit's in any order, an edited arrow last, a file opened again in its boards' order: `Reader.ordered`), so a shape's state is the same in every session and a file opened again asks nothing it asked today; `beside it, in the same place`, the container's other shapes but lines (a kind drawn as one, `StrokedShape`: `RuleKindReading.isLine`), by name; and for each chip with a query, `found by "<phrase>"`, what it found with the shape bound, in full, each container followed by what it holds, past the budget `{"and": "N more"}`, the prototype's form, and a Shown group of the same label: a chip refused, or with no query, says nothing), a group past `RuleContext.budget` (40) counted by type. Its footprint is every shape it read, and its place the container whose shapes it lists.
- **The checks** (`RuleChecks`, an `EditorService`): a shape is checked when its kind holds `ignoredRules`, it is of a type, it is no type, and its chain has rules. Every commit is heard once services have it (`documentDidSettle`): a shape a commit wrote (`added`, `removed`, `changed` but `moved`, `elsewhere`), the shapes whose states read one (the footprint index: every shape a state names, the types it names and the shapes its words mention among them, so a rename asks again), a type's instances when it changed (its rules, its words, what it builds on: `TypeIndex.allInstances(of:)`), a shape moved or written into or out of a container and the shapes that listed it or now would beside them (each check's place, the container it lists, kept as `readPlaces` until its answer lands), those beside a shape put down, and those whose edges changed (`topology.follow`; a stand-in's answer, which comes with no commit, the next turn) are marked; a commit that only moved shapes where they were marks none. The day turning over (`NSCalendarDayChanged`, a state says the day) marks every shape. What a chip finds may change with any write, however far away, so a commit that wrote anything (not one that only moved shapes) while a type's rules have a chip with a query (`typesWithChips`) has the next pass ask the chips of every other checked shape whose rules have one in the same batch, and check again only those whose finds changed (`chipFinds`, what each shape's last state was built with), so an unrelated write asks the model nothing; a change to the edges with no commit (a stand-in's answer) does the same. Two rules' chips of one phrase are both asked, their finds one. The file is quiet `RuleChecks.quiet` (3 s) after the last mark, or editing ends (`editingDidEnd`, the next turn), or an agent wrote the commit (`ChangeSet.byAgent`, `canvas draw`'s: the next turn, as it may ask straight after and no one is typing), and a pass asks every chip of the marked shapes in one batch (`queries.matches`, each request bound to its shape, at most the budget), then builds the marked shapes' contexts off the main actor from one snapshot, the board shown first, a shape being edited waiting for its editing to end, and asks through `editor.decisions` (which answers what it asked today from memory, [decision models](decision-models.md)), at most `RuleChecks.inFlight` (8) at once. An answer to an older check is dropped (each shape's generation). As the file opens (the next turn) and when another is put in its place, every shape a rule may check is marked, so a file reopened asks nothing it asked today, and a picture (Quick Look's) is gone before the wait is out: it asks nothing.
- **A shape's state for any question**: `states(of:)` builds what a rule sees of each of some shapes (its free context: no chip's finds) from one snapshot off the main actor, the state `canvas decide` asks its question with ([command line](command-line.md)).
- **Where a check stands** (`check(of:)`, a `RuleCheck`: `.checking(last)`, `.checked(RuleVerdict)`, `.failed(error, last)`; `allChecks`): a `RuleVerdict` is the rules asked, how likely the rules read together flag the shape (`together`), each rule's (`each`), the model that answered and what it was sent (`sent`: the state and the questions). While a shape is checked again its last answer counts. A failure for want of a key fails every call waiting, none is made, and the key saved (`.decisionModelKeyDidChange`) asks again; `retry()` asks again what failed. `isBusy` while a pass is coming with shapes marked, contexts are built or calls are out; Topology settles while it is, though no edge does (`keepsSettling(edges: false)`), and the checks tell it when they're done (`settleSourceChanged`), so a query that holds a word that waits for answers (Validation's three) waits for them, and one that reads only edges doesn't ([topology](topology.md), [queries](queries.md)). An editor let go cancels its calls (`isolated deinit`).
- **A chip that can't be read** warns on its type: `RuleValidationPlugin`'s rule on every shape holding `schema` (`unreadableChips(in:queries:)`: each chip whose query `checkStored(_:binding: .scope)` refuses, "“the member's district” can’t be read: " and the engine's words, its `path` `/properties/$rules/const/<r>/find/<c>/query` on `schema`; checked again whenever a commit writes a type or deletes anything, `affected`, since what the engine reads, a relationship type's edge type among it, is the file's), which reads the committed engine through `ValidationContext.committedService(_:)`, so `canvas draw` reports it on the type's line when it writes such a schema ([validation](validation.md), [command line](command-line.md)).
- **The warnings** (`issues(of:)`, `RuleValidationPlugin`'s rule, `ignoringMoves`): nothing unless the rules read together flag the shape (`cutoffs.verdict(together) == .yes`, the model's cutoffs); then a `.warning` for each rule whose own answer flags it, highest first, less those the shape ignores (by sentence: an ignore lapses when its sentence changes); where none flags alone, the rule answered highest that isn't ignored, unless an ignored one flags (that was the one) (`flaggingRules(of:in:)`, the one function the warnings and the counts read). Each warning carries an `IssueSource(plugin: RuleValidationPlugin.id, key:)` made from its `RuleKey` (`RuleKey.source`, the type's id and the sentence, the key `refreshFlags` files by), and `RuleKey(source:)` leads back, nil for another plugin's or a key no rule made, so `flagged(by:)` is asked by a warning's source; a reworded rule is a new key; the unreadable-chip warnings carry none. The warning is the rule's sentence word for word, with two actions (`IssueAction`, [validation](validation.md)): Ignore (`ruleWriter.ignore`, with the `.ignore` role) and Show Rule (`editor.showRule`: the type selected on its board, one step back, the next turn, and its Rules tab chosen); Validation adds a third button after them on the warning's line, "9 of 63" (the shape's place among the shapes the rule flags, a click beginning the rule's walk at this shape: the scope's `candidates` make it a line that has one; [validation](validation.md)). As answers land the checks ask Validation to check the shape again (`recheck`): an orange ring, the Issues line, the table of contents' dot, `:warned` and its row's `problems` (`class` `warning`), with nothing of their own.
- **What the panel reads** (`RuleChecks` is `@Observable`, its state `@ObservationIgnored`, observed by key as `TypeSync` is): `check(of:)`, `isChecking(_:)` (marked or asked again: the panel's "checking") and `hasCheck(_:)` (O(1), a check or a first mark: the Issues section's `shownWhile`, so the section comes with no commit) are observed by the shape (`\.[observed: id]`, told as its check is set, `setCheck`, and as it's marked); `flagged(by: RuleKey)` (a rule by its type and sentence: the shapes whose warnings are for it, kept by `refreshFlags` as a check lands or goes and as a commit names a checked shape, what it ignores perhaps changed) and `failure` (the want of a key first, else the first failure) are observed by one count (`flagsCount`). `verdicts(of:)` is each rule of the shape's chain now (`RuleStanding`, `RuleState`): checking while it's marked or asked again, and for a rule its last answer didn't ask; else ignored, flags (a warning's), holds (its own answer's no, or its yes while the rules read together hold: qualified by another), unsure (its answer between the cutoffs). `sent(for:)` is the last answer's state and its groups (`RuleShownGroup`: a label of the state, the shapes in it by id, every one though the model was told how many past forty, and what is no shape, "the board's top level"; `RuleContext` makes them beside the state). `rules(ofType:)` and `currentChain(for:)` are what the tab and the menus read.
- **A rule's walk** (`RuleWalk.swift`; Validation owns walking issues, so a rule gets no walker of its own: [validation](validation.md), the guide's [Walking issues](../../docs/plugin-system.md#walking-issues-validations-issue-walk)). `RuleValidationPlugin` gives Validation's `IssueScopes` point the scope of a rule's warnings for its own sources (`registrar.issueScopes`, found by `registry.issueScope(for: key.source, in: editor)`; nil for a source that is no rule's, a sentence the type no longer holds, or another plugin's): `includes` the warnings of the rule's key, `candidates` the shapes `flagged(by:)` the key (so the walk is read at once, nothing asked of the query engine), and a label of "Rule n" (n the rule's place among the type's own rules, `RulesStyle.rule`) with the type as `owner` and its chip (`IssueScopeChip`: the type's name and the swatch of Types' `look(of:)`, the color its pill and chips are drawn in, none in a list without Types), which the band draws beside "Rule n", spoken "Rule n of <type's name>" (`RulesStyle.ruleOf`). The walk's stops are the shapes whose warning shows, in board order; a shape the rules are unsure of has no warning, so is never one. A rule is named by its type and sentence, and a reworded sentence is another key (the warnings and `flagged(by:)` carry it), so the scope follows the rule (`RuleFollow`, `isChanged`): the sentence wherever the type moved it (the label's number follows), else, when the type has as many rules as it did, the one now at the old rule's place; the walk is made again from the new words with its place kept on its shape, and the stops come back as the model answers (an undo of the reword follows it back). A rule deleted (or its type) has no label, which ends the walk. Ignore on a stop is the warning's Ignore (`.ignore` role): `ruleWriter.ignore`, one step, "Ignore Rule", the row reading ignored. The scope says why a row's warning is gone (`isSetAside`: the rule's sentence is in the shape's `IgnoredRulesProperty`), so the row reads ignored however the ignore was written (the walk's, the line's, Ignore Rule ▸, a redo), in an All Issues walk as in the rule's, and a fix reads holds. The scope's `id` is the type and the sentence as the walk began.
- **The list in a rule's walk** (Go › Issue List, ⌃⌘', Validation's: [validation](validation.md); LanguageRules draws none of it). A rule's count, "9 of 63" and Walk Flagged ▸ begin the walk and ⌃⌘' (or the band's count) opens its list over the current stop's Issues tab, so a rule's whole set and what the model was shown are one screen: the rows are the rule's flagged shapes by board, each ending in its first value from Types (`IssueRowValuesPlugin`, [types](types.md): a listing's make, "Toyota"), the heads the boards' names with their open stops, an ignored row dimmed and reading "ignored" and one a fix made hold reading "holds". The evidence under the list is the stop's Issues tab as its panel draws it, through the panel's `DetailTabView`: the warning line (Ignore, Show Rule; the current stop's line says no place, the band does) and LanguageRules' own sections in the tab, Rules (each rule read together, its state) and Shown (the shapes the model was shown), following each step; Ignore there is the walk's, one step, the list staying. Return closes the list on the stop with the band staying, ⌃⌘' and the count toggle it, and with no walk running ⌃⌘' begins All Issues with the list open.
- **The writer** (`RuleWriter`, the one way a type's rules and a shape's ignores change): `setRules(_:of:)` writes the member through Types' `HeldDirective(name:)` (`text(_:setting:)`: the `const` replaced or the member put first, none taking it away, the rest of the JSON as typed; `RuleBook.literal(_:)` writes the list a person would type, a rule a line of JSON), one step "Change Rules"; `ignore(_:on:)` and `stopIgnoring(_:on:)`, "Ignore Rule" and "Stop Ignoring Rule" (the property off with the last; a type ignores none, being checked by none); each false and no step for what changes nothing, and a shape that can't change refused before any transaction (`refuseChanges`: it shakes).
- **The panel** (`RulesPanel.swift`, `RuleBoxes.swift`). A type's Rules tab (`RulesTab`, `.after(TypeSchemaTab.self)`, a section on every shape holding `schema`, shown while it holds one): its own rules, each a `RuleTextArea` ("Rule 1": what Tab and VoiceOver call it; a `DetailTextBox` over a `MentionTextView` with no owner, so no `/` list, its sentence plain text) that writes on Return or as the keyboard leaves (`RuleChips.commit`; emptied, the rule goes; a sentence changed keeps the chips whose phrase is still in it), where `@` lists shapes and then the words typed (`onShape`, `onWords`: [mentions](mentions.md)) and a pick makes a chip, drawn as one (TextEditing's highlights derived from the text, `highlighting`: a magnifier in room a kern makes, on the light's cyan once it has a query, gray while its words are asked about, orange where they're no query, `RulesStyle.chipFill`, each made once and faded as it's drawn, Palette's `fading(to:)`, so it follows the appearance); Tab walks the sentence and then each chip from the first (selected, VoiceOver hearing "Chip, <phrase>"), then leaves the box, ⇧Tab its exact way back (from the first chip to where the caret was, `caretBeforeChips`), entering from below on the last chip (`onEnter`); a draft is written over the rule it began from wherever that is by then (`bases`: a rule above it gone, an undo), and added when that rule is gone, so typing never writes over another rule; under the rule a line for each chip (`RuleChipLines`, QueryTranslation's `QueryPreview`: the query, colored and edited in place as code, with completions of the words (their colon and all) and kinds, `RuleChipCompletions`, written as the keyboard leaves it if the engine reads it bound, else its refusal under it ("Not written" for a type that can't change); the sample with ‹ › and ← →; what it found, a row each, a click going to it; Edit Words; a failure's line with Open Settings… or Retry, or Edit Words where the words are no query; Interpret for words nobody is asking about, a file reopened while they were asked), which lights what it found while the pointer is over it; with its count beside it (`FlagCount`, nothing while it flags none: a button, orange, "flags 5", whose click, or Return with it focused, begins the rule's walk at its first stop (`RuleLights.walk(_:at:orGoTo:)`: `registry.issueScope(for: key.source, in:)` then `walk.begin(scope, at: .first)`, on the next turn as `go(to:)` is, since the walk chooses the Issues tab and takes the Rules tab's button with it; with no `IssueWalkPlugin` in the list it selects the shapes as it did) and no longer selects them (Select Flagged ▸ does), and lights them while under the pointer or with the keyboard); then the rules it inherits, dimmed, "from Car" (`InheritedRule`, a button that goes to that type); then the `add rule` box (a `RuleTextArea` too, its chips' lines under it while it's typed); and a line while checks fail (`failure`: Open Settings… for want of a key, else Retry). A checked shape's Issues tab has a section "Rules" (`ShapeRulesView`, `enabledWhileReadOnly`, `shownWhile` `hasCheck`): the failure's line with its button, each rule (`RuleLine`: a triangle where it flags, a spinner while checking, its sentence, its type's chip in its color, its state at the right, and Stop Ignoring under an ignored one), then Shown (`ShownList`: one `DetailRowList`, a head a group with its count, folded by ← →, a row a shape by its title, its kind's symbol and its types, a type with its words under it; a highlighted line lights its shapes, Return on a row goes to its shape).
- **The chip** (`RulesChip`, `RulesChip.swift`, `RuleDetailsPlugin`'s, on every shape holding `schema`): a type's rules on the board as a count, "Rules 2" (`AdornmentLabel("Rules").counted(n)`, neutral), in the row above its top-left corner after its pill and the chips of what it builds on (`.aboveTopLeft`, `.after(TypeChips.self)`), counting the rules it holds itself as the checks keep them read (`rules(ofType:)`); none for a type with none of its own, and none on a skeleton (no adornment is drawn on one). Nothing to click: each rule and what it flags are in the Rules tab (the owner's pick of the plan's two ways, B: no new point in Types and no line on the note), so it is no part ⌥Tab reaches.
- **The light** (`RuleLights`, `editor.ruleLights`, `RuleDetailsPlugin`'s service, and its `outlines`): the shapes a view lights (`light(_:by:)`, by a name of its own, so only what lit them lets them go), in one cyan (`IssueWalkStyle.light`, Validation's, `#0B7BB5` light and `#64D2FF` dark: 4.6 and 9.9 to 1 against the canvases, clear of Validation's orange and red, the accent and Neighbors' lights' hues), the same cyan the issue walk's light is, so there is one (`RulesStyle.light` is gone; the chip fills and a group head's dot read the walk's); the two never show at once, and the rules' yields (held by CompositionTests: the walk's group alone with the keyboard in the band, the rules' back with it on the canvas): `RuleDetailsPlugin`'s outlines source gives nothing while the walk's `IssueLights` is lit (the walk's is lit by the keyboard being in its band, the rules' by a pointer passing over a count: [validation](validation.md)), each on the board shown as itself, else as the shape there that holds it; placed again for a commit that removes shapes or reaches another board, and a board shown; `setNeedsDisplay` only when what's outlined changed. `go(to:)` is `editor.goTo` on the next turn (the click's view goes with the panel).
- **The menus** (`RuleMenus.swift`; P2 of [keyboard navigation](keyboard-navigation.md): the panel's buttons are standard controls, which Keyboard navigation off leaves out of Tab, so each has a menu item): Ignore Rule ▸ in a checked shape's menu (one shape: each rule of its chain, cut to a line, a check by those it ignores; a pick ignores it or stops, its step), Select Flagged ▸ in a type's (each of its rules that flags shapes, "… (5)"; a pick selects them), Walk Flagged ▸ beside Select Flagged ▸ in a type's menu, in the same form (each rule that flags shapes, "… (5)", `RulesStyle.walkFlagged`; a pick begins that rule's walk at its first stop), and in a checked shape's menu beside Ignore Rule ▸ (each rule of its chain that flags it, in the chain's order, "… (5)", the rule's count; a pick begins that rule's walk at this shape, `.shape(id)`): the menu way of the line's "9 of 63" and of the count, so Keyboard navigation off, which leaves those standard controls out of Tab (P2, P10), leaves no way to begin a rule's walk out; neither is in a list without Validation's walk (`issueWalk` nil), and Retry Rules (`retryRuleChecks`, a command in a checked shape's menu, shown while a check failed for a reason trying again may mend). The Shape menu has them as it has every item of the selection's menu ([context menus](context-menus.md)).

## Testing
