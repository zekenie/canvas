# Find

Find (⌘F): the `FindableTexts` point and what contributes to it, the index and its upkeep, the search off the main actor, scope and order, the highlights, the panel in the detail panel's place, and the commands.

Related: [fuzzy matching](fuzzy-matching.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [keyboard](keyboard.md), [selection](selection.md), [topology](topology.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [titles](titles.md), [types](types.md), [arrows](arrows.md), [boards](boards.md), [text editing](text-editing.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [benchmarks](benchmarks.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Find](../../docs/plugin-system.md#find).

## Architecture

- Find (platform, a horizontal in the window, on Canvas, DetailPanel, FuzzyMatching, Geometry, Keyboard, Selection and Topology; `FindPlugin`, id `find-in-boards`, never `find`, the command's name) searches the board shown and every board under it for text matching what's typed (FuzzyMatching's `TextSearch`: folded, a phrase, exact hits then word-anchored one-typo ones, at most 100 a text), as a browser's ⌘F searches a page. It knows no kind and no shape trait: what a shape shows as text, and where it draws it, comes through the `FindableTexts` point (keyed by kind, or every kind, and a name: `scope.findableTexts(_:look:followsZoom:affected:zoomedOut:texts:rects:)` for every shape of the kinds a scope reaches (`registrar.forShapes(adopting: .titled) { $0.findableTexts("title", …) }`: [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md); `FindableTexts` takes scopes); `registrar.findableTexts(_:of:…)` for another plugin's kind; `x.findableTexts(_:…)` on a kind's own registrar; a blank name refused; the report shows `findable title → every shape with a title`). A contribution's `texts` (cheap, plain text as the shape shows it, each a `FindableText(field:text:)`) are read into the index; `rects` (where each UTF-16 range of a field's text is drawn, a list of line rects per range, world units from the shape's kept bounds' top-left, as the render lays it out now) are asked only for shapes in view with hits, and kept by Find per shape for the query while its bounds' size (and the zoom, `followsZoom`, for text sized in view points: a pill; and where its geometry puts its label, `ShapeGeometry.label`, from the bounds' top-left, for a shape with one: an arrow's label moves within bounds of one size as the shapes it's bound to move, with no commit naming the arrow) stays, so a move lays nothing out; a contributor keeps its own layout by text and width, so asking costs a lookup; `zoomedOut` is where its text is said on a skeleton (a title's cover), else the shape's bounds; `affected` names the shapes a commit changes the texts of without changing them (O(changed)); `look` is how a hit is marked (`FindLook`: `.marker`, a highlighter multiplied over the shape's own surface, `.wash`, translucent over the canvas, `.ring`, round a dark badge). Contributors are plugin values of their owners', each after Find and its owner in the shipped list, so a list without Find leaves them out (the registry refuses a contribution whose point's owner isn't installed): Titles' `TitleFindPlugin` (the kinds that took a title, `.titled`: those holding `TitleProperty` or deriving one, `registry.title(of:)`, but no text for a shape whose pill a look hides, which has no frame to ring and says the name elsewhere; the pill's whole capsule ringed, Adornments' frame, which follows the zoom; zoomed out its cover's rect), Mentions' `MentionFindPlugin` (the kinds with `MentionTexts` placements, `.mentionText`: notes' text, arrows' labels; a placement's text with each mention its live name, a hit in a name the pill's one character; two contributions, each asked for its own placements and its own look, since a look is a contribution's: "mention text" for the placements on the shape's surface (marked) and "mention text over the canvas" (washed) for those with `MentionTextPlacement.overCanvas`, an arrow's label, which the report shows as `findable mention text over the canvas → every shape with mention text`; their fields are the placement's place in the kind's list, and the commit's renames (`affected`) are asked of the first alone; rects from the render's layout, which Find's asking makes and keeps with the drawn text in `MentionTextMemo` per text and width (drawing keeps none); `affected` = `rowsAffected(by:)`, asked before any text is read). An arrow's label is mention text, so it is Mentions' and not Connections' (there is no `ArrowFindPlugin`): its rects are looked up in the render's layout, from the label frame its geometry keeps. TextEditing's `StringDrawingLayout` is the range→line-rects layout both lay text out with.
- `FindState` (`editor.find`, a service, `@Observable`): `isActive`, `query` (setting it searches), `results` (`FindResult`: a shape with hits once, its `hitCount`, `first` hit, board), `total` (hits, exact and typos alike), `current` (`FindPosition`: a result and which of its hits) and `currentNumber`, `currentHit`, `focusRequest` (+1 each ⌘F: the panel focuses its field and selects what's typed), `isIndexing` (the field's spinner), `scope`, `heading(of:)` (what heads a board's rows in the panel: a plugin's, else "Board" for the root and no name), and for tests and benchmarks `isSettled`/`settled()`, `hits(of:)`, `texts(of:)`, `rects(of:in:)`, `indexSlices`, `indexedShapes` (and, debug builds, `recordsRead`, `searchesAnswered`, `shapesPatched`). It never transacts; its commands leave no undo step.
- `FindIndex` (internal): every text every shape shows on every board the editor can show (none kept aside), folded once (`FoldedText`), by shape (`[ShapeID: IndexedShape]`, never file order). Nothing before the first ⌘F: a commit costs one test. Built on the first ⌘F, the walk of the boards' ids, then a 2 ms slice of the main actor at a time (`FindStyle.buildSlice`, thread CPU) on the editor's scheduler, each shape read as its turn comes. Then per commit, once every service has it (`documentDidSettle`, [editor](editor.md)): contributions' `affected` first, then `removed`, `added` and `changed` less `moved`, `elsewhere` (a commit that only took boards drops what's gone, going through whichever is fewer, as `TitleIndex`), and what `affected` named; a file put in place reads everything again; a switch reads nothing (the index is the file's).
- The search runs off the main actor (`FindSearch.run`, `@concurrent`, over a `SearchRequest` handed over whole, `Handover`, Queries' pattern copied, not imported): the index's entries as a value (shared until a commit copies the table once), the scope's shapes in order (kept from the search before while nothing but moves changed the file, else ordered there from Topology's snapshot, `forEachShape(inFileOrder:)` bucketed by board; without Topology, documents' order), cancelled and restarted per keystroke, cancellation asked every 256 shapes and every 64K units of a text, answers tagged by generation (an older one dropped). Results are compact; a shape's hits are worked out again on demand (in view, current, a listed row) by matching that one text. While Find is open a commit patches the results for the shapes the index read again, O(those): a count changed or a shape gone in place, a shape gaining its first hit searched again off the main actor; a move does nothing. A commit landing while a search is under way starts it again (its answer, from the index as it began, would undo the patch), with the landing it had. Any commit but a move drops the kept order, whether or not anything's typed; a rebuild (a file put in place) turns the spinner on again (`isIndexing`).
- Scope: the board shown when the query last changed (or ⌘F opened Find) and every board under it; stepping and rows don't scope again. A scope that can't be shown any more (its board deleted, or a file put in place without it: `editor.canShow`, asked as the index changes) is scoped again to the board shown, and searched again, nothing retyped. Order: the scope board's shapes in file order (what a frame holds after it), then each board under it, depth first, in file order; the panel heads each board's rows. Typing's current hit: the first on the board shown that's in view, else its first (brought into view, `reveal`), else the first anywhere (no board shown: typing never switches boards); the first `FindStyle.landingProbe` (64) results on the board shown are asked their bounds, past them the shapes in view (`paintOrder(in:)`) are looked up among the results. Return and ⇧Return in the field, ⌘G and ⇧⌘G anywhere, and the panel's buttons step hit by hit, wrapping, showing a hit's board and bringing it into view unless it's in view; a row's click makes its shape's first hit current. With Find closed and a query, ⌘G opens it and lands on the first hit in view, which is its step, and ⇧⌘G opens it, lands there and steps back. Nothing changes the selection (showing another board empties it, as every switch does); a session's navigation is one step back (Selection's `editor.navigate(from:_:)` from `navigationStart`, taken as Find opens). An edit keeps the current hit (its shape and ordinal, clamped, else the next shape's), and the camera stays.
- Find's commands are its own, each declared once with its key and its item in Edit › Find (`menu: "Edit › Find"`, orders 200 to 220: [keyboard](keyboard.md)). ⌘F (`.find`, refused mid-gesture, `runs: .betweenPresses`): opens Find, or focuses its field with its text selected; ends editing first, and what's selected in the text being edited (`editor.editingSelectedText`, the core's, from the host's editing view: an attachment that adopts the core's `ReadsAsText`, a mention's pill, reads as its text, any other is dropped), one line, is what's typed. Closing: Escape or ⌘. in the panel (the takeover's `onEscape`, before the panel's `onExit`) or Done: the highlights go, the panel shows what the selection says, the keyboard goes to the canvas, what's typed is kept. Escape on the canvas is the canvas's (Find stays open), and so is a click on a shape. `.findNext` (⌘G) and `.findPrevious` (⇧⌘G) are enabled while there are hits, or with Find closed and a query.
- Field values (`FieldFindPlugin`, Types', id `field-find`, after Find and Types in the shipped list, `findable field value → every shape showing field values`): on the kinds that hold `fieldValues` and say where their rows go (and on the arrows that relate: Types' `.showingFieldValues`), a `FindableText` for each field of a note that is one that holds a value, keyed by the field's key, a folded field's too: a plain value as its row shows it (a date `Mar 3, 2026`, a number by its value, a choice its label), a row with more in it every text under it at any depth, one a line (a group's filled members, a list's elements, a list of groups' members, pairs' names and values, the branch an either's value fits, an `Other` field's strings); a yes or no has none, a row whose type is gone none, a type's own note none. An arrow that relates (a kind that holds `endRule` and `fieldValues`) gives the same for the types it is of, by the field's key (`RowBook.findTexts(ofRelationship:)`, a function of the arrow and the types alone): a relationship's values are found on its arrow, and the hit marks its relationship type's chip (the `TypeChips` frame whose tag is a relationship type, else the arrow's anchor, where no chip is shown), since the values are shown nowhere on the board; a relation row's pills are not searched, since each note they name is found on its own. The texts are read from the record and the resolved fields, never from the rows kept as shown. A hit marks the row's value as a highlighter does, the same mark for each range (`RowBook.findRect`: the value as it hugs it, the fold's words for a folded field, the row's, whether the match is in the value or in a row with more in it under the summary). Its `affected` is `RowBook.reached(by:)`: Find reads the instances of a type that changed, through the field book, which reads the index. The index follows the commit once every service has it (`documentDidSettle`), so the book has been told what the commit reached ([editor](editor.md)), and `reached` answers the instances of the types whose resolution changed (the date's kind, a choice's label, which rows fold), none when no type did; a commit that only moved shapes names nothing, hears nothing and wakes nothing (`findUpkeep` holds). `fieldFindTests` holds that the layout is announced once.
- The overlay (`FindOverlay`, world space) draws nothing, and asks no `paintOrder(in:)`, while Find is closed or has no hits. In full, per shape in view with hits by `drawsSkeleton(of:)`, each hit's rects offset by the shape's bounds' origin and `worldDrawingOffset(of:)`, a path per look and per current (`FindStyle`: an orange that reads on a note's yellow and a card's white, the same in either appearance; the current one deeper with a line of view points ÷ zoom); a frame lays out shapes not yet laid out for at most `FindStyle.layoutBudget` (2 ms; always one), tinting the rest's bounds meanwhile and asking the scheduler for another frame. Zoomed out: each shape's bounds (or its contributions' `zoomedOut`) washed lightly (`FindStyle.zoomedOutHit`), so a title said on it still reads, the current one washed alike and lined round in `currentLine` (2 view points wide, 2 out from its edge), a shape under a device pixel its pixel, once. A hit under a shape in front shows through it (accepted).
- `FindPanel(editor:onExit:)` (SwiftUI, DetailPanel's insets): Find's own header (no Back): the field ("Find", magnifier, a spinner while indexing, Return and ⇧Return step), Done, the count ("3 of 12") and Previous and Next (tooltips `registry.help(_:forCommand:)`); rows one per shape, a window of `FindStyle.rowWindow` (200) around the current one in a `LazyVStack`, each the kind's symbol in the shape's tint (`find.symbol(of:)`, read as the row is drawn with `editor.tint(of:)`: the kind's, the core's, unless the shape has a color of its own; secondary for a shape with none), drawn again by `find.tintRevision`, which a commit to a result's shape and the file's colors (`shapeTintsDidChange`) bump, the text around its first hit with hits semibold, its count when more than one ("2/5" on the current row); boards headed as `find.heading(of:)` says: what a plugin calls a board (the `FindHeadings` point, a `KeyedExtensionPoint`, at most one, a second `RegistryError.conflict`; `registrar.findHeading { board, editor in FindHeading(title, systemImage:) }`, the report's `find heading`; Boards' `BoardFindPlugin`, `Sources/Boards/BoardHeadings.swift`, gives the path's names: [boards](boards.md)), else "Board" for the root (`FindStyle.rootHeading`) and no name, so Find never learns what names a board; "No matches" (`FindStyle.noMatches`, as Jump To's and the @ picker's read); nothing listed with nothing typed. Rows are borderless buttons that don't take the keyboard, so the field keeps it. Return in the field steps unless an input method is composing there (the field editor has marked text: Return is the method's, and so are the arrows). Keys in the field (principle 1: what a click on a row does, the keys do): ↑ and ↓ make the previous and next result current (`FindState.move(by:)`, wrapping, its first hit, its board shown and the hit brought into view as a click on its row does; Page Up and Page Down move `FindStyle.pageRows` (8) results; the row window follows); they select nothing and change no document. ⌘↩ (`FindState.finish()`) is the one way to take the hit along: it closes Find and selects the current hit's shape with `goTo` inside `editor.navigate(from:)` of the session's start, so Back returns to where Find began as one step, even when the stepping already showed another board; the keyboard goes to the canvas (`onExit`). Escape, ⌘. and Done close it and change nothing of the selection, as before. Tab and ⇧Tab leave the field as in any text field (nothing traps it), and Escape always closes. VoiceOver: a row reads as its text, the kind's name, the board when it isn't the one shown, and "hit i of n" (`find.rowLabel(of:)`), the current row carries the selected trait, and each change of the current hit announces "Hit 3 of 12, Note, the text around it" (`find.announcement`, posted by the panel with `AccessibilityNotification.Announcement`). Key hints (`FindPanelPlugin`, under Editing Text in the Keyboard Shortcuts window, since no shortcut has them): ↩ or ⇧↩ Next or Previous Hit, ↑ or ↓ Previous or Next Result, ⌘↩ Select Hit and Close. Snippet text (`FindSnippet.attributed(of:in:)`, `text(of:in:)`) is shared by the row and the announcement. The text around a row's first hit (`FindSnippet.range(in:around:)`) starts at a word's start and runs `FindStyle.snippetLength` at most, both ends on composed character sequences, so a surrogate pair is never cut. `FindPanelPlugin` (id `find-panel`, in `FindPanel.swift`), a plugin value of its own after `FindPlugin` and `DetailPanelPlugin`, gives it as the detail panel's takeover while `isActive`, `onEscape` closing Find (`registrar.detailTakeover`, the panel's `DetailTakeovers` point: [detail panel](detail-panel.md)), so a list without the detail panel leaves it out and keeps Find's state, highlights and commands, and one with it and no panel is refused; the window shows a hidden inspector while Find is open and hides it again on close if that's what showed it, and hiding the inspector while Find is open closes Find (the keyboard back to the canvas), since its highlights and ⌘G would go on with no panel to close it by: the window's rule for whatever takes the panel over (CanvasUI's `BoardChrome`, through the detail panel's inspector: [host slots](host-slots.md)), which Find knows nothing of.

## Testing

- The field's keys (PanelTests, `KeyDriver` throughout): ↑ ↓ Page Up Page Down wrap through the results and change neither the selection nor the file; ⌘↩ selects the hit's shape (on this board and on another), closes Find, gives the canvas the keyboard and leaves exactly one Back step; Escape, ⌘. and Done select nothing; Tab never traps; rows are labeled (`find.rowLabel(of:)`) and each step's announcement (posted when `find.stepCount` moves, which a step, an arrow and a click on a row move and typing's landing does not, so typing is not talked over) is `find.announcement`; a row's label keeps its text, kind and board until the results change or a step goes to another hit (`labelStem(of:)`), so a step does not build 200 snippets again;, both read from Find's state, since SwiftUI's accessibility tree isn't built in an unshown window; the selected trait on the current row is in the view and unchecked by a test.
- A row's symbol tint (SymbolTests): `find.symbol(of:)` is the shape's tint, read as the row is drawn, and `tintRevision` (observed through `withObservationTracking`) moves for a commit to a result's shape and for `shapeTintsDidChange`, not for another shape's; CanvasTesting's `Frozen` flag and a contributor's `shapeTints` stand in for a color a shape holds.
- For Find, FindTests use fixtures only (`MemosPlugin`: a memo, text on one line in a monospaced grid, `MemoStyle.rect(_:)` a range's rect, `echo` showing another memo's text for `affected`, counting `textsRead` and `rectsAsked`; `BadgesPlugin`, every box's badge, ringed, `followsZoom`, `zoomedOut`; `SlowPlugin`, a millisecond's layout a shape, for the frame's budget; `PocketFile`, pockets two deep; `findHarness` with Keyboard, Selection, Topology, Find, fixtures and pockets, its viewport 800 × 600; `search(_:in:)` opens Find, runs the index's slices on the harness's scheduler and awaits the answer): the point (report, conflict, blank name, owner not installed, a scope), the index (nothing read before ⌘F, slices, a move reading nothing, elsewhere, a deleted pocket and its undo, `affected`), scope and order (pockets, corrals, the count and the cap), typing's current hit (past the results asked, found among the shapes in view), stepping across boards and its one Back step (a click or a pan between adding no second; a step that moves nothing adding none), rows, ⌘G with Find closed (landing on the first hit in view, none skipped), a scope no longer showable followed to the board shown (a pocket deleted, a file put in place), edits keeping the current hit, a shape added while nothing's typed found after, a commit while a search is under way starting it again, a drag patching nothing, the overlay's pixels in full (the current deeper, nothing closed, a move keeping the layout, a label moving within bounds of one size laid out again: `Leashed`, a box whose geometry's label hangs from another shape), the frame's budget, zoomed out (washed alike, the current one lined round), a contribution's `zoomedOut` and a ring following the zoom, and the panel in an unshown window (`FindWindow`: the canvas and `DetailPanelView` over `findPlugins` with `DetailPanelPlugin()` and `FindPanelPlugin()`, the real `TaskScheduler`; `FindPanelPlugin` refused without the detail panel and Find finding without either; a board's heading from a plugin, "Board" and no name without one, a second a conflict; ⌘F focusing the field, typing, Return and ⇧Return, Escape and ⌘. closing and giving the canvas the keyboard, Escape on the canvas and a click on a shape leaving Find open, a row's click keeping the field's keyboard, Done; MentionsTests' `mentionFindTests` (drawing keeping no text layout, Find's rects one, and `Banner`, a kind whose text is `overCanvas`, washed where a jot's is marked: the hit that isn't the current one, in blue, a quarter under a marker and over half under a wash), TitlesTests' `titleFindTests`, ArrowsTests' `arrowFindTests` (an arrow's label, found through Mentions' contributor) and TextEditingTests' `stringDrawingTests` hold each contribution's texts and rects to its render's ink; CompositionTests' `findShippedTests` the shipped wiring (Edit › Find's items from the canvas, Find's field and a note being edited, seeding from its selection, and across a mention's pill from the name it shows; hiding the inspector closing Find; ⌘F showing a hidden inspector and hiding it on close; a note, a type note, an arrow's label and a title found and highlighted; a hit orange and standing out on a note and a type note, light and dark; Find's list no table (`InspectorWindow.tables`); a subboard's contents from the root, each board's rows headed as the path names the board, and Back).
