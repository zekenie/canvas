# Find

Find (⌘F): the `FindableTexts` point and what contributes to it, the index and its upkeep, the search off the main actor, scope and order, the highlights, the panel in the detail panel's place, and the commands.

Related: [fuzzy matching](fuzzy-matching.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [keyboard](keyboard.md), [selection](selection.md), [topology](topology.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [titles](titles.md), [types](types.md), [arrows](arrows.md), [boards](boards.md), [text editing](text-editing.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [benchmarks](benchmarks.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Find](../../docs/plugin-system.md#find).

## Architecture

- Find (platform, a horizontal in the window, on Canvas, DetailPanel, FuzzyMatching, Geometry, Keyboard, Selection and Topology; `FindPlugin`, id `find-in-boards`, never `find`, the command's name) searches the board shown and every board under it for text matching what's typed (FuzzyMatching's `TextSearch`: folded, a phrase, exact hits then word-anchored one-typo ones, at most 100 a text), as a browser's ⌘F searches a page. It knows no kind and no shape trait: what a shape shows as text, and where it draws it, comes through the `FindableTexts` point (keyed by kind, or every kind, and a name: `scope.findableTexts(_:look:followsZoom:affected:zoomedOut:texts:rects:)` for every shape of the kinds a scope reaches (`registrar.forShapes(adopting: .titled) { $0.findableTexts("title", …) }`: [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md); `FindableTexts` takes scopes); `registrar.findableTexts(_:of:…)` for another plugin's kind; `x.findableTexts(_:…)` on a kind's own registrar; a blank name refused; the report shows `findable title → every shape with a title`). A contribution's `texts` (cheap, plain text as the shape shows it, each a `FindableText(field:text:)`) are read into the index; `rects` (where each UTF-16 range of a field's text is drawn, a list of line rects per range, world units from the shape's kept bounds' top-left, as the render lays it out now) are asked only for shapes in view with hits, and kept by Find per shape for the query while its bounds' size (and the zoom, `followsZoom`, for text sized in view points: a pill; and where its geometry puts its label, `ShapeGeometry.label`, from the bounds' top-left, for a shape with one: an arrow's label moves within bounds of one size as the shapes it's bound to move, with no commit naming the arrow) stays, so a move lays nothing out; a contributor keeps its own layout by text and width, so asking costs a lookup; `zoomedOut` is where its text is said on a skeleton (a title's cover), else the shape's bounds; `affected` names the shapes a commit changes the texts of without changing them (O(changed)); `look` is how a hit is marked (`FindLook`: `.marker`, a highlighter multiplied over the shape's own surface, `.wash`, translucent over the canvas, `.ring`, round a dark badge). Contributors are plugin values of their owners', each after Find and its owner in the shipped list, so a list without Find leaves them out (the registry refuses a contribution whose point's owner isn't installed): Titles' `TitleFindPlugin` (the kinds that took a title, `.titled`: those holding `TitleProperty` or deriving one, `registry.title(of:)`, but no text for a shape whose pill a look hides, which has no frame to ring and says the name elsewhere; the pill's whole capsule ringed, Adornments' frame, which follows the zoom; zoomed out its cover's rect), Mentions' `MentionFindPlugin` (the kinds with `MentionTexts` placements, `.mentionText`: notes' text, arrows' labels; a placement's text with each mention its live name, a hit in a name the pill's one character; two contributions, each asked for its own placements and its own look, since a look is a contribution's: "mention text" for the placements on the shape's surface (marked) and "mention text over the canvas" (washed) for those with `MentionTextPlacement.overCanvas`, an arrow's label, which the report shows as `findable mention text over the canvas → every shape with mention text`; their fields are the placement's place in the kind's list, and the commit's renames (`affected`) are asked of the first alone; rects from the render's layout, which Find's asking makes and keeps with the drawn text in `MentionTextMemo` per text and width (drawing keeps none); `affected` = `rowsAffected(by:)`, asked before any text is read). The engineering kit's `EngineeringFindPlugin` (`engineering-find`, after Find and the kit) gives its rows a text a row, in row order, each field a row's id (`PartID.description`): "rows" (`.marker`) a column's `name type` (its name alone where its type is an enum's pill) and an enum's value, marked where the row draws it (`ColumnRow`, the render's own layout, and the line's offsets, `CTLineGetOffsetForStringIndex`); "row pills" (`.ring`) an enum-typed column's pill, by the enum's name now, the pill ringed, its `affected` the tables on the board shown that name an enum a commit changed ([engineering](engineering.md)). Results stay one a shape. An arrow's label is mention text, so it is Mentions' and not Connections' (there is no `ArrowFindPlugin`): its rects are looked up in the render's layout, from the label frame its geometry keeps. TextEditing's `StringDrawingLayout` is the range→line-rects layout both lay text out with.
- `FindState` (`editor.find`, a service, `@Observable`): `isActive`, `query` (setting it searches), `results` (`FindResult`: a shape with hits once, its `hitCount`, `first` hit, board), `total` (hits, exact and typos alike), `current` (`FindPosition`: a result and which of its hits) and `currentNumber`, `currentHit`, `focusRequest` (+1 each ⌘F: the panel focuses its field and selects what's typed), `isIndexing` (the field's spinner), `scope`, and for tests and benchmarks `isSettled`/`settled()`, `hits(of:)`, `texts(of:)`, `rects(of:in:)`, `fieldsHit(of:)` (the fields of a shape's texts that hold hits, worked out from its hits when asked; empty while closed: what a plugin that hides some of a shape's texts shows while Find is open, LineRows' folds), `whenResultsChange(_:)` (told after new results, a commit's patch, and Find opening or closing, never during a search: LineRows lays the folded shapes whose hit rows changed out again once the change settles, a browser's `hidden=until-found`: [line rows](line-rows.md)), `indexSlices`, `indexedShapes` (and, debug builds, `recordsRead`, `searchesAnswered`, `shapesPatched`). It never transacts; its commands leave no undo step.
- `FindIndex` (internal): every text every shape shows on every board the editor can show (none kept aside), folded once (`FoldedText`), by shape (`[ShapeID: IndexedShape]`, never file order). Nothing before the first ⌘F: a commit costs one test. Built on the first ⌘F, the walk of the boards' ids, then a 2 ms slice of the main actor at a time (`FindStyle.buildSlice`, thread CPU) on the editor's scheduler, each shape read as its turn comes. Then per commit, once every service has it (`documentDidSettle`, [editor](editor.md)): contributions' `affected` first, then `removed`, `added` and `changed` less `moved`, `elsewhere` (a commit that only took boards drops what's gone, going through whichever is fewer, as Boards' `PathIndex`), and what `affected` named; a file put in place reads everything again; a switch reads nothing (the index is the file's).
- The search runs off the main actor (`FindSearch.run`, `@concurrent`, over a `SearchRequest` handed over whole, `Handover`, Queries' pattern copied, not imported): the index's entries as a value (shared until a commit copies the table once), the scope's shapes in order (kept from the search before while nothing but moves changed the file, else ordered there from Topology's snapshot, `boardOrder(from: scope)`: [topology](topology.md); without Topology, documents' order, Find's own fallback), cancelled and restarted per keystroke, cancellation asked every 256 shapes and every 64K units of a text, answers tagged by generation (an older one dropped). Results are compact; a shape's hits are worked out again on demand (in view, current, a listed row) by matching that one text. While Find is open a commit patches the results for the shapes the index read again, O(those): a count changed or a shape gone in place, a shape gaining its first hit searched again off the main actor; a move does nothing. A commit landing while a search is under way starts it again (its answer, from the index as it began, would undo the patch), with the landing it had. Any commit but a move drops the kept order, whether or not anything's typed; a rebuild (a file put in place) turns the spinner on again (`isIndexing`).
- Scope: the board shown when the query last changed (or ⌘F opened Find) and every board under it; stepping and rows don't scope again. A scope that can't be shown any more (its board deleted, or a file put in place without it: `editor.canShow`, asked as the index changes) is scoped again to the board shown, and searched again, nothing retyped. Order (`TopologySnapshot.boardOrder(from:)`, Topology's: other plugins order shapes by its `boardOrderKey(of:)`, which sorts as it does): the scope board's shapes in file order (what a frame holds after it), then each board under it, depth first, in file order; the panel heads each board's rows. Typing's current hit: the first on the board shown that's in view, else its first (brought into view, `reveal`), else the first anywhere (no board shown: typing never switches boards); the first `FindStyle.landingProbe` (64) results on the board shown are asked their bounds, past them the shapes in view (`paintOrder(in:)`) are looked up among the results. Return and ⇧Return in the field, ⌘G and ⇧⌘G anywhere, and the panel's buttons step hit by hit, wrapping, showing a hit's board and bringing it into view unless it's in view; a row's click makes its shape's first hit current. The stepping is Selection's `ShapeWalk` ([selection](selection.md#walking-shapes); `FindState.walk`, arrival `.shows`), of which Find is the first client: the results are its rows (`FindState: ShapeWalkSource`: a result is a row, its hits are the stops, its `rank` the order a row keeps when others come and go), and `current`, `currentNumber`, `step`, `select`, `move`, `finish` and `stepCount` forward to it with their signatures, so a step, like every walker move, does nothing mid-gesture or while shapes wait to be placed: Find's guards are the walker's, wider than the `editor.session == nil` they were before, and `canStep` (⌘G and ⇧⌘G's enabled state) refuses in the same two cases with Find open, so the items are dimmed exactly when the walker would refuse (with Find closed they only open it on the remembered query, as ⌘F does, and stay live while shapes wait). Find keeps its search, its landing (typing's current hit: `land(at:arriving:)`, which is no step), `pendingStep`, the kept hit across an edit (`WalkKept`, `rowsDidChange(keeping:)`) and its labels (cleared as the walk says it moved, `walkDidMove`). With Find closed and a query, ⌘G opens it and lands on the first hit in view, which is its step, and ⇧⌘G opens it, lands there and steps back; while shapes wait to be placed, when a step is refused, that step back is a landing too (`ShapeWalk.land(stepping:)`, [selection](selection.md#walking-shapes)): the hit before the first, wrapping, current at once and shown only if it is on the board shown, since showing another would let the waiting shapes go. Nothing changes the selection (showing another board empties it, as every switch does); a session's navigation is one step back (the walk's `begin()` at ⌘F takes `navigationStart`, and each move is made inside `editor.navigate(from:_:)` of it), gone back to by the camera where the walk moved nothing else (Find closed on the board it began on: [selection](selection.md)). An edit keeps the current hit (its shape and ordinal, clamped, else the next shape's), and the camera stays.
- Find's commands are its own, each declared once with its key and its item in Edit › Find (`FindSection`, whose menu is a submenu first in `EditMenu.FindAndSpelling`, beside Spelling and Grammar: [keyboard](keyboard.md)). ⌘F (`.find`, refused mid-gesture, `runs: .betweenPresses`): opens Find, or focuses its field with its text selected; ends editing first, and what's selected in the text being edited (`editor.editingSelectedText`, the core's, from the host's editing view: an attachment that adopts the core's `ReadsAsText`, a mention's pill, reads as its text, any other is dropped), one line, is what's typed. Closing: Escape or ⌘. in the panel (the takeover's `onEscape`, before the panel's `onExit`) or Done: the highlights go, the panel shows what the selection says, the keyboard goes to the canvas, what's typed is kept. Escape on the canvas is the canvas's (Find stays open), and so is a click on a shape. `.findNext` (⌘G) and `.findPrevious` (⇧⌘G) are enabled while there are hits, or with Find closed and a query.
- Field values (`FieldFindPlugin`, Types', id `field-find`, after Find and Types in the shipped list, `findable field value → every shape showing field values`): on the kinds that hold `fieldValues` and say where their rows go (and on the arrows that relate: Types' `.showingFieldValues`), a `FindableText` for each field of a note that is one that holds a value, keyed by the field's key, a folded field's too: a plain value as its row shows it (a date `Mar 3, 2026`, a number by its value, a choice its label), a row with more in it every text under it at any depth, one a line (a group's filled members, a list's elements, a list of groups' members, pairs' names and values, the branch an either's value fits, an `Other` field's strings); a yes or no has none, a row whose type is gone none, a type's own note none. An arrow that relates (a kind that holds `endRule` and `fieldValues`) gives the same for the types it is of, by the field's key (`RowBook.findTexts(byFields:)`, a function of the shape and the types alone, which the issue list's first value of a typed frame or subboard reads too): a relationship's values are found on its arrow, and the hit marks its relationship type's chip (the `TypeChips` frame whose tag is a relationship type, else the arrow's anchor, where no chip is shown), since the values are shown nowhere on the board; a relation row's pills are not searched, since each note they name is found on its own. The texts are read from the record and the resolved fields, never from the rows kept as shown. A hit marks the row's value as a highlighter does, the same mark for each range (`RowBook.findRect`: the value as it hugs it, the fold's words for a folded field, the row's, whether the match is in the value or in a row with more in it under the summary). Its `affected` is `RowBook.reached(by:)`: Find reads the instances of a type that changed, through the field book, which reads the index. The index follows the commit once every service has it (`documentDidSettle`), so the book has been told what the commit reached ([editor](editor.md)), and `reached` answers the instances of the types whose resolution changed (the date's kind, a choice's label, which rows fold), none when no type did; a commit that only moved shapes names nothing, hears nothing and wakes nothing (`findUpkeep` holds). `fieldFindTests` holds that the layout is announced once.
- The overlay (`FindOverlay`, world space) draws nothing, and asks no `paintOrder(in:)`, while Find is closed or has no hits. In full, per shape in view with hits by `drawsSkeleton(of:)`, each hit's rects offset by the shape's bounds' origin and `worldDrawingOffset(of:)`, a path per look and per current (`FindStyle`: an orange that reads on a note's yellow and a card's white, the same in either appearance; the current one deeper with a line of view points ÷ zoom); a frame lays out shapes not yet laid out for at most `FindStyle.layoutBudget` (2 ms; always one), tinting the rest's bounds meanwhile and asking the scheduler for another frame. Zoomed out: each shape's bounds (or its contributions' `zoomedOut`) washed lightly (`FindStyle.zoomedOutHit`), so a title said on it still reads, the current one washed alike and lined round in `currentLine` (2 view points wide, 2 out from its edge), a shape under a device pixel its pixel, once. A hit under a shape in front shows through it (accepted).
- `FindPanel(editor:onExit:)` (SwiftUI, DetailPanel's insets): Find's own header (no Back; its first line, the field and Done, in the window's title bar's row as the panel's first line is, the panel's `detailTitleRow`: [detail panel](detail-panel.md)): the field ("Find", magnifier, a spinner while indexing, Return and ⇧Return step), Done, the count ("3 of 12") and Previous and Next (tooltips `registry.help(_:forCommand:)`); rows one per shape, a window of `FindStyle.rowWindow` (200) around the current one in a `LazyVStack`, each the kind's symbol in the shape's tint (`find.symbol(of:)`, read as the row is drawn with `editor.tint(of:)`: the kind's, the core's, unless the shape has a color of its own; secondary for a shape with none), drawn again by `find.tintRevision`, which a commit to a result's shape and the file's colors (`shapeTintsDidChange`) bump, the text around its first hit with hits semibold, its count when more than one ("2/5" on the current row); boards headed as `editor.boardHeading(of:)` says, the detail panel's (its `BoardHeadings` point: what a plugin calls a board, else "Board" for the root and no name; Boards' `BoardHeadingsPlugin` gives the path's names: [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [boards](boards.md)), so Find never learns what names a board, and a row's VoiceOver label names its board by the same heading; "No matches" (`FindStyle.noMatches`, as Jump To's and the @ picker's read); nothing listed with nothing typed. Rows are borderless buttons that don't take the keyboard, so the field keeps it. Return in the field steps unless an input method is composing there (the field editor has marked text: Return is the method's, and so are the arrows). Keys in the field (principle 1: what a click on a row does, the keys do): ↑ and ↓ make the previous and next result current (`FindState.move(by:)`, wrapping, its first hit, its board shown and the hit brought into view as a click on its row does; Page Up and Page Down move `FindStyle.pageRows` (8) results; the row window follows); they select nothing and change no document. ⌘↩ (`FindState.finish()`) is the one way to take the hit along: it closes Find and selects the current hit's shape with `goTo` inside `editor.navigate(from:)` of the session's start, so Back returns to where Find began as one step, even when the stepping already showed another board; the keyboard goes to the canvas (`onExit`). Escape, ⌘. and Done close it and change nothing of the selection, as before. Tab and ⇧Tab leave the field as in any text field (nothing traps it), and Escape always closes. VoiceOver: a row reads as its text, the kind's name, the board when it isn't the one shown, and "hit i of n" (`find.rowLabel(of:)`), the current row carries the selected trait, and each change of the current hit announces "Hit 3 of 12, Note, the text around it" (`find.announcement`, posted by the panel with `AccessibilityNotification.Announcement`). Key hints (`FindPanelPlugin`, under Editing Text in the Keyboard Shortcuts window, since no shortcut has them): ↩ or ⇧↩ Next or Previous Hit, ↑ or ↓ Previous or Next Result, ⌘↩ Select Hit and Close. Snippet text (`FindSnippet.attributed(of:in:)`, `text(of:in:)`) is shared by the row and the announcement. The text around a row's first hit (`FindSnippet.range(in:around:)`) starts at a word's start and runs `FindStyle.snippetLength` at most, both ends on composed character sequences, so a surrogate pair is never cut. `FindPanelPlugin` (id `find-panel`, in `FindPanel.swift`), a plugin value of its own after `FindPlugin` and `DetailPanelPlugin`, gives it as the detail panel's takeover while `isActive`, `onEscape` closing Find (`registrar.detailTakeover`, the panel's `DetailTakeovers` point: [detail panel](detail-panel.md)), so a list without the detail panel leaves it out and keeps Find's state, highlights and commands, and one with it and no panel is refused; the window shows the inspector while Find is open, whatever the person chose and whatever is selected (Find's takeover wants it), and once Find closes it is as the person chose it, and shown only while shapes are selected; hiding the inspector while Find is open closes Find (the keyboard back to the canvas), since its highlights and ⌘G would go on with no panel to close it by: the window's rule for whatever takes the panel over (CanvasUI's `BoardChrome`, through the detail panel's inspector: [host slots](host-slots.md)), which Find knows nothing of.

## Testing

- The field's keys (PanelTests, `KeyDriver` throughout): ↑ ↓ Page Up Page Down wrap through the results and change neither the selection nor the file; ⌘↩ selects the hit's shape (on this board and on another), closes Find, gives the canvas the keyboard and leaves exactly one Back step; Escape, ⌘. and Done select nothing; Tab never traps; rows are labeled (`find.rowLabel(of:)`) and each step's announcement (posted when `find.stepCount` moves, which a step, an arrow and a click on a row move and typing's landing does not, so typing is not talked over) is `find.announcement`; a row's label keeps its text, kind and board until the results change or a step goes to another hit (`labelStem(of:)`), so a step does not build 200 snippets again;, both read from Find's state, since SwiftUI's accessibility tree isn't built in an unshown window; the selected trait on the current row is in the view and unchecked by a test.
- A row's symbol tint (SymbolTests): `find.symbol(of:)` is the shape's tint, read as the row is drawn, and `tintRevision` (observed through `withObservationTracking`) moves for a commit to a result's shape and for `shapeTintsDidChange`, not for another shape's; CanvasTesting's `Frozen` flag and a contributor's `shapeTints` stand in for a color a shape holds.
