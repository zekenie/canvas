# The editor

What `CanvasEditor`, the headless core, lends every plugin: labels and symbols, the default tool, outlines and their standoff, read-only shapes, drawing offsets, the glide, skeleton levels, commands and zoom, keys, the hand and the middle button, the translation adjuster, service and session hooks, edge scrolling.

Related: [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md), [undo](undo.md), [editing](editing.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [keyboard](keyboard.md), [selection](selection.md), [snapping](snapping.md), [locking](locking.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [The editor, the document, and drawing](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-editor-the-document-and-drawing), [Outlines](../../docs/plugin-system.md#outlines), [Skeletons](../../docs/plugin-system.md#skeletons).

## Architecture

- `CanvasEditor` is the headless brain (document, camera, tools, sessions, input, drawing). What it lends plugins generically: what a kind is called and its symbol (`registry.label(of:)`: what the kind declares, `x.label(_:systemImage:)`, else its placing tool's label and symbol, else its id), which every place a shape is shown by its kind reads (the panel's header, rows, a board's name, mentions' words, undo names, a title's pill that asks for its symbol); a default tool (`registrar.defaultTool`, else Hand) that Escape and placing return to; `registrar.outlines`, stroked with the hint in one pass, over the faint ones (`Outlines.faintShapes`), culled to the view ([drawing](drawing.md)), each closed shape's outline standing off its edge, the same for every kind (`editor.outlineStandoff`, host SPI: `Style.outlineStandoff`, 4 view points, divided by the zoom so it's constant on screen; 0 outlines each shape on its own path, as the POC did, and sets the POC goldens' boards drawn so: an intended deviation from the POC and from tldraw, whose outlines lie on the edge, made so that no kind's accent line is drawn over a border or shadow of its own and every kind reads alike): its `indicatorPath` grown outward by the gap, corners concentric, and the box around several (`Outlines.rects`) the same, so it holds their rings, while an open outline (an arrow's, a line's: its kept geometry isn't closed) has no inside and is stroked on its own path. A rect or a rounded rect, which every box kind's `indicatorPath` is, is added grown directly, a rounded rect of radius plus gap (a plain rect's corners are the gap's radius), costing what the plain outline did: the path is read in two passes over its end points with nothing made (`RoundedBox.of`: its straight edges lie on the sides of the box, the kept geometry's bounds, and leave the same room at both ends, which is the corner radius, whatever the corners' curves are made of); any other closed outline is grown by path arithmetic (`CGPath.grownOutward(by:isClosed:)`, far dearer than a rounded rect, and not kept from one frame to the next; debug builds' `outlinesGrownByArithmetic` counts them, and a test holds the shipped kinds to none). A plugin that decorates along an outline reads `editor.outlineGap` and `editor.outlinePath(of:)`, never the number (Validation's ring, Ports' targets). Zoomed out each shape's outline is its skeleton's, with no standoff (its kind's `CustomSkeletonOutline`, else its kept geometry's; the shape being edited, drawn in full, keeps its own, stood off; a shape a few pixels across its bounds), a rect filled as a ring with square corners on the frame, since ⌘A there outlines every shape in view and stroking costs far more; read-only shapes (`registrar.readOnlyShapes { record in … }`, `editor.isReadOnly`, `registry.isReadOnly`, the rules' alone, over one record, and `registry.isReadOnly(_:in:)`, which `editor.isReadOnly` and editing ask: a shape in a read-only container, at any depth, is read-only too, and changes not at all, its own lock included, while nothing joins or leaves the container; a transaction doesn't change, restack, `translate` or delete a shape that was in the document before it and is read-only, unless the change leaves it no longer read-only, nor delete one whose board, or a board under it, holds a read-only shape, and what a deletion does to keep the board whole still goes through: its `willDelete` handlers' changes (a read-only dependent goes with its shape) and letting go of a deleted shape (`ShapeReferencing`); a rule reads only the record; `TranslatingSession` leaves them out of what moves, `beginEditing` refuses them, and services hear each refusal once it's done, `EditorService.didRefuseChanges`, a drag's once past the drag threshold); drawing offsets (`editor.setDrawingOffset(_:for:)`, view points: the shape and its outlines draw there, overlays that decorate it (adornments, the validation outline) follow with `context.worldDrawingOffset(of:)` (and may grow the rect they cull by with `context.worldDrawingReach`, the farthest any shape is drawn from where it is, as adornments do), and nothing else moves: hit testing, handles, the minimap; and the core's glide, `editor.glide(_:)`, shapes that just moved, by deltas in world units, eased there from where they were drawn over 250 ms, a little past and back, `Style.glideDuration` and `glideOvershoot`, on `editor.scheduler`, ended where it is by a new press, a session starting, a commit that touches the shape, or another board shown); skeletons (`docs/plugin-system.md`, "Skeletons": `editor.drawsSkeletons`, the camera's zoom under `skeletonZoom`, host SPI, 35% by `Style.skeletonZoom`, 0 drawing every shape in full at every zoom and above 1 skeletons at 100%, for tests; `editor.drawsSkeleton(of:)`, false for the shape being edited, drawn in full under its editing view; `OverlayContext.drawsSkeletons` and `drawsSkeleton(of:)`, decided once a frame; what overlays and hit testing that mirror what a shape shows go by, never a shape's geometry, which isn't told the level (`ShapeContext` has no `drawsSkeletons`), so hit testing, the marquee, arrows' ends, snapping and the minimap go by each shape as they always did; `EditorService.drawsSkeletonsDidChange(in:)`, only when the level changes: a zoom across it, the host moving it across the zoom, a board shown at the other level, after `boardDidChange`, never editing; a kind's `CustomSkeleton`, found once, `registry.customSkeletons` (and `CustomSkeletonOutline`, `registry.skeletonOutlines`), else the default, `RenderContext.drawSkeleton(of:color:)`, its kept geometry closed filled or open stroked a view point wide, in one gray, by `Silhouettes.form`'s rule for shapes a few device pixels across (under one, the whole device pixel its middle is in); `fillSkeletonText(_:)`, any `Sequence<CGRect>` of greeked-text bars in one translucent fill; `SkeletonText.columns(of:)`, how long text is greeked, in columns as a terminal counts them (2 for a CJK character or an emoji, 0 for a combining mark, a variation selector, a skin tone or a zero-width joiner, nothing for a scalar a joiner joins to the one before, else 1; one pass over its UTF-8), which every kind's bars go by); reads of `document` and `file` that observe nothing (`editor.withoutObservingDocument { … }`: for a service reading the file for a view that observes a revision of the service's, as Mentions' backlinks; never keep what it reads, since a copy of the file kept past a commit makes the next copy its tables); commands (`registrar.command`, `editor.perform`: what menu items run, Edit › Delete and Edit › Select All from `CanvasView`, Go › Back, Enclosing Board and Open Selection and Edit › Cut, Copy and Paste from `BoardWindowController`, wherever the keyboard is but in text, and Edit › Undo and Redo from `BoardWindow`, the core's `.undo` and `.redo`; View › Zoom In, Zoom Out, Actual Size, Zoom to Fit and Zoom to Selection, and the toolbar's zoom menu (`ZoomMenu`, CanvasUI, its items sent up the responder chain as the View menu's are), from `BoardWindowController` wherever the keyboard is, the core's `.zoomIn` and `.zoomOut` (the next of `Camera.zoomStops`, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 400 and 800%, past where the camera's headed, around the view's center, dimmed at the last), `.zoomToActualSize` (dimmed at 100%) and `.zoomToFit` (every shape, with a first visit's inset, as close as the zoom goes; dimmed on an empty board), each eased over 320 ms, tldraw's, and Selection's `.zoomToSelection`; `isEnabled:` and `editor.canPerform` enable a menu item, and a command's keys, and Edit › Delete and Select All are dimmed as they say); `editor.zoomPercentage` (the zoom where the camera's headed, in whole percent, set only when it changes, so the toolbar's menu, titled with it, redraws as the zoom changes, never as the camera pans); `editor.unobscuredViewport` (the view less what the host covers of its top on the board shown, `coveredTop`, host API: the path's pill inside a subboard, which the app hands `BoardConfiguration`), what a fit fits shapes into (a first visit, Zoom to Fit, Selection's `reveal`, `canvas draw`'s `--reveal` and its `view`); the secondary click's menu (`registrar.menuForSecondaryClick`); key strokes (`registrar.keyStrokes`, which Keyboard claims) and their releases (`registrar.keyReleases`, Keyboard's too: host API `editor.keyUp`, from `CanvasView.keyUp`, and `keysReleased()`, every key, as the canvas loses the keyboard or its window stops being key or a menu begins tracking, where no release would reach it; a key let go with ⌘ held, which NSApplication keeps from the window, reaches the canvas through a local monitor); the hand held (`editor.isHandHeld`, Space's: a press pans as the Hand's does, whatever the tool, which stays the tool, since `setTool` resets arrow targeting, with the open and closed hand, no hover meanwhile, a pan under way going on to its release; held during another press, it waits for that press, whose cursor stays, and at its release the tool hears the pointer leave) and the middle button's pan (host API `editor.panPointerDown`, from `CanvasView.otherMouseDown`, the same pan, editing going on; its drags and release through `pointerDragged` and `pointerUp`; released over a view the canvas hosts, the pointer leaves the canvas there, as a left press's does; a left press during it ends it and is the press from then on); the translation adjuster (`registrar.translationAdjuster { editor, start in … }`, which Snapping claims: where a `TranslatingSession` puts what it moves, a `TranslationAdjuster` made as the drag begins, given what moves, read-only shapes left out, and the press, and asked on each drag event, and again with the same pointer when held keys change, how far to move them, and told the axis ⇧ keeps the drag to, if it does (`TranslationAxis`: the one the pointer has come further along since the press, tldraw's; what moves stays where it began across it, whatever the adjuster says, and ⇧ pressed or let go mid-drag takes effect at once, adjuster or not); an axis it gives back as it came moves exactly as with none, the POC's `pointer.world − grabOffset`, and one it changes moves each box by exactly what it says from where it began; it draws with the session); `Tool.cancel`; `EditorService.documentDidChange` (with each commit's `ChangeSet`, whose `replacedDocument` tells `replaceDocument`, a board read again from disk, from an edit, and `restoredStep` an undo or redo) and `didPlace`; `boardWillChange` and `boardDidChange` ([showing a board](boards-in-a-file.md#showing-a-board)); `Session.hidesOutlines`, `draw(in:)` and `scrollsAtEdges` (tldraw's edge scrolling, `EdgeScrolling.swift`: once the pointer is past the drag threshold from the press, within 8 view points of an edge or past it for 200 ms, the camera pans that way a 16 ms frame at a time, eased in over 200 ms, up to 25 view points a 60 Hz frame at the edge and past it, 0.612 of that along a side of the view under 1,000 points; and the session hears a drag where the pointer is on every camera change, a scroll or pinch mid-drag too, without the velocity being sampled; `TranslatingSession`, a placement's, the marquee and a port's connection ask, the Hand and a handle's drag don't; unlike tldraw's, every edge scrolls within its 8 points, since a Mac's pointer goes past any window edge). `CanvasView` (CanvasUI) only turns NSEvents into editor calls and hosts the editing view and the minimap. Plugins can't import CanvasUI. A tool may let a press pass as though it never came (`Tool.ignoresPress`, asked first of every press: the camera goes on moving, editing goes on, no session starts), for the rest of a click it has already acted on.
