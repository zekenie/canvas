# The editor

What `CanvasEditor`, the headless core, lends every plugin: labels and symbols, the default tool, outlines and their standoff, read-only shapes, drawing offsets, the glide, skeleton levels, cards, commands and zoom, keys, the hand and the middle button, the translation adjuster, service and session hooks, edge scrolling.

Related: [plugins and kinds](plugins-and-kinds.md), [undo](undo.md), [editing](editing.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [keyboard](keyboard.md), [selection](selection.md), [snapping](snapping.md), [locking](locking.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [The editor, the document, and drawing](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-editor-the-document-and-drawing), [Outlines](../../docs/plugin-system.md#outlines), [Skeletons](../../docs/plugin-system.md#skeletons).

## Architecture

- Cards (the core's, generic: a plugin's view under a rect of the board; `Cards.swift`, [the guide](../../docs/plugin-system.md#cards)). `editor.showCard(CanvasCard(id:about:anchor:view:firstResponder:stepName:size:didClose:))` shows one `NSView`, a world rect it hangs from and a `size` the host asks for with the room in view points (the core imports AppKit types only, as `EditingView` and `popUpMenu` do); `editor.card`, `closeCard()`, `moveCard(to:)` (its row moved), `cardDidResize()`, `setCardStep(_:)`. One at a time: a second replaces the first, which hears `.replaced` before the new one shows, and the host (`editorCardDidChange`, defaulted) is told once. Refused (false, a card showing left as it is) for a shape not on the board shown or drawn as a skeleton (`drawsSkeleton(of:)`: the shape being edited is drawn in full, so its card shows at any zoom), and with no shape while `drawsSkeletons`. The editor closes it, each with its reason (`CanvasCardClosing`, heard by `didClose` last, after the host has taken it down and given the keyboard back): a press off it and off the rect it hangs from (`pointerDown` and the pans, `.pressedElsewhere`, before the press's step opens so a card's step closes at depth 0; a press on that rect is the plugin's to answer), `beginEditing` once past its guards (a refused try leaves the card) and `endEditing` first, while the editing view still stands (`.editing`), `show` and `replaceFile` (`.board`), `undo` and `redo` once past `canUndo` (`.undo`), the camera or `skeletonZoom` taking its shape to skeletons (`.skeleton`), a commit that removes its shape (`.shapeGone`, before services hear), and its rect out of `unobscuredViewport` after a camera change, a resize or `moveCard` (`.leftView`; an empty viewport closes nothing). A commit from elsewhere leaves it open. The step it holds is [undo](undo.md)'s. The host's part (`CanvasView`, `CardView`): `editorCardDidChange` puts the plugin's view in a `CardView` (the white rounded box and its shadow, the view clipped to it) over the editing view, the minimap and the zoom and under the popover (the minimap, when it shows after, goes under it), and gives `firstResponder` the keyboard (taken down, the keyboard goes back to the editing view's responder, else the canvas, if the card had it, after the field it was in ends its edit); `layoutCard`, on every frame, a camera change and a resize, puts it 4 points under the rect (`camera.toView(card.anchor)`), left edges together, or over it when it doesn't fit below and there is more room above, 8 points inside `unobscuredViewport`, `card.size` asked with the room (once for how tall it would be, once for the side it goes on, never smaller than 44 high), `CardStyle`'s constants and the popover's shadow. The box takes the pointer where its bubble is (`PointerPlace.card`: the tool hears it leave, once; the arrow, but where the content sets a cursor of its own), its `hitTest` is nil in the shadow's margin, and what the content didn't take stops in it (`keyDown`, `doCommand`, scrolls, pinches, presses, the canvas's context menu), its `nextResponder` is the canvas's own (set again whenever the canvas lays it out, since AppKit sets it as the view is added), so a menu's action sent to the first responder (Edit › Select All, Delete) never climbs into the canvas while Edit › Undo still reaches the window; a modifier change alone goes to the canvas. A local monitor for mouse-downs, on while a card shows and the canvas is in a window (`pressBeganInWindow`, which tests call; the monitor itself is seen only in the app, and by `NSApp.sendEvent`), closes it `.pressedElsewhere` for a press in this window outside the box and the rect it hangs from (the canvas, the editing view, a view the host put over the canvas, the panel), and returns the event, so the press goes on to what it landed on; a press in another window (a caret list) or on a menu is not this window's. `BoardWindow`'s `seal()` leaves a card's step. CanvasUITests' `CardViewTests` hold the host's part, CanvasTests' `CardTests` the rest, with CanvasTesting's `CardProbe`; BenchmarkTests' `CardBenchmarks` a pan's frame with one open (`Budget.cardFrame`).
- Menus from canvas buttons (the core's, generic). `editor.popUpMenu(_:below:)` (a canvas button's click, which is still inside its press's bookkeeping) queues one `NSMenu` and a world rect (`@_spi(Host) menuToPopUp`, `menuDidPopUp()`, `editorMenuToPopUpDidChange`); `CanvasView` pops it up on the next run-loop turn at the rect's bottom-left (`menuPresenter`, the test seam) and a press that begins (`pointerDown`, a pan) or a file put in place drops it. CanvasTests' `PopUpMenuTests` (with a stub delegate) and CanvasUITests' `MenuPopUpTests` hold it (a menu's tracking doesn't run in an unshown window: the queued menu through `menuPresenter`).
- `CanvasEditor` is the headless brain (document, camera, tools, sessions, input, drawing). What it lends plugins generically: what a kind is called and its symbol (`registry.label(of:)`: what the kind declares, `x.label(_:systemImage:)`, else its placing tool's label and symbol, else its id), which every place a shape is shown by its kind reads (the panel's header, rows, a board's name, mentions' words, undo names, a title's pill that asks for its symbol); a default tool (`registrar.defaultTool`, else Hand) that Escape and placing return to; `registrar.outlines`, stroked with the hint in one pass, over the faint ones (`Outlines.faintShapes`) and those over a plugin's tinted groups (`Outlines.tinted`: its own colors, a ring a line and a gap further out, 4 view points, for each group a shape is in past the first, and one ring further for a shape outlined in full too; an open shape of a kind drawn as a line, `StrokedShape`, covered rather than striped; `Outlines` is `Equatable`, so a plugin that keeps what it hands over asks for a frame only when it changed; an overlay that decorates outside an outline reads `OverlayContext.tintedOutset(of:)`), culled to the view ([drawing](drawing.md)), each closed shape's outline standing off its edge, the same for every kind (`editor.outlineStandoff`, host SPI: `Style.outlineStandoff`, 4 view points, divided by the zoom so it's constant on screen; 0 outlines each shape on its own path, as the POC did, and sets the POC goldens' boards drawn so: an intended deviation from the POC and from tldraw, whose outlines lie on the edge, made so that no kind's accent line is drawn over a border or shadow of its own and every kind reads alike): its `indicatorPath` grown outward by the gap, corners concentric, and the box around several (`Outlines.rects`) the same, so it holds their rings, while an open outline (an arrow's, a line's: its kept geometry isn't closed) has no inside and is stroked on its own path. A rect or a rounded rect, which every box kind's `indicatorPath` is, is added grown directly, a rounded rect of radius plus gap (a plain rect's corners are the gap's radius), costing what the plain outline did: the path is read in two passes over its end points with nothing made (`RoundedBox.of`: its straight edges lie on the sides of the box, the kept geometry's bounds, and leave the same room at both ends, which is the corner radius, whatever the corners' curves are made of); any other closed outline is grown by path arithmetic (`CGPath.grownOutward(by:isClosed:)`, far dearer than a rounded rect, and not kept from one frame to the next; debug builds' `outlinesGrownByArithmetic` counts them, and a test holds the shipped kinds to none). A plugin that decorates along an outline reads `editor.outlineGap` and `editor.outlinePath(of:)`, never the number (Validation's ring, Ports' targets). Zoomed out each shape's outline is its skeleton's, with no standoff (its kind's `CustomSkeletonOutline`, else its kept geometry's; the shape being edited, drawn in full, keeps its own, stood off; a shape a few pixels across its bounds), a rect filled as a ring with square corners on the frame, since ⌘A there outlines every shape in view and stroking costs far more; read-only shapes (`registrar.readOnlyShapes { record in … }`, `registry.isReadOnly`, the rules' alone, over one record; `registry.isReadOnly(_:in:)`, the rules and the containers on a shape's board, is internal, blind to a read-only board, so what goes by a shape asks `editor.isReadOnly` (the board shown), `editor.isReadOnly(inFile:)` (any board), `transaction.isReadOnly(_:)` or `BoardReading.isReadOnly(_:)`: a shape in a read-only container, at any depth, is read-only too, and changes not at all, its own lock included, while nothing joins or leaves the container; a board whose owner is read-only is read-only, and so is every board under it (`editor.isReadOnly(board:)`, `registry.isReadOnly(board:in:)` internal, climbing owners to the root: a locked subboard, one in a locked frame), its shapes as a read-only container's are, and nothing goes on it: `editor.isBoardReadOnly`, the board shown's, with `editor.readOnlyStart`, where its path turns read-only (`registry.readOnlyStart(ofPathTo:in:)`, one climb, so the path's crumbs are told in O(depth), not its square), set from the editor's init (one opened on such a board is read-only at once, a placing default tool giving way to the Hand) and as each switch and commit leaves the file (a lock above it can come in `transact(on:)`, or a file put in place), each only when it changes, so a view dimming what can't be done there redraws only then, a tool left only once the board is shown and heard; a transaction there (`transaction.isBoardReadOnly`, the editor's for its board; `transaction(on:)`'s over another board worked out climbing owners as the transaction leaves them, which `move(_:to:)`'s owner check, `holdsOwner`, asks too) drops an insert and notes the board's owner in `refusedShapes`, `place` puts nothing down and says so (`refuseChanges(to: [owner])`; `place(_:around:)`, all or nothing, hears the shapes it would hold refused, which shake there), and a tool that places shapes (a kind's, `registry.placingTools`) isn't enabled (`editor.isToolEnabled(_:)`: `setTool` doesn't take it, `switchTool` is the core's own for the default tool coming back as another board is shown, then left for the default or the Hand once the board is known read-only, as one in use is when the board becomes read-only); a transaction doesn't change, restack, `translate` or delete a shape that was in the document before it and is read-only, unless the change leaves it no longer read-only, nor delete one whose board, or a board under it, holds a read-only shape, and what a deletion does to keep the board whole still goes through: its `willDelete` handlers' changes (a read-only dependent goes with its shape; on any board of the file, `transaction.update(_:on:_:)`, in the delete's own step: [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md)) and letting go of a deleted shape (`ShapeReferencing`); a rule reads only the record; `TranslatingSession` leaves them out of what moves, `beginEditing` refuses them, and services hear each refusal once it's done, `EditorService.didRefuseChanges`, a drag's once past the drag threshold); drawing offsets (`editor.setDrawingOffset(_:for:)`, view points: the shape and its outlines draw there, overlays that decorate it (adornments, the validation outline) follow with `context.worldDrawingOffset(of:)` (and may grow the rect they cull by with `context.worldDrawingReach`, the farthest any shape is drawn from where it is, as adornments do), and nothing else moves: hit testing, handles, the minimap; and the core's glide, `editor.glide(_:)`, shapes that just moved, by deltas in world units, eased there from where they were drawn over 250 ms, a little past and back, `Style.glideDuration` and `glideOvershoot`, on `editor.scheduler`, ended where it is by a new press, a session starting, a commit that touches the shape, or another board shown); skeletons (`docs/plugin-system.md`, "Skeletons": `editor.drawsSkeletons`, the camera's zoom under `skeletonZoom`, host SPI, 35% by `Style.skeletonZoom`, 0 drawing every shape in full at every zoom and above 1 skeletons at 100%, for tests; `editor.drawsSkeleton(of:)`, false for the shape being edited, drawn in full under its editing view; `OverlayContext.drawsSkeletons` and `drawsSkeleton(of:)`, decided once a frame; what overlays and hit testing that mirror what a shape shows go by, never a shape's geometry, which isn't told the level (`ShapeContext` has no `drawsSkeletons`), so hit testing, the marquee, arrows' ends, snapping and the minimap go by each shape as they always did; `EditorService.drawsSkeletonsDidChange(in:)`, only when the level changes: a zoom across it, the host moving it across the zoom, a board shown at the other level, after `boardDidChange`, never editing; a kind's `CustomSkeleton`, found once, `registry.customSkeletons` (and `CustomSkeletonOutline`, `registry.skeletonOutlines`), else the default, `RenderContext.drawSkeleton(of:color:)`, its kept geometry closed filled or open stroked a view point wide, in one gray, by `Silhouettes.form`'s rule for shapes a few device pixels across (under one, the whole device pixel its middle is in); `fillSkeletonText(_:)`, any `Sequence<CGRect>` of greeked-text bars in one translucent fill; `SkeletonText.columns(of:)`, how long text is greeked, in columns as a terminal counts them (2 for a CJK character or an emoji, 0 for a combining mark, a variation selector, a skin tone or a zero-width joiner, nothing for a scalar a joiner joins to the one before, else 1; one pass over its UTF-8), which every kind's bars go by); the text selected in the editing view (`editor.editingSelectedText`, while a shape is edited: the host's, host API `CanvasEditorDelegate.editorEditingSelectedText`, which `CanvasView` answers from an editing view that's text, each attachment in it that adopts `ReadsAsText` (a core protocol, `var readsAs: String`: a mention's pill) as what it reads as and any other attachment dropped, `NSAttributedString.readingAttachments(in:)`; nil headless): what Find's ⌘F seeds its query with; reads of `document` and `file` that observe nothing (`editor.withoutObservingDocument { … }`: for a service reading the file for a view that observes a revision of the service's, as Mentions' backlinks; never keep what it reads, since a copy of the file kept past a commit makes the next copy its tables); commands (`registrar.command`, `editor.perform`: what menu items run, Edit › Delete and Edit › Select All from `CanvasView`, Go › Back, Enclosing Board and Open Selection and Edit › Cut, Copy and Paste from `BoardWindowController`, wherever the keyboard is but in text, and Edit › Undo and Redo from `BoardWindow`, the core's `.undo` and `.redo`; View › Zoom In, Zoom Out, Actual Size, Zoom to Fit and Zoom to Selection, from `BoardWindowController` wherever the keyboard is, the core's `.zoomIn` and `.zoomOut` (the next of `Camera.zoomStops`, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 400 and 800%, past where the camera's headed, around the view's center, dimmed at the last), `.zoomToActualSize` (dimmed at 100%) and `.zoomToFit` (every shape, with a first visit's inset, `CanvasEditor.firstVisitInset`, host API, which a picture of a board fits with too, as close as the zoom goes; dimmed on an empty board), each eased over 320 ms, tldraw's, and Selection's `.zoomToSelection`; `isEnabled:` and `editor.canPerform` enable a menu item, and a command's keys, and Edit › Delete and Select All are dimmed as they say); `editor.zoomPercentage` (the zoom where the camera's headed, in whole percent, set only when it changes: what the canvas shows above the minimap while it changes, `ZoomIndicator` ([drawing](drawing.md)); the app has no zoom menu, since pinching and ⌘-scrolling are how it zooms); `editor.unobscuredViewport` (the view less what the host covers of its top on the board shown, `coveredTop`, host API: the path's pill inside a subboard, which the app hands `BoardConfiguration`), what a fit fits shapes into (a first visit, Zoom to Fit, Selection's `reveal`, `canvas draw`'s `--reveal` and its `view`); the secondary click's menu (`registrar.menuForSecondaryClick`); key strokes (`registrar.keyStrokes`, which Keyboard claims) and their releases (`registrar.keyReleases`, Keyboard's too: host API `editor.keyUp`, from `CanvasView.keyUp`, and `keysReleased()`, every key, as the canvas loses the keyboard or its window stops being key or a menu begins tracking, where no release would reach it; a key let go with ⌘ held, which NSApplication keeps from the window, reaches the canvas through a local monitor); the hand held (`editor.isHandHeld`, Space's: a press pans as the Hand's does, whatever the tool, which stays the tool, since `setTool` resets arrow targeting, with the open and closed hand, no hover meanwhile, a pan under way going on to its release; held during another press, it waits for that press, whose cursor stays, and at its release the tool hears the pointer leave) and the middle button's pan (host API `editor.panPointerDown`, from `CanvasView.otherMouseDown`, the same pan, editing going on; its drags and release through `pointerDragged` and `pointerUp`; released over a view the canvas hosts, the pointer leaves the canvas there, as a left press's does; a left press during it ends it and is the press from then on); the translation adjuster (`registrar.translationAdjuster { editor, start in … }`, which Snapping claims: where a `TranslatingSession` puts what it moves, a `TranslationAdjuster` made as the drag begins, given what moves, read-only shapes left out, and the press, and asked on each drag event, and again with the same pointer when held keys change, how far to move them, and told the axis ⇧ keeps the drag to, if it does (`TranslationAxis`: the one the pointer has come further along since the press, tldraw's; what moves stays where it began across it, whatever the adjuster says, and ⇧ pressed or let go mid-drag takes effect at once, adjuster or not); an axis it gives back as it came moves exactly as with none, the POC's `pointer.world − grabOffset`, and one it changes moves each box by exactly what it says from where it began; it draws with the session); `Tool.cancel`; `EditorService.documentDidChange` (with each commit's `ChangeSet`, whose `replacedDocument` tells `replaceDocument`, a board read again from disk, from an edit, and `restoredStep` an undo or redo) and `didPlace`; `boardWillChange` and `boardDidChange` ([showing a board](boards-in-a-file.md#showing-a-board)); `Session.hidesOutlines`, `draw(in:)` and `scrollsAtEdges` (tldraw's edge scrolling, `EdgeScrolling.swift`: once the pointer is past the drag threshold from the press, within 8 view points of an edge or past it for 200 ms, the camera pans that way a 16 ms frame at a time, eased in over 200 ms, up to 25 view points a 60 Hz frame at the edge and past it, 0.612 of that along a side of the view under 1,000 points; and the session hears a drag where the pointer is on every camera change, a scroll or pinch mid-drag too, without the velocity being sampled; `TranslatingSession`, a placement's, the marquee and a port's connection ask, the Hand and a handle's drag don't; unlike tldraw's, every edge scrolls within its 8 points, since a Mac's pointer goes past any window edge). `CanvasView` (CanvasUI) only turns NSEvents into editor calls and hosts the editing view, the minimap and the zoom above it. Plugins can't import CanvasUI. A tool may let a press pass as though it never came (`Tool.ignoresPress`, asked first of every press: the camera goes on moving, editing goes on, no session starts), for the rest of a click it has already acted on.
- A geometry that reads what no commit changes (the names a rename gave shapes on another board) has the editor lay it out again without one, through a layout input (a `LayoutInputKey`) that is either set or provided, one or the other for a key (`editor.setLayoutInput` asserts the key has no provider). Set: `editor.setLayoutInput(key, value, for: id)` sets what `context.layoutInput(key, for: id)` reads (the plugin that sets it owns it and keeps it up to date; kept for the session, written nowhere). Provided: `registrar.layoutInput(key) { record, reading in … }` (one provider a key: `CorePoints.LayoutProviders`, a second is a conflict, `registry.layoutProviders`) answers `context.layoutInput(key, of: record)` for a shape nothing set a value for, and nil for one with none; it is handed the record and a `LayoutReading`: `shape(inFile:)` (any shape of the file as whoever asks sees it), `service(_:)` (the editor's services) and `isCommitted` (true for the editor's own reading; false mid-transaction, when what a service keeps is of the file before, so answer from `shape(inFile:)`). Answer from the record and the reading alone, and cheaply; the provider's owner says when its answer changes for a reason other than the shape's own record, with `editor.shapesDidChangeLayout(ids)`, which lets go of the kept geometry of those shapes and of what looked them up, on the board shown and those kept ready, moves `editor.layoutRevision` (what keeps something worked out from geometry keys it by this beside `revision`) and redraws; the minimap hears it as a commit's damage. The trap: while a commit's services are being told, `isCommitted` is true and the file is the new one, but a service that hasn't heard yet still keeps the file before, so a provider that answers from a service's kept state can be asked too early by a service that hears first. The owner's announcement, after it hears the commit, must name every shape its change touches, shapes the same commit added or changed included. A provider's reads of other shapes are not tracked by the shape index: the owner announces. No transaction, no undo step, nothing edited. The editor's own contexts (`editor.shapeContext`, `reading(of:)`) and a transaction's `shapes` read inputs (`ShapeContext.layout`, a `LayoutSource`: the set values, the editor's services and a lookup of the file); a plain `ShapeContext(document:registry:)` and a board preview's lay nothing out and ask no provider. A transaction's `shapes` is the file as the transaction leaves it: a provider is handed what it inserted, changed and moved (`record(inFile:)`; nil for what it deleted), and a value set for a shape is read for one it has not changed (or only moved), so an arrow it only moves is measured as it is on screen, and reads nil for one it added or changed, as the value was made from the record as it was. Its one user so far is Mentions' `MentionLayout.names` (set; [mentions](mentions.md), [arrows](arrows.md)); CanvasTests' `shapeIndexTests` (set) and `laidOutTests` (provided, with `Gauged` and `Spacer`) hold it. A box kind may be laid out larger than it stores (`LaidOutBox`, one requirement, `laidOutSize(of:in:)`, answered from the shape's record but for where it is, and from its layout inputs): the laid-out frame has the stored origin and is the larger of each side against the stored box (a size that isn't finite counts as the box), so it always holds the stored box and a move never changes it. `context.frame(of:)` (by id, by record, by typed record; `record.box` is what is stored and what a write changes) is the one read for everything that asks how much room a shape takes, and a `LaidOutBox` kind's geometry, indicator path and editing frame default to it, so the readers that go through geometry follow with no edit; a container can't be one (the registry refuses `ShapeContaining` and `LaidOutBox` together), so a container's edge is always its box. The core keeps each laid-out size by shape id for the whole file (`LaidOutSizes`; asked once, when first needed): a commit lets go of the sizes of the shapes it added, removed or changed other than by moving (`ChangeSet.moved`; a commit that only moved shapes costs one comparison) and of those it names in `elsewhere`, before the index and the services hear it, `shapesDidChangeLayout` lets go of those named, and a file put in place of all, so a drag, an undo of a move and a board switch ask no kind and no provider. Which contexts lay out: the editor's (the committed file), a transaction's `shapes` (a shape it added or changed, or put on another board, is asked afresh with the file as the transaction leaves it and kept by that context alone; one it didn't touch, or only moved, reads the size kept before the transaction (one the editor has kept none for, a fresh board's or just after its owner said the layout changed, is asked against the transaction's file, and so sees an uncommitted change to its input), so paste, duplicate, draw and `/is-a` settle by the laid-out frame before they commit) and `shapes.layingOut(with: records, removing: ids)` (a run's, `removing` the shapes it deletes, read as not there: the records are read as shapes of the file and laid out afresh whatever is kept for their ids, as is anything the context it is called on reads as changed (a transaction's), and nothing is kept or touched; draw's, for a script's shapes); a plain `ShapeContext(document:registry:)` and a board preview's are as stored, with nothing asked. `editor.frame(inFile:)` is the laid-out frame of a shape on any board the editor can show (a board not shown is laid out without its geometry being worked out). A context is a snapshot of its transaction: two rules for a loop in one. Don't hold a context across a change (it holds the transaction's change sets, so the first change made while it is alive copies them): read what the loop needs from one context in a scope of its own, or as a temporary, then change. A size doesn't change by moving: a loop that moves shapes as it goes reads each one's laid-out size once, before it starts, and puts that size at the record's origin as it is by then. `editor.layoutRevision` is what a plugin that keeps something worked out from geometry keys it by, beside `revision` (Selection's kept bounds). A layout change is no commit and no step, and nothing is stored. CanvasTesting's `StretchesPlugin` (a `Stretch` laid out by the `Gauge` it names, with `StretchLog`) stands in for a typed note in tests.
- A transaction turns a shape into another kind (`transaction.turn(_:into:)`, with a record, or props): the record's kind and props in place of its own, the same shape otherwise, so whatever binds or names it, its place in the stack, its container, its metadata and the added values both kinds hold (a title) stay, and the record's added values (defaults, or what a placement set) fill in the rest (a value kept aside goes with it); a kind with no box leaves the container (only a box shape is in one). False, and nothing changes, for a shape not on the board or kept aside, a frame that isn't finite, and a read-only shape or one in a read-only container (refused as `update` refuses it, so services hear it); the same record is no change.
