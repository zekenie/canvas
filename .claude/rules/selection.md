## The selection, opening, resizing and Back

- Selection (a surface, after Keyboard in the shipped list) is built on those hooks alone: the selection state (a service, read as `editor.selectedShapeIDs` and `editor.selection`, the only selected shape, as tldraw's onlySelectedShape), the Select tool (the default) and marquee, handles (a kind's own, `HandleProviding`, and a resizable kind's, below), Delete/Return/⌘A/Back (their keys registered with Keyboard; Delete enabled while something's selected, a locked shape too, which shakes, ⌘A while the board has shapes), the arrow keys' nudge (1 world unit, 10 with ⇧, `nudgeSelection(by:)`, a `translate` in one "Move", no snapping, again and again while held, one undo step, while something's selected and nothing's under way or edited; a locked shape stays, refused, and shakes), Zoom to Selection (⇧2, `reveal` of the selection) and a trackpad's smart zoom (`.smartMagnify`, a two-finger double-tap: `reveal` of the shape under the pointer, where the gesture was, `editor.pointerLocated(at:)` (host API), since a window that isn't key hears no moves; and the next, while the camera's still there, back), and the double-click claim. Opening a shape is Selection's too: `x.opens("Open") { shape, editor in … }` (the `ShapeOpening` point; `registrar.opens(Kind.self, …)` for another plugin's kind) says what opening a kind's shape does (a board-owning kind shows its board); a double-click on a shape, Return, Enter and ⌘↓ (`.openSelection`, enabled while `editor.canOpen` the only selected shape and nothing is being edited, as ⌘↓ is then the text's; Return and Enter always take the key) run `editor.open(id)` (a double-click's `open(id, at:clickCount:)`, from where it's pressed): the kind's opener, else `beginEditing` for an `Editable` one (a read-only one counts as openable, so its refusal shakes; an opener runs whatever the lock). Resizing is Selection's too, a first for the app: a box kind opts in with `x.resizable(minimumSize:)` (the `ShapeResizing` point, one per kind, its minimum above zero and finite; `registrar.resizable(K.self, minimumSize:)` for another plugin's kind), and the only selected shape of such a kind, unless it's read-only (`editor.isReadOnly`) or being edited, has eight handles (`ResizeHandle`: its box's corners and the middles of its sides, on the box itself, drawn over its outline, which stands off the edge 4 points: the line runs under them, as it does the kind's own, and nothing about them moved with the standoff; after its kind's own), in the handles' look, size and hit radius, hovered (`hoveredHandle`) and pressed as any, none on a box under 24 view points either way and a side's only on a side at least 48 long (`SelectionStyle.resizeHandleRoom`), so the shape keeps room to be pressed; past the drag threshold, `ResizingSession` moves that corner or side with the pointer from where it was pressed, the opposite one staying put, a transaction a drag event and the release (its `end`), the press one undo step, "Resize"; ⇧ keeps the proportions the box had (a corner by the side that grows the most, a side's across it about the middle), ⌥ keeps its center, both taking effect as they're pressed or let go; never below the kind's minimum, and never inside out (a side dragged past the opposite one stops there); nothing snaps (no translation adjuster); Escape puts the box back, leaving no step. Over a resize handle, and while it's pressed and dragged, the cursor is its `CanvasCursor.resize(_:)` (the core's, by place on the box), which the app shows as macOS 15's frame-resize cursor, before it the left-right and up-down arrows on a side and the crosshair on a corner; the open hand stays over a kind's own handles. `editor.goBack()` is data-canvas's selection history: each change of the selection stacks the one it leaves, with its board and the camera as it was then (`editor.cameraTarget`, where it was headed if it was gliding; 50 steps; a select-tool press and its drag are one step; `canGoBack` only when going back would change the selection or the board); showing another board is a step too, even with nothing selected (Selection's `boardDidChange`: nothing is selected on arrival, not a step of its own, and the press, hover, handle and canvas buttons are let go), but not a file put in place, which no one took (`BoardChange.isFileReplaced`: a file opened on the board left open, a revert), and going back to a step on another board shows it, selects there and sets the camera (not animated), skipping a board that can't be shown any more; `editor.showBoard(_:selecting:)` switches and selects as one step (on the board shown, it only selects); the detail panel's header has the back button, and Go › Back (⌘[) works whatever has the keyboard. Every kind is selectable unless it opts out (`x.unselectable()`, or `x.selectable { ... }` per shape); the select tool sees through such shapes, nothing else does. A `LabeledShape` (Selection's capability, an `Editable` kind with text on part of it, `labelFrame(of:in:)`: an arrow's label) is edited by a click on the only selected shape only on its label, and a drag from its label, past the threshold, is its `labelDragSession`'s (the shape selected alone, ⇧ adding it), not the selection's; a read-only shape's is the selection's, refused. The shape being edited shows no handles (its editing view is over them, as tldraw's). A read-only shape (a locked one) is selected, hovered and outlined as any other, but shows no handles, so a press there presses the shape. A plugin can put buttons on the canvas (the `CanvasButtons` point: where they are, in view points, and what they do), clicked (`registrar.canvasButtons(at:click:)`, Adornments' clickable adornments: the lock chip) or dragged (`registrar.canvasButtons(at:drag:)`, Ports' dots): in the select tool only, they win over handles and shapes (the later plugin's first, as its overlay draws on top); a press and a release on a clicked one click it (dragged off, nothing), with the pointing hand over one (`CanvasCursor.pointingHand`); a dragged one's press, past the drag threshold, hands the drag to the session `drag` returns (given the button and where it was pressed, world units; nil ends the press), and released without a drag does nothing, with the open hand over one and the closed hand while it's pressed; Escape lets go of either. `editor.hoveredButton` and `editor.pressedButton` say what to draw hovered and pressed (both cleared as a drag hands over), a plugin whose buttons change without a commit calls `editor.canvasButtonsDidChange()`, and `editor.pointerOverCanvas` is where the pointer is over the canvas in the select tool (view points, as it last moved with nothing under way; nil once it's left the canvas or gone over the minimap or the editing view, another tool is current, or Escape let go of a dragged button's press, until it moves again), for buttons that show only while it's near (a port's dots). Something drawn for a shape outside it (`registrar.decorationHits`, the `DecorationHits` point: every adornment) is that shape for the select tool (a press, a drag, the release, a double-click), hover and the context menu: `editor.selectableShape(at:)` asks it first, the last plugin's first, with `isSelectable` as its `include`, so an unselectable shape's is seen through; buttons still come before it, and the core's `shape(at:)`, the marquee and an arrow's end never see it. Unlike tldraw (and the POC), the selection stays outlined while it moves. `editor.hovered` is the shape the pointer is over, set by the select tool on the canvas or by any plugin where it shows shapes (the table of contents' rows), in any tool; changing tools clears it. `editor.isHovered(id)` is observed per shape (a key path per shape), so a view of one shape hears only that shape's hover change. Its outline is faint: wider and lighter than the selection's (2.5 points, the accent at 60%), unlike tldraw's, which strokes a hover like the selection, and stands off a closed shape's edge as the selection's does (`outlineStandoff`), so the two are one line in one place. As the canvas starts or stops drawing skeletons (`drawsSkeletonsDidChange`), Selection looks again where a still pointer rests, in the select tool with nothing under way, by the lookup a move uses (`SelectionState.hover(atViewPoint:)`: a canvas button, else a handle, else the shape or the shape a face names), buttons first, so a canvas button that comes or goes with the level (a lock chip, shown only in full) takes or gives up the hover, and no plugin calls `canvasButtonsDidChange()` for the level. `editor.selectionBounds` is kept, by the selection's revision, `editor.revision` and the camera (any commit or camera change works it out again, since geometry may follow unselected shapes or read the camera: a pan's frame makes a pass over the selection's kept bounds), which saves each read of ⌘A's box (the canvas's frame, the minimap's, a hover's `isInSelectionBox`) a pass over the selection, though the selected shapes' outlines still visit every one each frame (`selectionBoundsWorkedOut`, debug builds). Code that reads the selection imports Selection; without its plugin, the selection is empty.

## Refusing a change

- `RefusalShake` (a public `EditorService` of `SelectionPlugin`, `Sources/Selection/RefusalShake.swift`) answers the core's one refusal signal, `didRefuseChanges` (`editor.refuseChanges(to:)`, which a transaction, a drag and editing send for read-only shapes), whoever's rule made them read-only: it shakes each shape tried through `editor.setDrawingOffset` (`SelectionStyle.shakeAmplitude` 5 view points side to side, `shakeCycles` 3 over `shakeDuration` 360 ms, dying away, `shakeFrame` 16 ms on `editor.scheduler`; `RefusalShake.offset(at:)` is the curve, `isShaking` says whether any shape is). A try while a shape shakes doesn't start it over; every refused shape on the board shown shakes (a shape whose board holds a refused shape, which isn't deleted either, is the one tried), and a container shakes with what it holds, at any depth (`document.descendants(of:containingKinds:)`), as it moves with it. Another board shown stops what's under way (it isn't kept per board), and a deleted shape stops shaking. A plugin whose rule makes shapes read-only (Locking's lock, Publishing's hold) installs nothing for the shake; a plugin list with `SelectionPlugin` has it. RefusalShakeTests (SelectionTests, on CanvasTesting's `FreezingPlugin` and `Frozen`, never Locking) hold the timing and curve, that any refusal shakes (a drag, a plugin's own `refuseChanges`) but a click doesn't, not starting over, containers, and the board change and deletion.

## Going to shapes

- Going to shapes is Selection's, one way for every plugin: `editor.reveal(ids)` only moves the camera (centers the shapes' bounds in `unobscuredViewport`, below the path over a subboard's canvas, fitted with 100 points of margin, no closer than 120%, over 200 ms: `SelectionStyle.reveal*`, data-canvas's zoomToBounds), and is what selecting table-of-contents rows does. `editor.goTo(ids)` is following a link (a mention): it ends editing, selects the selectable ones (a Back step only when that changes the selection; to what's selected already, or to unselectable shapes, it only moves the camera), and reveals unless they're all in view, where the camera is headed (`cameraTarget`), at a readable zoom: `SelectionStyle.readableZoom` (70%: a note's 16-point text reads at 11.2 points there, just over `NSFont.smallSystemFontSize`), or a kind's own, closer, for text set smaller (`x.readableZoom(_:)`, `registrar.readableZoom(K.self, _:)`, Selection's `ReadableZoom` point, above zero and at most 120%, `ReadableZoom.maximum`, as close as `reveal` brings shapes, `editor.readableZoom(of:)`: 11-point text reads at 100%; no shipped kind says one; the closest of the shapes' kinds counts), or as close as fitting them would bring them (a shape too big for 70% that's all in view stays put). The shapes may be on any board of the file: it goes to the board of the first of them on a board it can show, and to those of them there, showing that board and selecting them as one Back step (`showBoard(_:selecting:)`), then revealing them. `reveal` is the board shown's alone. Nothing mid-gesture, or when none of the shapes is on a board it can show. `editor.goInto(id)` is following a link into what it names (a mention of a subboard): `goTo`, then `open` there (`canOpen`), as one Back step to where it was followed from, whatever boards it went through (`SelectionState.asOneStep`: nothing inside records a step of its own); nothing for a shape whose own board is shown. A way around that takes many calls (Find's stepping, board to board) is one Back step too: `editor.navigationStart` (a `NavigationStart`: the board, the selection and the camera where it's headed) taken as it begins, and each move inside `editor.navigate(from:_:)`, which records nothing of its own; the first call that moves the board, the selection or the camera puts the start on the history, once (`SelectionState.asOneStep(from:_:)`; a later call finds it on the stack and adds none, even after a click or a pan between; a call that moves nothing adds none).

## Faces

- Faces (Selection's `ShapeFaces` point, `registrar.shapeFaces(at:click:doubleClick:hovering:)`) are parts of a shape's content that answer a click without taking the shape's press: a mention's pill in a note, a link, a checkbox on a card. `at` is asked only for a plain press (no ⇧, ⌘ or ⌥, not a Control-click) on the shape the select tool would press, selected or not, at a world point on that shape itself (a point on what `DecorationHits` draws for a shape is no face's), after canvas buttons and handles, and looks at that one shape; the last plugin's first. Never on a shape drawn as a skeleton (`editor.drawsSkeleton(of:)`, zoomed out), which shows none of its content: there a click, a double-click (which edits an `Editable` one from where it's pressed, then drawn in full, and is a refused edit on a read-only one) and the pointer are the shape's, and a lone box follows the pointer at once; as the level changes, Selection's `drawsSkeletonsDidChange` looks again under a still pointer, so a face's hovered target gives way to its shape; a face pressed in full whose shape is a skeleton by the release (a pinch past 35% mid-press) isn't clicked: the release is the shape's, as a canvas button gone by the release isn't clicked (`PointingShapeSession.end` checks `drawsSkeleton(of:)` again). The press is the shape's: it selects the shape as any press does and, past the drag threshold, moves the selection from the press; on a lone box it waits for the threshold too (an intended deviation from the lone box's press, pinned in SelectionTests). Released within the threshold, the shape is selected alone and `click` runs; Escape lets go first. A double-click whose press lands on one selects the shape alone and runs `doubleClick` in place of editing it, a read-only shape's too; without `doubleClick`, a double-click there edits an `Editable` shape and is otherwise one more click. The pointer over one hovers the shape `hovering` names (`editor.hovered`, so hover keeps one path), or its own shape when it names none, or one that isn't there or can't be selected; hover asks whatever keys are held, and the cursor stays the arrow. `click` runs once the press is over, so it may `goTo` or go back, and what it selects is a Back step of its own; it isn't given the press, so a face that needs a point carries it in its value (a pill edits its note from there, `editor.beginEditing(id, at:)`), and a click that begins editing leaves the pointer over the editing view, which ends the hover. CompositionTests' canvas view tests hold these pieces together with `pillsPlugin`, a pill-like face in a note: edited from the pill on a click, not hovered under its text, and gone to (`goTo`) from a locked note's double-click; Mentions' pills are faces built on them. A face that edits its shape checks `editor.isReadOnly` first (as `pillsPlugin` does): a refused edit is a try, which Selection shakes, and a click on a locked shape isn't one. A click on a face that goes somewhere (it changes the selection or moves the camera: a link's `goTo`) makes the rest of its double-click its own: the select tool ignores each press after it with a click count of 2 or more, until one of 1 (`ignoresPress`), so the camera goes on to where the click sent it and nothing is placed, edited or pressed where it has got to; a click that goes nowhere (a pill's) leaves the double-click to `doubleClick`.
