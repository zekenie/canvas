# Palette

Palette, the core's color math: a color as sRGB (`RGB`) and as lightness, chroma and hue (`OKLCH`), the seven roles one color is drawn in on the light canvas and the dark (`Swatch`, each a `Tone`), contrast, and the one test for a dark appearance and the one maker of a color that follows it.

Related: [build](build.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [neighbors](neighbors.md), [types](types.md), [adornments](adornments.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Palette](../../docs/plugin-system.md#palette).

## Architecture

- Palette (a core module that depends on no other of the project's, so it knows no canvas, registry, plugin, kind or named color; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Palette") is what any module makes colors with: pure, nonisolated and `Sendable` but for its AppKit file. No public type may be named `Palette` (the audit). It is the first landing of the color module (the working doc "Color"): the names, the property a shape holds and where a color is picked are a horizontal's, `Colors`, built on it and not here.
- `RGB` is a color as its sRGB components, 0 to 1, with no alpha (a faint one is mixed, `over(_:alpha:)`, or faded as it's drawn): `init(hex: 0x4465E9)`, `init?(hexString:)` (`#4465E9` or `#46E`, either case; nil for a name, a value with an alpha, any other length, a non-ASCII digit), `hexString` (six digits, capitals, each component rounded to its nearest step and held to 0 to 1), `luminance` (WCAG's relative luminance, sRGB's curve undone at 0.04045) and `contrast(with:)` (WCAG's ratio, 1 to 21, the same either way round), which every floor here is in.
- `OKLCH` (Oklab in polar form: `lightness` 0 to 1, `chroma`, `hue` in radians) is the space a color's variations are made in, since moving its lightness alone keeps its hue, where moving sRGB's components or HSL's lightness turns it. `OKLCH(rgb)` and `.rgb` are Ottosson's matrices; `.rgb` of a color sRGB can't show (`isDisplayable`) is the most colorful color of the same lightness and hue (the chroma halved toward it 32 times), never the components clipped, which would turn the hue; a lightness outside 0 to 1 is black or white.
- A `Tone` is a color in the light appearance and in the dark (`light`, `dark`, `Tone(color)` for the same in both, `tone[dark: isDark]`).
- A `Swatch` is the seven roles one color is drawn in, each a `Tone`, made by `Swatch(color, on: canvas)` (the canvas defaults to `Swatch.canvas`: gray 0.965 light and 0.11 dark, the core's `Style.canvasBackground` as the contrast math takes it) the same way for every color: `solid` (a line, a symbol, a fill that holds text: the color itself where it reads on the canvas at `lineContrast`, 3, else the nearest lighter or darker color of its hue that does), `onSolid` (text on it: white, or black where white is under `textContrast`, 4.5; one of the two always reads), `wash` (a faint surface: the solid at `washStrength`, 14%, over the canvas, the strength of a type's light chip), `surface` (a large surface that holds shapes, a region or a card: the solid at `surfaceStrength`, 5%, over `Swatch.raised`, white in the light and gray 0.15 in the dark, a step up from the canvas as a panel is; another `raised` may be handed in), `ink` (text in the color on the canvas, its wash or its surface: the solid moved until it reads on all three at 4.5), `paper` (a note's sheet: the hue at `paperLightness`, 0.905, with at most `paperChroma`, 0.07, the same in both appearances, since paper stays paper in the dark and the inks drawn on a note must read on every one) and `paperInk` (a note's ink, gray 0.12). A floor is met by construction: `Swatch.reading(_:against:atLeast:)` halves the gap between the color's lightness and black (on light grounds) or white (on dark ones) until it finds the nearest lightness that reads, against a floor 2% over the one asked so the color still meets it rounded to 8 bits; where nothing reads (a mid-gray ground asked for 21) it is black or white.
- AppKit (`Appearance.swift`): `NSAppearance.isDark` (`bestMatch(from: [.aqua, .darkAqua]) == .darkAqua`, so vibrant and high-contrast dark appearances are dark) is the one test for a dark appearance, and a module that needs it imports Palette: Canvas, CanvasUI, Selection, Neighbors, Connections, Types, StickyNotes, Subboards, Frames and References each had a copy. `NSColor(light: RGB, dark: RGB)` is a color that follows the appearance it's drawn in (resolved as it's drawn: read its `cgColor` in the drawing), `NSColor(rgb)` and `NSColor(tone)` the others, and `rgb.cgColor` an opaque sRGB `CGColor` for a drawing that knows its appearance. A style enum's dynamic color whose two values aren't opaque sRGB (a white at an alpha, a system color) still writes `NSColor(name: nil) { $0.isDark ? … : … }`. `color.fading(to: strength)` is a color at an opacity that keeps following the appearance (its alpha replaced, resolved as it's drawn), where a system color's own `withAlphaComponent` is fixed to the appearance current as it's made: the dark's white at 15%, made while the app was dark, is nothing on a light ground (a rule's chip asked about was, LanguageRules'); `NSColor.resolved(in:_:)` makes a color as it is in an appearance. References fades its labels, badges and symbols with it, and LanguageRules its chips' tints.
- Neighbors' `NeighborLight.color(light:dark:)` is `NSColor(light:dark:)` over its two `0xRRGGBB` values.

## Testing

- PaletteTests (TestKit alone; `sampleColors`: a grid of sRGB's cube, corners and grays included, and ten colors a person would pick): `rgbTests` (hex written and read, what isn't one, luminance and contrast against WCAG's and WebAIM's figures, `over`), `oklchTests` (white, black and Ottosson's red; every sample back as itself; grays have no chroma; a color too colorful shown at its lightness and hue, as colorful as sRGB has, every 15° at four lightnesses), `swatchTests` (a color that reads is its own solid; yellow darkened on the light canvas and navy lightened on the dark, to just over 3; every role of every sample at its floor on both canvases once rounded to 8 bits; text on a solid white or black; ink on its surface, which is the solid at 5% over the raised ground; paper one sheet in both appearances at its lightness, under its chroma cap, its luminance between 0.70 and 0.80 and a note's ink on it at 12 or more; the roles within 1.5° of the color's hue; the canvas handed in; black or white where nothing reads) and `appearanceTests` (which appearances are dark; a two-valued color resolved in each; a color faded, two-valued or a system color's, itself at that opacity in each appearance whichever was current as it was made; the components an `NSColor` and a `CGColor` hold).
