# Rows written as lines

LineRows (`Sources/LineRows`, platform, a horizontal a kind opts into): a shape's rows written a line each (a table's columns, an enum's values): the line field and the grammar a role's lines are read and printed in, what fits at the caret, the session's stops, keys and commits, the waiting line, hops to the next shape of the kind, the light an offer puts on the board, and what VoiceOver hears.

Related: [editing](editing.md), [text editing](text-editing.md), [titles](titles.md), [keyboard navigation](keyboard-navigation.md), [undo](undo.md), [shape references](shape-references.md), [engineering](engineering.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Rows written as lines](../../docs/plugin-system.md#rows-written-as-lines). The plan is the owner's doc "Engineering kit" (https://claude.ai/artifact/7bkKR1idCbPUmrZT8FVXBf); this landed as its K2, "Write columns".

## Architecture

- **The module** knows no table: a kind gives its model and its grammars. On Canvas, TextEditing (the field, the caret list), FuzzyMatching (the list's ranking), Selection (Tab's hops) and Titles (the title stop's field). Two parts (`Package.swift`'s table): `Field` (the line field and the grammar protocol, readings, looks, offers, the no-loss law) and `Session` (`LineModel`, the editing view, the service; uses Field). Its own source is the plugin (`LineRowsPlugin`, `line-rows`, shipped after Draw, Find, Connections, Selection and Titles and before the kinds that opt in): the service, the light's outline, and the key hints ("↩ in a Row", "⌥↑ ⌥↓ in a Row", "⌘⌫ then ⌫ in a Row", "⇧↩ in a Row", `.text`). Only Engineering imports it (Clipboard will, for rows on the pasteboard).
- **Opting in.** `x.lineRows(model)` (a `LineModel` whose `Props` are the kind's) contributes to the keyed point `LineRowsKinds`, one a kind, its owner LineRows; `ShapeCapability.lineRows` ("written as lines") scopes LineRows' faces to those kinds, and `registry.lineRows(of:)` says what a kind's rows are called. The kind's `Editable.makeEditingView` is `LineRowsView.make(shape, context:, model:, title:)`, `title` a `LineTitle` that makes its Titles `TitleField` (nil: no title stop).
- **The model** (`LineModel`): what its rows are called (`LineNoun`, "column", "columns"), how its lines look (`LineStyle`: the kind's fonts and inks at rest, where a line's text starts, the gutter, the focused row's band), its rows in order (`rows(of:)`, `setRows(_:in:)`, each by its `PartID`, `id(of:)`), a role a row (`LineRole`) and a grammar a role, where line n is drawn in a frame (`lineRect(_:in:)`), `merged(_:onto:)` (what a line doesn't say kept: a column's on delete), `apply(_:to:in:)` (by default the rows set in order and each row taken out handed to the core's `removeParts`, so nothing stays bound to it; a refusal shakes the shape) and `stepName(of:)` ("Add Column", "Paste Columns", "Rename Column" exactly when only the name changed, "Change Column", "Delete Column", "Move Column"). A delete that took shapes along is named for them too ("Delete Column and 1 Arrow", `DeleteSteps`' words).
- **The grammar** (`LineGrammar`, `@MainActor`, a primary associated `Row`): `read(_:id:in:)` (a `LineText`, the string and the runs that stand for a shape or part by id, `LineReference`, into a `LineReading`: the row, nil for a line with nothing on it, its spans, `LineSpan` with a `LineLook`: name, code, keyword, plain, a pill, a dashed pill, a chip, a tint, and its gutter `LineMark`); `print(_:in:)` (a reference printed as its shape is called now, carrying the reference); `offers(at:in:reading:context:)` (`LineOffers`: the range a pick replaces, what's typed, and `LineOffer`s, each a TextEditing `CaretCompletion` and the shape or row it lights); `lines(fromPasted:)` (by default a line each, blanks dropped); `substitutions` (`->` as `→`); `spoken(_:in:)`. `LineContext` lends the editor, the shape and the row. The law, `losses(in:unread:in:)` (for a grammar whose rows are `Equatable`): printing what was read reads back as the same row, and the words nothing read come out in order; each kind's tests hold their corpus to it.
- **The field** (`LineField`, an `EditingTextView` on TextKit 2, one line, in a clip that slides to keep the caret in view): nothing rewrites what's typed (no autocorrect, quotes, dashes, replacement, spelling), `@` and `/` are letters (no mentions, no slash commands), never a line break (⌥↩ and ⌃↩ type none). After each change the grammar reads it: looks are storage attributes, pills and chips highlights (`TextHighlight`, a dashed one stroked), the mark drawn in the gutter by the session. A run printed or picked carries `LineField.referenceKey` (a `LineField.Reference`, the `PartAddress` it stands for), so an unchanged line keeps naming the same shape when two share a name; typing inside it, over it, or onto its ends with a word character drops it, and the grammar finds the words by name. It holds no keys (`holdsKeys` false), so ⌥↑ ⌥↓, ⇧↩, ⌘↩ and ⌘↑ reach it with a list open (`onKeyDown`, the list closed first), ⌘↓ does nothing yet (the row's card is K7), a held Return is one press, and a held ⌫ that took its row out stops there (`swallowsDeleteRepeats`). It is `UndoesBoardWhenEmpty` (Canvas): with no typing of its own to undo, Edit ▸ Undo is the board's ("Undo Add Column").
- **What fits at the caret.** After every change the field asks the grammar and shows the one caret list (`CaretList`, `.actionsOnly`: Return never picks a word, it commits the line, and does an action row; Tab picks a word) where something is offered and what's typed ranks some (FuzzyMatching's matcher, through `CaretCompletions.listed(isBlank:scoring:)`); a caret move asks again while it's open; it isn't open over a word typed whole (what's typed is what a pick would put in, so ↑ ↓ go on to the rows: T4's `uuid` typed over as `int`, then ↓); Escape closes it until the offers move, then ends editing; a pick closes it in that word until the caret moves. The highlighted offer's light (a shape or a row) is outlined on the board through the core's outlines (`LineRowsService.light`); it goes out as the list does. A field in no window lists all the same (`offerList`, `listedOffers`, `highlightedOffer`), for tests and benchmarks.
- **The session** (`LineRowsView`, the editing view; `LineSession<Model>` within): its stops are the title (a `TitleField` on the pill's row; ↓ there goes to the first row, `TitleField.onDown`), each row, and one waiting line, an unstored empty line, last as editing begins, or right after the row where ↩ was pressed, back to the end once it's left empty. `focus` is a `LineStop` (`.title`, `.row(PartID)`, `.waiting`), `stops` all of them in order, `go(to:)` the caret to one, `line(of:)` a stop's line. Where it starts: a press's point (the pill's row is the title, a row the row with the caret where it was, else the waiting line), else an untitled shape's title, else the waiting line; a shape Tab hopped to at its first stop, ⇧Tab at its last. While it's edited the session sets two layout inputs on its shape alone (`LineRowsLayout.waiting`, the waiting line's index while it has the caret; `LineRowsLayout.focused`, the row the field draws), so the kind lays itself out a line taller while the waiting line has the caret (a row's caret adds no line: the frames draw a table being typed in at its own height, T2 and T4) and draws no row the field has (`LineRowsLayout.line(of:waiting:)` maps a row to its line); the service takes them back as editing ends. The view draws the band behind the focused line and its mark; presses on the title's chip and the paper's lines are its own, anywhere else the canvas's.
- **Commits.** A line is a draft until the caret leaves it (↑ ↓ Tab ⇧Tab ↩ ⌥↑ ⌥↓ ⇧↩, Escape ⌘↩ ⌘↑, the keyboard leaving, asked only while `editor.canTransact`), then one step the kind names, an ordinary commit of its own (never the editing session's), announced to VoiceOver: a waiting line with words is added ("Add Column"), a row's changed line written ("Rename Column", "Change Column"), an emptied row taken out ("Delete Column"), a line as it was nothing. The title is written as it's typed, the editing session's step ("Add Table" where it was placed, else "Rename Table").
- **Keys in a row** (each through `KeyMeaning`): ↩ commits and opens the waiting line below, and on an empty line ends editing (`line-return`); ↑ ↓ the stop above, below, the caret across from where it was, nothing past the ends; Tab ⇧Tab the next, previous stop, at its line's end, past the ends the next or previous shape of the kind in reading order (Selection's `shape(_:from:among:)`, read-only ones passed over, selected, revealed, edited), and with none round inside the shape; ⌥↑ ⌥↓ move the row a place (`line-option-arrows`), held or repeated one step (coalesced under the shape and row); ⌘⌫ empties the line (the text's) and ⌫ on an empty row takes it out, one step, the caret to the end of the row above (else the start of the one below), and on the waiting line goes to the row above; a pasted block of several lines is a row a line where the caret's line is (in place of an empty one, after one with words), one step ("Paste Columns"), the caret at the end of the last, and one line is typed; ⇧↩ writes the line, ends editing and puts a shape of the kind beside it (`editor.insert(ToolID(placing:), beside:)`, shaking where it can't); Escape, ⌘↩ and ⌘↑ write the line and end editing (Escape closes a list first). Return in the title goes to the waiting line, Tab and ⇧Tab on, Escape out.
- **VoiceOver.** The editing view is a group, "Columns of orders" (the plural noun, of the title); its children the title's field ("Table name"), then a text field element a row ("Column 3 of 5", its printed line the value, `spoken` the help, pressing it puts the caret there), the one with the caret the field itself, and the waiting line "New column"; a move posts the focus change, a commit announces its step.
