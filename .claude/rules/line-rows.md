# Rows written as lines

LineRows (`Sources/LineRows`, platform, a horizontal a kind opts into): a shape's rows written a line each (a table's columns, an enum's values, an API's endpoints and headers), and a fixed first line where a kind has one (an API's server): the line field and the grammar a role's lines are read and printed in, what fits at the caret, the session's stops, keys and commits, the waiting line, hops to the next shape of the kind, the light an offer puts on the board, what VoiceOver hears; a row's card (⌘↓: what more a row holds than its line says, a line each, in a core card hung from the row); folds, the lines a shape shows (⌃⌘↑ to its marked rows, a header closed, Find's hits peeking), remembered with the file's view on this Mac; and rows as wholes (K8): selected on the canvas as items of their shape (Selection's), selected whole while the shape is edited (⇧↑ ⇧↓), moved within a shape, into another and out into a new one (`moveRows`), each through the kind's own changes so a row keeps what it means by its neighbors, a header with the rows it heads, on the pasteboard, and dragged (K9: `RowDragSession`, from rows selected or a row's lead).

Related: [editing](editing.md), [text editing](text-editing.md), [titles](titles.md), [keyboard navigation](keyboard-navigation.md), [undo](undo.md), [shape references](shape-references.md), [engineering](engineering.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Rows written as lines](../../docs/plugin-system.md#rows-written-as-lines). The plan is the owner's doc "Engineering kit" (https://claude.ai/artifact/7bkKR1idCbPUmrZT8FVXBf); this landed as its K2, "Write columns", K11, "Fold and find", K7, "Change in place" (the card), K8, "Rows as items", K12, "Write an API" (the fixed line, action rows, indents, the "›"), K9, "Drag rows", and K16, "Change and retire an API" (take-outs of another kind, shapes that are their row, leads that act).

## Architecture

- **The module** knows no table: a kind gives its model and its grammars. On Canvas, TextEditing (the field, the caret list), FuzzyMatching (the list's ranking), Selection (Tab's hops, the "+N ›" line's face and part, rows as items), Titles (the title stop's field), Connections (⌘L in a row: `ConnectKey` and the picker it opens on the row; Connections imports no LineRows), Keyboard (⌃⌘↑ ⌃⌘↓), Find (the rows a fold shows while they hold hits), ContextMenus (the take-out in the selection's menu) and Topology (the arrows a row starts, which a copy carries). Five parts (`Package.swift`'s table): `Field` (the line field and the grammar protocol, readings, looks, offers, the no-loss law), `Folds` (the kinds written as lines, erased: `LineRowsKinds`, `LineRowsContribution`, `LineNoun`, `FoldRow`, `.lineRows`; `RowFold`, `RowsShown`, `LineRowsLayout`'s keys, the `RowFolds` service), `Moves` (rows as wholes, erased: `LineRowsMoves`, `LineRowsMoving`, `RowPlace`, `moveRows`, `RowSteps`; rows on the pasteboard, `RowsPayload`, `RowsPasteboard`; uses Folds), `Session` (`LineModel`, the card's model a kind gives, `LineCard`, the editing view, the service; uses Field, Folds and Moves) and `Card` (the card's view and what shows it, `LineCards`; uses Field, Folds, Moves and Session). Session names nothing of Card: it asks a `LineCardShowing` the service holds, which the plugin's own source makes (`service.cards = LineCards(editor:)`). Its own source is the plugin (`LineRowsPlugin`, `line-rows`, shipped after Draw, Find, Connections, Selection and Titles and before the kinds that opt in): the services, the light's outline, the two provided layout inputs, Fold Rows and Unfold Rows, and the fold's lines as a face and parts; and `lineRows(_:)`, which makes the kind's erased contribution from its model. The keys a row has ("↩ in a Row", "⌥↑ ⌥↓ in a Row", "⌘⌫ then ⌫ in a Row", "⇧↩ in a Row", `.text`) are listed by the kind that opts in (Engineering's plugin), so a file with the kit off lists none. Engineering imports it, and Clipboard, for rows on the canvas's pasteboard.
- **Opting in.** `x.lineRows(model)` (a `LineModel` whose `Props` are the kind's) contributes, besides, its rows as Selection's items (`RowItems.swift`, below) and as wholes (`LineRowsMoves`, one a kind: "row moves (columns) → table" in the report); and to the keyed point `LineRowsKinds`, one a kind, its owner LineRows: what its rows are called, each row's id and whether it heads a group (`FoldRow`), the rows its model marks (`marked(_:in:)`, as a layout reading reads the file) and where line n is drawn, erased (`LineRowsContribution`); `ShapeCapability.lineRows` ("written as lines") scopes LineRows' faces to those kinds, and `registry.lineRows(of:)` gives the contribution. The kind's `Editable.makeEditingView` is `LineRowsView.make(shape, context:, model:, title:)`, `title` a `LineTitle` that makes its Titles `TitleField` (nil: no title stop).
- **The model** (`LineModel`): what its rows are called (`LineNoun`, "column", "columns"), how its lines look (`LineStyle`: the kind's fonts and inks at rest, where a line's text starts, the gutter, the focused row's band), its rows in order (`rows(of:)`, `setRows(_:in:)`, each by its `PartID`, `id(of:)`), a role a row (`LineRole`) and a grammar a role, where line n is drawn in a frame (`lineRect(_:in:)`), `merged(_:onto:)` (what a line doesn't say kept: a column's on delete), `apply(_:to:in:)` (by default the rows set in order, each change made by `applying(_:to:)`, and each row taken out handed to the core's `removeParts`, so nothing stays bound to it; a refusal shakes the shape; a kind whose rows mean something by their neighbors, an API's path prefixes, overrides `applying` so a header taken out or moved leaves what's under it saying what it did), `placing(pasted:at:among:)` (the changes a pasted block makes: the block at the caret by default, a full path under its header in an API; the step is named for the block), `accessibleLabel(of:at:among:)` ("Column 2 of 5" by default; an API counts a header among headers) `marked(_:in:)` (the rows a fold keeps: none by default), `isHeader(_:)` (a row that heads the rows after it, which a fold can close: none by default), `widestLine` (the widest the shape is laid out while a line is typed in it: nil, no limit, by default), `card(of:in:)` (a row's card, nil by default: ⌘↓ does nothing on a row with nothing more, an enum's value), `showsCardChevron` (false by default: whether the row with the caret shows a "›" a click opens its card by, an endpoint's), `fixedLine` (nil by default: a `LineFixedLine`, below), `indent(at:of:isNew:)` (how far past the text inset a row's text starts, or a new line's at an index: a header's members, 0 by default), `renaming(_:to:)` (a row under another id: one moved into a shape that holds its id; every kind gives it), `takeOut(_:from:)` (the props of a new shape holding rows taken out: by default the shape's own props holding only them; a table's has its `id uuid pk` first, an API's its server) and `takeOutEditsTitle` (false by default; the kit's kinds true), `takeOutShape(_:from:)` and `takeOutStep(of:)` (a take-out of another kind, and its step: one endpoint is an endpoint of its own, "Take Out Endpoint"; nil by default), `goesWithItsRows` (a shape that is its one row: false by default), `takesRows(of:)` and `rows(taken:)` (another kind's rows its shapes take: an API an endpoint's; none by default), `leadAction(of:at:among:in:)` (what a lead does besides a click's selecting: an endpoint's chip pops its method menu; nil by default), `reordering(_:to:in:)` (rows moved as a block within the shape: a plain block by default; an API keeps its endpoints' paths as its line's move does) and `lead(of:at:among:line:in:)` (where a row's lead is in its line's rect: none by default; a column's gutter mark when it has one, an endpoint's method chip) and `stepName(of:)` ("Add Column", "Paste Columns", "Rename Column" exactly when only the name changed, "Change Column", "Delete Column", "Move Column"). A delete that took shapes along is named for them too ("Delete Column and 1 Arrow", `DeleteSteps`' words).
- **A fixed first line** (`LineFixedLine<Props>`, a kind's `fixedLine`: an API's server). A line above the rows that every shape of the kind has, never a row, so nothing adds or takes it out: its name for VoiceOver ("Server"), a placeholder the session draws faint and slanted while it's empty ("server"), its step ("Change Server"), where it's drawn in a frame (`rect`), and a grammar of its own with `get` and `set` on the props (erased, as a card's line is: its looks, offers and `->` as `→`). It is a stop, `LineStop.fixed`, after the title and before the rows (↓ from the title and ↑ from the first row come to it, Return goes on to the waiting line, Tab on); written as it's left, if it changed from what it was shown as, one step, announced, a refusal (a locked shape) shaking. While it has the caret the session sets `LineRowsLayout.fixed` on the shape, so the kind leaves it to the field. A press on its rect puts the caret there.
- **Action rows** (`LineOffer.action(_:_:isDefault:)`, a `LineAction`: its step and what it makes, a `PartAddress`). An offer can be an action ("New type Problem"): the caret list lists it after every word whatever its score (`CaretCompletions.listed`), highlighted though last where the grammar says it's the default (`isDefault`, TextEditing's `CaretCompletion.isDefault`, the list's `setRows(_:highlighting:)`: nothing else is called what was typed), Return does it (an `.actionsOnly` list's Return never picks a word) and so does Tab, which picks either, the list's rule; the field hands it to its host (`LineField.onAction`, with the range of the words it was offered for), which makes the shape and has the words stand for it (`stand(for:at:)`), and writes the line, in one group of the action's name (the session: a row's line, the caret kept and the line shown again so Undo is the board's, "Undo New Type"; a card: the card's line, written as it would be left). Nothing made, nothing written.
- **The grammar** (`LineGrammar`, `@MainActor`, a primary associated `Row`): `read(_:id:in:)` (a `LineText`, the string and the runs that stand for a shape or part by id, `LineReference`, into a `LineReading`: the row, nil for a line with nothing on it, its spans, `LineSpan` with a `LineLook`: name, code, keyword, plain, a pill, a dashed pill, a chip, a tint, and its gutter `LineMark`); `print(_:in:)` (a reference printed as its shape is called now, carrying the reference); `offers(at:in:reading:context:)` (`LineOffers`: the range a pick replaces, what's typed, and `LineOffer`s, each a TextEditing `CaretCompletion` and the shape or row it lights); `lines(fromPasted:)` (by default a line each, blanks dropped); `substitutions` (`->` as `→`); `spoken(_:in:)`. `LineContext` lends the editor, the shape and the row. The law, `losses(in:unread:in:)` (for a grammar whose rows are `Equatable`): printing what was read reads back as the same row, and the words nothing read come out in order; each kind's tests hold their corpus to it. It holds the other way too: a row a kind stores by any other writer (a paste, `canvas draw`'s JSON) prints as a line that reads back as that row, or an edit of only its name commits a change the person never made, so a grammar quotes what its words can't say alone (the engineering kit's column grammar quotes a name, an enum's name or a default of several words, and its tests print every column the SQL reader writes and read it back: [engineering](engineering.md)).
- **The field** (`LineField`, an `EditingTextView` on TextKit 2, one line, in a clip that slides to keep the caret in view): nothing rewrites what's typed (no autocorrect, quotes, dashes, replacement, spelling), `@` and `/` are letters (no mentions, no slash commands), never a line break (⌥↩ and ⌃↩ type none; one dropped in, by drag, Services or dictation, is a space, inserted as typing is, so the text's undo holds what went in). After each change the grammar reads it: looks are storage attributes, pills and chips highlights (`TextHighlight`, a dashed one stroked), the mark drawn in the gutter by the session (and a row's text starts at the style's inset plus the kind's `indent`). A run printed or picked carries `LineField.referenceKey` (a `LineField.Reference`, the `PartAddress` it stands for), so an unchanged line keeps naming the same shape when two share a name; typing inside it, over it, or onto its ends with a word character drops it, and the grammar finds the words by name; undoing the typing puts it back (an undo action registered ahead of the text's own, so it runs after it, and is redone as it is: the attribute isn't the text's own undo). It holds no keys (`holdsKeys` false), so ⌥↑ ⌥↓, ⇧↩, ⌘↩ (`LineKey.finish`), ⌘↑ (`.end`, as Escape) and ⌘↓ (`.card`) reach it with a list open (`onKeyDown`, the list closed first), a held Return is one press, and a held ⌫ that took its row out stops there (`swallowsDeleteRepeats`). It is `UndoesBoardWhenEmpty` (Canvas): with no typing of its own to undo, Edit ▸ Undo is the board's ("Undo Add Column"), unless it holds a draft (`holdsDraft`: words on the waiting line, a row changed): Undo and Redo are its own then, Redo off, since the board's would end editing, write the draft and clear the redo it was to put back.
- **What fits at the caret.** After every change the field asks the grammar and shows the one caret list (`CaretList`, `.actionsOnly`: Return never picks a word, it commits the line, and does an action row; Tab picks a word) where something is offered and what's typed ranks some (FuzzyMatching's matcher, through `CaretCompletions.listed(isBlank:scoring:)`); a caret move asks again while it's open, and opens it where the person moves the caret to the end of a dashed highlight (words naming nothing yet: an unmade type, so its "New type" is a Return away again; never where the session placed the caret, arriving on a row by Tab, ⇧Tab or ↑ ↓, `LineField.placing`, so no walk of the keys makes a type); a list's `LineOffers.anchor` is where the typed word starts where that isn't the caret less the query (a quoted name's closing quote is no part of the query); it isn't open over a word typed whole (what's typed is what a pick would put in, so ↑ ↓ go on to the rows: T4's `uuid` typed over as `int`, then ↓); Escape closes it until the offers move, then ends editing; a pick closes it in that word until the caret moves. The highlighted offer's light (a shape or a row) is outlined on the board through the core's outlines (`LineRowsService.light`); it goes out as the list does. A field in no window lists all the same (`offerList`, `listedOffers`, `highlightedOffer`), for tests and benchmarks.
- **A word typed whole that longer offers still extend** (U1, tables 12): the list closes only when every offer listed is the word as typed; otherwise it stays, the word itself first and highlighted (`time`: time, timestamp, timestamptz), and ↑ ↓ while that word is highlighted are the line's, as with no list (the named exception `line-list-word`), so T4's `uuid` typed over as `int`, then ↓, is still the next row; Escape is the list's as any list's (it closes it, the next ends the line, writing it).
- **The session** (`LineRowsView`, the editing view; `LineSession<Model>` within): its stops are the title (a `TitleField` on the pill's row; ↓ there goes to the first row, `TitleField.onDown`), each row, and one waiting line, an unstored empty line, last as editing begins, or right after the row where ↩ was pressed, back to the end once it's left empty. `focus` is a `LineStop` (`.title`, `.row(PartID)`, `.waiting`), `stops` all of them in order, `go(to:)` the caret to one, `line(of:)` a stop's line. Where it starts: a press's point (the pill's row is the title, a row the row with the caret where it was, else the waiting line), else an untitled shape's title, else the waiting line; a shape Tab hopped to at its first stop, ⇧Tab at its last. While it's edited the session sets two layout inputs on its shape alone (`LineRowsLayout.waiting`, the waiting line's index while it has the caret; `LineRowsLayout.focused`, the row the field draws; a commit that moves the caret to another stop, a waiting line becoming its row or an emptied row going, sets them again as it writes, for a caller that goes on without showing a stop), so the kind lays itself out a line taller while the waiting line has the caret (a row's caret adds no line: the frames draw a table being typed in at its own height, T2 and T4) and draws no row the field has (`LineRowsLayout.line(of:waiting:)` maps a row to its line); the service takes them back as editing ends. The view draws the band behind the focused line and its mark; presses on the title's chip and the paper's lines are its own, anywhere else the canvas's.
- **Commits.** A line is a draft until the caret leaves it (↑ ↓ Tab ⇧Tab ↩ ⌥↑ ⌥↓ ⇧↩, Escape ⌘↩ ⌘↑, the keyboard leaving, asked only while `editor.canTransact`), then one step the kind names, an ordinary commit of its own (never the editing session's), announced to VoiceOver: a waiting line with words is added ("Add Column"), a row's changed line written ("Rename Column", "Change Column"), an emptied row taken out ("Delete Column"), a line as it was shown, nothing: what a line reads as can differ from what is stored with nothing typed (an enum renamed under a column's type: the line prints its name now), so the session keeps the line it showed and the row it stood for, writes nothing while the line is that, and names a changed one against that row ("Rename Column" when the name alone changed). A row taken out by another writer under the caret leaves its line a draft on the waiting line. The title is written as it's typed, the editing session's step ("Add Table" where it was placed, else "Rename Table").
- **Keys in a row** (each through `KeyMeaning`): ↩ commits and opens the waiting line below (under the row the line became, wherever the kind put it), above with the caret at the very start of a row's line (a text editor's Return, every kind written as lines: UX walk apis 26), and on an empty line ends editing (`line-return`); ↑ ↓ the stop above, below, the caret across from where it was, nothing past the ends; Tab ⇧Tab the next, previous stop, at its line's end, past the ends the next or previous shape of the kind in reading order (Selection's `shape(_:from:among:)`, read-only ones passed over, selected, revealed, edited), and with none round inside the shape; ⌥↑ ⌥↓ move the row a place (`line-option-arrows`), held or repeated one step (coalesced under the shape and row); ⌘⌫ empties the line (the text's) and ⌫ on an empty row takes it out, one step, the caret to the end of the row above (else the start of the one below), and on the waiting line goes to the row above; a pasted block of several lines is a row a line where the caret's line is (in place of an empty one, after one with words), one step ("Paste Columns"), the caret at the end of the last, and one line is typed; ⇧↩ writes the line, ends editing and puts a shape of the kind beside it (`editor.insert(ToolID(placing:), beside:)`, shaking where it can't); Escape, ⌘↩ and ⌘↑ write the line and end editing (Escape closes a list first); ⌘L, Connect's key (Connections' `ConnectKey`, which the field answers as a key equivalent before the menu bar's Connect, which would end editing), writes the line and opens ⌘L's picker on the row (`editor.connectPicker?.open(on: ConnectEnd(shape, part: row), returningKeyboardTo: field)`), the shape still edited and the keyboard back on the line as it closes; once the pick's commit settles (`LineRowsService.awaitingReprint`, told by `documentDidSettle`; `LineField.onFocus`), the row's line is printed again, with the key the pick drew (`LineRowsView.reprint()`, nothing written where the line holds a draft); only a pick that made a connection waits for it (`ConnectPickerHost.isPicking` when the keyboard comes back, `didPick` after), so Escape, or a refused pick, leaves the line as it was and no later commit prints it again; the menu bar's Connect while a row has the caret does the same (`ConnectPickerHost.connectsEditedRow`), and ⌘L pressed again in the picker's field does nothing; on the title or an empty waiting line, editing ends and the picker opens on the shape, as Connect does. LineRows imports Connections for it. Return in the title goes to the waiting line, Tab and ⇧Tab on, Escape out. A press on the title's chip (or VoiceOver's) from a row writes the row and makes the title the stop (`TitleField.began`), so Tab and ↓ go on from it.
- **Lines of what a row doesn't hold** (K14): `LineCardLine.list(_:grammar:get:writing:)` is a card's list of lines whose values the row doesn't hold (an endpoint's connections, each its arrow): `get` reads them (from the board), and when a line of them is left changed the card hands the lines' values to `writing`, which writes them in a step of its own it names (the row isn't written, `LineCardText.writesApart`), told with them how many lines had words and read to none (`unreadLines`: a name mistyped), so a write can refuse rather than take a value out for words that don't read; false refuses (the shape shakes), what was typed staying as a refused row's line does. The engineering kit's endpoint card's "relations" ([engineering](engineering.md)).
- **A row's card** (`Card`, K7). ⌘↓ in a row writes its line (the waiting line with words becomes its row first) and shows the card the kind gives for it (`LineModel.card(of:in:)`, a `LineCard`: a `LineCardHeader`, the row's name and mark, and its lines, and a `width` where the kind's values are longer than the usual card's (an endpoint's, 360; a field without the keyboard shows its text from its start, cut short of the card's edge between two characters and ending in "…" where it runs past it, as every cut text does, `isCut(_:)`, connect 19 to 21; only the one with the keyboard slides to its caret, whole), `LineCardLine`: `.field(label, grammar:, get:, set:)` words in a grammar of its own, one line; `.toggle(label, get:, set:)` said yes or no; `.choice(label, options:, get:, set:)` one of several, the first shown faint while none is held; `.list(label, grammar:, get:, set:)` lines, a value each), in a core `CanvasCard` hung from the row's rect (`LineCards`; its id `LineCardID`, the shape and row; no step of its own; `returnsKeyboardTo` the row's field), its first stop with the keyboard, VoiceOver's popover "Details of customer_id". It follows the row as each commit settles (`LineRowsService.documentDidSettle` asks `cards.follow(changes)`: moved under the row's rect now, its lines shown as the row holds them, a line with the person's words in it kept, its header asked of the kind again, so its mark, its name and the label VoiceOver hears, `editor.setCardLabel`, follow the row; every commit reloads it, since a line reads what other shapes hold, and the host lays it out again only when its lines changed in number) and as the session lays itself out with nothing written (a waiting line opened above the row, `setInputs` asks `follow(nil)`: moved only), and closes as the row goes, and as every card closes (editing ends, an undo, a press elsewhere). Its view (`LineCardView`, the public face tests read: `stopNames`, `focusedStop`, `value(of:)`, `field(of:)`, `go(to:)`, `menuPresenter`; `LineCardSession<Model>` within) is a stop a line, a list's an item each: a `LineField` for words (its looks, offers and light as a row's line has them, in the card's inks, `LineCardStyle.lineStyle(from:)`), a `LineCardControl` for a toggle (a checkbox to VoiceOver) or a choice (a pop-up button; its options a menu, the current checked). Keys: Tab and ⇧Tab round its stops; ↑ ↓ between them, staying at the ends; Escape, ⌘↑ and ⌘. write the line and close it, the row's line with the keyboard back; ⌘↩ writes it and ends editing; Return in a field is done with it (`text-return`: written, the row has the keyboard back), in a list writes it and opens a line below (on an empty one, done: `line-return`), and ⌫ on a list's empty line takes it out; Space and Return toggle a toggle and open a choice; ⌥↑ ⌥↓, ⇧↩, ⌘L and ⌘↓ do nothing there. Each line commits as its stop is left (a toggle and a pick at once, a line left as it was shown nothing): the row the line's `set` makes from the row as stored, written over it as a row's line is (`LineSession.commitCard`, a `CardCommit`: one step the kind names by what changed, announced, the row's line printed again), a refusal shaking the shape and keeping what was typed where it is, as a row's line keeps its draft (Tab and ↑ ↓ go on with the words still in the line, Return stays; Escape, which the row's own does too, goes). A card closed while a commit is being delivered (`canTransact` false) writes its words a turn later (`editor.scheduler`). A list's line left empty goes, and so does a line Return opened and nothing was typed in; the keyboard lands on the line it aimed at, found again by what went before it. A toggle or a pick is spoken after the step's name ("null yes"), and a choice picked at the option shown while none is held writes nothing. VoiceOver reads the lines in the order the keys walk them (`accessibilityChildren` is the stops). ⌘↓ on a line the file refused opens no card: the card would write over the draft. `LineRowsService.openCard(of:)` is the way in for a "›" drawn elsewhere: the shape edited at the row, its card shown. A kind that asks (`showsCardChevron`) gets a "›" the session draws at the right of the row with the caret, whose row has a card (room kept for it, `LineRowsStyle.chevronRoom`), a click on it opening the card as ⌘↓ does, and a button for VoiceOver after the line, "Show Details".
- **The width while typing.** After each change to a row's or the waiting line's text the session asks for the shape to be laid out as wide as the line needs (the text, the row's text inset and trailing inset, the field's slack), never narrower than it stores nor past `widestLine` (past it the line slides in its clip as before), through `LineRowsLayout.width`, a layout input `LineRowsService` provides (so a commit mid-session keeps it, as a fold is kept): nothing written, no step; nil again on the title and as editing ends. The kind reads it in its `laidOutSize` (`max(stored, width)`), so bounds, culling, its row rects and the arrows bound to them follow (T2 panel 3: `updated_at timestamptz default now()` whole in products as it's typed).
- **The width at rest** (U1, apis 17, tables 30, paste 03): a kind may say how wide its rows read whole at rest (`LineModel.wholeWidth(of:in:)`, at most its own widest; nil by default). A commit that leaves the shape narrower writes that width in the same step (`widening`, never narrower, held to `widestLine` where the kind has one): `write` (a typed line, the card, rows selected on the canvas), rows pasted or dropped into a shape (`LineRowsMoving.widening`), and a take-out's new shape before it's placed. So what was typed reads whole once the caret leaves, and the shape doesn't jump narrower; the width while typing then has nothing left to do. While a typed line lays the shape out wider than it stores, or the waiting line taller, the core paints and hits it above what it overlaps and no other shape's pill is drawn in its room (U4, [editor](editor.md)), so a shape put down beside it never covers its rows; a wide typed line still moves nothing.
- **Folds** (`Folds`). `RowFolds` (a service, `editor.rowFolds`) keeps what the person asked each shape to show, never which rows are hidden, so rows added or taken out need nothing done: `RowFold`, `toMarked` (⌃⌘↑) and `closed` (headers shut: an API's path prefixes, K12; ⌃⌘↑ closes every one, K17), and, never remembered, peeks (`peek(_:of:)`, `release(of:)`: rows shown in place though folded) and Find's peeks (the rows whose texts hold hits while Find is open, `FindState.rangesHit(of:)`, heard through `whenResultsChange`, laid out again once the change settles; a kind may keep a row folded whose hits it draws elsewhere, `LineModel.peeks(_:at:of:hitAt:)`, true by default: an API's endpoint hit only in its group's prefix, drawn on the header, E8 panel 3). The lines a folded shape shows are `RowsShown` (`RowsShown.make(rows:fold:marked:peeked:)`, pure): a closed header's rows hidden behind it with their count (`.header`; an open header's rows all shown, whatever the marks), then, folded to its marks, the rows under no header that its kind marks, the first two of those (`unmarkedKept`) where none is marked, each peeked row in its place, and a "+N ›" line last for the rest (`.more`); `line(standingFor:)` is a row's own line, its closed header's or the "+N ›" line's; nil where nothing is hidden. It is a provided layout input, `LineRowsLayout.shown` (`registrar.layoutInput`, answered by `RowFolds.shown(of:in:)`), never a set one, since a set value reads nil for a shape its transaction changed: a commit to a folded table keeps it folded, and a mid-transaction reading lays it out folded. Kept per shape for the editor's own reading while `editor.revision` and the folds' generation stay, so a frame reads it once a shape. The kind lays itself out (`LaidOutBox`) at the height of its shown lines, draws them, and puts a hidden row's part rect at the line standing for it, marked not shown (`PartFrame.isShown` false: a shown part is its own line, so the ports' search by height halves `PartFrames.shownFrames`, the shown ones in reading order, never the hidden ones that stand at one line out of row order; ↑ ↓ on a key's end and a drop on "+N ›" meet shown rows only), so its ports, an arrow bound to it (drawn together with the others standing there: Bundles, below) and Find's rects follow; bounds, culling and the minimap follow the frame. A fold settles nothing: unfolding may overlap a neighbor (accepted). Editing a folded shape shows every row (the provider answers nil for the shape being edited; the session tells `RowFolds` as it starts, `editingWillBegin`, and `editingDidEnd` lays it out folded again). A press that began the editing was made against the fold: the session reads the lines the shape showed folded (`foldedLines(of:)`, which ignores the editing) and finds the row pressed there, and a press on "+N ›" is the waiting line. A key drawn or deleted is a table's mark: the kit's key index tells `marksMayHaveChanged`, and a table folded to its marks is laid out again once the commit settles. No step, no write, no `ChangeSet`: a fold is a view.
- **Fold Rows and Unfold Rows** (`.foldRows` ⌃⌘↑, `.unfoldRows` ⌃⌘↓, `RowFoldsSection` in View, run when idle so a row's text keeps the keys): on the selection's shapes written as lines, or, with nothing selected, every one on the board shown. ⌃⌘↑ folds a shape as far as it goes (`foldAsked(of:)`): to its marks and every header closed (E8: an API's groups each a closed header with its count), and ⌃⌘↓ lets the whole fold go; Fold Rows is on while one of them isn't folded so (`isFoldedWhole`), Unfold Rows while one is folded. A connection a fold has just bundled that was selected alone is selected with its bundle (the selection setter takes a member's unit: `Selection`'s `holdingUnitsOfMembers`), so what holds the selection is always drawn and spoken. - **Rows on the pasteboard** (`RowsPayload.swift`).
