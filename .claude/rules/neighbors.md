# Neighbors

Neighbors: hover a shape, on the canvas or by its row in the table of contents, and everything a light's step leads to from it is outlined in a color that says how: downstream on a plain hover, upstream with ⌥ (what points to it), its mentions both ways with ⌃⌥, each shape fading with its hop and its share, and the rest of the board dimmed once the pointer rests, the reach coming in a hop at a time (the hover is the whole reach, [The hover](#the-hover) below). Select one, and its panel's Connections tab lists what's one step away by the same ways, then what it's inside and what it holds; the lights (a title, a color, the keys that show it and a query), the keys held, the subject, asking and asking again, where what's matched is drawn, the core's tinted outlines and the minimap's fills it hands, and the lists (folding, searching, their rows).

Related: [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [fuzzy matching](fuzzy-matching.md), [publishing](publishing.md), [queries](queries.md), [topology](topology.md), [selection](selection.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [editor](editor.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [connections](connections.md), [references](references.md), [jump to](jump-to.md), [moving](moving.md), [benchmarks](benchmarks.md), [types](types.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Neighbors](../../docs/plugin-system.md#neighbors), [Outlines](../../docs/plugin-system.md#outlines), [The table of contents](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-table-of-contents).

## Architecture

- Neighbors (platform, an engine: what an edge's owner shows its edges on; plugin id `neighbors`) is on Canvas, DetailPanel, FuzzyMatching, Queries, Selection, TableOfContents and Topology, and knows no kind, edge type or concern. It shows a shape's edges two ways, from the same lights: outlined on the canvas for the shape hovered (`NeighborsPlugin`, The hover, below), and listed in the panel for the shape selected (`NeighborDetailsPlugin`, a plugin value of its own: The lists, below). `NeighborsPlugin()` (with `NeighborLight`, its point `NeighborLights`, `registrar.neighborLight(_:color:keys:query:between:caption:)`, `registry.neighborLights`) registers a service, `ReachState` (`editor.reach`, a `@MainActor` class and an `EditorService` in an extension, made with the registry's lights and its settings, `ReachState(editor:lights:settings:)`), which follows the hover, asks the engine and hands the core its outlines, and `registrar.outlines { $0.reach?.outlines ?? Outlines() }`, between `query-commands` and the lights' three (`arrow-lights`, `mention-lights` and `type-lights`, each `neighbor light · neighbor light` and its key hint), since it comes right after the query plugins in the shipped list. The report's line is `neighbors service ReachState · outlines`. It contributes to no point whose owner a list might lack, so a list without Queries, Topology or Selection isn't refused: it lights nothing (no engine or no Topology, nothing is asked; no Selection, nothing is hovered; no light, nothing to ask).
- **The point** (`NeighborLights`, an `ExtensionPoint` in an extension, owner `neighbors`, its `summary` "neighbor light"): a plugin that owns a kind of edge gives a light with `registrar.neighborLight(_:color:keys:query:between:caption:)`, in a plugin value of its own (a face), after Neighbors and the plugin that declares the edges, so a list without Neighbors leaves it out and one with the face and no Neighbors is refused (`ownerNotInstalled`). The registry keeps them in plugin order, and a plugin's in the order it gives them (`registry.neighborLights`). Shipped: Arrows' `ArrowLightsPlugin` (id `arrow-lights`, `Sources/Arrows/ArrowLights.swift`: points to, points to it) then Mentions' `MentionLightsPlugin` (id `mention-lights`, `Sources/Mentions/MentionLights.swift`: mentions, mentioned by), and then Types' `TypeLightsPlugin` (id `type-lights`, `Sources/Types/Engines/TypeLights.swift`: instances, subtypes), so Arrows, Mentions and Types import Neighbors, and nothing writes a light for them: the list of what ships names the four plugins, in that order, and then the tab's (`NeighborDetailsPlugin`, below).
- **A light** (`NeighborLight`, `Sendable`): a `title` (what the panel heads its list with), a `color: NSColor`, `keys: Modifiers` (the keys held while hovering that show it, `[]` by default: a plain hover; the panel lists every light whatever is held), and three closures. `query: @Sendable (String) -> String` is given the selector that names the shape (`#` and its id, `"#\(id)"`: the query language's, `uuidString`) and returns the query that finds the shapes at the other end: outlined in the color, and listed. `between`, optional, finds what runs between the shape and those (the arrows): outlined with them, never listed. `caption`, optional and `@MainActor`, is a line under a listed shape's name, from the editor, the shape the list is of and the listed one, asked of the rows in view as each is drawn. `lit(_:)` is what a hover asks: the query, then `, ` and `between`'s, one query a light. The shipped six, by their keys a plain hover what a shape points to, ⌥ what points to it (and what is of a type, whose `is-a` edges run to it), ⌃⌥ its mentions both ways: Points To (green, `ArrowStyle.pointsToLight`, no keys: `:edge-from(X, arrow|stands-for), :end-of(X, arrow)`, between `:starts-at(X, arrow)`), Pointed To By (purple, `ArrowStyle.pointedToByLight`, ⌥: `:edge-to(X, arrow|stands-for), :start-of(X, arrow)`, between `:ends-at(X, arrow)`), Mentions (teal, `MentionStyle.mentionsLight`, ⌃⌥: `:edge-from(X, mention)`), Mentioned By (lavender, `MentionStyle.mentionedByLight`, ⌃⌥: `:edge-to(X, mention)`, its caption the mentioner's text around its mention, `MentionState.quote(of:around:)`), Instances (pink, `TypesStyle.instancesLight`, ⌥: `:is-a(X[schema]):not([schema])`, what is of it however far while it is a type, never a type (a shape whose schema was taken away lists none, though shapes still name it); a relationship type's are its arrows) and Subtypes (the same pink, ⌥: `[schema]:is-a(X[schema])`, the types built on it however far; a shape is one or the other, so the two share the color and their lists' titles tell them apart; no reverse light, since a shape's chips and Types tab say what it is), their titles in `ArrowStyle`, `MentionStyle` and `TypesStyle`, their queries written from `ArrowEdge.id`, `StandingEdge.id`, `MentionEdge.id` and `IsAEdge.id`, never as literals (the audit holds that). The colors were chosen together in a design audit, from renders of the lights over both canvases, as fixed sRGB values for each appearance (`NeighborLight.color(light:dark:)`, two `0xRRGGBB` values in [Palette](palette.md)'s `NSColor(light:dark:)`), never system colors (system pink read as Validation's error red, system purple as the selection's blue, and light-mode green and teal were under 3:1): green #07933B light and #2FD359 dark, purple #8C21A4 and #B851FD, teal #0E97B0 and #329AB4, lavender #A76FE2 and #D0B0FE. Each is clear of Validation's red and orange and Find's orange, at least 3:1 against the canvas it's drawn over in both appearances, and not the accent; two of the four, the purples, are close for some color-vision deficiencies. Types' pink, #C2307A light and #F07AB5 dark (4.9:1 and 6.6:1), came after the audit, by the same rules, from renders of the hover beside the four and an orange warning ring: gold, the other hue the four leave, read as a yellow note's own edge on the dark canvas and sat beside Validation's orange; pink is the nearest of the five to Validation's red. The words are the language's own (`:edge-from(S, types)`: the shapes the anchor has an edge to, `:starts-at(S, types)`: the connections that start at it, `:end-of(S, types)` and `:start-of(S, types)`: where a connection the anchor matches ends and starts, so an arrow hovered lights the shape it ends at as what it points to and the one it starts at as what points to it; a type takes in those under it). The arrows between are matched too, as carriers, so they take their light's color. Order matters: a shape's first light shown is its first ring. Each owner says its keys beside its lights with `registrar.keyHint(…, while: .pointer)`, once for its keys (the Keyboard Shortcuts window lists a key once): Arrows' "⌥ Hover", "Show What Points to It"; Mentions' "⌃⌥ Hover", "Show Mentions"; Types' "⌥ Hover a Type", "Show Its Instances and Subtypes".
- **The lists** (`NeighborList`, `editor.neighborList`, a `@MainActor @Observable` service `NeighborDetailsPlugin` registers; `Sources/Neighbors/NeighborList.swift`): what's connected to the shape a panel shows, in lists (`groups`, `NeighborGroup`: a title, a color or none), one for each light in the lights' order, each holding its light's `query` (not `between`: the arrows aren't listed), then two it gives itself in the language's own words for Topology's one tree: Inside (`:has(#id)`: what the shape is in, outermost first, since file order is) and Contains (`#id > *:not(:starts-at(*))`: what's directly in it, a frame's shapes or a subboard's board's in no frame, less the connections among them, since no list has what runs between; an arrow bound at one end is no connection, so it's a shape the board holds). So it lists what hovering the shape lights at hop 1 (the same lights' queries), less what runs between, and knows no kind, edge type or concern. **The subject** is the shape whose lists are on screen: the panel's view says so as it appears and goes (`watch(_:)`, `unwatch(_:)`), so nothing is asked while no list shows (another tab, a hidden inspector, a takeover). **Asking** (`ask`): one `Task` for the subject, an ask under way cancelled; every folded list counted in one `queries.matches` batch of ids (`admitting` leaves the subject out), every open one run for its rows with `queries.run(QueryRequest(text, limit: QueryRequest.every, properties: .none, edges: view))` (id, kind, title, board, inside: the engine's names, so Neighbors imports no Titles), all at once in a task group, each in `.resolved` and, where `topology.hasStandIns`, `.literal` too, the views' rows merged a shape once, resolved first, as the hover's are; the subject is never listed. A request the engine refuses (a note asked what it holds: `cannot_contain`) is an empty list. The answer lands whole (`listing`, a `NeighborListing`: the subject, each list's `count` and `rows`, nil for a folded one, and a `stamp`); until it does the shape before's stays and the view shows nothing of it (`hasLanded`). No list is cut: an open one has every row, however many (99,999 for the hub of a board of 100,000); the rows are made, joined and compared with the lists shown (`readsAsShown`) off the main actor (`@concurrent`), which only the engine's own turns and the landing take, so a list of 100,000 costs the main actor what a list of ten does; a folded list is counted without asking for an id (with one view, the answer's own count). **Asking again**: `documentDidChange` re-arms one wait of `NeighborStyle.settle` (100 ms, on `editor.scheduler`) for any commit that did more than move shapes (a name, an edge, a container joined or left: a resize's every event is a commit, so they're one ask), and `topology.follow` asks at once for a change no commit made (a provide). A drag costs a look at its change set. An answer that reads as the one shown shows nothing (`land`: a shape watched again), unless a change to the file asked for it (`followsChange`): then the stamp moves, the names kept are let go of and every row is drawn again, since what a row is called and what its caption quotes are read as it's drawn, from shapes the commit may have changed (an untitled note's words, a name its text mentions). `boardDidChange` is overridden with nothing (a row says which board it's on as it's drawn). A row's place is `NeighborLine.place`, steps (`PlaceStep`s: the containers it is inside, a subboard's name a board, the board's name leading for a row on another board in none), which `DetailShapeRow` folds in the room the row has (`PlaceFold`, as every address is: the first, "…", the nearest whole, boards semibold); `detail` is the same as plain text, and what VoiceOver hears whole. **Folding** (`isFolded`, `setFolded`): by a list's place in `groups`, kept while the editor lives, for every shape; a folded list is only counted, and unfolding one whose rows weren't read asks. **Searching** (`search`, `setSearch`): typed text keeps, in every list, folded or not (their rows are asked for), the rows whose names it matches, ranked as every list ranks what's typed (`FuzzyMatcher.matches`, the best first, ties in the list's order); of several words (`PathMatcher`'s pieces) also the rows whose containers the words before the last name, as the `@` picker and Jump To match a path ([mentions](mentions.md)): the row's `inside` and its name are the path (`matcher.match(path:placingAtLeast: 1)`, an untitled container matched by what the engine calls it), in three bands, the rows whose name has the whole text from a substring up first, then those found by their path, then the loose name matches, each by score and then the list's order; a new subject clears it. `shown(in:)` is a list as shown (count and rows: all it has, none while folded, or the matches); `total` is of all, searched or not; `line(for:in:)` is a row as drawn (`NeighborLine`: the kind's symbol, in the tint the shape has, `editor.tint(of:)`, its own color where it has one, else its kind's, read as the row is drawn; the file's colors changing bumps the listing's `stamp`, which draws every row again; its name; where it is (`place(of:isElsewhere:)`): its path, the containers it is inside, outermost first, joined by " › " as every path says them (the row's `inside`, as the engine named them: a shape's identity is its path, so two called alike are told apart), less those the board shown is inside and the subject is when the row is inside them (`topology.ancestors(of:)` of the row, compared by id with the chain of each: so what a subboard or a frame holds, and what is directly on the board shown, say nothing); else, for a shape in no container left but on another board, that board's name (read from the row's `inside` at its board's owner's depth, the root `editor.rootName` else "Board"); and its light's caption; spoken as it draws: the name, where it is, the caption), reading the file without observing it (`withoutObservingDocument`), so a row is drawn again as an answer lands and never on every commit. **A row's name** is its title, as the engine names it; for a shape with none, what the table of contents calls it (`ContentsState.name(of:)`: an untitled note's text on one line, "Empty Note", a pill by what it stands for), read once for an answer (`names`) as the row is drawn, and for every row by a search's first keystroke; else, with no table of contents, its kind's name. `go(to:)` is `editor.goTo` on the scheduler's next turn (its board first, one step back); `hover(_:)` and `endHover(_:)` set `editor.hovered`, or `hoveredElsewhere` for a shape the canvas doesn't show, and let go only of the hover the row set, as the table of contents' rows do: so the pointer on a row lights that shape's own neighbors. Debug builds count `asked`, `shown` and `linesDrawn`. **Costs** (release, `./build.sh bench`): the main actor's time from a shape's lists being asked for to their landing is some 0.4 ms with 1,000 neighbors and 1.3 with 10,000 (0.2 with ten, whatever the board; folded, 0.2 and 0.7), the engine's answers off it some 3 and 47 ms (every row is made, none cut); with 99,999 neighbors the same 1.5 ms (folded, 0.6), the engine's answers off the main actor some 0.8 s; a keystroke searching 10,000 rows some 7 ms, and the first, which reads every untitled row's name, some 45 ms once, and over 99,999 rows some 100 ms and, the first, some 1.2 s, all on the main actor ([benchmarks](benchmarks.md)).
- **The tab** (`NeighborDetailsPlugin`, id `neighbor-details`, `Sources/Neighbors/NeighborDetails.swift`): a plugin value of its own after Neighbors and `DetailPanelPlugin` (last of Neighbors' four lines in the shipped list), so a list without the panel leaves it out and keeps the lights, and one with it and no panel is refused (`ownerNotInstalled`); it needs no light and no `NeighborsPlugin` (its lists are then Inside and Contains). It registers the service and one headerless section on every shape (`forEveryShape { $0.details { … } }`, `enabledWhileReadOnly`) in `NeighborsTab` ("Connections", which says no order: it goes where it first appears, after a kind's own tabs and the feature tabs of the plugins before it, before Style, which is last, and a type's Schema, which is after it), always there: a tab that came with its lists would come a turn late, so with nothing connected it says "No Connections" once the answer is in. It replaced Mentions' Mentioned In and Publishing's Referenced In: a pill that stands for a public shape is among what points to it (arrows' light names `stands-for`), which is where Publishing sends the panel when Make Private is refused, and where what stands for a shape is listed ([publishing](publishing.md)). The view (`NeighborLists`): a search field once the lists hold `NeighborStyle.searchFrom` (20) shapes in all, more than a panel shows without scrolling, or while something's typed (`DetailSearchField`), then, in one `LazyVStack`, each list that has shapes: its head (`DetailGroupHeader`: a disclosure triangle, a dot in the light's color, the title, the count), which folds it, and its rows (`DetailShapeRow` in a borderless button, a row with a caption 4 points clear of the next, `captionedRowBottom`), made as they scroll into view, so a list of thousands draws the rows in view; "No Matches" for a search that finds none. The group header and the search field are DetailPanel's ([detail panel](detail-panel.md)).

- **The list by keys** (P1, P3, P4, P9 of [keyboard navigation](keyboard-navigation.md)): the lists are one `DetailRowList` ([detail panel](detail-panel.md)), a stop of Tab, each list's head (`DetailGroupHeader(isRow: true)`, folded with ← → Return Space like a click) and its rows (`NeighborRowView`, a `DetailShapeRow`) lines of it, ↑ ↓ Home End walking them. The line the keys are on lights its shape as the pointer on its row does (`onHighlight` calls `list.hover` and the line before's `endHover`), and the keyboard leaving lets go; Return goes to the shape (`list.go(to:)`). The search field's ↓ enters the list and its Escape clears the text first. A row reads `NeighborLine.spoken` (name, the board when it isn't the one shown, caption), a head its title, count and "expanded" or "collapsed". A row's height is a line, and a caption's more (`DetailRowItem.height(captionLines:)`), so the list sizes itself; only the rows in view are made, as before (`linesDrawn`). AppKit logs "a reentrant operation in its NSTableView delegate" for the 3,000-row test (the table of contents' list does the same: [table of contents](table-of-contents.md)).

## The hover

- **What it is** (the owner's approved doc "Reach on hover", 2026-11): the one hover, with no switch and no user default. It lights everything each light's step leads to from the shape, and on from there: downstream on a plain hover, upstream with ⌥, mentions' and types' lights the same, each following its own query; Neighbors knows no edge type. Hop 1 is the one step, what a hover has always lit, shown at once; the rest comes once the pointer rests. The hover is `ReachState` (`editor.reach`), the service `NeighborsPlugin` registers. The View menu has no item for it, and the panel's lists stay one step (The lists, above).
- **The settings** (`ReachSettings`, `Sources/Neighbors/Reach/ReachSettings.swift`, `@MainActor`): one setting, `reducesMotion`, the system's Reduce Motion (`ReachSettings.systemReducesMotion()`, `NSWorkspace.shared.accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion`) unless a test gives its own (`ReachSettings(reducesMotion:)`). `ReachSettings.app` is the app's and `NeighborsPlugin()` reads it; `NeighborsPlugin(reach:)` takes a caller's. ShippedTesting's `ShippedPlugins` supplies one for each call (`ShippedPlugins.reach`, a new one each time, the pulse on whatever the system says), and every test that builds `NeighborsPlugin` by hand, or the list through `ShippedList.plugins(supplying:)`, passes one of its own (`NeighborsPlugin(reach: ReachSettings(reducesMotion: { false }))`), since `NeighborsPlugin()`'s editors read the app's.
- **The keys** (the user's ask, 2026-10-25: "when you _just_ hover, you should just see things the shape points _to_. when you press option and hover you should see things pointing _at it_. ctrl + option should show mentions"): the lights shown are those whose `keys` are exactly the held keys (`editor.modifiersHeld`, the core's, [editor](editor.md)) of the keys some light names (`choosing`, the union of the lights' keys, made once: ⌃ and ⌥ as shipped), so ⇧ (a click that adds to the selection) and ⌘ change nothing, ⌃ alone shows no light, and a list whose lights name no key shows them all whatever is held. The keys are read in the same tracking as the subject (`watch()` returns both; `keys` is the last look's); a look with the same subject and other keys restarts the reach (`restart()`: the dim's rest and the pulse begin again, and what has landed is worked out again for the keys: `compose()`), asking nothing, and keys changing with nothing hovered ask nothing. Every light is still asked once a hover whatever is held (a key's switch is at once, and the hover costs what it did), and the upkeep follows every light's matches, shown or not (the `matched` of both answers). `editor.modifiersHeld` hears the keys wherever the keyboard is in the window (the canvas's `flagsChanged`, CanvasView's window monitor, `modifiersChangedInWindow`, and every pointer event's), so ⌥ works over a table of contents' row and with a panel's field focused, and a key let go is let go at once.
- **The subject**: `editor.hovered`, else `editor.hoveredElsewhere` (Selection's: the shape the pointer is on where a plugin shows a shape the canvas doesn't, which the table of contents' row of a board nested under its owner's sets; [selection](selection.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md)). Both are read in one `withObservationTracking` (so a change of either tells it, not only the one that's used now), re-armed by every look (the tracking fires once): its `onChange`, told as the property is about to change (`willSet`), only schedules a look on `editor.scheduler` at zero delay (`hoverWillChange`, one at a time: `turn`), never reading or asking inside it. `look()` reads the subject and arms the next. A new subject begins (`begin`): its hop 1 is asked for at once, and the rest after the rest (`ReachStyle.rest`, 150 ms). No subject lets go after `ReachStyle.release` (150 ms: `stop()`), and the shape left, hovered again meanwhile, keeps what it showed. `subject` is public (the shape hovered, as the last look found it); `showing` is the shape whose reach shows, the subject or the one left while the release waits. `isAsking` is whether an answer is on its way or a look is due, and `landed()` (async) waits until none is (a benchmark's wait, which leaves the main thread idle, never polling). Debug builds count `asked`, `requested` (the one-hop queries the asks batched), `reachRequested` (the reach requests), `landed`, `landedOnMain` (none should be) and `composed` (the times what shows was worked out into new outlines).
- **Asking** (`ReachState.ask(about:)`): a new shape asks twice at once, two tasks: the lights' one hop (`light.lit(selector)` in one `queries.matches` batch, in `.resolved` and, where `topology.hasStandIns`, `.literal`, each shape once, the first view's first), which shows as hop 1 as soon as it lands (the walk costs some 9 µs a shape off the main actor, 180 ms for a reach of 20,000, so hop 1 can't wait for it); and each light's reach (`queries.reaches`, one `QueryReachRequest` a light: `light.query(":scope")`, `light.between?(":scope")`, `.resolved`, `strength: ReachStyle.strength` so a pill or an arrow that leads to several shapes is at the strongest of them, the engine reading both views and adding a pill the reach goes into at what it stands for), which the rest and the pulse show (its hop 1 the same shapes). A new subject or another board cancels both. A pill hovered: the reach's first step is taken in the literal view too (the engine does it for a start that is a stand-in: [queries](queries.md)), so its own arrows' far ends are hop 1 in the reach as in the one hop, and the walk goes on from them. A reach that fails (too slow, a refusal) shows the one hop in its place and no pulse when every light's failed, and a failed light's one hop beside the others' reach otherwise (`oneHopFound`); the reach waits for the one hop's answer to have it.
- **The landing** (`ReachLanding`, `Sources/Neighbors/Reach/ReachLanding.swift`, pure `Sendable` values): each answer is made into `ReachFound`s (a reach's shapes, then its carriers at their far ends' hops and shares, its spared with their fade and tier; a one-hop answer's all hop 1, share 1) and landed off the main actor (`@concurrent`) from `editor.file` and `editor.board` as the answer came: each match drawn as itself on the board shown, else the card that holds it (`file.shape(on:holding:)`, a walk a board), at the nearest hop and strongest strength of what it holds, none on a board above; the subject and its card never lit; for each set of keys some light has, the lights shown together in light order, a shape's ring how many of them it was in before, its rings in groups by tier (hop 1, 2, 3, and 4 and on), ring step and ring, and the dim's focus for each step of the pulse (`focus[0...4]`: the subject as drawn at 1, each shape of the tiers shown at its fade, the arriving tier's at 1, the spared). The main actor swaps the landing in and builds the outlines from its groups (a `TintedOutlines` a group, its color and width from its step, the arriving tier's at full) and the focus (`Dimming(focus:)`, a dictionary handed over as it is), so a pulse step picks groups and costs O(groups), never O(lit). Neither task holds the state across an `await` (each takes it again, `self?`, as it resumes, and reads the file and the board as values before it lands), so a window closed while an answer is on its way lets the editor and its services go and what resumes lands nothing. Debug builds count `landed` and `landedOnMain` (a landing made on the main thread: none).
- **Asking again**: a change to the edges (`topology.follow`) that touches the shape shown or a shape either answer matched, and a commit that names the shape other than by moving it, adds a shape, names a shape matched or drawn as a card other than by moving it, reaches another board or replaces the document, ask again once commits have stopped for 100 ms (`ReachStyle.settle`, re-armed by each: one ask for a run of them, as the panel's lists wait), keeping the pulse at its step (a reach settled stays settled). An edge change of any kind while an ask is out asks again once both answers have landed (`changedWhileAsking`), so an answer older than the edges doesn't stay, though nothing it touched was matched. A drag's moves ask nothing. A change lists the ends of the edges it changed, never the arrow that carries one, so an arrow hovered is asked about again by its ends: the end it's re-aimed away from, or the pill a provide re-points at its end. `boardDidChange` lets go of everything (`subject = nil`, `stop()`), keeping nothing per board.
- **What shows** (`compose()`): from what landed for the shape: its reach once it has rested, at the pulse's step, else its hop 1, whichever answer landed. The dim is drawn while the board dims, and only when something is lit (a shape that reaches nothing leaves the board as it is). What each answer draws is worked out when it lands, off the main actor (the landing above), so `compose()` only picks groups and works out the same outlines once (`Composed`: the landing, the keys, the step and the dim); it runs for a landing, a pulse step, the rest and a change of keys, never per frame, and a drag's events work out nothing (a session's outlines are empty).
- **What the core gets** (`outlines`, internal): empty while `editor.session != nil` (a drag; what was lit shows again when it ends, and an answer that lands meanwhile is kept), else `shown`, the `Outlines(tinted:dim:)` made by `compose()` (`Outlines()` when nothing is lit), handed over as it is: a frame costs a copy of four array references and nothing is allocated, and the core strokes only what's in view ([drawing](drawing.md)); the core's tinted groups are under every other outline, so a selected or hovered shape's line shows over its tint, ring 0 where the selection's line stands (a line 2 wide, 4 off a closed shape) and each ring after it a line (2) and a gap (2) further out, so a ring's center is 4, 8, 12 off: whole pixels at 1× and 2×. The core does the rest of how it looks, for any group, knowing no light: a shape that's selected too has its tint one ring further out, clear of the selection's line, which would cover most of ring 0 (a note both lit and selected shows its green at 8); an arrow lit is covered, its outline as wide as its line is on screen and a point more (an arrow says its width, `StrokedShape`) rather than a stripe along it, so a lit arrow is its light's color, heads and all; and the hovered shape, whose faint outline Selection draws, reads above the lights: 85%, not 60%, while any is drawn. Validation's ring stands outside the lit rings (`OverlayContext.tintedOutset(of:)`), so a warned neighbor's ring isn't drawn over its tint. The canvas is asked to draw (`editor.setNeedsDisplay()`) only when a new `shown` differs from the old (`Outlines` and `TintedOutlines` are `Equatable`): an answer that lights nothing, after one that lit nothing, draws nothing. The minimap fills the same shapes in the same colors, so shapes off screen show there: a shape in several lights shows each, one inset inside the other, and while any is lit the rest of the picture dims and the lit fills are over the viewport's veil ([drawing](drawing.md)). Neighbors never outlines a shape faintly itself: `faintShapes` are Selection's alone (the hover, and the shape that holds the one hovered elsewhere).
- **Strength** (`ReachStyle`, `Sources/Neighbors/Reach/ReachStyle.swift`: every number of the look and the timing): 1 at hop 1; past it 0.6^(hop−1) × min(1, share), the share being the engine's (what reaches a shape splits among the shapes it reaches next, and adds up where paths meet: [queries](queries.md)). A ring is drawn at its strength in steps of 1/40 (`ringStep`), 1 + 2.5 × strength view points wide (`TintedOutlines.width`, [drawing](drawing.md)), in its light's color mixed toward the canvas (`Swatch.canvas`, each appearance's) by the step's strength, opaque, so an arrow's line doesn't show through its cover (`ReachStyle.color`, made once a light and step: `colors`); none under 4%, whose shape sinks into the dim. The dim keeps each lit shape at 1 for hop 1, else the nearest of 0.65, 0.35 and 0.12 (`fade`: people tell three or four ordered steps apart, and each step the core draws is a transparency layer, some 3.5 ms a frame offscreen).
- **The arrows.** One the reach travels (from a hop to the next: the answer's `carriers`) is lit at the strength of the shape it reaches; one between two shapes of the reach, or one and the shape hovered, that it didn't travel (`spared`: a cycle's, two at one hop) is kept by the dim, untinted, at its weaker end's strength (the start's 1) and never above the second step (`ReachStyle.sparedMost`, 0.35: it isn't the reach's path, and at full strength on a dense board such arrows read as patches of noise beside the lit ones), arriving with its later end; every other arrow dims. A cycle never lights the shape hovered.
- **The timing** (all on `editor.scheduler`): the subject and the keys are watched as above. A new shape shows its hop 1 at once, with no dim; once the pointer has rested on it 150 ms (`ReachStyle.rest`) the board dims (`dims`; a shape that lights nothing leaves the board as it is: there is nothing to pick out) and, once its reach has landed, the pulse begins: each 80 ms (`pulseStep`) a tier arrives, drawn as hop 1 is (full strength, the widest ring) for that step and at its own strength from the next, hops 4 and on together at 240 ms, settled (`pulseStep` 4) by 320; with `ReachStyle.pulses` off or `reducesMotion`, settled at once. The dim stays as the pointer goes from shape to shape (each showing its hop 1 until it rests: the pulse waits for the rest, so a sweep never pulses). Other keys on the same shape (⌥ held or let go) show the other pole's hop 1 at once and its pulse after a new rest, asking nothing (every light was asked). With nothing hovered the dim and the lights stay 150 ms (`release`), then go (`stop()`); the shape left, hovered again meanwhile, keeps what it showed and asks nothing. A drag hides everything (`outlines` is empty while `editor.session` is set, and the core hides a dim then), and what was shown comes back with the drag's end. While the camera moves (a pan, a pinch, a slide, an animation) the core leaves the dim out and draws the board as the hover alone does, the rings and every other outline as ever, and draws it again 150 ms after the camera last changed (`Style.dimCameraRest`, the core's, [drawing](drawing.md)): ReachState keeps offering its dim the whole while and knows nothing of the camera; the dim's transparency layers had a pan on a reach under 60 frames a second on screen (about 41 around a card with ⌥ held). Another board lets go of everything.
- **What it costs.** The main actor's part of a hover is reading the subject, building the two batches and swapping each landing in; the engine's batches and the landing run off it; a pulse step is O(groups), a few hundred at most. The engine's walk is some 9 µs a shape with between (Queries'). The dim's frame cost grows with the populated strength steps (three and full), some 3.5 ms a layer offscreen, not with what's lit ([drawing](drawing.md)). `ReachBenchmarks.swift` holds the hover, the walk, the landing, a pulse step and the frame ([benchmarks](benchmarks.md)); a fan-out, all hop 1, lands at some 0.1 µs a lit shape on the main actor, which the chain's 200,000 lit (1.2 ms) does not.
