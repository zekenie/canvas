# Neighbors

Neighbors: hover a shape, on the canvas or by its row in the table of contents, and every shape connected to it is outlined in a color that says how, in rings where a shape is in two; the lights (a color and a query), the subject, asking and asking again, where what's matched is drawn, and the core's tinted outlines and the minimap's fills it hands; a pointer is a mention to the lights.

Related: [queries](queries.md), [topology](topology.md), [selection](selection.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [editor](editor.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [connections](connections.md), [references](references.md), [jump to](jump-to.md), [moving](moving.md), [benchmarks](benchmarks.md), [types](types.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Neighbors](../../docs/plugin-system.md#neighbors), [Outlines](../../docs/plugin-system.md#outlines), [The table of contents](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-table-of-contents).

## Architecture

- Neighbors (a surface: what an edge's owner shows its edges on; plugin id `neighbors`) is on Canvas, Queries, Selection and Topology, and knows no kind, edge type or concern: `NeighborsPlugin()` (with `NeighborLight`, its point `NeighborLights`, `registrar.neighborLight(color:query:)`, `registry.neighborLights` and `NeighborState`, its whole public surface) registers a service, `NeighborState` (`editor.neighbors`, a `@MainActor` class, an `EditorService` in an extension), made with the registry's lights (`NeighborState(editor: $0, lights: $0.registry.neighborLights)`), and `registrar.outlines { $0.neighbors?.outlines ?? Outlines() }`; the report's line is `neighbors service NeighborState · outlines`, between `query-commands` and the lights' two (`arrow-lights` and `mention-lights`, each `neighbor light · neighbor light`), since it comes right after the query plugins in the shipped list. It contributes to no point whose owner a list might lack, so a list without Queries, Topology or Selection isn't refused: it lights nothing (no engine or no Topology, nothing is asked; no Selection, nothing is hovered; no light, nothing to ask).
- **The point** (`NeighborLights`, an `ExtensionPoint` in an extension, owner `neighbors`, its `summary` "neighbor light"): a plugin that owns a kind of edge gives a light with `registrar.neighborLight(color:query:)`, in a plugin value of its own (a face), after Neighbors and the plugin that declares the edges, so a list without Neighbors leaves it out and one with the face and no Neighbors is refused (`ownerNotInstalled`). The registry keeps them in plugin order, and a plugin's in the order it gives them (`registry.neighborLights`). Shipped: Connections' `ArrowLightsPlugin` (id `arrow-lights`, `Sources/Connections/ArrowLights.swift`: points to, points to it) and then Mentions' `MentionLightsPlugin` (id `mention-lights`, `Sources/Mentions/MentionLights.swift`: mentions, mentioned by), so Connections and Mentions import Neighbors, and nothing writes a light for them: the list of what ships names the three plugins, in that order.
- **A light** (`NeighborLight`, `Sendable`): `color: NSColor` and `query: @Sendable (String) -> String`, given the selector that names the shape hovered (`#` and its id, `"#\(id)"`: the query language's, `uuidString`) and returning the query whose matches are outlined in that color. The shipped four: points to (green, `ArrowStyle.pointsToLight`: `:edge-from(X, arrow|stands-for), :starts-at(X, arrow), :end-of(X, arrow)`), points to it (purple, `ArrowStyle.pointedToByLight`: `:edge-to(X, arrow|stands-for), :ends-at(X, arrow), :start-of(X, arrow)`), mentions (teal, `MentionStyle.mentionsLight`: `:edge-from(X, mention)`) and mentioned by (lavender, `MentionStyle.mentionedByLight`: `:edge-to(X, mention)`), written from `ArrowEdge.id`, `StandingEdge.id` and `MentionEdge.id`, never as literals (the audit holds that). The colors were chosen together in a design audit, from renders of the lights over both canvases, as fixed sRGB values for each appearance (`NeighborLight.color(light:dark:)`, two `0xRRGGBB` values in an `NSColor(name:)`, dark by `bestMatch(from: [.aqua, .darkAqua])`, as `ArrowStyle.color` is), never system colors (system pink read as Validation's error red, system purple as the selection's blue, and light-mode green and teal were under 3:1): green #07933B light and #2FD359 dark, purple #8C21A4 and #B851FD, teal #0E97B0 and #329AB4, lavender #A76FE2 and #D0B0FE. Each is clear of Validation's red and orange and Find's orange, at least 3:1 against the canvas it's drawn over in both appearances, and not the accent; two of the four, the purples, are close for some color-vision deficiencies. The words are the language's own (`:edge-from(S, types)`: the shapes the anchor has an edge to, `:starts-at(S, types)`: the connections that start at it, `:end-of(S, types)` and `:start-of(S, types)`: where a connection the anchor matches ends and starts, so an arrow hovered lights the shape it ends at as what it points to and the one it starts at as what points to it; a type takes in those under it). The arrows between are matched too, as carriers, so they take their light's color. Order matters: a shape's first light is its first ring.
- **The subject**: `editor.hovered`, else `editor.hoveredElsewhere` (Selection's: the shape the pointer is on where a plugin shows a shape the canvas doesn't, which the table of contents' row of a board nested under its owner's sets; [selection](selection.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md)). Both are read in one `withObservationTracking` (so a change of either tells it, not only the one that's used now), re-armed by every look (the tracking fires once): its `onChange`, told as the property is about to change (`willSet`), only schedules a look on `editor.scheduler` at zero delay (`hoverWillChange`, one at a time: `turn`), never reading or asking inside it; `look()` reads the subject (arming the next), and a subject that changed asks (`ask(about:)`) or, none, lets go (`stop()`). `subject` is public; `isAsking` is whether an answer is on its way or a look is due, `landed()` (async) waits until none is on its way (a benchmark's wait, which leaves the main thread idle, never polling), and debug builds count `asked`, `requested` (the queries those asks batched) and `placed`.
- **Asking** (`ask(about:)`): without `editor.queries`, `editor.topology` or a light it lets go of everything (`stop()`) and asks nothing; else any ask running is cancelled (the engine's evaluation looks every 256 shapes, a cancelled batch fails every request `cancelled`), then one batch: `QueryMatchRequest(query(selector), edges: view, limit: QueryBudget.maxRows)` for each light in each view, ids only, in a `Task` (`asking`) that calls `queries.matches`. The views are `.resolved` and, only where some shape stands in for others (`topology.hasStandIns`, O(1)), `.literal` too: with no stand-in the two read the same edges, so one is asked, and the answers are read by `views.count`. The batch takes up the file once (one capture, run alongside any other query: the engine runs them at once) however many lights, and checks each text once (a light's two views share its check). When it lands (not cancelled: every change of the subject cancels the ask, `look` and `boardDidChange` both, so one that lands is for the subject now) each light keeps the union of its views' ids (the resolved view's first; lists that are equal, as they are when nothing that stands in matters, are one array comparison and nothing more), a refused request lights nothing, `lit` (`Lit`: the subject the answer is for, each light's ids, and `matched`, a set of every id any light matched, made as the answer lands) is replaced, and what shows is worked out (`place`). Until it lands the lights of the shape before stay (`lit` is that answer's, so a commit meanwhile places it against its own subject); when the subject goes (`stop()`: it lets go of the ask and of `lit`) they go at once, and the next hover asks as the first did.
- **Asking again** (one ask for a commit): `editor.topology?.follow { … }` (`following`, kept while the service lives) calls `edgesChanged` when a change touches the subject or a shape the last answer matched (`lit.matched`): a commit's, an undo's, a file read afresh, or a provide's (a dynamic reference's answer arriving changes what a stand-in stands for with no commit). A change lists the ends of the edges it changed, never their carrier, so for an arrow hovered, whose answer is its own edge (where it starts and ends: `:start-of`, `:end-of`), the end it was matched at is what says it moved, and a provide or a commit that re-points a pill at the arrow's end touches the pill, matched in the literal view; a change to the edges of what was matched asks again though the subject's own are as they were, since nothing says which of the matches the lights follow. `documentDidChange` asks again too for a commit that names the subject other than by moving it (an arrow's own record changed: an end it had none at is bound, which no change lists but by the ends, or the undo of a re-aim: `changed`, `added` or `elsewhere` holds it) or adds a shape the answer didn't match (`added`; `elsewhere` for what came to another board, a shape still in the file): a name outlives its shape, so a mention of a shape deleted before the hover left its edge as it was, and the shape back changes no edge, but is what the answer left out (the lights are queries, and a new shape may be what one finds); a shape moved back onto the board shown, already matched, only places again. `askedAt` (`editor.revision` as the last ask began) leaves it at one ask when the commit's change to the edges asked already. Each calls the same `ask(about:)`, which lets go of one under way, and it's called inside Topology's delivery or the commit's, so it only starts the `Task` (nothing is taken up then: the task runs after the commit, over the file as the commit left it). A commit that changes the edges of shapes the answer didn't match, or only moves shapes, asks nothing (debug builds' `asked`). `boardDidChange` (overridden, so the default's change set of both boards is never worked out) lets go of everything (`subject = nil`, `stop()`): the hover is let go of with the switch anyway, and the look it schedules finds nothing.
- **What shows** (`place(_:)`): from `lit` and the file as it is: each matched id is drawn as itself if it's on the board shown (`file.board(containing:) == editor.board`), else as the shape on the board shown that holds it (`file.shape(on: editor.board, holding:)`: a subboard's card, the pocket it's in, at any depth), and none if no shape on it does (a shape on a board above, or not in the file). The walk up the boards is one for each distinct board the matches are on, kept in a dictionary for the pass, never one for each match. The subject is dropped, and, where the subject isn't on the board shown, the card holding it, which Selection outlines faintly instead (it's `hoveredElsewhere`'s holder, [selection](selection.md)), as that's where the subject is. Each shape once per light, and its ring is how many lights it's in before: `visits[shape, default: Visit()].enter(light)` is one dictionary update per id (nil for a shape already in this light, which also dedupes a card drawn for several matches and one matched itself), so the pass costs a lookup and an update per id, O(what's lit, and a walk for each board of those that aren't the one shown). One `TintedOutlines` for each (light, ring) that has shapes, in light order then ring, as one `Outlines(tinted:)`; `cards` keeps the shapes a match is drawn as where it isn't itself (the subject's card among them). It runs when an answer lands and in `documentDidChange`, only for a commit that names a shape matched or one of `cards` other than by moving it (added, removed, changed by an edit or a restack, or `elsewhere`), reaches another board (`boardsReached`: where a shape is drawn follows what hangs from what, and any move between boards, and any deletion that takes one, reaches one) or replaces the document; any other commit, a drag's every event and a keystroke in an unrelated note among them, costs a look at its change set and a few set intersections (`names(_:in:)`). It asks nothing: the edges are Topology's to say, and a move down a board or a deletion changes where a shape is drawn, not its edges (a mention is a name that outlives the shape, so a shape moved or deleted leaves its mention edge as it was). Never per frame.
- **What the core gets** (`outlines`, internal): empty while `editor.session != nil` (a drag; what was lit shows again when it ends, and an answer that lands meanwhile is kept), else `shown`, the `Outlines(tinted: groups)` made by `place` (`Outlines()` when nothing is lit), handed over as it is: a frame costs a copy of four array references and nothing is allocated, and the core strokes only what's in view ([drawing](drawing.md)); the core's tinted groups are under every other outline, so a selected or hovered shape's line shows over its tint, ring 0 where the selection's line stands (a line 2 wide, 4 off a closed shape) and each ring after it a line (2) and a gap (2) further out, so a ring's center is 4, 8, 12 off: whole pixels at 1× and 2×. The core does the rest of how it looks, for any group, knowing no light: a shape that's selected too has its tint one ring further out, clear of the selection's line, which would cover most of ring 0 (a note both lit and selected shows its green at 8); an arrow lit is covered, its outline as wide as its line is on screen and a point more (an arrow says its width, `StrokedShape`) rather than a stripe along it, so a lit arrow is its light's color, heads and all; and the hovered shape, whose faint outline Selection draws, reads above the lights: 85%, not 60%, while any is drawn. Validation's ring stands outside the lit rings (`OverlayContext.tintedOutset(of:)`), so a warned neighbor's ring isn't drawn over its tint. The canvas is asked to draw (`editor.setNeedsDisplay()`) only when a new `shown` differs from the old (`Outlines` and `TintedOutlines` are `Equatable`): an answer that lights nothing, after one that lit nothing, draws nothing. The minimap fills the same shapes in the same colors, so shapes off screen show there: a shape in several lights shows each, one inset inside the other, and while any is lit the rest of the picture dims and the lit fills are over the viewport's veil ([drawing](drawing.md)). Neighbors never outlines a shape faintly itself: `faintShapes` are Selection's alone (the hover, and the shape that holds the one hovered elsewhere).
- **Costs** (release, as `./build.sh bench` measures them): a hover on a shape with 10,000 neighbors costs the main actor some 0.8 ms (some 0.2 at 1,000, 0.14 with ten, whatever the board's size) from the hover to the lights landing (the set of what matched some 0.1 of it), and the engine's answer, off the main actor, some 10 ms (one view is asked where nothing stands in); 10,000 lit in view at 10% add some 7 ms to a canvas frame (the rings' paths, as ⌘A's do) and 4 to the minimap's, and all out of view some 0.35 ms (a look at each one's kept extent). Only what's one edge away is lit (the shipped queries are the language's one-step words).
- A pointer is a mention to the lights: Types' `field-link` is a child of `mention`, so the mention lights' `:edge-from($, mention)` and `:edge-to($, mention)` take it in with no light of its own: hovering an Order outlines the Person it points at in the lights' teal, hovering her outlines the Order in the lavender, and asking again follows the edges' changes as for a mention. [types](types.md).

## Testing

- NeighborsTests use fixtures only (CanvasTesting's boxes, links, which are wires, memos, whose lead is a quote, pockets, corrals, `NamingPlugin`; a `Pill` that stands for the shapes it cites and a `Lens` for what's provided, over `standsIn`; the edge types `wire` and `quote` declared by `graphPlugin()`; `testLights()`, four lights of the tests' own in pure colors (`Light`), written as the shipped ones are, an arrow's ends included, and `lightsPlugin(_:)`, a contributor that gives them as a plugin does; `neighborHarness(file)`: `KeyboardPlugin`, `SelectionPlugin`, `TopologyPlugin`, the fixtures, `QueriesPlugin`, then `NeighborsPlugin()` and `lightsPlugin(lights)`, with no table of contents, so a shape hovered elsewhere is set as a list would), driven with `h.editor.hovered = id`, `h.editor.hoveredElsewhere = id` and `h.move(to:)` over a shape, and read as `h.lit` (`editor.outlines.tinted` as `Lit(light, ring, shapes)`) and `h.faint` after `h.settle()` (a turn of the scheduler, then the run loop until `isAsking` is false); `Redraws` counts what the editor tells its view. The scenarios: `Star` (S, a memo quoting Q; B, which S has a wire to; C, which has one to S; M, a memo quoting S; and U, which touches nothing S does) and `Nest` (S; pocket P, holding X, Z, a wire from X to Z and pocket Q, whose board holds Y; wires from S to X and to Y). LightTests: each light's matches in its color and the arrows with them, one batch for four lights (`captures`) in the one view when nothing stands in and in two once a pill does (`requested`), an arrow hovered lighting where it ends and starts, a shape in two and in three lights (rings 0, 1, 2) and the subject never lit though it matches itself, a pocket lit once for what's on its board and two boards down, nothing from a board above, a frame's shape lighting the pocket, a reference's pill and what it stands for (all three hovers), a refused light lighting nothing while the others do, the lights in plugin order and each plugin's own (none from Neighbors alone, and a list with a light and no Neighbors refused), and a shape deleted dropping out at once and back with its undo, nothing asked. SubjectTests: the pointer on a shape, a shape hovered elsewhere (its card faint, never lit; two boards down; one on the board shown isn't one), the canvas's hover winning over it, the lights before staying until the next answer lands and going at once with the hover, `landed()` returning once the answer is in (at once with none on its way, and after a re-ask), a changed hover cancelling the ask (`captures` once), an answer for a shape that isn't the subject landing nothing, a board switch (with an ask under way, and with a shape hovered elsewhere), a session hiding the lights and an answer meanwhile kept, and no engine or no light. UpkeepTests: asking again for a wire added, deleted and undone and for a change to the edges of a lit shape, a shape added that no answer matched asking too, and none for others' edges or moves (`asked`, once for a commit whose change to the edges asked already); an arrow hovered re-aimed and undone, an end let go of and bound again, and an end deleted and undone; a provide, and a commit, that re-point a lens or a pill at an arrow's end; a shape deleted before the hover and back after it, lit when it comes back (on this board and on another); a commit in the middle of an ask letting go of it; a lit shape moved onto a pocket's board and back, drawn as the pocket and then itself with nothing asked (`placed` once, not for a drag's moves, nor for an edit or a restack of a shape that isn't lit, but for those of one that is); a pocket deleted and undone; a provide changing what a lens stands for (and one that changes nothing asking nothing); the canvas asked to draw again only when what's outlined changed (`Redraws`); and nothing worked out with nothing hovered.
