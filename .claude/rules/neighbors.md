# Neighbors

Neighbors: hover a shape, on the canvas or by its row in the table of contents, and what it points to is outlined (with ⌥ held, what points to it; with ⌃⌥, its mentions both ways) in a color that says how, in rings where a shape is in two lights shown; select one, and its panel's Connections tab lists them by the same ways, then what it's inside and what it holds; the lights (a title, a color, the keys that show it and a query), the keys held, the subject, asking and asking again, where what's matched is drawn, the core's tinted outlines and the minimap's fills it hands, and the lists (folding, searching, their rows); and the whole reach (View › Show Whole Reach): everything downstream of the shape hovered, or upstream with ⌥, each shape fading with its hop and share, the rest of the board dimmed once the pointer rests, the reach coming in a hop at a time.

Related: [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [fuzzy matching](fuzzy-matching.md), [publishing](publishing.md), [queries](queries.md), [topology](topology.md), [selection](selection.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [drawing](drawing.md), [editor](editor.md), [mentions](mentions.md), [connections](connections.md), [references](references.md), [jump to](jump-to.md), [moving](moving.md), [benchmarks](benchmarks.md), [types](types.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Neighbors](../../docs/plugin-system.md#neighbors), [Outlines](../../docs/plugin-system.md#outlines), [The table of contents](../../docs/plugin-system.md#the-table-of-contents).

## Architecture

- Neighbors (platform, an engine: what an edge's owner shows its edges on; plugin id `neighbors`) is on Canvas, DetailPanel, FuzzyMatching, Queries, Selection, TableOfContents and Topology, and knows no kind, edge type or concern. It shows a shape's edges two ways, from the same lights: outlined on the canvas for the shape hovered (`NeighborsPlugin`, below), and listed in the panel for the shape selected (`NeighborDetailsPlugin`, a plugin value of its own: The lists, below). `NeighborsPlugin()` (with `NeighborLight`, its point `NeighborLights`, `registrar.neighborLight(_:color:keys:query:between:caption:)`, `registry.neighborLights` and `NeighborState`) registers a service, `HoverLights` (`editor.hoverLights`, a `@MainActor` class, an `EditorService` in an extension), made with the registry's lights and its settings (`HoverLights(editor: $0, lights: $0.registry.neighborLights, settings:)`), which holds `NeighborState` (`editor.neighbors`, a `@MainActor` class made with `NeighborState(editor:lights:)`) or, while Show Whole Reach is on, `ReachState` (below), and `registrar.outlines { $0.hoverLights?.outlines ?? Outlines() }`, between `query-commands` and the lights' three (`arrow-lights`, `mention-lights` and `type-lights`, each `neighbor light · neighbor light` and its key hint), since it comes right after the query plugins in the shipped list. `NeighborState` is one hop, everything below but The whole reach, and `ReachState` (`editor.reach`) the whole reach, as `ReachSettings.showsWholeReach` says (The whole reach, below); only that one exists, and `HoverLights` hands commits and board switches to it. The report's line is `neighbors service HoverLights · outlines · command show-whole-reach last in ViewMenu.Panels`. It contributes to no point whose owner a list might lack, so a list without Queries, Topology or Selection isn't refused: it lights nothing (no engine or no Topology, nothing is asked; no Selection, nothing is hovered; no light, nothing to ask).
- **The point** (`NeighborLights`, an `ExtensionPoint` in an extension, owner `neighbors`, its `summary` "neighbor light"): a plugin that owns a kind of edge gives a light with `registrar.neighborLight(_:color:keys:query:between:caption:)`, in a plugin value of its own (a face), after Neighbors and the plugin that declares the edges, so a list without Neighbors leaves it out and one with the face and no Neighbors is refused (`ownerNotInstalled`). The registry keeps them in plugin order, and a plugin's in the order it gives them (`registry.neighborLights`). Shipped: Arrows' `ArrowLightsPlugin` (id `arrow-lights`, `Sources/Arrows/ArrowLights.swift`: points to, points to it) then Mentions' `MentionLightsPlugin` (id `mention-lights`, `Sources/Mentions/MentionLights.swift`: mentions, mentioned by), and then Types' `TypeLightsPlugin` (id `type-lights`, `Sources/Types/Engines/TypeLights.swift`: instances, subtypes), so Arrows, Mentions and Types import Neighbors, and nothing writes a light for them: the list of what ships names the four plugins, in that order, and then the tab's (`NeighborDetailsPlugin`, below).
- **A light** (`NeighborLight`, `Sendable`): a `title` (what the panel heads its list with), a `color: NSColor`, `keys: Modifiers` (the keys held while hovering that show it, `[]` by default: a plain hover; the panel lists every light whatever is held), and three closures. `query: @Sendable (String) -> String` is given the selector that names the shape (`#` and its id, `"#\(id)"`: the query language's, `uuidString`) and returns the query that finds the shapes at the other end: outlined in the color, and listed. `between`, optional, finds what runs between the shape and those (the arrows): outlined with them, never listed. `caption`, optional and `@MainActor`, is a line under a listed shape's name, from the editor, the shape the list is of and the listed one, asked of the rows in view as each is drawn. `lit(_:)` is what a hover asks: the query, then `, ` and `between`'s, one query a light. The shipped six, by their keys a plain hover what a shape points to, ⌥ what points to it (and what is of a type, whose `is-a` edges run to it), ⌃⌥ its mentions both ways: Points To (green, `ArrowStyle.pointsToLight`, no keys: `:edge-from(X, arrow|stands-for), :end-of(X, arrow)`, between `:starts-at(X, arrow)`), Pointed To By (purple, `ArrowStyle.pointedToByLight`, ⌥: `:edge-to(X, arrow|stands-for), :start-of(X, arrow)`, between `:ends-at(X, arrow)`), Mentions (teal, `MentionStyle.mentionsLight`, ⌃⌥: `:edge-from(X, mention)`), Mentioned By (lavender, `MentionStyle.mentionedByLight`, ⌃⌥: `:edge-to(X, mention)`, its caption the mentioner's text around its mention, `MentionState.quote(of:around:)`), Instances (pink, `TypesStyle.instancesLight`, ⌥: `:is-a(X[schema]):not([schema])`, what is of it however far while it is a type, never a type (a shape whose schema was taken away lists none, though shapes still name it); a relationship type's are its arrows) and Subtypes (the same pink, ⌥: `[schema]:is-a(X[schema])`, the types built on it however far; a shape is one or the other, so the two share the color and their lists' titles tell them apart; no reverse light, since a shape's chips and Types tab say what it is), their titles in `ArrowStyle`, `MentionStyle` and `TypesStyle`, their queries written from `ArrowEdge.id`, `StandingEdge.id`, `MentionEdge.id` and `IsAEdge.id`, never as literals (the audit holds that). The colors were chosen together in a design audit, from renders of the lights over both canvases, as fixed sRGB values for each appearance (`NeighborLight.color(light:dark:)`, two `0xRRGGBB` values in [Palette](palette.md)'s `NSColor(light:dark:)`), never system colors (system pink read as Validation's error red, system purple as the selection's blue, and light-mode green and teal were under 3:1): green #07933B light and #2FD359 dark, purple #8C21A4 and #B851FD, teal #0E97B0 and #329AB4, lavender #A76FE2 and #D0B0FE. Each is clear of Validation's red and orange and Find's orange, at least 3:1 against the canvas it's drawn over in both appearances, and not the accent; two of the four, the purples, are close for some color-vision deficiencies. Types' pink, #C2307A light and #F07AB5 dark (4.9:1 and 6.6:1), came after the audit, by the same rules, from renders of the hover beside the four and an orange warning ring: gold, the other hue the four leave, read as a yellow note's own edge on the dark canvas and sat beside Validation's orange; pink is the nearest of the five to Validation's red. The words are the language's own (`:edge-from(S, types)`: the shapes the anchor has an edge to, `:starts-at(S, types)`: the connections that start at it, `:end-of(S, types)` and `:start-of(S, types)`: where a connection the anchor matches ends and starts, so an arrow hovered lights the shape it ends at as what it points to and the one it starts at as what points to it; a type takes in those under it). The arrows between are matched too, as carriers, so they take their light's color. Order matters: a shape's first light shown is its first ring. Each owner says its keys beside its lights with `registrar.keyHint(…, while: .pointer)`, once for its keys (the Keyboard Shortcuts window lists a key once): Arrows' "⌥ Hover", "Show What Points to It"; Mentions' "⌃⌥ Hover", "Show Mentions"; Types' "⌥ Hover a Type", "Show Its Instances and Subtypes".
- **The keys** (the user's ask, 2026-10-25: "when you _just_ hover, you should just see things the shape points _to_. when you press option and hover you should see things pointing _at it_. ctrl + option should show mentions"): the lights shown are those whose `keys` are exactly the held keys (`editor.modifiersHeld`, the core's, [editor](editor.md)) of the keys some light names (`choosing`, the union of the lights' keys, made once: ⌃ and ⌥ as shipped), so ⇧ (a click that adds to the selection) and ⌘ change nothing, ⌃ alone shows no light, and a list whose lights name no key shows them all whatever is held. The keys are read in the same tracking as the subject (`watch()` returns both; `keys` is the last look's); `look()` with the same subject and other keys only places `lit` again (`place`, which skips the lights not shown, so their matches take no ring and no card), asking nothing, and keys changing with nothing hovered do nothing. Every light is still asked once a hover whatever is held (a key's switch is at once, and the hover costs what it did), and the upkeep follows every light's matches (`lit.matched`), shown or not. `editor.modifiersHeld` hears the keys wherever the keyboard is in the window (the canvas's `flagsChanged`, CanvasView's window monitor, `modifiersChangedInWindow`, and every pointer event's), so ⌥ works over a table of contents' row and with a panel's field focused, and a key let go is let go at once.
- **The subject**: `editor.hovered`, else `editor.hoveredElsewhere` (Selection's: the shape the pointer is on where a plugin shows a shape the canvas doesn't, which the table of contents' row of a board nested under its owner's sets; [selection](selection.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md)). Both are read in one `withObservationTracking` (so a change of either tells it, not only the one that's used now), re-armed by every look (the tracking fires once): its `onChange`, told as the property is about to change (`willSet`), only schedules a look on `editor.scheduler` at zero delay (`hoverWillChange`, one at a time: `turn`), never reading or asking inside it; `look()` reads the subject (arming the next), and a subject that changed asks (`ask(about:)`) or, none, lets go (`stop()`). `subject` is public; `isAsking` is whether an answer is on its way or a look is due, `landed()` (async) waits until none is on its way (a benchmark's wait, which leaves the main thread idle, never polling), and debug builds count `asked`, `requested` (the queries those asks batched) and `placed`.
- **Asking** (`ask(about:)`): without `editor.queries`, `editor.topology` or a light it lets go of everything (`stop()`) and asks nothing; else any ask running is cancelled (the engine's evaluation looks every 256 shapes, a cancelled batch fails every request `cancelled`), then one batch: `QueryMatchRequest(light.lit(selector), edges: view, limit: QueryMatchRequest.every)` for each light in each view, every match, ids only, in a `Task` (`asking`) that calls `queries.matches`. The views are `.resolved` and, only where some shape stands in for others (`topology.hasStandIns`, O(1)), `.literal` too: with no stand-in the two read the same edges, so one is asked, and the answers are read by `views.count`. The batch takes up the file once (one capture, run alongside any other query: the engine runs them at once) however many lights, and checks each text once (a light's two views share its check). When it lands (not cancelled: every change of the subject cancels the ask, `look` and `boardDidChange` both, so one that lands is for the subject now) each light keeps the union of its views' ids (the resolved view's first; lists that are equal, as they are when nothing that stands in matters, are one array comparison and nothing more), a refused request lights nothing, `lit` (`Lit`: the subject the answer is for, each light's ids, and `matched`, a set of every id any light matched, made as the answer lands) is replaced, and what shows is worked out (`place`). Until it lands the lights of the shape before stay (`lit` is that answer's, so a commit meanwhile places it against its own subject); when the subject goes (`stop()`: it lets go of the ask and of `lit`) they go at once, and the next hover asks as the first did.
- **Asking again** (one ask for a commit): `editor.topology?.follow { … }` (`following`, kept while the service lives) calls `edgesChanged` when a change touches the subject or a shape the last answer matched (`lit.matched`): a commit's, an undo's, a file read afresh, or a provide's (a dynamic reference's answer arriving changes what a stand-in stands for with no commit). A change lists the ends of the edges it changed, never their carrier, so for an arrow hovered, whose answer is its own edge (where it starts and ends: `:start-of`, `:end-of`), the end it was matched at is what says it moved, and a provide or a commit that re-points a pill at the arrow's end touches the pill, matched in the literal view; a change to the edges of what was matched asks again though the subject's own are as they were, since nothing says which of the matches the lights follow. `documentDidChange` asks again too for a commit that names the subject other than by moving it (an arrow's own record changed: an end it had none at is bound, which no change lists but by the ends, or the undo of a re-aim: `changed`, `added` or `elsewhere` holds it) or adds a shape the answer didn't match (`added`; `elsewhere` for what came to another board, a shape still in the file): a name outlives its shape, so a mention of a shape deleted before the hover left its edge as it was, and the shape back changes no edge, but is what the answer left out (the lights are queries, and a new shape may be what one finds); a shape moved back onto the board shown, already matched, only places again. `askedAt` (`editor.revision` as the last ask began) leaves it at one ask when the commit's change to the edges asked already. Each calls the same `ask(about:)`, which lets go of one under way, and it's called inside Topology's delivery or the commit's, so it only starts the `Task` (nothing is taken up then: the task runs after the commit, over the file as the commit left it). A commit that changes the edges of shapes the answer didn't match, or only moves shapes, asks nothing (debug builds' `asked`). `boardDidChange` (overridden, so the default's change set of both boards is never worked out) lets go of everything (`subject = nil`, `stop()`): the hover is let go of with the switch anyway, and the look it schedules finds nothing.
- **What shows** (`place(_:)`): from `lit` and the file as it is: each matched id is drawn as itself if it's on the board shown (`file.board(containing:) == editor.board`), else as the shape on the board shown that holds it (`file.shape(on: editor.board, holding:)`: a subboard's card, the pocket it's in, at any depth), and none if no shape on it does (a shape on a board above, or not in the file). The walk up the boards is one for each distinct board the matches are on, kept in a dictionary for the pass, never one for each match. The subject is dropped, and, where the subject isn't on the board shown, the card holding it, which Selection outlines faintly instead (it's `hoveredElsewhere`'s holder, [selection](selection.md)), as that's where the subject is. Each shape once per light, and its ring is how many lights it's in before: `visits[shape, default: Visit()].enter(light)` is one dictionary update per id (nil for a shape already in this light, which also dedupes a card drawn for several matches and one matched itself), so the pass costs a lookup and an update per id, O(what's lit, and a walk for each board of those that aren't the one shown). One `TintedOutlines` for each (light, ring) that has shapes, in light order then ring, as one `Outlines(tinted:)`; `cards` keeps the shapes a match is drawn as where it isn't itself (the subject's card among them). It runs when an answer lands and in `documentDidChange`, only for a commit that names a shape matched or one of `cards` other than by moving it (added, removed, changed by an edit or a restack, or `elsewhere`), reaches another board (`boardsReached`: where a shape is drawn follows what hangs from what, and any move between boards, and any deletion that takes one, reaches one) or replaces the document; any other commit, a drag's every event and a keystroke in an unrelated note among them, costs a look at its change set and a few set intersections (`names(_:in:)`). It asks nothing: the edges are Topology's to say, and a move down a board or a deletion changes where a shape is drawn, not its edges (a mention is a name that outlives the shape, so a shape moved or deleted leaves its mention edge as it was). Never per frame.
- **What the core gets** (`outlines`, internal): empty while `editor.session != nil` (a drag; what was lit shows again when it ends, and an answer that lands meanwhile is kept), else `shown`, the `Outlines(tinted: groups)` made by `place` (`Outlines()` when nothing is lit), handed over as it is: a frame costs a copy of four array references and nothing is allocated, and the core strokes only what's in view ([drawing](drawing.md)); the core's tinted groups are under every other outline, so a selected or hovered shape's line shows over its tint, ring 0 where the selection's line stands (a line 2 wide, 4 off a closed shape) and each ring after it a line (2) and a gap (2) further out, so a ring's center is 4, 8, 12 off: whole pixels at 1× and 2×. The core does the rest of how it looks, for any group, knowing no light: a shape that's selected too has its tint one ring further out, clear of the selection's line, which would cover most of ring 0 (a note both lit and selected shows its green at 8); an arrow lit is covered, its outline as wide as its line is on screen and a point more (an arrow says its width, `StrokedShape`) rather than a stripe along it, so a lit arrow is its light's color, heads and all; and the hovered shape, whose faint outline Selection draws, reads above the lights: 85%, not 60%, while any is drawn. Validation's ring stands outside the lit rings (`OverlayContext.tintedOutset(of:)`), so a warned neighbor's ring isn't drawn over its tint. The canvas is asked to draw (`editor.setNeedsDisplay()`) only when a new `shown` differs from the old (`Outlines` and `TintedOutlines` are `Equatable`): an answer that lights nothing, after one that lit nothing, draws nothing. The minimap fills the same shapes in the same colors, so shapes off screen show there: a shape in several lights shows each, one inset inside the other, and while any is lit the rest of the picture dims and the lit fills are over the viewport's veil ([drawing](drawing.md)). Neighbors never outlines a shape faintly itself: `faintShapes` are Selection's alone (the hover, and the shape that holds the one hovered elsewhere).
- **Costs** (release, as `./build.sh bench` measures them): a hover on a shape with 10,000 neighbors costs the main actor some 0.8 ms (some 0.2 at 1,000, 0.14 with ten, whatever the board's size) from the hover to the lights landing (the set of what matched some 0.1 of it), and the engine's answer, off the main actor, some 10 ms (one view is asked where nothing stands in; a type with 10,000 instances, some 32 ms, Types' lights testing each for `[schema]`); 10,000 lit in view at 10% add some 7 ms to a canvas frame (the rings' paths, as ⌘A's do) and 4 to the minimap's, and all out of view some 0.35 ms (a look at each one's kept extent). All of them lit, however many (the cap of 10,000 matches is gone), the costs grow with what is lit: with 99,999 lit the landing costs the main actor some 12 to 18 ms (a lookup of the board each match is on and one of its ring, 100 ns apiece, and the set of what matched), a frame with them in view at 10% some 24 ms more and with them out of view some 15 (the same look at each kept extent), the minimap's drawing some 60; they are reported, not held (`BENCH_SIZES=10000,100000`), and the core's per-frame look at a lit shape's extent is what a board of that size would have to make cheaper. Only what's one edge away is lit by the shipped queries but Types', whose `:is-a` follows the types built on the one hovered.
- **The lists** (`NeighborList`, `editor.neighborList`, a `@MainActor @Observable` service `NeighborDetailsPlugin` registers; `Sources/Neighbors/NeighborList.swift`): what's connected to the shape a panel shows, in lists (`groups`, `NeighborGroup`: a title, a color or none), one for each light in the lights' order, each holding its light's `query` (not `between`: the arrows aren't listed), then two it gives itself in the language's own words for Topology's one tree: Inside (`:has(#id)`: what the shape is in, outermost first, since file order is) and Contains (`#id > *:not(:starts-at(*))`: what's directly in it, a frame's shapes or a subboard's board's in no frame, less the connections among them, since no list has what runs between; an arrow bound at one end is no connection, so it's a shape the board holds). So it lists what hovering the shape outlines, less what runs between, and knows no kind, edge type or concern. **The subject** is the shape whose lists are on screen: the panel's view says so as it appears and goes (`watch(_:)`, `unwatch(_:)`), so nothing is asked while no list shows (another tab, a hidden inspector, a takeover). **Asking** (`ask`): one `Task` for the subject, an ask under way cancelled; every folded list counted in one `queries.matches` batch of ids (`admitting` leaves the subject out), every open one run for its rows with `queries.run(QueryRequest(text, limit: QueryRequest.every, properties: .none, edges: view))` (id, kind, title, board, inside: the engine's names, so Neighbors imports no Titles), all at once in a task group, each in `.resolved` and, where `topology.hasStandIns`, `.literal` too, the views' rows merged a shape once, resolved first, as the hover's are; the subject is never listed. A request the engine refuses (a note asked what it holds: `cannot_contain`) is an empty list. The answer lands whole (`listing`, a `NeighborListing`: the subject, each list's `count` and `rows`, nil for a folded one, and a `stamp`); until it does the shape before's stays and the view shows nothing of it (`hasLanded`). No list is cut: an open one has every row, however many (99,999 for the hub of a board of 100,000); the rows are made, joined and compared with the lists shown (`readsAsShown`) off the main actor (`@concurrent`), which only the engine's own turns and the landing take, so a list of 100,000 costs the main actor what a list of ten does; a folded list is counted without asking for an id (with one view, the answer's own count). **Asking again**: `documentDidChange` re-arms one wait of `NeighborStyle.settle` (100 ms, on `editor.scheduler`) for any commit that did more than move shapes (a name, an edge, a container joined or left: a resize's every event is a commit, so they're one ask), and `topology.follow` asks at once for a change no commit made (a provide). A drag costs a look at its change set. An answer that reads as the one shown shows nothing (`land`: a shape watched again), unless a change to the file asked for it (`followsChange`): then the stamp moves, the names kept are let go of and every row is drawn again, since what a row is called and what its caption quotes are read as it's drawn, from shapes the commit may have changed (an untitled note's words, a name its text mentions). `boardDidChange` is overridden with nothing (a row says which board it's on as it's drawn). A row's place is `NeighborLine.place`, steps (`PlaceStep`s: the containers it is inside, a subboard's name a board, the board's name leading for a row on another board in none), which `DetailShapeRow` folds in the room the row has (`PlaceFold`, as every address is: the first, "…", the nearest whole, boards semibold); `detail` is the same as plain text, and what VoiceOver hears whole. **Folding** (`isFolded`, `setFolded`): by a list's place in `groups`, kept while the editor lives, for every shape; a folded list is only counted, and unfolding one whose rows weren't read asks. **Searching** (`search`, `setSearch`): typed text keeps, in every list, folded or not (their rows are asked for), the rows whose names it matches, ranked as every list ranks what's typed (`FuzzyMatcher.matches`, the best first, ties in the list's order); of several words (`PathMatcher`'s pieces) also the rows whose containers the words before the last name, as the `@` picker and Jump To match a path ([mentions](mentions.md)): the row's `inside` and its name are the path (`matcher.match(path:placingAtLeast: 1)`, an untitled container matched by what the engine calls it), in three bands, the rows whose name has the whole text from a substring up first, then those found by their path, then the loose name matches, each by score and then the list's order; a new subject clears it. `shown(in:)` is a list as shown (count and rows: all it has, none while folded, or the matches); `total` is of all, searched or not; `line(for:in:)` is a row as drawn (`NeighborLine`: the kind's symbol, in the tint the shape has, `editor.tint(of:)`, its own color where it has one, else its kind's, read as the row is drawn; the file's colors changing bumps the listing's `stamp`, which draws every row again; its name; where it is (`place(of:isElsewhere:)`): its path, the containers it is inside, outermost first, joined by " › " as every path says them (the row's `inside`, as the engine named them: a shape's identity is its path, so two called alike are told apart), less those the board shown is inside and the subject is when the row is inside them (`topology.ancestors(of:)` of the row, compared by id with the chain of each: so what a subboard or a frame holds, and what is directly on the board shown, say nothing); else, for a shape in no container left but on another board, that board's name (read from the row's `inside` at its board's owner's depth, the root `editor.rootName` else "Board"); and its light's caption; spoken as it draws: the name, where it is, the caption), reading the file without observing it (`withoutObservingDocument`), so a row is drawn again as an answer lands and never on every commit. **A row's name** is its title, as the engine names it; for a shape with none, what the table of contents calls it (`ContentsState.name(of:)`: an untitled note's text on one line, "Empty Note", a pill by what it stands for), read once for an answer (`names`) as the row is drawn, and for every row by a search's first keystroke; else, with no table of contents, its kind's name. `go(to:)` is `editor.goTo` on the scheduler's next turn (its board first, one step back); `hover(_:)` and `endHover(_:)` set `editor.hovered`, or `hoveredElsewhere` for a shape the canvas doesn't show, and let go only of the hover the row set, as the table of contents' rows do: so the pointer on a row lights that shape's own neighbors. Debug builds count `asked`, `shown` and `linesDrawn`. **Costs** (release, `./build.sh bench`): the main actor's time from a shape's lists being asked for to their landing is some 0.4 ms with 1,000 neighbors and 1.3 with 10,000 (0.2 with ten, whatever the board; folded, 0.2 and 0.7), the engine's answers off it some 3 and 47 ms (every row is made, none cut); with 99,999 neighbors the same 1.5 ms (folded, 0.6), the engine's answers off the main actor some 0.8 s; a keystroke searching 10,000 rows some 7 ms, and the first, which reads every untitled row's name, some 45 ms once, and over 99,999 rows some 100 ms and, the first, some 1.2 s, all on the main actor ([benchmarks](benchmarks.md)).
- **The tab** (`NeighborDetailsPlugin`, id `neighbor-details`, `Sources/Neighbors/NeighborDetails.swift`): a plugin value of its own after Neighbors and `DetailPanelPlugin` (last of Neighbors' four lines in the shipped list), so a list without the panel leaves it out and keeps the lights, and one with it and no panel is refused (`ownerNotInstalled`); it needs no light and no `NeighborsPlugin` (its lists are then Inside and Contains). ## The whole reach

- **What it is** (the owner's approved doc "Reach on hover", 2026-11): a second way to hover, behind View › Show Whole Reach (`CanvasCommand.showWholeReach`, Neighbors' own command, no key, `ViewMenu.Panels` placed last, after Show Minimap, checked while on: [keyboard](keyboard.md)). A hover lights everything each light's step leads to from the shape, and on from there: downstream on a plain hover, upstream with ⌥, mentions' and types' lights the same, each following its own query; Neighbors knows no edge type. With it off, the hover is exactly `NeighborState`'s, and its suites are unchanged.
- **The switch** (`ReachSettings`, `Sources/Neighbors/Reach/ReachSettings.swift`, `@MainActor`): `showsWholeReach`, off until turned on, kept as it's set (`ReachSettings.app`, the app's, in `UserDefaults.standard` under `ShowsWholeReach`; `ReachSettings(defaults:)` a suite's; `ReachSettings(showsWholeReach:keep:)` kept nowhere), and `reducesMotion`, the system's Reduce Motion (`NSWorkspace.shared.accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion`) unless a test gives its own. `NeighborsPlugin()` uses the app's, `NeighborsPlugin(reach:)` a caller's (ShippedTesting's `ShippedPlugins` supplies one per test, `ShippedPlugins.reach`, off, kept nowhere, so no test of the shipped list reads or writes the app's defaults; every test that builds `NeighborsPlugin` by hand, or the list through `ShippedList.plugins(supplying:)`, passes `NeighborsPlugin(reach: ReachSettings())` itself, since `NeighborsPlugin()`'s editors read the app's). Every editor made with the settings follows a change at once (`follow`, a weak list of `HoverLights`, as Spelling's texts follow theirs): `HoverLights` drops the state it had (`ReachState.stop()` first) and makes the other, so the inactive way asks nothing and hands the core nothing; the shape hovered as the switch turns on is the new state's subject, so it asks its reach at once and rests, dims, pulses and is let go of as any is; the one hop made as it turns off asks about the shape hovered at once too (`NeighborState.askAboutSubject()`), so neither way waits for the hover to change.
- **Strength** (`ReachStyle`, `Sources/Neighbors/Reach/ReachStyle.swift`: every number of the look and the timing): 1 at hop 1; past it 0.6^(hop−1) × min(1, share), the share being the engine's (what reaches a shape splits among the shapes it reaches next, and adds up where paths meet: [queries](queries.md)). A ring is drawn at its strength in steps of 1/40 (`ringStep`), 1 + 2.5 × strength view points wide (`TintedOutlines.width`, [drawing](drawing.md)), in its light's color mixed toward the canvas (`Swatch.canvas`, each appearance's) by the step's strength, opaque, so an arrow's line doesn't show through its cover (`ReachStyle.color`, made once a light and step: `colors`); none under 4%, whose shape sinks into the dim. The dim keeps each lit shape at 1 for hop 1, else the nearest of 0.65, 0.35 and 0.12 (`fade`: people tell three or four ordered steps apart, and each step the core draws is a transparency layer, some 3.5 ms a frame offscreen).
- **The arrows.** One the reach travels (from a hop to the next: the answer's `carriers`) is lit at the strength of the shape it reaches; one between two shapes of the reach, or one and the shape hovered, that it didn't travel (`spared`: a cycle's, two at one hop) is kept by the dim, untinted, at its weaker end's strength (the start's 1) and never above the second step (`ReachStyle.sparedMost`, 0.35: it isn't the reach's path, and at full strength on a dense board such arrows read as patches of noise beside the lit ones), arriving with its later end; every other arrow dims. A cycle never lights the shape hovered.
- **Asking** (`ReachState.ask(about:)`): a new shape asks twice at once, two tasks: the lights' one hop (`light.lit(selector)` in one `queries.matches` batch, in `.resolved` and, where `topology.hasStandIns`, `.literal`, the views merged as `NeighborState` merges them), which shows as hop 1 as soon as it lands (the walk costs some 9 µs a shape off the main actor, 180 ms for a reach of 20,000, so hop 1 can't wait for it); and each light's reach (`queries.reaches`, one `QueryReachRequest` a light: `light.query(":scope")`, `light.between?(":scope")`, `.resolved`, `strength: ReachStyle.strength` so a pill or an arrow that leads to several shapes is at the strongest of them, the engine reading both views and adding a pill the reach goes into at what it stands for), which the rest and the pulse show (its hop 1 the same shapes). A new subject, another board or a switch cancels both. A pill hovered: the reach's first step is taken in the literal view too (the engine does it for a start that is a stand-in: [queries](queries.md)), so its own arrows' far ends are hop 1 in the reach as in the one hop, and the walk goes on from them. A reach that fails (too slow, a refusal) shows the one hop in its place and no pulse when every light's failed, and a failed light's one hop beside the others' reach otherwise (`oneHopFound`); the reach waits for the one hop's answer to have it.
- **The landing** (`ReachLanding`, `Sources/Neighbors/Reach/ReachLanding.swift`, pure `Sendable` values): each answer is made into `ReachFound`s (a reach's shapes, then its carriers at their far ends' hops and shares, its spared with their fade and tier; a one-hop answer's all hop 1, share 1) and landed off the main actor (`@concurrent`) from `editor.file` and `editor.board` as the answer came: each match drawn as itself on the board shown, else the card that holds it (`file.shape(on:holding:)`, a walk a board), at the nearest hop and strongest strength of what it holds, none on a board above; the subject and its card never lit; for each set of keys some light has, the lights shown together in light order, a shape's ring how many of them it was in before (as `NeighborState`'s), its rings in groups by tier (hop 1, 2, 3, and 4 and on), ring step and ring, and the dim's focus for each step of the pulse (`focus[0...4]`: the subject as drawn at 1, each shape of the tiers shown at its fade, the arriving tier's at 1, the spared). The main actor swaps the landing in and builds the outlines from its groups (a `TintedOutlines` a group, its color and width from its step, the arriving tier's at full) and the focus (`Dimming(focus:)`, a dictionary handed over as it is), so a pulse step picks groups and costs O(groups), never O(lit). Neither task holds the state across an `await` (each takes it again, `self?`, as it resumes, and reads the file and the board as values before it lands), so a window closed while an answer is on its way lets the editor and its services go and what resumes lands nothing. Debug builds count `landed` and `landedOnMain` (a landing made on the main thread: none).
- **The timing** (all on `editor.scheduler`): the subject and the keys are watched as `NeighborState` watches them. A new shape shows its hop 1 at once, with no dim; once the pointer has rested on it 150 ms (`ReachStyle.rest`) the board dims (`dims`; a shape that lights nothing leaves the board as it is: there is nothing to pick out) and, once its reach has landed, the pulse begins: each 80 ms (`pulseStep`) a tier arrives, drawn as hop 1 is (full strength, the widest ring) for that step and at its own strength from the next, hops 4 and on together at 240 ms, settled (`pulseStep` 4) by 320; with `ReachStyle.pulses` off or `reducesMotion`, settled at once. The dim stays as the pointer goes from shape to shape (each showing its hop 1 until it rests: the pulse waits for the rest, so a sweep never pulses). Other keys on the same shape (⌥ held or let go) show the other pole's hop 1 at once and its pulse after a new rest, asking nothing (every light was asked). With nothing hovered the dim and the lights stay 150 ms (`release`), then go (`stop()`); the shape left, hovered again meanwhile, keeps what it showed and asks nothing. A drag hides everything (`outlines` is empty while `editor.session` is set, and the core hides a dim then), and what was shown comes back with the drag's end. While the camera moves (a pan, a pinch, a slide, an animation) the core leaves the dim out and draws the board as the hover alone does, the rings and every other outline as ever, and draws it again 150 ms after the camera last changed (`Style.dimCameraRest`, the core's, [drawing](drawing.md)): ReachState keeps offering its dim the whole while and knows nothing of the camera; the dim's transparency layers had a pan on a reach under 60 frames a second on screen (about 41 around a card with ⌥ held, where today's hover held 59). Another board lets go of everything.
- **Asking again**: a change to the edges (`topology.follow`) that touches the shape shown or a shape either answer matched, and a commit that names the shape other than by moving it, adds a shape, names a shape matched or drawn as a card other than by moving it, reaches another board or replaces the document, ask again once commits have stopped for 100 ms (`ReachStyle.settle`, re-armed by each: one ask for a run of them, as the panel's lists wait), keeping the pulse at its step (a reach settled stays settled). An edge change of any kind while an ask is out asks again once both answers have landed (`changedWhileAsking`), so an answer older than the edges doesn't stay, though nothing it touched was matched. A drag's moves ask nothing.
- **What it costs.** The main actor's part of a hover is reading the subject, building the two batches and swapping each landing in; the engine's batches and the landing run off it; a pulse step is O(groups), a few hundred at most. The engine's walk is some 9 µs a shape with between (Queries'). The dim's frame cost grows with the populated strength steps (three and full), some 3.5 ms a layer offscreen, not with what's lit ([drawing](drawing.md)). `ReachBenchmarks.swift` holds the hover, the walk, the landing, a pulse step and the frame ([benchmarks](benchmarks.md)); a fan-out, all hop 1, lands at some 0.1 µs a lit shape on the main actor, which the chain's 200,000 lit (1.2 ms) does not.
