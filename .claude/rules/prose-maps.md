# Prose maps

Prose maps: which words of a text in plain words say which part of a text in a lower language (a JSON Schema, code, a query, data), asked of the model; targets, maps, the service, the request, and their tests.

Related: [language models](language-models.md), [types](types.md), [drawing](drawing.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

In the guide, `docs/plugin-system.md`: [Prose maps](../../docs/plugin-system.md#prose-maps).

## Architecture

- ProseMaps (platform, an engine, on LanguageModels, that declares no extension point; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Prose maps") says, for any plugin that keeps a text in words beside a text in a lower language, which words say which part, as a source map does: a type's highlights are its words mapped into its schema (Types' `SchemaTarget`). A map is a list of links, each the words as the prose writes them and an address in the target, both ends found by what they are in the texts as they are now, never by a position (words typed around a link keep it; a link whose words or part are gone drops). The model says the links in a call of its own, once the target exists, whoever wrote it, so a map checks what made the target rather than restating it; nothing is saved, nor an undo step.
- A target (`ProseTarget`) is a type with no value, its language's plugin's: `name` (the model's word for it and its block's tag, "schema") and `guide` (what the model is told of it inside every target's frame, ending in a line break as the prompt literals do, which the frame takes off); its `Address` is by default a quote of the target's text, found where it's written exactly once, runs of whitespace in it matching any run (`ProseMapText.find`); a language with an address of its own names it (`LosslessStringConvertible`, as the model writes it), finds it (`locate`, every link's at once, so a parser reads the text once; nil where it leads nowhere) and says what each leads to (`role(of:)`, default `.element`). Conform in an extension. One legend for every target: `LinkRole` `.element` (something the target has: a field, a function) or `.rule` (something it keeps: a bound, a condition).
- A map (`ProseMap<Target>`, an immutable final class, `Sendable`): its texts and its links (`ProseMap.Link`: `quote`, `inProse`, `address`, `inTarget`, `role`), in the prose's order, none overlapping, each found on both sides (`ProseMapText.anchor`, the old `SchemaHighlights.anchor`: in order, from where the last ends then from the start, whole words first, as written before ignoring case; a link whose address doesn't parse still holds its words, as the model listed them); `link(at:)`, `links(atTarget:)` (innermost first), `excerpt(of:)` (`ProseMapText.excerpt`: each line after the first less the indent of the line it starts on). `ProseMap(placing:prose:target:)` places links you have; `ProseLink` is a link as said.
- `ProseMapService` (`editor.proseMaps`, `RenderContext.proseMaps`): `map(_:of:prose:target:context:askingIf:)` is the shape's pair's answer placed, else its last answer for that target placed on the texts as they are, else nil; the same object while its texts (the same strings: one string compare each when they are), its context and the answers it could show are the same; it places again (`placed`, debug builds, counts) only when they change, and finds the links in the target again only when the target or the links' addresses changed (prose being typed finds none). Give it the texts as the model reads them; a reader that draws other text moves the ranges itself (a type's, with Mentions' `MentionReading.drawnRange`). A pair read with no answer is wanted, if `askingIf` (called only then, and only for a pair that hasn't failed and isn't out) says so, and handed over as the frame ends (`ProseMapFrames`, an overlay that draws nothing), so only what's drawn in full is asked about; it's asked if its prose isn't blank, its shape isn't being edited and is on the board shown, the editor has a model, and nothing was said of it this session (answers are process-wide, `ProseMapAnswers`, by target, trimmed prose, target and context, 1,024 kept), it isn't being asked or waiting, and it didn't fail: at most 4 at once (`concurrentCalls`), the rest waiting, which another board shown drops for shapes not on it. An answer is kept for its pair whatever the shape says when it lands, and is the shape's last unless one asked after it landed first, and an answer a shape reads for its pair is its last too (a copy's, one undo brought back: `clock` orders both); the canvas draws again and `revision(of:)` (observed per shape) changes. A failure keeps nothing, its pair not asked again until a sign-in in Settings (`languageModelSignInDidChange`: the failures forgotten and the canvas drawn again), but for one that may pass (`LanguageModelError.isTransient`: too many requests, overloaded, the network, a timeout, a 5xx), which is cooled for `ProseMapService.retryDelay` (30 s, on `editor.scheduler`) and asked again when next drawn, so a burst of calls that meets a rate limit doesn't leave shapes without highlights for the session; a cancelled call is asked again when next read; a shape taken out of the file, from any board, keeps nothing but its answers (one moved to another board keeps its last); a file put in place keeps nothing. No call is cancelled while the editor lives; the service going with its editor (a file closed; an `isolated deinit`) cancels every call out (`calls`, by the number each was asked as), so none runs on spending tokens on an answer nothing lands (an answer is kept only as it lands). Texts are the same for a kept map only when they're equal and as long in UTF-8 (`same`: an accent written as two scalars is another map, its ranges its own text's), and an answer's key is hashed once, as it's made, since a pair with no answer is looked up on every frame that draws its shape. A reader whose readiness changes without a commit (a sync's `NO_CHANGE`) calls `editor.setNeedsDisplay()`, so its frame asks.
- The request (`ProseMapPrompt`): `system(for:context:)` is the `frame` with the target's name (`{name}`) and guide (`{guide}`, less its final line breaks) in it, and the context after it, a blank line apart, only when there's one (so a request without is sent what it always was: a type's `highlightsReferences` only for words that name other types); `request(_:prose:target:context:)` is one user message of `<prose>` and the target's block, each trimmed, an empty one left out, to Haiku 5.5 (`model`) at `effort` medium, its temperature its own, `maxTokens` 2,048, no `fallbacks` (the service has none for it); `links(from:)` reads the answer, a JSON array of `{"text", "address"}` (Foundation's `JSONSerialization`): an end-of-turn answer, unwrapped (`ModelAnswer.unwrapped`, tag `links`), whole or its last run of lines from `[` to `]` that reads; items without words or an address left out. The prompt literals follow Types' rule (`SchemaPrompts`): raw, flush left, closed on a line of their own after a blank line. `scripts/schema-prompts/highlight_evals.py` reads the frame and the settings out of ProseMapPrompt.swift and the schema's guide out of SchemaPrompts.swift, so the evals ask what ships.

## Testing

- ProseMapsTests use fixtures only: `Snippet` (code, its addresses quotes) and `Steps` (a recipe's lines by number, `3!` a check and a rule, its `locate` counted in `Steps.located`), read on CanvasTesting's boxes, pockets and cards with a `ScriptedLanguageModel` that answers every request with `answer` (`MapBench`: `read`, `frame()`, whose `renderBitmap` ends a frame and hands over what was read, `settle()`, `keySaved()`); `ProseMapService.forgetEveryAnswer()` (debug builds) forgets the process-wide answers a test before kept. The service's "a call out when the file closes is cancelled" lets go of a bench whose model holds the call: the editor goes and the call ends, cancelled. A type's highlights are TypesTests' (`HighlightBench`, "Type highlights", "Type highlights shown", and the references' tests) and, under a note's editor, StickyNotesTests' ("Type notes").
