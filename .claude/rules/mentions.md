# Mentions

Mentions: mentionable kinds, mention text and its stored form, scopes, mention edges, `MentionState`, pills on the canvas and in text, the `@` picker, mention text in the panel, Mentioned In, and mentions' warnings.

Related: [fuzzy matching](fuzzy-matching.md), [topology](topology.md), [titles](titles.md), [validation](validation.md), [text editing](text-editing.md), [slash commands](slash-commands.md), [detail panel](detail-panel.md), [table of contents](table-of-contents.md), [context menus](context-menus.md), [selection](selection.md), [jump to](jump-to.md), [shape references](shape-references.md), [boards in a file](boards-in-a-file.md) ([every topic](../../CLAUDE.md#map)).

## Architecture

- Mentions (a concern, on ContextMenus, DetailPanel, FuzzyMatching, Selection, SlashCommands, TableOfContents, TextEditing, Titles, Topology and Validation; `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Mentions") lets text mention shapes, each mention an inline pill: the kind's symbol and the shape's name, no "@" (monochrome on a shape's surface, tinted on a neutral one, `MentionSurface`). A kind opts into being mentioned with `x.mentionable(label:untitled:symbol:detail:tint:following:)` (`registrar.mentionable(K.self, …)` for another plugin's kind, `mentionableEveryShape(where:)`; the `ShapeMentions` point): its name is `label`, else its title (Titles' `registry.title(of:)`, named or derived), else `untitled`, else "Untitled" and the kind's name (`isPlaceholder`: the picker leaves such a shape out, since its kind gives it no name, though a mention of it shows it live), each read from the shape alone (`registry.mentionAppearance(of:)`, a `MentionAppearance`). Shipped: notes (`untitled: { $0.props.text.firstLine }`: the first line with words, each mention in it "@…", so no name holds another shape's, which would grow as notes that mention each other are written), subboards (`following: .opens`: following a mention of one enters its board, `editor.goInto`, where every other kind's is `goTo`), and schema cards (by their titles; mentioned from each other's descriptions, below), each tinted as its row is (a note's yellow, `NoteStyle.tint`; a subboard's indigo, `SubboardStyle.border`, the system's, lighter in the dark), so a kind has one color in the list, the picker and Mentioned In; the panel's header keeps its symbol neutral. Text holds mentions when its type is `ScopedMentionText<Scope>` (`MentionText` is `<AnyMentionable>`), stored as one JSON string with inline `@[label](shape:UUID)` tokens (in a label `\]` and `\\`; `\@[` is a literal `@[`; backslashes just before one are doubled), so `canvas draw`'s `text: "…"` works (a draw's string spells a backslash `\\`) and an older reader sees text; its `runs` (`MentionRun`: `.text`, `.mention(id, label:)`) are parsed once; equality compares `stored`, so a value read writes back byte for byte, and one made from runs is canonical (ids in uppercase); a string literal and `init(plain:)` are plain text. Its schema is `mentions.text`, a string, which takes no length or pattern (`MentionTextFormat.checkBounds`). The label a mention holds is a snapshot, shown only while its shape isn't live. A mention is a name (`ShapeNaming`), not a binding: deleting its shape writes nothing, its pills show the name they hold, dashed (`MentionResolution.missing`; `.unavailable` for a shape kept aside, not mentionable, or on a board the app can't show), and undoing the deletion makes them live (redoing it, missing again); a copy renames the mentions of shapes copied along, and a cut and paste is a move (Clipboard's), so a mention from outside what moved shows it missing between the cut and the paste and live again once it's pasted, on whatever board. A note's text became `MentionText` in its v3 migration, which escapes what would now read as a mention through a frozen escaper (`MentionTextFormat.escapeLookalikesV1`). What a text may mention is its scope (`MentionScope`): `AnyMentionable`, `Only<Kind>` (a vertical's own kind), and `DeclaredMentionScope`s any plugin declares (`registrar.mentionScope(S.self)`, the `MentionScopes` point) and kinds join (`x.mentionable(in: S.self)`, `registrar.mentionable(K.self, in:)`); a scope limits what the picker offers and says so in the schema (`x-canvas-mentions`), and never refuses a mention that's there; the one shipped text restricted is a schema card's description (`SchemaPromptText`, `ScopedMentionText<Only<SchemaCard>>`). Who mentions whom is Topology's: each mention is a `mention` edge (`MentionEdge`, a root) from the shape whose text holds it, slot the property, repeats counted (Mentions' source over `registry.namingKinds`, through `forEachNamingLeaf`). `MentionState` (`editor.mentions`, a service) reads it (`mentions(in:)`, in text order; `mentioners(of:)`, file-wide: the board shown's in document order, then the other boards' in the file's order), after `topology.catchUp(changes)` (and it looks Topology up as it's made, so Topology's service hears each commit first); it keeps how each shape mentioned anywhere in the file shows now, resolved again for what each change to the edges touched (the mention-typed ends of each change Topology tells it, `topology.follow`, whatever caused it, and a commit's `elsewhere`; a move costs nothing; an undo or redo is such a commit, so a rename undone gives every pill its name back), so a switch of board reads no mention text and resolves nothing, and the catalog the picker searches, which is the whole file's, every mentionable shape with a name on every board the editor can show (walked from the root, each owner asked `registry.ownsShowableBoard`, `canShow`'s rule), read as the editor opens or a file or board is put in place, and kept up to date by each commit, `elsewhere` included, so a switch reads none of it; never transacting; `labels` (`MentionLabels`, `Sendable`, taken in O(1): it shares the state's resolutions until their next change copies them once) is what `plainText` reads for every shape the file mentions, to read off the main actor (`text.plainText { labels.label(of: $0) }` is `plainText(text)`; a query's mention text, through QueryLexicon's `MentionQueriesPlugin`, which takes them as a fact of the file, `MentionedNames`: they're a function of the records, so a text search reads only the file), as of the last commit the state heard; a mention names a shape on any board of the file and shows it live (`resolve` looks through `editor.shape(inFile:)`, the canvas through the core's `context.shape(inFile:)`, a render's lookup anywhere in the file; both find only shapes on boards `canShow` allows, so one the file keeps without using is unavailable, never followed), so a rename, a deletion with its board, and their undo show whatever board is shown when they happen (`rowsAffected` names the mentioners of the `elsewhere` the table of contents hands it on going back); following one (`goTo`) shows its board first, one Back step; the picker lists shapes on every board (matches from anywhere, the board shown's first on a tie and with nearness; an empty `@` lists what's near on the board shown, then the boards it's inside, so a subboard is mentioned from inside itself (on the line of a command that names its kinds, then the rest of those, on every board: below), and a shape may mention itself (left out of its own empty `@`'s nearby, and of a typed query's matches while its name, a note's first line, holds the `@query` being typed); `docs/plugin-system.md`, "Mentions across boards"): `observe(_:_:)` tells a view which targets changed, `labelRevision(of:)` observes one target for SwiftUI, `rowsAffected(by:)` names the mentioners whose rows a rename changes (`MentionRows.affected`, with `context.plainText(_:)` in the row: a note's), `candidates(matching:in:for:)` ranks with every list's tiers (FuzzyMatching's `FuzzyMatcher`, over the catalog's folded names) on the folded query (exact 1000, prefix 800, word prefixes 600, substring 400, an fzf-style subsequence at most 300, one Damerau typo 100 for a query of 4 or more that found fewer than 8 above; two linear passes, the best 50 through FuzzyMatching's `BoundedHeap` (Jump To's list uses the same); then recent picks and nearness), over the catalog's shapes the scope admits: for a scope but `AnyMentionable` its **members** (`ScopeMembers`, made at the scope's first ask in one pass and kept up to date as each catalog entry is read, taken out with it), so a scope that admits few, the public shapes a reference names, costs what it admits and an empty query reads a narrow scope's members whole (`MentionRanking.narrowScope`, 512, or an eighth of the catalog) for the nearest, never squares grown over a board; `candidates(matching:in:for:kinds:admitting:)` takes what a slash command asks of the one shape it takes, besides the scope ([slash commands](slash-commands.md)): `kinds`, the most kinds of shape to list, and `admitting`, a rule asked wherever a shape would be listed (a recent pick, a nearby shape, a board the text is inside, a match, a member) of the shapes the scope and the kinds admit and never of the rest, so a shape either refuses counts toward nothing (not the eight that keep the typo pass from running, nor the 50) and a typed query's order is what it was less those; with kinds the shapes ranked are the catalog's of those kinds, **members** kept as a scope's are (`MemberSet`, keyed by the set of kinds, made at the first ask in one pass, some 0.5 ms at 10,000, and kept up to date as entries are read and taken out; the scope is asked of each, so a narrow scope's own members are not the ones ranked then), so a command that takes few of a big board's shapes (`/move-to`'s frames and subboards) costs what they are: an empty query reads them whole for the nearest (`narrowScope`), never squares grown over the board, which a rule alone, asked of every shape the squares gather and then of the whole board, took some 4.6 ms at 10,000 to find five subboards, and a typed one ranks them alone; and an empty query lists as many of them as a typed one may (`maxMatches`, not `nearbyShown`), on every board, since a command's few shapes are the places it can go: the nearest on the board shown (those on other boards not asked there), the boards the text is inside, then the rest (`farther`), the nearest board first (the fewest boards apart, up the file's tree to where the two paths meet and down again), on a tie in the catalog's order, so `/move-to @` inside a subboard lists a subboard on the root with nothing typed; with no kinds the shapes ranked are as they were, and with neither every text but a command line's asks nothing more of a shape, `follow(_:)` goes to a shape on the next scheduler turn (`goTo`, no undo step; ⌘[ comes back), and `hover(_:)`/`endHover(_:)` set `editor.hovered`. What mentions a shape in the panel and the menus is the file's, read from Topology's edges into it: `isMentioned(_:)` (another shape on any board the editor can show mentions it; its own mention doesn't count; its edges, never a board's), `backlinks(of:)` (`MentionBacklink`s in `mentioners(of:)`'s order, the board shown's first, the shape left out: each mentioner named as it's mentioned, else by its title, else its kind's label, with its symbol and its kind's tint, and a quote, about 60 characters of its text as plain text with live names around its first mention of the shape, on one line, cut between words with "…"; each read from its record wherever it is, in the board shown and the file read unobserved, `editor.withoutObservingDocument { … }`, the core's, and never kept, so a view of it redraws only as the revision says and no commit copies the file for it), `backlinkRevision(of:)` (observed per target: it changes for a commit that adds, changes or removes a shape that mentions or mentioned it on any board (the ends of the mention edges each change Topology tells it names, `TopologyChange.touched(types:in:)`, so an arrow bound to a mentioned shape isn't one; a provide's are bumped as it's told, a commit's as Mentions hears the commit, and what each shape it added or changed, or touched `elsewhere`, mentions, since an edit that keeps a mention changes its quote and no edge), not one that only moves it, for one that changes a name a mentioner's quote shows, and on a switch of board, which puts that board's mentioners first; only targets read since their last change tell observers), and `showWhatMentions(_:)` (on the scheduler's next turn, since a menu in text is being handled by the view it ends: ends editing, then `goTo`s the mentioners, the board shown's first, so it stays if any are here and else shows the first one's board, one Back step; nothing if none). A kind's opt-in (`x.mentionable`, `registrar.mentionable(K.self, …)`; not `mentionableEveryShape`, since the panel has no section for every shape's record) brings its Mentioned In tab (`MentionsTab`, order 300: one section, `shownWhile` the shape `isMentioned`, `enabledWhileReadOnly`, a line per mentioner, its symbol, name and quote, the pointer on it hovering it if it's on the board shown and a click going to it (`goTo`: its board first, one Back step); `MentionState.backlinksShown`, debug builds, records what each list drew), so a list with a mentionable kind needs `DetailPanelPlugin()`; and Show What Mentions is in the canvas's context menu (an item for the canvas in `MentionsMenuGroup`, order 50, after a kind's own items, shown when a shape the menu is for is mentioned), so a list with Mentions needs `ContextMenusPlugin()`. Drawing: a kind lists where it draws mention text (`x.mentionText { shape in [MentionTextPlacement(text, in: box, style:, lines:, overCanvas:)] }`, the `MentionTexts` point; `x.mentionText { shape, shapes in … }` when where the text is depends on the shapes around it, through the `ShapeContext` (from the kept geometry: `shapes.geometry(of:)`), and `registry.mentionTextPlacements(of:in:)` takes the editor's `shapeContext`; `overCanvas` says the text is drawn over the canvas itself and not on the shape's surface, which only Find reads, to wash a hit), its render draws exactly those (`context.drawMentionText`: string drawing, each built string kept by what it says and how each mention shows, `MentionTextMemo`: the 2,048 used last, and never one the frame being drawn has drawn, so it keeps all a frame draws and the next builds none, each frame's end marked by an overlay that draws nothing; a pill's image is never kept as a bitmap, `cacheMode` `.never`; zoomed out a note is its skeleton, which draws no mention text, so a frame there builds no string and lays out no pill, and the memo trims back to 2,048 at its end), and `editor.mentionPills(of:)` is where each pill is (world units, `isLive`); Find's asking for a range's line rects makes the text's layout and keeps it with the text (`MentionTextMemo.Entry.lineRects`: TextEditing's `StringDrawingLayout` over the string with its pills' stand-ins, per width), so its rects are a lookup; drawing keeps none (`Entry.pills` places the pills with a layout already kept, else with one made for the call and let go, as it did before Find: a layout is a text storage and a layout manager, which the canvas would hold for every text with a pill in it; debug builds' `MentionDrawingCounts.layoutsKept` counts the kept). `MentionFindPlugin` (Mentions', after Find and Mentions in the shipped list: [find](find.md)) gives Find every `MentionTexts` placement's text (`registrar.findableTexts("mention text", where:)`, the kinds that list placements), each mention its live name (`MentionState.resolve`), a placement's place in the kind's list its field; the placements `overCanvas` are another contribution, "mention text over the canvas", whose hits are washed ([find](find.md)); a hit in a name is its pill's one character, mapped from the text read to the text drawn (`text.reading(spelling:)`, a `MentionReading`: the text with each mention spelled as its reader says, and `drawnRange(of:)`, a spelling or any of it its pill; a schema card's highlights map the model's words with it too); `affected` is `rowsAffected(by:)`, which Find asks before reading any text. One `MentionTextStyle` (font, color, fixed line height, surface, and the lines' alignment, natural unless a kind says) is shared by a kind's render and its editor; a pill (`MentionAttachment`, one U+FFFC, which adopts the core's `ReadsAsText` with the name it shows, `writtenLabel`, no "@": a selection across it, which Find's ⌘F seeds its query with, reads as that name) is 17 points tall in 16-point text, so a note's lines keep their 19, and its kind's symbol is centered in a slot an em wide (`MentionStyle.symbolSlot`, as wide as the widest shipped symbol; a wider one is drawn smaller), so a live pill is as wide as its label makes it whatever its kind: text measured from itself alone, where no shape is looked up (`MentionTextStyle.measuredString(_:width:)`: a kind's `DerivedProps`, a note's height and a schema card's, from their text), makes each mention a live pill by the name it holds, as wide as it's drawn while its shape is called that (one drawn missing has no symbol, so it's narrower). Its faces (Selection's): a click on a pill edits its shape from the pill (`beginEditing(_:at:)` at its center, unless the shape is read-only), a double-click on a live one of a read-only shape goes to it (an editable shape's text view took the click before), and the pointer over a live one hovers what it names, if that's on the board shown; none on a note drawn as a skeleton, zoomed out, where a click selects it, a double-click edits it (a read-only one's is refused, going nowhere) and the pointer hovers it.
